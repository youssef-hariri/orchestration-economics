## Timing disclosure (read first)

This OSF registration is a **memorialization**: the documents above were authored on the
dates shown, but they are registered on OSF on 2026-08-05 — i.e., after the start of
main-run generation (2026-08-04) and after Amendment A1 had been approved. The author
had no prior OSF registration for this study; this record is created to make the full
protocol and its amendment history publicly inspectable.

Key ordering facts (each verifiable from the repository provenance log `MANIFEST.md`):

1. Protocol 01–04 were frozen before any pilot or main-run data informed them.
2. Amendment A1 was drafted and approved **before the first main-run generation call**;
   it concerns only measurement infrastructure (full-text judging, judge output
   integrity, audit fields), not hypotheses, cells, or stopping rules.
3. Amendment A2 was drafted and author-approved on 2026-08-05 **while main-run judging
   was still running and before interim-2 data lock**; it adds estimation and robustness
   layers (bootstrap CIs, BH-FDR, Type-II ANOVA with partial η², Rouder JZS Bayes
   factors, leave-one-family-out / leave-one-problem-out sensitivity, sensitivity power)
   and contains an integrity clause (§4): A2 outputs may never alter the pre-registered
   stopping decisions, which rest solely on the primary tests.

No amendment altered hypotheses, design factors, cells, family composition, problem set,
sample plan, or stopping rules.
