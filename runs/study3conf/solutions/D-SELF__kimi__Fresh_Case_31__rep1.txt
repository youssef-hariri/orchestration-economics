## Executive Brief for Leadership

This policy replaces fragmented, country-by-country salary bands with a single **Global Equity Band (GEB)** architecture. It resolves three converging pressures: employee perceptions of inequity driven by public cross-border pay comparisons; volatile and divergent labor-market data; and rapidly expanding, non-uniform pay-transparency mandates.

**Our Strategic Choice:** We reject both pure cost-of-labor arbitrage and naive global flat-pay equality. In their place, we adopt **intentional, legally defensible compression**. Geographic differentials are capped at 25%, statistically grounded, and fully published. The result is a system that respects local labor markets without sacrificing the trust and administrative coherence required by a remote-first culture.

> **Core Thesis:** Compensation is determined first by **globally consistent role value**, second by a **compressed, published, and statistically grounded** Geographic Index, and third by **individual placement within a transparent band**.

## I. Scope & Exclusions

| Population | Status | Governing Instrument |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Regular employees (indefinite, fixed-term, full-time, part-time pro-rata) | **In scope** | This policy |
| Cross-border transferees / relocating employees | **In scope** | This policy + Mobility Addendum |
| Fixed-fee independent contractors, vendors, consultancies | **Explicitly excluded** | *Vendor & Contractor Compensation Protocol* (deliverable-based fees, no band linkage) |
| Statutory agency / leased workers | **Excluded** | Agency master agreement |

**Contractor Firewall.** No contractor fee schedule may be derived from, benchmarked against, or mathematically linked to an internal GEB Tier Band. This severance defeats misclassification claims under U.S. ABC tests, EU false-self-employment directives, and Canadian common-law employee tests.

## II. Guiding Principles

1. **Role Value Precedes Geography.** Compensation is anchored to the global level of the role (scope, complexity, impact), not the employee’s residence.
2. **Gender-Neutral Job Architecture.** All levels are validated by an external industrial-organizational psychology firm for gender neutrality per **EU Pay Transparency Directive Article 4** and are mapped to Canadian **SEARC** criteria (Skill, Effort, Responsibility, Working Conditions).
3. **Transparent Compression.** Geographic differentials are capped, published, and statistically grounded. The maximum spread between Tier 3 and Tier 1 is **25%**.
4. **Converged Methodology, Jurisdictionally Adaptive Format.** Underlying bands and indices are identical worldwide; disclosure format respects local statutory wording, timing, co-determination, and data-privacy rules.
5. **Pay Portability Without Punitive Cuts.** Downward relocation never reduces base salary. Upward relocation triggers immediate band alignment.
6. **Subsidiarity & Social Partnership.** In works-council, CBA, or mandatory-consultation jurisdictions, the GEB is introduced through prior local consultation. It operates as a **global floor**, never as a ceiling that undercuts locally bargained minima.
7. **Data Integrity & Auditability.** Market benchmarks are triangulated across multiple sources, documented, and externally audited annually.
8. **Currency Neutrality.** Compensation is contractually denominated in USD. Local-currency payouts use a published, semi-annual conversion protocol.
9. **Anti-Retaliation.** Employees who inquire about, discuss, disclose, or challenge compensation—internally or on public forums—are legally protected from adverse action.

## III. Job Architecture & Gender-Neutral Classification

### A. Global Level Definitions
All roles map to levels (IC3–IC7, M1–M5) based on four gender-neutral factors:
- **Scope of Accountability:** Autonomy and decision-making authority;
- **Technical/Functional Complexity:** Depth of specialized knowledge;
- **Organizational Impact:** Revenue, product, or strategic influence;
- **Leadership & Interaction:** Cross-functional coordination and mentorship obligations.

**Validation Protocol:**  
An external I-O psychology firm audits level definitions every **24 months** to certify gender neutrality. The audit report is available to works councils and regulators upon request. This satisfies **EU Pay Transparency Directive Article 4** and underpins defensible pay-equity globally.

### B. Canadian SEARC Mapping
For Canadian federal and provincial pay-equity regimes, each global level is cross-walked to Canadian job classes using the **SEARC** framework. If pay-equity adjustments are required by the *Pay Equity Act* or provincial analogues, they are administered as a **separate Canadian Pay Equity Adjustment** line item, distinct from the GEB formula.

## IV. The GEB Architecture

**Total Cash Compensation (TCC)** = Annual Base Cash + Target Variable Compensation (where applicable)  
**Annual Base Cash** = Placement within (GRP × Geographic Index)  
**Total Rewards Value** = TCC + Equity + Benefits + Statutory Employer Contributions

### A. Global Reference Point (GRP)
- Expressed as an annual USD range (minimum–maximum) per career level.
- Calibrated to the **65th percentile of the global remote software labor market** for that level.
- Identical for all employees at the same level worldwide.
- Updated semi-annually (June / December) based on the Triangulated Market Index (Section V).

### B. Geographic Index (GI) & Tier Bands
Markets are collapsed into **three published tiers**. The GI is a multiplier applied to the GRP *range*, producing a **Tier Band** (the actual hiring and pay range for that role in that tier).

| Tier | Definition | GI | Illustrative Markets |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **T1: Global Hubs** | Major high-cost tech labor markets with top-decile remote wages and elevated statutory costs. | **1.00x** | Canada, Germany, UK, USA, Switzerland, Netherlands, Nordics |
| **T2: Growth Markets** | Strong emerging tech ecosystems with moderate labor costs and rising remote-wage pressure. | **0.85x** | Poland, Portugal, Spain, Italy, Czech Republic, Estonia, Argentina (selected hubs), Mexico (selected hubs), India (selected hubs) |
| **T3: Strategic Frontiers** | Markets where we hire selectively for specific talent pools or time-zone coverage. | **0.75x** | Selected Southeast Asian, African, and Eastern European markets |

**Maximum Spread:** 25% (T3 vs. T1). Adjacent tier gap: 15%.

### C. Band Placement Factor (BPF)
Determines an individual’s position *within* the Tier Band.
- **Range:** –10% to +15% relative to the Tier Band midpoint.
- **Bases:** Specialized skill scarcity, performance trajectory, and depth of experience relative to level expectations.
- **Hard Ceiling:** No base salary may exceed the Tier Band maximum. Exceptional scarcity cases are addressed via **sign-on equity or signing bonus**, not base-pay exceptions, preserving transparency compliance.
- **Calibration:** Anonymous BPF distributions are visible to managers during annual compensation reviews to prevent clustering at the top of bands.

## V. Geographic Tiering & Market Data Protocol

### A. Tier Assignment Scorecard
Country assignment is governed by a quantitative scorecard, reviewed annually by the Compensation Committee.

| Criterion | Weight | Data Source |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Blended Labor Cost (remote software roles, P75) | 40% | Radford (Aon), Mercer, WTW, Option Impact, local survey providers |
| Talent Availability & Time-to-Fill | 25% | Internal ATS data; offer acceptance rates by region |
| Statutory Employer Burden & Tax Differential | 20% | EY/PwC global employment tax models |
| Macroeconomic Volatility (currency, inflation) | 15% | IMF / World Bank 12-month trailing volatility indices |

The methodology and annual scorecard results are published on the internal transparency portal.

### B. Market Deviation Monitor
To prevent arbitrary tier churn while preserving accuracy, a country is flagged for **tier reassessment** only if its blended TMI-derived index diverges from its assigned tier’s midpoint by **>0.10x (10 percentage points)** for **two consecutive semi-annual review cycles**. This stability mechanism prevents annual anxiety while ensuring bands do not detach from market reality.

### C. Triangulated Market Index (TMI)

| Source Type | Weight | Examples |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Primary: Global Salary Surveys** | 50% | Radford, Mercer, Willis Towers Watson, Option Impact |
| **Secondary: Internal Talent Signals** | 30% | Offer acceptance/rejection rates, time-to-fill, voluntary turnover correlation with pay position |
| **Tertiary: Public Compliance Data** | 20% | U.S. H-1B disclosures, EU national pay reports, Canadian pay-equity filings, U.K. gender pay gap data |

- **Update Cadence:** June and December.
- **Volatility Guardrail:** A tier’s GI changes only if the TMI moves the index by **>5%** from the current multiplier. Otherwise, the index is held constant to prevent wage whiplash.
- **Tier Reassignment:** Occurs only at the annual Q4 review; requires Compensation Committee approval and **90-day advance notice** to affected employees.

### D. Currency Conversion & Local Floor Protocol
- **USD Peg:** All bands are set in USD. Local-currency payroll conversions use the **prior 6-month average interbank rate**, updated semi-annually.
- **Hyperinflation Shield:** For jurisdictions with >20% annual CPI (e.g., Argentina, Turkey), a **temporary cost-of-living stipend** is reviewed quarterly. This is **not** embedded in base salary.
- **Absolute Floor:** No employee may be paid below the higher of:
  1. The statutory minimum wage or CBA rate in their jurisdiction; or
  2. The company’s global living-wage floor (**USD $45,000 equivalent TCC** for full-time software roles).

## VI. Legacy Workforce Transition & Red-Circle Protocol

### A. Green-Circle Employees (Below New Tier Band Minimum)
- **Action:** Immediate base-salary increase to the new Tier Band minimum upon Live Transition.
- **Effective Date:** First payroll cycle of Month 7.
- **Budget:** Modeled as a one-time compression cost in the FY26 transition reserve.

### B. Red-Circle Employees (Above New Tier Band Maximum)
- **Grandfathering:** Current base salary is protected; no reduction is permitted.
- **Base Freeze:** Merit increases to base salary are deferred until the Tier Band maximum catches up via semi-annual GRP updates or the employee is promoted to a new level.
- **Variable & Equity Continuity:** Red-circled employees remain eligible for performance-based variable compensation and equity refresh grants; these are not frozen.
- **Convergence Modeling:** Finance models a **36-month median catch-up horizon** based on projected GRP growth. If convergence is not achieved within 48 months, the Compensation Committee will review a special market-adjustment carve-out.
- **Communications:** Written notice 30 days before Live Transition, followed by a prescribed 1:1 manager conversation.

### C. Contractor Firewall (Reinforced)
No incumbent contractor may be converted to an employee using a grandfathered red-circle salary as a benchmark. New employee offers are derived strictly from the Tier Band and BPF.

## VII. Mobility & Relocation

| Direction | Base Salary Action | Future Trajectory |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Upward (e.g., T2 → T1)** | **Immediate re-alignment.** Base is increased to the *equivalent BPF percentile* within the new Tier Band (e.g., if at the 60th percentile of T2, moved to the 60th percentile of T1). If current pay is below the new band minimum, base is raised immediately to the minimum. | Follows new tier’s band progression. |
| **Lateral (e.g., T1 → T1)** | No change unless role level changes. | Follows current band. |
| **Downward (e.g., T1 → T2)** | **Protected.** No reduction to base salary. | Future merit increases follow the new tier’s band trajectory until pay converges within band. Promotions reset to the new tier’s equivalent BPF placement. |

## VIII. Pay Transparency & Compliance Matrix

We operate a **“Converged Methodology, Adaptive Format”** protocol. Underlying bands are consistent globally; disclosure format adapts to local law.

| Regime | Jurisdictional Trigger | Company Action |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Regime A: Pre-Interview / Job-Ad Ranges** | EU/EEA (Pay Transparency Directive), U.S. states with mandatory posting (CA, CO, WA, NY, IL, etc.), British Columbia, Ontario. | Post the **Role-Specific Posted Range** (min–max). This is a **good-faith subset** of the Tier Band, historically representing the 25th–75th percentile of actual placements for that role in that tier over the trailing 12 months (typically **≤1.6:1 width**). In jurisdictions explicitly requiring the *full* range, the full Tier Band is posted. **All postings state Annual Base Cash**; where jurisdiction mandates (e.g., Washington State, evolving California guidance), **Target Variable Compensation and estimated annualized equity value** are included. Disclaimer: *“Final compensation is based on role level, geographic tier, and individual qualifications. See [careers.page/compensation] for methodology.”* |
| **Regime B: On-Demand Disclosure** | Jurisdictions requiring range disclosure upon candidate/employee request. | Provide the requester’s specific Tier Band in writing within 48 hours. |
| **Regime C: Incumbent Right to Information** | EU Pay Transparency Directive Art. 7 (right to know pay range for own role before interview / during employment). | All employees receive their level’s GRP band, their tier assignment, the GI table, and the methodology document annually. |
| **Regime D: Pay Equity Reporting** | EU gender pay gap reporting (100+ employees), UK gender pay gap, Canadian *Pay Equity Act*, emerging ethnicity/identity reporting regimes. | **Annual** pay-equity analysis. **Canada:** SEARC-based job-class comparison administered as a separate adjustment. **EU/UK:** Statistical gender analysis. Summary published internally. |
| **Regime E: Internal Transparency Floor** | Global company standard (exceeds most local law). | All employees access the Geographic Index table, 3-layer formula, Tier Assignment Scorecard, and methodology via secure HR portal. |

### Works Council & Co-Determination
- **Consultation Timeline:** In Germany, Netherlands, Poland, Nordics, and other co-determination jurisdictions, GEB introduction follows a **mandatory consultation track** beginning in Month 2 and concluding before Live Transition (Month 8). Local works councils receive the full methodology, gender-neutrality audit, and budget impact analysis.
- **IT Access Logging:** The secure HR portal used for internal transparency is subject to local works-council approval where IT co-determination rules apply (e.g., Germany §87 BetrVG). No employee-level access logging is activated until documented approval is obtained.

### Global Salary-History Ban
Recruiters and hiring managers are prohibited from requesting prior compensation at any stage. Offers are derived exclusively from the Tier Band and BPF.

## IX. Internal Fairness, Perception Management & Anti-Retaliation

### A. The “Labor Market, Not Living Costs” Narrative
Managers are **explicitly prohibited** from explaining geographic differentials by referencing cost-of-living, housing, or personal expenses. The approved framing, included in the certified Manager Playbook:

> *“Your Tier Band is derived from the Global Reference Point, which is identical for every [Level X] engineer in the company. The Geographic Index reflects competitive labor-market conditions for remote software talent in your region, triangulated from multiple salary surveys and audited semi-annually. It is a market-parity adjustment, not a talent discount or a cost-of-living calculation.”*

### B. Specific Protocol: Warsaw / Lisbon ↔ Berlin / Toronto Comparisons
To preempt the specific conflicts identified in the business problem:

| Employee Question | Manager Response (Certified Playbook) | Escalation if Unresolved |
|:---|:---|:---|
| “Why does my peer in Berlin earn more for the same IC4 title?” | “You share the same IC4 Global Reference Point and career ceiling. The 15% difference is the published Geographic Index between T2 and T1, based on audited remote labor-cost data. Your compensation is not discounted for talent; it is adjusted for geographic market positioning.” | Fairness Challenge Protocol (below). |
| “If I move to Berlin, what happens?” | “Your base would be immediately aligned to the T1 band for your level at your equivalent placement. If you move from Berlin to Warsaw, your base is protected and would not decrease.” | Mobility team + HRBP. |

### C. Pay Band Communication Strategy
To ensure the GEB is understood as a fairness mechanism rather than a source of resentment:

1. **Pre-Launch Leadership Cascade:** C-suite and senior directors receive the policy 30 days before all-staff disclosure to ensure message consistency.
2. **Manager Certification:** All people managers complete a 90-minute certification on the GEB formula, approved narrative, and relocation rules before Month 6. Uncertified managers may not extend offers.
3. **Public Careers Page Explainer:** A **Global Compensation Philosophy** page is published externally, displaying the three tiers, the 25% maximum spread, the TMI sources, and a candidate FAQ. This preempts Reddit/Blind/Glassdoor speculation by making the formula the single source of truth.
4. **Regional AMAs:** In Month 6, regional HRBPs host “Ask Me Anything” sessions segmented by tier to address localized concerns without cross-tier confrontation.
5. **Quarterly Fairness Pulse:** A 3-question sentiment check (perception of fairness, understanding of tier rationale, trust in process) is fielded quarterly; results are reviewed by the Compensation Committee.

### D. Fairness Challenge Protocol
Any employee may file a **Compensation Review Request** to People Analytics.

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. Intake** | Employee submits written request via HR portal. | Day 0 |
| **2. Analysis** | People Analytics produces: (a) market data supporting tier assignment; (b) anonymized internal peer comparison (same role/tier); (c) BPF placement rationale. | 10 business days |
| **3. Determination** | Written response issued. If error is found, correction is made retroactively to the next payroll. | 15 business days |
| **4. Escalation** | If unresolved, employee may escalate to the **Chief People Officer** for administrative review. | Within 5 days of Step 3 |
| **5. Final Arbitration** | If still unresolved, the **Compensation Committee** issues binding determination at next quarterly meeting (or emergency session if >90 days). | Next CC meeting |

### E. Anti-Retaliation Covenant
No employee shall suffer adverse employment action—including termination, demotion, denial of promotion, reduction of hours, punitive relocation, or exclusion from equity grants—because they:
- Inquire about, discuss, or disclose their own compensation or that of others (to the extent permitted by law);
- File a Fairness Challenge;
- Post compensation information on public forums (e.g., Reddit, Blind, Glassdoor); or
- Assist another employee in exercising rights under this policy.

Violations are subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination of the responsible manager. This provision is incorporated into the Company Code of Conduct and Manager Accountability Standards.

## X. Governance & Implementation Roadmap

### A. Governance Bodies
1. **Compensation Committee (CC):** C-suite, VP People, external compensation advisor. Approves tier movements, GRP updates, and red-circle exceptions. Meets quarterly.
2. **Pay Equity Task Force (PETF):** Legal, People Analytics, Regional HRBPs. Manages compliance calendars, audit schedules, disclosure templates, gender-neutral classification audits, and works-council liaison.
3. **Global Mobility & Local HRBPs:** Enforce relocation rules, local statutory minima, and escalation of jurisdictional anomalies.

### B. Implementation Roadmap

| Phase | Timeline | Action |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. Audit & Architecture** | Months 1–2 | Map all employees to GRP levels and tiers. Model red/green-circle population and budget impact. Finalize external gender-neutral job evaluation audit. |
| **2. Co-Determination & Consultation** | Months 2–6 | **Parallel track:** Initiate works-council consultation in DE, NL, PL, Nordics. Secure IT portal approvals. Adjust timeline if local process extends beyond Month 6. |
| **3. Legal & Compliance Build** | Months 3–5 | Finalize Compliance Matrix for all 30 countries. Draft localized posting templates. Complete Canadian SEARC mapping. Build secure HR portal with access controls. |
| **4. Communications & Training** | Months 5–7 | Certify all managers on labor-market narrative and relocation rules. Publish public Global Compensation Philosophy. Launch internal transparency portal. Host regional AMAs. |
| **5. Pay Equity Repair** | Month 7 | Immediate green-circle raises to new minimums. Issue red-circle grandfathering notices. |
| **6. Live Transition** | Month 8 | All new offers use GEB. All open roles reposted with Regime A disclosures. |
| **7. Continuous Review** | Ongoing | Semi-annual TMI updates (June / December); annual external audit; quarterly fairness pulse; annual gender-neutral job architecture re-validation; bi-annual GRP calibration. |

## XI. Risk Register & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|:---|:---|
| **Contractor misclassification** | Explicit exclusion from GEB; fee schedules firewalled from employee bands. |
| **Budget shock from compression** | 36-month red-circle convergence model; one-time FY26 reserve for green-circle raises. |
| **Good-faith range litigation** | Role-Specific Posted Range (≤1.6:1) backed by 12-month hiring distribution data; full Tier Band used only where statutorily required. |
| **Tier instability / forum anxiety** | Market Deviation Monitor (10% threshold across 2 cycles) prevents annual churn. |
| **Works council delay or veto** | Parallel consultation track (Months 2–6) built into roadmap; local CBA minima respected as superseding floors. |
| **Canadian pay-equity tribunal exposure** | SEARC-based job-class mapping and adjustment line items administered separately from GEB formula. |
| **EU gender-neutrality challenge** | External I-O psychology audit of level definitions; report available to regulators/works councils. |
| **Anti-retaliation claims** | Explicit covenant in policy, Code of Conduct, and manager training; whistleblower channel for reporting violations. |
| **Intra-tier resentment (e.g., Berlin vs. Toronto)** | Acknowledged as inherent limitation of three-tier compression; mitigated by BPF flexibility and published methodology emphasizing labor-market blending, not individual purchasing-power parity. |
| **Upward-relocation inequity** | Immediate re-alignment rule eliminates lag penalty for T2→T1 moves. |
| **Transparency law fragmentation** | “Converged Methodology, Adaptive Format” ensures consistency of substance while respecting local form requirements. |

## Approval & Authority

This policy is effective as of **1 July 2026** and supersedes all prior global compensation guidelines. All functional leaders, people managers, and HR business partners are directed to implement the GEB Framework in accordance with the phases and governance structures outlined herein.

**Questions or escalations** should be routed through the Chief People Officer or the Pay Equity Task Force.

---

*This policy acknowledges that sustainable global compensation requires trading maximal labor-cost arbitrage for trust, compliance, and administrative coherence. We choose intentional, transparent compression: enough differentiation to respect local labor markets, and enough convergence to maintain a unified remote culture.*
