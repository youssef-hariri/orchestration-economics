## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

In 2026, we are executing a structural pivot from a centralized USD pricing architecture to an **Affordability-First, Glocal Revenue Model**. The objective is to maximize penetration in high-growth, price-elastic markets while maintaining consolidated USD unit economics that withstand public-market scrutiny.

This playbook codifies five strategic pillars:

1. **Discretionary-Income Pricing:** Prices are anchored to ≤1.5% of urban median household income for the Standard tier, inclusive of mandatory data bundling, eliminating the fiction of macro PPP algorithms.
2. **Payment-Maturity Segmentation:** We integrate “first-mile” local rails—M-Pesa push notifications, UPI deeplinks, Pix QR, and OXXO vouchers—while loading the true cost of collection into our unit economics rather than burying it in CAC.
3. **Culturally Native Household Monetization:** We replace Western nuclear-household definitions with region-specific mechanics—**Patron Packs** for African patronage networks, **Joint Family Plans** for South Asia, and **Plan Familiar** for Latin America—monetizing extended-family norms instead of policing them.
4. **Bifurcated Margin Transparency:** We report two distinct economic buckets—**Hard Currency Margin (HCM)** and **Soft Currency Margin (SCM)** markets—treating global content amortization as a corporate overhead allocation rather than a variable cost falsely loaded onto a $0.92 Pakistani ARPU.
5. **FX & Trapped-Cash Alchemy:** A rigorous GAAP/IFRS consolidation framework, bifurcated indexation, and the **Local Content Investment Reserve** turn non-repatriable cash from balance-sheet rot into a competitive localization moat.

**Financial Anchor:** The EM portfolio reaches global content-ROI break-even at **8.5 million blended EM subscribers**. At scale, the EM marginal contribution pool de-risks our fixed content base by expanding audience monetization without diluting Hard Currency economics.
