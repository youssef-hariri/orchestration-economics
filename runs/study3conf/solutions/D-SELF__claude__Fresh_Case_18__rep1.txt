## Wearable Sleep-Apnea Screening + Multi-Feature Portfolio: Global Regulatory Rollout (2026–2029)

# DELIVERABLE 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (ONE-PAGE)

## VISION & STRATEGIC IMPERATIVE

**Clinical Opportunity:** Wearable sleep-apnea screening addresses global diagnostic bottleneck (30–80% of ~1B sleep-apnea patients undiagnosed); time-to-diagnosis reduction (6 months → 2 weeks) translates to earlier CPAP initiation, cardiovascular risk reduction, and $5K–$20K per-patient lifetime savings.

**Regulatory Reality:** Divergent classifications across markets (EU/Japan: Class IIa/II medical devices; US: pending 510(k)/De Novo; Emerging: wellness-unregulated) require region-specific validation, physician-engagement, and reimbursement strategies.

**Strategic Response:** Phased 36-month rollout sequence prioritizes clinical credibility over speed; leverages EU/Japan approvals (Q2–Q4 2026) to de-risk US FDA pathway (Q1–Q3 2027); builds consumer trust through peer-review publications + professional-society endorsements before mass-market launch.

## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

1. **Regulatory Clarity:** FDA Type C pre-submission (Q3 2026) must confirm 510(k) pathway ≥70% confidence; if De Novo required, dual-track prep absorbs 6-month contingency.
2. **Clinical Credibility:** ≥2 peer-review publications in high-impact journals (JAMA, Circulation, Eur Respir J) before US market launch; establishes KOL confidence.
3. **Reimbursement Pathways:** EU HTA submissions (NICE, HAS, G-BA) initiated Q1 2027 target approval by Q4 2027; US Medicare HCPCS code application Q1 2027 target approval Q3–Q4 2027.
4. **Physician Adoption:** ≥8–10 thought-leader endorsements per region (EU, Japan, US) secured by Q2 2027; professional-society position statements by Q3 2027.
5. **Consumer Trust:** App rating ≥4.5 by Q2 2027 (100K+ reviews); real-world-evidence campaigns demonstrating health outcomes (time-to-CPAP initiation, adherence improvement) by Q2 2028.

| **Sample Size & Endpoint** | n=200–400; Sensitivity ≥85%, Specificity ≥80% (AHI ≥5 threshold) |
| **Timeline** | **12–18 months** (validation + regulatory review) |
| **Key Milestones** | ✓ Clinical trial completion (Q3 2026) → ✓ Technical file compilation (Q4 2026) → ✓ Notified Body submission (Q4 2026) → ✓ CE approval (Q1 2027) |

---

### **VALIDATION ROADMAP (DETAILED)**

**Phase 1: Pre-Clinical & Pilot (Q1–Q4 2026)**
- Retrospective algorithm validation on existing PSG database (n=100–150)
- Pilot wearable RCT (n=50–100 subjects) concurrent with PSG; identify technical failure modes
- Algorithm refinement based on pilot findings

**Phase 2: Confirmatory RCT (Q2–Q4 2026, concurrent Phase 1b)**
- Multi-center RCT: 4–6 EU sleep centers (Germany, France, UK, Spain, Italy, Nordic)
- Enrollment: n=250 subjects (50% high-risk OSA candidates, 50% general population)
- Concurrent wearable wear + PSG (1 night gold-standard)
- Primary endpoint: Non-inferiority to STOP-BANG + clinical judgment
- Secondary endpoints: Sensitivity/specificity vs. AHI ≥5, ≥15, ≥30 thresholds; cost-effectiveness; user satisfaction

**Phase 3: Regulatory Submission & Approval (Q4 2026–Q1 2
