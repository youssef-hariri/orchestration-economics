# PART 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & FRAMEWORK PHILOSOPHY

## 1.1 Framework Philosophy

**Core Principle:** *Local expertise drives strategy; global consistency sets guardrails.*

This framework operationalizes authentic cultural representation across three major faith-based/cultural holidays by embedding:
- **Early cultural vetting** (consultant-led, not consultant-optional)
- **Tiered approval gates** with quantified decision criteria
- **Real-time escalation protocols** with sub-2.5-hour response SLAs
- **Decentralized execution** (regional agencies lead) + **centralized risk oversight** (global compliance gates)
- **Transparent documentation** enabling institutional memory & cross-regional learning

**Expected Outcome:** Campaigns that are culturally authentic, commercially effective, and resilient to cross-border social-media scrutiny.

---

## 1.2 What Success Looks Like

| Metric | Target | Validation |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Cultural Authenticity** | >70% of audience endorses "authentic," "respectful," "well-researched" in post-campaign survey | External cultural consultant sign-off + community listening validation (Section 2.2c) |
| **Backlash Containment** | <2.5-hour response time; backlash resolved within 24–72 hours without brand credibility damage | Real-time monitoring dashboard; escalation log review |
| **Approval Compliance** | 100% of campaigns complete all 4 gates; zero shortcuts | Compliance audit (monthly) |
| **Business Performance** | Reach, engagement, conversion aligned with KPIs; brand favorability lift in target region (M-4w to M+4w) | Standard marketing analytics; survey-based brand lift measurement |
| **Institutional Learning** | Post-campaign debrief captured; insights fed forward to next campaign cycle | Debrief template completion rate; evidence of learnings applied in subsequent year |

---

## 1.3 Framework Assumptions & Constraints

**Assumptions:**
- Regional MDs have authority to approve/reject campaigns (not held hostage by agency preferences)
- Budget allocated for cultural consultant retainers (~$80–120K USD per hub annually) and monitoring infrastructure (~$120–180K per hub annually)
- Legal, Compliance, and Diversity Officer teams can respond within SLAs (not under-resourced)
- Agencies retain creative ownership but accept consultant-driven revisions as non-negotiable

**Constraints:**
- Timelines are compressed (Ramadan 22-week lead time; Lunar New Year 20 weeks; Diwali 22 weeks); no shortcuts to approval gates
- Cross-regional campaigns require separate regional consultants (no "one expert fits all markets")
- Social-media scrutiny operates at velocity faster than traditional media; response infrastructure must be 24/7

**Sign-Off Tiers (Ramadan-Specific):**

```
TIER 1: UNQUALIFIED APPROVAL ("Culturally Authentic, Ready to Launch")
├─ All 4 consultants rate concept ≥4/5 on 5-point authenticity scale
├─ Zero "High Risk" concerns raised
├─ Agency addressed all "Medium Risk" concerns
├─ No dissenting notes
└─ Timeline: Proceeds directly to Gate 2 (Compliance), M-16w

TIER 2: CONDITIONAL APPROVAL ("Culturally Sound with Minor Revisions")
├─ All 4 consultants rate ≥3/5 authenticity
├─ Up to 2 unresolved "Medium Risk" concerns (documented; consultant consent obtained)
├─ No "High Risk" concerns, OR High Risk concerns fully mitigated
├─ Majority agreement (3 of 4 consultants approve conditions)
└─ Timeline: Proceeds to Gate 2 with documented conditions; flag in approval memo

TIER 3: SIGNIFICANT REVISIONS REQUIRED ("Culturally Misaligned")
├─ Any consultant rates <3/5 authenticity
├─ Multiple unresolved High Risk concerns
├─ Lack of consensus (2 or fewer approve)
├─ Core narrative misalignment with Ramadan spiritual intent
└─ Action: Agency must re-concept and resubmit for consultation; restart M-18w cycle (campaign delays 2–4 weeks)

TIER 4: DO NOT APPROVE ("Fundamental Misrepresentation or Harm Risk")
├─ Consultants identify core cultural/religious violation (e.g., sacred text inappropriate invocation)
├─ Consensus rejection (3+ of 4 consultants)
├─ Risk of significant, preventable backlash flagged
└─ Action: Campaign cannot proceed; Regional MD + Global CMO decide re-concepting or cancellation
```
