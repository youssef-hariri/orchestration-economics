## Product Roadmap: Ambient Domestic Steward (2026 International Expansion)

This roadmap outlines the strategic transition of our smart-home voice assistant from a Western "Command-and-Control" model to an **"Ambient Domestic Steward"** model, specifically tailored for the high-context, multigenerational, and privacy-sensitive markets of Japan, Korea, Thailand, and the Gulf.

---

### **Strategic Vision: "The Invisible Guardian"**
Our core value proposition is to provide intelligent home management that respects domestic sanctity, social hierarchy, and the desire for unobtrusive, anticipatory service.

---

### **Key Product Pillars**

#### **1. Interaction Calibration (Linguistic Flexibility)**
*   **User-Defined Rapport:** Users select their preferred "Social Distance" (Formal, Balanced, or Casual) during onboarding. The VA dynamically adjusts *Keigo*/*Jondaetmal* usage based on this selection.
*   **Modernizing Particles:** Defaulting to gender-neutral linguistic patterns in Thailand, with manual overrides available for specific user-persona needs.

#### **2. Adaptive Household Stewardship**
*   **Decoupled Admin Profiles:** We are separating **Technical Stewardship** (system updates, network settings) from **Social Priority** (personalized responses, hearing-aid optimized audio). 
*   **Contextual Clarity:** Audio fidelity is governed by AI-driven acoustic adaptation—ensuring clear communication for all users without resorting to ageist or patronizing "elderly" voice personas.

#### **3. Privacy as "Domestic Sanctity"**
*   **Hardware "Deep Sleep":** The device physical exterior will be non-emissive and matte when inactive, eliminating the Western "always-listening" LED ring anxiety.
*   **Presence Awareness:** Visual and haptic feedback will only occur upon active engagement. Marketing will emphasize the device as a "Protector of Home Life," prioritizing the privacy of the family sphere over "always-on" functionality.

#### **4. Anticipatory Intelligence (Feature Prioritization)**
*   **The "Low-Voice" Paradigm:** We are moving away from vocal commands. Sensors will detect time-of-day, occupancy, and environmental changes to offer subtle, non-intrusive suggestions (e.g., "The living room is becoming quite warm, would you like me to adjust the cooling?") rather than waiting for commands.
*   **Market-Specific Utilities:**
    *   **Japan/Korea:** Hyper-local integration with domestic routines (commute tracking, neighborhood apartment systems).
    *   **Gulf:** Integrated "Guest Mode" for privacy-compliant hosting; automated scheduling for prayer/Adhan.
    *   **Thailand:** Seamless LINE messaging bridge to unify multi-generational family communication.

---

### **Success Metrics & KPIs**

1.  **Command-to-Suggestion Ratio:** Target a 40% reduction in spoken commands within 6 months, replaced by sensor-based anticipatory interactions.
2.  **User Sentiment (Privacy):** Achieve a >85% satisfaction rating on "Domestic Privacy/Trust" metrics in local user surveys.
3.  **Onboarding Retention:** Monitor the selection rate of "Social Distance" settings to ensure the "Interaction Calibration" feature is actively used to define the AI's persona.
4.  **Hardware Aesthetic Reception:** Measure brand perception scores regarding the "Deep Sleep" design, ensuring it is perceived as an interior-design object rather than an invasive surveillance tool.

**Stakeholder Note:** This roadmap represents a fundamental pivot in our market approach. Success relies on resisting the urge to force "Western-standard" feature parity and instead leaning into the cultural nuances of our target markets. We are building not just a device, but a trusted member of the household.
