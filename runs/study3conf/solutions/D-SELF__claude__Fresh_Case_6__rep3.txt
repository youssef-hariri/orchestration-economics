## DELIVERABLE 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1-PAGE)

### SOVEREIGN CLOUD TRANSITION 2024–2026
#### Strategic Overview & Execution Timeline

**WHAT WE'RE DOING**
By 2026, we're transitioning from single-region global cloud to **localized sovereign-cloud deployment** across EMEA, APAC, LATAM, and North America. This is required by evolving data-sovereignty regulations (GDPR, PIPEDA, LGPD, PDPA) and demanded by enterprise customers.

**WHY NOW**
- **Regulatory mandate**: EMEA, Canada, Brazil require (or strongly prefer) local data residency
- **Customer demand**: Enterprise buyers (especially in regulated sectors) won't close deals without local residency proof
- **Competitive necessity**: Datadog, MongoDB, Figma already offer sovereign clouds; we risk losing market share

**EXECUTION TIMELINE**

```
PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Q1–Q4 2024)
├─ EU-Central Frankfurt deployment → GO-LIVE Q4 2024
├─ Infrastructure build, security hardening, pilot migrations
├─ Tier 0 (top 20 customers) migration planning & readiness

PHASE 2: EXPANSION (Q1–Q4 2025)
├─ UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore, Japan deployments
├─ Tier 1 (100–200 customers) staged migrations
├─ Feature parity acceleration for existing regions

PHASE 3: SCALE (Q1–Q2 2026)
├─ Brazil, Mexico, South Korea, MENA deployments
├─ Tier 2–3 (500–10,000 customers) self-service migrations
├─ Global cloud sunset planning (continues until 2028)
```

**CHURN PREVENTION COMMITMENTS**

| Cohort | Baseline Risk | Risk Mitigation | Target |
|--------|---|---|---|
| **Sovereignty Champions** (15%, $2–50M ARR) | 2–3% | Performance holdback (15% locked 12 months), exec sponsorship, 3-year pricing lock | <1.5% churn |
| **Pragmatic Adopters** (30%, $500K–10M ARR) | 6–10% | Transparent cost model, quarterly value QBRs, feature parity SLA + workaround API | <4% churn |
| **Price-Sensitive Skeptics** (35%, $50K–2M ARR) | 12–18% | Optional migration (no mandate), 24-month risk-free trial at 25% discount, migration optionality | <6% churn |
| **Mandatory Migrators** (20%, $100K–5M ARR) | 5–12% | Managed migration service (included), dedicated CPO escalation, Big4 audit program | <3% churn |

**FINANCIAL IMPACT**
- **Capex**: $65–90M (24-month build)
- **Annual opex increase**: +$22–28M (+35–40% engineering base)
- **Breakeven**: 6–7 years (vs. 3-year assumption; requires >50% adoption)
- **Year 3 projected sovereign ARR**: $95–100M (conservative; vs. $140M optimistic forecast)
- **Margin**: 15–20% (vs. 35% global cloud; intentional trade-down for adoption)

**SUCCESS METRICS**

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----|--------|---|
| Enterprise retention (>$1M ARR) | >92% | Month-12 renewal rate post-migration |
| Mid-market attrition | <8% | Month-6 early churn warning; <8% annual |

| Support satisfaction (regional teams) | 4.5/5 | Quarterly survey; escalation if <4.0 |
| Uptime (regional tiers) | 99.9% | CloudWatch/Azure Monitor; credit if <99.7% |

**CRITICAL DECISIONS REQUIRED (BY DATE)**

| Decision | Date | Owner | Impact |
|----------|------|-------|--------|

| **Lock EU-Central infrastructure readiness** (staffing, security certs, data validation tooling) | Oct 2024 | VP Engineering | Q4 2024 launch feasibility |
| **Finalize Tier 0 customer list & contractual terms** (20 champions) | Oct 2024 | VP Sales, Legal | Go/no-go for Q4 2024 cutover |
| **Establish adoption KPI thresholds & contingency triggers** (Q2 2025 go/no-go gate) | Dec 2024 | CFO, CRO | If adoption <40%, activate contingency (consolidate regions) |

**COMMUNICATION CADENCE (STARTING IMMEDIATELY)**

- **Now–Sep 2024**: Stakeholder education (webinars, white papers, cost calculators, transparent messaging on regulatory reality)

- **Apr–Jun 2025**: Tier 2 customer launch (self-service migration portal, community support, expansion incentives)
- **Jul–Dec 2025**: Tier 3 customer migration (automated migration workflows, email/in-app notifications, community escalation)

**RISK MITIGATION (Top 5)**

1. **Feature parity slips >6 months** → Activate "global API workaround" (customers use global region API as temporary bridge); 15% service credit if slip >4 months
2. **Adoption falls <40%** → Q2 2025 go/no-go gate; consolidate to EU + Singapore only (shut down low-adoption regions)
3. **Regulatory landscape shifts** → Quarterly legal monitoring; contractual "regulatory change clause" allows customer exit if cost increase >20%
4. **Competitor launches better offer** → Speed feature parity (reallocation of engineering); pre-negotiate customer discounts to hold incumbents
5. **Major customer migration fails** → Rollback insurance (7-day post-cutover rollback window); incident response SLA (24-hour remediation or 10% credit)
