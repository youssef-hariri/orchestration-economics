## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Uniform USD pricing has become a structural impediment to growth in Africa, South Asia, and Latin America (ASA/LatAm), artificially inflating churn and ceding share to locally priced competitors. Indiscriminate discounting, however, would collapse consolidated margin credibility.

This document approves a **“Tax-Adjusted Net ARPU-First”** architecture. Local tiers are priced backward from what actually hits the P&L—net of payment processing, sender fees, and all indirect taxes—rather than from aspirational sticker prices. The model replaces Western billing conventions with cash-flow-native instruments: a non-expiring **Hustle Wallet** in Sub-Saharan Africa; **28-day “4-week packs”** in South Asia aligned to telecom recharge rhythms; and **Pix Cobrança / OXXO voucher references** in Latin America that respect push-payment behavior. FX exposure is managed through a mechanical monthly pass-through, not through fictional forward markets or contradictory “USD floor” promises.

**Investor Bridge:** We acknowledge that blended emerging-market Net Revenue will be flat-to-down in Q1–Q2 of Year 1 as high-sticker, high-churn legacy subscribers convert to prepaid, low-friction local tiers. We project explicit **crossover to net-revenue growth in Q3 of Year 1** and exit the year with accelerating contribution margins.

**Bottom Line (Tax-Adjusted, Post-Payment):** By Year 3, we project **approximately 55 million emerging-market subscribers** at a blended **Net ARPU of $3.10–$3.35**, generating **$1.9 billion+ in tax-adjusted Net Revenue** at a **50–54% Direct Contribution Margin** and a **positive Fully-Loaded Margin by Year 3**. Emerging markets shift from an ARPU dilutent to a self-funding, margin-accretive growth vector.

## 2. MARKET-BY-MARKET PLAYBOOK

### 2.1 SUB-SAHARAN AFRICA (SSA)
*Profile: Lumpy, event-driven informal incomes; mobile-first; mobile-money economies; high carrier-billing costs; communal viewing norms.*

#### Pricing Architecture: Sticker → Tax-Adjusted Net ARPU
All consumer prices are tax-inclusive. VAT is remitted locally.

| Tier | Consumer Sticker (USD Eq.) | Tax-Adj. Net ARPU | Target Segment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily Sachet** | $0.35 | **~$0.22–$0.24** | Ultra-price-sensitive youth (paid acquisition) |
| **Hustle Wallet** | Prorated (~$0.07/day) | **~$0.04–$0.05/day** | Informal workers with irregular cash flows |
| **Mobile Lite** | $2.00/mo | **~$1.64–$1.86** | Urban youth / students |
| **Family Compound** | $4.50/mo | **~$3.71–$4.21** | Extended household |
| **Community Screen** | $9.00/mo | **~$7.44–$8.42** | Bars, video halls, barbershops (SMEs) |
| **Premium** | $6.50/mo | **~$5.37–$6.08** | Affluent / expatriate |

**Strategic Rationale:** The **Hustle Wallet** replaces rigid weekly passes. Users deposit any amount ($0.20, $0.50, $1.00+) via mobile money when liquidity permits; days unlock automatically at ~$0.07/day. This respects event-driven, lumpy incomes and eliminates forced-expiry churn. The **Community Screen** tier legitimizes and monetizes commercial communal viewing that enforcement cannot stop.

#### Payment Rails & Cost Stack
- **Primary (50% target volume):** Direct merchant API integrations with **M-Pesa (Kenya), MTN MoMo (Ghana/Nigeria),** and **Wave (Senegal / Côte d’Ivoire)**. Negotiate premium merchant rates capped at **1.5%** via quarterly volume commitments and co-marketing.
- **Secondary (30% target volume):** **B2B2C data-bundle inclusion** with Safaricom, MTN, and Airtel. Streaming is embedded in data packs (e.g., “2GB weekly + Mobile Lite included”). Revenue share is 20–25% wholesale—cheaper than carrier billing and invisible to the user, reducing churn.
- **Tertiary (15% target volume):** Carrier / airtime billing **only** for the Daily Sachet tier where mobile-money friction would cause drop-off. The 25% revenue share is treated as customer acquisition cost.
- **Fallback (5% target volume):** Agent cash-in / USSD for the unbanked corridor.
- **Sender Fees:** Absorbed by the platform through merchant-paid pricing. UX deep-links to the native app’s merchant flow (never P2P).
- **KYC Limits:** For the Family Compound tier, allow split-wallet payments (two sequential transactions) only as a fallback, with in-app guidance to fund the Hustle Wallet instead.

#### Billing Cadence & Dunning
- **Default:** Hustle Wallet (non-expiring prepaid balance) or 30-day prepaid.
- **Auto-renew:** Activated only if wallet balance exceeds 1.2x the subscription price. If insufficient, service **pauses immediately**; reactivation is instant upon top-up. No receivables risk.
- **No annual plans:** Income seasonality makes 12-month commitments unrealistic for the mass market.
- **Dunning:** SMS top-up nudge only.

### 2.2 SOUTH ASIA (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka)
*Profile: Extreme price sensitivity at scale; 28-day recharge mental model; joint-family structures; cricket/IPL as anchor.*

#### Pricing Architecture: Sticker → Tax-Adjusted Net ARPU

| Tier | Consumer Sticker (USD Eq.) | Tax-Adj. Net ARPU | Target Segment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mini Mobile AVOD** | Free / $0.40/mo | **~$0.23–$0.34** | Bottom 40%; rural |
| **Mini Mobile** | $1.10/mo | **~$0.87–$0.90** | Individual youth |
| **Family** | $2.75/28-days | **~$2.21–$2.29** | Joint-family household |
| **Premium** | $4.99/mo | **~$3.99–$4.13** | Affluent urban |
| **Annual Saver** | $2.34/mo eq. | **~$1.88–$1.94/mo eq.** | Commitment-oriented |
| **Cricket Event Pass** | $0.15/day | **~$0.12–$0.13** | Non-subscriber acquisition only |

**Strategic Rationale:** The **Mini Mobile AVOD** tier is essential to compete with JioCinema and YouTube. SSAI ad inventory (FMCG, handsets) monetizes users below the $1.10 threshold. The Family tier runs on a **28-day cycle** (“4-week pack”), aligning with telecom recharge rhythms and eliminating calendar-drift churn. The 15% Annual Saver discount is the maximum accretive threshold given 2.5–3.5% monthly Family-tier churn assumptions. Cricket Event Passes are **restricted to non-subscribers** via account-status gating to prevent Family-tier cannibalization.

#### Payment Rails & Cost Stack
- **India Default (60% target volume):** **UPI Collect Request via WhatsApp Business API**. Two days before expiry, the user receives a dynamic QR code and UPI deep link; they authorize the pull in their app. This achieves >90% success because the user is present. **True UPI Autopay is reserved for Premium opt-in only**, given documented 30–40% mandate-failure rates at mass-market price points.
- **Pakistan / Bangladesh (25% target volume):** Easypaisa, JazzCash, bKash, Nagad. dLocal is used only if direct wallet APIs are unavailable; FX spreads are negotiated down via quarterly minimum commitments.
- **Physical Retail (10% target volume):** Scratch-card and QR voucher top-ups via **Paytm / JioPOS (India)** and **Easypaisa agents (Pakistan)**. Critical for rural trust and prepaid culture.
- **Cards / EMI (5% target volume):** Debit/credit for Premium.
- **Dunning:** WhatsApp (India); SMS (Pakistan / Bangladesh). Email is deprecated.

#### Billing Cadence
- **28-day** default cycle for all tiers.
- **Annual:** Prepaid 12-month pack, 15% discount.
- **Event Passes:** Sold as match-day bundles with telecom data partners (e.g., “₹20 for 3 hours + match access”).

### 2.3 LATIN AMERICA (Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Andean, Southern Cone)
*Profile: Higher card penetration but FX volatility; installment culture; Pix push-payment dominance; nuclear-family sharing with young-adult leakage.*

#### Pricing Architecture: Sticker → Tax-Adjusted Net ARPU

| Tier | Consumer Sticker (USD Eq.) | Tax-Adj. Net ARPU | Target Segment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | $3.99/mo | **~$3.18–$3.53** | Young professionals |
| **Standard** | $6.49/mo | **~$5.17–$5.74** | Nuclear family (1 stream) |
| **Platinum** | $9.99/mo | **~$7.97–$8.84** | High-income / dual-income |
| **Amigos Add-On** | +$1.99/mo | **~$1.59–$1.76** | Adult children / friends |

**Strategic Rationale:** The **Amigos Add-On** is priced at **30% of Standard** to make credential separation economically rational versus informal password sharing. The Standard plan is reduced to **1 concurrent stream** (device-bound to the home TV plus one mobile device), making the add-on the viable path for multi-location sharing. Brazil soccer seasonality is built into the model: Platinum includes Brasileirão; Standard users can upgrade via a one-tap “Season Pass.”

#### Payment Rails & Cost Stack
- **Brazil — Pix Cobrança (50% target volume):** Dynamic QR codes and copy-paste keys sent via WhatsApp 48 hours and 24 hours before expiry. Webhook confirmation is instant. Assume manual payment behavior for the majority.
- **Brazil — Pix Automático (10% target volume):** Offered as an opt-in with a 5% discount. Assumes automated recurring push payment.
- **Cards (20% target volume):** Debit/credit for Platinum and Standard.
- **Cuotas / Installments (10% target volume):** Restricted to Annual plans. The 13–18% merchant discount fee is treated as a retention cost, not payment COGS.
- **Mexico — OXXO Pay (10% target volume):** **Recurring voucher reference**—one fixed CLABE/account for 12 months so users pay without generating a new barcode. The Essential tier is priced at **$4.49 via OXXO** (versus $3.99 via digital) to absorb the fixed merchant convenience fee (~Mex$ 12–15 per transaction).
- **Colombia / Peru / Chile:** PSE, Redcompra, and dLocal for local card acquiring.

#### Billing Cadence
- **Monthly** standard.
- **Bi-weekly (quincenal)** offered in Mexico for the Essential tier to match salary cycles.
- **Quarterly** in high-inflation markets (Argentina) to balance purchasing-power erosion against billing fatigue.
- **Annual:** Offered only via 3–6 cuotas (installments), treated as a retention tool.

## 3. PAYMENT & BILLING ARCHITECTURE

To execute without engineering fragmentation, we will deploy a unified **Payment & Billing Orchestration Layer** with regional rule sets.

| Capability | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Dynamic Pricing Engine** | Mechanical monthly FX pass-through: local-currency prices adjust within a ±5% band based on a trailing 30-day FX average. A ±12% cumulative move triggers a structural repricing review with 15-day consumer notice. |
| **Smart Payment Routing** | Direct API priority per market: M-Pesa / MoMo / Wave (SSA) → UPI Collect Request (India) → Pix (Brazil) → OXXO voucher (Mexico). Failover to aggregators (MFS Africa, Cellulant, dLocal) only when direct rails are unavailable. |
| **Hustle Wallet Engine** | Non-expiring prepaid balance; prorated daily entitlement unlock; low-balance SMS nudges; immediate pause on zero balance. |
| **Recurring Logic** | “Soft pause” architecture: if wallet or account is empty, entitlement suspends instantly; reactivation is instant upon top-up or payment confirmation. No receivables. |
| **Entitlement Management** | Time-based entitlements (daily, 28-day, event-based) decoupled from rigid “subscription” objects to support sachet, wallet, and pass models. |
| **Revenue Assurance** | Device fingerprinting for account-sharing analytics; Pix / OXXO webhook confirmation; carrier-billing fraud thresholds; forensic watermarking on offline downloads. |

## 4. HOUSEHOLD & FAMILY MONETIZATION FRAMEWORK

The strategy explicitly productizes cultural sharing norms while capping revenue leakage.

| Layer | Mechanism | Economic Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Household Fingerprint 2.0** | **Primary Payment Anchor:** Account bound to one national-ID-linked payment instrument. **Statistical Device Graph:** Up to 5 devices allowed; anomalies (3+ persistent locations >50km apart over 14 days) are flagged. **Soft Cap:** If extra-household usage exceeds 15% of streams, the account receives an in-app nudge to upgrade or add a sub-account. No hard blocks that spike churn. | Caps extra-household leakage at <15% without brittle enforcement |
| **Forensic Watermarking** | Invisible account ID burned into offline downloads; traceable without user-facing DRM friction | Deters neighborhood redistribution in SSA |
| **Community Screen Tier** | $9.00/month commercial license for bars, video halls, and barbershops | Monetizes leakage that enforcement cannot stop |
| **Amigos Add-On** | LatAm only: Clean, credential-separated sub-account at $1.99 (30% of Standard) | Captures value from young adults leaving the nuclear household |
| **Soft Delegation** | South Asia only: Primary account holder may designate a “Co-Manager” via in-app settings without mandatory OTP friction for every profile change | Reduces patriarchal governance friction in joint-family structures |

## 5. USD CONSOLIDATION & UNIT ECONOMICS DASHBOARD

### 5.1 Segment Reporting & Investor Narrative
- **Discontinue defense of global blended ARPU.** Instead, report four transparent metrics:
  - **Developed Markets ARPU** (North America, Western Europe, Oceania)
  - **Emerging Markets Net ARPU** (after payment costs, FX slippage, and indirect taxes)
  - **Direct Contribution Margin** (local revenue less delivery, payment processing, tax, local marketing, and local content)
  - **Fully-Loaded Margin** (Direct Contribution less global content amortization allocated by viewership hours)
- **Key Investor Message:** Emerging markets carry near-zero incremental content cost for the existing global library. At a $3.20 Tax-Adjusted Net ARPU with approximately $0.80 in delivery, payment, and local costs, the Direct Contribution is roughly **75%**, which is accretive to enterprise free cash flow per subscriber.

### 5.2 Local Operating Entity (LOE) & Transfer Pricing
- Establish local subsidiaries in the top 10 markets.
- **Revenue Recognition:** Booked in local functional currency; governed by IFRS 15.
- **Repatriation:** Local entities pay HQ IP Holding Co. a **royalty (12–15% of local revenue)** and a **tech platform fee (cost-plus 5–7%)**. Combined intercompany strip: **17–22%**, defensible under OECD transfer-pricing guidelines for thin-margin distribution markets.
- **Tax Efficiency:** Local content production (India originals, Nollywood co-productions, Brazilian formats) creates natural tax offsets and justifies profit retention for reinvestment.

### 5.3 FX Risk Management
| Market Volatility | Strategy |
|---|---|
| **High (Nigeria, Argentina, Pakistan, Egypt)** | Mechanical monthly pass-through (±5%) is the primary hedge. No reliance on illiquid forward markets. Natural hedge via local content and marketing spend where possible. |
| **Moderate (India, Mexico, Colombia)** | Natural hedge: match local revenue against local content, marketing, and payroll. |
| **Low (Kenya, Chile)** | Spot-market quarterly repatriation. |

**Argentina Exception:** Price in ARS but index monthly to an official blended rate. Offer a **quasi-durable “Credit Wallet”** denominated in viewing hours, purchased in local currency but consumed against a fixed USD content value. This isolates the user from daily FX trauma while keeping revenue recognizable. No USDC-stablecoin exposure.

### 5.4 Sports & Premium Content Economics
We do not assume billion-dollar exclusive sports rights are fundable by mass-market ARPU. Sports are deployed as a dedicated cost center.

| Market | Anchor | Strategy | Cost Model |
|---|---|---|---|
| **India** | Cricket (IPL, International) | Non-exclusive digital windows or revenue-share with broadcast partner | 8–12% of India revenue |
| **Brazil** | Brasileirão / Libertadores | Seasonal add-on; Platinum includes, Standard pays uplift during season | 6–10% of Brazil revenue |
| **SSA** | Local football + EPL highlights | Low-cost highlights and studio programming, not live full-match exclusives | 2–4% of SSA revenue |

**Revised Local Content Spend:** India ~24% of revenue; Brazil ~22%; SSA ~15%; Pakistan/Bangladesh ~12%.

## 6. IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

### Phase 1: Board Sanction & Pre-Launch (Q1 2026)
- **Board Approval:** Sanction the “Tax-Adjusted Net ARPU-First” framework, dual-layer reporting, and mechanical FX pass-through.
- **Accounting Memo:** CFO to publish IFRS 15 revenue recognition policy for Hustle Wallet breakage (20% cap), content credits, and event-pass deferral.
- **Sports LOIs:** Secure signed letters of intent for non-exclusive cricket and Brasileirão digital windows prior to mass-market launch.
- **Working Capital Annex:** CFO to model trapped-cash dynamics, OXXO reconciliation lags, and mobile-money float requirements.

### Phase 2: Pilot Validation (Q2 2026)
Launch in three testbed markets to validate CAC, payment-rail mix, and retention KPIs:
- **Nigeria:** Hustle Wallet + MTN MoMo direct API + B2B2C data-bundle partnerships.
- **India:** Mini Mobile AVOD + UPI Collect Request via WhatsApp + 28-day Family tier.
- **Brazil:** Pix Cobrança + Amigos Add-On + seasonal soccer upsell.

**Board Contingency:** If pilot Net ARPU or 90-day retention misses plan by >10%, pause full regional rollout and reprice sachet tiers.

### Phase 3: Payment Partnership Execution (Q1–Q2 2026)
- Execute direct APIs with M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Wave, NPCI UPI aggregators, and Pix processors.
- Negotiate B2B2C data-bundle terms with Safaricom, MTN, and Airtel.
- Distribute physical retail vouchers via Paytm, JioPOS, and Easypaisa agents.

### Phase 4: Pricing Simulation & Investor Pre-Positioning (March–April 2026)
- Run 10,000-scenario Monte Carlo on FX sensitivity, demand elasticity, and rail-mix Net ARPU.
- Conduct investor roadshow pre-positioning the Direct Contribution Margin story and Year 1 crossover bridge ahead of Q2 earnings.

### Phase 5: Scale & Optimize (Year 2–3)
- Roll out to secondary markets (Kenya, Ghana, Bangladesh, Mexico, Colombia).
- Launch Community Screen field-sales pilot in Nigeria and Kenya.
- Optimize smart-routing algorithms based on pilot cost data.

## 7. RISK MITIGATION

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Currency Devaluation Spiral** | Mechanical monthly FX pass-through (±5% band); no “USD floor” fiction. Quarterly structural reviews at ±12% cumulative movement. |
| **Indirect Tax Erosion** | All unit economics rendered net of VAT/GST/ISS/PIS/COFINS. Local entities responsible for remittance; prices are tax-inclusive to consumers. |
| **Payment Cost Leakage** | Net ARPU disclosed by rail. Direct API priority minimizes aggregator spreads. B2B2C bundling lowers effective CAC. |
| **UPI Autopay Failure (India)** | Default to UPI Collect Request (user-present authorization). Reserve true autopay for Premium opt-in only. |
| **Pix “Silent Churn” (Brazil)** | WhatsApp-led Cobrança with dynamic QRs at T-48 and T-24 hours. Assume manual payment behavior; offer Pix Automático only with a 5% opt-in discount. |
| **Account-Sharing Backlash** | Productize via Compound / Joint Family / Amigos tiers. Enforce via Household Fingerprint 2.0 (soft nudge, not hard block). |
| **Cricket Pass Cannibalization** | Event passes restricted to non-subscribers via account-status gating. |
| **Regulatory Price Caps / Content Quotas** | Pre-empt with 2–3 regional originals per major market and local production spend to demonstrate economic contribution. |
| **Investor ARPU Anxiety** | Dual-layer reporting; investor materials highlight Tax-Adjusted Net ARPU, Direct Contribution, and LTV/CAC by pilot phase. |
| **Competitive Response** | AVOD/FAST tier defends the bottom 40% against free competitors. Hustle Wallet and Pix Cobrança create payment-native switching costs. |
