# DELIVERABLE 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
## Strategic Recommendation & Go/No-Go Decision

### PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION: Phased "Hub-and-Spoke Plus" Model

**Strategic Positioning:**
Establish a three-tier ASEAN manufacturing footprint with **Thailand as primary hub (65% capacity), Vietnam as secondary cost node (25% capacity), and Malaysia as premium audit-risk hedge (optional 5–10%)**. This architecture delivers 11–13% tariff cost recovery while distributing geopolitical risk across three countries, achieving supply-chain resilience targets, and managing organizational change through structured phased implementation with explicit decision gates.

### GO/NO-GO DECISION FRAMEWORK

**PROCEED WITH PHASE 1 (Thailand Hub) IF:**
- ✅ Board approves USD 15M capex allocation (Nov 2025)
- ✅ BOI provides binding written commitment on 8-year tax holiday (Dec 2025)
- ✅ GM hire confirmed + 5+ expatriate technical recruits secured (Jan 2026)
- ✅ 8–10 lead suppliers qualified with audits + Cpk ≥1.1 confirmed (Feb 2026)

**CONDITIONAL PROCEED WITH PHASE 2 (Vietnam Node) IF:**
- ✅ Thailand Phase 1 achieves <1.0% defect rate by Q2 2027
- ✅ RoO audit failure rate <10% (measured across 20+ audited shipments) by Q3 2027
- ✅ Cost reduction ≥8% vs. East Asia hub baseline (confirmed, 500+ units) by Q3 2027
- ✅ HQ engineering team confidence ≥70% (survey) by Q3 2027
- ✅ Customers accept Thailand origin without repricing (≥85% contract review) by Q3 2027

**If ANY Phase 2 gate fails:** Cancel Vietnam Phase 2; consolidate 100% in Thailand; re-evaluate Malaysia option.

**OPTIONAL: PROCEED WITH MALAYSIA (5–10% Niche) IF:**
- ✅ Thailand/Vietnam audit failure rate exceeds 20% by Q2 2028, OR
- ✅ Customer demand for "zero-audit-risk" products exceeds 5% of portfolio by Q2 2028

**Otherwise:** Maintain Malaysia as strategic reserve; do not implement.
