**PRODUCT STRATEGY: GLOBAL ADAPTIVE LEARNING LOCALIZATION (2024–2026)**
*From US Base to Vietnam, Kenya, Saudi Arabia, and France*

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

By 2026, the platform will operate as **four pedagogically sovereign learning systems**—each indistinguishable from local best practice—powered by a single adaptive intelligence engine. The competitive moat is the speed at which the **Shared Core** ingests a national ministry scope-and-sequence and outputs a curriculum-compliant, culturally native experience without forking the codebase.

To avoid lethal overextension, expansion is sequenced in two waves:

| Wave | Markets | Thesis | Revenue Horizon |
|------|---------|--------|-----------------|
| **Wave 1** | **Vietnam, Kenya** | Content-first, approval-second. B2C/B2B revenue launched immediately while regulatory runway runs in parallel. | H1 2024 |
| **Wave 2** | **Saudi Arabia, France** | Approval-first, revenue-second. Heavy investment in certification, inspectorate credibility, and conformity before any material revenue. | H2 2025–2026 |

**Capital Allocation:** 2024 burn is split **70% Wave 1 / 30% Wave 2**. Wave 2 markets are treated as 18-month runway-burn investments where the payoff is contract size, not speed.

**Hard Strategic Decisions Embedded in This Plan**
- **France B2C is a no-go.** The €500M B2C market is saturated by well-funded incumbents (Kartable, SchoolMouv, Superprof). Capital is directed 100% to B2B institutional.
- **Kenya is teacher-phone-first, not student-tablet-first.** Government-issued DLP tablets are >40% non-functional; the teacher’s Android phone is the actual classroom device.
- **Saudi public-school gender segregation is physical infrastructure, not a UI toggle.** Boys’ and girls’ schools require isolated stacks with separate databases, CDNs, and content libraries.
- **Vietnam requires a local corporate entity and a Zalo Mini App strategy.** A representative office and standalone parent app are insufficient for data compliance or parent adoption.

## 2. MARKET-BY-MARKET STRATEGY

### 2.1 Vietnam — Wave 1

**Curriculum & Localization**
- **Mandate:** Full alignment with the **Chương trình Giáo dục phổ thông mới (CT 2018)** and Circular 32/2018/TT-BGDĐT.
- **Page-Level Alignment:** Content maps to the three state-censored textbook series—***Cánh Diều***, ***Chân Trời Sáng Tạo***, and ***Kết Nối Tri Thức***—at the page/section level, not abstract topic mapping. If a Grade 10 Math teacher is on *Kết Nối Tri Thức* Chapter 3, §2, the adaptive path surfaces that exact progression.
- **English Fragmentation:** Three distinct adapters are required: (1) SGK/Global Success for public schools, (2) Cambridge for international/private schools, and (3) TOEFL/IELTS for B2C.
- **Out of Scope:** History, Geography, and Civic Education are excluded due to South China Sea and socialist-history sensitivities.

**Regulatory & Compliance**
- **PDPD (Decree 13/2023/ND-CP):** Effective July 2023, this imposes GDPR-equivalent obligations, including a **local Data Protection Officer**, **Privacy Impact Assessments** for cross-border transfer, and auditable consent management in Vietnamese.
- **Corporate Presence:** A **Vietnamese-incorporated entity** (not a rep office) is mandatory to bid as a Tier-2 subcontractor to state-owned integrators (Viettel, FPT IS, CMC TS). Direct foreign bidding on *muasamcong.mpi.gov.vn* is effectively impossible.

**Product & UX**
- **“Luyện đề” Mode:** The UI must resemble a digital test paper—muted blues/greys, no cartoony avatars. Parents fear **“nghiện game”** (game addiction); gamification is toxic.
- **Rank, Not Mastery:** Parents buy **xếp hạng (percentile rank)**. Every notification must show class/school/district percentile; never lead with abstract proficiency scores.
- **Zalo-First:** The parent dashboard is built as a **Zalo Mini App** (not a standalone download). Mock-exam score notifications push as Zalo messages showing “Điểm thi thử” and percentile.
- **Session Control:** Mandatory PIN-locked 20-minute default timer.

**Teacher & School Adoption**
Teachers fear platform adoption because effective digital homework reduces demand for their private tutoring income.
- **Teacher Premium Dashboard:** Generates printable worksheets and bonus problems that teachers can distribute in their own paid after-school sessions.
- **Positioning:** Frame as **“giáo cụ trợ giảng”** (teaching aid), never as a replacement. Provide aggregate gap analytics and teacher-controlled classroom mode (session start/stop).

**Procurement Motion**
- **Primary (B2C/B2B2C):** Direct parent subscriptions via Momo, ZaloPay, VNPay.
- **Secondary (B2B Formal Schools):** Bundle through formal schools as “digital homework” (*bài tập về nhà số*), not independent cram-school chains. Circular 29/2022/TT-BGDĐT has severely restricted *dạy thêm* (extra/cram classes).
- **Tertiary (B2G):** Target DOET “smart city” budgets (e.g., Đà Nẵng Smart City, Hà Nội Đề án 06). MOET national scale is a political-economic designation within the Digital Transformation master program, not a linear approval from a provincial pilot.

### 2.2 Kenya — Wave 1

**Curriculum & Localization**
- **Digitizable Scope:** Strictly limited to **literacy, numeracy, and select science strands**. Performative assessment strands (art, movement, practicals) cannot be digitized and are excluded to avoid KICD false-advertising complaints.
- **Dual Track:**
  - **CBC Track (PP1–Grade 9):** Digital **CBA rubrics** with the four-tier scale (*Exceeding, Meeting, Approaching, Below Expectations*).
  - **Legacy Track:** **KPSEA** (upper-primary exit, replacing the abolished KCPE) and **KCSE** (secondary). Never market “8-4-4 prep.”
- **KICD Approval:** Submit early for the digital content stamp. Realistic timeline is **12–18 months** of examinations-grade scrutiny with multiple revision rounds.

**Regulatory & Compliance**
- **Data Protection Act 2019:** Host in East Africa; consent required for export.
- **Donor IP Firewall:** Wall off proprietary adaptive algorithms under a strict SaaS license. Grant-funded content (Mastercard Foundation, UNICEF, USAID) may carry CC-BY obligations; under no circumstances allow donor licensing to contaminate Core IP.

**Product & Access**
- **Teacher-Phone First:** The target device is the **teacher’s personal Android phone** (Tecno/Itel, 1GB RAM, 320×480 screen, 3G). Government-issued DLP tablets are >40% non-functional. The product is a **responsive PWA** or lightweight APK (<15MB base) for front-of-class projection or small-group rotation.
- **Offline-First:** Content sync via **SD card** (distributed through textbook supply chains: Longhorn, OUP East Africa) or school Wi-Fi hotspot. Assessments batch-sync nightly. No streaming dependency.
- **CBC Portfolio Automation:** Auto-generate **PDF rubrics, photo checklists, and observation logs** for teacher filing. The value proposition is **time saved**, not student learning gains.
- **Dual Reporting:** Display the official CBC rubric for the school, and a **traditional grade equivalent** (A/B/C/D or percentage) for parents.

**Localization**
- **Audio:** English + Kiswahili at launch. Add Kikuyu/Dholuo TTS in Year 2 based on telemetry. Do not let mother-tongue scope delay launch.
- **Contextualization:** Kenyan names (Wanjiku, Otieno), KES currency, and local contexts (*shamba*, *matatu*, *sukuma wiki*).

**Procurement & Monetization**
- **Pricing:** **Term packages (KES 300–500/term)** via M-Pesa Paybill. Weekly micro-transactions fail due to ~15–20% Safaricom Daraja fees and extreme churn. Account for **18% VAT and Digital Service Tax (DST)** on non-resident providers.
- **B2B Private:** Target **APBET schools** and mid-tier academies in Nairobi, Mombasa, and Kisumu. Sell per-seat, per-term licenses aligned to Kenyan school terms (Jan–Mar, May–Jul, Sep–Nov).
- **B2G/Donor:** Bypass slow Ministry procurement via NGO/donor channels. Target **2–3 counties** (Nairobi, Kiambu, Mombasa) with digitally savvy County Directors. County Assembly budget approval follows the fiscal calendar (July–June) and adds **3–6 months**.

### 2.3 Saudi Arabia — Wave 2

**Curriculum & Localization**
- **Private School Integration:** Build **LTI 1.3 / OneRoster** for private-school LMS stacks (ManageBac, iSAMS, PowerSchool). **Madaris** integration is a B2G Wave 2 requirement only; private schools do not use it.
- **Scope:** Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and English. **Islamic Studies, Hadith, and Saudi Social Studies are out of scope** for a foreign platform.
- **Evolution:** Strip or rewrite **all evolutionary references through Grade 12** in public-school tracks. Private IB/American tracks may retain evolution only under a completely separate curriculum flag with distinct content repositories.
- **Numerals:** Default to **Western Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3)** in all STEM. Offer Eastern Arabic numerals **only** in Arabic Language Arts (*Lughah Arabiyyah*).

**Regulatory, Gender & Culture**
- **Language:** Full RTL Arabic in **Modern Standard Arabic (MSA)**. TTS may use Najdi dialect for voice prompts; never use written dialect.
- **Gender Architecture:** Public education is strictly gender-segregated with separate inspectorates. Deploy **physically separate tenant instances** (separate DBs, CDNs, content libraries). A UI toggle is non-compliant. Girls’ schools require same-gender avatars and voice tutors only.
- **Wali Dashboard:** Parental oversight of daughters’ education is legally mandatory. The **Wali guardian dashboard must deploy before any student-facing feature** in girls’ schools, with **Absher-linked authentication** where feasible. This is market access, not a product feature.

**Product & UX**
- **Positioning:** Position as a **Tawjihi-track accelerator**. Build study paths for Natural Sciences, Administrative Sciences, and Humanities. Include **Qiyas GAT and SAAT** modules.
- **Teacher Authority:** The adaptive tutor must be **“obedient to the teacher’s command.”** Deploy a “Classroom commander dashboard” and teacher-controlled mode. Any UX implying the algorithm “knows better than the teacher” triggers rejection.

**Procurement & Legal**
- **Commercial Agent:** Etimad/Tawreed registration requires a **100% Saudi-owned commercial agent** or JV with education-sector **MoE Supplier Category Codes**. This is a **board-level strategic decision**. Agent due diligence takes **4–6 months**; the agent must already hold **NCeL** relationships. Budget **12–18 months** for NCeL content certification from submission.
- **Teacher Training:** **Mandatory in-person training**, with trainers **gender-matched to the school**. Sending a male trainer to a girls’ school is a legal and cultural violation.
- **B2B Private (Primary Revenue, H2 2025):** Fastest path to revenue. Target premium private chains (Misk Schools, Dhahran Academy, Manarat Al-Riyadh). These buy directly in USD/SAR with 30–60 day terms.
- **B2G (Secondary, 2026):** Register on **Etimad** and **Tawreed**. Pursue **Human Capability Development Program (HCDP)** smart-school pilots.

### 2.4 France — Wave 2

**Curriculum & Content**
- **Terminology:** Alignment with **Programmes 2023** (Eduscol) must use exact French didactic terms: *compétence*, *capacité*, *attendu*, *maîtrise*, *situation problème*. Franglais (“skill,” “learning objective”) is unacceptable.
- **Choc des Savoirs:** Frame the platform as **“remédiation ciblée”** reinforcing *le socle commun* and *les fondamentaux*. Avoid UX language suggesting “exploration,” “discovery,” or “student-led inquiry” in B2G materials.
- **CNED Sensitivity:** The **CNED** holds a statutory monopoly on distance learning. Frame strictly as **“remédiation ciblée en complément de la classe présentielle”** with explicit teacher orchestration. Never position as home-schooling or autonomous remote learning.
- **Grading:** Output **diagnostic indicators only**; the teacher assigns the grade. Automated letter grades trigger union opposition and violate pedagogical norms.

**Regulatory & Technical**
- **EU AI Act:** Educational AI is classified as **high-risk** (Article 6 + Annex III). Requires **CE conformity assessment**, bias testing and mitigation documentation, **human-in-the-loop (HITL) logging**, and algorithmic transparency. Budget **€100k–200k and 6–9 months**; begin Q1 2024.
- **RGAA 4.1:** Mandatory for public procurement. Budget **3–4 months of front-end engineering** for WCAG 2.1 AA + RGAA 4.1 (high-contrast modes, keyboard navigation, MathML screen-reader support). This is a **technical disqualifier**.
- **SCELL Interoperability:** Required for LMS integration in public networks.
- **Hosting:** **OVH Cloud SecNumCloud** or **3DS Outscale**. Using AWS, Azure, or GCP for French education data is politically toxic in several académies regardless of EU region.

**Screen Time & Product**
- **Digital Sobriety:** Publish a **quantified “data diet”** metric (e.g., “10 minutes = 2.3MB vs. 45MB video”). Default 20-minute sessions, enforce a 20-20-20 eye-break, and disable push notifications during school hours (08:30–16:30).
- **Aesthetic:** No gamification sounds, coins, or explosions. Use a minimalist, ink-on-paper aesthetic. Position as *travail personnel encadré*, never as a “jeu éducatif.”

**Teacher, Union & Inspectorate Relations**
- **Unions:** Budget **€30,000–€50,000 per académie** for union “consultation fees” (paid pedagogical training sessions). Success metric: **“No académie-level boycott or strike motion citing our platform.”** Expect critical communiqués regardless.
- **The Inspecteur:** Recruit a **recently retired Inspecteur d’académie** or **IA-IPR** as a paid advisor. Their endorsement letter is a prerequisite for Rectorat meetings.
- **Teacher Workload:** Integrate with ENT gradebooks via **LSU-compatible exports** or automated Pronote CSV ingestion. **Pronote has no public API**; manual rostering is a death sentence for adoption.

**Procurement Motion**
- **B2B Private (Primary):** Target *collèges* and *lycées privés*. Decision cycles: 3–6 months.
- **Fast Track:** Target **Departmental Councils (Conseils Départementaux)** and **Regional Councils (Conseils Régionaux)** before Rectorats. They fund digital equipment with 6–9 month cycles and less political friction.
- **B2G (Long-term):** Rectorat/REP+ networks. Requires EduConnect interconnection (budget 4–6 months), UAI registration, and explicit GDPR/DPD documentation.

## 3. TECHNOLOGY & LOCALIZATION ARCHITECTURE

### 3.1 Dual-Layer Model: Core vs. Fabric

The architecture enforces strict API contracts between the invariant **Shared Core** and the per-country **Local Fabric**.

| Layer | Component | Responsibility |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| **Shared Core** | **Adaptive Engine** | Bayesian proficiency updates, spaced-repetition scheduler, knowledge-graph traversal. Accepts any JSON-LD curriculum map via REST API. |
| | **Multimodal Runtime** | Speech recognition, TTS orchestration, math-formula renderer, Arabic STEM bidirectional rendering. Calls sovereign frontier API endpoints with regional fine-tuning and RAG grounding. |
| | **Assessment Generator** | Item-difficulty calibration, IRT scoring, distractor algebra. Consumes question templates and forbidden-topic lists from the Local Fabric. |
| | **Sync Layer** | State-management abstraction. Consumes locale-specific sync strategy (SQLite delta, online-native, nightly batch). |
| | **Trust & Safety Mesh** | PII vault, encryption, audit logging, regional data residency. Reads per-market Rego policy rules. |
| **Local Fabric** | **Curriculum Adapter** | Maps every node to official ministry codes. |
| | **Pedagogy Policy Module** | Local feedback logic (e.g., France = *indicateurs de maîtrise* only; Vietnam = high-stakes timed drills; Kenya = CBC four-tier rubrics). |
| | **Regulatory Compliance Proxy** | Rego automated interception + human veto layer. |
| | **Commerce & Identity Shell** | Payment rails, tax logic, parental consent, tender APIs, national identity integration (EduConnect, Absher). |
| | **Infrastructure Profile** | Sync strategy, asset packages, connectivity model, hardware target. |

### 3.2 AI & Content Generation

The “swappable SLM” assumption is abandoned. Production-grade subject-specific SLMs do not exist for these markets in 2024–2026.

**Decision:** Deploy **sovereign frontier model APIs** (GPT-4/Claude/Gemini) with **regional fine-tuning layers** and **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** over local curriculum corpora, hosted in regional sovereign clouds. This prevents hallucinated math and dangerous cultural errors while satisfying data residency.

### 3.3 Connectivity & Sync by Market

| Market | Strategy | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Kenya** | **Offline-first mandatory.** SQLite timestamp-based delta sync; nightly batch sync. **CRDTs eliminated entirely.** | Computationally prohibitive on 1GB-RAM DLP tablets and low-end Android Go. Last-write-wins for non-critical conflicts. |
| **Vietnam** | Offline-capable for tier-2/rural; online-native for urban. | Variable connectivity. |
| **Saudi Arabia** | **Online-native.** | >95% 4G/5G coverage and school Wi-Fi make offline sync wasteful. |
| **France** | Online-native with CNIL-compliant local caching only. | No student data held offline longer than a single session without explicit consent logging. |

### 3.4 Modular Asset Pipeline

- **Base APK/PWA:** <15MB runtime only.
- **Regional Asset Packs:** Downloaded on first launch (language packs, fine-tuning adapters, voice skins) or sideloaded via **SD card** in Kenya (distributed through textbook supply chains).

### 3.5 Data Sovereignty & Hosting

| Market | Hosting | Compliance |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Vietnam** | **Viettel Cloud** or **FPT Telecom** (HCMC/Hanoi) | Local corporate entity required. Decree 13/2023/ND-CP (PDPD): local DPO, Privacy Impact Assessments, auditable consent. |
| **Saudi Arabia** | **STC Cloud** or **Mobily Nebula** (Riyadh) | Physically isolated stacks by gender. PDPL + NCA Cloud Cybersecurity Controls. |
| **France** | **OVH Cloud SecNumCloud** or **3DS Outscale** (Gravelines) | GDPR; EU AI Act high-risk system; RGAA 4.1; SCELL interoperability. |
| **Kenya** | **AWS Cape Town** or **Node Africa** (Nairobi) | Data Protection Act 2019. |

### 3.6 Content Compliance Engine

- **Automated Layer:** Open Policy Agent (Rego) rules intercept all LLM and CMS output before student delivery.
- **Human Layer:** A **Regional Content Integrity Team** (one senior pedagogical editor per market) holds final veto power. Rego handles flagging; humans handle adjudication.

### 3.7 Integration & Identity Standards

- **Vietnam:** **Zalo Mini App** for parent dashboard.
- **Saudi Arabia:** **LTI 1.3 / OneRoster** for private LMS; **Absher-linked Wali authentication** for girls’ schools.
- **France:** **EduConnect** mandatory for public scale (budget 4–6 months); **Pronote** has no public API—plan for manual CSV roster import + LSU-compatible exports.
- **Kenya:** **M-Pesa Daraja API** (Paybill). Negotiate Safaricom zero-rating/revenue-share.

## 4. GO-TO-MARKET & PROCUREMENT STRATEGY

### 4.1 Organizational & Capital Model

- **Global Center of Excellence (US):** Owns the Shared Core. ~15 FTEs (platform engineering, AI/ML, security, DevOps). Funded as a platform cost center.
- **Regional Pods:** Each market receives a Local Fabric squad:
  - **Country Lead:** P&L owner.
  - **Senior Pedagogical Editor:** Veto power over content.
  - **Regulatory & Partnerships Lead:** Ministry navigation, legal entity incorporation, donor/grant relationships.
  - **GTM Lead:** Sales and expansion.

**Resourcing & Budget**
- **Wave 1 (Vietnam, Kenya):** Full pods hired Q1 2024 (5 FTE each + curriculum-mapping teams of 2–3 specialists). These teams generate revenue while hardening adapters.
- **Wave 2 (Saudi, France):**
