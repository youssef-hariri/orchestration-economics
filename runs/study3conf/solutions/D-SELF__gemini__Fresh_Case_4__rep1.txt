# Executive Policy: Global Sync & Sovereignty Model

**To:** All Country Managing Directors, HR Leads, and Department Heads
**Subject:** Implementation of Global Flexible-Work Policy (Effective Q1 2026)

---

### 1. Executive Summary
To sustain our competitive advantage in a shifting global landscape, the company is transitioning from a "presence-based" attendance model to a **"Value-Delivery" model**. The *Global Sync & Sovereignty Model* provides a unified framework for performance while granting country offices the operational autonomy required to respect local labor laws and cultural nuances.

### 2. The Global Core: Universal Standards
These three pillars are non-negotiable for every employee, in every office, to ensure consistency in performance assessment and career development:

*   **Pillar I: Role-Based Output Standards (RBOS):** Performance is tied strictly to OKRs and objective data (Jira/Git/Project Management metrics). Physical presence is no longer a metric for performance reviews. Flexible work models are **eligibility-based benefits** awarded for sustained high-performance.
*   **Pillar II: Synchronous Collaboration Windows (SCW):** To respect local labor laws and time zones, teams will operate within a **"Local Synchronous Window" (10:00–14:00 local time).** All non-critical collaboration must occur within this block. Global meetings outside this window are considered exceptions and trigger local overtime/compensation protocols.
*   **Pillar III: The "Right to Disconnect" Protocol:** Internal non-critical communication is prohibited outside of an individual’s contracted hours. All asynchronous updates must be tagged "Non-Urgent/Action-Not-Required-Until-Next-Shift" to preserve legal compliance.

### 3. The Sovereignty Tiers: Local Operational Modules
Each of our 12 offices will function under one of three Tiers, determined by the Country Lead and vetted by the Regional Compliance Officer:

| Tier | Model | Best For | Cultural/Operational Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | **Compressed Week** | UK, Nordics | 100/80/100 model. Eligibility-based. |
| **B** | **Hybrid Flex** | Japan, UAE, China | Focus on "Contribution Transparency" (Mentorship/Knowledge Transfer KPIs). |
| **C** | **Results-Only (ROWE)** | Engineering Hubs | Fully distributed; autonomy-first. |

### 4. Governance & Fairness
To ensure fairness and prevent regional friction, we have instituted:

*   **The Calibration Council:** A quarterly cross-functional body that monitors promotion rates and burnout across all regions. This Council is empowered to audit for bias—if data shows that "in-office" employees are being promoted faster than "flexible" employees for equivalent output, corrective action will be taken.
*   **Team Operating Protocol (Dashboard):** An internal portal will explicitly state the "Working Mode" of every office. This transparency eliminates the "disloyalty" stigma by confirming that an off-site colleague is working within a company-approved structure.
*   **Compliance Override:** In the event of a conflict between a global directive and a local labor law, **local law supersedes company policy.** Regional Compliance Officers are mandated to issue a "Local Override" to protect the company and the employee.

### 5. Implementation Timeline
*   **Month 1 (Audit Phase):** Regional Compliance Officers conduct a Legal Impact Study for their respective markets.
*   **Month 2 (Designation Phase):** Country Leads submit their proposed "Sovereignty Tier" to the Calibration Council for validation.
*   **Month 3 (Go-Live):** The "Team Operating Protocol" dashboard goes live, and all managers attend the "Managing by Output" certification workshop.

---

**Directive Statement:**
Our goal is not to force a singular global experience, but to foster a unified global *commitment*. Whether an employee works 32 hours or 40 hours, their value to this company is measured by their output, their mentorship of others, and their contribution to our clients.
