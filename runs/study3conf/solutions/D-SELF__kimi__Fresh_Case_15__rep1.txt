## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The legacy fixed-USD royalty model is structurally incongruent with operating reality in high-inflation, depreciating-currency markets. Franchisee distress has metastasized from liquidity risk into collective political risk, while company-owned stores in stable-currency markets continue to generate hard-currency profits.

**The Proposal.** Replace the flat USD levy with **DTAR 2.0**, a jurisdiction-specific architecture comprising:
*   **Local Value Component (LVC):** 3.0–3.5% of gross revenue, payable in local currency (LCY), indexed to market-appropriate inflation and FX benchmarks.
*   **Global Brand Component (GBC):** 2.0–3.125% of gross revenue, payable in USD (or blocked for hard-currency preservation), capturing IP, technology, and ad-fund value.
*   **Hardship Relief Corridor (HRC):** Auto-activated counter-cyclical relief that converts foregone USD obligations into collateralized local-currency receivables—not forgiven debt.
*   **Synthetic Hedging & Trapped-Cash Management:** NDF programs, dual-rate benchmarks, and blocked-reserve accounts to defend consolidated hard-currency NPV.

**Status of Readiness.** Turkey, Egypt, and Nigeria architectures are advanced. Indonesia is cleared for a **100% LCY interim bridge** pending STPW re-registration. **Brazil remains undeveloped**—a material portfolio gap that requires immediate remediation before board approval.

**Critical Financial Exposure.** The plan preserves headline hard-currency value but embeds unquantified balance-sheet risk: (i) an **unsized FX mismatch** on USD-procured equipment leasing, estimated at **$30M–$50M+**; and (ii) an initial **$15M liquidity seed** that is insufficient for portfolio-scale stress. Consolidated revenue quality will degrade without active hedge management and honest ceiling-setting on deferred obligations.

**Decision Required.** The Board is asked to approve Phase 0 (regulatory pre-clearance and counsel engagement), authorize Treasury to size the FX mismatch reserve, and mandate a 60-day sprint to finalize Brazil and Indonesia post-registration mechanics.

## 2. DTAR 2.0 JURISDICTION-SPECIFIC ARCHITECTURE

### 2.1 Turkey — High Volatility / Entrenched Associations

**Legal & FX Architecture**
*   **Law No. 7346 Compliance:** FX-denominated contracts between Turkish residents are prohibited. The LVC is payable to the Turkish master franchisee in TRY. The GBC is payable directly to the offshore franchisor as a cross-border IP royalty under a separate License Agreement.
*   **Withholding Tax Gross-Up:** Turkish WHT on IP royalties is **20%**. The GBC is invoiced at **3.125%** of gross revenue so that the net receipt to the franchisor equals **2.5%**. The franchisee bears the WHT as a pass-through.
*   **FX Benchmark (LVC):** The “Deliverable TRY/USD Rate” equals the CBRT 90-day official selling average **plus** the **Commercial FX Adjustment Factor (CFAF)**. CFAF = 50% of the Bloomberg/REEL street premium, **capped at 12%**.

**Royalty Mechanics**
*   **LVC:** **3.5%** of gross revenue, payable in TRY.
*   **GBC:** **3.125%** gross (**2.5%** net) of gross revenue, payable in USD offshore.
*   **CPI Indexation (Monthly):**
    *   ≤ **5%** MoM CPI: 100% pass-through.
    *   **5%–10%** MoM CPI: 100% of first 5% + 75% of excess; deferred 25% trued up in 6 months.
    *   \> **10%** MoM CPI: 100% of first 5% + 75% of next 5% + 50% of excess above 10%; balance deferred 12 months at market interest.
*   **Numeric Example:** On TRY 10M monthly revenue with 7% MoM CPI, the effective LVC escalates to **3.73%**. Total gross royalty = **6.875%**; net to franchisor post-WHT = **6.23%**.

**Hardship Relief Corridor (HRC)**
*   **Triggers:** (i) Monthly CPI > **8%** MoM for 2 consecutive months; (ii) 6-month official TRY depreciation > **30%**; or (iii) non-standard CBRT import deposits.
*   **Terms:** GBC reduced by 50% (net **1.25%** to franchisor). Foregone **1.25%** converts to a **TRY-denominated, collateralized deferred note** (3-year amortization at market rates). LVC remains due in full.

**FX Risk Management**
*   Franchisor enters **12-month TRY/USD non-deliverable forwards (NDFs)** rolled quarterly. Notional cover = **80%** of projected quarterly TRY LVC inflow. Strike = Deliverable Rate + 5% risk premium.

**Governance & Dispute Resolution**
*   **Vertical Consultation Committee** constituted under the Turkish Commercial Code. Agenda restricted to supply chain and local marketing; **royalty rates are excluded from vote**.
*   Annual **4054 Competition Law Compliance Audit** conducted by external counsel.
*   Sanitized minutes to prevent information-exchange liability.

> **Risk Disclosure:** The 12% CFAF cap creates an implicit subsidy if the street premium exceeds 24%. NDF roll risk during a CBRT intervention freeze is unmodeled.

### 2.2 Egypt — Non-Convertible / Parallel-Market Premium

**Legal & FX Architecture**
*   **Dual-Rate Benchmark:** The “Effective EGP/USD Rate” is the higher of (i) CBE 90-day official average, or (ii) CBE rate × [1 + (0.5 × Parallel Market Premium)], **capped at 1.35× official**.
*   **GBC Remittance Split:** Only **1.0%** is remitted offshore via approved IP channels (GAFI/Ministry of Trade registration required). **1.5%** is retained in an **EGP Blocked Reserve Account** at tier-1 banks for local procurement or queued CBE conversion.

**Royalty Mechanics**
*   **LVC:** Payable in EGP. Benchmarked to the Effective Rate above.
*   **GBC:** **2.5%** total (1.0% offshore / 1.5% blocked reserve).
*   **CPI Indexation:** Monthly adjustment with **100% pass-through** of headline urban CPI (CAPMAS). **No cap**—structural >20% inflation must be reflected to prevent real obligation collapse.

**Hardship Relief Corridor (HRC)**
*   **Triggers:** (i) CPI > **30%** YoY; (ii) Parallel premium > **40%**; or (iii) CBE import deposit requirements.
*   **Terms:** Offshore GBC remittance is suspended (accrues as deferred FX receivable). The 1.5% reserve portion continues to fund local imports. LVC remains due in full.

**Financing & Liquidity**
*   Egyptian bank partners require a **50% first-loss guarantee** plus **1:1 offshore USD cash collateral** (back-to-back structure) to extend EGP revolving credit at commercially viable spreads.

**Governance & Dispute Resolution**
*   Economic relief is filed with **GAFI** and the **Ministry of Manpower** as a sector-wide stimulus, not a selective inducement, to mitigate union-busting exposure.
*   Disputes resolved via **Cairo Regional Centre for International Commercial Arbitration (CRCICA)** under Law No. 27/1994.

> **Risk Disclosure:** The HRC CPI trigger (>30%) is the **modal state**, not a tail risk; expect permanent activation. The 1.5% blocked EGP reserve is exposed to real-time devaluation and convertibility rot.
