**PRODUCT STRATEGY: ADAPTIVE LEARNING PLATFORM — GLOBAL LOCALIZATION & EXPANSION**

**Markets:** Vietnam | Saudi Arabia | France | Kenya  
**Horizon:** 2026  
**Strategic Doctrine:** *Invariant Cognitive Core. Psychometrically Validated Pedagogical Grammar. Institutionally Authentic Shell.*

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

We will expand into four institutionally distinct education markets by deploying a single adaptive engine (L1) that interprets **Psychometrically Validated Pedagogical Grammar Profiles (PGPs)**—runtime configurations that alter mastery thresholds, sequencing logic, and assessment interfaces without forking the codebase. This preserves engineering velocity while respecting the fact that Vietnam’s high-stakes exam economy, Saudi Arabia’s sovereign digital ecosystem, France’s publisher-guarded public sector, and Kenya’s exam-anchored political economy each impose distinct pedagogical rules.

We explicitly reject a capital-obliterating four-market parallel launch. Entry is sequenced by revenue velocity and regulatory drag: **Vietnam (B2B2C tutoring integration) → Kenya (ultra-light D2C) → Saudi Arabia (sovereign B2G) → France (publisher white-label)**. Early markets generate cash and training data that fund later markets’ compliance costs.

**Funding Requirement:** **$7.5 million over 18 months**, supporting 29 FTEs (13 US HQ, 16 local). Inter-phase cash gates and kill criteria preserve runway if early markets underperform.

## 1. SHARED TECHNOLOGY CORE FRAMEWORK

### 1.1 The Invariant Core (L1)
Owned exclusively by US HQ. Local pods configure via API; they cannot modify source code.

| Domain | Invariant Rule |
|--------|----------------|
| **Proficiency Graph** | Bayesian Knowledge Tracing / Deep Knowledge Tracing hybrid. Learner models are latent-variable graphs independent of content domain. |
| **Learning Objective Ontology** | A strict atomic schema (the “learning atom”) defining prerequisites, evidence weights, and mastery states. Local teams map *to* this schema; they do not redefine it. |
| **Data & Privacy Vault** | Zero-knowledge architecture for PII. Offline-first delta sync. SOC 2 Type II baseline. |
| **Sequencing Runtime** | A grammar-aware interpreter that loads PGPs at runtime. |

### 1.2 The Psychometric Validation Layer (L1.5)
A PGP cannot be deployed by engineering fiat. Before any market launch, HQ Data Science and a local psychometric partner must complete:

*   **Item Calibration:** Local norming of item difficulty and discrimination parameters.
*   **Differential Item Functioning (DIF):** Ensuring items perform equivalently across gender, region, and language subgroups.
*   **Local Efficacy Trials:** Controlled study (minimum 8 weeks, N≥400) demonstrating adaptive gains against local benchmark assessments.
*   **Validation Gate:** HQ Product Council reviews trial data and approves PGP activation.

**Timeline Rule:** Validation runs concurrently with L2/L3 localization but must complete **90 days before** commercial launch. Phase I (Vietnam) validation commenced **Q4 2024**.

### 1.3 The Localization Interface (L2) & Transparency Layer (L2.5)

| Component | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| **Curriculum Adapter** | Ingests national standards and outputs a JSON map to the invariant ontology. Requires in-country experts to negotiate scope-and-sequence authority with ministries, KICD, and publishers. |
| **Assessment-Format Converter** | Renders local item types (Vietnamese MCQ grids, French *maîtrise* scales, Kenyan rubrics) within the engine’s evidence model. |
| **Compliance Wrapper** | Data residency shards, content moderation queues, NCA/KICD/ANSSI/EU AI Act certification pipelines, and accessibility rendering. |
| **Explainability API** | Every adaptive recommendation must be renderable as a human-readable justification. |
| **Teacher Override** | The teacher can override any engine recommendation; the engine treats the override as new evidence. |
| **Regulatory Pre-Architecture** | Pre-built algorithmic impact assessments for CNIL/EU AI Act (France), SDAIA/NCA (Saudi Arabia), ODPC (Kenya), and MIC/MOET (Vietnam). |

### 1.4 The Market Shell (L3)
Owned by in-market product pods. This is where the product *feels* native: UX components, voice talent, device optimization, payment rails, procurement dashboards, and SSO connectors.

### 1.5 The Four Pedagogical Grammar Profiles (PGPs)

| Market | Profile | Mastery Threshold | Sequencing Logic | Session Cadence | Assessment Interface |
|--------|---------|-------------------|------------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Vietnam** | **HSG** | 85% confidence | Rapid pattern recognition; blocks scaffold toward exam-format fluency | 15–20 min sprints; countdown alignment | THPTQG MCQ grid + ĐGNL complex-item emulator; printable MOET-format mocks |
| **Saudi Arabia** | **SSG** | 75% confidence | Recursive STEM scaffolding; Qiyas-blueprint priority tagging | Teacher-controlled session blocks (institutional timetable) | Standard international math notation; Qiyas-style items; Tatweer ingestion format |
| **France** | **TCG** | 70% confidence | Spiral progression across *Cycles*; prerequisite review distributed over weeks | Student: 15-min diagnostic sprints. Teacher: explainable intervention dashboard | *Maîtrise* scale; argumentation-stage scaffolding; *contrôle continu* alignment |
| **Kenya** | **CPG** | Multi-dimensional rubric (Knowledge/Skill/Attitude) | Exam-aligned foundational drill with portfolio reporting layer | Offline-first; 20–30 min sprints | KNEC rubric upload; lightweight text/rubric teacher inputs; KPSEA alignment |

## 2. MARKET-BY-MARKET LOCALIZATION PLAYBOOKS

### 2.1 VIETNAM: “The Dual-Track Exam Ally”

#### Regulatory & Curriculum Constraints
*   **Curriculum:** The *Chương trình giáo dục phổ thông mới* (CTGDPT 2018) is rolling out through 2030. The market is in a **dual-track transition**: the **THPTQG** (graduation/university entrance exam) persists with MCQ science grids, while elite universities (VNU-HCM, VNU-HN) have adopted autonomous competency assessments (**ĐGNL/ĐGTD**) incorporating complex item types.
*   **Textbooks:** Three state-published series exist (*Chân trời sáng tạo*, *Kết nối tri thức*, *Cánh diều*), revised biennially. Page-by-page mapping to all three is a content-debt death spiral.
*   **Content Sovereignty:** **Decree 13/2023/ND-CP** mandates data localization and verifiable parental consent for children’s data. The **Cybersecurity Law 2019** grants authorities onshore inspection rights, decryption demands, and content takedown powers.
*   **Extra Teaching Regime:** **Decree 17/2021/NĐ-CP** imposes strict licensing on supplementary academic instruction (*dạy thêm*). Platforms white-labeled to unlicensed centers face joint liability.
*   **Legal Structure:** A 100% foreign-owned LLC distributing localized curriculum content is legally precarious under Vietnam’s Investment and Publishing Laws. **We will establish a 100% foreign-owned software enterprise** (permitted under WTO commitments for IT services) for platform/IP ownership, paired with an **arms-length distribution agreement** with a licensed Vietnamese educational content company. VIE and nominee structures are categorically rejected.

#### Localization Imperatives
*   **Single-Series Launch:** Launch mapped to **one** dominant series, *Kết nối tri thức* (prevalent in Hanoi/HCMC). Competency-align to the national framework for future crosswalks.
*   **Dual-Track Assessment Engine:** Build both THPTQG MCQ bubble-sheet emulators *and* ĐGNL complex-item reasoning grids.
*   **Human Voice Talent Only:** Commission Northern (Hanoi) and Southern (HCMC) **human voice actors** using a formal pedagogical register (*tiếng phổ thông trong trường học*). Robotic TTS is culturally rejected.
*   **Zalo as Logistics Layer:** Use Zalo Official Accounts for homework alerts, score reports, and payment reminders. **Do not use Zalo Mini Apps as the learning venue**—their memory caps and API restrictions make rich adaptive rendering unreliable. Learning occurs in a dedicated mobile app.
*   **Geopolitical Content Filter:** Mandatory pre-deployment audit ensures “Biển Đông” nomenclature and Hoàng Sa/Trường Sa sovereignty depictions.
*   **Teacher-Influencer Co-Branding:** Vietnamese parents buy **famous teachers (*thầy nổi tiếng*)**, not algorithms. Partner with individual teacher influencers who have large YouTube/Zalo followings. License their problem sets and co-brand content.

#### Procurement & Channel Strategy
*   **Primary Channel — B2B2C (2026):** White-label to **licensed** tutoring centers and teacher influencers. The startup charges centers a SaaS license or revenue share. Parents pay the center.
*   **Pricing:** Pure adaptive exercise tools command **$3–5/month** D2C, or a **$2/student/month** upsell bundled inside existing online schools.
*   **Payment Rails:** MoMo, ZaloPay, VietQR (via distribution partner).
*   **B2G:** Foreign software startups are legally ineligible for public-school *gói thầu* procurement without a Vietnamese publishing partner. **Direct B2G is abandoned for 2026.**

#### Entry Sequence
*   **Q4 2024–Q2 2025:** Psychometric validation & L2/L3 build.
*   **Q3 2025:** B2B2C launch with *Kết nối tri thức* mapping and first teacher-influencer partnership.
*   **Q1 2026:** Expand to second influencer and 10–15 licensed tutoring centers.

### 2.2 SAUDI ARABIA: “The Sovereign Ecosystem Guest”

#### Regulatory & Curriculum Constraints
*   **National Ecosystem:** **Madrasati/iEN** is the sovereign national education portal. Third parties are **admitted** after MoE/Tatweer vetting and often must ingest content into Tatweer-controlled repositories. It is not an open LMS.
*   **Assessment:** **Qiyas** (ETEC) standardized tests remain high-stakes.
*   **Cyber & Data:** **NCA** (National Cybersecurity Authority) compliance is required for K–12 edtech. **SDAIA/NDMO** classify educational student data as likely **“Restricted”** or **“Highly Restricted,”** requiring sovereign encryption key management. **Assume zero PII replication to US HQ.**
*   **Procurement:** Centralized through the **Etimad** platform. Pre-qualification requires past performance, financial guarantees, and Saudization (*Nitaqat*) compliance.

#### Localization Imperatives
*   **Madrasati as Ingestion:** Pursue admission as a Tatweer-approved content provider. Expect to ingest content into MoE-controlled repositories; the student experience likely remains inside Madrasati.
*   **Standard Math Notation:** RTL applies to **text and UI navigation only**. Do not mirror Cartesian planes or number lines. Saudi schools use standard international mathematical notation.
*   **Teacher-Controlled Sessions:** **Remove the prayer-time auto-pause.** Saudi schools operate on Ministry-mandated timetables that already institutionalize prayer breaks. Provide teacher-controlled session blocks only.
*   **Cultural Moderation:** Automated flagging (no uncovered knees/elbows, no mixed-gender scenarios, no non-Islamic religious symbols, no music) plus a **Riyadh-based human review board**. Zero generative AI imagery.
*   **Vendor-Funded PD:** MoE frameworks typically require vendors to fund teacher training. **Budget $150,000–$250,000 annually** for Tatweer-delivered PD and change management to overcome platform fatigue.

#### Procurement & Channel Strategy
*   **Primary Channel — B2G (2027 Revenue):** Structure as a **commercial agency (*wakalah*) or branch office** with a Saudi service partner for Etimad access, or a **minority JV with explicit IP licensing (not transfer), audited code escrow, and exit clauses.** US HQ retains codebase ownership.
*   **Timeline Realism:** MoE/Tatweer/NCA sovereign admission requires **12–24 months**. The 2026 goal is pilot execution and trust signaling; material revenue scales in 2027. If vetting slips beyond Q1 2026, preserve cash by pivoting to D2C private-school sales.
*   **Secondary Channel — D2C:** Target affluent families and private schools (Ma’arif, GEMS, SABIS) only after B2G endorsement. Price in SAR; Mada, STC Pay, Apple Pay.
*   **Data Architecture:** STC Cloud or equivalent in-country. Sovereign key management. No cross-border PII.

#### Entry Sequence
*   **Q2 2025:** Establish commercial agency or minority JV; initiate Tatweer political engagement.
*   **Q3 2025:** Submit for MoE content, theological, and NCA security vetting.
*   **Q1 2026:** Pilot in 10 schools via Tatweer/MoE directive (contingent on vetting velocity).
*   **Q3 2026:** Launch D2C private-school tier using MoE endorsement.

### 2.3 FRANCE: “The Publisher-Integrated Pedagogical Tool”

#### Regulatory & Curriculum Constraints
*   **Curriculum:** Centralized under the *Ministère de l’Éducation nationale*. The **Socle commun** and **Cycles** define progression. The **Bac 2021 reform** diluted the dissertation’s centrality; **contrôle continu** and specialized tracks (STMG, STI2D) now carry significant weight.
*   **EU AI Act:** Adaptive learning in K–12 is likely a **high-risk AI system** under Article 6(2), mandating CE marking, risk management, and conformity assessment. **Budget €200,000–€500,000 and 6–12 months.**
*   **CNIL/GDPR:** **Article 22** (automated decision-making) and CNIL EdTech guidance require algorithmic transparency. **RGAA 4.1 Level AA** is a mandatory technical clause in public procurement; non-declaration means automatic disqualification.
*   **EduConnect:** Certification requires ANSSI alignment and DGESCO *référencement* (18–24 months).

#### Localization Imperatives
*   **Publisher Partnership (Existential):** The market is a walled garden dominated by *éditeurs scolaires* (Nathan, Belin, Hachette, Bordas, Sérier). **Do not attempt direct Rectorat sales.** Secure a **white-label partnership** to embed the adaptive engine inside a publisher’s digital ecosystem. Pilot first with private *sous contrat* schools and *soutien scolaire* networks to generate efficacy data, then negotiate publisher integration.
*   **EU AI Act Compliance:** Commission a conformity assessment partner immediately. Without CE marking, the product cannot legally be placed on the EU education market.
*   **Explainable Teacher Co-Pilot:** Unions characterize opaque data tracking as *évaluomation*. The engine must surface human-readable justifications for every recommendation. Teachers retain full veto power.
*   **Algorithmic Transparency & DPO:** Engage a French **DPO** before collecting any student data. Publish a CNIL-compliant algorithmic transparency notice.
*   **Laïcité & Neutrality:** Neutral geometric avatars. No religious references, holiday themes, or surveillance-style nudges.

#### Procurement & Channel Strategy
*   **Primary Channel — B2B Institutional:** Sell through **publisher partnerships** or private *sous contrat* school networks. Public Rectorat procurement requires *accord-cadre* framework agreements and is closed to foreign startups without a French publisher or distributor.
*   **Pricing:** Avoid per-student pricing in public discourse. Embed pricing as a **royalty per licensed student** or institutional site license through the publisher’s existing contracts.
*   **D2C:** Avoid pure parent marketing in the public context. Use *soutien scolaire* tutoring networks for limited consumer exposure.

#### Entry Sequence
*   **Q1 2025 (Phase 0 Pre-Work):** Initiate publisher partnership negotiations and EU AI Act conformity assessment. No capital deployed to French entity; pre-work funded through HQ overhead.
*   **Q1 2026:** Pilot in 3–5 private *sous contrat* schools and 1 tutoring network.
*   **Q2 2026:** Use pilot data to negotiate publisher white-label LOI.
*   **Q4 2026:** Begin EduConnect/ANSSI certification process for 2028 public-sector readiness.

### 2.4 KENYA: “The Exam-Anchored Lifeline”

#### Regulatory & Curriculum Constraints
*   **KICD Gatekeeping:** The **Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD)** vets all educational materials for CBC alignment. KICD approval is an **existential compliance gate.** Budget 3–6 months for review.
*   **CBC Political Flux:** The **Competency-Based Curriculum (2-6-3-3-3)** is politically contested. The Ruto administration has signaled reviews to reduce parental cost burden. Do not bet the architecture entirely on Strand/Sub-Strand fidelity.
*   **Exam Anchoring:** Despite CBC rhetoric, parents and headteachers remain fixated on **examinations** as the legitimate quality signal. The **KPSEA** is a high-stakes sorting mechanism.
*   **Data:** The **Data Protection Act 2019** (ODPC) requires registration and adequacy determinations for cross-border transfers.

#### Localization Imperatives
*   **KICD Approval Pathway:** Submit core content for KICD review **before** any school deployment.
*   **KPSEA Core + CBC Reporting:** The engine’s primary sequencing targets **foundational literacy, numeracy, and KNEC exam readiness.** The portfolio layer auto-generates CBC rubric reports, but the core survives even if CBC is reformed.
*   **Teacher Portfolio Compiler:** **Do not auto-populate TPAD.** TPAD is an evaluative surveillance tool. Build a **Teacher Portfolio Compiler** that helps teachers *manually* assemble and review their own TPAD evidence. The teacher controls submission; the platform organizes.
*   **Native App for Android Go:** Build a **lightweight native app (<30MB)** optimized for Android Go (1GB RAM) devices. PWAs are unreliable on low-end Tecno/Infinix/Itel hardware.
*   **Lightweight Offline Inputs:** Abandon rich-media (photo/video) offline sync. Use **compressed text/rubric inputs** (JSON forms) that function on 2G and 1GB RAM.
*   **Multi-Language Toggling:** Allow English/Kiswahili switching within a single session.

#### Procurement & Channel Strategy
*   **Primary Channel — D2C (2026):** Native app distributed via WhatsApp viral loops and Safaricom SMS shortcodes. Use **weekly ($0.50) or monthly ($1.50–$2.00) M-Pesa bundles** via Safaricom Daraja API. Abandon sub-$0.10 daily microtransactions—Daraja fees and 20% excise tax destroy unit economics.
*   **Secondary Channel — B2G (Brand-Building):** Core curriculum procurement is **national** (Ministry/donor-funded), not county-led. Target the national **Digital Literacy Program (DLP)** or donor-funded initiatives (World Bank, GPE). Treat as data-gathering and brand equity, not primary revenue. Hardware bundling is typically required.

#### Entry Sequence
*   **Q3 2025:** Native app D2C launch in Nairobi with KPSEA content and weekly M-Pesa bundles.
*   **Q4 2025:** Onboard 50 teachers as Portfolio Compiler ambassadors.
*   **Q1 2026:** Submit for KICD review; bid for national/DLP pilot.

## 5. TECHNICAL & DATA GOVERNANCE

### 5.1 Data Residency & Sovereignty
*   **Vietnam:** Viettel/VNPT hosting; local DPO; Cybersecurity Law 2019 compliance including onshore inspection and takedown protocols; all curriculum content pre-cleared by MIC.
*   **Saudi Arabia:** STC Cloud; SDAIA/NDMO “Restricted” classification with sovereign key management; **no PII replication to US**; analytics engine deployed locally on anonymized cohort data.
*   **France:** OVHcloud/Scaleway (EU); CNIL-registered DPO; GDPR Article 22 explainability documentation; RGAA 4.1 Level AA from wireframe stage.
*   **Kenya:** Local host or AWS Cape Town with ODPC registration; M-Pesa API integration via Safaricom Daraja.

### 5.2 Offline-First Protocol
*   **Vietnam/France:** Full sync nightly; high-connectivity assumption.
*   **Saudi:** Sync on school Wi-Fi; content-filtered caching.
*   **Kenya:** Native app <30MB; **80% functionality offline**; 14-day text/rubric competency cache; auto-sync on Wi-Fi/3G; rich-media sync abandoned for mass-market tier.

### 5.3 Security & Explainability Architecture
*   The **Explainability API** and **Teacher Override** are global defaults, not local wrappers. Every recommendation must be reducible to a human-readable sentence.
*   **Zero-Knowledge Vault:** PII is encrypted at the device edge with keys held by the local data controller (or parent, in D2C contexts). This satisfies Decree 13 and ODPC requirements while providing a defensible position against lawful access demands—authorities can inspect servers, but cannot decrypt learner profiles without the localized key shard.
