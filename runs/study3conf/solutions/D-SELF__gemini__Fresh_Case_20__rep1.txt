# Governance Document: Nexus Protocol Monetization Framework

**Version:** 1.0.2026  
**Status:** Operational Governance  
**Subject:** Global Monetization & Regulatory Compliance Strategy

---

## 1. Global Core Mechanics
The game operates on a "Unified Prestige" model, decoupling the method of asset acquisition from the item's inherent value. The **Global Core Engine** maintains a single item database where visual fidelity and rarity tiers are identical globally, ensuring the social experience remains uniform across all regions.

*   **Total Cost of Acquisition (TCA) Mandate:** To maintain economic balance, the Global Governance Council enforces a maximum 10% variance in the real-world cost to acquire high-tier assets across all regions.
*   **Cryptographic Transparency:** All loot-rate disclosures are backed by server-side, third-party audited hashes. This provides verifiable data to satisfy regional mandates (China/Korea) while protecting against US-based litigation regarding data transparency.
*   **Synchronized Seasonal Beats:** Marketing and content events occur on a global, unified timeline to focus community discourse on gameplay, narrative, and social status, rather than regional monetization disparities.

## 2. Regional Compliance Adapters (RMAs)
The Nexus Protocol employs server-side middleware to intercept store requests and inject the appropriate **Commerce Manifest** based on the player’s verified residency.

| Region | Regulatory/Cultural Logic | Implementation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EU (BE/NL)** | Anti-Gambling / Asset Value | **Locked-to-Account:** Items acquired via RNG are non-transferable to prevent classification as "prizes with economic value." Uses "Transparency Bundles" (Predictable paths) instead of loot boxes. |
| **Japan** | *Kompu Gacha* Prevention | **Selection Vouchers:** Replaces collection-based bonuses with a pity-driven voucher system, ensuring players choose their rewards rather than being forced into prohibited "complete-the-set" loops. |
| **China/Korea** | Mandatory Disclosure | **Audit-Ready Dashboards:** Real-time visibility into probability drops with mandatory spending cooling-off periods for protected player segments. |
| **Gulf (GCC)** | High ARPU / Collectivism | **Social Prestige Focus:** Monetization centered on social signaling (custom UI frames, titles, guild-wide buffs) rather than individual mechanical power. |
| **US/Global** | Engagement-First | **Hybrid Model:** Battle Pass base, supplemented by personalized, churn-mitigating offers supported by cryptographic drop-rate verification. |

## 3. Cultural Monetization Guidelines
Governance of the game economy is dictated by regional sensitivity to prevent "Pay-to-Win" (P2W) stigma and regulatory friction.

*   **Financial Symmetry (The "Regional Wallet" Policy):** To satisfy international tax and VAT compliance, currency is strictly siloed in regional wallets. Migration between regions utilizes a one-time "Conversion Token" that automatically reconciles VAT/Sales Tax deltas, ensuring financial records remain audit-ready.
*   **Governance of Social Status:** In high-ARPU regions (Gulf), monetizing through "Social Prestige" is preferred over mechanical advantages to preserve the integrity of the game's competitive, skill-based loop.
*   **Ethics in Monetization Council:** This internal board will review all RMA updates on a bi-weekly basis, utilizing "TCA Stress Tests" to ensure that as the game evolves, the cost-to-acquire balance does not drift beyond the 10% tolerance threshold, preventing market arbitrage or player-perceived inequity.
*   **Annual Transparency & Equity Report:** The publisher will release an annual public report demonstrating that all regional monetization methods comply with local law and adhere to the "Economic Equivalence" mandate, serving as a proactive trust-building measure for player advocacy groups.
