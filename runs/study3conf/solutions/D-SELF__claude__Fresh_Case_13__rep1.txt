# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: INTEGRATED STRATEGY FOR LEADERSHIP

## Strategic Positioning

This plan synthesizes **regional trust dynamics, actuarial constraints, and intermediary capacity** into a **regionally differentiated, phased pilot strategy** for 2026–2027. The approach acknowledges that **one-size-fits-all distribution models fail in heterogeneous trust environments**; instead, it deploys:

- **East Africa:** Cooperative-led hybrid (70% cooperative, 25% mobile agents, 5% digital) — leveraging high cooperative legitimacy (0.72 credibility).
- **South Asia:** SHG + Government Extension + MFI tripartite hybrid — with **formal contractual commitments** to address extension worker capacity constraints.
- **Central America:** Mobile + fintech hybrid — exploiting digital infrastructure (45–60% smartphone penetration) and mobile money normalization.

## Critical Success Factors

| **Factor** | **East Africa** | **South Asia** | **Central America** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trust Alignment** | ✓ Cooperatives highly credible (0.72); low intermediary friction | ⚠ Extension worker non-cooperation structural risk; requires MoU | ✓ Mobile/fintech acceptable (0.62–0.68); local branding essential |
| **Claim Speed Delivery** | ✓ 8–12 day SLA achievable (within 14–21 day expectations) | ⚠ 12–16 days promised; 18–28 days likely if extension unavailable | ✓ 5–8 days exceeds expectations (7–14 day acceptable range) |
| **Basis Risk Management** | ⚠ 35–45% mismatch inherent; NDVI mitigation partially addresses | ⚠ 40–50% mismatch; lower farmer product literacy increases frustration | ✓ Basis risk acceptable given digitally-literate cohort |
| **Subsidy Sustainability** | ✓ 47% subsidy taperable to 30% by Year 3 | ✗ 65% subsidy dependency; post-pilot affordability crisis likely | ✓ 30% subsidy; rapid path to affordability independence |
| **Portfolio Viability** | ✓ Reinsurance + capital ($150K minimum) adequate for $500K portfolio | ⚠ Smaller portfolio ($350K) requires proportionate capital; higher leverage risk | ✓ Profitable by Year 2 with digital automation benefits |

### **Interpretation:**

**High CLV/CAC Ratio (✓ Desirable):**
- **East Africa cooperatives** (0.31): Higher CAC offset by 70% renewal = best long-term economics.
- **Central America cooperative** (0.48): Strongest lifetime value; but geographic/scale limitations.

**Low CLV/CAC Ratio (⚠ Caution):**
- **South Asia (all channels)** (0.08–0.11): Subsidy dependency + low farmer affordability = poor lifetime economics.
  - **Implication:** South Asia model only viable if **government/donor commits to sustained subsidy (Years 1–5)** or **product redesigned to lower cost** ($4.50 vs. $6.00).
- **South Asia MFI+Fintech** (0.04): AVOID as primary channel.

**Recommendation:**
- **Prioritize East Africa** (highest CLV, sustainable without perpetual subsidy).
- **De-prioritize South Asia** (requires sustained donor funding OR major product redesign).
- **Scale Central America** (strong CLV + digital cost advantages).

### **PILLAR 1A: EAST AFRICA PILOT**
### **Architecture:** Cooperative-led hybrid | **Scale:** 30,000 farmers | **Budget:** $120,000

#### **Phase 1A-1: Kenya Launch (Q3 2026 – Q1 2027, 4 months)**

**Enrollment Targets:**
- **15,000 farmers** across 5 cooperatives (Kenya Farmers Association, Muricho Dairy, Meru Co-op, Karatina Coffee, Nyeri Dairy).
- **Distribution:** 10,500 cooperative (70%), 3,750 MNO agents (25%), 750 direct digital (5%).
- **Premium:** $5.00/season; farmer contribution $2.60 (52%); subsidy $2.40 (48%).
- **Total premiums:** $75,000.

**Enrollment Campaign (Q3 2026, 6 weeks):**

```
WEEK 1–2: Cooperative Enrollment Kickoff
  └─ Cooperative AGMs present insurance product to members
  └─ Distribute translated brochures (Swahili + local languages)
  └─ Enroll 2,000 farmers/cooperative (10K total)
  └─ Target: First 5,000 farmers by mid-September

WEEK 3–4: Mobile Agent Enrollment Acceleration
  └─ 50 Safaricom agents conduct village enrollment meetings
  └─ USSD enrollment (no smartphone required): 60–80 farmers/agent
  └─ Target: 3,000–4,000 farmers by end September

WEEK 5–6: Direct Digital & Catch-up
  └─ WhatsApp/app-based enrollment for digitally-literate farmers
  └─ Cooperative + Agent follow-up on incomplete KYC
  └─ Target: 15,000 total enrollment by 30 Sept; >95% KYC complete

PREMIUM COLLECTION (October 2026):
  └─ Cooperatives: Bulk collection at monthly AGM; 90%+ remittance to insurer by 10/31
  └─ MNO agents: Weekly mobile money collection; insurer settlement weekly
  └─ Direct digital: Auto-billing via mobile wallet; real-time settlement
  └─ TARGET: 90%+ payment by 10/31; Coverage start 11/1
```

**Coverage Period & Claims (Q4 2026 – Q1 2027):**
- **Short rains season (October–December 2026):** Coverage active Q4 2026 – Q1 2027.
- **Peak claims period:** Jan–Feb 2027 (end of season; loss assessment begins).
- **Expected claims volume:** 15,000 farmers × 1.0 claims/farmer/season = 15,000 claims.
- **Claim settlement targets:**
  - **Parametric small claims** (70%, ~10,500 claims): 5–7 day settlement.
  - **Large community-verified claims** (20%, ~3,000 claims): 10–14 day settlement.
  - **Disputed/investigation** (5%, ~750 claims): 18–25 day settlement.
  - **Blended average:** 8–10 days (**target: meet or beat farmer expectations of 14–21 days**).

**Farmer Satisfaction & Trust Tracking (Post-Claims, Feb–March 2027):**

|---|---|---|---|
| **Claim approval rate** | ≥90% | Audit 20% of approved claims; count denials | March 2027 |
| **Settlement time (median)** | ≤10 days | Claims database timestamp review | March 2027 |

#### **Phase 1A-2: Uganda Expansion (Q4 2026 – Q2 2027, 6 months)**

**Rationale:**
- Validate cooperative model in weaker governance context (35% data quality vs. Kenya 50%).
- Adjust for infrastructure gaps (payment delays, satellite data latency).
- Premium loading +8% for data uncertainty.

**Enrollment Targets:**
- **12,000 farmers** across 3–5 cooperatives (Kabarole Tea, Ankole Dairy Alliance, Fort Portal Coffee).
- **Distribution:** 65% cooperative (7,800), 30% MNO agents (3,600), 5% direct digital (600).
- **Premium:** $5.30/season (+6% loading for Uganda data risk); farmer contribution $2.80 (53%).
- **Total premiums:** $63,600.
- **Coverage period:** Q4 2026 – Q1 2027 (concurrent with Kenya, different harvest timeline).

**Key Operational Differences (vs. Kenya):**
- **Intermediary training:** 4 days (vs. Kenya 3 days) due to weaker cooperative baseline.
- **Claims settlement SLA:** 10–12 days (vs. Kenya 8–10 days) due to infrastructure gaps.
- **
