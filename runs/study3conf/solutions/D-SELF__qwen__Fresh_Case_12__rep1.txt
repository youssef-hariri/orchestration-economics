# PROJECT GLOCAL: STRATEGIC BUSINESS MODEL & PRICING ARCHITECTURE FOR EMERGING MARKETS

**CONFIDENTIAL BOARD & INVESTOR MEMORANDUM**
**Target Markets:** Africa, South Asia, Latin America (2026–2027)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
As the global streaming landscape matures, maintaining uniform USD pricing in emerging markets creates an artificial ceiling on subscriber growth and inadvertently drives users toward piracy. Conversely, simply dropping prices to match local purchasing power destroys consolidated unit economics. 

**Project Glocal** resolves this dichotomy. It transitions our paradigm from a rigid, B2C, credit-card-driven model to a **B2B2C, localized-rail, and structurally hedged ecosystem**. By aligning pricing with Purchasing Power Parity (PPP), integrating indigenous payment infrastructure, and monetizing communal viewing norms, we will capture massive subscriber volume. 

Crucially, this strategy defends consolidated USD-reported margins not through unrealistic financial assumptions, but through **Tiered Content Architecture, restructured B2B2C Telco agreements, and internal wallet mechanics**. This ensures our unit economics remain mathematically robust, scalable, and highly defensible to global investors.

---

## STRATEGIC PILLAR 1: PPP-ALIGNED PRICING & THE "SACHET" ECONOMY
To capture the bottom-of-the-pyramid and emerging middle-class demographics without destroying Average Revenue Per User (ARPU), we are transitioning away from rigid monthly subscriptions in favor of flexible, micro-transactable tiers that mirror local cash-flow realities—while structurally eliminating micro-transaction fee bleed.

### 1. The Tiered Pricing Architecture
*   **Tier 0: Freemium / Ad-Supported (Mobile-Only)**
    *   *Price:* Free.
    *   *Format:* Ad-heavy, mobile-only, 720p.
    *   *Target:* The mass market. Acts as the ultimate top-of-funnel acquisition tool.
*   **Tier 1: "Sachet" / Micro-Pass (Mobile-Only, Ad-Lite)**
    *   *Price:* $0.10 - $0.50 USD equivalent.
    *   *Format:* Daily, weekly, or "data-bundle" passes.
    *   *Target:* Daily wage earners, students.
    *   *Economics Fix:* To prevent payment gateway fees (which can consume 30%+ of a $0.10 transaction) from destroying margins, we will implement a **Pre-funded Streaming Wallet**. Users load a minimum of $1.00 via mobile money (incurring one flat transaction fee). Daily passes are then deducted from this internal wallet balance, bypassing per-transaction micro-fees entirely.
*   **Tier 2: Standard (Multi-Device, Ad-Free)**
    *   *Price:* $2.00 - $4.00 USD equivalent (calibrated to local PPP).
    *   *Format:* Monthly recurring, 1080p, 2 devices.
    *   *Target:* The emerging middle class.
*   **Tier 3: Premium (4K, Offline, No Ads)**
    *   *Price:* $5.00 - $7.00 USD equivalent.
    *   *Format:* Monthly/Annual.
    *   *Target:* Upper-middle class, expats, affluent urbanites.

### 2. Regional Pricing Nuances
*   **Africa:** Heavy reliance on "sachet" daily passes via the Pre-funded Wallet. Introduction of "Weekend Passes" for users whose disposable income peaks at the end of the week.
*   **South Asia:** Introduction of "Ad-Watch" unlocking. Users can pay micro-amounts from their wallet or watch 30 seconds of localized ads to unlock the next episode (a hybrid SVOD/AVOD model maximizing monetization per user).
*   **Latin America:** Introduction of "Annual Pre-pay" at a 15% discount to lock in users and mitigate monthly churn, utilizing local installment plans (*cuotas sin interés*) to break the annual lump sum into manageable monthly payments.

## STRATEGIC PILLAR 2: FRICTIONLESS PAYMENT RAILS & BILLING MECHANICS
With credit card penetration below 15% in target regions, forcing card-on-file causes massive friction. We must integrate local rails natively and solve the recurring billing trust deficit.

### 1. Regional Rail Integration
*   **Africa (Mobile Money & Telco):** Deep API integration with M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, and Airtel Money. Implementation of **Direct Carrier Billing (DCB)** to charge subscriptions directly to prepaid airtime balances.
*   **South Asia (UPI & QR):** Full integration with India’s UPI (utilizing e-mandates for auto-debit), Bangladesh’s bKash, and Pakistan’s Raast. Deployment of "Scan-to-Pay" QR codes at local retail kiosks for cash-to-digital top-ups.
*   **Latin America (PIX & Cash Vouchers):** Native integration with Brazil’s PIX for instant, zero-fee recurring debits. Integration of OXXO and Baloto cash voucher systems for the unbanked in Mexico and Colombia.

### 2. The "Cash-to-Recurring" Conversion Funnel
Cash vouchers have high acquisition rates but high friction for recurring billing. 
*   **The Mechanism:** Cash payments are strictly utilized for *initial acquisition and wallet top-ups*. Upon paying cash at a kiosk, the user receives an SMS with a link to set up a "Wallet Auto-Top-Up" or UPI mandate.
*   **Grace Periods & Nudges:** Implementation of a 48-hour "grace period" with WhatsApp/SMS reminders before suspending service, accommodating the timing of daily/weekly cash flows and significantly reducing involuntary churn.

## STRATEGIC PILLAR 3: MONETIZING COMMUNAL VIEWING & HOUSEHOLD NORMS
In the Global South, the extended family and communal living structures are the norm. Password sharing is a cultural reality; cracking down on it will cause catastrophic churn. Instead, we will formalize and monetize it.

### 1. The "Community" & "Household" Tiers
*   **The "Extended Household" Tier:** Expanding profile limits from 4 to 6 for a 20% premium. This officially monetizes the multi-generational household while increasing perceived value.
*   **"Guest Pass" Micro-transactions:** Allowing Premium users to generate 3-day "Guest Passes" via WhatsApp for extended family or friends. A micro-fee ($0.50) is charged per pass, creating a high-margin, incremental revenue stream.

### 2. B2B2C "Group Buy" via Community Agents
*   **Agent Network Deployment:** Leveraging a localized agent network (similar to micro-finance or insurance models). Agents sell bulk physical "streaming scratch cards" at a 10% discount to their village or neighborhood network, earning a small commission. This formalizes the sharing economy, guarantees upfront cash, and drastically reduces Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).

## STRATEGIC PILLAR 4: DEFENDING USD UNIT ECONOMICS & MITIGATING MACRO RISKS
The critical challenge for Headquarters is reporting healthy margins in USD when local ARPU is low, Telcos demand significant distribution cuts, and emerging market currencies are highly volatile. We shift the investor focus from **Gross ARPU** to **Cohort Contribution Margin**, utilizing structural hedging and aggressive cost localization.

### 1. The "Structural Hedge": Tiered Content Architecture
A global streamer's core infrastructure and premium global content are strictly USD-denominated. When local currencies devalue, local revenues plummet in USD terms, but USD costs remain fixed. We solve this structural mismatch via **Tiered Content Architecture**:
*   **Tiers 0 & 1 (Low ARPU):** Served almost exclusively by **Local-Currency-Funded Content** (local originals, regional libraries, ad-supported catalog). This perfectly matches local currency revenues with local currency content costs, insulating the bottom line from FX shocks.
*   **Tiers 2 & 3 (High ARPU):** Expensive, USD-denominated Hollywood blockbusters and global premium content are gated behind the Standard and Premium tiers. The higher ARPU provides the necessary margin buffer to absorb global content costs and FX volatility.

### 2. Restructuring Telco Deals for Margin Protection
Pure revenue-share models (where Telcos take 30-50%) destroy margins on low-ARPU subscriptions. We will restructure B2B2C partnerships:
*   **Fixed USD-Pegged Wholesale Rates:** For the Telco's postpaid and high-tier prepaid subscribers, we will negotiate fixed USD-pegged wholesale rates. This shifts the FX risk to the Telco (who are better hedged and often carry USD-denominated debt) and guarantees stable USD revenue for HQ.
*   **Local-Currency True-Up Fallback:** In highly volatile markets where Telcos reject USD-pegging, we will deploy a local-currency wholesale rate with a built-in devaluation buffer and quarterly true-up mechanisms, ensuring margin protection without stalling negotiations.
*   **Fee Absorption Mandates:** For pure revenue-share models on lower-tier prepaid subs, we will contractually mandate that the **Telco absorbs the payment gateway and mobile money transaction fees** out of their distribution cut, protecting our gross margin floor.

### 3. Dynamic FX Pricing with Consumer Smoothing
*   **Algorithmic FX Adjustments:** We will implement automated pricing algorithms. If a local currency devalues beyond a set threshold against the USD, the system triggers a localized price adjustment.
*   **Consumer Smoothing Function:** To mitigate PR backlash and churn risks associated with sudden price hikes in price-sensitive markets, the algorithm will employ a smoothing function: absorbing minor fluctuations, capping annual increases, and prioritizing the migration of users to annual locked-in rates rather than executing immediate monthly price hikes.

### 4. Infrastructure Localization & Regulatory-Compliant Ad Monetization
*   **Infrastructure Localization:** Bandwidth is a massive USD cost. We will establish deep peering agreements and local caching servers (Open Connect model) with regional ISPs, reducing global CDN egress costs by up to 40%.
*   **Regulatory-Compliant "Sponsored Data":** Acknowledging net-neutrality headwinds in regions like India and LatAm, we will pivot from traditional "zero-rating" to "sponsored data" models. Telcos or brand partners pay us a data-subsidy fee (often in hard currency) to sponsor user data consumption, creating a high-margin revenue stream independent of subscription ARPU while maintaining regulatory compliance.
*   **Volume & Integration over CPM:** Recognizing that local FMCG CPMs are structurally low ($1-$3), we will offset this through **Hyper-Local Brand Integrations** (e.g., FMCG brands sponsoring local original shows) and high-volume dynamic ad insertion.

## KEY FINANCIAL METRICS & INVESTOR NARRATIVE
We are fundamentally reframing the narrative for HQ investors, shifting the focus from top-line ARPU to bottom-line efficiency and lifetime value.

*   **Target Blended LTV/CAC:** **> 4.0x** 
    *(Driven by near-zero CAC via Telco bundles/agent networks and structurally lower churn via cultural integration).*
*   **Target Cohort Contribution Margin:** **20% - 25%**
    *(Protected by the Pre-funded Wallet, Tiered Content Architecture, and Telco fee-absorption mandates).*
*   **Strategic Advantage:** The Lifetime Value (LTV) of a $3/month user retained for 4 years vastly outperforms the LTV of a $15/month Western user who churns after 8 months. The absolute Free Cash Flow generated by this massive, sticky user base will be utilized to fund global content and drive consolidated profitability.

## IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP (2026–2027)

**Phase 1: Foundation & Wallet Mechanics (Q1–Q2 2026)**
*   Finalize API integrations for M-Pesa, UPI e-mandates, and PIX.
*   Launch the "Pre-funded Streaming Wallet" and Tier 0/1 Sachet passes in 3 pilot markets (Kenya, India, Brazil).
*   Initiate local CDN peering and caching negotiations with regional Tier-1 ISPs to reduce USD egress costs.

**Phase 2: B2B2C & Structural Rollout (Q3–Q4 2026)**
*   Launch "Extended Household" tiers and WhatsApp Guest Passes.
*   Sign restructured B2B2C agreements (Fixed USD wholesale for postpaid; fee-absorption/true-up for prepaid) with at least two major Telcos per region.
*   Roll out local cash-voucher agent networks (OXXO) in LatAm, implementing the "Cash-to-Recurring" conversion funnel.

**Phase 3: Content Architecture & Scale (2027)**
*   Fully operationalize the "Tiered Content Architecture," gating global USD-content behind Tiers 2/3, and scaling local-currency-funded originals for Tiers 0/1.
*   Activate Dynamic FX Pricing Triggers (with consumer smoothing) across all emerging markets.
*   Report first full-year "Blended LTV/CAC" and "Cohort Contribution Margin" metrics to HQ investors, demonstrating robust, mathematically defensible unit economics.

## CONCLUSION
By treating Africa, South Asia, and Latin America not as "discounted Western markets" but as distinct ecosystems requiring indigenous business models, we unlock hundreds of millions of new subscribers. Through restructured Telco agreements, the Pre-funded Streaming Wallet, and the structurally hedged Tiered Content Architecture, **Project Glocal** ensures that while the local price tags are small, the consolidated USD unit economics remain mathematically defensible, highly scalable, and deeply profitable.
