## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

By 31 December 2026, zero active tenant workloads may remain in our legacy single-region architecture. This document governs the transition to a **cell-based sovereign cloud topology** that satisfies emerging data-residency regimes without sacrificing customer trust or commercial viability.

We commit to a hard boundary: **all enterprise tenants will operate on sovereign cells with localized data-at-rest, regional control planes, and executed Data Residency Addenda by Q4 2026.** The legacy region will transition to encrypted, read-only archival on 1 January 2027 and complete cryptographic erasure by Q2 2027.

We accept structural reality markets where full final-state buildout exceeds the mandate. France will launch via a **SecNumCloud-ready bare-metal partnership** (not full accreditation), and China will operate through a **PIPL-compliant interim partner-hosted solution** while a JV-owned cell proceeds on a 36-month track. These interim solutions satisfy the 2026 boundary for active data processing while preserving the integrity of our sovereignty claims.

This strategy abandons operationally impossible promises—universal 72-hour feature parity, 90-day post-migration dual-run periods, and monolithic global control planes—and replaces them with **verified trust**: Regional Control Planes, a Tiered Feature Parity Framework, a Cryptographic Cutover Protocol, and geo-gated pricing that aligns cost to structural reality.

**Primary Objectives**
- **100%** active enterprise tenant migration by Q4 2026
- **<2%** gross churn throughout the transition
- **Maintain or improve** regional trust scores in every operating market

## 3. HARDENED ARCHITECTURE

### 3.1 Regional Control Plane, Global Orchestration Layer
A global control plane holding customer metadata or IAM events is a cross-border data transfer under GDPR and emerging sovereignty regimes. We adopt a **Regional Control, Global Orchestration** model:

- **Regional Control Plane (per cell):** Hosts regional IAM, KMS, billing aggregation, audit logs, and tenant metadata. No customer data leaves the cell boundary without explicit contractual opt-in.
- **Global Orchestration Layer:** A lightweight, non-tenant-facing configuration bus for software updates and vendor financial consolidation. It **never** stores customer PII, IAM tokens, or encryption keys.
- **Cross-Cell Governance Gateway (US Global Enterprises):** Client-side federated query layer. The customer’s authorized client queries each regional cell API separately and aggregates results locally, satisfying US e-discovery obligations without violating EU data boundaries.

### 3.2 Zero-Trust Boundaries & Inter-Cell Resilience
- **Intra-Cell Resilience:** Each sovereign cell is deployed across multiple in-jurisdiction availability zones with a 99.99% uptime SLA.
- **Inter-Cell Resilience:** Cross-cell failover is **disabled by default**. Sovereign islands do not implicitly replicate to foreign jurisdictions. Customers requiring DR across cells must execute a separate **Cross-Cell DR Addendum** with explicit legal review.
- **Inter-Cell Traffic:** Treated as external. AES-256-GCM encrypted, mTLS-vetted, and logged. No trust assumptions between regional IAM domains.

### 3.3 AI/ML Data Governance
- **Cell-Isolated Training:** Each cell operates dedicated model-training infrastructure. Shared vector databases and cross-cell fine-tuning pipelines are prohibited by architecture.
- **Binding DPA Schedule:** *“Provider warrants that Customer Data is not used to train or fine-tune machine learning models accessible to other tenants or cells without explicit written opt-in.”*
- **Audit Right:** Annual third-party provenance audit available to enterprise customers.

### 3.4 Cryptographic Cutover Protocol
We replace the legally toxic 90-day dual-run with a protocol that achieves actual residency at cutover:

- **Day 0:** Final incremental sync; traffic switch to sovereign cell; immediate cryptographic erasure of primary data in the legacy region. A tamper-evident Merkle-tree certificate is issued to the customer.
- **Day 0–30:** An encrypted, air-gapped backup is retained in the legacy region solely for disaster recovery. Keys are held by the customer or a neutral escrow; the provider maintains zero access.
- **Day 30:** Automated cryptographic shredding of the air-gapped backup.
- **Rollback:** Rollback is executed to the new cell’s own backup. Any rollback to the legacy region is classified as a *new migration* requiring full legal and residency re-validation.

## 4. EXECUTION ROADMAP (2024–2026)

We decompress engineering risk by focusing Phase 1 on a **Top 5 Cell Extraction** (Frankfurt, Singapore, São Paulo, Montreal, Sydney). Additional markets are served via API-gateway routing to these anchors until their own control planes are extracted in 2027.

### Phase 1: Foundation & Strip-Scoped Extraction (Q3 2024 – Q3 2025)
- **Engineering:** Refactor Top 5 Regional Control Planes from the monolith. Freeze cell-boundary API specification.
- **Legal/Compliance:** Complete Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA) Matrix for Top 20 jurisdictions. File US EAR/BIS encryption export classifications.
- **Entity Sprint:** Incorporate local entities in Germany, France, Brazil, KSA, UAE, Norway, Korea, Mexico, Australia, and Canada. Appoint statutory directors. Initiate China JV track (36-month horizon).
- **Infrastructure:** Stand up cloud-native anchor cells. Issue bare-metal RFPs for France and KSA.
- **Procurement:** Deploy Modular DPA Factory (Base Module + Jurisdiction Schedules).

### Phase 2: Certification, Pilot & Initial Mass Migration (Q3 2025 – Q2 2026)
- **Certifications:** Begin ISO 27701 / SOC 2 Type II evidence collection on live cloud-native cells. Target SaaS-layer audits: BSI Grundschutz (Germany), IRAP (Australia), ENS (Spain), PIPA (Korea). Regulated-sector pilot migrations occur *after* 90 days of evidence collection.
- **Complex Cells:** France bare-metal partner cell live (pilot). KSA dedicated cell live (pilot). China interim partner-hosted solution live.
- **Cultural Pilots:** Execute *Betriebsrat* reviews (Germany), *nemawashi* alignment (Japan), chaebol sponsor matching (Korea), Customer Sounding Board (Netherlands).
- **Migration Start:** Mass migration begins for cloud-native cells using the Tiered Migration Protocol.

### Phase 3: 2026 Compliance Boundary (Q3 2026 – Q4 2026)
- **Migration Surge:** Complete migration of all remaining tenants. Legacy region enters read-only archival mode by 31 December 2026.
- **China/France:** All new China bookings route to interim partner solution. All new French public-sector bookings route to SecNumCloud-ready partner cell.
- **Contractual Close:** 100% localized DPAs executed.
- **Go/No-Go Gate (15 November 2026):** Formal steering committee review against the 2026 Compliance Boundary checklist.

## 6. REGIONAL EXECUTION MANDATES

Culture is a structural variable, not a communications veneer. Every regional playbook specifies the **Ritual** (consensus or relationship act required), **Proof** (technical or legal evidence), and **Decision Maker** (actual local authority).

### 6.1 European Union

**France — *Souveraineté Réelle***
- **Structure:** Bare-metal partnership (Thales, Orange, or OVHcloud). Provider operates as the software layer only.
- **Certification:** ISO 27701 (SaaS). Positioned as **SecNumCloud-ready**, not certified.
- **Ritual:** Frame as *European Technological Independence* aligned with Gaia-X. French-language executive roundtables and government co-certification ceremonies.
- **Non-Negotiable:** French-resident Managing Director and DPO with statutory authority. French entity as contractual counterparty.

**Germany — Co-Determination as Gatekeeper**
- **Structure:** Cloud-native Frankfurt cell. Local GmbH counterparty.
- **Certification:** BSI Grundschutz (SaaS layer). C5 cited only for underlying infrastructure partner.
- **Ritual:** **Betriebsrat Engagement Package**: German-language privacy impact assessments and information sessions 90 days before any migration. Works Council concurrence is required before employee-data migration.
- **Non-Negotiable:** Works Council (*Betriebsrat*) concurrence.

**Norway**
- **Structure:** Separate Oslo sub-cell with Norwegian law wrapper.
- **Ritual:** Consensus-driven stakeholder review with explicit Norwegian-law guarantees.
- **Non-Negotiable:** Do not default into the Frankfurt cell.

**Netherlands**
- **Structure:** EU cell with Dutch consultative track.
- **Ritual:** **Customer Sounding Board** launched six months before migration. Publish a white paper and invite public comment. Process legitimacy equals trust.
- **Non-Negotiable:** No default assignment without consultative process.

### 6.2 Asia-Pacific

**Japan — *Nemawashi* and Dual-Primary Architecture**
- **Structure:** Cloud-native Tokyo cell.
- **Ritual:** **12-Month *Nemawashi* Program**: low-stakes executive visits to allow internal consensus-building (*ringi*) before any public migration request. Eliminate “lag” language entirely; rebrand regional release as **Dual-Primary Architecture**. Offer Japanese-language architectural review boards.
- **Non-Negotiable:** C-level consensus before migration scheduling. Never lead with a global CTO broadcast.

**China — The Operating License Reality**
- **Structure:** **Interim** partner-hosted solution on Alibaba Cloud or Tencent Cloud with localized encryption.
- **Ritual:** Position as the software layer, not a data custodian. No cryptographic promises against state access.
- **Non-Negotiable:** Local legal counterparty (licensed entity). JV track proceeds in parallel for 2028 final cell.

**South Korea — The Chaebol Network**
- **Structure:** Seoul cell.
- **Certification:** PIPA + ISO 27701.
- **Ritual:** **Chaebol Sponsor Matching**: Korean country manager with government or chaebol alumni status assigned to mirror C-level sponsor relationships. Technical specs are discussed only after executive alignment.
- **Non-Negotiable:** C-level sponsor alignment before technical procurement.

**ASEAN — Regulatory Theater and Relationship Reality**
- **Structure:** Singapore primary cell + satellite storage commitments for Indonesia/Vietnam.
- **Ritual:** Bilingual Thai/Indonesian-speaking support as **baseline**, not premium. Use local CIO references, not multinational logos.
- **Non-Negotiable:** Acknowledge regulatory ambiguity; lead with price and relationship, not checklists.

**India — *Atmanirbhar Bharat***
- **Structure:** Cloud-native Mumbai/Hyderabad cell.
- **Ritual:** Frame localization as commitment to self-reliance. Visible Bangalore R&D center with Indian nationals in engineering roles. Integrate with India Stack (Aadhaar eKYC, UPI, GST).
- **Non-Negotiable:** **No premium pricing.** Local engineering headcount must exceed sales headcount. MeitY engagement for public-sector empanelment.

**Australia — The Dual-Track Trust Market**
- **Structure:** Sydney cell.
- **Certification:** IRAP (SaaS assessment, not just region).
- **Ritual:** Segment explicitly: **IRAP Protected** for government/defense; **Australian Data Sovereign** for private sector.
- **Non-Negotiable:** No one-size-fits-all messaging across Australian segments.

## 8. ECONOMIC MODEL & ANTI-ARBITRAGE

Cross-subsidizing standard-tier migrations with a premium tier is economically incoherent when bare-metal France, Saudized KSA, and dedicated Australia IRAP cells drive 40–100% per-tenant COGS increases. We align price to structural cost through three geo-gated tiers.

| Tier | Markets | Structural Model | Pricing |
|------|---------|------------------|---------|
| **Sovereign Standard** | Frankfurt, Singapore, São Paulo, Montreal, Sydney, Oslo, London | Cloud-native local regions (AWS/Azure/GCP). Low marginal COGS (~+10%). | **Standard pricing.** No premium. |
| **Sovereign Assured** | France, Germany (optional dedicated), KSA, UAE, Australia (IRAP Protected) | Bare-metal partner, dedicated infra, or complex regulatory build. High COGS (+40–100%). | **+35–50% premium.** Geo-gated; customer legal HQ must match cell jurisdiction. |
| **Sovereign Essential** | India, ASEAN, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, South Africa, Nigeria | Cloud-native with deep local integrations (UPI, PIX, GST, CFDI). Market-entry investment. | Standard pricing for first 24 months / first 100 enterprise tenants; then cost-adjusted to market. |

### Anti-Arbitrage Guardrails
- **Jurisdiction Lock:** Cell assignment is determined by customer legal headquarters and tax residency. A US multinational cannot provision into the India cell to avoid the Assured premium.
- **Migration Repricing:** Any cross-cell tenant relocation requires provider executive approval and repricing at the destination market rate.
- **Transfer Pricing Documentation:** Inter-company billing between local entities and parent is documented to satisfy OECD and local tax authority scrutiny.
