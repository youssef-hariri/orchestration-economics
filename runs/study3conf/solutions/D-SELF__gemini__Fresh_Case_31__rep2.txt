# Global Compensation Policy: 2026 Framework
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Subject:** Total Remuneration and Global Pay Equity

---

### I. Our Philosophy: "Total Cost-to-Company (TCC) Equality"
As a globally distributed organization, we are committed to compensating our team members fairly, competitively, and transparently. We are moving away from traditional "geographic arbitrage" toward a **Value-Based Tiered Model**. 

We do not pay for where you live; we pay for the **global impact of your role**, moderated by the specific regulatory, tax, and competitive labor requirements of your employment jurisdiction. Our goal is to ensure that every employee is compensated at the top tier of their local market while maintaining a cohesive, equitable global standard.

---

### II. The Three-Tiered Structure
To maintain clarity and fairness, all 30 countries are categorized into three macro-economic tiers. These tiers are based on tech-labor demand and local competitive density, not generic cost-of-living indices.

*   **Tier 1 (Anchor Markets):** Primary innovation hubs with high-density global talent competition.
*   **Tier 2 (Growth Markets):** Established tech ecosystems with competitive regional salary anchors.
*   **Tier 3 (Emerging Markets):** Rapidly developing tech hubs where we prioritize long-term growth and equity-based rewards.

**What this means for you:** Your salary is anchored to the 60th–75th percentile of the top-tier employers within your specific market. This ensures we remain an "Employer of Choice" in every country where we operate.

---

### III. The Components of Your Pay
We recognize that "Base Salary" is only one part of your compensation. We provide a **Total Remuneration Statement** that clarifies how your pay is calculated based on three objective pillars:

1.  **Base Value (60%):** A standardized, global grade based on your specific role, level, and technical competency.
2.  **Local Competitive Premium (20%):** A market-adjusted variable that reflects the hiring difficulty and competitive landscape of your employment hub.
3.  **Statutory & Social Burden (20%):** The significant investment the company makes in your local mandatory benefits, employer-side taxes, and social security—costs that vary by country but are essential to your long-term security.

---

### IV. Transparency and Fairness
We support the global move toward pay transparency. Our approach ensures compliance with regional mandates while protecting the integrity of our salary bands:

*   **Objective Job Evaluation (OJE):** Every role is mapped to a global competency framework. An "L5 Engineer" in Warsaw and an "L5 Engineer" in Toronto are held to the same competency standards. 
*   **Salary Ranges:** Every job requisition will include the Tier-specific salary range. We provide these ranges to ensure you have full visibility into your potential growth within our company.
*   **The Remediation Commitment:** Before the implementation of this policy, we have conducted a rigorous pay-equity audit to ensure that pay differences are driven by objective data. We have proactively adjusted compensation for any unexplained gaps to ensure internal fairness.

---

### V. Mobility and Flexibility
*   **Workplace Location:** Compensation is tied to the legal entity where you are employed. Should you relocate, please notify HR immediately. We have a formalized process to recalibrate your compensation to your new jurisdiction, ensuring compliance with local tax and labor laws.
*   **Performance Multipliers:** High-performers who consistently exceed output targets may be eligible for a "Performance Multiplier," which allows for total compensation to reach into the next tier’s range. These adjustments are governed by objective, audited KPI data to ensure equity and prevent bias.

---

### VI. Frequently Asked Questions
*   **"Why is there a pay difference between countries?"** 
    We compare you to the best employers in your neighborhood, not just the best employers in Silicon Valley. Our framework ensures that you are rewarded for your impact within your local market, while accounting for the different tax/legal costs of doing business in your region.
*   **"Is my salary confidential?"** 
    While individual salaries remain private, our *methodology* and *ranges* are open. We encourage you to review your annual Total Remuneration Statement to see the full value of your benefits, equity, and company-paid contributions.

---

**Commitment to Excellence**
This policy creates a defensible, audit-ready, and transparent compensation ecosystem. It allows us to recruit and retain the best talent globally while ensuring that every member of our team is treated with the respect and fairness they deserve.
