## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

In 2026, a fully remote software company operating across 30 countries faces a trilemma: cost-of-labor variance threatens fiscal sustainability; pixel-level pay comparisons corrode trust in global teams; and a patchwork of pay-transparency laws exposes legacy band structures to regulatory and reputational risk.

**Project Convergence** resolves this through the GLIDE Protocol. We replace 30-country micro-bands with a single Global Role Anchor (GRA), four published zones with guaranteed identical multipliers within each zone, and a flat global equity grant per level that narrows total-compensation disparity. Geographic spread compresses annually on a published geometric schedule, backed by a dedicated budget and an EBITDA circuit breaker. The result is analytically defensible, legally compliant, and perceptually robust.

| Design Commitment | What It Means for the Business |
|---|---|
| **Hard Zone Rule** | Every employee in the same zone receives the exact same multiplier. No city-level micro-adjustments means no “Warsaw vs. Lisbon” weaponization on public forums. |
| **Equity Equalizer** | Base pay varies by zone; equity does not. Total Compensation (TC) spread between highest and lowest zones is held under 18%. |
| **Geometric Convergence** | Non-Alpha zones close 15% of their remaining gap to Alpha annually, with a pre-announced schedule through 2031. |
| **Anti-Pixel Guarantee** | We publish zone multipliers and convergence logic. We do not publish city-level economic scores, preventing reverse-engineering of individual pay. |
| **Dual-Track Disclosure** | “Good faith” job-posting laws are satisfied through a Practical Hiring Range (PHR) validated by actual offer data, not theoretical band extremes. |
| **Procedural Justice** | An elected Fairness Tribunal gives employees binding voice on zone and band disputes, converting fairness from an outcome into a process. |

**How This Resolves the Three Tensions**

| Tension | Resolution |
|---|---|
| **Cost-of-labor variance** | Zone multipliers acknowledge macro reality but cap base spread at 30% and TC spread at 18%. A budgeted geometric convergence schedule treats geographic arbitrage as a depreciating asset, not a permanent strategy. |
| **Fairness perception** | Coarse zones, flat equity, and a published convergence roadmap replace resentment with a narrative of progress. The Total Reward Parity Statement (TRPS) reframes comparison from base salary to total employer investment. |
| **Pay-transparency law** | The 6-Factor Defense Standard, Dual-Track Disclosure Engine, and per-regime codetermination protocols convert disclosure from a liability into a structural safe harbor. |

**Recommended Board Motion:** Approve the GLIDE architecture; authorize the geometric convergence budget; ratify the Compensation Congress charter with Fairness Tribunal and Works Council Coordination subcommittees; and mandate Q2 2026 activation under the phased roadmap below.

## 1. PHILOSOPHY: CONTRIBUTION SOVEREIGNTY, STRATEGIC TRANSPARENCY, CONVERGENT EQUITY

We reject the false choice between fiscal sustainability and fairness. Our philosophy treats three tensions as engineering constraints with measurable tolerances, not competing values to be compromised away.

| Tension | Failure Tolerance | Concrete Control |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost-of-labor variance** | Base-pay spread ≤30% between Alpha and Delta; TC spread ≤18%. | Hard Zone Rule + ZDP formula + geometric convergence. |
| **Fairness perception** | Zero pixel-level variance within a zone; employee confidence in pay process must remain high. | Anti-Pixel Guarantee + Fairness Tribunal + certified manager narratives. |
| **Pay-transparency & equal-pay risk** | Zero regulatory penalty; zero successful equal-pay challenge; zero unilateral pay-cut enforceability risk in codetermination jurisdictions. | 6-Factor Defense + Dual-Track Disclosure + per-regime consent protocols. |

This rests on three mutually reinforcing pillars:

| Pillar | Principle | Operational Implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Contribution Sovereignty** | The value of code, architecture, and product judgment is geographically agnostic. | Every role has a single **Global Role Anchor (GRA)**—a USD-denominated midpoint derived from the 75th percentile of blended Toronto, London, and San Francisco market data. |
| **Strategic Transparency** | We publish the rules and the zones, not the raw economic micro-data that invites pixel-level resentment. | We communicate four **Zone Multipliers** only. The underlying Economic Review Triad is used internally by the Compensation Congress to set zone boundaries; individual city scores are never disclosed. |
| **Convergent Equity** | Geographic arbitrage is a depreciating asset, not a permanent strategy. | We commit to **geometric convergence**: each year, non-Alpha zones close 15% of their remaining gap to Alpha. Employees see a published roadmap through 2031. |

**The Narrative:** We pay *above* local market rates in every zone because we compete for global talent, not local bargains. The residual gap between a Toronto engineer and a Warsaw engineer reflects a temporary economic context that we are actively, measurably closing. Fairness is not sameness today; it is equal upside, transparent reasoning, audited process, and a published path toward parity.

## 2. FRAMEWORK: THE GLIDE ARCHITECTURE

### 2.1 Global Role Anchor (GRA)
All roles (IC3–IC8, M1–M6) map to a single global job architecture.

- **GRA Midpoint:** Set at the 75th percentile of blended Alpha-anchor markets.
- **Band Width:** ±20% around the midpoint, calibrated by a **published proficiency rubric** (never negotiation leverage or prior salary history).
- **Currency Peg:** 12-month trailing USD average. Treasury maintains a **natural hedge reserve** to absorb up to six months of adverse FX swing without band disruption.
- **Hard Floor:** No offer may fall below the higher of (a) local statutory minimum, (b) any applicable collective bargaining agreement (CBA) rate, or (c) the **80th percentile of the local professional market** for that function.

### 2.2 The Hard Zone Rule & Anti-Pixel Guarantee
To eliminate the “Warsaw vs. Lisbon vs. Prague” fracture, we establish an inviolable rule: **every employee in the same zone receives the exact same multiplier.** There are no country-level, city-level, or individual micro-adjustments within a zone.

**The Narrative:** *“We do not pay you the Warsaw rate or the Lisbon rate. You are both in Zone Gamma, and the zone multiplier is 0.82. If you want to know why Warsaw and Lisbon are in the same zone, the answer is: because our Economic Review Triad places them there. The specific micro-scores that drew the line are congressional deliberation material, not individual compensation inputs.”*

### 2.3 Zone Derivation Protocol (ZDP) — Internal Congressional Use Only
The Compensation Congress sets zone boundaries annually. **This formula is never applied to individuals.** It is used solely to assign countries to zones and to audit the economic rationality of boundaries.

**ZDP Formula (2026 Refresh):**
```
Zone Boundary Score = (0.15 × PPP_Index) + (0.35 × MECI_Index) + (0.40 × LMPI_Floor) + (0.10 × RCPI_Index)
```

| Index | Weight | Source | Purpose / Legal Standing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PPP_Index** | 15% | OECD / World Bank | **Contextual only.** Normalizes consumption value of take-home pay. Capped at 15% because national consumption baskets poorly reflect remote-tech-worker spending patterns. **Not relied upon as a standalone equal-pay defense.** |
| **MECI_Index** (Mandatory Employer Cost) | 35% | National social-security, pension, health, payroll-tax tables | **Primary legal defense.** Statutorily mandated employer cost differentials are universally recognized as objective, gender-neutral factors under EU and North American equal-pay jurisprudence. |
| **LMPI_Floor** (Labor Market Premium) | 40% | Blended Radford, Option Impact, Levels.fyi | **Overriding validator.** Ensures no zone assignment exploits market distress. |
| **RCPI_Index** (Remote Cost Premium) | 10% | Internal cost survey (co-working, connectivity, mobility) | Corrects for imported costs faced by remote workers in cheaper locales (e.g., international schooling, dollar-denominated equipment, hub travel). |

**Published Communication Zones:**

| Zone | Definition | Illustrative Markets (2026) | **Published Multiplier** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alpha** | Premium talent markets; highest cost-of-labor and regulatory overhead | Toronto, San Francisco, New York, London, Zurich, Tel Aviv | **1.00** |
| **Beta** | Established high-skill ecosystems with mature tech sectors | Berlin, Amsterdam, Dublin, Singapore, Tokyo, Sydney | **0.90** |
| **Gamma** | High-growth remote hubs with appreciating talent pools | Warsaw, Lisbon, Barcelona, Prague, Buenos Aires, Mexico City, Tallinn | **0.82** |
| **Delta** | Emerging and expansion markets | Remaining jurisdictions | **0.76** |

*Mathematical consistency check:* On a representative GRA midpoint of $200,000 with flat equity of $150,000, Alpha TC = $350,000 and Delta TC = $302,000, yielding a **TC spread of 13.7%**—well within the 18% hard constraint.

**Zone Stability Lock:** Once assigned, a country’s zone is locked for **24 months minimum**. Reclassification requires a sustained >10% ZDP shift over two consecutive years and a **two-thirds vote** of the Compensation Congress.

### 2.4 The Equity Equalizer & Tax-Transparency Framework
Base pay differs by zone; **nominal equity grants do not.**

- **Equity (RSUs/Options):** Issued at a **flat global USD value** per level. An IC5 in Warsaw receives the same gross equity grant as an IC5 in Toronto.
- **Tax-Context Disclosure:** Because flat gross equity does not equal flat *net* equity, the TRPS displays both **Gross Equity Value** and an **Estimated Net Equity Value** based on the employee’s tax residence and vesting schedule. The company does not gross-up equity tax across 30 jurisdictions, but commits to transparent disclosure of estimated net value.

### 2.5 Local Range Formula
```
Local Min = (GRA Midpoint × 0.80) × Zone Multiplier
Local Max = (GRA Midpoint × 1.20) × Zone Multiplier
```
Placement within the band is based on **proficiency rubric, impact, and skill scarcity**, never on negotiation aggression.

### 2.6 The Convergence Covenant with Circuit Breaker
We compress geographic spread through **geometric convergence**: each year, non-Alpha zones close **15% of their remaining gap to 1.00**. Example: Gamma at 0.82 moves to 0.847; Beta at 0.90 moves to 0.915.
