**Product-Adaptation Strategy: Voice Assistant for Honorific-Language Markets (2026)**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Western voice-AI assumes a direct, individualistic, cloud-first user. The 2026 target markets—Japan, Korea, Thailand, and the Gulf—require a **sociolinguistic operating system**: the device must function as a culturally literate household member that recognizes vertical hierarchy, acoustic privacy, and gendered spatial boundaries. This strategy replaces generic “polite modes” with **dynamic register engines**, nuclear-family user accounts with **multigenerational household constitutions**, and implicit cloud trust with **hardware-signaled, sovereign privacy architectures**.

---

## 2. Sociolinguistic Interaction Architecture

### 2.1 Japan: The Clerk in the Wall
The device occupies the position of a **humble institutional servant**—*soto* (outside) to the family yet *uchi* (inside) to the house. Its language must reflect liminal, deferential status.

| Element | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| **Baseline Register** | **Teineigo** (です/ます) for neutral statements. |
| **Self-Reference** | **Kenjōgo** (humble): verbs in *~ itashimasu, ~ orimasu, ~ ukagaimasu*. Institutional *kochira* (“this side”). Never *boku* or *ore*. |
| **User-Reference** | Kinship titles: **O-kā-sama, O-tō-sama, O-bā-sama, O-jī-sama**, or surname + **-sama**. Avoid *anata*. |
| **User Actions / Status** | **Sonkeigo** (respectful): *~ ni natte irasshaimasu*. Objects elevated: *o-tenki, o-shirase*. |
| **Input Parsing** | Parse rough masculine speech (*ore, da-yo*, dropped particles), elderly slurred speech, and Kansai/Kanto prosodic variance without command failure. Response register never descends to match crude input. |

**Acoustic & Spatial Behavior**
- **Murmur Mode:** Detects whispered wake-words after 22:00 and responds via smartphone, haptic, or non-verbal LED “breath” patterns to avoid *meiwaku* (neighbor nuisance).
- **Genkan Protocol:** Default to visual-only responses near entryways, preventing household information from leaking to shared corridors.
- **Disaster Broadcasts:** Partner with FDMA for J-Alert integration. Deliver evacuation guidance in clear, elevated register (*O-hinan nasatte kudasai*), never using foreign honorific systems.
