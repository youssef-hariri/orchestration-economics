# Executive Document: Project Horizon
## Strategic Integration and Market Consolidation Strategy

**Date:** October 26, 2023  
**Subject:** Integration Roadmap for Pan-African Banking Acquisition (Nigeria, Kenya, Morocco)  
**Status:** Board-Approved Strategy Framework

---

### 1. Executive Summary
Project Horizon aims to create a unified Pan-African banking entity that scales digital infrastructure while maintaining local regulatory sovereignty. By adopting a **Federated Operating Model**, the Group will achieve operational efficiency through a common technology middleware layer while preserving the market-specific "Trust Anchors" required to maintain local competitive advantage.

### 2. The Federated Operating Model
We will operate as a global powerhouse with local execution. To address the **"Trust Paradox"** (the need for stability in Morocco vs. the need for agile innovation in Nigeria/Kenya), the brand narrative will be segmented:
*   **Nigeria/Kenya:** Positioned as "Global Digital Partners" with local agility.
*   **Morocco:** Positioned as the "Stable, Global Institution" with deep historical roots.

### 3. Technology Migration: The "Sovereign-Cloud & API" Strategy
To mitigate systemic risk, we reject "Big Bang" migration in favor of a three-phase evolution:

*   **Phase I: Orchestration Layer (Months 0-6):** Deployment of an **API Middleware Layer** across all entities. This "Regulatory Shim" intercepts data to ensure compliance with local data residency laws (NDPR, CBK, BAM) before any cross-border reporting occurs.
*   **Phase II: Functional Specialization (Months 6-18):** Implementation of a "Lightweight Core" for new digital products, while legacy cores remain as systems of record.
*   **Phase III: Core Modernization (Months 18-36):** Phased sunsetting of local legacy systems, subject to individual regulatory approval from each regional supervisor.

### 4. Regulatory & Governance Architecture
We will replace centralized control with a **"Trio Council"** governance structure to ensure equitable representation across jurisdictions.

| Pillar | Focus | Lead Entity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Risk & Compliance** | IFRS 9 & AML/Basel III alignment | Morocco (BAM) |
| **Digital Product** | Mobile-first UX & Interoperability | Kenya (CBK) |
| **Operational Efficiency** | FX-Liquidity & Lending Velocity | Nigeria (CBN) |

**Key Regulatory Mechanism:** We will appoint **Regional Liaison Offices** rather than a single Global Compliance Head, ensuring real-time alignment with the shifting priorities of the CBN, CBK, and BAM.

### 5. Actionable Milestones

#### Q1–Q2 (Governance & Compliance)
*   **Action:** Formalize the Trio Council and establish the "Regulatory Liaison Offices."
*   **Milestone:** Complete audit of data residency requirements for all three nations; initiate deployment of local cloud anchors.

#### Q3–Q6 (Digital Product Launch)
*   **Action:** Deploy the unified Mobile Design System.
*   **Milestone:** Launch a "Group-strength" retail credit product, localized for each market (e.g., FX-hedged in Nigeria, mobile-money integrated in Kenya, Sharia-compliant options in Morocco).

#### Q7–Q12 (Cultural Integration & Talent)
*   **Action:** Launch the "Pan-African Exchange" program.
*   **Milestone:** Completion of the first rotation of 50 mid-level managers across Lagos, Nairobi, and Casablanca to build cross-border institutional capital.

### 6. Risk Mitigation & Continuity
*   **Operational Continuity:** The use of the API Middleware layer ensures that customers experience no service disruption during back-end migrations.
*   **Linguistic Integration:** Mandatory bilingual (French/English) operational documentation for the Moroccan entity ensures that local staff and regulators remain fully integrated in the governance loop.
*   **Data Sovereignty:** By keeping core data on local instances with "Regulatory Shims," we satisfy all three sovereign regulators simultaneously without compromising the group’s unified digital product capabilities.

---
**Approval Authority:** Board of Directors  
**Strategic Intent:** To build a durable, compliant, and hyper-responsive Pan-African financial institution that wins through local relevance and global scale.
