## DOCUMENT 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1 PAGE)

### INVESTMENT THESIS

**Opportunity:** Emerging markets (Africa, South Asia, Latin America) represent **2B+ addressable subscribers** but remain <0.5% penetrated due to pricing, payment friction, and sharing norms misalignment. A **region-specific subscription model**—combining PPP-adjusted pricing, payment-rail optimization, and family-sharing tiers—unlocks **16–20× subscriber growth** (10M → 160–200M by 2029) while maintaining **≥50% gross margins** and **5.4–6.8× LTV/CAC** unit economics.

**Month 7–12 (Jul–Dec): Expansion**
- **Contingency:** If WhatsApp Pay launches (delayed from 2025), integrate as secondary QR option

- **Target:** <1% monthly churn attributable to payment method failure (80% recovery within grace period)

#### 2.C FAMILY-SHARING POLICY & ENFORCEMENT

**Household Eligibility:**
- 2–6 primary users per family plan; all registered in single household account
- Household verification: Primary payer registers with phone number + email + optional postal code (KYC-lite)
- Concurrent stream limit: 2 (T2) or 3 (T3); 4th simultaneous stream = account warning; 5th+ = 24h suspension

**Concurrent Stream Monitoring:**
- **IP-based geofencing:** Flag if >2 different IPs simultaneous for >5 min; warn user
- **Device fingerprinting:** Allow up to 4 trusted devices per household; 5th device = re-verification required
- **Enforcement:** Soft suspension (content paused; can reactivate via device confirmation) for persistent violations

**Revenue Model:**
- T2 (Standard): 1.4× T1 price; targets 62% of households (remainder 30% solo, 8% T3)
- T3 (Family+): 1.8× T1 price; targets 22% of households; includes parental controls, offline download
- **Blended family-plan adoption:** 84%; revenue per household = 1.54× solo subscription

**Enforcement Timeline:**
- Month 1–2: Education (in-app messaging, email); warnings only
- Month 3+: Policy enforcement (soft suspensions, account review)
- **Churn risk:** <0.5% monthly from enforcement (users prefer compliance to cancellation)

#### 2.C FAMILY-SHARING POLICY & CULTURAL ALIGNMENT

**Household Norm (South Asia Baseline):**
- **Avg household size:** 5.2 people (multi-generational: elders + parents + kids)
- **Comfort with shared login:** 78% (highest global; cultural default)
- **Expected concurrent users:** 4–6 (TV + 2–3 phones simultaneously; typical evening watching)
- **Willingness-to-pay premium for family tier:** 1.5× solo price (vs. 1.4× Africa, 1.55× LATAM)

**Family Plan Design:**
- **T2 Standard (₹219):** Positioned as "pura parivar" (whole family); 4–6 user accounts; 3 concurrent streams
  - Use case: Extended family (grandparents + parents + children) on shared household WiFi
  - Revenue logic: Replace 4–6 individual solo subscriptions with 1 family plan; slight cannibalization acceptable because:
    1. Majority of new household acquisition (not migration from existing solo subs)
    2. Lower churn (multi-generational households "sticky"; elders prefer to keep for kids)
    3. Family premium (1.5×) partially offsets cannibalization
  
- **T3 Premium (₹339):** "Parivaar Premium" with enhanced features
  - 5–6 user accounts; 5 concurrent streams (family + guest OK)
  - Parental controls: Content filtering by age (kids, teens, adults); Hindi/Tamil/Telugu language filter
  - Offline download: Download for travel, limited data scenarios (common in rural India)
  - Target adoption: 24% of families (affluent urban + salaried households)

**Enforcement & Trust-Building:**
- **Month 1–2:** User education via in-app messaging ("Ek account, ek parivar" = one account, one family)
- **Month 3+:** Automated monitoring (IP geofencing; warn if >2 different IPs for 5+ min)
- **Enforcement approach:** Permissive initially; strict enforcement only if abuse flagged (low churn risk expected due to cultural norm alignment)

**Cannibalization Mitigation:**
- Conservative model: 20% of family-plan revenue from solo-sub migration; 80% pure acquisition
- Cannibalization impact: Revenue per household = 1.5 × (80% + 20% × 1/4 family-to-solo conversion) = 1.35× solo
- Still positive; breaks only if cannibalization >60% (unlikely given cultural norms)

#### 2.C FAMILY-SHARING POLICY: PARENTAL CONTROL PREMIUM

**Household Norm (LATAM Baseline):**
- **Avg household size:** 3.5 (Brazil 3.2, Mexico 4.1); nuclear families dominant
- **Multi-generational:** Only 37% (vs. 45% Africa, 67% South Asia); weaker multi-gen norms
- **Comfort with shared login:** 58% (lower than Africa/Asia); preference for separate profiles + parental controls
- **Willingness-to-pay premium for family tier:** 1.55–1.60× solo (driven by parental control demand, not sharing convenience)

**Family Plan Design (Brazil):**
- **T2 Standard (R$ 33.90):** "Plano Família" (family plan); 2–3 user profiles; 2 concurrent streams
  - Use case: Parents + 1–2 kids; parents watch content separately from kids' profiles
  - Parental control: Filter kids' content by age rating (0–4, 5–12, 13+); block specific content; monitor watch history
  - Revenue logic: Parents willing to pay 1.55× solo for parental peace-of-mind

- **T3 Premium (R$ 52.50):** "Premium Família" with enhanced controls
  - 4–5 user profiles; 3 concurrent streams; 4K video
  - Parental controls: Time-based access (e.g., kids content only 17:00–21:00); spending limit (kids cannot purchase extras)
  - Spanish audio option: Appeal to Brazilians consuming Latin American content
  - Target adoption: 20% of households (affluent urban; high willingness-to-pay for child safety)

**Family Plan Design (Mexico) – With Duo Tier:**
- **T1.5 Duo (MXN 86):** "Plan Pareja" (partner account); 2 users; separate profiles; 1 concurrent stream
  - Use case: Couples, roommates; emerging in Mexico where individual preference is default
  - No parental controls; assumes adult-only household
  - Target adoption: 35% of subs (intermediate tier; bridges solo → family market)

- **T2 Family (MXN 121):** "Plan Familia Estándar"; 3–4 users
