# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Korean content is a primary subscriber acquisition and retention driver across five macro-regions. In 2026, our mandate is to **maximize reach without diluting brand quality or breaching regulatory compliance** under a constrained localization budget of **$42.0M**.

This strategy allocates budget through a **"Sub-First / Prestige Dub / Compliance-First"** framework, mandates **Korean-source translation** for all simulcast and prestige-tier titles, and treats transnational K-fandom as our highest-ROI marketing channel.

**Strategic North Star:**  
*Subtitles own the fan. Dubbing owns the household. Compliance owns the license. Fandom owns the conversation.*

**2026 Phased Rollout**
- **Q1:** Southeast Asia (SEA)
- **Q1–Q2:** Latin America (LATAM)
- **Q2–Q3:** Europe Tier 1 + Turkey Standalone
- **Q3–Q4:** Middle East & North Africa (MENA)

**Strategic Governance & Success Metrics**
- **Cost-per-Subscriber (CAC):** Track by region and audio format quarterly.
- **Dub Penetration Rate:** Target 35%+ of total K-content hours in LATAM; 15–20% in Europe; <10% in SEA.
- **Sub-Quality CSAT:** Maintain >4.2/5.0 fan-reported subtitle satisfaction.
- **Retention Lift:** Measure +90-day retention for dub-viewing households vs. sub-only households.
- **Regulatory Incident SLA:** Zero unlicensed launches; 100% pre-clearance in statutory markets; <5 budget variance triggers per quarter.

## Functional Trade-Offs

- **The Dubbing Cap:** Dubbing consumes 8–15x subtitling resources. Under 2026 talent scarcity:
  - **LATAM:** Full-series dubbing for all scripted titles at **$15k–$22k/episode** (union-tier novela talent).
  - **SEA:** Selective dubbing for **top 25% of titles by projected co-viewing appeal** (family/romance only).
  - **Europe:** **6–8 prestige dubs per major language annually** (French, German, Castilian Spanish, Italian, Polish). Tier-1 casting on tentpoles; Tier-2 dramatic casting on mid-tier titles to maintain frequency.
  - **MENA:** Arabic dubbing for **4 titles maximum**; subs for remainder.
  - **Turkey:** Turkish dubbing for **2–3 curated titles** within standalone compliance budget.

- **Adaptation Edit Tiers:** MENA’s allocation is 55% editing/compliance. Turkey’s TUR-Edit is funded within the Europe allocation but tracked separately. We accept lower title volume in high-friction markets to avoid bans.

- **AI-Assisted Pre-Processing (Catalog Only):** AI-assisted translation memory and timing for subtitle first drafts is **restricted to back-catalog and library content** in English, Spanish, and Portuguese, with human QC. This reduces non-simulcast per-episode costs by ~25%.
  - **Simulcast Rule:** Day-and-date releases are **human-first** with TM reuse. AI PEMT is prohibited for same-hour simulcast due to Korean broadcaster master delivery windows (24–72 hours pre-air).

- **Pivot Language Policy:** All simulcast, prestige-tier, and tentpole localizations originate from **Korean source**. English-pivot workflows are permissible **only for back-catalog titles** in low-friction markets, subject to spot-check audits.

- **Marketing Localization vs. Fandom Leverage:** Traditional paid media localization is cut by 20% across all regions. Savings are redirected into community-driven marketing and platform-native fandom infrastructure, which generates higher trust and lower CAC.

# 3. REGIONAL LOCALIZATION PLAYBOOKS

## 3.1 Phase 1: Q1 2026 — Southeast Asia (SEA)

**Rationale:** Highest K-content affinity, lowest per-unit cost, established payment ecosystems.  
**Core Markets:** Indonesia, Thailand, Philippines, Vietnam, Malaysia, Singapore.

**Action Items:**
- **Day-and-date drops with same-hour subtitles** in Thailand, Philippines, and Singapore.
- **Regulatory buffer:** Submit titles to Vietnam (Decree 72 / MoTT registration) and Malaysia (FINAS / JAKIM) **2 weeks before release**. Titles containing supernatural, horror, or LGBTQ+ subplots require **4-week advance submission** to preempt outright bans.
- **Sub-first across all markets.** Selective dubbing reserved strictly for family/romance co-viewing titles (Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, Tagalog).
- **Singapore:** Add Mandarin Chinese subtitle track for prestige titles.
- **Philippines:** Deploy Tagalog dub for telenovela-style and mainstream romance to capture familial co-viewing; English subs for all others.
- **Malaysia:** Pre-clear supernatural K-dramas with JAKIM; if bans are threatened, deploy the **MYS-Edit** picture master rather than age-gating alone.
- **Cultural context:** Retain Korean honorifics in subtitles with brief contextual glosses. Add optional PEFTOK context card for Korean War-set dramas in the Philippines.

## 3.2 Phase 2: Q1–Q2 2026 — Latin America (LATAM)

**Rationale:** Fastest-growing non-Asian K-content consumer base; strong dub culture; high social engagement.  
**Core Markets:** Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, Peru.

**Action Items:**
- Full Latin Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese dubbing for all scripted originals and tentpole acquisitions via **telenovela/novela dramatic studios** using live-action casting directors. **Anime VO studios are explicitly excluded** from K-drama casting.
- **Southern Cone test:** Commission **Rioplatense dubs (Buenos Aires)** for 2–3 prestige titles targeting Argentina and Chile; monitor sentiment and willingness to pay.
- **K-variety remains sub-only.** Idol reality/variety offers a **Hybrid Accessibility Track** (subbed idol dialogue + dubbed narration for game rules and context).
- **Talent security:** Lock union-tier dubbing talent **90 days before release**; secure overflow studio partnerships to prevent São Paulo/Mexico City scarcity delays.
- **Diaspora bridge:** Partner with Korean-Latino cultural institutes (e.g., Centro Cultural Coreano en México) for authentic influencer seeding.
- **Cultural context:** Produce 60-second "K-History Primer" social videos to accompany major sageuk launches. Reflect Korean hierarchical language (*jondaemal/banmal*) via Spanish/Portuguese register variation (formal/informal).

## 3.3 Phase 3: Q2–Q3 2026 — Europe Tier 1 + Turkey Standalone

**Rationale:** High ARPU but extreme fragmentation. Turkey operates under a separate RTÜK regime and must not piggyback on MENA workflows.  
**Core European Markets:** UK & Ireland, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Poland, Nordics, Netherlands, CEE.  
**Core Turkey Market:** Türkiye (standalone compliance zone).

**Action Items:**
- **Europe Sub-First:** Subtitles for all titles. Minimum **6–8 prestige dubs per major language annually** (French, German, Castilian Spanish, Italian, Polish). Use Tier-1 casting for 3–4 tentpoles; Tier-2 dramatic casting for remaining titles to manage cost.
- **Poland/CEE Expansion:** Increase Polish prestige dubs to **4–6 titles/year**; leverage lower studio costs versus Western Europe to build audio loyalty in Europe’s fastest-growing K-market outside the Big 5.
- **Nordics/Netherlands:** Sub-only. Mandate **Korean-source subtitles** for all top-tier titles; English-pivot permissible for back-catalog only.
- **Germany:** Avoid marketing that frames Korean division narratives as generic "dictatorship thrillers." Emphasize specifically Korean historical context to avoid triggering Stasi/Nazi surveillance trope sensitivities.
- **Eastern Europe:** Lean into Korean partition narratives in Polish and CEE marketing; these audiences strongly resonate with division themes.
- **Turkey Standalone:** Establish a **Turkish Edit Master (TUR-Edit)** with Istanbul-based compliance editors. Do not piggyback on MENA edits or MSA audio. Turkish dubbing for curated slate; RTÜK-specific review for alcohol, supernatural, and relationship content.

## 3.4 Phase 4: Q3–Q4 2026 — Middle East & North Africa (MENA)

**Rationale:** Heavy adaptation-edit pipeline that must be built and tested before volume release.  
**Core Markets:** Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, Kuwait, Qatar, Jordan, Lebanon, Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia.

**Action Items:**
- **Curated slate only:** romance, family drama, and workplace thrillers. Avoid horror with heavy shamanistic possession and explicit BL central narratives for the inaugural slate.
- **Three picture masters:**
  1. **Gulf-Conservative** (KSA, Kuwait, Qatar): Trim passionate kissing and bed scenes; reframe/blur alcohol via editing (no VFX); trim explicit shamanism/exorcism.
  2. **Levant/Egypt-Standard** (UAE, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon): Retain PDA and mild occult but age-gate (18+); blur visible alcohol brand labels.
  3. **Maghreb-French** (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia): Leverage Phase 3 French dub/sub assets plus MSA subtitle overlay.
- **Arabic subtitles for all titles.** Arabic dubbing limited to **4 edit-friendly titles maximum** in year one to control synchronization costs with altered picture masters.
- **No VFX blurring** for alcohol brands; use reframing, alternative B-roll, or prop replacement during adaptation editing.
- **MSA dub scripts** must be reviewed by a cultural compliance officer to ensure no inadvertent misuse of Islamic religious phrases.

# 4. CULTURAL SENSITIVITY & ADAPTATION PROTOCOLS

## Global Baseline (All Regions)
- **Historical Integrity:** Korean historical narratives (Japanese colonialism, Joseon social structures, Division of Korea) are never cut for comfort. Context cards may be added, but history is not sanitized.
- **Age Gating Over Censorship:** Where legally permissible, apply age ratings and content warnings rather than narrative edits.
- **Localization Notes:** All regions receive an optional **"K-Context"** subtitle track or in-app explainer for Korean idioms, honorifics, and historical references.
