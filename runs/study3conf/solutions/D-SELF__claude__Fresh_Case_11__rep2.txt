# COMPREHENSIVE CROSS-BORDER PAYMENTS FINANCIAL-OPERATIONS STRATEGY
## Executive Deliverable for Board & C-Suite Review (2026)

---

## SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Strategic Imperative
A mid-size remittance firm must navigate four distinct regulatory blocs (EU, Gulf, Southeast Asia, Latin America), each with fragmented licensing regimes, divergent KYC standards, volatile settlement currencies, and heterogeneous banking partners. Simultaneous global expansion across all corridors is **not viable** due to capital constraints (€25M–€30M) and compliance cost burden (€2.7M–€3.8M Year-1).

### Strategic Recommendation: Phased Expansion with Margin-Threshold Gating

**Tier-1 (2026 – Critical Path)**: EU↔Gulf + EU↔Mexico
- **Capital Required**: €8M–€10M (licensing, hubs, working capital)
- **Projected Year-1 Net Profit**: €3.7M–€4.3M
- **ROI on Capital**: 41–43% annually
- **Compliance Cost as % of Revenue**: 8–12% (acceptable)

**Tier-2 (2027 – Contingent on Tier-1 Success)**: EU↔Brazil, EU↔Singapore, Gulf↔Mexico
- **Entry Conditions**: Tier-1 corridors generating €3M+ profit; €5M–€8M capital available for expansion
- **Projected Incremental Profit**: €1.5M–€2.0M

**Tier-3 (2028+ – Optional)**: Thailand, Philippines, Colombia; **Argentina excluded** (negative margin; capital-control risk)

### Key Financial Outcomes (2026 Tier-1)

| **Metric** | **Value** | **Sustainability** |
|---|---|---|
| **Total Corridor Volume** | €140M (4 corridors) | ✅ Achievable (pilot partner channels + direct-to-FX-desk origination) |
| **Blended Gross Margin** | 5.1% | ✅ Market-tested (Remitly 4.5–5.5%, OFX 5–6%) |
| **Settlement Cost (FX+Float+Hedging)** | 1.36% | ✅ Realistic (based on market rate quotes) |
| **Compliance & Hub Cost** | 0.41% | ⚠️ Aggressive; assumes no major audit failure |
| **Partner Revenue-Share** | 12.5% of net margin | ⚠️ Negotiable; 15–17.5% may be required for willing partners |
| **Final EBITDA Margin** | 2.67% | ✅ Above WACC (8–10%); viable |
| **Net Profit (Pre-Tax)** | €3.74M | ✅ Covers hub costs 4.9× over; profit positive Year-1 |
| **Required Capital** | €9.1M (€7.6M vostro + €1.5M hub/licensing) | ⚠️ Requires Series A equity raise if capital <€5M today |

### Three Strategic Pillars

**1. Compliance Efficiency**: Hub-based KYC + federated regional adaptation (Frankfurt core + Dubai/Mexico hubs) reduces duplicate processing 30–40% vs. country-by-country model.

**2. Margin Protection**: Revenue-share partnerships (12.5% to banks) align incentives; avoid unsustainable fee-based vostro model. Apply 1.5× margin-to-compliance-cost ratio threshold for corridor inclusion.

**3. Risk Resilience**: Pre-positioned credit facilities (€1M), backup settlement corridors, quarterly political-risk monitoring, and regulatory-contingency protocols protect against geopolitical/liquidity shocks.
