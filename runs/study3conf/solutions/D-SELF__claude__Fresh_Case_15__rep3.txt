## THE CRISIS & THE CASE FOR IMMEDIATE ACTION

### Problem Statement
A 10,000+ unit QSR franchise system across Turkey, Egypt, Nigeria, Indonesia, and Brazil faces cascading financial distress among 30–60% of franchisees due to structural currency depreciation (25–45% additional erosion expected 2024–2026) and persistent inflation (14–30% annual CPI). Fixed USD-denominated royalties—unchanged since 2019—now consume 6–10% of deteriorated local revenues, rendering franchisees functionally insolvent despite operational viability.

**Financial Reality (Q2 2024):**
- Franchisee EBITDA margins: 2–9% (down from 15–16% historical)
- Payment default rate: 3–10% rolling 12-month (from 0.5% baseline)
- Franchisee associations mobilizing collectively in Turkey and Brazil; emerging coordination in Egypt/Nigeria
- Company-owned stores 500–1000 bps more profitable, creating resentment and political vulnerability

**Window for Action:** 12–18 months before cascading defaults destroy franchisee trust, lock in unfavorable regulatory responses, and force reactive (vs. proactive) restructuring at far higher cost.

---

## RECOMMENDED SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE

### Multi-Pillar Restructuring Framework

**Pillar 1: Hybrid Currency Royalty Model (Core)**
- Shift from fixed USD rates to 60% local-currency / 40% USD hybrid structure
- Local rates indexed to local inflation (50% pass-through escalation; 5-year recovery path)
- Monthly country-risk multiplier (automatic adjustment for macro conditions)
- Annual performance-based adjustments (tie rates to franchisee quality/EBITDA achievement)
- **Impact:** Restore franchisee EBITDA from 2–9% → 10–12% within 18 months

**Pillar 2: Comprehensive Working Capital Solutions**
- Franchisor-backed $450–500M term loan facility (5–7% pricing; 5-year tenor)
- Supply chain financing program (45–60 day payables extension via SPO partners)
- Convertible royalty deferrals (2-year moratorium; converts to equity if non-recoverable)
- Multi-unit franchisee equity swaps (3–7% stakes; aligns incentives for restructuring)
- **Impact:** Bridge $20–40K working capital gap per unit; restore DSCR to 1.1x+

**Pillar 3: Portfolio Management & Brand Protection**
- Quality assurance scorecard (quarterly audits; transparency gates for rate adjustments)
- Performance-based termination framework (preserve brand standards; exit persistently weak franchisees)
- Company-owned expansion program (acquire 560–700 distressed units 2025–2027; natural FX hedging)
- Supply chain consolidation (1.8–2.4 pp COGS savings via centralized procurement)
- **Impact:** Maintain brand equity; de-risk FX exposure; create innovation hubs

**Pillar 4: Proactive Stakeholder Engagement & Communication**
- Phased rollout (Phase 1: Indonesia/Brazil Jan 2025 → Phase 2: Turkey Feb 2025 → Phase 3: Egypt/Nigeria Jun 2025)
- Transparent performance reporting (quarterly dashboards; benchmarking data)
- Collective bargaining pre-emption (frame restructuring as "partnership stabilization," not "imposed concessions")
- Political & regulatory alignment (early engagement with CBs in Turkey/Egypt/Nigeria)
- **Impact:** Achieve 65–75% voluntary adoption; minimize forced negotiations; preserve franchisor credibility

| **Currency Crisis Escalation** | MEDIUM-HIGH (50–60%) | Rates escalate faster than projected; franchisee EBITDA re-compresses | **Mitigation:** Collar mechanism (cap 2026 rate escalation at +1.0 pp max); activate equity swap program for worst-affected; accelerate company-owned acquisitions |
