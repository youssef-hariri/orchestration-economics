# Framework: The Global Cultural Integrity Matrix
**Directive for 2026 Seasonal Marketing Campaigns**

This framework governs the planning, execution, and review of faith-based and cultural seasonal campaigns. It transitions our brand from a **"Global-First" (transcreation)** model to a **"Cultural-First" (co-creation)** model, prioritizing *Cultural Humility* over brand-centric messaging.

---

### 1. The Governance Structure (The Cultural Triad)
Every campaign must pass three gates to ensure authenticity and mitigate cross-border risk.

*   **Gate 1: The Inclusive Custodian Board:** A 5–7 member local committee (employees/consultants) representing diverse generational, socioeconomic, and religious perspectives (e.g., secular-cultural vs. practicing). They hold **absolute veto power** over all creative assets.
*   **Gate 2: The Contextual Audit:** A review of the brand’s local standing. If the brand has active local controversies (e.g., supply chain or labor issues), campaigns must remain "silent" or strictly minimalist to avoid charges of cultural extraction.
*   **Gate 3: The Sub-culture Stress Test:** An external cultural auditor reviews assets to ensure they do not "Other" the target community, avoid pan-regional caricatures, and verify that the content is accurate for the specific sub-region (e.g., distinguishing between Levantine and Gulf Ramadan traditions).

---

### 2. Seasonal Protocols & "Cultural Humility"
We no longer treat holidays as global monoliths. All agencies must adhere to these baseline requirements:

| Holiday | Core Mandate | Prohibitions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ramadan** | Facilitate communal connection and *Sadaqah* (charity). | Never conflate *Iftar* with "party" imagery. No food consumption visuals during daylight hours. High-energy music is prohibited. |
| **Lunar New Year** | Honor regional family reunions; reject the "Red/Gold" monolith. | No "one-size-fits-all" Asian aesthetics. All scripts must be checked by professional calligraphers for accuracy and kerning. |
| **Diwali** | Focus on the spirit of "Light/Knowledge." | Avoid generic firework themes. Ensure *Diya* placement is historically and contextually accurate to the specific region. |

---

### 3. Critical Implementation Directives
To prevent performative or exploitative marketing, the following requirements are **mandatory** for all regional agencies:

*   **The "Why Us" Justification:** Every campaign proposal must include a written statement explaining why the brand is the right entity to celebrate this moment. *“To sell product”* is an automatic rejection. The intent must be to facilitate a specific, locally recognized joy or connection.
*   **Zero-Overlap Policy:** Under no circumstances should marketing assets for different holidays bleed into each other’s windows (e.g., no Diwali imagery during Ramadan, regardless of the global diaspora).
*   **Cultural Humility Clause:** If the brand’s global social values (e.g., gender-neutrality) conflict with local conservative norms, do not force "universal values" as a political statement. Instead, lean into the role of a **respectful guest**, focusing exclusively on the holiday’s traditions without attempting to reshape them.
*   **The "Language Integrity" Rule:** Zero machine translation or AI-generated copy for cultural content. All copy must be drafted by native speakers and vetted by a cultural consultant.

---

### 4. Crisis Management: The "3-Step Protocol"
In the event of a negative social media response, the Crisis Response Pod will execute the following:

1.  **Acknowledge:** Publicly validate the feedback as a legitimate cultural concern rather than a "misunderstanding."
2.  **Listen:** Engage with local leaders/critics to understand the specific harm caused.
3.  **Pivot:** If an error occurred, apologize, remove the content, and issue a transparent explanation. **Never delete without a statement.**

---

### Final Strategic Directive for Agencies
**Less is more.** When in doubt regarding a cultural nuance, it is better to produce a **minimalist, respectful campaign** that honors the spirit of the holiday than an elaborate, high-budget campaign that risks being "performative." 

**Agencies must sign and adhere to the "Cultural Integrity Charter" confirming they understand these terms before budget release.**
