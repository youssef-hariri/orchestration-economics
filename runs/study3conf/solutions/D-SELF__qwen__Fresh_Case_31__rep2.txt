# Global Compensation Policy Framework (2026)

**Document Owner:** Global Total Rewards & Compensation Committee  
**Effective Date:** Q3 2026  
**Scope:** All full-time employees across 30 countries  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Guiding Principles

As a fully remote software company operating across 30 countries, our compensation philosophy must balance global market competitiveness with internal equity. In response to friction caused by hyper-localized pay bands, macroeconomic volatility, and emerging 2026 pay-transparency regulations, this final framework shifts our paradigm to a **Continuous Cost-of-Labor model**. This approach is fortified by robust legal compliance, psychological fairness guardrails, strict global mobility governance, and a phased operational rollout.

**Our Guiding Principles:**
1. **Pay for Impact, Adjusted by Continuous Labor Markets:** Base pay is determined by role level and competency, adjusted by a continuous, data-driven Labor Market Cost Curve. *Crucially, geographic adjustments reflect local talent competition, not personal cost of living.*
2. **Compress Disparities While Guaranteeing Local Market Parity:** We minimize the pay gap between regions for identical work, while guaranteeing no employee is paid below the 25th percentile of their specific local remote tech market (The "Local Market Guarantee").
3. **Total Rewards Parity & True Wealth Creation:** Equity is standardized globally in USD, supported by a "Net-Neutrality Mechanism" (localized tax gross-ups) to ensure net-after-tax wealth parity without breeding cross-border jealousy. Base cash is modeled on "fully loaded" employer costs to ensure financial sustainability.
4. **Proactive, Strictly Compliant Transparency:** We will meet and exceed statutory pay-transparency requirements by utilizing single-region job postings, avoiding conditional modifiers, mandating pre-interview disclosures, including all variable pay in statutory reports, and utilizing privacy-protected internal portals.
5. **Strict but Flexible Global Mobility Governance:** Compensation is intrinsically tied to authorized work locations. We maintain rigorous protocols for permanent relocations to prevent Permanent Establishment (PE) tax risks, balanced with a structured framework for temporary "workations" to support modern remote work realities.
6. **Continuous Governance and Adaptation:** We maintain a Quarterly Global Compensation Governance Review to ensure the continuous curve, outlier exceptions, and market recalibrations remain mathematically sound, legally compliant, and strategically aligned.

## 2. Pay-Band Methodology: The "Global Base + Continuous Curve" Model

To resolve geographic friction, eliminate "micro-cliffs," and account for macroeconomic variables, we are implementing a continuous cash compensation structure backed by rigorous financial modeling.

### A. The Continuous Labor Market Cost Curve
We have abandoned discrete geographic tiers. Locations are now mapped to a continuous regression curve based on Radford’s local tech labor supply/demand data, making the math infinitely more defensible to employees and auditors.
*   **Global Base (90% of Global Remote Market Rate):** A standardized global baseline for each job level. 
*   **Continuous Location Multiplier (1.00x to 1.15x):** An additive premium applied based on the specific city/region's exact position on the continuous curve (e.g., San Francisco = 1.15x, Berlin = 1.10x, Warsaw = 1.03x). 
    *   *Strategic Clarification:* This multiplier strictly reflects **local talent competition and labor supply/demand dynamics**, not the employee's personal cost of living. 
*   **Outlier Market Exception (Strictly Governed):** If annual benchmarking proves the 75th percentile of a specific local micro-market exceeds the 1.15x continuous curve maximum, hiring managers may petition the Compensation Committee for a localized override. To prevent budget creep, all exceptions are:
    1. Hard-capped at a **1.20x maximum multiplier**.
    2. Granted on a **12-month sunset clause** and must be re-petitioned annually.
    3. Restricted so that no more than **5% of the global headcount** is on an outlier exception at any given time.
*   **Critical Skills Differential:** A +5% to +10% premium for critical, hard-to-fill skills (e.g., AI/ML, specialized security, Rust) that applies *regardless of geography*, shifting the competitive narrative from "where you live" to "what you can do."
*   **The Local Market Guarantee:** Regardless of the continuous curve calculation, no employee shall be paid below the **25th percentile of their specific local *remote* tech market** (or the **50th percentile of the local in-person tech market** where remote-specific data is unavailable). 
    *   *Statistical Significance Threshold:* If the local survey data sample size for a specific role/level is fewer than 50 data points, the Local Market Guarantee defaults to the broader regional or global remote baseline to prevent over-indexing on statistical anomalies.
*   **Annual Recalibration Mandate:** The continuous regression curve is a dynamic tool. Total Rewards and Comp Analytics are mandated to perform an "Annual Regression Model Recalibration" every fiscal year, updating the HRIS lookup tables with fresh market data.

### B. FX Management & Currency Bands
*   **Local Currency Bands:** All Global Base and continuous multipliers are converted and locked into local currency bands.
*   **The FX Collar:** We implement a +/- 5% exchange rate threshold. Local currency salaries are only adjusted if the USD-to-local currency exchange rate fluctuates beyond 5% from the baseline rate set at the start of the fiscal year. Minor market noise is absorbed; major shocks are shared.

### C. Fully Loaded Cost Modeling & Statutory Bonuses
*   **Employer Burden Multiplier:** While the Global Base represents the *employee gross*, Finance calculates budget allocations using a "Local Employer Burden Multiplier" to account for vast disparities in employer social security and payroll taxes.
*   **Statutory Bonuses Clarification:** The Global Base represents the standard monthly gross base pay. Mandatory statutory bonuses (e.g., 13th/14th-month pay, vacation bonuses) required by local jurisdiction are paid **in addition** to the Global Base and are strictly excluded from the band maximum calculations.

### D. Band Architecture
*   **Band Width:** 40% from minimum to maximum.
*   **Data Sourcing:** Bands are benchmarked annually using Radford, filtering for "remote-first" companies.

## 3. Global Mobility & Work Location Governance

To manage cross-border relocations, mitigate corporate tax risks, ensure data security, and accommodate modern work flexibility, we have established strict yet pragmatic mobility protocols.

### A. Authorized Work Locations & IT Security Integration
Employees must be formally approved by HR and Legal to work in a specific country/city. **Unauthorized permanent relocation is a breach of policy.** 
*   **IT Security Checkpoint:** All mobility approval workflows include a mandatory IT Security checkpoint. Before HR finalizes any location approval, the employee must pass Mobile Device Management (MDM) compliance checks and secure network verification to prevent data breaches from unsecured foreign networks.

### B. Work Location Change Protocol (Permanent)
When an employee formally requests and is approved for a permanent location change, their compensation is adjusted according to the following rules:
*   **Upward Relocations (Low to High Multiplier):** If an employee moves to a higher-cost labor market, their pay is adjusted immediately if the new location's band minimum is higher than their current pay; otherwise, it is adjusted at the next annual merit cycle.
*   **Downward Relocations (High to Low Multiplier):** We implement a strict **"Pay Protection" clause**. Base pay is frozen at the current higher rate and will not be reduced (as unilateral reductions are illegal in most jurisdictions). However, future merit increases will be paused until the new location's band catches up to their current salary.

### C. Temporary Work Abroad (Digital Nomad Framework)
Recognizing the reality of modern remote work, employees may request to work from an unapproved country for **up to 30 days per calendar year**. 
*   **Pay Alignment:** During this temporary period, pay remains strictly tied to their primary authorized work location. 
*   **Immigration Compliance:** Employees bear the sole burden of ensuring their temporary work complies with local visa and immigration laws. The company reserves the right to immediately revoke approval and initiate disciplinary action if local laws are violated.
*   **Streamlined Approval:** This requires a simplified, expedited approval workflow involving Legal (for short-term tax/PE checks) and IT Security (for network/MDM compliance).

### D. Local Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA) Precedence
In jurisdictions with mandatory union or Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA) requirements (e.g., Brazil, France, Germany), local statutory or CBA provisions supersede the Global Base or continuous curve where legally mandated.

## 4. Internal Fairness Mechanisms

To ensure global teams feel valued, we are institutionalizing mechanisms that address legacy anomalies, purchasing power realities, and net-equity disparities.

### A. Standardized Global Equity & Net-Neutrality Mechanism
Equity grants (RSUs/Options) are standardized globally in USD value. To address varying global tax regimes and prevent cross-border jealousy:
*   **Net-Neutrality Mechanism:** We strictly frame localized tax gross-ups not as "bonuses," but as a mechanical "Net-Neutrality Mechanism" designed to equalize net wealth creation. 
*   **Tax Equalization Formula:** The Net-Neutrality Mechanism utilizes a standardized "tax equalization formula" that calculates the net target value and mathematically accounts for the taxability of the gross-up itself. This prevents infinite calculation loops, ensures cost predictability, and sets clear expectations for employees.
*   **Net Equity Value Calculator:** We will provide an intranet calculator showing employees side-by-side how a $100k grant results in the exact same *after-tax* wealth in Lisbon as it does in London. 

### B. "Red Circle" Policy & Thaw Trigger
For employees currently paid *above* the new compressed band maximum:
*   **Base Pay Freeze & Thaw Trigger:** Their base pay will be frozen until the band catches up. To prevent permanent loss of parity, we implement a **Thaw Trigger**: *If the market midpoint of the band increases by 5% due to annual benchmarking, the red-circled employee’s base pay is automatically unfrozen and adjusted to the new minimum of the band.*
*   **TTC Protection:** During the freeze, these employees receive a one-time lump-sum bonus or accelerated equity vesting equivalent to their target merit increase.

### C. Global Competency & Impact Framework
Pay progression within the 40% band is tied strictly to a transparent, globally applied Competency Rubric. 

### D. Transparent Critical Skills Criteria
To ensure the Critical Skills Differential is perceived as fair and objective, the exact criteria, required certifications, and skill assessments needed to qualify are **published on the company intranet** alongside the Competency Rubric. This eliminates perceptions of manager discretion or favoritism.

### E. Unified "Remote Enablement" Stipends
The Home Office & Tech stipend is a **flat global USD amount** (e.g., $1,000 annually). We have removed all Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) adjustments.

### F. Cross-Border Parity Audits
The Compensation Committee will conduct bi-annual "Geographic Parity Audits," analyzing compa-ratios across all 30 countries. If a specific country's average compa-ratio drops below 0.90 while the global average is 1.00, an off-cycle market adjustment budget is triggered.

## 5. Pay-Transparency & Compliance Strategy

We have restructured our transparency strategy to ensure strict adherence to statutory definitions of pay, data privacy thresholds, and local posting laws.

### A. External Job Postings & Pre-Interview Disclosures
*   **Pre-Interview Disclosure Workflow:** In strict compliance with the EU Pay Transparency Directive, recruiters must provide the applicable localized or EU-wide pay range to the candidate *prior to scheduling the first interview*.
*   **US State Laws (CA, NY, CO, WA):** We will **not** use conditional modifiers. For fully remote roles where location is unknown, we will post a single, broad, un-caveated range encompassing all multipliers (e.g., "$160,000 - $205,000").
*   **EU & Global Postings:** For roles posted as "Remote - EU," we will post a **single, binding EU-wide range** based on the median EU market rate, with a note: *"Final compensation will be determined by the specific Member State of work location in accordance with our continuous labor market curve."*

### B. Internal Transparency & GDPR Privacy Guardrails
*   **Open Bands:** The Global Base bands, continuous curve methodology, and Local Market Guarantee data are published on the company intranet.
*   **GDPR Minimum Headcount Threshold:** If a requested pay category (level + country + gender) contains **fewer than 3 employees**, the portal will automatically return a standardized legal message denying the specific breakdown to protect individual privacy.

### C. Statutory Disclosures & Variable Pay Inclusion
In compliance with the EU Pay Transparency Directive and relevant local laws, **all variable compensation components—including the Critical Skills Differential and Red Circle lump-sum bonuses—are explicitly included in statutory pay range disclosures and gender pay gap calculations.** We do not hide variable pay from regulatory reporting.

### D. Managing the "Internal Gap" Exposure (EU Pay Transparency)
*   **Mechanical, Gender-Neutral Justification:** For EU statutory Gender Pay Gap reporting, our "Objective Justification" will strictly rely on the **mechanical, gender-neutral application of the continuous labor market curve and the Competency Rubric**. We will explicitly avoid relying on "market forces" or "global business necessity."

## 6. Communication Plan

Shifting to a continuous, transparent, and fully-loaded model requires precise change management.

*   **Phase 1: Leadership, Finance & Legal Alignment (Weeks 1-3)**
    *   **Finance:** Finalize the Local Employer Burden Multipliers and model the financial impact of the FX Collar and continuous curve.
    *   **Legal:** Finalize single EU-wide posting templates and pre-interview disclosure workflows. Finalize GDPR threshold logic. Draft the mechanical Objective Justification document for EU compliance. **Initiate Works Council and union consultations in applicable EU jurisdictions.**
*   **Phase 2: Manager Enablement (Weeks 4-6)**
    *   **Manager Playbook:** Distribute a guide covering the continuous curve, the Local Market Guarantee, the Red Circle thaw trigger, the Net-Neutrality narrative, the Authorized Work Location policy, and the Temporary Work Abroad framework.
    *   **Training Sessions:** Mandatory training for all Engineering Managers. Focus on explaining how to use the Net Equity Value Calculator, framing the Local Market Guarantee, and utilizing **"Downward Mobility" scripts** for employees relocating from high-cost to low-cost hubs.
*   **Phase 3: Global All-Hands Rollout (Week 7)**
    *   Host a global virtual town hall. 
    *   **Key Message:** "We are implementing a continuous labor market model to ensure fairer pay globally, protecting your purchasing power with an FX collar, guaranteeing your local market parity, and standardizing our equity with a Net-Neutrality Mechanism so everyone shares in our financial success."
*   **Phase 4: 1-on-1 Compensation Conversations (Weeks 8-10)**
    *   Managers hold dedicated 1-on-1s to review compa-ratios. 
    *   **Red Circle & Mobility 1-on-1s:** Managers specifically trained to handle conversations with out-of-band employees and those undergoing location changes, focusing on their lump-sum bonuses, pay protection, and the automatic thaw trigger.

## 8. Ongoing Governance & Review Cadence

To ensure the policy remains a living, adaptable framework rather than a static document, the Compensation Committee will institute the following ongoing governance mechanisms:
*   **Quarterly Global Compensation Governance Review:** A formal quarterly meeting to monitor the health of the continuous curve, review and approve/reject Outlier Market Exceptions, and assess the overall budget impact of the Critical Skills Differential.
*   **Annual Recalibration Execution:** A mandatory annual cycle (concluding in Month 12 of the roadmap and repeating annually) where Total Rewards and Comp Analytics update all HRIS lookup tables with fresh Radford market data, recalibrate the FX Collar baselines, and refresh the Net Equity Value Calculator via the external tax provider SLA.
