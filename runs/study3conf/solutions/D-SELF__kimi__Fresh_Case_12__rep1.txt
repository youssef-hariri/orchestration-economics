## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

We are executing a controlled pivot from a uniform USD pricing monoculture to a **“Same Platform, Local Economic Reality”** operating model. By decoupling consumer-facing local-currency (LCY) pricing from HQ reporting currency, we unlock mass-market TAM across 500M+ underserved households in Africa, South Asia, and Latin America without sacrificing investor-defensible USD unit economics.

**Core Strategic Pillars**
*   **PPP-Anchored Micro-Pricing:** Tiered entry points set at 5–30% of US benchmark ARPU, calibrated to Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) corridors rather than reactive discounting.
*   **Payment-Rail Arbitrage:** Enforce a hard **<3% payment cost ceiling** on recurring billing; replace high-cost card rails with low-cost local push-payment networks (PIX, UPI, OPay, PalmPay) to preserve **75–85% Local Operating Cash Margins** even at sub-$3 ARPUs.
*   **Social Household Topology:** Monetize extended-family and communal sharing norms through operationally enforced, permissive-but-tiered plans (“Hogar,” “Joint Family,” “Community”), converting illicit password sharing into paid revenue.
*   **Defensible USD Reporting:** Deploy a **Constant Currency + Triple-Layer Margin** framework with an explicit global-content allocation key, giving investors full transparency into how emerging-market volume translates into global value accretion.

**Key Financial Guardrails & Decision Points for Leadership**
*   **Price Floor Protocol:** No tier may price below 4x the sum of marginal payment cost, bandwidth delivery cost, and local opex.
*   **Bandwidth Cost Cap:** Marginal streaming delivery must remain **<5% of tier ARPU**, enforced via AV1 codec, ISP edge-cache agreements, and concurrency auto-limits.
*   **FX Circuit Breakers:** Consumer prices adjust automatically if LCY depreciates >10% versus USD within a quarter. Argentina is USD-denominated and ARS-displayed, re-indexed weekly to eliminate hyperinflationary mismatch.
*   **Phase-Gated Capital:** Year 1 deployment is restricted to four deep-density markets (India, Brazil, Nigeria, Mexico). Phase 2 funding is contingent on **Layer 1 margins >70%** and **90-day retention >65%**.
*   **10% Emerging Market Contingency Reserve:** Held within Year 1 OPEX for competitive marketing response, *not* price cuts.

**Board Decision Required:** Approval of the Phase 1 capital plan ($XXM) for India and Brazil, and adoption of the Triple-Layer Margin reporting standard commencing Q2 2026.

**Margin Rule:** Every recurring-subscription payment rail must clear a **Payment Cost Ceiling of <3%**. High-cost rails (>15%) are quarantined to wallet top-ups or first-month acquisition channels only.

### 3.2 Stream Wallet — Regulatory-Compliant Architecture

To support prepaid wallets, weekly mini-plans, and salary-cycle alignment without assuming e-money licensing risk, the company will **white-label via licensed aggregators** (Flutterwave, dLocal, EBANX, Paytm, Interswitch).

*   **Float & KYC:** Licensed partner holds customer funds and manages AML/KYC compliance.
*   **Auto-Renewal:** Wallet auto-tops up via primary low-cost rails (OPay, UPI, PIX).
*   **Revenue Recognition:** Wallet balances are deferred revenue until consumption; breakage (>12 months inactive) recognized as other revenue per IFRS 15 / ASC 606.

**Financial Impact:** White-labeling eliminates $2–5M per market in direct licensing costs and 12–18 month regulatory lead times while preserving churn-mitigation benefits.

## 4. HOUSEHOLD TOPOLOGY & SHARING ENFORCEMENT

Rather than criminalizing culturally embedded sharing, we monetize it through operationally enforced, tiered plans.

### 4.1 Regional Enforcement Models

| Region | Tier Nomenclature | Concurrent Streams | Registered Devices | Operational Enforcement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LATAM** | **Hogar** | 4 | Up to 6 | IP-geofenced to single postal code / ISP ASN cluster; annual re-authentication via credit-bureau or utility-bill match. |
| **South Asia** | **Joint Family** | 4 | Up to 8 | Mobile-number verification per profile; download rights restricted to 2 primary devices to prevent gray-market offline resale. |
| **Africa** | **Community** | 4 | Up to 8 | In low-KYC markets, agent-assisted or biometric device registration during first login to prevent village-wide credential distribution. |
| **All Markets** | **Standard** | 2 | Up to 4 | Standard email/password; no geofence. |
| **All Markets** | **Mobile Lite** | 1 | 1 mobile + 1 cast | Device fingerprint locked; no profile sharing. |

### 4.2 Bandwidth Concurrency Guardrail

The 4-stream cap for Family/Community tiers is calibrated to a **40% peak simultaneous usage assumption** under AV1 compression and edge-cached delivery. If real-world concurrency exceeds the model and threatens the **<5% bandwidth cost cap**, the platform automatically reduces concurrent streams to 3 in that operating zone until infrastructure adjustments are complete.

**Financial Rationale:** Enforcement and fraud-ops costs are capped at **<2% of regional revenue** via automated rule engines. A permissive-but-enforced Community tier at 2.5–4x Mobile Lite ARPU captures value that would otherwise be lost to illicit sharing.

## 5. BILLING CADENCE & CHURN MITIGATION

*   **Default Cadence:** Monthly recurring, supplemented by a **“Pause”** function (2 pauses per year, max 30 days each). This neutralizes recurring-billing anxiety without the operational complexity of weekly permutations.
*   **Salary-Cycle Alignment:** In markets with irregular income rhythms (Nigeria, Kenya, Bangladesh), monthly billing is auto-timed to popular salary dates (25th–30th) via Stream Wallet pre-funding to reduce involuntary churn from insufficient funds.
*   **Weekly Mini-Plans:** Offered only as a conditional test SKU in Phase 1 markets. Permanent availability requires passing strict cohort economics:

| Cohort Metric | Kill Threshold | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| CAC Payback | >90 days | Extended payback destroys cash flow at sub-$3 ARPUs. |
| Churn vs. Monthly Control | >15% higher | If weekly users churn faster, the SKU becomes a CAC incinerator. |
| Support Tickets per User | >2x monthly baseline | Micro-billing permutations strain CS costs. |

**Revenue Recognition:** Weekly plans recognized ratably over 7 days; monthly over 30 days. All deferred revenue tracked in LCY and converted to USD monthly.

## 6. CONSOLIDATED UNIT ECONOMICS & INVESTOR METRICS

### 6.1 Triple-Layer Margin Framework

Emerging-market performance is reported through three reconciled layers to ensure full investor transparency.

| Layer | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Layer 1: Local Operating Cash Margin** | LCY Revenue − Payment Costs − Local Marketing − Local CS − Local Infrastructure | **75–85%** |
| **Layer 2: Regional Content Contribution** | Layer 1 − Regional Content Amortization (allocated by regional consumption hours) | **40–60%** |
| **Layer 3: Global Fully-Loaded Margin** | Layer 2 − Global Content Pool Allocation − G&A − R&D | **15–25% at maturity** |

**Global Content Allocation Key:** The global content pool is allocated to Layer 3 based on **regional consumption hours** as a proxy for value attribution. This aligns cost assignment with actual usage and prevents high-ARPU, low-engagement markets from subsidizing low-ARPU, high-engagement markets.

**Reporting Standard:** Quarterly disclosures include a Constant Currency reconciliation table, translating Layer 1–3 results at a fixed FX rate (prior-quarter end) to isolate operational performance from currency volatility.

### 6.2 Illustrative Unit Economics — India Mobile Lite (Monthly)

*Ad-supported model required to clear the 70% Layer 1 funding gate.*

| Line Item | INR | USD Equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer Price (incl. GST) | 89 | $1.07 |

| **Layer 3: Global Fully-Loaded Margin** | **42%** | **$0.52** |

**Investor Narrative:** Emerging-market CAC is 60–80% lower than US CAC ($8–12 vs. $40+). At scale, a $1.25 net ARPU with an 83% Layer 1 margin generates viable cash economics because content amortization per hour is diluted by high engagement and regional content costs.

### 6.3 Global EBITDA Volume Bridge

The pivot is accretive, not dilutive, at volume. Every 10M incremental emerging-market subscribers generates ~10B annual incremental viewing hours. Because 60–70% of emerging-market viewing is regional content with a per-hour amortization cost ~40% below global scripted average, the blended global content cost per hour declines as EM hours scale.

*   **Breakeven Threshold:** At **15M incremental EM subscribers**, fixed-cost dilution and regional-content mix reduce global amortization cost per hour by approximately $0.05, unlocking **~$75M in annual global EBITDA leverage**.
*   **Sensitivity:** At a 30% regional-content cost discount, the EBITDA unlock is ~$55M; at 50% discount, ~$95M. Beyond the 15M threshold, EM subscriber growth is unambiguously accretive to global margins.

## 7. FX, TREASURY & REPATRIATION POLICY

| Element | Policy | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Consumer Pricing** | Billed and displayed in LCY. | Maximizes conversion; reduces sticker shock. |
| **HQ Reporting** | Translated to USD using blended monthly commercial FX rate. | Smooths daily volatility for investor modeling. |
| **Circuit Breaker** | If LCY depreciates >10% vs. USD in a quarter, consumer prices adjust automatically at quarter-start. | Protects USD-reported ARPU and prevents sudden margin collapse. |
| **Argentina** | USD-denominated; ARS-displayed. Re-priced weekly via CCL/MEP blended rate or monthly IPC index. | Eliminates hyperinflationary mismatch between LCY revenue and USD content obligations. |
| **Nigeria Repatriation** | Repatriate via the I&E FX window; maintain 60-day LCY float to buffer illiquidity. Utilize natural hedges (local content production, payroll) where financial hedging is unavailable. | Reduces trapped-cash risk and parallel-market slippage. |
| **Other Volatile Markets** | Kenya: open capital account; monthly repatriation. Egypt: pre-negotiate Central Bank repatriation permits with local counsel. Brazil/Mexico/India: hedge 90-day intercompany balances via non-deliverable forwards. | Protects Layer 3 from FX-driven margin volatility. |
