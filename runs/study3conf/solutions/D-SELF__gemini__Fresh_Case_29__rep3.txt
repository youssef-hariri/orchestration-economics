### **Executive Summary**
To mitigate supply-chain disruption caused by export controls, we are shifting from a transactional purchasing model to a **Structured Capacity Agreement (SCA)** framework. This strategy resolves the "qualification-capacity-finance" trilemma by treating resilience as an operational insurance policy rather than a capital-heavy inventory burden.

### **1. The Strategic Pivot: Resilience as an "Operational Hedge"**
We are transitioning away from high-risk, long-term purchase commitments toward an asset-light, partnership-driven model. This approach protects our fab throughput while satisfying internal financial discipline requirements.

*   **Financial Framing:** Procurement costs associated with alternate sourcing will be reclassified as **"Operational Resilience Premiums."** These are presented as essential insurance against the multi-billion-dollar risk of fab downtime due to material shortages.
*   **Capacity Access vs. Asset Ownership:** We will utilize **Capacity Reservation Fees (CRF)** to secure "call options" on supplier production lines. This grants us priority access during crises without the balance-sheet burden of guaranteed volume ownership.

### **2. Execution Roadmap (24-Month Horizon)**

| Phase | Focus | Strategic Lever |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0–6 Mo** | **Shadow Qualification** | "Golden Spec" dossiers and virtual pilot-scale testing in non-critical lines to bypass the 12–24 month qualification blind-spot. |
| **6–18 Mo** | **Financial Architecture** | Milestone-linked Escrow funding. Payments released only upon validated quality gates, transferring technical risk to the supplier. |
| **18–24+ Mo** | **Dynamic Sourcing** | Implementation of a **70/30 Split**. We maintain 30% volume with secondary suppliers to ensure readiness and technical alignment without over-extending inventory. |

### **3. Key Financial & Risk Safeguards**
To ensure compliance with corporate treasury and finance mandates, every new supplier agreement will integrate:

*   **Performance-Linked Funding:** Pre-payment credits are structured as milestone-based escrows, protecting cash flow against supplier execution failure.
*   **The "Slider" Clause:** Tiered offtake agreements (base vs. flex) utilize indexed pricing to share material cost volatility, shielding our margins from raw feedstock spikes.
*   **Exit & Transfer Rights:** Contracts include "Right of Resale" clauses, allowing us to offload excess reserved capacity to the market should our internal demand forecast shift.
*   **Force Majeure 2.0:** Specific geopolitical clauses that trigger contract suspension or re-negotiation in the event of new export controls, capping our long-term legal exposure.

### **4. Business Impact**
*   **Technical Resilience:** The "Quality-at-Source" model reduces the probability of qualification failure by embedding our QA teams directly into the supplier production lifecycle.
*   **Financial Discipline:** By utilizing "Capacity Options" instead of direct Capex or heavy inventory holdings, we maintain an asset-light balance sheet while securing supply chain redundancy.
*   **Strategic Agility:** The 70/30 model ensures our fab floor is "bilingual"—able to pivot instantly between primary and secondary suppliers, turning a geopolitical threat into a managed operational variable.

### **Recommendation**
We request approval to pilot the **"Shadow Qualification"** program with two critical-path suppliers immediately. This represents a low-cost, high-impact investment in technical readiness that serves as the foundation for the broader SCA framework. 

**Decision Required:** Approval of the "Resilience Premium" budget to initiate the 6-month Pilot Phase.
