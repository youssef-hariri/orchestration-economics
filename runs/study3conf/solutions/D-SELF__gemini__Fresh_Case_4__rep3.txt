# Global Synchronicity & Autonomy Framework (GSAF) 2.0
**Official Corporate Policy: Effective January 2026**

## 1. Executive Summary
The Global Synchronicity & Autonomy Framework (GSAF) establishes a principle-based approach to work that prioritizes **output and accountability** over physical location. Recognizing that our twelve global offices operate within distinct legal, cultural, and market environments, this framework allows for localized execution while enforcing a global standard of performance equity.

---

## 2. Core Global Standards (Non-Negotiable)
To ensure all employees are judged by the same rigorous standards regardless of their location, the following pillars apply to every business unit globally:

*   **The Output-First Covenant:** Performance is measured strictly through the *Global Milestone Registry*. Desk presence, instant response times, and visibility are removed as criteria for performance reviews and promotions.
*   **The "Golden Hours" Protocol:** Every team is required to establish a 3-hour daily synchronous overlap window to facilitate collaboration. Outside this, asynchronous work is the default mode of operation.
*   **The Right-to-Disconnect:** Employees are not required to respond to digital communications outside of their designated *Local Operational Window*. Only system-critical outages or pre-defined, high-stakes client deadlines constitute an "emergency" exempt from this rule.
*   **The "Homecoming" Requirement:** To maintain global culture, all employees must participate in quarterly in-person team events focused on social cohesion and strategic alignment.

---

## 3. Local Adaptation Framework (The "Flex-Menu")
Country Leads, in consultation with HR and Legal, select one of the following operating tiers to align with local labor laws and cultural expectations.

| Tier | Model | Best For | Cultural Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier A** | **Compressed / 4-Day** | High-flexibility markets (e.g., UK, NL). | Prioritizes talent attraction & output. |
| **Tier B** | **Hybrid / Mentorship** | Collaboration-heavy (e.g., US, AUS). | 3 days in-office for structured networking. |
| **Tier C** | **Visible-Output** | Traditional/Hierarchical (e.g., JP, KR, DE). | Uses "Milestone Showcases" to satisfy visibility norms. |

*   **Works Council Supremacy Clause:** In all regions, local labor laws, union agreements, and Works Council mandates supersede this policy. Where local law provides greater protections, those protections become the baseline.

---

## 4. Operational Execution & Calibration
To prevent proximity bias and ensure fair advancement, the company implements the following management systems:

*   **Cross-Tier Calibration Committees:** All annual promotion and bonus reviews must include at least one senior leader from a *different* Operational Tier. This ensures candidates in high-visibility cultures (Tier C) and remote-first cultures (Tier A) are evaluated using identical output metrics, neutralizing cultural bias.
*   **Total Compensation Parity:** To ensure fairness between offices with different work schedules, the firm utilizes local market benchmarking. If a region cannot legally or culturally adopt a 4-day week, employees in that region will receive an adjusted "Well-being Allowance" to ensure the total value of their employment package remains equitable across the global network.
*   **Scheduled Notifications:** To avoid the risks associated with "Quiet Zones" (which encourage shadow IT), the company mandates a global "Scheduled Send" culture. Managers and colleagues may draft communications asynchronously at any time, but delivery to the recipient’s device is delayed until the start of their next *Local Operational Window*.

---

## 5. Governance & Oversight
*   **The Global Flex-Council:** A bi-annual review board composed of regional leads and employee representatives. The Council holds the authority to review attrition, productivity, and culture data to re-evaluate tier assignments for specific regions.
*   **Notification & Consultation:** Any change to local work structures requires a 30-day notice and consultation period. The "Crisis Clause" is strictly prohibited; temporary adjustments to working conditions must be documented and agreed upon according to local labor regulations.
*   **Data Integrity:** The use of personal communication tools for work-related tasks is strictly prohibited to maintain compliance with regional data privacy laws. All collaborative work must remain within approved, company-monitored environments.

---

### Implementation Statement
This framework treats **cultural context as a strategic business variable** and **accountability as a non-negotiable global constant**. By formalizing output-based metrics and enforcing cross-tier management, we ensure that our global talent remains aligned, fairly compensated, and empowered to contribute regardless of their physical location or local cultural norms.
