# CBAM SUPPLIER PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL
## For European Industrial Buyer: Supporting Emerging-Market Steel & Aluminum Suppliers Through EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism Compliance

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) transitions from reporting-only phase to binding financial liability on January 1, 2026. This creates a dual challenge for European industrial buyers: (1) secure supply chain compliance with emerging-market suppliers who face structural compliance barriers, and (2) preserve long-standing commercial relationships strained by legitimate fairness concerns about CBAM's discriminatory impacts on high-coal-grid countries and developing-economy supplier bases.

This proposal positions the buyer as a **trusted compliance partner and fairness advocate**, not a compliance enforcer. Through differentiated, tiered support pathways—coupled with transparent cost-sharing, contractual protections, and shared value creation—the buyer can:

- **Enable 95%+ supplier compliance by Q1 2026** (full compliance by Q1 2027-2028)
- **Preserve 85-90% of historical supplier relationships** (reduce churn risk from 40-50% baseline to 10-25%)
- **Capture €25-50M annual competitive advantage** from ESG-premium market positioning and supply chain resilience
- **Address protectionism concerns with evidence-based fairness narratives** grounded in grid decarbonization opportunity, cost transparency, and differential regional support

The strategy requires **€15-30M investment over 24 months** in direct supplier support (capex subsidies, financing, advisory), generating **3-5x ROI through ESG-premium pricing, supply chain risk mitigation, and competitive differentiation**.

## PART I: TIERED SUPPORT FRAMEWORK

### 1. Supplier Readiness Assessment & Classification

**Objective:** Differentiate 85-120 suppliers across India, Turkey, and North Africa into three tiers based on CBAM compliance capability, determining intensity and type of buyer support required.

#### 1.1 Four-Dimensional Readiness Scorecard

Assess all suppliers on a validated scoring model across four equally-weighted dimensions:

| **Dimension** | **Measure** | **Tier 1 (8-10)** | **Tier 2 (5-7)** | **Tier 3 (1-4)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Emissions Data Systems (30%)** | Tracking maturity; audit readiness; data quality | Real-time automated GHG accounting; ISO 14064 certified; <3-month audit lag; 3rd-party verified baseline | Semi-automated tracking; quarterly consolidation; basic ISO 14001; 6-month audit history | Manual (Excel); annual reporting only; no EMS; no prior audits |
| **Process Technology (25%)** | Equipment efficiency; capex intensity; BAT alignment | <55 kg CO₂/tonne steel (BAT-aligned); recent capex (€10M+/5yr); advanced control systems | 55-75 kg CO₂/tonne; moderate capex (€3-10M/5yr); partial modernization | >75 kg CO₂/tonne; minimal capex (<€2M/5yr); legacy furnaces (>20yr old) |
| **Regulatory/Compliance Infrastructure (25%)** | Team capacity; governance; prior carbon scheme experience | Dedicated 2+ FTE carbon team; documented procedures; ISO 14001+14064 certified; prior ETS/voluntary carbon market experience | 0.5-1 FTE environmental role; ISO 14001 only; basic procedures; limited prior experience | Part-time environmental person; no certifications; no carbon scheme participation; informal governance |
| **Financial/Institutional Capacity (20%)** | Balance sheet strength; capex budget; green finance access; leverage | Strong EBITDA (>15%); €10M+/year capex budget; green finance access; leverage <3x | Moderate EBITDA (8-15%); capex competing with debt service; limited green finance; leverage 3-5x | Tight EBITDA (<8%); no capex reserves; no green finance access; leverage >5x or restructuring risk |

**Readiness Score Formula:**
```
Composite Score = (Data Systems × 0.30) + (Technology × 0.25) 
                  + (Compliance Infrastructure × 0.25) + (Financial × 0.20)

Range: 1-10
Classification:
• Tier 1: Score 8-10 → Fast-Track (4-6 month compliance pathway)
• Tier 2: Score 5-7 → Structured (12-18 month compliance pathway)
• Tier 3: Score 1-4 → Intensive (18-30 month compliance pathway)
```

#### 1.2 Assessment Process (Timeline: Q4 2024 - Q2 2025)

**Phase 1: Self-Assessment Portal (Weeks 1-2, Q4 2024)**
- Web-based diagnostic tool with guidance; low-friction data submission
- Suppliers complete ~50 assessment questions; system auto-calculates preliminary score
- Produces confidential readiness report; no public scoring
- Estimated completion: 2-3 hours per supplier
- Output: Preliminary tier classification + gap identification

**Phase 2: Third-Party Verification Audit (Weeks 3-8, Q4 2024 - Q1 2025)**
- Neutral auditor (Big 4 accounting firm or specialized carbon auditor) conducts verification
- Methodology:
  - Tier 1 suppliers: Desk review only (5 business days); focus on data quality validation
  - Tier 2 suppliers: Desk review + optional site visit (7-10 business days); verify systems functionality
  - Tier 3 suppliers: Site visit required (10-15 business days); hands-on infrastructure assessment
- Deliverable: Verified Tier Classification + Detailed Gap Analysis (by dimension) + Recommended Improvement Roadmap
- Cost allocation: Buyer funds 50% of audit fee (€2-5K per supplier); supplier funds 50%

**Phase 3: Pathway Assignment & Contracting (Weeks 9-12, Q1 2025)**
- Buyer communicates tier classification and support pathway to each supplier
- Initiate supply agreement negotiations (see Part II: Contractual Framework)
- Establish governance structure (quarterly business reviews, single point of contact)
- Finalize detailed compliance timelines by supplier

**Phase 4: Annual Re-Assessment (Q4 2025, Q4 2026)**
- Suppliers eligible for tier upgrade if readiness score improves ≥2 points
- Upward mobility incentive: encourage Tier 2 → Tier 1, Tier 3 → Tier 2
- Transparent progress tracking; public-facing anonymized benchmarking (supplier cohorts vs. peers)
- Avoid penalizing newly compliant suppliers; emphasize trajectory over absolute baseline

### 2. Tier 1 Support Pathway: "Compliance Fast-Track"

**Target Profile:** 15-25% of supplier base (estimated 12-18 suppliers across three regions)

**Profile Examples:**
- Turkey: ArcelorMittal Turkey (1.2M tonnes/year integrated steel); Iş Çelik (200K tonnes/year secondary steel)
- India: JSW Steel Dolvi (1.2M tonnes/year); Tata Steel Jamshedpur (2.8M tonnes/year); Hindalco Aluminum
- North Africa: Integrated smelters with recent capex (e.g., Morocco's Aluminium du Maroc)

**Support Model Overview:**

| **Activity** | **Mechanism** | **Timing** | **Buyer Cost** | **Supplier Benefit** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Verification Audit** | Streamlined third-party validation; 5-7 business days; focus on data quality | Q1-Q2 2025 | €2-3K per supplier (50% buyer subsidy) | Fast-track CBAM readiness confirmation; audit-ready certification |
| **Price Premium** | 2-3% price uplift for CBAM-compliant volumes; locked contractually; escalation clause for over-performance | 2026-2028 (3-year commitment) | Buyer absorbs CBAM cost; retains 1% margin | +€50-75/tonne incremental margin; pricing stability |
| **Market Co-Branding** | Supplier marketed as "CBAM-Verified Partner"; joint press releases; customer visibility | 2026 launch; ongoing | Buyer provides distribution channel; co-branded marketing collateral | Market differentiation; ESG-customer access; brand recognition |
| **Regulatory Navigation** | Dedicated single point of contact for compliance questions; advance notice of EU guidance updates; quarterly reviews | Continuous 2025-2027 | 0.2 FTE buyer resources (~€20K/year) | Clarity on evolving EU rules; direct access to buyer's compliance expertise |
| **Advanced Certifications** | Co-funding for ISO 14067 (product carbon footprint) or PAS 2050; up to €30K subsidy | By Q4 2025 | 50% buyer subsidy (€15K max per supplier) | Enhanced market credibility; ESG premium access; product differentiation |
| **Compliance Leaders Consortium** | Peer learning network; quarterly webinars; industry working groups; exclusive to Tier 1 | Monthly (1-2 hours) | Hosting + facilitation (~€5K/year) | Peer benchmarking; regulatory intelligence sharing; relationship deepening |

**Tier 1 Timeline to Full Compliance:**
```
Q1-Q2 2025:    Verification audit completed; minor data system enhancements (if needed)
Q3-Q4 2025:    Final certifications obtained; market positioning prep; customer communications
Q1 2026:       FULL CBAM REPORTING BEGINS (100% compliant volumes eligible for price premium)
2026-2028:     Price premium locked; volume guarantees maintained; relationship deepens
```

┌─ Baseline Margin Floor:
│  Buyer commits to hold nominal EBITDA margin above historical 3-year baseline.
│  
│  Formula:
│  Margin Floor = Historical Avg. EBITDA % - [Published CBAM Price × Facility Emissions Intensity]
│  
│  Example: 
│  Historical EBITDA: 10%
│  CBAM Price: €85/tonne CO₂
│  Facility Intensity: 65 kg CO₂/tonne
│  CBAM Cost Component: €85 × 0.065 = €5.53/tonne ≈ 0.22% margin impact (on €2,500/tonne baseline)
│  Margin Floor: 10% - 0.22% = 9.78% minimum
│
│  PROTECTION: Compliance reporting/audit costs are NOT deducted from margin floor.
│  These are amortized into capex subsidy (see Section 3).
│
├─ Verification:
│  Margin floor is auditor-verified triennially (end of 2025, 2028).
│  If Buyer's COGS data shows margin < floor, Buyer provides price adjustment in next fiscal period.
│  Disputes resolved via third-party auditor (agreed-upon firm; cost-shared 50/50).
│
└─ Adjustment Triggers:
   Margin floor is recalibrated annually (Q1) based on:
   ├─ Updated CBAM certificate prices (published weekly by EU)
   ├─ Facility emissions performance (if Supplier achieves >5% reduction, CBAM cost component declines, floor rises)
   └─ Inflation (margin floor escalates with CPI; protects Supplier from real wage/input cost increases)

┌─ Eligible Capex Categories:
│  1. Data Systems & CBAM Infrastructure:

│
│  2. Process Efficiency (from Technology Roadmap):
│     • Waste heat recovery, power factor correction, scrap efficiency, furnace controls
│     • Buyer subsidy: 30-40% (capped at €50K per supplier over 24 months)
│     • Financing: Blended finance facility (60% concessional loan + 40% equity/grant)
│
├─ Cost-Sharing Transparency:
│  Example: Turkish Mini-Mill, €3M Efficiency Capex:
│  ├─ Buyer Direct Grant: €0.6-0.9M (30% subsidy)

│  └─ Energy Efficiency Benefit: €1.5-2M/year (50% ROI on capex; 2-3 year payback)

│
└─ Financing Facility Terms:
   • Amount: Up to €2-3M per supplier
   • Tenor: 5-7 years
   • Rate: Market rate - 1.5-2% (Buyer absorbs 0.5-1% subsidy; lender absorbs 0.5-1%)
   • Covenants: Supplier must maintain ≥80% compliance reporting + ≥3% annual emissions reduction

   • Survivor: Loan obligations survive supply relationship termination

Section 4: VOLUME PROTECTION & RAMP-DOWN SCHEDULE
┌─ Volume Guarantee by Compliance Phase:
│  2025 (Pre-Financial Phase):
│  ├─ Baseline volumes: 100% of historical 3-year average
│  ├─ Pricing: Baseline + 0.5-1.5% phased premium (schedule visible to Supplier)
│
│  2026 (Financial Phase, Phased Reporting):
│  ├─ Minimum volumes: 90% of historical (if ≥80% compliant); 95% if ≥95% compliant
│  ├─ Pricing: Baseline + 1.5-2% premium (locked contractually)
│  ├─ Compliance contingency: If Supplier achieves <80% compliant, volumes reduce proportionally
│  │   (e.g., 75% compliant = 85% volume guarantee)
│
│  2027 (Financial Phase, Enhanced Reporting):
│  ├─ Minimum volumes: 95% of historical (if ≥95% compliant); 100% if 100% compliant
│  ├─ Pricing: Baseline + 2-3% premium (ramps to Tier 1 levels)

│  2028+ (Full Compliance Expected):
│  ├─ Minimum volumes: 100% of historical
│  ├─ Pricing: Baseline + 2-3% locked; escalates based on performance/market

│  ├─ Volume Reduction Trigger: Supplier misses compliance milestone (e.g., phased reporting deadline)

│  ├─ Adjustment Magnitude: Maximum 10% quarterly reduction (not cliff)

│
└─ Documentation Requirements:
   Supplier provides audit trail for all proxy data:
   ├─ Sector benchmark source (published reference)
   ├─ Supplier's historical actual data (if available; justification if unavailable)

   └─ Monthly reconciliation vs. actual data (as actual systems come online)

┌─ Escalation Protocol:
