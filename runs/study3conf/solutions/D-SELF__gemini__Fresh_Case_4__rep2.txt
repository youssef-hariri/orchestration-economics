# Internal Policy Directive: Global Equitable Agility Policy (GEAP)

**To:** All Regional HR Managers, Country Leads  
**Subject:** Implementation of the Global Equitable Agility Policy (GEAP)  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

---

### I. Purpose
The Global Equitable Agility Policy (GEAP) transitions our organization from a culture of *time-in-seat* to a culture of *measurable impact*. This policy provides a standardized framework that respects local labor laws, cultural norms, and business continuity while ensuring fair treatment for all employees across our twelve global offices.

### II. Operational Models
Offices will be classified into one of three operational models based on local business requirements and cultural expectations. Note: **These labels are for internal operational alignment only and do not constitute a status hierarchy.**

1.  **Focus Model:** Ideal for deep, independent work. Supports compressed schedules (e.g., 4-day weeks).
2.  **Blend Model:** A hybrid approach balancing remote flexibility with scheduled in-person collaboration.
3.  **Connect Model:** Prioritizes in-person mentorship and team cohesion, supporting cultures that value visible presence.

### III. Global Non-Negotiables
Regardless of the model, the following standards apply globally:

*   **Core Collaboration Hours:** Every office must define a **3-hour window of daily synchronous overlap** (local time). During this time, team members must be reachable for meetings and urgent decision-making.
*   **Zero-Gap Client Coverage:** Compressed schedules (e.g., in Focus Model offices) must be managed through **staggered rotation rosters**. No client-facing function may be unstaffed during standard local business days.
*   **Output-Based Performance:** Promotions and bonuses are tied strictly to KPIs and agreed-upon deliverables. "Visibility" is not a performance metric.
*   **Compliance Lock:** In jurisdictions with strict rest-period laws, management software will prevent the assignment of tasks that exceed local legal working-hour thresholds.

### IV. Bridging the Visibility Gap
To ensure fairness, managers must adapt their leadership style based on their office model:

*   **In "Connect Model" offices:** "Visibility" must be redefined from digital log-keeping to **Community Impact**. Managers are required to reward efforts such as mentorship, peer-to-peer training, and cross-departmental documentation.
*   **Calibration Penalties:** Global HR will conduct bi-annual audits. If a manager’s team shows a statistical correlation between office presence and high performance ratings, that manager will be flagged for mandatory "Outcome-Based Management" coaching.

### V. Communication Charters & Right to Disconnect
Every country office must publish a **Communication Charter** by Month 4 of implementation. This charter must explicitly define:
1.  **Synchronous Channels:** Reserved for urgent client escalations (e.g., phone, emergency Slack triggers).
2.  **Asynchronous Channels:** Reserved for non-urgent tasks (e.g., email, project dashboards).
3.  **Right to Disconnect:** No employee is required to respond to non-urgent communication outside of their defined daily output window.

### VI. Governance & Compliance
*   **Local Law Precedence:** In the event of a conflict between the GEAP and local labor law, **local law takes absolute precedence.**
*   **Performance Calibration Audits:** Global HR will audit performance reviews twice yearly. If "Focus Model" employees are consistently outperformed in promotions by "Connect Model" employees without an objective gap in KPIs, the promotion criteria must be recalibrated.
*   **Mobility Exchange:** We encourage participation in the *Annual Mobility Exchange*. Employees are invited to spend two weeks working in a different model office to foster empathy and cultural understanding across our network.

### VII. Action Required
1.  **Month 2:** Complete the Local Readiness Survey to determine the best operational model for your region.
2.  **Month 3:** Submit your chosen model to the Global Board for approval.
3.  **Month 4:** Draft and publish your office-specific *Communication Charter*.
