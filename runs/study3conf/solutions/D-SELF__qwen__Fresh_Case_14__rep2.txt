### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
To finance our 2026-2030 industrial decarbonization retrofits across Europe, North Africa, and Southeast Asia, the Group will execute **Project Meridian**, a dual-tranche green bond issuance. This strategy navigates a fragmented 2026 regulatory landscape characterized by divergent regional taxonomies (EU, ASEAN, Gulf), inconsistent legacy emissions data in emerging market plants, and conflicting guidance from Credit Rating Agencies (CRAs) and regional banks.

Project Meridian resolves these challenges through a **Multi-Standard Green Bond Framework**. By structurally separating strictly EU-compliant assets from regional transition assets, we protect the premium "European Green Bond" (EuGB) label while capturing diverse global liquidity. Furthermore, we mitigate measurement-quality risks by funding data remediation outside the bond proceeds via a Sustainability-Linked Loan (SLL), and we proactively manage CRA quantitative scoring through pre-issuance technical engagement. 

This approach ensures strict regulatory compliance, protects the Group’s credibility with institutional investors, and optimizes capital costs through targeted "greenium" pricing.

---

### **1. FRAMEWORK ARCHITECTURE & TAXONOMY HARMONIZATION**
*Objective: Structure a legally robust, multi-standard framework that satisfies divergent regional taxonomies without contaminating the EU GBS integrity.*

To comply with the strictures of the EU GBS Regulation—which prohibits the EuGB label if any underlying asset fails EU Taxonomy alignment—we will deploy a **Multi-Standard Master Framework** divided into two distinct eligibility pools:

*   **Section A (EuGB-Eligible Pool / Tranche A):** Strictly aligned with the EU Taxonomy. Restricted to "Dark Green" activities (energy efficiency, electrification of industrial heat, on-site renewables). Proceeds allocated here will bear the "European Green Bond" (EuGB) label.
*   **Section B (ICMA/Regional-Eligible Pool / Tranche B):** Aligned with the ICMA Green Bond Principles (GBP) and mapped to the ASEAN Taxonomy (including "Transition" categories) and Gulf frameworks. This pool accommodates broader industrial transition activities permitted in these regions but excluded from the EU framework.

**Strategic Advantage:** This bifurcation allows us to access Asian and Middle Eastern sovereign wealth funds and regional banks via Tranche B, without compromising the premium pricing and regulatory safety of the EuGB-labeled Tranche A targeted at European ESG-mandated funds.

---

### **2. MEASUREMENT-QUALITY RISK & DATA INTEGRITY**
*Objective: Mitigate legacy data inconsistencies in North African (NA) and Southeast Asian (SEA) plants without violating Use of Proceeds (UoP) eligibility rules.*

**Decoupling Data Funding from Bond Proceeds**
Under both the EU Taxonomy and ICMA GBP, internal corporate monitoring and general IoT deployment are classified as ineligible corporate overhead. Therefore, **green bond proceeds will not be used for "Data CAPEX."** 
*   **Alternative Funding:** We will fund NA/SEA data remediation (IoT sensors and smart meters) through **General Corporate CAPEX** or a separate **Sustainability-Linked Loan (SLL)**, where the margin ratchet is explicitly tied to data-quality and measurement KPIs.

**Project-Level Baselines & Digital Twin Calibration**
Green bonds are "use-of-proceeds" instruments; credibility relies on the *project's* emissions reduction, not the issuer's total corporate footprint. 
*   For NA/SEA plants with poor historical data, we will use engineering models and digital twins to establish defensible *ex-ante* project baselines. 
*   **Pre-Issuance Validation:** To satisfy the strict EuGB label for Tranche A, our external reviewer will be mandated to validate this digital twin methodology against quantitative EU Taxonomy Technical Screening Criteria (TSCs) *prior* to issuance. The framework will explicitly state that these models are calibrated against the corporate-funded IoT data, transforming a historical weakness into a transparent governance strength.

**Phased Assurance Strategy**
*   **Pre-Issuance:** "Limited Assurance" on the Multi-Standard Framework and baseline methodologies.
*   **Post-Issuance (Years 1-2):** "Limited Assurance" on allocation reporting.
*   **Post-Issuance (Year 3+):** Upgrade to "Reasonable Assurance" on impact reporting as NA/SEA sensor data matures.

---

### **3. STAKEHOLDER NAVIGATION & GOVERNANCE**
*Objective: Neutralize conflicting advice from Rating Agencies and Regional Banks through quantitative engagement and strict internal governance.*

**Green Finance Steering Committee (GFSC) with Veto Power**
A cross-functional committee (CFO, CSO, General Counsel, Head of IR) will be formally chartered. To prevent internal fragmentation and regional bank pressure, the Board will grant the GFSC a **strict internal veto rule**: *Local bank preferences for localized, less rigorous green definitions cannot override the Master Framework's eligibility criteria.* A top-tier Second Party Opinion (SPO) will act as the objective external shield to enforce this standard.

**Proactive CRA Quantitative Engagement**
In 2026, CRAs use highly quantitative, materiality-weighted ESG Credit Indicators. A poor 'E' score due to NA/SEA data quality will mechanically drag down our credit score. 
*   **Materiality Mapping:** The GFSC will map the retrofits directly to the CRAs' specific sector materiality matrices.
*   **Technical Briefings:** Prior to the investor roadshow, we will invite CRA ESG analysts to a dedicated technical session. We will demonstrate the digital twin methodology and the corporate-funded IoT remediation plan (via the SLL), proving exactly how our quantitative 'E' score will improve over 24 months.

---

### **4. CAPITAL MARKETS EXECUTION**
*Objective: Optimize pricing, maximize the "greenium," and access diverse liquidity pools.*

**Dual-Tranche Benchmark Issuance**
*   **Tranche A (EUR Benchmark, 7 or 10-year):** Aligned with Section A (EuGB-Eligible). Targeted at European funds. Expected to command the highest "greenium."
*   **Tranche B (USD or SGD/AED, 5-year):** Aligned with Section B (ICMA/Regional). Targeted at Asian and Middle Eastern funds.

**Tailored Investor Roadshows**
*   **London/Frankfurt (Tranche A):** Focus on EuGB compliance, strict EU Taxonomy alignment, and third-party assurance.
*   **Singapore/Dubai (Tranche B):** Focus on tangible industrial impact, regional energy security, and specific mapping to ASEAN/Gulf taxonomies. *Note: The Tranche B roadshow will be heavily fortified with specific regional technical screening criteria to preempt "greenwashing" accusations from international NGOs regarding transition assets.*

---

### **5. POST-ISSUANCE REPORTING & COMPLIANCE**
*Objective: Maintain long-term trust and prevent "greenwashing" accusations using legally sound, bifurcated fallback mechanisms.*

**Bifurcated Fallback Waterfalls**
To avoid unacceptable treasury and secondary market risks, the bond term sheets will include globally recognized, legally sound fallback mechanisms for allocation failures, tailored to each tranche's regulatory regime:
*   **Tranche A (EuGB Fallback):** If a project's data is found materially flawed or ineligible, unallocated proceeds must be invested in specific EU Taxonomy-aligned financial assets, or the bond must be redeemed at par. *(Note: Charitable donation is not permitted under EuGB regulations).*
*   **Tranche B (ICMA Fallback):** 1) Extended reallocation to a compliant project (24-36 months); 2) If unfeasible, donation to a recognized environmental charity; 3) Early redemption at par as a last resort.

**Standardized Impact Reporting**
*   **Allocation:** Full traceability of funds, clearly delineating Section A and Section B allocations.
*   **Impact:** Standardized metrics (MWh saved, tonnes of CO2e avoided). For NA/SEA plants, *avoided emissions* based on engineering models will be clearly labeled as "Estimated Impact (Calibrated)," alongside the "Actual Impact" from EU plants.
*   **Integration:** All reporting will feed directly into the Group’s broader 2026-2030 Transition Plan, proving the bond is an integral component of our overarching decarbonization strategy.
