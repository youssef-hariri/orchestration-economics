# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The acquisition of three distinct banking franchises in Nigeria, Kenya, and Morocco creates a **USD 45 billion+ asset platform** spanning Anglophone, Francophone, and Arabic-speaking Africa. Our strategic objective is **“Unity through Differentiation”**: a federated operating model that delivers a single digital experience to mobile-first customers and cross-border corporates, while preserving the regulatory licenses, local trust, and cultural legitimacy of each acquired entity.

This document presents the **48-Month Master Integration Program (MIP)**. Execution is **conditionally feasible but elevated-risk**. Success requires three hard predicates:
1. **Kenya delivers a validated Target Operating Model (TOM) Core template by Month 26;**
2. **Board approval of a total envelope of USD 582 million** (USD 485 million base + USD 97 million contingency reserve); and
3. **Written regulatory non-objection (not mere notification) from the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) and Bank Al-Maghrib (BAM)** prior to major capital deployment.

## 1.1 Non-Negotiable Principles

1. **Regulatory License Preservation.** No action will jeopardize a banking license. Local data sovereignty, foreign-exchange controls, and supervisory precedence are absolute.
2. **Customer Continuity.** Zero forced account closures; zero involuntary re-KYC for legacy retail customers; 24-month dual-channel service availability.
3. **Digital-First Convergence.** The customer-facing digital layer unifies by Month 20; back-office core banking harmonization completes by Month 48.
4. **Independent Risk Oversight.** A **Red Team** reports directly to the Board Risk Committee with authority to halt any migration wave.

# 3. TECHNOLOGY ARCHITECTURE & MIGRATION ROADMAP

## 3.1 Guiding Architecture: “Common Face, Adaptive Core”

We will not attempt a big-bang core replacement. The target state is an **API-first, modular ecosystem** where a unified digital orchestration layer sits atop local core systems, which are migrated sequentially onto a single cloud-native core.

## 3.2 Target Core Platform Decision Matrix

| Option | Description | Scalability | Risk Profile | Time to Value | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Extend Legacy** | Retain Finacle, T24, and SBS indefinitely with interfaces only | Low; prohibitive long-term cost | Medium; escalating technical debt | Immediate | **REJECTED** |
| **Big-Bang Replacement** | Simultaneous core replacement across all three markets | High if successful | Critical; existential failure probable | 60+ months | **REJECTED** |
| **Federated Core (API Wrapper)** | Maintain disparate cores indefinitely behind a unified API layer | Medium; digital scale without operational scale | Medium; persistent reconciliation risk | 12 months | **REJECTED** |
| **Sequential Cloud Core (Temenos Transact)** | API wrapper now; migrate cores sequentially to sovereign-cloud Temenos | High; proven multi-tenant architecture | Medium-High; manageable via phased cutover | 48 months | **SELECTED** |

**Platform Designation:** **Temenos Transact** (cloud-native, containerized, API-first), deployed in independent sovereign-tenant instances in Lagos, Nairobi, and Casablanca. Source-code escrow, data portability guarantees, and termination-for-convenience clauses are non-negotiable. Encryption keys are held locally; no administrative backdoor from vendor HQ.

## 5.3 Controlled Deadlock Protocol

To prevent headquarters imperialism without paralyzing execution, the **Cultural Veto** mechanism is codified:

**Process Flow:**
1. **Trigger:** Any Country CEO may pause a Group initiative for **30 days** if it violates local norms.
2. **Negotiation:** 30-day compromise period.
3. **Escalation:** If unresolved, **Group CEO + Independent Chair of the Group Integration Committee** hold **joint tie-break authority**.
4. **Emergency Override:** For statutory/regulatory deadlines, Group CEO may override with 72-hour notice supported by local regulatory counsel.

**Controls:**
- **Cap:** Maximum **3 vetoes per annum per Country CEO** after Month 12.
- **Sunset:** Veto rights automatically expire at Month 36.

## 5.4 Independent Red Team

A **Red Team** independent of the IMO reports directly to the **Board Risk Committee**. Authority includes:
- Halting any migration wave if parallel-run monetary variance exceeds **0.01%**.
- Halting capital deployment if regulatory non-objection is withdrawn.
- Unannounced audits of API fabric reconciliation and data sovereignty compliance.
