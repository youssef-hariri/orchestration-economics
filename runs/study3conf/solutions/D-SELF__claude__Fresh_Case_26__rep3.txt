# SECTION A: STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK & CORE PRINCIPLES

## A.1 Three Pillar Strategy

### Pillar 1: AUTHENTICITY OVER COMMERCIALISM
- Cultural representation led by community insiders, not brand/agencies.
- Spiritual/theological dimensions equal to or greater than commercial messaging.
- Transparent about brand motivation; not hiding commercial intent behind cultural celebration.

### Pillar 2: MULTI-STAKEHOLDER GOVERNANCE
- Regional Cultural Review Boards with **unilateral veto authority** on cultural grounds.
- Independent consultants (not brand-affiliated) sign off on theological/community representation accuracy.
- Community feedback circles (diverse, external facilitators) validate representation before launch.

### Pillar 3: RAPID-RESPONSE ARCHITECTURE
- Real-time social-media monitoring (4-hour detection target for emerging issues).
- Pre-prepared response templates for foreseeable criticism vectors.
- Unified messaging across regions (prevent cross-regional contradiction during multi-market campaigns).
- Executive-level escalation for reputational crises (CMO/CCO decision authority within 6 hours of mainstream media pickup).

---

## A.2 Red Lines (Automatic Disqualification)

Any campaign exhibiting the following is **rejected regardless of other approvals**:

| Red Line | Definition | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **Theological Misrepresentation** | Core spiritual claim contradicts established religious text/doctrine; presents inaccurate prayer times, theological concepts, or religious history | Campaign claiming product embodies blessing; conflating different faiths; stating inaccurate Ramadan dates |
| **Sacred Symbol Appropriation** | Using sacred imagery (mandalas, henna, prayer beads, Om, cross) as pure decoration without permission, context, or reverence | Using Qur'anic verse for jingle; Ganesha as logo element without Hindu consultation |
| **Stereotypical Gender Roles (Extreme)** | Depicting women exclusively as caregivers/servers with zero representation in decision-making, earning, or leadership roles | Campaign showing only mothers/wives preparing food; zero professional women; implicit messaging women's value is family service |
| **Poverty Exploitation** | Using imagery of suffering/disadvantage to sell products; framing celebration as charity moment where brand "saves" marginalized community | Campaign showing poor families celebrating and brand positioned as their benefactor; deficits-focused narrative |
| **Caricature or Mockery** | Even "celebratory" caricature of accents, costumes, practices, or cultural stereotypes | Exaggerated accents in dialogue; oversimplified "exotic" imagery presented without context |
| **Sectarian Erasure (Sole Representation)** | Presenting one religious sect/school as sole legitimate tradition; completely erasing minority traditions within same faith | Sunni-only Ramadan campaign with zero Shia representation in Muslim-majority region; Hindu-only Diwali erasing Sikh/Jain traditions |
| **Discriminatory Messaging** | Any implicit/explicit exclusion of body types, family structures, gender identities, socioeconomic statuses, caste/class, or abilities | "Diwali is for families with children"; campaign showing only thin bodies, traditional marriages, able-bodied individuals |
| **Opportunistic Commercialism (Flagrant)** | No visible community engagement; campaign purely transactional; zero community benefit or partnership | Campaign running Ramadan sale with zero mention of spiritual dimension, community partnership, or social benefit |

**ENFORCEMENT:** Any Red Line breach triggers automatic escalation to Global Head of Cultural Strategy. Campaign halted. Rework required or cancellation. No exceptions.

## A.3 Cultural Representation Standard (CRS) Scorecard

**Minimum Score Required: 75/100**

| Dimension | Criteria | Max Score |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| **Theological/Textual Accuracy** | All spiritual/religious claims verified by ≥2 independent consultants; no contradictions to core tenets or teachings | 20 |
| **Insider Leadership & Co-Creation** | ≥50% of core creative team from faith/culture; cultural lead has documented veto power; decision-making authority, not advisory | 20 |
| **Nuance & Internal Diversity** | Campaign reflects sectarian/regional/demographic diversity; avoids monolithic stereotypes; includes minority traditions | 15 |
| **Community Benefit & Reciprocity** | Formalized community partnerships; 2–5% budget allocation to community benefit; transparent impact measurement plan | 15 |
| **Brand Positioning Clarity** | Transparent reason for engagement; authentic connection articulated; not exploitative or purely opportunistic | 15 |
| **Visual/Linguistic Ethics** | Imagery reviewed for unintended offense; language culturally appropriate; accessibility (captions, translations, alt-text) provided | 10 |
| **Historical Honesty** | No sanitized/distorted history; acknowledges complexity; acknowledges colonial/systemic contexts where relevant | 5 |

**Scoring Process:**
- Regional Cultural Lead + 2 Independent Consultants each score independently.
- Average score calculated.
- Any dimension scoring <70% of max triggers mandatory revision.
- Scores documented and archived (5-year record).

## C.2 Veto Procedures & Documentation

### Unilateral Veto (Chair Only)

**Authority:** Chair can unilaterally reject campaign if:
- Theological/mythological inaccuracy contradicts established religious text or doctrine.
- Campaign treats sacred symbols, figures, or practices disrespectfully.
- Red Line breach detected (Section A.2).

**Process:**
1. Chair issues **written veto letter** within 48 hours of review.
2. Letter specifies: (a) exact cultural/theological grounds; (b) specific campaign element(s) in violation; (c) recommended remediation path.
3. Veto letter copied to: Regional Marketing Lead, Global Head of Cultural Strategy, Regional Cultural Lead, Independent Consultants.
4. **No override without mutual agreement:** Global Head + Chair must jointly agree to override; documented in writing with override rationale.

**Timeline:** 48-hour escalation window. If override not resolved within 48 hours, campaign automatically postponed until resolved.

---

### Paired Veto (Members 1 & 2, or Chairs + Members)

**Authority:** Two board members can jointly veto if:
- Campaign perpetuates harmful stereotypes (ethnic erasure, gender stereotypes, caste bias).
- Campaign presents incomplete/distorted community image; excludes key voices.
- Cultural authenticity significantly compromised.

**Process:**
1. Both members issue **joint written veto memo** within 72 hours.
2. Memo specifies: (a) representation/authenticity concern; (b) which community voices excluded; (c) remediation path (e.g., "add Sikh Bandi Chhor Divas representation," "include South Indian languages").
3. Veto memo copied to Regional Marketing Lead, Board Chair, Global Head of Cultural Strategy.
4. **One-week remediation window:** Agency has 7 days to revise creative addressing concerns; resubmit with cultural lead sign-off.
5. **Veto reconsideration:** Within 48 hours of resubmission, members confirm concerns addressed or maintain veto.

**Timeline:** 72-hour veto window; 7-day remediation; 48-hour reconsideration. Total: Up to 10 days. Delays beyond 10 days trigger escalation to Global Head.

---

### Consultative Veto (Members 3–6)

**Authority:** Members 3–6 flag concerns; if 2+ members flag same issue, campaign cannot proceed without addressing concern.

**Process:**
1. Members flag concerns in **written memo** within 96 hours of creative review.
2. Memo specifies: (a) concern (e.g., "limited gender diversity in professional roles"); (b) specific examples; (c) recommendation.
3. **Escalation trigger:** If 2+ members flag same concern, Regional Lead must document **written response** explaining how concern was addressed or rationale for not incorporating feedback.
4. Response shared with flagging members; members can accept or escalate to Board Chair for determination.

**Timeline:** 96-hour flag window; 1-week remediation; 48-hour member acceptance. Total: Up to 10 days. If unresolved, escalates to Board Chair for final determination.

## C.3 Board Member Accountability & Evaluation

### Annual Performance Review

Each board member completes:
1. **Self-assessment** (reflecting on decision quality, engagement, adherence to charter).
2. **Peer review** (other board members evaluate collaboration, intellectual rigor).
3. **Brand assessment** (Regional Lead + Global Head evaluate timeliness, quality of guidance, prevention of backlash).
4. **Community feedback** (survey of consultants, community partners, faith leaders: "Does board represent community voice authentically?").

**Metrics Tracked:**
- **Timeliness:** % of reviews completed within deadline.
- **Decision quality:** Vetos issued only for genuine cultural grounds (not subjective/personal preference).
- **Community impact:** Post-campaign sentiment among community members whose traditions represented.
- **Backlash prevention:** Campaigns approved by board with high cultural scores have lower backlash rates (correlate CRS score with post-campaign sentiment).

### Retention & Succession

- **3-year term minimum** for permanent members (Chair, cultural expert, theologian).
- **Rotational members** (youth, diaspora, lay representative) 1–2 year terms.
- **Replacement process:** If member departs, recruitment within 30 days (interim member if extended search needed).
- **Exit process:** Departing member provides 2-week transition; successor onboarded with prior-campaign archive review.

## D.2 Rapid-Response Playbook: Phase-Based Protocols

### PHASE 1: SEED CRITICISM (Hours 0–6)

**Objective:** Detect issue early; assess severity; prepare response draft before Phase 2 hashtag mobilization.

**Monitoring Lead Responsibilities:**
1. **Detect:** Flag any 10+ negative mentions or influential critic within 1 hour of launch.
2. **Assess:** Read criticism; determine category (theological concern, gender representation, ethnic erasure, vague community benefit claim, etc.).
3. **Alert:** Contact Regional Social-Media Lead + Regional Communications Manager within 1 hour.
4. **Document:** Log issue in **Incident Tracker** (timestamp, critic/source, exact criticism, severity estimate).

**Regional
