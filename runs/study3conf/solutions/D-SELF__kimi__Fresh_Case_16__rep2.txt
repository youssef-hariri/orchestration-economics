**PRODUCT-ADAPTATION STRATEGY**
**Smart-Home Voice Assistant — Japan, Korea, Thailand & Gulf GCC Launch (2026)**
*A Pragmatic-Competence Framework for Honorific-Language Markets*

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Western voice-assistant conventions—imperative command logic, anthropomorphized persona design, individual-account architectures, and entertainment-centric feature roadmaps—generate face-threatening pragmatic failures in East Asian and Gulf markets. In these contexts, honorifics index **human social relationships**, not human–machine relations; multigenerational homes are managed by invisible operational hierarchies rather than vertical elder supremacy; and privacy expectations are rooted in domestic sanctity, hospitality ethics, and sovereign data governance rather than generic “user control.”

This strategy abandons the fiction of the assistant as a social actor or “family member.” Instead, it re-architects the product as a **pragmatically competent household tool** that avoids committing *mentsu* (Japan), *chemyeon* (Korea), *kreng jai* (Thailand), or *haji* (Gulf) violations. The assistant does not perform kinship, humility, or gendered deference. It speaks correctly, addresses users accurately, defers to configured household authority, signals trust through hardware-tangible transparency, and prioritizes capabilities that reduce social friction rather than increase performative burden.

The strategy is organized across four pillars: **Interaction Style**, **Household Roles**, **Privacy Signaling**, and **Feature Prioritization**.

## PILLAR 1: INTERACTION STYLE
*Register Calibration, Command Comfort, and Sociolinguistic Safety*

### Japan
- **Neutral-Polite Default (*Teineigo*).** The assistant speaks in standard business-polite (*teineigo*) for all users. It does not autonomously switch to *sonkeigo* (respectful) or *kenjōgo* (humble). A machine has no social status to elevate or abase; synthetic self-abasement registers as obsequious (*mendokusai*) and erodes trust.
- **No Unprompted Kinship Address.** The assistant addresses users by configured names or neutral honorifics (*-sama* / *-san* only if explicitly configured). It never infers or deploys kinship terms (*Ojii-san, Okaa-san*). Unsolicited familial address from an object violates *uchi/soto* boundaries and triggers uncanny discomfort.
- **Indirect Intention Parsing: Opt-In Only.** A “Contextual Intention” layer (*Samuku natte kimashita ne*) is available per user profile, but **disabled by default**. Treating ambient commentary as a command strips the user of *yomi* (contextual reading) agency, a core communicative value. When enabled, the assistant confirms softly before acting.
- **Commercial Hospitality Exception.** For ryokan, clinic, or retail deployments only, a statically configured *sonkeigo/kenjōgo* mode may be activated by the proprietor. This is a **tenant setting**, not an AI inference.
- **Acoustic Comfort.** Whisper-range wake-word detection and “Hush Mode” (dimmed LED + subdued response volume) are standard, reducing the performative burden in thin-walled homes and shared tatami rooms.

### Korea
- **Relationship-Indexed Register Tags.** Honorific selection is determined entirely by explicit profile tags—**Household Head, Spouse, Child, Guest, Elder**—not by acoustic inference. Biological or vocal age is never used to assign *banmal* or *jondaemal*. Korean honorifics encode **social relationship and relative status**, not age.
- **Default Unknown-Voice Protocol.** All unrecognized voices receive *haeyo-che* (standard polite), the maximally safe unmarked register. Downshifting to *banmal* (intimate/plain) requires **bilateral opt-in**: explicit consent from both the speaker and the household manager during profile creation.
- **Context-Gated Personas.** Selectable response styles are limited to “Respectful” and “Friendly.” *Aegyo* (cute/playful) personas are **geo-fenced from formal living rooms and elder-facing contexts**; they are available only in child-designated spaces or after explicit bilateral pairing.
- **Konglish Command Acceptance.** An NLU layer accepts English nouns embedded in Korean syntax (*“Light off haejwo”*), acknowledging heavy code-switching norms without imposing foreign-language fluency requirements.
- **Accessibility, Not Hierarchy.** Simplified wake-word sensitivity for elder voices is framed as **motor-accessibility support**, not Confucian deference.

### Thailand
- **Fixed Service Register; No Gender Mirroring.** Thai polite particles (*khráp/khâ*) index the **speaker’s** identity, not the addressee’s. The assistant adopts a **fixed, consistent gender identity** (or formal gender-neutral register) at device setup and uses the corresponding particle set permanently. It never switches particles based on user voice detection.
- **Addressee-Oriented Titles.** User addressing prioritizes addressee honorifics: *Khun* (general respectful), *Than* (high respect), and *Phran* (monastic). Correct title usage matters more than particle gymnastics.
- **Royal & Buddhist Deflection Protocol.** The assistant **never synthesizes content** about the monarchy, Buddhism, or Thai politics. For any query touching these domains, it delivers a polite, hard-coded redirect to official or ecclesiastical sources. Monk-chant audio and Dharma content must originate from **licensed human recordings** of certified authorities.
- **Kreng Jai Mode.** The assistant never contradicts, corrects, or volunteers information in front of guests or elders unless explicitly queried. Error correction is routed privately to the requester via smartphone notification, haptic feedback, or a personal light signal only.
- **Warm Prosody.** TTS tuning favors a *jai-yen* (cool-hearted) cadence: patient, non-interruptive, and non-corrective.

### Gulf (GCC)
- **Dialect-First NLU with Diglossic Boundaries.** Primary natural-language understanding operates in Gulf Arabic (*Khaliji*). MSA is retained for fallback and pan-Arab expat coverage. **Religious audio (Quran, *Adhan*, *dua*) is exclusively human-recited**, sourced from licensed *qaris* with proper *tajweed*. Neural TTS synthesis of Quranic verse is prohibited. All religious audio is badged *“Certified Human Recitation.”*
- **User-Configured Titles Only.** Titles (*Sheikh, Sheikha, Hajj, Hajja*) are **manually entered by the user** during onboarding. The assistant never infers religious or tribal honorifics. Default address uses *Sayyid/Sayyida* (Mr./Ms.) or configured first name only.
- **Segregated Voice Persona by Zone.** During household zone setup, the installer selects a male-voiced persona for *majlis* and public reception spaces and a female-voiced persona for family salons and women’s quarters. This is a **cultural-prerequisite configuration**, not an aesthetic preference.
- **Register Switching.** *Majlis* mode employs fuller MSA/fusha responses to index formality with guests and elders. Family mode uses warm Khaliji colloquial.
- **Code-Switching Engine.** Heavy Arabic-English mixing (*“Yalla turn on the AC”*) is supported via an in-house Gulf *Arabizi* parser for both voice and text.

## PILLAR 2: HOUSEHOLD ROLES
*Operational Authority, Intergenerational Harmony, and Face-Safe Execution*

### Japan
- **Household-Manager Default Admin.** Admin rights default to the adult who purchases, installs, and manages the home—typically the middle-generation wife or daughter-in-law (*yome*)—not to the eldest resident. Elders often do not desire the cognitive burden of IoT management. The household manager retains invisible operational control, with optional elder dashboard access that can be granted or revoked.
- **Elder Preference Nudge (Not Override).** When intergenerational conflicts arise (e.g., a grandchild requests loud music while a grandparent is present), the assistant proposes spatial compromises—**“Shall I route audio to the child’s room only?”**—rather than unilaterally denying the younger user. It never contradicts a child in front of a grandparent.
- **Kinship-Aware Device Orchestration.** The system optimizes for the household manager’s workflows: grocery-list tagging by store aisle, integration with *Cookpad*, and *ofuro* bath scheduling. Elder profiles receive simplified, high-contrast interfaces and optional voice-only summaries.

### Korea
- **Intergenerational Harmony Mode.** Household dynamics are framed as **intergenerational harmony**, not “Confucian protocol.” Voice profiles are tagged by social role (*Halabeoji, Eomma, etc.*) for functional routing only.
- **Configurable Household Dashboard Templates.** Log visibility and control templates are selected at setup by the household manager:
  - **Traditional Extended:** Elders and household head receive visibility into shared functions; the household manager retains hidden admin override.
  - **Nuclear + Elders:** Adult children retain primary admin; elders receive limited guest-style access.
  - **Dual-Income Egalitarian:** Equal adult access to shared logs, with private lockable profiles.
- **Myeoneuri Operational Sovereignty.** The daughter-in-law or primary home manager possesses **invisible admin access**—the “power behind the throne.” The UI displays deference to elders, but she can lock her own voice logs, medical reminders, and personal queries from mother-in-law (*shimmo*) oversight, preserving autonomy without surface-level conflict.
- **Study-Room Authority.** During designated *hagwon* hours, child profiles trigger Study Mode: entertainment queries are blocked, educational Q&A prioritized, and focus reports routed to the household manager (not broadcast to elders by default).

### Thailand
- **Phu Yai Deference Without Contradiction.** Elder voices receive priority for climate and media control, but the assistant never contradicts a younger family member in front of them. If an elder issues a command that affects another’s space, the assistant executes gracefully or seeks private confirmation from the household manager via app.
- **Temple Quiet & Guest Hospitality.** “Monk/Guest Mode” suppresses all non-essential audio and switches to visual/light-only responses when monks visit or during meditation. The system auto-detects *tak baat* (morning alms) and temple-visit calendar entries to enter quiet states proactively.
- **Extended-Family Scalability.** Support for 8+ voice profiles (aunts, uncles, cousins) and a one-touch “Guest” profile with 24-hour auto-purge. Guest data deletion is marketed through the Buddhist framing of *“letting go.”*

### Gulf (GCC)
- **Zoned Household Architecture.** Devices are mapped to physical zones: *Majlis*, Family Salon, Women’s Quarters, Service Access Areas, and Staff Zones. Profiles are restricted by zone and functional role.
- **Role-Based Operational Access.** Operational staff profiles are limited to device-control commands (lights, AC, intercom) and are **excluded from family calendars, communications, camera feeds, and financial functions**. This is framed as **family-data privacy protection**, not containment. Dignified terminology (“Operational Role,” “Service Access”) replaces “sandboxing.”
- **Female Digital Sovereignty.** Female family members independently control their own voice-profile data and bedroom-zone devices. By default, no other family member (including the Head of Household) can review their voice logs or query history. This is framed through the Islamic ethic of domestic sanctity and bedroom privacy (*hijab* of the home), not Western individualism.
- **Ramadan Coordination.** Suhoor/Iftar timers and volume controls respect staggered generational sleep schedules. Elder wake times take precedence for morning prayer alerts.

## PILLAR 3: PRIVACY SIGNALING
*Trust Through Tangibility, Sovereignty, and Culturally Coded Transparency*

### Japan
- **Omotenashi Hardware Transparency.** A visible amber LED ring indicates live mic; a physical camera shutter and a **tactile mechanical mute button** with an audible click are mandatory. Software-only muting is distrusted.
- **Data Localization Pledge.** Voice data is processed and stored exclusively in Japan (Tokyo/Osaka cloud regions). Packaging and setup explicitly state this, co-branded with a trusted local governance partner (e.g., NTT docomo, SoftBank).
- **Phrase-Level Deletion & Digital Minimalism.** *“Kore wo sakujo shite”* (“Delete this”) erases the previous utterance immediately. Auto-purge settings (daily/weekly) are framed as *digital minimalism*, aligning with cultural de-cluttering values.

### Korea
- **Hardware-Trust Architecture.** A physical disconnect switch for the microphone array and a camera shutter with hardware interlock are required.
- **Anti-Molka Signaling.** A visible hardware LED indicates recording status and **cannot be disabled by software**. This directly addresses national trauma around hidden recording.
- **On-Device ASR & Cloud Visualization.** Basic commands (lights, volume, timers) execute locally. Cloud transmission occurs only for complex queries, with a UI visualization distinguishing local vs. remote processing. KISA compliance certification is displayed on-box.
- **Configurable Log Access.** Log visibility follows the household template selected during setup. In egalitarian templates, both spouses share access; in traditional templates, the household manager controls visibility, protecting *myeoneuri* privacy.

### Thailand
- **Soft, Trust-Building Signals.** Audio chimes use gentle, culturally resonant tones (xylophone or *phin* pluck); visual indicators employ warm gold/white. Harsh beeps and aggressive red lights are avoided.
- **Localized Governance Partnership.** Data custody is co-branded with an established Thai conglomerate or banking partner. Data resides in Bangkok or Singapore with ASEAN cross-border assurance.
- **Buddhist Ethic Framing.** Privacy is marketed as *“respect”*; data deletion as *“letting go.”* Legalistic GDPR-style notices are secondary to spiritual-framed trust narratives.
- **Guest “Forget Me.”** *“Luem khun khun nee”* (“Forget this guest”) instantly purges guest interactions, critical in a hospitality-centric culture.

### Gulf (GCC)
- **Maximum Hardware Assurance.** Camera-free SKUs are offered for bedrooms. Microphones require a physical kill switch or detachable module. “Always-listening” in women’s quarters is **disabled by default** and enabled only by explicit, renewed consent of the female occupant herself.
- **Sovereign Cloud Guarantee.** Voice data never leaves GCC sovereign infrastructure (AWS Bahrain, Azure UAE, STC Cloud). NESA (UAE) and SDAIA (KSA) compliance certifications are displayed prominently in Arabic on packaging.
- **Modest Signaling.** Visual indicators are subtle and directional, avoiding intrusive glowing lights that feel surveillant in private family spaces.
- **Silent/Text Mode.** Full operation via smartphone app or touch panel is available for rooms where audible voice interaction is intrusive or immodest.

## CROSS-MARKET IMPLEMENTATION GOVERNANCE

1. **Regional PM Pods with Veto Power**
   Each market has a dedicated in-market Product Lead with veto authority over global feature-parity demands. What ships in Frankfurt or Seattle must not block Japan’s static *teineigo* default, Korea’s bilateral *banmal* opt-in, or the Gulf’s human-only recitation rule.

2. **Pragmatic Competence & Face-Risk QA Guild**
   Native-linguist QA teams in Tokyo, Seoul, Bangkok, and Dubai test not merely translation accuracy, but **social appropriateness and face-risk avoidance**. Test suites include simulated multigenerational conflict scenarios, guest-arrival sequences, and religious-context queries to verify deflection protocols.

3. **Anti-Anthropomorphism Design Charter**
   The assistant is never marketed or engineered as a “family member,” “friend,” or “servant.” It is a **domestic tool** that achieves cultural competence through grammatical accuracy, configurable deference, and silence at the right moments—not through performed personality.

4. **Ethics Review for Role-Based Access**
   All access restrictions for operational staff or zone-based profiles undergo regional ethics review to ensure they protect family privacy **without reifying exploitative labor frameworks**. Language, UI copy, and system architecture must preserve the dignity of all home occupants.
