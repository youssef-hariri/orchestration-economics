# POLISHED SUPPLIER ONBOARDING AND INTEGRATION STRATEGY
## For Mexican, Moroccan, and Vietnamese Supply Base Expansion

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following tariff realignments and export-control expansions in 2025, this consumer-electronics manufacturer is rapidly diversifying its supply base from East Asia to Mexico, Morocco, and Vietnam. Early audits reveal misunderstandings about specifications, delivery commitments, and quality expectations—rooted not in incompetence but in unfamiliar business customs, quality traditions, and communication styles.

**This strategy provides a practical, 18-month phased approach to onboarding and integrating suppliers across three culturally distinct regions while ensuring quality, delivery reliability, and relationship stability.**

### Core Principles
1. **"Trust, but document and verify at every stage."** Formal processes provide clarity; relationship investment drives commitment.
2. **Customize every integration lever to regional norms while maintaining transparent, verifiable quality standards.** One-size-fits-all fails; thoughtful differentiation succeeds.
3. **Early wins establish credibility.** Pilot phases (Months 3–8) are relationship-building and process-validation; full-scale production (Months 9–18) consolidates maturity.

### Key Outcomes (Month 18 Target)
- ✓ ≥95% on-time delivery (tiered by region: Mexico 95%, Morocco 90%, Vietnam 90%)
- ✓ Defect rate ≤0.5% (critical characteristics); ≤1.5% overall
- ✓ Cpk ≥1.33 for all critical processes
- ✓ Zero critical audit findings (after Month 12)
- ✓ Supplier NPS (Net Promoter Score) ≥50 across all three regions

# SECTION 1: SUPPLIER SELECTION AND QUALIFICATION FRAMEWORK

## 1.1 Tiered Selection Criteria (35% Strategic Fit, 30% Quality, 20% Organizational, 15% Compliance)

| Criterion | Mexico | Morocco | Vietnam |
|-----------|--------|---------|---------|
| **Capacity** | Production volume + 15–20% headroom for 18–36 months | Same | Same |
| **Product Category Match** | PCB assembly, metal stamping, plastic injection | Same; textile/pharma precedents valued | Same; manufacturing hub position advantageous |
| **Geographic/Logistics** | USMCA proximity (2–4 week lead time) | EU/Africa access (4–6 weeks) | Southeast Asia manufacturing hub (4–8 weeks) |
| **Certifications** | ISO 9001; IATF 16949 (if automotive) | ISO 9001; EU standards compliance | ISO 9001; export experience |
| **Financial Stability** | 3+ years audited financials; no restructuring | Same | Same; verify parent company stability |
| **Quality-System Maturity** | SPC infrastructure; Cpk ≥1.33 demonstrated | ISO 9001 baseline; SPC less common | ISO 9001 baseline; SPC emerging |
| **English/Local Language** | Bilingual quality lead; Spanish support | French/English; formal communication expected | English at operations level; Vietnamese translation available |
| **Cultural Fit** | Relationship-driven; values long-term partnership | Formal protocols; written documentation preferred | Hierarchy-respected; rapid learning orientation; high-frequency communication preferred |

## 1.2 Four-Phase Selection Process (Weeks 1–16)

### Phase 1: Desk Research & Initial Qualification (Weeks 1–4)
- Identify 8–12 candidates per region using Thomas Net, Alibaba, local chambers of commerce
- Screen on Tier 1 (Strategic Fit) and Tier 4 (Compliance): disqualify sanctions flags, financial distress
- **Deliverable**: Shortlist of 4–6 per region

### Phase 2: On-Site Assessment (Weeks 5–12)
- 2-person OEM team (Procurement + Quality Engineer) visits each shortlisted supplier for 2 days
- **Day 1**: Leadership discussion, strategic fit, compliance review, factory tour
- **Day 2**: Technical deep-dive (process capability, test labs, material traceability, communication trial)
- **Scoring Matrix**: Weighted evaluation across all Tiers 1–4
- **Deliverable**: Ranked list of 2–3 finalists per region

### Phase 3: Finalist Negotiation & Pilot Agreement (Weeks 13–16)
- Engage top 2–3 per region in **Lead-Time Pilot Test**: Supplier produces small test batch (100–200 units) within stated lead time
  - If on-time: Advance to formal supply agreement
  - If delayed >3 days: Require corrective action plan or de-select
- Execute **Partner Charter** (2-3 page joint commitment document signed by both CEOs) outlining:
  - Mutual vision (long-term partnership, not transactional)
  - Quality, delivery, and improvement commitments
  - Communication protocol and dispute-resolution approach
  - Language for all formal communications
