**EXECUTIVE BRIEF**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Executive Strategy & Restructuring Committee  
**DATE:** October 24, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Global Franchise Restructuring & Macroeconomic Risk Mitigation Strategy  

---

### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
In 2026, our global quick-service restaurant (QSR) network faces severe macroeconomic headwinds in key emerging markets (Turkey, Egypt, Nigeria, Indonesia, and Brazil). Franchisees in these regions are being squeezed by currency depreciation and localized inflation, rendering our legacy fixed-USD royalty model structurally unsustainable. Concurrently, we are witnessing the rise of adversarial, collective franchisee bargaining. 

To preserve long-term brand economics and protect the corporate balance sheet, we must transition from a **rigid, fixed-USD extraction model** to a **dynamic, macroeconomically grounded risk-sharing framework**. This brief outlines a legally defensible, financially prudent restructuring strategy that aligns franchisor revenue with franchisee reality, neutralizes collective bargaining threats, and strictly maintains our independent contractor legal firewall.

---

### **STRATEGIC CONTEXT & CORE CHALLENGES**
*   **Macroeconomic Squeeze:** Severe currency depreciation and inflation in five key markets are destroying franchisee margins, threatening deferred maintenance and brand degradation.
*   **Balance Sheet Risk:** Legacy fixed-USD royalties transfer 100% of the macroeconomic risk to the franchisee, inevitably leading to unit closures and system-wide revenue leakage.
*   **Collective Action:** Franchisee associations are organizing collectively, threatening unified strikes and litigation.
*   **Stable-Market Subsidization:** While emerging markets struggle, company-owned stores in stable-currency markets remain highly profitable, providing the capital necessary to fund this strategic transition.

---

### **PILLAR 1: DYNAMIC ROYALTY & MACROECONOMIC RESTRUCTURING**
*Objective: Align franchisor revenue with local economic realities utilizing auditable metrics and automated triggers to prevent prolonged revenue leakage.*

*   **The "Collar and Trigger" Royalty Conversion:** 
    Replace fixed USD royalties with a percentage of local gross sales, converted at the daily spot rate. To protect against violent, sudden devaluations, we will implement a **10% volatility collar**. If a local currency depreciates by >10% against the USD within a 30-day window, an automatic "trigger" resets the royalty conversion baseline, preventing multi-month lag losses.
*   **Proprietary QSR Input Index & "Macro-Hardship Waivers":** 
    We will maintain a strict **Gross Sales** royalty basis to ensure absolute auditability and avoid the fraud risks of EBITDA-tiering. To address inflation, we will deploy a proprietary, independently verified QSR Input Index (tracking local meat, dairy, oil, and packaging costs), bypassing artificially suppressed government CPI data. If this index spikes >20% Year-over-Year, an automatic, temporary 6-month royalty reduction (e.g., 5% to 3.5%) triggers, reverting automatically when the index normalizes.

---

### **PILLAR 2: FRANCHISE FINANCING & SUPPLY CHAIN (BALANCE SHEET PROTECTION)**
*Objective: Provide liquidity and cost certainty to franchisees without assuming uncollateralized local-currency banking risks or subsidizing exorbitant forward premiums.*

*   **Transparent "Cost-Pass-Through" FX Hedging:** 
    The franchisor will utilize its strong balance sheet to execute centralized forward contracts for essential imported supply chain goods. However, the *actual cost of the forward points* (the interest rate parity premium) will be transparently passed through to franchisees. Franchisees are given a monthly choice: pay the volatile spot-market rate, or pay the locked forward rate inclusive of the premium. This secures supply chain stability without bleeding corporate cash.
*   **USD-Denominated Equipment Leasing with Local Revaluation:** 
    To fund essential CapEx without acting as an unsecured local-currency bank, the franchisor will purchase equipment in USD and lease it strictly in **USD**. Franchisees will pay in local currency, subject to a **monthly local currency revaluation clause** tied to the official central bank exchange rate, ensuring the physical assets retain their USD value.
*   **Subordinated Debt for Royalty Deferrals:** 
    To assist franchisees facing acute liquidity crises, we will allow deferrals of up to 30% of royalty obligations. To avoid triggering complex Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) and securities laws in markets like Indonesia and Brazil, these deferrals will convert into **subordinated promissory notes** (bearing below-market interest) secured by a secondary lien on the franchisee’s physical store assets, rather than equity conversion.

---

### **PILLAR 3: LEGAL COMPLIANCE & COLLECTIVE BARGAINING TACTICS**
*Objective: Neutralize adversarial franchisee associations and execute contract changes while strictly maintaining the independent contractor legal firewall and complying with local civil laws.*

*   **"Mutual Consent" Addendums via Give-to-Get:** 
    Unilaterally imposing new royalty models violates franchise laws in our target markets. We will execute a "System Restructuring Addendum" through voluntary, mutual consent. The franchisor will offer a tangible, immediate benefit (e.g., a 3-month royalty holiday or localized marketing subsidy) in exchange for the franchisee’s signed consent to the new dynamic models.
*   **Strictly Advisory Global Franchise Advisory Council (GFAC):** 
    We will institutionalize the franchisee associations by forming the GFAC, but it will be structured as a **strictly advisory, non-binding body**. The GFAC charter will explicitly state it has zero fiduciary or operational decision-making power, preserving the franchisor's absolute final say on brand standards and maintaining the independent contractor legal firewall.
*   **"Standstill Agreements" over Coercive Litigation Waivers:** 
    Demanding franchisees drop active lawsuits in exchange for relief constitutes "economic duress" under local civil law. Instead, we will negotiate **Standstill Agreements**, wherein associations agree to *pause (toll)* active or threatened collective litigation for 12 to 18 months while the new economic model is implemented. If the model fails, litigation rights automatically revive.
*   **Documented "Objective Macro-Justifications":** 
    To prevent a unified global franchisee front and defend against local antitrust investigations, every localized concession will be strictly documented against our objective, third-party QSR Input Index, proving that pricing differences are driven by macroeconomic necessity, not arbitrary discrimination.

---

### **EXECUTION ROADMAP**

**Phase 1: Triage, Legal Structuring & Communication (Months 1-2)**
*   Declare a "System Health Initiative," acknowledging macroeconomic pain while emphasizing a legally compliant path forward.
*   Draft the "System Restructuring Addendum," "Standstill Agreements," and the GFAC Advisory Charter with local legal counsel across all five target countries.
*   Announce the formation of the strictly advisory GFAC.

**Phase 2: Negotiation & Pilot (Months 3-6)**
*   Initiate "Give-to-Get" negotiations with local associations to secure voluntary signatures on the Addendums and Standstill Agreements.
*   Pilot the "Collar and Trigger" royalty, the Proprietary Input Index, and the Cost-Pass-Through FX hedge in two initial markets (e.g., Brazil and Indonesia).

**Phase 3: Global Rollout & Capitalization (Months 7-12)**
*   Roll out the USD-denominated Equipment Leasing program, capitalized by excess cash flows from stable-currency markets.
*   Transition all remaining franchisees in the five target countries to the new dynamic models, achieving 100% system alignment under the mutually consented framework.

---

### **STRATEGIC IMPACT & CONCLUSION**
This restructuring strategy successfully balances franchisee relief with strict financial discipline. By shifting to a dynamic, macroeconomically grounded model, we stop the bleeding of our franchisee base without destroying the corporate balance sheet. Passing through the true cost of FX hedges, utilizing USD-denominated leases, and relying on auditable Gross Sales protects our corporate economics. 

Furthermore, by securing mutual consent, utilizing standstill agreements, and strictly limiting the GFAC to an advisory role, we transform our most significant operational and legal threats into aligned partners. This approach ensures the preservation of our long-term brand equity, operational standards, and global profitability in an increasingly volatile macroeconomic landscape. 

**Recommendation:** The Executive Strategy Committee requests the Board of Directors' approval to initiate Phase 1 of this restructuring roadmap immediately.
