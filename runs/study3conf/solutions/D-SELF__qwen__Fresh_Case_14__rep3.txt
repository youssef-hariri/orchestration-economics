**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Executive Board and C-Suite Leadership  
**FROM:** Corporate Treasury & Sustainable Finance Task Force  
**DATE:** October 24, 2023  
**SUBJECT:** Strategic Financing Plan: 2026 Multi-Jurisdictional Green & Transition Bond Issuance  

---

### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
In 2026, the Group will execute a comprehensive decarbonization retrofit program across its European, North African, and Southeast Asian assets. To finance this initiative, we will issue a multi-tranche sustainable debt package. Given the highly fragmented regulatory landscape—characterized by divergent taxonomies (EU, Gulf, ASEAN) and inconsistent internal emissions data—a single, rigid financing framework poses severe greenwashing and regulatory risks.

This document outlines a **Bifurcated, Legally Binding, and Financially Quantified Financing Strategy**. By separating our issuance into strict "Green" and "Transition" frameworks, financializing our data remediation roadmap, and translating ESG metrics into hard credit-enhancing metrics, the Group will successfully access diverse, global capital pools while fortifying its reputation as a tier-one sustainable issuer.

---

### **1. BIFURCATED ISSUANCE & STRICT TAXONOMY ALIGNMENT**
To protect the integrity of our "green" label while accommodating regional transition needs, the issuance will be split into two distinct legal frameworks. This structural bifurcation isolates our most stringent EU-aligned assets from more flexible regional activities, neutralizing greenwashing risks.

**The Green Bond Framework (Tranches A & C)**
*   **Tranche A: EuGB-Aligned Eurobond (Targeting EU & Global Dark-Green Funds)**
    *   **Standard:** Explicitly aligned with the **EU Green Bond Standard (EuGB)**, requiring 100% EU Taxonomy alignment.
    *   **Verification:** Mandates pre- and post-issuance reviews by an independent, ESMA-registered EuGB External Reviewer.
    *   **Use of Proceeds:** Strictly ring-fenced for European plant retrofits and advanced SE Asia/North Africa projects that pass strict Technical Screening Criteria and Do No Significant Harm (DNSH) requirements.
*   **Tranche C: ASEAN-Aligned Green Bond (Targeting SE Asian & Regional Investors)**
    *   **Standard:** Aligned with the **ASEAN Green Bond Standards** and the **Singapore-Asia Taxonomy**.
    *   **Use of Proceeds:** SE Asia plant retrofits, including early coal-phase-down and energy efficiency projects permitted under Asian frameworks. 
    *   *Strategic Note:* To prevent ESG cross-contamination, Tranche C will be strictly marketed to regional or specialized transition funds, ensuring European/global dark-green investors are not inadvertently exposed to controversial assets.

**The Transition Bond Framework (Tranche B)**
*   **Tranche B: Regional Transition USD/AED Bond (Targeting Gulf & North African Investors)**
    *   **Standard:** Issued under a separate **Transition Bond Framework** aligned with the **ICMA Transition Guidelines** and the **UAE Green Finance Framework**.
    *   **Use of Proceeds:** Deep energy efficiency, industrial electrification, and renewable energy integration in North Africa and the Middle East. 
    *   *Risk Mitigation:* Coal-to-gas switching is **strictly excluded** from all tranches to prevent global institutional divestment and protect the Group's broader ESG credibility.

---

### **2. EMISSIONS MEASUREMENT & FINANCIALIZED DATA REMEDIATION**
Inconsistent emissions data across older assets is a critical vulnerability. To satisfy the strict quantitative requirements of the EuGB and manage data risk, we are upgrading our measurement strategy to rely on conservative mapping backed by financial guarantees.

**Conservative Emissions Factor Mapping**
*   Physical metrics (e.g., MWh saved) are insufficient for EU Taxonomy compliance. For Tier 2 and Tier 3 assets, we will develop a localized **Emissions Factor Mapping** using the most conservative, publicly available grid emission factors.
*   To satisfy EU assurance requirements, we will apply a built-in **20% "uncertainty discount"** (reporting only 80% of the calculated tCO2e reduction). This ensures reported impacts remain conservative and defensible despite baseline data imperfections.

**The "Data Remediation Step-Up" Clause**
*   The 24-month data remediation roadmap is fortified by a legally binding financial mechanism embedded directly into the bond terms.
*   If Tier 3 assets are not upgraded to Tier 1 or Tier 2 via IoT/smart metering within 24 months of issuance, it will trigger a mandatory **Coupon Step-Up of +5 basis points** OR a **Mandatory Reallocation of Proceeds** to fully verified Tier 1 projects.
*   *Execution Safeguard:* To mitigate the execution risk of reallocation, Treasury will maintain a pre-vetted, oversized pipeline of Tier 1 projects to ensure seamless capital absorption if the trigger is activated.

---

### **3. STAKEHOLDER GOVERNANCE & CRA ENGAGEMENT**
We will balance global ESG integrity with local distribution realities, while providing Credit Rating Agencies (CRAs) with the hard financial data required to adjust credit outlooks.

**The Green Steering Committee**
*   To optimize local distribution without compromising global baselines, we will establish a **Green Steering Committee** comprising the Global Lead Structuring Agent, regional bookrunners (Gulf and Asia), and the Group’s CFO/CSO. 
*   This committee will ensure local market nuances (e.g., specific Gulf investor yield preferences) are integrated into the tranches efficiently.

**The "Climate Financial Materiality Memo" for CRAs**
*   To secure credit-enhancing benefits, we will provide CRAs with a quantitative memo translating ESG metrics into hard financial risk mitigation:
    *   **EU CBAM Mitigation:** Exact quantification of how retrofits reduce Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism liabilities.
    *   **Carbon Pricing Exposure:** Modeled reduction in exposure to regional and global carbon pricing.
    *   **Financial ROI:** Detailed payback periods, IRR, and direct EBITDA impact.
*   *Objective:* Frame the issuance not as an ESG cost center, but as a direct, quantifiable hedge against future cash-flow erosion.

**Cost-Optimized Verification**
*   The extensive verification apparatus (EuGB reviewers, SPOs, satellite verifiers) will be strictly managed to ensure administrative costs do not erode the financial benefits of the "greenium" (pricing advantage). We will leverage bundled verification services where possible.

---

### **4. INVESTOR CREDIBILITY, SCOPE 3 & ADVANCED VERIFICATION**
To achieve radical transparency and satisfy the stringent 2026 CSRD requirements, we will implement next-generation verification and comprehensive scope reporting.

**Satellite Baseline + Engineering Delta Verification**
*   For Tier 2 and Tier 3 assets, internal engineering models will be cross-checked using **third-party satellite monitoring** (e.g., ClimateTrace). 
*   *Methodology:* Recognizing that satellites cannot isolate the specific emissions *delta* of a targeted retrofit, we will use satellite data to establish a verified top-down Scope 1 baseline, paired with our engineering data to calculate the precise reduction delta. This dual-verification will be published in the annual impact report.

**Scope 3 Integration & Downstream Impact**
*   The frameworks will explicitly state how Scope 1 and 2 retrofits contribute to the Group's broader Scope 3 targets.
*   We will quantify how producing lower-carbon intermediate goods reduces the "embodied carbon" for downstream customers (Scope 3, Category 11). The annual report will include a formal Scope 3 baseline and reduction target.

**Investor Advisory Panel & "Comply or Explain" Reporting**
*   A pre-issuance **Investor Advisory Panel** (comprising 4-5 anchor ESG funds) will co-create the framework, transforming potential critics into committed buyers.
*   The annual **"Comply or Explain" Allocation Report** will explicitly detail data quality tiers, the applied uncertainty discounts, and the results of the satellite/engineering verification.

---

### **5. EXECUTION TIMELINE TO 2026 ISSUANCE**

*   **Q3 2025:** Finalize bifurcated Green and Transition Frameworks. Appoint the Global Lead Agent, establish the Green Steering Committee, and form the Investor Advisory Panel.
*   **Q4 2025:** Draft the "Climate Financial Materiality Memo" and begin preliminary CRA engagement. Commission the EuGB External Reviewer and SPO providers.
*   **Q1 2026:** Conduct internal data audit. Implement the Data Quality Tiering system. Accelerate IoT/metering upgrades in Tier 3 plants to avoid the Step-Up penalty. Ensure the Tier 1 reallocation pipeline is fully vetted.
*   **Q2 2026:** Publish the Green and Transition Frameworks. Obtain the EuGB pre-issuance review, SPO, and Transition SPO. Conduct CRA and investor roadshows.
*   **Q3 2026:** Execute the multi-tranche issuance (EuGB Eurobond, ASEAN Green Bond, Transition USD/AED Bond).
*   **Q4 2026:** Publish the first Allocation and Impact Reports, featuring conservative tCO2e mapping, Scope 3 downstream impacts, and satellite/engineering verification.

---

### **STRATEGIC OUTLOOK**
By bifurcating our frameworks, legally binding our data remediation roadmap, and quantifying financial materiality for rating agencies, this strategy transforms a highly fragmented and risky scenario into a robust financing plan. The Group will not only successfully navigate the complex 2026 sustainable debt landscape but will also establish a definitive competitive advantage, securing lower-cost capital and reinforcing our position as a global leader in industrial decarbonization.
