**Tri-Regional Supplier Onboarding & Integration Strategy**
*Mexico · Morocco · Vietnam*

**Consumer-Electronics Manufacturer | Post-2025 Nearshoring Deployment**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Following the 2025 tariff realignments and expanded U.S. BIS export-control regimes, this manufacturer is executing a rapid, strategic decoupling from a concentrated East-Asian supply base. The new tri-regional footprint—**Mexico** (Americas serving), **Morocco** (Euro-Atlantic bridge), and **Vietnam** (Asia-Pacific resilience)—reduces tariff exposure and geopolitical concentration risk. It also introduces acute onboarding friction: mismatched quality ontologies, high-context communication failures, nascent trust deficits, and the operational physics of capacity-constrained nearshoring.

This document establishes a **GLOCAL Phased Maturity Model**: globally standardized governance, compliance, and technical architecture, activated through region-specific cultural, linguistic, and operational protocols. Rather than pursuing an operationally reckless 90-day “partnership,” we move from **transactional containment** to **integrated co-manufacturing partnership** through a disciplined, legally compliant, and commercially feasible timeline.

**Core Design Principles**
- **Staggered Deployment:** Human and capital assets are concentrated sequentially, not diluted simultaneously across three continents.
- **One Quality Language, Local Dialect:** A Unified Supplier Quality Manual (USQM) and single SQE rubric, localized only in execution language, not in standard.
- **Verified Trust, Not Declared Trust:** Measurable milestones replace anthropological aspirations.
- **Dignified Exit Economics:** Supplier transparency improves when failure is governed by a humane, pre-negotiated off-ramp.

## 2. Strategic Deployment Framework

### 2.1 Phased Maturity Model

Post-2025 capacity constraints, bureaucratic latency, and electronics NPI velocity make a 90-day “partnership” commercially reckless. The following phases align supplier obligations with OEM support.

| Phase | Timeline | Objective | External Designation |
|-------|----------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Containment & Cadence** | Days 1–90 | OTIF stabilization, boundary-sample lock, defect-escape containment, union/regulatory alignment, sub-tier mapping. | Integrated Supplier Development Program |
| **Process Integration** | Days 90–180 | Harmonized SPC, joint FMEA, localized metrology correlation, IPC certification, data-sovereignty compliance, validated 8D/SCAR workflow. | Qualified Partner |
| **Strategic Co-Investment** | Days 180+ | VMI, joint P&L targets, co-developed tooling, long-term forecasting. | Co-Manufacturing Partner |

**External Vocabulary Rule:** The term “partnership” is reserved for the Day-180 contract amendment ceremony. All external-facing communications prior to Day 180 shall use only “Integrated Supplier Development Program.”

### 2.2 Sequenced Launch: The Tiger Team Rotation

Simultaneous Week-1 blitzes across three continents exhaust bilingual, technically deep talent. A single **Onboarding Tiger Team** rotates through regions, building playbook muscle that is then transferred to permanently hired regional anchors.

| Region | Launch Window | Strategic Rationale |
|--------|---------------|---------------------|
| **Vietnam** | Week 1–4 | Asia-Pacific resilience anchor; fastest NPI velocity; establishes APQP-Lite and data-sovereignty templates for later regions. |
| **Mexico** | Week 5–8 | Americas proximity; benefits from Vietnam-tuned APQP-Lite playbook; allows union-listening and customs pre-positioning. |
| **Morocco** | Week 9–12 | Euro-Atlantic bridge; longest bureaucratic lead time; launches after Mexican legal templates are stress-tested. |

By Day 60, each region retains a **permanently hired local SQE anchor** (Moroccan national in Casablanca, Vietnamese national in HCMC/Hanoi, bilingual Mexican national in Monterrey/Querétaro) who assumes ownership from the rotating tiger team.

## 3. Unified Quality-Management Architecture

The departing East-Asian hub operated on dense SPC, poka-yoke, and zero-defect documentation. Rather than imposing mismatched regional dialects (APQP, VDA, Samsung-style), the OEM publishes a **Unified Supplier Quality Manual (USQM)** with a single 0–100 SQE scoring rubric, localized only in *language of execution*.

### 3.1 The Unified Technical Baseline

| Element | Global Standard | Regional Execution |
|---------|----------------|--------------------|
| **Scorecard** | Single SQE index: OTIF 30%, PPM 25%, Audit Score 20%, Responsiveness 15%, Compliance 10%. | Local-language dashboards; risk-matrix appendix mandatory for all reviews. |
| **Risk Visibility** | Unrolled **Probability × Severity matrix** by category: solder-joint integrity, logistics, documentation, sub-tier governance, ESG. | Prevents an 80-score Morocco (strong paperwork, weak SPC) from masking the same risk as an 80-score Vietnam (strong SPC, weak documentation). |
| **Electronics-First Standards** | **IPC-A-610 + J-STD-001** certification required for all line leaders in PCBA/Box-Build. | Automotive analogies permitted only for logistics and traceability, never for solder-joint criteria. |
| **Metrology Correlation** | MSA/GR&R correlation to OEM master lab within 90 days. | Reference artifacts pre-positioned during supplier selection (before PO award) to bypass customs delays. |

### 3.2 APQP-Lite for Consumer-Electronics Velocity

Full automotive PPAP Level 3 is incompatible with 4–6 month electronics NPI cycles.

| Component Type | Protocol | Submission Timeline | Acceptance Gate |
|----------------|----------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Mechanical / Molded** | PPAP Level 2 (dimensional + material + PSW) | 5 weeks | Sealed golden sample + GR&R |
| **PCBA / FPCA** | **First-Artlet + DFM Validation** | 3 weeks | ICT/functional correlation + CTQ checklist sign-off |
| **System / Box-Build** | Functional correlation + ICT/RF test + CTQ dimensional | 4 weeks | System-level burn-in pass |

**First-Artlet Requirement:** Sealed golden sample + 3D CAD master + 2D CTQ drawing + supplier written paraphrase (24-business-hour read-back).

### 3.3 Regional Electronics Bridging

**Mexico — From Automotive Discipline to Microelectronics Precision**
- **Ownership Segmentation (Day 1):** 
  - *Family-owned* (interior states): *Confianza* protocol with *dueño* access via Thursday/Friday *cena de negocios* or Saturday *desayuno* by explicit invitation. **Never Sunday.**
  - *PE-backed or multinational-affiliated* (Guadalajara, Tijuana, Querétaro): Professionalized quarterly business reviews with board-observer governance.
- **Electronics Gap Assessment (Days 1–30):** Audit ISO 14644 cleanroom equivalence, solder-paste volume repeatability (SPI), ESD/ionograph baseline, and dust-seal validation (IP5X minimum for Northern desert particulate). Do not assume automotive capability equals electronics readiness.
- **Union Alignment:** Day 14 = union listening session + mutual NDA only. Signed cooperation protocol deferred to Day 45, after evidence of OEM investment (training, tooling deposits).
- **IPC Certification:** Train-the-trainer model: certify 2 internal supplier champions via IPC CIS by Day 45; cascade to line leaders by Day 90. Risk-based tiering: 100% certification for soldering/inspection leaders, 50% for general assembly by Day 90.
- **Sub-Tier Mapping:** By Day 30, mandate *compadrazgo* disclosure map for laminates, connectors, and resins.
- **Shop-Floor Language:** Use **“Recorrido de Piso de Producción”** or **“Caminata de Calidad Conjunta”** under Mexican *NOM* tradition, not imported Japanese *gemba* jargon.

**Morocco — European Roots, Electronics Velocity Gap**
- **Audit Language:** **Eliminate VDA 6.3.** Deploy an OEM-customized electronics process audit in **French**, explicitly mapped to existing Renault/Stellantis methods (EAQF, QSB+) where present.
- **Technical Documentation:** Provide full French datasheets (BE, BEQ, cotation standards). Visual work instructions supplement text; they do not replace it. Appoint a **Language Authority** (bilingual French/Arabic engineering manager or sworn technical translator) to back-translate all CTQ terms from Arabic floor summaries to French for verification.
- **Capability Transfer:** **Fly-fly program** (2 weeks on-site, 1 week off) supplemented by a permanently hired Moroccan SQE anchor and third-party support (Bureau Veritas or SGS Morocco). Avoid 90-day expatriate residents due to *régime de résidence* tax and social-security reciprocity costs.
- **Environmental Specificity:** Salt-fog testing for PCBAs stored within 50 km of the Atlantic; VCI packaging with silica gel for golden samples; humidity-controlled dry rooms for PCBA.
- **Business Rhythm:** Publish a **Moroccan Business Week Calendar** treating Friday 11:30–13:30 (*Jumu’ah*) as non-operational. Build a **Ramadan-adjusted calendar**: reduced response clocks, no ECO releases in final 10 days of Ramadan.

**Vietnam — Execution Strength, Documentation & Data Sovereignty**
- **Segmentation:** Tier-1 (Samsung ecosystem) → audit firmware version control and traceability only. Tier-2/3 → full capability build.
- **Change Control:** **4M + 1E** (Man, Machine, Material, Method + Environment) with mandatory customer notification and OEM approval gate.
- **Data Compliance:** **Do not direct-upload to OEM global cloud.** Deploy an **edge-computing gateway** (on-premise, OEM accesses via VPN) or a **Vietnamese-certified cloud** (Viettel Cloud, FPT Cloud) with a locally compliant data-processing agreement. Extend edge-gateway sovereignty to Mexico (Azure Mexico / LFPDPPP compliance) and Morocco (Azure Morocco via European sovereignty partners / *Loi 09-08*).
- **Subcontracting Control (*Lậu* Prevention):** “No unauthorized subcontracting” clause with random right-of-access audits. Require **indelible source-marking** (laser-etched lot codes traceable to exact SMT line).
- **Work Instruction Semiotics:** Poka-yoke visuals with Vietnamese text; English-only error-proofing prohibited. **Do not use red arrows** for defect location (red signals loss/debt). Use **blue or orange arrows** with green checkmarks for acceptable reference.
- **Provincial Protocol (North):** Day-7 courtesy visit to provincial *Hiệp hội Doanh nghiệp* with compliance-cleared gift.

### 3.4 Global Approved Materials List (AML) with Regional Riders

Suppliers in all three regions source raw materials through traders with dubious authenticity. Impose a **Global AML** with regional conformance layers:

| Region | AML Rider |
|--------|-----------|
| **Mexico** | *NOM* material conformity; *NOM-001* wastewater baseline. |
| **Morocco** | *CE* documentation in French; hazardous-waste manifests under Law 28-00. |
| **Vietnam** | Smelter verification for tin/gold/tantalum; Decree 08/2022 e-waste/circular economy registration. |

## 4. Glocalized 8D / SCAR & Audit Discipline

### 4.1 Standardized Defect-Resolution Workflow

| Step | Timeline | Owner | Output |
|------|----------|-------|--------|
| **Containment** | 0–24 hours | Supplier QA + OEM SQE | Sort/rework instruction; quarantine lot locked |
| **Problem Description** | 24–48 hours | Supplier QE | Quantified defect taxonomy; photos/data package |
| **Interim Correction** | 48–72 hours | Supplier Operations | Short-term fix verified; line restart authorized |
| **Root Cause (D4)** | 5 business days | Supplier Engineering + OEM | 5-Why / Ishikawa; GR&R check if dimensional |
| **Permanent Corrective Action (D6)** | 10 business days | Supplier + OEM | ECN, poka-yoke, or SPC revision |
| **Verification (D7)** | 15 business days | OEM SQE | 3-lot run-at-rate; PPM check |
| **Prevention / SCAR Close** | 20 business days | Supplier + OEM | Knowledge-base entry; scorecard update |

### 4.2 Cultural Activation Layers

- **Mexico:** 8D presented in Spanish as *“Análisis de Mejora Continua.”* *D4* Root Cause must be validated by union safety steward if defect involves line changeover. Frame as continuous improvement, not disciplinary investigation.
- **Morocco:** 8D conducted in French. *D6* must account for Friday *Jumu’ah* blackouts and Ramadan staffing. Use Bureau Veritas Morocco for independent *D7* verification to overcome high-context reluctance to admit internal failure.
- **Vietnam:** For the first 60 days, deliver as a **“technical clarification memo”** (*công văn làm rõ kỹ thuật*); never issue a formal SCAR in a group setting. *D7* must include a *mặt mũi*-saving “lessons learned” credit to the supplier’s QC manager.

### 4.3 Recurring Audit Cadence

| Audit Type | Frequency | Scope | Regional Tailoring |
|------------|-----------|-------|--------------------|
| **Process Audit** | Every 90 days | 4M+1E, SPC, IPC adherence | Morocco: French-language audit mapped to Stellantis methods. |
| **Product Audit** | Monthly | Golden-sample correlation, ICT/functional drift | Vietnam: Includes subcontracting spot-check. |
| **System Audit** | Every 180 days | ISO 9001, data sovereignty, ESG, labor | Mexico: Includes *sindicato* and *NOM* compliance. |
| **Random Right-of-Access** | Quarterly (48-hour notice) | Sub-tier, unauthorized subcontracting | Vietnam: Lot-code traceability to SMT line. Mexico: *Compadrazgo* raw-material mapping. |

## 5. Communication Protocols: Zero Ambiguity

Early audits revealed spec drift and delivery-commitment hallucinations. The following protocol is mandatory globally, with regional activation layers.

### 5.1 Global “3-Layer + Read-Back” Spec Protocol

1. **Digital Master:** 3D CAD model with GD&T in native PLM (Windchill/Teamcenter).
2. **2D Drawing:** Dimensionally tolerant drawing with CTQ callouts highlighted in **local language + English**.
3. **Physical Golden Sample:** Sealed, signed, photographed with ruler/context, stored in **climate-controlled conditions**:
   - **Vietnam:** Desiccated cabinet (<30% RH) with temperature logging.
   - **Morocco (Coastal):** VCI packaging with silica gel; inspect seal integrity monthly against salt-air corrosion.
   - **Mexico (Northern):** Dust-sealed enclosure; inspect for desert particulate infiltration.
4. **24-Business-Hour Read-Back Rule:** Supplier must paraphrase the spec and delivery promise in writing within **24 business hours from 08:00 local time**, excluding local weekends, Ramadan/Eid, and Tết. OEM validates alignment before work proceeds. **Verbal assent alone is invalid.**

### 5.2 Delivery Commitment Architecture

- **Capacity Visibility:** Suppliers report constraint resources (bottleneck machines, mold cavities, SMT lines) weekly.
- **Buffer Stock Stabilization:** During Days 1–90, the OEM finances a 2-week safety stock via **consignment inventory** or a **90-day safety-stock pre-payment**. Do not force cash-poor nearshored suppliers to finance buffer inventory.
- **Forecast Discipline:** Issue a **12-week frozen schedule + 13-week flexible forecast**, updated weekly. Include a **capacity reservation fee** (paid monthly, deductible from invoice) so the supplier receives real cash for reserved slots even if mix changes. Embed a “change-in-law” break clause for tariff volatility.
- **Replenishment Mode:** Use **Fixed-Period Ordering (FPO)** or **CONWIP** for the first 180 days. Transition to Kanban only after demand variance falls below 15% for three consecutive months.

### 5.3 Regional Communication Activation

| Region | Language of Record | Escalation Clock | Anti-Hallucination Tactic |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|---------------------------|
| **Mexico** | Spanish technical packs; English for finance/contracts. | 4-hour response for quality holds (08:00–18:00 CST); next-business-day for ECOs. | **“Sesión de Bandera Roja”:** Weekly mandatory disagreement forum where supplier must raise at least one concern or ambiguity. Silence is not acceptance. |
| **Morocco** | French technical specs; Arabic floor summaries; English for contracts. | 24-hour response (non-Ramadan, excluding Friday 11:30–13:30); 48-hour during Ramadan. No ECO releases in final 10 days of Ramadan. | **3-Checkbox Confirmation Trigger:** Every verbal communication requires explicit selection—(1) Approved as written, (2) Approved with noted exception, (3) Requires discussion. Defaults to (3) if not returned. Named-owner Gantt charts with structural triggers replace open-ended promises. |
| **Vietnam** | Vietnamese work instructions; English for contracts/engineering. | 4-hour daytime (ICT, 08:00–17:00); 8-hour overnight. | Visual defect cards (photo + **blue/orange arrow**, never red) replace textual descriptions. Structured checklist sign-offs for every specification gate. Use Zalo for logistics pings only; engineering flows through PLM/Teams. |

### 5.4 IT Channel Governance & Data Sovereignty

| Policy | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **WeChat** | **Explicitly banned.** PRC-owned, unacceptable IP exfiltration vector. |
| **Zalo** | Logistics chat only. No file transfers >5MB. No business-critical decisions. No auto-delete. |
| **Microsoft Teams / PLM** | Immutable audit trails required for all engineering, quality, and commercial communication. Retention: 3–7 years per ISO/IPC. |
| **Global Cloud** | Mexico: Azure Mexico (LFPDPPP). Morocco: Azure Morocco / European sovereignty partner (*Loi 09-08*). Vietnam: Viettel / FPT Cloud or edge gateway (Decree 53/2022/ND-CP). |

## 6. Regional Trust & Integration Playbooks

### 6.1 Mexico — *Desarrollo Integrado*

**Cultural Profile:** Warmly hierarchical, relationship-driven (*confianza*), unionized post-2019, increasingly diverse in ownership (family, PE, Asian JV), and time-fluid outside formal hours.

| Dimension | Bridging Mechanism |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Decision-Making** | *Family-owned:* Bilingual *Socio* Lead builds rapport with *dueño* via Thursday/Friday *cena de negocios* or Saturday *desayuno* by explicit invitation. **Never unsolicited Sunday contact.** *PE-backed/JV:* Quarterly board-observer reviews. |
| **Formality** | *Usted* precedes *tú*; titles matter. Transition to informality only when explicitly invited. |
| **Union Reality** | Day 14: Union listening session + mutual NDA. Day 45+: Cooperation protocol signed after evidence of OEM investment. Union steward joins weekly Ops reviews. |
| **Account Manager Title** | *Gerente de Cuenta Estratégico*—not “Customer Success Manager.” |
| **Gender Inclusion** | All relationship events must include gender-neutral seating and conversation. Do not assume male-only attendance. |

**Trust Milestones — Mexico**

| Day | Milestone |
|-----|-----------|
| **14** | Union listening session held; mutual NDA signed; sub-tier disclosure map requested. |
| **30** | Electronics gap assessment completed; closed-loop CAPA issued; *compadrazgo* raw-material map received. |
| **45** | 2 IPC-CIS champion candidates certified; union cooperation protocol signed. |
| **60** | First successful First-Artlet approval; line leaders 50% certified. |
| **90** | OTIF ≥90% and PPM ≤500; *Red Flag Session* documented concerns = zero unresolved. |
| **180** | Co-investment tooling agreement signed under **retention of title (*reserva de dominio*)** registered in *Registro Público de la Propiedad*; OEM ownership steps down with volume thresholds. |

### 6.2 Morocco — *Développement Fournisseur*

**Cultural Profile:** French-colonial administrative rigor layered over Arab/Berber hospitality and high-context communication. Bureaucratic navigation is a core competency; relationships precede contracts. Islamic finance and labor rhythms are non-negotiable.

| Dimension | Bridging Mechanism |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Communication** | Deploy French-speaking *Directeur de Développement Fournisseur* with P&L authority. Use the **3-Checkbox Confirmation Trigger** after every verbal discussion. Never interpret silence as agreement. |
| **Business Rhythm** | Publish Moroccan Business Week Calendar (Friday 11:30–13:30 non-operational). Ramadan-adjusted calendar: reduced clocks, no ECO releases in final 10 days, Iftar with production leadership. |
| **Regulatory Navigation** | Retain **Big-4 customs/brokerage affiliates** (Deloitte Morocco, EY Casablanca) to lawfully expedite paperwork via MOUs with *l’Administration des Douanes*. No informal *fixateurs*. |
| **Islamic Finance** | Use **performance-based malus/bonus** (+2% for 99.5% OTIF; −1% for non-compliance) framed as **“commercial price adjustment,”** not penalty interest. Allow separate bonus invoicing to facilitate *Murabaha* / *Mudarabah* accounting. |
| **Gender Inclusion** | Evening events are gender-neutral; include female managers without alcohol pressure. |

**Trust Milestones — Morocco**

| Day | Milestone |
|-----|-----------|
| **14** | Moroccan legal counsel and Big-4 customs broker retained; bank FX pre-clearance initiated; Language Authority appointed. |
| **30** | First article approved under OEM custom audit (French); no critical findings. |
| **45** | Senior executive kickoff visit completed; hospitality reciprocity event held. |
| **60** | Bilingual (French/Arabic) floor instructions posted; first process audit complete. |
| **90** | Ramadan-adjusted OTIF ≥85%; 3-checkbox confirmation compliance = 100% of verbal discussions. |
| **180** | Rolling 13-week frozen schedule + capacity reservation fee active; supplier secures local financing against OEM credit rating and 6-month indicative outlook. **No 24-month guarantees.** |

### 6.3 Vietnam — *Phát triển Bền vững*

**Cultural Profile:** Confucian-hierarchical, face-saving (*mặt mũi*), relationship-centric (*quan hệ*), execution-fast but documentation-uneven. Regional bifurcation (North vs. South) is decisive.

| Dimension | Bridging Mechanism |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Hierarchy & Face** | Hold “offline” technical clinics (one-on-one or small group) separate from formal reviews. Never correct a supplier publicly in the first 60 days; use “technical clarification” memos. |
| **“Yes” as Acknowledgment** | *Vâng/Dạ* means “I hear you,” not agreement. Mandate structured checklist sign-offs and written paraphrase for every gate. |
| **Regional Nuance** | **North:** Use *Giám đốc Phát triển Nhà cung cấp* and state-style audit protocols. **South:** Use *Trưởng phòng Hợp tác* and heavier contractual/IP emphasis. |
| **Tết Maintenance** | Transfer bonuses **officially through supplier payroll** (declared, taxed, contracted). Nominal hampers (<$25/person) cleared by compliance permitted. **Cash in red envelopes is prohibited** under Vietnam’s 2023 Anti-Corruption Law and U.S. FCPA. |
| **Evening Bonding** | Schedule **“evening relationship dinners”** with explicit opt-in alcohol and premium non-alcoholic alternatives. Never schedule critical business discussions during alcohol-focused social time. Always include female staff. |
| **Provincial Respect** | Day 7: Courtesy visit to provincial *Hiệp hội Doanh nghiệp* (North) with compliance-cleared gift. |

**Trust Milestones — Vietnam**

| Day | Milestone |
|-----|-----------|
| **7** | Provincial *Hiệp hội* courtesy visit completed (North only). |
| **14** | Subcontracting audit completed; “no unauthorized subcontracting” clause signed; indelible lot-code protocol active; edge-gateway procurement initiated. |

## 7. Governance, Compliance & Risk Architecture

### 7.1 Tiered Governance Structure

| Level | Frequency | Participants | Purpose |
|-------|-----------|--------------|---------|
| **Operational** | Weekly | Plant managers, quality engineers, logistics leads | OTIF, PPM, ECO closure, holds, containment |
| **Tactical** | Monthly | Procurement directors, supplier GM/VP | SQE scorecard + risk-matrix review, cost, capacity, 4M changes |
| **Strategic** | Quarterly | C-suite / owners from both firms | Roadmap alignment, co-investment, VMI transition |

### 7.2 ESG, Labor & Compliance Guardrails

| Domain | Global Requirement | Regional Activation |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Anti-Corruption** | All gifts, Tết bonuses, hospitality clear OEM compliance office. Cash/cash-equivalent gifts to supplier staff prohibited globally. | Vietnam: Payroll-only Tết bonuses. Morocco: Bonus invoicing structured for Islamic accounting. |
| **Forced-Labor Due Diligence** | UFLPA-style labor audits by Day 60. | Morocco: Subcontracted female assembly workers. Vietnam: *Lao động thời vụ* seasonal migrants. Mexico: Union-sanctioned overtime verification. |
| **IP Segmentation** | “Need-to-know” tiers enforced. | Firmware source code: Never shared with Moroccan or Mexican mechanical suppliers. Test algorithms: Shared only under encryption with Vietnamese Tier-1 partners via secure PLM vault. |
| **Currency / FX Risk** | USD-denominated pricing with local-currency escalation clauses. | MAD (non-convertible capital account) and VND (exchange controls) managed via OEM-managed FX hedging for supplier payments >$500k/quarter. |

### 7.3 Supplier Development Off-Ramp

Trust is built by demonstrating a humane exit. In the 2025 scramble, 20–30% of onboarded suppliers may fail Phase 1 containment.

| Stage | Trigger | OEM Action | Supplier Protection |
|-------|---------|------------|---------------------|
| **Alert** | Missed Week 6 interim milestone | Joint diagnostic; additional tiger-team support days funded. | Transparency without penalty. |
| **Remedial** | Missed Day 90 containment targets | 30-day funded remedial plan with resident engineering support; scorecard frozen. | Paid support, not punitive withholding. |
| **Transition** | Failure after remedial plan | OEM commits to return tooling within 15 days under retention-of-title clauses; WIP purchased at pre-negotiated scrap-rate formula; final payment for conforming goods within 10 days
