# DELIVERABLE 1: COMPLIANCE IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP
## Phased Timeline & Milestones (2025-2026)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This roadmap operationalizes compliance across 15 jurisdictions through three sequential phases, with explicit **go/no-go decision gates**, **dependency tracking**, and **resource allocation**. The timeline prioritizes **high-enforcement jurisdictions** (EU, Canada) by Q1 2026, **secondary enforcement** (Australia, India, Singapore) by Q3 2026, and **monitoring jurisdictions** (Japan, South Korea, Mexico, UAE) through Q4 2026.

**Total Implementation Duration**: 18-24 months (Jan 2025 - Dec 2026)
**Total Resource Investment**: 280-350 FTE weeks (~$500K-800K for mid-sized company)
**Critical Path**: EU notified body assessment (must start Q1 2025 or deployment delayed)
