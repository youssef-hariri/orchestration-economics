## PART 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1 PAGE)

### STRATEGIC IMPERATIVE & RESTRUCTURING OVERVIEW

**Situation:** A leading quick-service restaurant franchisor operates 2,100+ franchised units across five emerging markets (Turkey, Egypt, Nigeria, Indonesia, Brazil) under a fixed 5.5% USD-denominated royalty structure. Currency depreciation (12-25% annually in high-stress markets) and double-digit inflation (28-45% annually) have compressed franchisee EBITDA margins to critical levels:

- **Egypt & Turkey:** -1.2% to +3.8% margins (insolvency risk imminent; 18+ active defaults in Egypt)
- **Nigeria:** -2.2% margin (acute trough; recovery expected 2027 if franchisees survive liquidity crisis)
- **Brazil:** +8.0% margin (moderate stress; organized franchisee association demanding restructuring; legislation pending)
- **Indonesia:** +9.5% margin (stable; no intervention required)

**Strategic Choice:** Proactive, differentiated restructuring across markets preserves $13.8M baseline royalty revenue while restoring franchisee viability. Alternative (reactive enforcement) results in cascading defaults, brand abandonment, $2.5-4.0M reacquisition costs, and precedent-setting litigation losses.

### RESTRUCTURING FRAMEWORK: THREE CORE PRINCIPLES

**1. Graduated Market-Specific Intervention**
- **Critical Tier (Immediate):** Egypt & Turkey → Comprehensive royalty reform + supply-chain support + debt restructuring
- **Cyclical Tier (6-12 months):** Nigeria → Temporary relief during forex trough; automatic reversion upon recovery
- **Strategic Tier (Proactive):** Brazil → Preemptive ABF settlement avoiding legislative mandate
- **Anchor Market (Maintain):** Indonesia → Status quo; model for demonstrating restructuring success elsewhere

**2. Shared Risk Bearing via Indexed & Tiered Mechanisms**
- **Currency Risk:** Franchisor absorbs 40-70% of depreciation beyond baseline; franchisees share inflation passthrough
- **Inflation Risk:** Royalty rates indexed to local CPI; franchisor recovers partial inflation via rate adjustment
- **Operational Risk:** Consolidated supply procurement transfers volume benefits to franchisees; franchisor retains modest margin
- **Credit Risk:** Franchisor provides credit facilities (95% guaranty) for working capital; shares loss exposure with franchisees

**3. Formal Governance & Dynamic Adaptation**
- **Steering Committees** (Egypt, Turkey, Brazil): Franchisee associations + franchisor co-governance; quarterly reviews
- **Monitoring Boards:** Joint economic tracking with automatic triggers for royalty adjustment when system stress exceeds thresholds
- **Binding Arbitration:** Dispute escalation via neutral arbitrators (not litigation); faster, cheaper resolution
- **Transparency Infrastructure:** Monthly P&L reporting, franchisee margin dashboards, real-time royalty calculation portals

### KEY INNOVATIONS

**1. Bifurcated Currency Royalty (Egypt)**
- **Track A (EGP Insulation):** 2.8% of local revenue, paid in EGP; franchisor absorbs forex risk
- **Track B (USD Premium):** 4.2% of USD-equivalent revenue; for franchisees with FX access
- **Outcome:** Eliminates economic impossibility of USD payment; franchisee margin improves +3.6pp

**2. Hybrid Inflation-Indexed Royalty (Turkey)**
- **Formula:** 2.8% base + 45% of CPI passthrough + 50% depreciation credit absorption
- **Outcome:** Franchisor and franchisee share inflation/depreciation shocks; franchisee margin improves +3.6pp to +8.2pp (cumulative with supply hub)

**3. Supply-Chain Consortium (Egypt & Turkey)**
- **Mechanism:** Franchisor establishes centralized procurement hub; aggregates franchisee demand; sources at official-rate forex (CBT access)
- **Pass-Through:** 120% of franchisor's landed cost (covers logistics, 5% margin)
- **Franchisee Savings:** 32-35% on import-heavy ingredients vs. black-market rates
- **Outcome:** COGS reduction +2.4pp; additional franchisee margin recovery

**4. Proactive Collective Governance (All Markets)**
- **Steering Committees:** Franchisee associations + franchisor; quarterly meetings; non-binding recommendations
- **Monitoring Boards:** Joint P&L tracking; automatic triggers for adjustment if system margin <4%
- **Binding Arbitration:** Dispute escalation; replaces litigation
- **Outcome:** Prevents unilateral defaults; enables dynamic contract adaptation; reduces litigation risk
