The manufacturer must onboard three culturally distinct supplier bases within a 90- to 180-day window without sacrificing IPC/JEDEC quality integrity or triggering FCPA / data-sovereignty violations.

## 1. PLAYBOOK FOUNDATION

### 1.1 The Six Cross-Cutting Principles
1. **One Backbone, Local Musculature.** The manufacturer’s IPC/JEDEC/ISO 9001 QMS, spec libraries, and delivery cadence are immutable; implementation tactics are co-designed with each supplier.
2. **Presumption of Partnership.** Days 0–60 prioritize relationship capital over transactional penalty.
3. **Closed-Loop Communication Doctrine.** No specification, schedule change, or corrective action is considered “transmitted” until the supplier demonstrates comprehension via documented *teach-back* or digital evidence.
4. **Radical Early Transparency (180-Day Safe Harbor).** Contractual protection from punitive chargebacks for self-disclosed spec misunderstandings or delivery risks, provided the Early Warning Protocol triggers within 48 hours.
5. **Capability Transfer as Collateral.** The manufacturer embeds resident engineers and co-funds training for the first two quarters.
6. **Metrology-First Validation.** Every quality gate requires objective evidence: **Cpk ≥ 1.33**, **%GRR ≤ 10%**, and First-Article conformance to **IPC-A-610 Class 2** (or agreed class).

### 1.2 Zero-Tolerance Compliance Perimeter
| Rule | Global Standard |
|------|----------------|
| **Anti-Bribery** | Strict adherence to FCPA, UK Bribery Act 2010, and local statutes (Mexico: LFACP; Morocco: Law 06-99; Vietnam: Anti-Corruption Law 2018/2022). |
| **Hospitality Ceilings** | Mexico: USD $150/event; Morocco: USD $150/event; Vietnam: USD $100/event. Dual-sign-off required above 50% of cap. Real-time corporate card tracking; itemized receipts within 48 hours. **No cash reimbursements.** |
| **Prohibited Items** | Cash, cash equivalents, and gift cards are banned. Non-cash recognition requires Legal review. |
| **Intermediaries** | Only vetted legal counsel, Big-4 logistics partners, or licensed customs brokers. No opaque agents. |
| **Government Officials** | No gifts or meals to SOE / party-linked directors without pre-clearance from Compliance. |
| **Data Sovereignty** | SSOT portal provisioned in-country from Day 0. Vietnam: local residency per Cybersecurity Law 2018/Decree 53. Morocco: GDPR-compliant Franco-Moroccan channels. Mexico: LATAM-approved cloud zones. **No spec-critical data on Zalo, WhatsApp, or personal email.** |

### 1.5 Unified Technical Backbone & SSOT Protocol
All suppliers operate under a harmonized QMS scaffold:
- **ISO 9001:2015** (baseline QMS)
- **IPC-A-610** (Acceptability of Electronic Assemblies) — Class 2 default, Class 3 where safety-critical
- **IPC-J-STD-001** (Requirements for Soldered Electrical and Electronic Assemblies)
- **JEDEC J-STD-020 / J-STD-033** (Moisture sensitivity and MSD handling)
- **AIAG-VDA FMEA** (1st Edition) for process risk analysis
- **AIAG PPAP** (4th Edition) or VDA/ISIR equivalent
- **AIAG MSA** (4th Edition) — %GRR ≤ 10% on all CTQ measurements
- **AIAG SPC** (2nd Edition) — Cpk ≥ 1.33, Ppk ≥ 1.67 for all CTQ characteristics
- **ISO 19011:2018** (Auditing Management Systems)

**Single Source of Truth (SSOT) & Controlled Translation Protocol (CTP)**
1. **Terminology Anchoring:** Extract a Controlled Technical Lexicon (CTL) from IPC-A-610, JEDEC, and OEM drawings.
2. **Technical Translation:** Translate by subject-matter expert using the CTL.
3. **Back-Translation Verification:** Independent technical auditor translates back to English blind. >2% lexical variance triggers rework.
4. **Redline Verification Workshop:** Supplier engineering leads redline their own drawings against the English master.
5. **CTQ Flow-Down Matrix:** Bilingual table linking every OEM drawing note to a local-language process parameter, control method, and acceptance criterion. Signed by RIL and Supplier Quality Director.
6. **Golden / Boundary Sample Cross-Validation:** OEM resident engineer validates that the local instruction correctly discriminates golden vs. boundary samples.
7. **FAI as Linguistic Proof:** The FAI report must be completed in the local language and checked against the English master. Mismatch = translation defect.

**Legal Supremacy & Divergence Protocol:** English is the sole language for contractual dispute resolution. Local derivatives are operational working documents. Divergence is a non-conformance event adjudicated by the Joint SSOT Change Control Board (OEM RIL + Supplier Quality Director). The English master always prevails.

| Channel | Cadence | Purpose |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Executive Relationship** | Weekly (virtual / in-person) | Strategic alignment, relationship maintenance |
