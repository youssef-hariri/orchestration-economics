# Executive Summary: Global Franchise Financial Restructuring (2026–2028)

**To:** The Board of Directors  
**Subject:** Strategic Realignment of International Franchise Operations  

---

### **1. Executive Overview**
The current USD-denominated royalty model is unsustainable under 2026 macroeconomic conditions in Turkey, Egypt, Nigeria, Indonesia, and Brazil. Currency depreciation, hyper-inflation, and emerging franchisee collective bargaining pose an imminent risk of a "default cascade" and long-term brand erosion. To preserve our global enterprise value, we must pivot from an **extractive** model to a **resilient partnership** framework.

### **2. Strategic Framework: "Revenue-Sharing Resilience"**
We are transitioning to a **Dynamic Hybrid Royalty (DHR)** model that aligns franchisor incentives with franchisee viability. The plan rests on three pillars:

*   **Adaptive Royalty Architecture:** We are shifting to a local-currency-based percentage model capped by an inflation-indexed USD ceiling. This protects franchisees from hyper-inflationary shocks while ensuring the brand captures upside during periods of stability.
*   **Macro-Volatility "Circuit Breakers":** To remove administrative friction and the threat of litigation, we have instituted an automated "Circuit Breaker." If local currency devaluation against the USD exceeds 20% in a quarter, the royalty cap adjusts automatically without the need for manual, adversarial renegotiation.
*   **Capital Preservation via Asset Conversion:** In markets with strict capital controls (Nigeria, Egypt), we are moving from a cash-extraction strategy to an **Asset-Backing Strategy**. Trapped local currency will be converted into real estate and supply chain infrastructure, transforming inflationary liabilities into appreciating, brand-owned assets.

### **3. Institutionalizing Cooperation**
We are neutralizing the adversarial pressure from franchisee associations through a **Regional Governance Council** approach:
*   **Transparency as a Deflector:** We will provide associations with a "Macro-Dashboard" detailing the global EBITDA impact of devaluations. By demonstrating that the franchisor is sharing the economic burden, we preserve our moral authority and avoid protracted labor disputes.
*   **Bifurcated Incentives:** We have replaced flat rebates with a two-track system: **Maintenance Rebates** (ensuring brand standards) and **Growth Accelerators** (tied to volume growth). This prevents franchisee complacency while rewarding performance in high-volatility markets.

### **4. Implementation Matrix**

| Market Strategy | Primary Objective |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Turkey/Egypt** | Deploy aggressive **Tier 2 USD Caps** to prevent menu-price shocks. |
| **Nigeria** | Utilize **Right of Conversion** to move trapped cash into local real estate. |
| **Brazil/Indonesia** | Drive performance through **Growth-based Rebates** to expand market share. |

### **5. Financial and Legal Safeguards**
To ensure regulatory compliance and avoid transfer-pricing challenges, all performance-based rebates are contractually categorized as **"Operational Support Grants"** with strictly defined, auditable KPIs. This structure ensures that our local financial support is legally defensible as a business necessity rather than a profit-shifting mechanism.

### **6. Recommendation for the Board**
The franchisor’s long-term enterprise value depends on maintaining a healthy, functional network. We recommend the immediate issuance of an **Addendum to International Franchise Agreements** to codify these stabilization protocols. 

**Conclusion:** This restructuring trades short-term, high-friction cash flow for sustained, long-term market dominance. By acting now, we position the brand as the "Partner of Choice" in emerging markets, effectively securing our footprint while competitors suffer the consequences of rigid, outdated fiscal models.
