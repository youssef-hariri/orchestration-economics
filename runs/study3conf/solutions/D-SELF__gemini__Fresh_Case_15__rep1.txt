**SUBJECT:** Executive Proposal: Dynamic Royalty & Franchise Sustainability Restructuring

### 1. Executive Summary
The current "Fixed-USD Royalty" model is experiencing systemic failure in Turkey, Egypt, Nigeria, Indonesia, and Brazil. Currency devaluation and hyper-inflation have driven the Effective Royalty Burden (ERB) beyond the threshold of franchisee solvency. This proposal outlines the transition to a **Hybrid-Peg Royalty Model (HPRM)**, designed to replace rigid extraction with a data-driven, liquidity-responsive system that preserves brand equity, mitigates litigation risk, and stabilizes franchisee operations.

### 2. The Core Problem
Static USD-denominated royalties have become an unsustainable tax as local input costs surge and currencies depreciate by >25% annually. The resulting financial distress has led to the formation of organized franchisee associations, creating significant legal and reputational risks for the parent brand.

### 3. The Solution: The Hybrid-Peg Royalty Model (HPRM)
We will move to a tiered, algorithmic royalty structure governed by an "Economic Stability Addendum" (ESA) to existing franchise agreements:

*   **Tier 1: Base Local-Currency Royalty:** Royalties will be indexed to local-currency equivalents, adjusted semi-annually via the Local Consumer Price Index (CPI), ensuring stable purchasing power for the franchisor without tracking volatile USD spot rates.
*   **Tier 2: Currency-Stabilization Fund (CSF):** During extreme volatility (depreciation >25% YoY), a portion of royalties (1.5% of gross sales) will be diverted into a franchisee-specific, ring-fenced escrow account. These funds are legally restricted for mandatory capital expenditures and brand-standard maintenance.
*   **Tier 3: Variable Safety Valve:** If the Effective Royalty Burden exceeds pre-agreed operating margins, the system automatically triggers a shift to a 4% gross-revenue share until the currency stabilizes within a 10% corridor for two consecutive quarters.

### 4. Governance & Collective Bargaining Strategy
To neutralize the threat of collective litigation, the transition will be managed through:
*   **The Joint Transparency Protocol:** Utilizing a "Trigger Dashboard" fueled by independent third-party audit data (e.g., Big Four accounting firms) to automate adjustments, removing executive discretion and preventing allegations of "bad faith" or "corporate greed."
*   **Joint Governance Committee (JGC):** A quarterly forum for franchisee associations to review performance data and approve local supply chain initiatives, shifting the relationship from "adversarial" to "partnership-in-pain."

### 5. Legal Safeguards
*   **Individualized Arbitration:** The ESA mandates binding, individual arbitration in neutral jurisdictions (e.g., Singapore/London), effectively preventing class-action consolidation.
*   **Clawback Clauses:** Royalty relief is strictly conditional. Failure to meet Quarterly Quality, Service, and Cleanliness (QSC) standards allows the franchisor to instantly revert to the original Fixed-USD royalty, ensuring that financial aid is not used for profit-taking at the expense of brand standards.
*   **Local Compliance:** Implementation will be tailored to local law (e.g., using "Local Currency Equivalency" in Brazil to ensure compliance with domestic payment regulations).

### 6. Strategic Rationale
This restructuring is a preemptive defensive maneuver. By sacrificing short-term USD-denominated revenue, the company avoids the high cost of mass-default and store liquidation. By tying relief to mandatory brand reinvestment, we ensure the network emerges from this period stronger, modernized, and loyal to the brand.

### 7. Implementation Roadmap
| Phase | Focus | Expected Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1** | Transparency | Data alignment and "Letter of No Objection" from associations. |
| **Phase 2** | Pilot | Deployment in the Egypt/Nigeria markets to validate the Dashboard. |
| **Phase 3** | Formalization | Execution of the ESA addendums across all high-volatility markets. |

**Recommendation:** We request Board approval to initiate Phase 1 immediately, utilizing the "Transparency Dividend" strategy to secure franchisee cooperation and stabilize the network before further market deterioration occurs.
