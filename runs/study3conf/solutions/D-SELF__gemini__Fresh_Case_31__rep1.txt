# Global Compensation Philosophy: Our Commitment to Transparency and Equity

At [Company Name], we believe that our strength lies in our ability to hire the best talent, wherever they may be. As we continue to scale across 30 countries, we are moving to a **Global Total Rewards Architecture**. This framework is designed to move beyond arbitrary geographic assumptions and toward a data-driven model that balances global consistency with local economic realities.

---

### 1. The Core Philosophy: "Value, Not Location"
We have shifted from a "cost-of-labor" model to a **Value-Tiered Framework**. Our compensation strategy is built on two primary pillars:

*   **Global Base Salary (80%):** A unified pay scale based strictly on job level, responsibility, and global impact. This ensures that a software engineer in Warsaw and a software engineer in Toronto are recognized for the same level of contribution.
*   **Regional Economic Premium (20%):** A transparent, data-driven adjustment that accounts for the specific market competitiveness and cost-of-labor in your location. This ensures we remain competitive in your local market, whether you are in an emerging tech hub or a mature financial center.

**The Result:** When you compare your compensation with a peer in another country, you will see a consistent **Global Base**. Differences in total pay reflect objective, market-based premiums specific to the local talent market, not a reflection of your personal value to the company.

---

### 2. Our Global Market Tiers
To ensure fiscal discipline and market competitiveness, we have organized our 30 countries into four economic clusters:

*   **Tier 1 (High-Liquidity Markets):** US, Switzerland, Singapore.
*   **Tier 2 (Mature Markets):** Germany, Canada, Netherlands, etc.
*   **Tier 3 (Growth Markets):** Poland, Portugal, Brazil, etc.
*   **Tier 4 (Emerging/Remote-Friendly Hubs):** Region-specific emerging markets.

*Note: We utilize rolling 6-month market data to ensure these tiers reflect real-time shifts in global economic conditions.*

---

### 3. Our Commitment to Fairness
Transparency is not just a regulatory requirement for us—it is a core value. To ensure internal equity, we are implementing the following protocols:

*   **Universal Equity Parity:** Regardless of your location or economic tier, your eligibility for equity (stock options/RSUs) is tied strictly to your **Global Base level**. We believe that long-term wealth creation potential should be identical for all employees performing at the same level.
*   **Global Benefits Parity:** We are standardizing our non-cash compensation. Every employee, regardless of their location, will receive the same baseline for home-office stipends, global health coverage, and professional development budgets.
*   **Internal Equity Audits:** We conduct biannual compensation reviews to identify and close potential gender, tenure, or structural pay gaps across all 30 countries.

---

### 4. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why is my pay different from a colleague in a different country?**
Your compensation consists of a **Global Base** and a **Regional Economic Premium (REP)**. While your Global Base is identical to peers in the same level, your REP is benchmarked against the specific market data of your region. This ensures you are paid according to the competitiveness of your local talent market.

**Q: What happens if I move to a different country?**
If your primary residence changes, your Regional Economic Premium will be adjusted to align with the market data of your new location, while your Global Base remains protected.

**Q: How do I know my pay is fair?**
Your compensation is the result of an objective calculation, not individual negotiation luck. We use industry-standard, third-party benchmark data to ensure our ranges are competitive. Managers have been trained to provide you with the data behind your tiering, ensuring that our pay decisions are defensible and transparent.

We know that pay is more than a paycheck; it is a sign of how much we value your contribution. By adopting this model, we are ensuring that we remain a high-growth, remote-first company where talent—not geography—determines your path to success.

If you have questions regarding your specific level or how your tier is calculated, your manager is your first point of contact. They have been equipped with the necessary tools and scripts to have an open, transparent, and data-backed conversation with you.
