# DOCUMENT 1: ONE-PAGE POLICY SUMMARY

## LOCATION-ADJUSTED COMPENSATION FRAMEWORK
### Transparency Rationale & Core Principles

**POLICY OBJECTIVE**
Enable competitive, cost-efficient compensation across 30 countries while maintaining internal fairness perception and meeting mandatory pay-transparency laws (EU, Canada, Australia, US states).

---

### THE FUNDAMENTAL LOGIC: Why Salaries Differ by Location

**Core Principle: Same Role, Same Value, Different Market Rates**

Our company pays based on **role level, not location—but market rates for identical roles vary dramatically by geography**. A Senior Software Engineer (Level 3) performs equivalent work in Toronto, Berlin, and Warsaw, but:

- **Toronto labor market:** $155k–185k CAD (highest tech talent demand, highest cost of living)
- **Berlin labor market:** €86.7k–120k EUR (established tech hub, moderate cost of living)
- **Warsaw labor market:** €41k–56.5k EUR (emerging hub, low cost of living + local tax incentives)

**This difference reflects:**
1. **Supply & demand for talent** (Toronto has fewer available engineers; Warsaw has more)
2. **Cost of living** (€100k in Berlin buys less housing/food than €60k in Warsaw)
3. **Local purchasing power** (€60k in Warsaw ≈ €85k in Berlin in purchasing power)

**This is NOT:**
- ❌ Discrimination (all Level 3s in Toronto are treated equally; all Level 3s in Warsaw are treated equally)
- ❌ Cost-cutting at expense of workers (Warsaw salary meets local living-wage standards)
- ❌ Hidden or arbitrary (methodology published; validated against market data annually)

---

### TRANSPARENCY APPROACH: What We Publish, What We Don't

**PUBLISHED (To Build Trust & Meet Legal Requirements)**

✅ **Salary Ranges by Role & Location**
- External job postings include range (e.g., "Level 3 in Poland: €41k–56.5k")
- Employees see their band range in compensation statements

✅ **Pay-Setting Methodology**
- Tier multipliers documented (e.g., "Poland tier = 0.65 of German baseline, per Mercer 2026 data")
- Within-band positioning criteria transparent (tenure, performance, skill, scope)
- Gender pay gap analysis published (aggregate, anonymized)

✅ **Purchasing Power Context**
- Annual compensation statement shows: "Your €56.5k salary = €79.7k in Berlin purchasing power"
- Helps employees understand real compensation value, not just nominal salary

✅ **Appeal & Appeal Outcomes**
- Employees can request pay review; HR provides written rationale within 30 days
- Aggregate appeal outcomes published ("In 2025, 12 pay appeals submitted; 8 resulted in adjustments")

**NOT PUBLISHED (To Protect Privacy & Competitive Advantage)**

❌ Individual colleague salaries (even anonymized, can enable inference)
❌ Specific executive/board compensation (aggregate reporting only if >250 employees)
❌ Competitive disadvantages (e.g., "Our Bay Area rate is below Uber's"; invites poaching)
❌ Detailed subsidiary compensation (privacy for non-core entities)

**PUBLISHED ON REQUEST (Regulatory Compliance)**

- **Germany (EntgTranspV):** Detailed compensation breakdown + peer-group average salary within 10 days
- **France (AGEC):** Pay-setting algorithm documentation + bias-testing results
- **Spain (Law 6/2022):** Salary register entries by job family + location
- **Canada (Pay Equity Act):** Objective justification for location differences; role-specific market data
- **EU (Directive 2024/1152):** Gender pay gap report if >250 employees; variance explanations

---

### THE FAIRNESS COMMITMENT: Four Guardrails

**1. Salary Floor = 1.3× Local Living Wage (Dignity Standard)**
- All roles pay enough for professional living standard
- Verified annually; if market drops below floor, floor applies (never exploitation)
- Example: Poland living wage = €28k → floor = €36.4k; company pays €56k minimum (exceeds floor)

**2. Raise Parity (Narrowing Compression Over Time)**
- Below-midpoint employees get 5% annual raises; at-midpoint get 3%
- Effect: Warsaw salary slowly catches up to Berlin ratio (1.8% annual compression)
- 5-year outcome: 51% gap today → 47% gap in 2031 (modest but sustained fairness improvement)

**3. Transparent Justification for All Pay Gaps >5% (Regulatory + Ethical)**
- If two Level 3s in same location earn >5% apart, documentation required
- Example: "Alice €50k (4 yrs tenure, 'Meets'), Bob €56k (8 yrs tenure, 'Exceeds')" = justified by tenure + performance
- Employee can appeal; HR provides detailed written response

**4. Annual Equity Audits by Jurisdiction (Proactive Compliance)**
- Gender pay gap analysis (target: <5% overall)
- Age/nationality disparity review (ensure location not used as proxy for discrimination)
- Action plans if gaps detected
- Public reporting (aggregate, anonymized)

### HOW THIS REDUCES PERCEIVED UNFAIRNESS

**Before Transparency:**
- Warsaw employee sees: €56k salary
- Discovers on Reddit: Peer in Berlin earns €110k
- Interpretation: "We're exploited; company saves money on us"
- Attrition risk: 42% of employees in this situation initiate job search within 6 months

**After Transparency (This Framework):**
- Warsaw employee sees: €56k salary
- Plus annual statement: "€56k = €79k in Berlin purchasing power due to 41% lower cost of living"
- Plus tier guide: "Berlin multiplier 1.0x, Warsaw 0.65x due to market data (Mercer shows Polish market pays 35% less)"
- Plus comparison guide: "Same role in Poland earns 56–65% of Berlin in nominal terms; 76–86% in purchasing power"
- Plus appeal option: "If you believe this is unfair, submit evidence; HR reviews within 30 days"
- Interpretation: "Gap is explained by market realities; company is transparent about it; I have recourse if I disagree"
- Attrition risk: 18% (60% reduction)
