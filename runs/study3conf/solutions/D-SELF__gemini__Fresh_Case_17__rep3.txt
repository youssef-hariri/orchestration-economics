# Executive Roadmap: Global Expansion 2026

## 1. Executive Summary
To successfully launch in Vietnam, Saudi Arabia, France, and Kenya, the startup will adopt a **"Sovereign Core, Adaptive Edge"** strategy. We move from a monolithic AI model to a **Federated Pedagogical Model**, where the global AI core is constrained by local policy engines ("Governance-as-Code") to satisfy strict pedagogical, cultural, and data sovereignty mandates.

---

## 2. Strategic Pillars & Tactical Execution

### A. The Federated Architecture (Technical Sovereignty)
*   **Decoupled AI Control:** Implement "Pedagogical Policy Injection." The AI core will no longer force a "reflection-first" vs. "drill-first" approach globally. Instead, regional settings will dictate the AI’s recommendation weightings (e.g., Vietnam: 80% Drill/20% Reflection; France: 40% Drill/60% Reflection).
*   **Version Pinning:** To satisfy government tenders in France (SecNumCloud) and Saudi Arabia, we will deploy "State-Locked" model versions. These ensure that AI behaviors remain constant during the lifecycle of a multi-year public contract, preventing "black-box" updates from breaching regulatory standards.
*   **Edge-Syncing (Kenya):** Prioritize offline-first functionality with asynchronous, low-bandwidth metadata syncing to optimize performance in connectivity-constrained regions.

## 3. Operational Implementation Roadmap (Q1 2026 – Q4 2026)

### Phase 1: Preparation & Compliance (Months 1–3)
*   **Infrastructure:** Deploy regional cloud instances (OVHcloud for France; Local Saudi hosting) to guarantee residency and sovereignty.
*   **Audit:** Conduct the "Cultural Sensitivity Audit" for KSA and "Pedagogical Policy Audit" for the French Ministry.
*   **Governance-as-Code:** Build the "Regional Configurator" layer into the Knowledge Graph to allow local teams to tune AI behavior.

### Phase 2: Pilot & Validation (Months 4–8)
*   **Vietnam:** Launch pilot within two major private tutoring chains.
*   **Kenya:** Distribute hardware-optimized PWA to three urban and two rural school pilot sites.
*   **France:** Secure "Lighthouse" school partnerships to pilot GAR-compliant integration.
*   **Saudi Arabia:** Complete legal entity registration and formalize MOE tender participation.

### Phase 3: Scaling & Optimization (Months 9–12)
*   **Data Feedback Loop:** Enable anonymized (PII-stripped) performance data return to the US core to refine the global Knowledge Graph without violating residency laws.
*   **Teacher Agency Review:** Evaluate pilot feedback; refine "Teacher-in-the-Loop" controls to ensure digital tools empower, rather than replace, local classroom authority.

---

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
*   **Regulatory Alignment:** 100% adherence to regional data residency (GDPR, SecNumCloud, Saudi Local Sovereignty).
*   **Product Fit:** 80%+ teacher satisfaction in France (via Teacher-Override tools) and 80%+ score improvement in Vietnam (via drill-optimized pathways).
*   **Operational Readiness:** Successful integration with local ecosystems (GAR in France, M-Pesa in Kenya).

## 5. Risk Management
*   **The "Bias" Risk:** Continuous oversight by local pedagogical consultants to ensure the AI's "learning path" aligns with local classroom norms.
*   **The "Procurement" Risk:** Appoint local Country Managers with prior government/sector-specific experience to navigate public tender bureaucracies.
*   **The "Sovereignty" Risk:** Maintain "freeze-states" on AI models to ensure stability for public sector clients.
