# Playbook: Tri-Regional Supply Chain Integration
**Target Audience:** Internal Procurement, Engineering, and Quality Teams; Regional Supplier Partners

---

## 1. Overview: The "Partnership-First" Mandate
This playbook outlines the operational standards for our 2025 supply chain expansion. Our goal is to shift from "transactional oversight" to "collaborative integration." Success depends on our ability to adapt our management style to the local business cultures of **Mexico, Morocco, and Vietnam.**

---

## 2. Standardized Communication Protocols
We are moving away from monolithic, top-down instruction. Use the following protocols to ensure technical clarity and cultural alignment.

| Region | Primary Channel | "Accountability" Tactic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mexico** | WhatsApp & Video | **"Agreed Minutes":** Follow every informal call with: *"To keep my team aligned, could you confirm our discussion in an email?"* |
| **Morocco** | Formal/Executive | **"Executive Alignment":** Engage the Director General/Owner on quality KPIs; tie standards to "institutional prestige." |
| **Vietnam** | In-Person/Co-Creation | **"Technical Optimization":** Frame all feedback as a collaborative effort to improve the *shared system* rather than a critique of the factory. |

---

## 3. The "Three-Phase" Onboarding Sprint
*Mandatory for all new supplier engagements.*

*   **Phase I: Cultural Calibration (Weeks 1–4):** A designated Regional Liaison will spend 14 days on-site. The role is to act as an "Interpreter of Intent"—translating our technical specifications into the local shop-floor reality.
*   **Phase II: Visual Specification Library (Weeks 5–8):** Move away from text-heavy PDFs. Develop "Functional Spec Books" that use 3D renderings and "Use-Case" photography to show *how* a part fits into the final product.
*   **Phase III: The "Trust-Equity" Summit (Ongoing):** Quarterly executive meetings.
    *   **Mexico:** Include informal social elements.
    *   **Morocco:** Focus on long-term prestige and corporate legacy.
    *   **Vietnam:** Focus on operational efficiency and cost-of-quality gains.

---

## 4. Conflict Resolution & Root-Cause Transparency
To avoid the "blame-game," we implement the **Root-Cause Transparency Rule**:

1.  **24-Hour Self-Report:** Suppliers may report issues within 24 hours of detection without punitive penalty.
2.  **Joint Forensic Audit:** If a discrepancy occurs, we perform a video-linked forensic audit to identify if the failure was due to translation, tooling, or workflow.
3.  **Cultural Application of the "No-Blame" Debrief:**
    *   **Mexico:** Emphasize that resolving failures together builds *relational resilience*.
    *   **Morocco:** Take responsibility for translation errors; it is viewed as a sign of **integrity** and strengthens the partnership.
    *   **Vietnam:** Focus on **"System Evolution."** Avoid self-blame or supplier-blame; frame the fix as an adjustment to our *shared process*.

---

## 5. Strategic "Rooting" (The 3-Year Vision)
*Instructions for Procurement Leadership:*
To prevent suppliers from viewing us as a "stop-gap" partner, procurement teams must present a **3-year volume forecast** during every Quarterly Executive Summit. 
*   **Why:** Suppliers invest in quality, tooling, and labor retention only when they believe we are "planting roots" rather than "renting space." 
*   **Action:** When discussing quality gaps, always anchor the request for improvement in the context of the 3-year growth plan.

---

## 6. Liaison Checklist: "Audit the Auditor"
Before any liaison begins an on-site assignment, they must be vetted against these three criteria:
1.  **Hierarchy Awareness:** Do they know whom to approach (Owner vs. Floor Manager) in Morocco?
2.  **Harmonious Feedback:** Can they deliver technical critiques to a Vietnamese partner without causing "loss of face"?
3.  **Documentation Discipline:** Are they skilled at converting informal "relationship-driven" agreements into "Agreed Minutes" for our legal/compliance files?
