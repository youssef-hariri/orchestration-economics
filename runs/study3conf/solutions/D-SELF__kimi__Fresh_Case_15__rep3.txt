## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The fixed-USD royalty model has triggered a **doom loop** in high-volatility markets: currency depreciation inflates local-currency obligations; franchisees defer wages and maintenance to preserve cash; brand standards decay; and unit-level economics collapse. Franchisee associations in Turkey, Brazil, and Indonesia are consolidating bargaining power, while company-owned stores in stable-currency markets fear hidden cross-subsidies and brand contagion.

**Operation Twin Anchor v2.0** replaces the old model with a **Blended Local-Currency First (BLCF)** architecture. It is stress-tested, negotiation-viable, and ring-fenced to protect corporate and stable-market P&Ls.

| Design Pillar | Mechanism | Board Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash-Flow Surgery** | 100% local-currency (LCY) invoicing. No USD bill ever reaches a franchisee. | Eliminates the primary insolvency trigger. |
| **Soft Franchisor Anchor** | **Deferred Floor Obligation (DFO):** a non-cash, non-interest-bearing, non-recourse, subordinated claim on terminal value only. Capped and frozen during stress. | Preserves long-run IP asset value without extracting operating cash. |
| **Recovery Upside** | **Convertible Royalty Obligations (CROs):** LCY revenue-based capital injections with no fixed maturity, approved by joint Resilience Councils. | Franchisor participates in recovery, not extraction. |
| **Automatic Relief** | **Macro Stress Protocol (MSP):** pre-negotiated, data-driven triggers that automatically cut royalties, defer brand charges, and release liquidity. | Prevents ad-hoc bargaining and payment strikes. |
| **Joint Governance** | **Resilience Councils:** two-thirds franchisee-elected, independent chair, binding authority over MSP declarations and CRO approvals. | Neutralizes militancy through perceived fairness and transparency. |
| **Stable-Market Firewall** | **Brand Integrity Levy (BIL):** 0.75% of gross system sales from *all* markets funds a bankruptcy-remote **System Resilience Fund**. | Ring-fences corporate and stable-market P&Ls while treating support as catastrophic brand insurance, not charity. |

**Stress-Tested Verdict:** Under sustained macro stress (20–50% annual inflation; 30–100% currency depreciation over three years), the BLCF architecture **decouples franchisee solvency from FX extraction**. Royalty-driven mass defaults are avoided. A realistic 15–25% store-closure rate may still occur due to pure macro violence (input-cost inflation, energy shocks, volume collapse), but systemic collapse is averted.

## 2. MARKET CONTEXT & RISKS

### 2.1 The Doom Loop
In Turkey, Egypt, and Nigeria, the legacy fixed-USD royalty created a pro-cyclical death spiral:
1. **FX Shock** → local-currency cost of USD royalty spikes;
2. **Liquidity Squeeze** → franchisees cut crew wages and CAPEX;
3. **Brand Erosion** → mystery-shop scores and food-safety compliance slip;
4. **Contagion** → viral consumer narratives damage global brand equity, penalizing stable-market stores;
5. **Franchisor Impairment** → receivables become uncollectable, store count collapses, and development fees vanish.

### 2.2 Risk Matrix by Market

| Market | Primary Risk | Association Militancy | FX Regime | Systemic Threat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Turkey** | Hyperinflation + TRY volatility | High (consolidated, politically aware) | Managed float, deep forwards | Payment strikes; systemic NPLs |
| **Egypt** | Parallel-market premium + FX rationing | Medium (relationship-driven, hierarchical) | CBE-managed, dual rate | Trapped cash; import-payment default |
| **Nigeria** | Naira illiquidity + energy fragility | High (fragmented, anti-extractive narrative) | Multiple windows, informal market | Abandonment; supply-chain collapse |
| **Indonesia** | Moderate IDR volatility + institutional discipline | Medium (rules-oriented, AFI-led) | Managed float | Slow-burn localization failure |
| **Brazil** | Frequent BRL depreciation + legalistic bargaining | Medium-High (ABF sectoral tradition) | Inflation-targeting float | Convenção-driven system-wide renegotiation |

### 2.3 Stable-Market Concerns
Company-owned and stable-currency franchise stores fear:
- **Cross-subsidy arbitrage:** Emerging-market operators receive backstopped financing and royalty holidays while stable-market GMs do not.
- **Capital allocation leakage:** Contingent liabilities in stressed markets crowd out stable-market remodel and innovation budgets.
- **Brand contagion:** Physical decay in Lagos or Cairo becomes a global consumer narrative overnight.

These risks are addressed through the **SPV Firewall and Brand Integrity Levy** (§7.3).

## 3. RESTRUCTURING PRINCIPLES & DEFINED TERMS

All instruments in this plan are governed by five principles and the following precisely defined terms.

### 3.1 The Five Principles
1. **No Hard-Currency Recourse.** Base royalties are 100% LCY, percentage-of-revenue obligations. No USD invoice is generated at the store level.
2. **Shared Pain / Shared Gain.** The franchisor accepts temporary royalty compression during macro stress in exchange for participation in recovery upside via CROs and prosperity kickers.
3. **Joint Market Governance.** Each country establishes a legally recognized **Franchisee-Franchisor Resilience Council** (two-thirds franchisee association representatives; one-third franchisor; plus an independent chair). The Council declares macro stress, administers relief, and audits methodology.
4. **Counter-Cyclical Liquidity.** Escrows and reserves are *released* during stress, not increased. Franchisor-backed financing expands automatically when MSP triggers are hit.
5. **Global Covenant, Local P&L.** The **Brand Integrity Levy** (0.75% of gross system sales), funded by *all* markets—including profitable stable-currency stores—finances the ring-fenced **System Resilience Fund**. Stable-market assets are protected by bankruptcy-remote SPVs.

### 3.2 Defined Financial Instruments

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Deferred Floor Obligation (DFO)** | A contingent, non-interest-bearing, non-recourse claim that accrues only when a store’s LCY base royalty falls below a USD-reference floor (expressed in LCY via a 12-month moving average FX rate). It is **not debt**. It is payable only upon store sale, refinancing, or post-MSD recovery. It is **subordinated** to all trade, tax, and bank creditors; **capped** at 18 months of floor; and **frozen** during MSP. Recognized under IFRS 9 at probability-weighted recoverable amount. |
| **Convertible Royalty Obligation (CRO)** | An equity-like capital injection from the franchisor (via a local SPV) to a franchisee. Repaid via a fixed LCY percentage of monthly sales (δ, typically 1.0%–1.5%) until a multiple (μ, typically 1.25×–1.35×) is reached. **No fixed maturity.** No USD denomination. Requires majority approval by the local Resilience Council. |
| **System Standard Charge (SSC)** | A mandatory 1.5% of Net Sales held in a blocked local spend-account for CAPEX, crew training, and digital infrastructure. Not a profit royalty. Halved and deferrable under MSP. |
| **Macro Stress Protocol (MSP)** | A pre-negotiated automatic relief regime triggered when market-level inflation, FX volatility, or real-activity indicators breach thresholds for >45 days. Declared by the local Resilience Council. |
| **Brand Integrity Levy (BIL)** | A 0.75% assessment on gross system sales from *all* markets, funding the bankruptcy-remote System Resilience Fund. Treated as catastrophic brand insurance. |
| **Symmetric Collar** | A transparent, hedgeable band around the 24-month moving average FX rate. If local currency depreciates beyond the band, the base royalty rate (β) auto-reduces for two quarters; if it appreciates beyond the band, β auto-increases. |
| **Performance Corridor** | A store-level P&L linkage where the base royalty rate adjusts based on store-level EBITDA margin brackets, replacing or supplementing the symmetric collar in high-operational-volatility markets. |
| **Resilience Council** | The local joint-governance body with binding authority over MSP declarations, CRO approvals, and DFO-to-CRO conversions. |
| **Franchise Resilience SPV** | A bankruptcy-remote special-purpose vehicle in each emerging market that holds cash collateral, issues CROs, books guarantees, and absorbs first-loss. Creditors have **no recourse** to the corporate parent or stable-market cash flows. |

### 4.1 Turkey — High Inflation, Deep FX Volatility, Militant Franchisee Associations

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Base Royalty (β)** | **4.0% of Net Sales in TRY.** Invoiced exclusively in TRY. |
| **Volatility Absorber** | **Symmetric Collar:** If USD/TRY vs 24-month moving average (MA) is within ±15%, β = 4.0%. If TRY depreciates >15% beyond the band, β auto-reduces to **3.5%** for two quarters. If TRY appreciates >15% below the band, β rises to **4.5%** for two quarters. |
| **DFO Reference** | **$18,000 / store / annum**, converted to TRY at the 12-month MA rate. Accrues only if (β × Net Sales) < DFO reference in TRY terms. **Frozen during MSP.** Capped at 18 months of floor. |
| **SSC** | **1.5%** of Net Sales to blocked spend-account. Halved to 0.75% and deferrable for 180 days under MSP. |
| **MSP Triggers** | • TÜFE >30% y/y<br>• USD/TRY spot >20% above 24M MA<br>• Industrial PMI <45 |
| **Working Capital** | 90-day revolving TRY facility via **Garanti BBVA / İş Bankası**. Rate: TRL reference + 250 bps. Secured by SPV cash collateral. **No corporate guarantee.** |
| **CAPEX Financing** | Local TRY-denominated equipment leasing via Turkish lessors. Franchisor provides **15% second-loss guarantee**, capped at **TRY 120 million system-wide.** |
| **CRO Terms** | Available during MSP. δ = 1.5% of monthly sales; μ = 1.30×. |
| **FX / Hedging** | Franchisor maintains a central TRY/USD NDF book in London; basis risk minimized because DFO uses the same 12M MA. |
| **Guardrails** | • Labor: Crew wages ≥ legal minimum +15%. Failure triggers **Council-mandated remediation plan**, not automatic penalty.<br>• Digital: POS offline >3 days/month triggers **Council investigation** (not automatic sweep).<br>• Mystery shop <80/100 for two consecutive quarters = mandatory ops consultant (franchisee-paid) + Council review. |
| **Governance Note** | Resilience Council elects 6 association reps + 3 franchisor reps + 1 independent economist (e.g., Istanbul Chamber of Commerce). Binding arbitration under ICC rules. |

### 4.5 Brazil — Frequent Depreciation, Deep Credit Markets, Legalistic Associations

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Base Royalty (β)** | **4.0% of Net Sales in BRL.** Invoiced exclusively in BRL. |
| **Volatility Absorber** | **Real Indexed Floor:** Instead of a USD debt obligation, the franchisor’s minimum BRL receipt is indexed to preserve real purchasing power against US supply-chain costs:<br>$$\text{Floor}_{t}^{\text{BRL}} = \text{Floor}_{t-1}^{\text{BRL}} \times \frac{(1 + \text{IPCA}_{t})}{(1 + \text{US PPI}_{t})}$$<br>If US PPI > IPCA, the BRL floor deflates—**shared pain.** If IPCA > US PPI, it inflates—**shared protection.** This is a settlement mechanics adjustment, not a loan to the franchisee. |
| **DFO Reference** | **Not applicable.** Brazil uses the Real Indexed Floor as the franchisor’s protection mechanism, eliminating the need for a USD-referenced DFO. |
| **SSC** | **1.5%** to blocked spend-account. Halved/deferrable under MSP. |
| **MSP Triggers** | • IPCA >15% y/y<br>• BRL/USD >15% above 24M MA<br>• Industrial output contraction >2 consecutive quarters |
| **Working Capital** | **FIDC (Fundo de Investimento em Direitos Creditórios)** structure. Franchisee receivables are securitized; franchisor SPV subscribes to the **senior tranche (60%)**; franchisees hold junior. Rate: CDI + 4.5%. |
| **CAPEX Financing** | Parallel facility with **BNDES** green-energy lending (solar, efficient kitchens). Franchisor provides 15% cash collateral. |
| **CRO Terms** | Available during MSP. δ = 1.0% of monthly sales; μ = 1.25×. |
| **Multi-Year Framework** | **5-Year “Convenção de Royalties”** negotiated with **ABF (Associação Brasileira de Franchising)** and notarized. Fixes β, MSP trigger table, and pre-agreed royalty holidays (up to 6 months) in exchange for automatic 24-month contract extensions. |
| **Guardrails** | • Labor: FGTS and union-dues compliance audited semi-annually; failures trigger Council-mandated remediation.<br>• Quality: PROCON-relevant consumer complaints >system median = mandatory ops review. |
| **Governance Note** | Brazilian Resilience Council is constituted under the **Convenção** and uses **Aditivo ao Contrato de Franquia** for all amendments. Portuguese-first documentation; English versions secondary. Binding arbitration in São Paulo under CAM-CCBC rules. |
