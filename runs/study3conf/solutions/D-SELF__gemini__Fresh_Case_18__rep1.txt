# Executive Strategy: Global Health Feature Rollout (2026)

## 1. Regulatory & Compliance Architecture
To mitigate "look-back" risks and misbranding, the company will adopt a **Strict Segregated Intended-Use (SSIU) Model**.

*   **Geo-Fenced Content Ecosystem:** All digital assets, whitepapers, and clinician portals are geo-fenced. 
    *   **Medical Markets (EU/Japan):** Assets emphasize *diagnostic screening* and *clinical pathways*.
    *   **Wellness Markets (US/EM):** Assets are strictly limited to *health trends* and *wellness insights*.
    *   **Control Protocol:** Access to clinical whitepapers and peer-reviewed studies is blocked based on user IP and regional account settings to prevent "intended use" expansion.
*   **Unified Vigilance Data Lake:** Despite software segregation, all adverse events (AEs) and device malfunctions are routed to a single, global **ISO 13485-compliant Quality Management System (QMS)**. This ensures that a "near-miss" in a wellness market triggers the same safety review and regulatory reporting standards as a medical-grade failure in the EU, insulating the firm from liability.
*   **Harmonized Labeling:** All global packaging and digital IFUs will utilize a standardized "Conditional Labeling" framework. This provides consistent risk communication regardless of local classification, protecting the firm in international product liability litigation.

## 2. Clinical Evidence & Validation Strategy
The **Master Clinical Protocol (MCP)** is recalibrated to address regulatory nuances:

*   **PMDA Ethnobridging:** The MCP includes a statistically significant, pre-defined Japanese sub-cohort. This ensures clinical findings meet PMDA ethnobridging requirements, eliminating the need for a costly, secondary localized study.
*   **Integrated PMCF (Post-Market Clinical Follow-up):** The Wellness Core will be designed to capture data with the same fidelity as the Medical Core (though processed through different algorithmic filters). This allows the firm to aggregate a **global longitudinal data set** for risk/benefit assessment, satisfying MDR mandates for continuous post-market monitoring without compromising the segregation of diagnostic utility.

| Timeline | Milestone | Strategic Goal |

## 4. Physician Engagement & Credibility
The transition moves away from "marketing to doctors" toward **Clinical Utility Integration**:

*   **EHR-Ready Data:** By adopting **HL7 FHIR standards**, the device data enters the physician's workflow as a standardized input, not a proprietary report.
*   **Gatekeeper Framing:** Clinical communications emphasize the wearable as a **triage filter**. By providing a "Confidence Score" for each screening, we ensure that physicians only see the most clinically relevant data, positioning our product as a partner in reducing diagnostic backlog rather than a source of "data noise."

## 5. Risk Mitigation Summary
*   **Regulatory Entrapment:** Addressed by separating the *logic* (Dual-Core software) from the *marketing* (Geo-fencing) and the *safety reporting* (Unified Vigilance Data Lake).
*   **Liability Exposure:** Mitigated by mandatory "Conditional Labeling" and strict adherence to the "Instructions for Use" (IFU) language in all markets, regardless of local regulatory status.
*   **Operational Fatigue:** Version control is managed by treating the Medical Core as the "Master Release," with the Wellness Core updated as a subset, ensuring that compliance updates are propagated efficiently across the global portfolio.
