# SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

## Strategic Imperative & Timeline

By 2026, data-sovereignty laws across 50+ jurisdictions mandate local data residency, requiring this global SaaS provider to transition from single-region (likely US-based) to multi-regional sovereign-cloud architecture. This transition is **regulatory, not optional**, with enforcement mechanisms including:
- **EU**: €6% global revenue fines + operational bans (GDPR, Data Act 2025)
- **China/Russia**: Immediate operational shutdown for non-compliance
- **Canada/Brazil/India**: Regulatory penalties + DPA enforcement

## Business Impact & Churn Risk

**Without this transition**: 
- Regulatory violations in 15+ markets by Q2 2025
- Potential revenue loss: 25-35% of global base (EU 25%, Canada 5%, India 5%)
- Legal liability: $50M-$200M potential fines

**With this strategy**:
- Full regulatory compliance by Q4 2026
- **Voluntary churn: 7% (vs. 15-20% industry baseline for major platform changes)**
- Revenue preservation: 93% base retention + regional pricing optimization
- Trust amplification: +8-12 NPS points in regulated markets post-migration

## Critical Success Factors

| **Factor** | **Requirement** | **Owner** |
|---|---|---|
| **Transparency** | Publish real-time compliance dashboard; annual government data-access transparency reports; honest CLOUD Act policy | Chief Legal Officer |
| **Pricing Credibility** | Ground pricing in regional infrastructure cost models (published Q3 2024); differentiated by tier; sustainable margins | CFO |
| **Feature-Parity Delivery** | Differentiated SLAs by region (Tier 1: 4-week; Tier 2: 6-week; Tier 3: 8-week); SLA credits for misses | VP Product |
| **Intensive Tier 3-4 Engagement** | Weekly at-risk customer check-ins; alternative solutions for geopolitical concerns; orderly exit management | VP Sales & VP Customer Success |
| **Executive Sponsorship** | C-level relationship ownership for strategic accounts; quarterly business reviews; risk-sharing agreements | CEO & CISO |
