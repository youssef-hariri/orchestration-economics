# LATAM MARKET ENTRY STRATEGY
## Disciplined Phasing Through a Mexico-First MVP

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

To capture share in Latin America without engaging multilevel-marketing (MLM) incumbents on their home turf—physical relationship selling, catalog distribution, and consignment credit (*fiado*)—ABB will redefine the battleground around **digital product authority, inclusive shade innovation, and skincare ritual education**.

The strategy deploys a **“D2C Flagship + Marketplace Outpost + Social Commerce”** hybrid. Mercado Libre ecosystems provide reach and trust transference; owned channels deliver margin and first-party data; TikTok Shop, WhatsApp, and Kwai capture conversion and community.

**Board Ask:** Approve a **6-month, capital-efficient Mexico MVP** (Year 1 envelope: **$5.2M**; local team: **8–10 FTEs**) to validate unit economics, shade accuracy, and logistics before authorizing sequential expansion. Colombia and Brazil are fully developed as **conditional Phase 2 and 3**, gated by Mexico performance and regulatory readiness. Brazil’s timeline reflects ANVISA’s 8–18 month cosmetic registration reality.

**Strategic Moat:** Product velocity + digital intimacy. Not consultant networks.

---

## 1. STRATEGIC THESIS

| Incumbent Strength | ABB Counter-Move |
|---|---|
| **Trust via personal relationship** | Async Virtual Skin Concierge: personalized 90-second video shade matches delivered via WhatsApp |
| **Distribution ubiquity + *fiado*** | BNPL everywhere (Kueski/Addi/Slope) + same-evening urban delivery via quick commerce |
| **Community & belonging** | **The Ritual Circle:** single-tier advocacy (15% store credit, no downlines, no inventory buy-in) |
| **Catalog convenience** | D2C subscriptions + marketplace same-day shipping; zero social obligation to sell |

**Narrative Anchor:** *“Beauty advice should be free. Your skin data should belong to you. And the best ingredients shouldn’t wait for a catalog cycle.”*

## 2. BOARD DECISIONS REQUIRED

> **DECISION 1: YEAR 1 BUDGET & HEADCOUNT ENVELOPE**
> Approve a **$5.2M Year 1 investment cap** focused entirely on Mexico MVP, with **8–10 local FTEs** (GM, regulatory lead, community manager, logistics coordinator, 4x concierge aestheticians, 2x performance marketing). Colombia and Brazil carry $0 deployable budget until Mexico go/no-go is passed.

> **DECISION 2: MEXICO-ONLY MVP MANDATE**
> Authorize a **6-month Mexico soft launch** (Month 6 entry) before any Colombia/Brazil market commitment. Expansion beyond Mexico requires explicit board sign-off at the Month 5 gate.

> **DECISION 3: SHADE-RANGE MINIMUM VIABLE LAUNCH**
> Mandate **24 complexion SKUs for Mexico** anchored on “Golden,” “Neutral-Cálido,” and “Profundo-Rojizo” clusters (MAC NC25–NC55 range). Approve **150-skin model validation panels** plus environmental oxidation testing in CDMX/Monterrey conditions. Deep-shade accuracy is a non-negotiable brand-permission ticket.

> **DECISION 4: HYBRID CHANNEL MIX & PRICING ARCHITECTURE**
> Ratify the **“Flagship + Outpost + Social”** model. Initial Mexico revenue mix target: **25% D2C / 60% Marketplace / 15% Social**. Marketplace listings will carry **MSRP + 10–15% markup** to offset 15–25% platform take rates. No discounting on marketplaces to prevent channel conflict.

> **DECISION 5: REGULATORY FLOAT & RISK RESERVE**
> Build a **+90-day regulatory buffer** into all filings (COFEPRIS/INVIMA/ANVISA). Approve a **$400K risk reserve** for regulatory delays, returns logistics, and FX volatility.

## 3. FINANCIAL GUARDRAILS & UNIT ECONOMICS

> **BUDGET IMPLICATION 1: MARKETPLACE COMMISSIONS REQUIRE MARGIN ARCHITECTURE**
> Mercado Libre total take rates (commission + fulfillment + ads) range from **18–25% in Mexico**, up to **20–30% in Brazil**. ABB will absorb this via a **marketplace markup**, treating these channels as paid acquisition. D2C repurchase and subscription LTV must recover first-order losses.

> **BUDGET IMPLICATION 2: FREE SHIPPING IS A CONTROLLED BURN**
> Free shipping thresholds are set at **1.0x AOV** (~$38 MXN equivalent basket). At launch volumes, this implies a **$4.50–$6.00 per-order subsidy**. Model assumes **$85K/month shipping burn** during Mexico MVP, declining to $45K/month as AOV rises through bundling.

> **BUDGET IMPLICATION 3: RETURNS ECONOMICS**
> “No questions asked” 30-day D2C returns with **prepaid labels** are a trust weapon against MLMs, but expensive. Estimated **12% return rate** on color cosmetics at launch; reverse logistics cost **$8–12 per parcel**. Monthly returns provision: **$22K** (Mexico MVP run-rate).

| Channel | Target AOV | Take Rate / Commission | Shipping Subsidy | Returns Cost | Contribution Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Owned D2C** | $42 | 2.9% (payment) | $5.25 | $1.10 | **18–22%** |
| **Mercado Libre** | $38 | 22% | $0 (marketplace FBA) | $0 | **8–12%** |
| **TikTok Shop** | $35 | 15% | $3.50 | $0.80 | **10–14%** |
| **Blended** | $39 | — | — | — | **12–16%** |

**Target:** Achieve **blended 15% contribution margin** by Month 6 of Mexico operations.

## 4. PHASE 1: MEXICO MVP (MONTHS 1–12)

### 4.1 Entry Criteria & Timeline
- **Month 0–3:** COFEPRIS filing (with +90-day buffer), local entity incorporation, 3PL contracting.
- **Month 2–4:** Shade validation (150 models, CDMX/Monterrey/GDL), AR try-on QA, local warehousing fit-out (Teoloyucan corridor).
- **Month 4–5:** Nano-creator seeding (max **50 relationships**), waitlist build, skin quiz beta.
- **Month 6:** Soft launch (D2C + Mercado Libre + TikTok Shop).
- **Month 9:** Go/No-Go review for Colombia authorization.
- **Month 12:** Mexico optimization; subscription and refill rollout.

### 4.2 Mexico Channel Architecture
| Channel | Role | Target Mix |
|---|---|---|
| **Owned D2C** | Margin, data, subscriptions, AR try-on | 25% |
| **Mercado Libre** | Acquisition, trust, installments | 55% |
| **TikTok Shop** | Virality, live selling, Gen Z | 15% |
| **Amazon / Other** | Search test only | <5% |

**Payments:** Oxxo, Mercado Pago, Kueski Pay, NuPay, PayPal, Meses Sin Intereses.  
**Fulfillment:** Local 3PL (Teoloyucan) with **2-day national / same-day Mexico City metro** via courier partners (Redpack/Jadlog).

### 4.3 Creator & Social Commerce (Mexico)
- **Seeding Cohort:** **50 nano-creators (3k–20k)** with personalized shade-matched kits. One local community manager manages relationships, UGC rights, and tax documentation (*RFC* verification for deductible gifting).
- **Performance Cohort:** **20 micro-creators (20k–150k)** on 3-month retainers (not 6) with affiliate codes (10%).
- **TikTok Shop:** Live-selling hosted by brand team (not external agency) during MVP to preserve margin and test product-market fit.

## 5. PHASES 2 & 3: CONDITIONAL EXPANSION

### 5.1 Colombia (Month 10–12 Entry, Contingent)
**Gate Criteria:** Mexico blended contribution margin ≥12%; shade return rate <10%; Mercado Libre store rating ≥4.5.

- **Assortment:** 28 complexion SKUs heroing “Piel Canela / Dorado Profundo.”
- **Channels:** D2C (40%) / Mercado Libre (35%) / Rappi Quick Commerce (12%) / Falabella (8%).
- **Payments:** PSE, Efecty, Baloto, Addi, Nequi.
- **Logistics:** 3PL partner in **Funza/Mosquera corridor** (Cundinamarca).
- **Creator Model:** 40 micro-creators; affiliate payouts via PSE/Nequi with *retención en la fuente* compliance.

### 5.2 Brazil (Month 18–20 Entry, Contingent)
**Gate Criteria:** Colombia and Mexico combined unit economics positive; ANVISA approval in hand; local entity + tax ID (*CNPJ*) established.

- **Assortment:** 40 complexion SKUs + body shades + alcohol-free SPF 50+ portfolio.
- **Channels:** Mercado Livre (40%) / D2C (25%) / Shopee (15%) / Magalu/Americanas (7%) / Amazon (8%) / Quick Commerce (5%).
- **Payments:** Pix (table stakes), Boleto, 10x parcelamento via Mercado Pago/PagSeguro. Pix discount (-5%) funded as CAC.
- **Logistics:** Guarulhos/São Paulo hub; reverse logistics via Loggi/Jadlog.
- **Creator Model:** Kwai mandatory for Class C/D Northeast reach; “resenha sincera” UGC prioritized over polished ads.

## APPENDIX A: MEXICO PLAYBOOK

### A.1 Consumer Profile
- **Aesthetic:** “Arreglada” — defined brows, matte-but-luminous skin, bold lip. Gen Z rapidly adopting “glass skin” via TikTok.
- **Pain Point:** Makeup must survive pollution and long commutes (CDMX/Monterrey); long-wear claims are trust signals.
- **Underserved Segment:** Indigenous/mestiza consumers with deep golden and red-brown undertones historically mismatched by pink/neutral Asian formulations.

### A.2 Shade Mandate
- **24 SKUs at launch**, MAC NC25–NC55 equivalent.
- **Hero Clusters:** Golden, Neutral-Cálido, Profundo-Rojizo.
- **Naming:** Local lexicon only (e.g., “Dorado Medio,” “Rojizo Profundo”); no transliterated Korean shade names.

### A.3 Platform & Channel specifics
- **Mercado Libre:** Full/Envíos integration; aggressive Mercado Ads required for organic ranking.
- **TikTok Shop:** Live selling budgeted as primary conversion channel, not test.
- **WhatsApp Business:** Catalog + payment confirmation core funnel.
- **O2O:** Oxxo cash payment and pickup mandatory for underbanked Gen Z.

### A.4 Influencer Tiers
- **Nano (3k–20k):** Highest trust-to-CAC. Focus on “tías beauty” and pharmacy-prestige mixers.
- **Representation:** Explicit quota for “raza”-coded creators reflecting Indigenous/mestiza identity.

## APPENDIX B: COLOMBIA PLAYBOOK *(Phase 2 — Contingent)*

### B.1 Consumer Profile
- **Aesthetic:** “Bien arreglada” + pageant grooming discipline. Skincare as ritual, not trend. Disproportionate hair-care spend.
- **Shade Literacy:** Most sophisticated in LATAM due to Yanbal/Belcorp consultant education. Consumers buy by undertone name, not generic depth.

### B.2 Shade Mandate
- **28 SKUs at launch**, heroing “Piel Canela / Dorado Profundo.”
- **Critical Detail:** Neck-and-chest matching is culturally mandatory; mismatched jawlines drive immediate returns.

### B.3 Platform & Channel specifics
- **Mercado Libre:** 35% target.
- **Rappi / Quick Commerce:** 12% target; impulse beauty rates exceed Mexico.
- **Falabella.com / Linio:** Prestige signaling, low volume.
- **Payments:** PSE, Efecty, Baloto non-negotiable. BNPL via Addi.

### B.4 Influencer & Operations
- **Mid-tier (100k–500k)** set aspirational tone from Bogotá/Medellín; **Micro creators** in Cali/Barranquilla/Bucaramanga drive conversion.
- **Compliance:** Affiliates require prompt PSE/Nequi payment; Net-60 terms prohibited. *Retención en la fuente* must be automated.

---

## APPENDIX C: BRAZIL PLAYBOOK *(Phase 3 — Contingent)*

### C.1 Consumer Profile
- **Aesthetic:** “Beleza natural” executed with rigorous self-care. Heavy spend on body care, haircare, SPF.
- **Priority Hierarchy:** Performance > scent (“cheiroso”) > price-per-mL > sustainability. Vegan/cruelty-free is a bonus, not a driver.

### C.2 Shade Mandate
- **40 SKUs minimum**, deepest 30% rivaling Mari Maria / Boca Rosa / Fenty.
- **Body Category Essential:** Bronzers, glow lotions, body concealers, body SPF.
- **Undertone Vocabulary:** Mirror consumer self-ID—“tom amarelado,” “dourado profundo,” “tom acobreado.”

### C.3 Formulation Notes
- **SPF 50+** must be alcohol-free (no alcohol denat.). High-alcohol Asian sunscreens trigger stinging and negative reviews in humid climates.

### C.4 Platform & Channel specifics
- **Mercado Livre:** 40% target.
- **Shopee:** 15% target; mandatory due to subsidized shipping/livestreaming. Commission can drop to 0–5% in aggressive periods.
- **Magalu / Americanas (B2W):** 7%; critical for Southeast female consumers.
- **Kwai:** Mandatory for Class C/D Northeast and interior reach.
- **Payments:** Pix is table stakes. Offer **5% Pix discount**. **10x parcelamento** expected even on D2C.
