**MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Executive Strategy & Restructuring Committee  
**DATE:** October 24, 2023  
**SUBJECT:** Executive Brief: Franchise Restructuring Strategy for Emerging Market Resilience  
**MARKETS IN SCOPE:** Turkey, Egypt, Nigeria, Indonesia, Brazil  

---

### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
The franchisor’s footprint in Turkey, Egypt, Nigeria, Indonesia, and Brazil is currently exposed to severe currency depreciation, hyper-inflation, and organized collective bargaining by franchisee associations. Our legacy model—reliant on fixed, USD-denominated royalties—is economically unsustainable in these volatile environments and risks triggering mass defaults and brand erosion. 

To preserve long-term brand economics and protect the corporate balance sheet, the Executive Committee has developed a **legally bulletproof, economically sustainable restructuring strategy**. By leveraging the robust cash flows from our stable-market, company-owned stores, we will transition these emerging markets from an extractive royalty model to a resilient, asset-light, and strictly localized economic partnership. 

This brief outlines the four strategic pillars of the restructuring plan, engineered to ensure absolute regulatory compliance, neutralize collective adversarial action, and optimize trapped liquidity.

---

### **PILLAR 1: LEGALLY BULLETPROOF ROYALTY RESTRUCTURING**
*Objective: Transition to localized, inflation-adjusted royalties with absolute compliance regarding local franchise and foreign exchange laws.*

*   **Consideration-Backed Contract Novation:** 
    Unilateral contract modifications or coercive threats violate the implied covenant of good faith and local franchise laws (e.g., Brazil’s Lei 13.966/19). We will offer a "Relief Addendum" backed by tangible legal consideration (e.g., a 12-month cap on marketing fund contributions or a CapEx subsidy). In exchange, franchisees voluntarily sign the addendum to transition to the new local-currency royalty model, ensuring mutual consent and legal defensibility.
*   **Strictly Localized Indexation:** 
    Direct or indirect USD-pegging violates Brazil’s Real Plan Law and Turkey’s Decree 32. All foreign currency floors are strictly prohibited.
    *   *Brazil & Indonesia:* Royalties will be a percentage of local currency sales, adjusted quarterly based strictly on official government-published indices (e.g., Brazil’s IPCA, Indonesia’s IHKG) combined with official agricultural price indices. 
    *   *Turkey:* Royalties will be denominated strictly in TRY, indexed to the domestic Producer Price Index (TÜFE).
    *   *Egypt & Nigeria:* Implementation of a Pure Local Currency Percentage. The obligation and calculation remain 100% domestic.
*   **Monthly Menu Price Sync & Volume Protection:** 
    To address structural depreciation without triggering illegal indirect dollarization, we will implement a Monthly Menu Price Sync. If local currency depreciates significantly month-over-month, franchisees are authorized to immediately adjust menu prices. **Crucially, the royalty baseline is not recalibrated based on FX rates.** The royalty remains a fixed percentage of the resulting local currency gross sales, preserving our legal shield while allowing franchisees to protect their gross margins.
    *   *Risk Mitigation:* To offset the lag in official government indices, the Monthly Menu Price Sync allows franchisees to react to real-time, street-level market shocks.

---

### **PILLAR 2: BALANCE-SHEET NEUTRAL LIQUIDITY & TRAPPED CASH MANAGEMENT**
*Objective: Inject working capital into the system without acting as an unlicensed bank, while strategically deploying "trapped" local currency in restricted FX markets.*

*   **Embedded Finance & Data Underwriting:** 
    Direct corporate supply-chain factoring risks triggering unlicensed banking regulations. We will partner with licensed local fintechs (e.g., Nubank, Paystack). The franchisor will provide secure Data Underwriting (sharing POS data), allowing the fintech to issue working capital loans. The franchisor assumes zero direct loan risk but negotiates preferential interest rates based on bulk volume.
*   **Supplier Backstops via Standby Letters of Credit (SBLC):** 
    We will leverage our strong global credit rating to issue SBLCs to key global and local suppliers. This guarantees payment in the event of franchisee default, enabling suppliers to extend 90-to-120-day payment terms to franchisees. Balance sheet exposure is limited to catastrophic, systemic defaults.
*   **"Trapped Cash" Recycling & Reinvestment Framework:** 
    In Egypt and Nigeria, severe FX restrictions make USD repatriation nearly impossible. We will recycle trapped local currency (EGP/NGN) into local corporate assets to prevent cash erosion. Trapped funds will be deployed to:
    1. Acquire prime real estate for new company-owned stores (converting illiquid cash into hard assets).
    2. Finance local corporate marketing and overhead, reducing global USD outflow requirements.
    3. Fund asset-light supply chain initiatives (detailed in Pillar 4).
*   **FDI-Compliant HoldCo for Strategic Assets:** 
    For highly strategic, at-risk stores, we will execute debt-to-equity swaps via a Local HoldCo Structure (compliant with local FDI registries like Indonesia’s BKPM). Past-due royalties will be forgiven in exchange for preferred, non-voting equity. Dividends from these entities will be retained locally to fund the "Trapped Cash Recycling" program.

---

### **PILLAR 3: LEGALLY DEFENSIBLE ASSOCIATION ENGAGEMENT**
*Objective: Neutralize collective adversarial action through transparent, framework-based negotiations that respect local collective-bargaining norms.*

*   **Master Framework Agreements with Transparent Opt-In Tiers:** 
    Secret individual deals trigger class-action injunctions. We will negotiate a Master Relief Framework directly with association leadership, establishing a baseline of relief for all members. Within this, we will introduce Transparent Opt-In Tiers based on objective, published metrics (e.g., Tier 2 enhanced relief requires opening audited books and signing a 3-year renewal). This ensures non-discriminatory, legally defensible criteria.
*   **The "Total System Value" Pivot:** 
    We will shift negotiations from a zero-sum royalty battle to a Value Exchange Matrix. If associations demand a 1% royalty cut, we will counter with a 0.5% cut *plus* the Embedded Finance program (saving 3% in working capital costs) *plus* localized menu engineering (reducing COGS by 4%). This expands the pie, protecting top-line revenue while improving franchisee net margins.
*   **Joint Crisis Communication & PR Contingency Protocol:** 
    The Master Framework will include a Mutual Non-Disparagement and Joint Communication Clause. 
    *   *Governance Mitigation:* Because enforcing non-disparagement against decentralized members is difficult, corporate will maintain a Pre-Drafted PR Contingency Plan. This will only be deployed for egregious, demonstrably false leaks, with corporate leadership maintaining transparent back-channel communication with association leaders to preserve the diplomatic relationship.

---

### **PILLAR 4: ASSET-LIGHT OPERATIONAL HEDGING**
*Objective: Address the root causes of margin compression without forcing an asset-light QSR franchisor into capital-intensive vertical integration or triggering banking regulations.*

*   **Asset-Light Supply Chain Localization:** 
    Outright M&A of agricultural manufacturers destroys our asset-light model, and direct "supplier financing" triggers money-lending laws. Instead, we will execute **Advance Payments and Pre-Purchases**. Using trapped cash, the franchisor will pre-purchase future inventory from local manufacturers. 
    *   *Risk Mitigation:* To avoid recharacterization as disguised financing by tax or central bank authorities, pre-payment terms will be strictly aligned with actual inventory turnover cycles (60-90 days). Advance payments will be limited to highly vetted, systemically important suppliers; riskier suppliers will be backstopped via the SBLC mechanism.
*   **Localized Value Architecture ("Shrinkflation" & Tiering):** 
    In hyper-inflationary environments, consumers trade down. We will temporarily suspend strict brand standard enforcement regarding portion sizes for specific "value tier" menu items, allowing franchisees to introduce smaller, lower-price-point versions of core products to maintain psychological entry prices. 
    *   *Risk Mitigation:* Corporate will actively monitor local price control decrees. If governments impose sudden price freezes, the franchisor will immediately pivot to aggressive COGS engineering rather than price increases, and work with associations to lobby for QSR exemptions.

### **IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP & BOARD OVERSIGHT**

*   **Days 1–30 (Triage & Legal Structuring):** 
    *   Issue formal "Standstill Agreements" to all franchisee associations, pausing terminations and litigation for 60 days. 
    *   Engage local counsel in Brazil and Turkey to finalize the Consideration-Backed Novation and ensure strict compliance with local currency laws.
    *   Initiate RFPs for local fintech partners and identify local suppliers for advance-payment agreements.
*   **Days 31–60 (Association Negotiation & Framework Design):** 
    *   Convene Joint Strategic Councils and present the "Total System Value" matrix. 
    *   Negotiate and sign Master Relief Frameworks with associations in Brazil and Turkey.
    *   Finalize SBLC arrangements and establish Local HoldCo structures for trapped-cash recycling.
*   **Days 61–90 (Rollout & Opt-In Execution):** 
    *   Launch Transparent Opt-In Tiers to the franchisee base. 
    *   Execute Embedded Finance loans and roll out the Monthly Menu Price Sync mechanism.
    *   Execute 60-to-90-day advance-payment supply chain agreements using recycled trapped cash.
*   **Day 90+ (Stabilization & Monitoring):** 
    *   Transition remaining holdouts utilizing the improved unit economics of early adopters. 
    *   Establish a quarterly Board review to monitor Monthly Menu Price Sync triggers, FX liquidity, and central bank policy shifts.

---

### **CONCLUSION & RECOMMENDATION**
By strictly adhering to local currency indexation laws, separating menu pricing adjustments from royalty baselines, replacing capital-intensive M&A with commercial advance-purchases, and strategically recycling trapped cash, the Executive Committee has engineered a legally unassailable and economically brilliant restructuring. 

This strategy neutralizes collective bargaining threats, protects the corporate balance sheet from toxic debt, and transforms a looming crisis of mass defaults into a modernized, resilient franchise system. **The Executive Committee recommends the Board of Directors approve this restructuring strategy and the associated capital allocation for immediate execution.**
