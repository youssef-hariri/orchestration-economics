# EXECUTIVE BRIEFING: GREEN FINANCING STRATEGY
**TO:** Board of Directors / Investment Committee  
**FROM:** Strategic Finance & Sustainability Office  
**SUBJECT:** Global Decarbonization Funding Strategy: Tiered Taxonomy & Governance Framework  

---

### 1. Executive Summary
To finance the decarbonization of our European, North African, and Southeast Asian operations, we propose a **"Global Core, Regional Add-on"** financing architecture. This strategy addresses the fragmentation of international taxonomies by utilizing the EU Taxonomy as our technical "floor," while deploying Sustainability-Linked Bonds (SLBs) to achieve regional capital market alignment. By implementing a centralized **Internal Carbon Ledger (ICL)** and a rigorous **Tri-Fold Verification Protocol**, we insulate the Group from greenwashing risks and ensure regulatory compliance under evolving EU CSRD and global standards.

### 2. The Tiered Green Bond Framework (TGBF)
We move away from bespoke regional reporting in favor of a standardized, multi-layer classification system that ensures institutional investor confidence:

*   **Tier 1: The Global Core:** All projects must meet the **EU Taxonomy’s "Substantial Contribution"** criteria. This acts as our global benchmark, ensuring eligibility for high-prestige, lower-cost European capital and future-proofing the portfolio against tightening global standards.
*   **Tier 2: Regional Transitions:** Leveraging SLBs, we link bond coupons to verified Scope 1 & 2 emission-intensity reductions. This accommodates regional priorities (e.g., CCUS and hydrogen in the Gulf; industrial efficiency in ASEAN) that may fall outside current strict EUT categories.
*   **Tier 3: Exclusion Buffer:** To protect the Group’s credibility, only projects with "Level A" (Metered/Verified) or "Level B" (Estimated/Reconciled) data quality are permitted into the Green Bond portfolio. "Level C" (Proxied) data is strictly excluded from all green-labeled issuances.

### 3. Financial & Operational Governance
To manage the risk of inconsistent plant-level reporting and potential audit friction, we are establishing a **Decarbonization Data Governance Committee (DDGC)**:

*   **Data Integrity:** The DDGC, chaired by the CFO and CSO, mandates a **15% "Risk-Adjusted Decarbonization Discount"** on all reported savings. This conservative buffer serves as a statistical hedge against data volatility in emerging markets and demonstrates to regulators that our methodology prioritizes integrity over yield.
*   **Methodology Manual:** All verification logic is codified in a publicly accessible, auditor-reviewed *Methodology Manual*. This document transforms our internal risk-management buffers into a defensive legal asset, proving institutional "due diligence."
*   **Physical-to-Digital Reconciliation:** We are deploying a unified API layer across all sites to enforce cross-reference logic (e.g., fuel-input to CO2-output matching) and moving toward physical IoT anchoring for all "Level A" assets.

### 4. Roadmap to Investor Credibility
We are transitioning from *aspirational reporting* to *audited sustainability performance*:

| Horizon | Goal | Strategic Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Short-Term (0-12m)** | Establishment of DDGC & Methodology Manual. | Solidify foundation for "Limited Assurance" reporting. |
| **Mid-Term (12-24m)** | Phased inclusion of Scope 3 (Category 1). | Alignment with tightening EU CSRD requirements. |
| **Long-Term (24m+)** | Full Scope 3 integration for all regions. | Market-leading transparency and "Reasonable Assurance" status. |

### 5. Risk Mitigation & Conclusion
The primary risk to our financing strategy is the variance in measurement quality across our global footprint. By **disclosing the weighted average data quality score** of our assets in every prospectus, we provide investors with radical transparency. We are choosing to be honest about data immaturity today rather than risking "greenwashing" litigation tomorrow.

This strategy protects our cost of capital, ensures regulatory compliance in our highest-value jurisdictions, and positions the Group as a leader in industrial transition financing.

**Recommendation:** Approve the formation of the **DDGC** and authorize the launch of the initial **EU-aligned Green Bond** and **Regional SLB tranches** per the proposed tiering structure.
