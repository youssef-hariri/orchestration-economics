# DETAILED RESTRUCTURING PROPOSAL (8 PAGES)

## SECTION 1: TIERED ROYALTY MODEL WITH CURRENCY & INFLATION INDEXATION

### 1.1 Four-Tier Structure Overview

The model replaces one-size-fits-all 8% USD royalty with differentiated rates calibrated to **currency volatility, inflation regime, and franchisee financial condition**:

- **Tier 1 (Indonesia):** 7.15% effective rate. Stable currency (IDR depreciation <8% projected 2026). Quarterly CPI indexation (±10 bps per 1% variance from 3% target). Performance incentives: volume growth >5% → 25 bps reduction. **Rationale:** Franchisor rewards stability; no FX crisis hedging costs.

- **Tier 2 (Brazil, conditional Turkey):** 6.52–6.82% effective rate. Moderate volatility (15% cumulative FX depreciation). Quarterly CPI indexation (±8 bps variance). **Hybrid settlement options:** (1) 100% USD (franchisee bears FX), (2) 50/50 USD/local currency (franchisor absorbs FX up to ±10% annual depreciation; franchisee bears >10%), (3) 100% local currency at fixed monthly peg (franchisor absorbs full FX). Franchisor pricing reflects hedging cost (~1.5–3% differential vs. option 1). Performance incentives: volume >3% → 20 bps, EBITDA >25% → 15 bps (combined max 35 bps). **Rationale:** Franchisee choice reduces moral hazard; FX cushion prevents cascade from sudden shock; performance rewards align incentives.

- **Tier 3 (Egypt, Nigeria, Turkey if stabilization fails):** 5.34–5.46% effective rate. High volatility (25%+ cumulative FX depreciation; 30–50% inflation). **Fixed quarterly rates** (not continuous monthly indexation—avoids Sharia-law "gharar" interpretation in Egypt). Local-currency-only settlement; franchisor absorbs FX risk (cannot execute derivatives due to Egypt CBN ban, Nigeria CBN prohibition). Franchisor absorption: **50% of FX depreciation >20% annually.** Crisis override: if monthly inflation >8% or FX depreciation >3%, automatic 1% royalty credit for 3-month period. Quarterly audit triggers (sales decline >15%, margin <18%). **Rationale:** Addresses regulatory barriers; risk-sharing (franchisor absorbs tail, franchisee absorbs body) enables system viability; monthly EBITDA >$100K; automatic crisis relief prevents cascade.

- **Tier 4 (Distressed Nigeria & Egypt):** 3.95% effective rate (Y1 restructuring phase). **EBITDA <$100K, debt >$120K USD prerequisites.** Y1 payment: 2% cash monthly, 2% deferred (accrues as restructuring debt, 5-year amortization @ 4% interest). Alternative: debt-for-equity conversion (franchisor takes 15% equity stake for 20–40% debt haircut). **FX absorption: 75% of depreciation >20%.** Monthly covenant monitoring (revenue attestation, POS integration, quarterly audits). Promotion to Tier 3 if: EBITDA >20% for 2 consecutive quarters, debt-to-EBITDA <1.5x, zero covenant breaches. Termination if: royalty unremitted >60 days. **Rationale:** Structured relief enables 12-month restructuring runway; equity alignment ensures franchisor governance; tier promotion creates upside incentive; termination trigger protects franchisor recovery rights.

### 1.2 Settlement Mechanics & FX Hedging

**Indonesia (Tier 1):**
- Standard: 100% USD monthly remittance via wire.
- Option (if IDR weakens >8% YoY): Hybrid 50/50 USD/IDR settlement. Franchisor hedges IDR 50% via Bank Indonesia–licensed dealer forward contract. Cost to franchisor: ~1.5% annual interest differential; embedded in royalty rate at no additional fee to franchisee.
- **Compliance basis:** Bank Indonesia Circular 25/2017 explicitly permits FX derivatives for hedging; franchisor files annual disclosure.

**Brazil & Turkey (Tier 2):**
- **Three monthly settlement options (franchisee elects):**
  1. **100% USD:** Franchisee remits USD; bears full FX risk. Used by 30–40% of franchisees (those with export revenue or natural USD hedges).
  2. **50/50 Hybrid:** Franchisee remits 50% USD, 50% BRL/TRY at month-start FX rate. Franchisor hedges 50% FX via forward contract with major bank (Santander Brazil, Türkiye Garantibank Turkey). Franchisor cost: 1.5% interest differential; included in royalty rate (no surcharge). Used by 40–50% of franchisees (preferred option).
  3. **100% Local Currency:** Franchisee remits BRL/TRY at prior-month fixed rate (announced by franchisor on 1st of month). Franchisor absorbs full FX depreciation/appreciation. Cost to franchisor: 3–4% annual interest; reflected as 40 bps increase in effective royalty rate (e.g., 6.52% → 6.92% if franchisee elects option 3). Used by 10–20% of franchisees (high-cost; franchisor discourages via pricing).

**Egypt (Tier 3):**
- **Local-currency-only (EGP) settlement.** No hybrid option (FX derivative ban under Central Bank Law 97/1992 makes hybrid settlement illegal).
- Franchisee remits EGP monthly. Franchisor absorbs all FX risk (receives EGP, converts to USD at-market or holds EGP-denominated assets).
- **Franchisor FX mitigation:** Establish "Egypt Holdings" subsidiary (local operating company). Accumulate EGP receivables in subsidiary; invest excess in EGP short-term securities (Central Bank T-bills 12–18% yields), creating natural EGP hedge. Reduces need for explicit FX conversion.
- **Franchisee benefit:** Royalty calculation in EGP (remit in operating currency); no USD conversion burden.

**Nigeria (Tier 3/4):**
- **Local-currency-only (NGN) settlement.** CBN prohibition on private-sector FX derivatives makes hybrid settlement legally unworkable.
- Franchisee remits NGN monthly. Franchisor absorbs FX risk (currency mismatch on franchisor balance sheet).
- **Franchisor FX mitigation:** Establish "Nigeria Holdings" subsidiary. Accumulate NGN receivables; deploy in NGN money-market accounts (12–18% yield) or NGN-denominated T-bills. Quarterly USD re-evaluation of NGN asset base (mark-to-market accounting).
- **Tier 3 franchisee benefit:** 50% of FX depreciation >20% absorbed by franchisor (automatic quarterly adjustment).
- **Tier 4 franchisee benefit:** 75% of FX depreciation >20% absorbed by franchisor (crisis-level support).

### 1.3 Inflation Adjustment Mechanisms & Formula Transparency

**Tier 1 (Indonesia) — Quarterly Indexation:**
- Published benchmark: Bank Indonesia CPI (20-day lag to release).
- Formula: Royalty rate = 7.5% + (Actual CPI% − 3.0% target) × 10 basis points.
- **Example Q1 2026:** Bank Indonesia publishes March CPI = 2.8% (month-over-month). Implied annualized CPI trend = 3.3%. Royalty adjustment: (3.3% − 3.0%) × 10 bps = +3 bps. Q2 rate = 7.5% + 0.03% = 7.53%.
- **Rate range (protected bounds):** 7.1% (floor if CPI undershoots) to 7.9% (ceiling if CPI overshoots 5%).
- **Renewal gate:** If cumulative IDR depreciation >8% in 12-month window, Tier 1 franchisee automatically demoted to Tier 2 (no choice; currency stability lost). 30-day notice required.

**Tier 2 (Brazil/Turkey) — Quarterly Indexation:**
- Published benchmark: Central Bank of Brazil IPCA (Brazil) / Turkish Central Bank CPI (Turkey). Both government sources, transparent, 20-day lag.
- Formula: Royalty rate = 6.8% + (Actual CPI% − target) × 8 basis points.
  - Brazil target: 2.5% (BCB inflation target midpoint).
  - Turkey target: 5.0% (CBT inflation target, adjusted if changed by central bank).
- **Rate range:** 5.8% floor (protects franchisor if deflation), 7.5% ceiling (protects franchisee if hyperinflation).
- **Crisis relief trigger:** If currency depreciates >15% in any 12-month window, automatic 50 bps royalty reduction for 6-month period (emergency relief). Example: Brazil real depreciates 18% Jan–Dec 2026 → Q1 2027 rate reduced to 6.32% for 6 months (vs. normal 6.7%–6.9% range).

**Tier 3 (Egypt/Nigeria) — Fixed Quarterly Rates (NOT continuous monthly):**
- **Rationale:** Eliminates "gharar" (Sharia-law uncertainty) concern in Egypt (continuous indexation deemed "too uncertain" by some courts). Fixed quarterly rates are pre-announced (reducing legal challenge exposure).
- Published benchmark: Egyptian Central Bank CPI / Nigerian Bureau of Statistics CPI (20-day lag to release).
- **Quarterly rate-setting process:**
  - On the 20th of each quarter-ending month (March 20, June 20, Sept 20, Dec 20), franchisor publishes next-quarter royalty rate in writing to all franchisees.
  - Formula example (Egypt Q2 2026): If CBE published Q1 CPI = 2.0% (monthly avg), Q2 rate = 5.34% + (2.0% − 3.0% target) × 8 bps = 5.26%. (Note: typically results in slight reduction if inflation low.)
  - **Rate range:** 4.8% floor (protects franchisor) to 6.2% ceiling (protects franchisee from hyperinflation).
  - Franchisees notified 30 days in advance of quarter-start. Rate effective retroactively from quarter-start (e.g., Q2 rate announced March 20, effective April 1).

**Tier 4 (Distressed Restructuring) — Monthly CPI Indexation with Floors:**
- Base rate: 4.0% for Y1 restructuring phase.
- Monthly adjustment: (Actual monthly CPI% − 3% target) × 8 bps. Range: 3.6% − 4.4%.
- **Example Nigeria Y1:** June monthly CPI = 4.5% (high inflation). Y2 (July onward) base rate = 4.0% + (4.5% − 3.0%) × 8 bps = 4.12%.
- If unit promotes to Tier 3 mid-year, rate resets to Tier 3 formula retroactively for remainder of year (no rate lock-in).

### 1.4 Performance Tier Incentives (Aligned to Franchisor Economics)

**Tier 1 (Indonesia):**
- Volumetric growth >5% YoY (12-month rolling average) → additional 25 bps rate reduction.
- EBITDA margin >28% → no additional reduction (already favorable).
- **Rationale:** Indonesia franchisees performing well; incentive to expand wins units; franchisor captures system growth.
- **Example:** Indonesia franchisee achieves 6% unit growth (e.g., added satellite location). Q3 rate = 7.15% − 25 bps = 6.90% for subsequent 12 months (subject to continued growth verification).

**Tier 2 (Brazil/Turkey):**
- Volume growth >3% YoY → 20 bps reduction.
- EBITDA margin >25% → 15 bps reduction.
- Combined max: 35 bps reduction.
- **Rationale:** Moderate-volatility markets; incentive to improve margin (franchisor upside via retained royalty) and grow (system expansion captures macroeconomic recovery).
- **Example:** Brazil franchisee achieves 3% growth + 26% EBITDA margin. Rate = 6.52% − 35 bps = 6.17% for 12 months.

**Tier 3 (Egypt/Nigeria):**
- Volume growth >2% YoY → 15 bps reduction (lower threshold; distressed markets need encouragement).
- EBITDA margin >20% → 12 bps reduction (achievable target despite macro stress).
- Combined max: 25 bps reduction.
- **Rationale:** Tier 3 franchisees operating under stress; lower growth/margin hurdles provide achievable targets and rate relief.
- **Example:** Egypt franchisee achieves 2% growth + 21% EBITDA margin. Rate = 5.34% − 25 bps = 5.09% for 12 months.

**Tier 4 (Distressed Restructuring):**
- Volume growth >1% YoY → 12 bps reduction.
- EBITDA margin >18% → 10 bps reduction.
- Combined max: 20 bps reduction.
- **Rationale:** Survival-mode market; minimal growth expectation; margin >18% is major achievement.
- **Example:** Nigeria Tier 4 franchisee achieves 1% growth + 19% EBITDA margin (major improvement from <15% distress baseline). Rate = 3.95% + (0% base Y2, assumes full 4.0% becomes payable) − 22 bps = 3.78% (reflects transition from Y1 2% cash payment to Y2 full 4% payment, with partial incentive credit).

## SECTION 2: DIFFERENTIATED FINANCING MECHANISMS ($156M STACK)

### 2.1 Vendor Credit Extension ($43M Deployed, $1.075M Annual Franchisor Cost)

**Objective:** Bridge immediate working capital gap (Q4 2025 − Q1 2026) using supplier credit to fund 30–60 day extension of payables float.

**Mechanism:**
1. **Franchisor identifies top 15–20 suppliers per market** (representing 80% of COGS): meat suppliers (major cost), beverage distributors, packaging vendors, equipment suppliers.
2. **Comfort letter issuance:** Franchisor notifies suppliers: "We will guarantee payment of invoices for our franchisees within 15 days of invoice date (vs. franchisees paying in 60 days). Aggregate guarantee cap: $500K–$2M per supplier depending on volume."
3. **Supplier benefit:** Franchisor payment certainty (credit enhancement) enables supplier to extend terms without increasing credit risk.
4. **Franchisee benefit:** 30–60 day payables float = $60–80K liquidity per franchisee (improvement in cash conversion cycle).
5. **Franchisor cost recovery:** Embed 1.5–2.0% "vendor credit facility fee" in royalty calculation (separate line item in royalty statement, transparent to franchisee). Example: franchisee owes 5.34% royalty + 0.18% facility fee = 5.52% total.

**2026 Deployment by Market:**
- Turkey: 12–15 suppliers, $18M aggregate extended credit, 180 franchisees benefit.
- Egypt: 8–10 suppliers, $12M aggregate, 100 franchisees.
- Nigeria: 6–8 suppliers, $8M aggregate, 120 franchisees (smaller suppliers due to higher risk).
- Indonesia: 5–6 suppliers, $3M aggregate, 80 franchisees (lower need; currency stable).
- Brazil: 6–8 suppliers, $2M aggregate, 70 franchisees (lower need; inflation manageable).
- **Total: $43M deployed to ~550 franchisees across five markets.**

**Supplier Credit Covenant:**
- If >10% of franchisees default on vendor payments (unremitted >60 days), comfort letter terminates for new transactions. Suppliers revert to standard 30-day terms (de facto cancellation of program).
- Franchisor loss provision: Assume 2% of vendor credit extended is not repaid (suppliers absorb loss or franchisor guarantees = $860K provision).

**Franchisor Economics:**
- Interest cost differential (60-day terms @ 4% annual rate vs. 30-day): $43M × 2% = $860K.
- Administrative overhead (vendor relationship management, franchisee tracking, covenant monitoring): $300K/year.
- Loss reserve: $860K (2% of extended credit).
- **Total annual cost: $2.02M. Facility fee revenue: $43M × 1.8% = $774K. Net cost to franchisor: $1.246M annually.**

**Franchisor Loss Reserve & Annual Cost:**

### 2.3 ABS Securitization ($50M Issuance, $45M Net Proceeds to Franchisees)

**Objective:** Refinance franchisor lending to franchisees via capital markets, reducing corporate debt burden + creating sustainable long-term working capital facility.

**ABS Structure:**

```
Franchisor Issues $50M Asset-Backed Securities
├─ Senior Tranche ($35M, 70% of deal):
│   ├─ Rating: AAA/Aaa (agency-rated)
│   ├─ Coupon: 4.0–4.5% annually
│   ├─ Credit Enhancement: 120% overcollateralization (150% reserve fund)
│   ├─ Buyer Base: Pension funds, insurance companies, banks (conservative, low-yield-seeking)
│   └─ Priority: First claim on asset cash flows
│
├─ Mezzanine Tranche ($10M, 20%):
│   ├─ Rating: A/A2 (agency-rated)
│   ├─ Coupon: 7.0–8.0% annually
│   ├─ Credit Enhancement: Subordinated to senior
│   ├─ Buyer Base: Hedge funds, institutional investors, some banks (yield-seeking)
│   └─ Priority: Second claim after senior tranche paid
│
└─ Subordinated/Equity Tranche ($5M, 10%):
    ├─ Rating: Not rated (franchisor retains, non-tradeable)
    ├─ Coupon: 12–15% annually (residual return to franchisor)
    ├─ Credit Enhancement: Loss absorption (first losses taken here)
    ├─ Holder: Franchisor (retains alignment of interest; absorbs first losses)
    └─ Priority: Last claim; maximum upside if pool performs
```

**Underlying Asset Pool:**
- **Franchisee royalty payment streams:** Monthly royalties owed to franchisor (estimated 60% collection rate; franchisor continues to collect, remits to ABS servicer).
- **Equipment lease obligations:** Franchisor captive leasing subsidiary loans to franchisees (~$100–150K per unit for equipment). Lease payments remitted to ABS pool.
- **Franchisor credit support:** Corporate parent guarantee on senior tranche (50% coverage; improves credit rating, compensates for emerging-market franchisee risk).

**Servicer & Payment Waterfall:**
1. **Collections:** Franchisor collects franchisee royalties + lease payments (ongoing operational function).
2. **Payment waterfall (monthly remittance to ABS trustee):**
   - **Step 1:** Pay senior tranche coupon (4.0–4.5% × $35M = $1.4–1.6M annually / $117–133K monthly).
   - **Step 2:** Pay mezzanine tranche coupon (7.5% × $10M = $750K annually / $62.5K monthly).
   - **Step 3:** Replenish reserve account (hold 12 months of senior coupon).
   - **Step 4:** Pay subordinated/equity tranche coupon + return excess spread to franchisor.
3. **Principal amortization:** 3–5 year bullet (principal repaid at maturity) or accelerated amortization if defaults exceed threshold.

**Rating Agency Assumptions (Moody's/S&P Methodology):**
- Franchisee default rate: 4% annually (typical emerging-market QSR assumption).
- Loss severity: 50% (collateral recovery assumed 50% if franchisee defaults).
- Expected loss: 2% of pool (4% default × 50% severity).
- Haircut/overcollateralization required for AAA senior: 125–130%.
- Haircut for A mezzanine: 115–120%.
- **Implication:** $35M senior tranche requires $43.75M assets (150% coverage); $10M mezzanine requires $11.5M assets. Franchisor portfolio of $55–60M franchisee receivables + leases supports $50M issuance.

**Franchisor Economics:**
- **Proceeds:** $50M gross, less underwriting fees (0.75–1.5% = $375–750K), legal/rating fees (~$250K). **Net proceeds: ~$45M.**
- **Deployment:** $45M re-lent to franchisees at 9% coupon (9-year amortization) or work capital revolving facility (18–24 month tenor).
- **Spread capture:** Franchisee coupon (9%) minus ABS blended coupon (5.2% weighted average of 4.0% senior + 7.5% mezzanine) = 380 bps spread. On $45M deployed: $45M × 3.8% = $1.71M annually.
- **Franchisor costs:** Servicing ($200K/year), loss reserve draw-down if defaults exceed 4% ($500K–$1M potential annual hit), franchisor guarantee claim if >losses threshold ($250–500K potential).
- **Net franchisor return: $1.71M gross spread − $0.95M costs = $0.76M annually (conservative; assumes 5% default rate on pool).**
- **Balance sheet benefit:** $50M ABS issued removes debt from franchisor balance sheet (if structured as off-balance-sheet SPV); improves leverage ratio.

**Rating Agency Downside Scenarios:**
- **If franchisee pool defaults reach 6% (vs. 4% assumption):** Expected loss increases to 3%; senior tranche ratings maintained (overcollateralization sufficient), but mezzanine downgraded to Baa/Ba; equity tranche absorbs full loss.
- **If country crisis triggers 15% default rate in one market (e.g., Nigeria political collapse):** Senior tranche likely downgraded to A/A2; mezzanine to Caa/Ca; franchisor absorbs $500K–$1M loss from equity tranche.
- **Franchisor contingency:** Pre-negotiate "excess spread" reinvestment triggers (if defaults exceed 4%, excess spread directs to reserve account vs. franchisor dividend).

**2026 Deployment Timeline:**
- Q4 2025: Investment bank engagement; preliminary structuring.
- Q1 2026: Rating agency meetings; 1st cut rating.
- Q2 2026: Investor roadshow; final ratings;
