# EXECUTIVE-READY BOARD PRESENTATION
## Pan-African Banking Group: Multi-Country Integration Strategy (2026-2028)

---

## SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES

### 1.1 BUSINESS CONTEXT & STRATEGIC IMPERATIVE

**Situation**: Pan-African Banking Group has acquired systemically important banks in Nigeria (CBN jurisdiction), Kenya (CMA jurisdiction), and Morocco (BANK AL-MAGHRIB jurisdiction) in 2026. Each operates on distinct legacy systems, under different regulators, with entrenched local management cultures and varying customer expectations.

**Strategic Tension**: Group mandate calls for unified digital banking platform to unlock operational efficiency, cross-border capabilities, and capital optimization. However, regulators and customers in each country prioritize local continuity and responsiveness.

**Strategic Opportunity**: Sequenced, phased integration approach achieves consolidation benefits while preserving regulatory compliance and customer/employee continuity. Positions group as pan-African fintech leader while respecting local market autonomy.

---

### 1.2 INTEGRATION OBJECTIVES (PRIMARY & SECONDARY)

#### **Primary Objectives** (Non-Negotiable)
1. **Regulatory Compliance**: All three regulators formally approve system migration plans; zero supervisory objections
2. **Customer Continuity**: Zero involuntary customer churn; <10 customer complaints per market per phase; >99% service availability
3. **Technology Consolidation**: 100% customer migration to GDP (Group Digital Platform) within 24–30 months; legacy systems decommissioned by Month 36
4. **Operational Resilience**: Unified platform reduces operational risk; shared fraud detection, cyber security, compliance monitoring across markets

#### **Secondary Objectives** (Value-Add)
5. **Cross-Border Capability**: Enable pan-African payment services, intra-group settlement, regional lending products (Months 18–24)
6. **Fintech Acceleration**: Kenya positioned as group fintech hub; 3+ new digital products launched by Month 12 (SME lending, mobile money, API partnerships)
7. **Capital Optimization**: Consolidated capital ratios enable more efficient capital deployment; estimated $50–100M capital release by Month 24
8. **Brand Coherence**: Three-country brand strategies converge to unified "Pan-African Banking Group" by Month 36 (Option A: country autonomy; Option B: consolidated brand)
