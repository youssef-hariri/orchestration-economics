# SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY WITH STRATEGIC VISION & KEY MILESTONES

## 1.1 Strategic Vision Statement

**Mission:** Build Africa's leading integrated banking platform by combining deep local market expertise with pan-African scale, delivering superior customer experience, operational efficiency, and cross-border value creation.

**Core Tension (Managed via Strategy):** Operational unification (efficiency, risk control, digital capability) vs. market customization (regulatory approval, customer retention, local relevance).

**Resolution Approach:** 
- **Local brand preservation** (retail products maintain Zenith/KCB/Attijariwafa identity)
- **Unified group digital products** (mobile banking, APIs, investment platforms branded as "Pan-African Group")
- **Centralized risk/compliance governance** (Group CRO authority over all three countries)
- **Decentralized P&L accountability** (Country MDs retain revenue responsibility; Group CRO has risk veto, not override)

| **CBN Question** | **Anticipated Timing** | **Response Framework** | **Evidence Required** |

| **"Explain monetary transmission impact (RTGS/interbank liquidity)"** | Day 40–50 of filing | Minimum ₦X billion REPO/interbank lending commitment (3 years); monthly reporting to CBN | Treasury policy; CFO sign-off; legal commitment letter signed by Group CEO; historical interbank lending baseline |
| **"Describe technology risk mitigation (FIS Cloud)"** | Day 40–50 of filing (if tech question raised) | FIS RTO/RPO commitments; disaster recovery plan; quarterly third-party security audits; CBN audit clause in FIS contract | FIS SLA excerpt (signed); disaster recovery test plan (to be executed month 8–10); SOC 2 Type II audit roadmap |
| **"Address market concentration risk (combined ≥8% market share)"** | Day 35–45 of filing | Maintain CBN's pro-rata loan share; no competitive restrictions; cross-border lending transparency | Market share analysis; competitive impact assessment; commitment to CBN monitoring |

| **Criteria** | **GO** | **NO-GO** |
