# OPERATIONS IMPROVEMENT PLAN
## Industrial Equipment Manufacturer – Certified Quality-Protected Lead-Time Reduction
### Executive Presentation: 14 Weeks → 9–10 Weeks (40% Reduction)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Current State**
- Quoted lead time: **14 weeks (98 calendar days)**
- Competitor lead time: 6 weeks
- Sales loss rate: **One in three deals lost on lead time alone**
- Prior compression attempt: Unilateral shortcut to quality gates → **2-year warranty crisis**
- Quality constraint: Certified inspection gates (pressure test, IPC, FAI/PPAP) are non-negotiable

**Root Cause: Where Time Is Actually Lost**
Of 98 days quoted, only **13.5 days (14%) is manufacturing work**; **84.5 days (86%) is queue, batching, and padding**.

Top time-loss drivers:
1. Long-lead procurement (6 weeks + RFQ-to-PO lag) = 42–45 days
2. Inspection batching (IPC Mon/Wed only; final test Tue/Thu only) = 14–21 days
3. Engineering release batching (weekly Thursday meeting) = 12–13 days
4. Final assembly rework loops (15–20% rework rate) = 5–10 days
5. Order-to-RFQ spec indecision (30% incomplete specs) = 3–7 days
6. QA document batching (weekly review meeting) = 3–7 days

**Program Approach**
All acceleration derived from **queue elimination, parallelization, staffing, early procurement, and supplier decoupling**—never from rigor shortcuts. All 15 mandatory certification gates remain in place.

**Target & Payback**
- **Lead time:** 9–10 weeks (40% reduction; competitive with 6-week competitor for standard products)
- **Revenue impact:** Recovering one-in-three lost deals = ~$3–5M incremental annual revenue
- **Program cost:** ~$300–500K (capex + opex, 18 months)
- **Payback:** 6–12 months post-implementation
- **Quality commitment:** Zero warranty regression; all certified gates held; leading indicators monitored monthly
