**CONFIDENTIAL MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors and Executive Leadership Team  
**FROM:** Office of the CEO / Strategy & Transformation Office  
**SUBJECT:** Strategic Business Model Transition: Perpetual Licensing to Subscription (SaaS)  
**DATE:** October 24, 2023  

---

### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: THE STRATEGIC IMPERATIVE**

We are not merely changing a billing model; we are fundamentally re-rating the enterprise. Our current perpetual licensing model produces lumpy, unpredictable revenue that the market discounts heavily, capping our valuation multiple at 3x–5x Revenue. 

By transitioning to a subscription (SaaS) model, we will unlock compounding Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) and shift our market valuation multiple to 8x–12x ARR. This transition will result in a mathematically inevitable and substantial increase in Enterprise Value.

However, this transformation requires navigating a deliberate "J-Curve"—a multi-year GAAP revenue trough caused by the cessation of large, upfront perpetual license sales. This document outlines a **mathematically robust, ASC 606-compliant, and operationally sound transition framework** designed to flatten the revenue trough, retain our entitled installed base, align our sales force, and maintain absolute confidence from our investors and the Board.

---

### **PILLAR 1: CUSTOMER MIGRATION & RETENTION**
**Objective:** Migrate the installed base to subscriptions with <5% churn, avoiding the 20% attrition experienced by competitors who utilized abrupt "rip-and-replace" tactics.

**1. The "Value Wedge" (Making Perpetual Obsolete)**
We will not abruptly revoke perpetual rights or cut off security patches, which triggers immediate churn. Instead, we will make the perpetual model functionally stagnant.
*   **Feature Partitioning:** All new cloud-native features, AI integrations, and mobile capabilities will be *exclusive* to the subscription tier. 
*   **The "Good Enough" Trap:** The legacy perpetual version remains secure for basic tasks, but power users and IT departments will naturally demand the subscription tier to access the future product roadmap.

**2. Ramped Transition Pricing (ASC 606 Compliant)**
To prevent the extreme Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) shock that drives entitled customers to drop support, we will implement a **Ramped Subscription Model**.
*   **The Mechanism:** Customers signing a 3-year transition contract will see a smoothly ramping net price (e.g., Year 1: 85%, Year 2: 100%, Year 3: 115% of the legacy Year 1 cost). 
*   **Accounting & Sustainability:** This ramp is structured as a ratable discount recognized evenly over the contract term, ensuring strict ASC 606 / IFRS 15 compliance. Crucially, the steady-state renewal price (Year 4+) will be anchored at 110% of the legacy Year 1 cost, ensuring long-term unit economics without creating a severe Year 3 renewal cliff.

**3. Frictionless Trade-Up**
*   **White-Glove Migration:** We will provide dedicated, zero-cost professional services for data migration and integration setup for migrations completed in the first 9 months, removing the primary operational barrier to switching.

---

### **PILLAR 2: PRICING & REVENUE TROUGH MITIGATION**
**Objective:** Mathematically minimize the GAAP revenue trough, protect Free Cash Flow (FCF) margins, and safely expand the Total Addressable Market (TAM).

**1. Anchor Pricing & Ratable Recognition**
To ensure migrating a customer does not immediately drop the annualized run-rate, the gross subscription will be anchored at **110% to 115% of the old "Perpetual License + Year 1 Maintenance & Support (M&S)" cost.** The Ramped Transition Pricing (Pillar 1.2) will be applied against this gross anchor to smooth the customer's cash outflow while protecting our recognized ARR baseline.

**2. Securing Multi-Year Terms Without COGS Bloat**
Multi-year commitments are paramount for SaaS valuation. However, offering ongoing discounts or free seats inflates Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) and degrades FCF margins during the trough.
*   **One-Time PS Credits:** We will secure 3-year commitments by offering **One-Time Professional Services (PS) Credits** or implementation fee waivers. This drives the desired customer behavior without increasing marginal ongoing costs or degrading FCF margins.

**3. "Land and Expand" (Strictly Gated)**
*   **Core/Lite Tier:** We will introduce an entry-level tier priced at 40% of the legacy perpetual license to capture net-new logos previously blocked by high CapEx barriers. 
*   **Crucial Guardrail:** This tier is *strictly gated for net-new logos only*. Existing perpetual customers must select Standard or Enterprise tiers to prevent base cannibalization and protect the ARR recovery curve.

---

### **PILLAR 3: SALES COMPENSATION REALIGNMENT**
**Objective:** Shift the sales force from hunting lumpy, upfront Total Contract Value (TCV) deals to building compounding recurring revenue, while preventing top-performer attrition.

**1. The "Transition Shield" (OTE Protection)**
Moving from TCV to ARR will temporarily slash a rep's cash compensation. To prevent panic-resignations during the transition year, we will implement an **OTE Protection Floor**. For the first 12–18 months, reps are guaranteed 90% of their historical On-Target Earnings, provided they meet leading indicators (e.g., migration pipeline generated, demos completed).

**2. Additive Multipliers (Capped at 2.5x)**
We must financially reward reps for the extra effort of negotiating multi-year migrations.
*   **Base Rate:** 1.0x on Net Recognized ARR.
*   **Migration Multiplier:** +0.75x for converting existing perpetual customers.
*   **Multi-Year Multiplier:** +0.50x for securing 3-year terms.
*   **The Math & Cap:** A 1-year migration pays 1.75x; a 3-year migration pays **2.25x**. The maximum multiplier is strictly capped at **2.5x** to protect deal margins while ensuring reps are incentivized to lock in long-term commitments.

**3. The 80/20 Renewal Holdback**
To align sales with long-term customer success and Net Retention Rate (NRR), 80% of the commission is paid on booking, and **20% is held back** and paid only upon the customer's first successful renewal.

---

### **PILLAR 4: INVESTOR & BOARD COMMUNICATION**
**Objective:** Control the narrative, shift the market's focus to leading SaaS metrics, and prove the mathematical inevitability of the long-term valuation upside.

**1. The "Valuation Bridge" Narrative**
We will proactively frame the transition around Enterprise Value (EV), not just top-line revenue. 
*   **The Narrative:** *"While GAAP revenue will dip X% in Year 1 as we exit perpetuals, our Enterprise Value will grow. Legacy software trades at 3x Revenue; SaaS trades at 8x ARR. Even with a 10% revenue trough, the 150%+ multiple expansion results in a net-positive Enterprise Value increase by Year 2."*
*   **Investor Day:** The CFO will host a dedicated session to walk the market through this exact bridge, detailing the peak revenue, the trough, and the higher terminal valuation.

**2. Shifting the North Star Metrics**
We will immediately shift official guidance away from GAAP Revenue to prevent short-seller attacks on artificially depressed margins (intentionally excluding the "Rule of 40" during the J-Curve). We will guide the market strictly on:
*   **Net Recognized ARR:** The true measure of the new business size.
*   **NRR (Net Retention Rate):** Proving the base is expanding (Target: >110%).
*   **CAC Payback Period:** Demonstrating improved customer acquisition efficiency.

**3. Downside Contingency Triggers**
To provide the Board with a concrete safety net, we have established predefined intervention triggers:
*   **Migration Lag (<30% by Month 6):** Deploy executive sponsors to close stalled deals and unlock targeted, ASC 606-compliant transition discounts for at-risk accounts.
*   **Retention Drop (NRR <105%):** Activate the **Accelerated Migration Protocol**, offering a one-time "Perpetual Buyout" (forgiving remaining perpetual value as a credit toward a 3-year subscription) to force holdouts into the model and accelerate trough recovery.
*   **Sales Attrition (>10%):** Temporarily increase the OTE Protection Floor to 100% and introduce a one-time retention bonus for top performers.

### **EXECUTION TIMELINE & GOVERNANCE**

**Phase 1: Internal Architecture (Months 1–2)**
*   Finalize ASC 606-compliant ramped pricing and CRM/ERP billing updates.
*   Roll out the additive multiplier sales comp plan and OTE Shield.
*   Brief the Board and secure alignment on the Valuation Bridge and Downside Triggers.

**Phase 2: External Announcement (Month 3)**
*   Announce the transition to the market.
*   Launch the "J-Curve" and "Valuation Bridge" narrative to investors.
*   Announce the "Value Wedge" and "Ramped Transition Pricing" to customers.

**Phase 3: The Migration Gold Rush (Months 4–12)**
*   Sales executes additive multiplier plays; Customer Success executes white-glove onboarding.
*   Push 3-year terms using One-Time PS credits.

**Phase 4: Perpetual Sunset & Normalization (Months 13–24)**
*   **Month 13:** Stop selling new perpetual licenses. Legacy M&S limited to break-fix.
*   **Months 13–24:** Sales comp multipliers normalize; OTE Shield is lifted. GAAP revenue trough bottoms out. Focus shifts entirely to Net-New ARR and NRR.

**Phase 5: Recovery & Multiple Expansion (Month 24+)**
*   ARR compounds, exceeding the peak perpetual run-rate.
*   The market re-rates the stock, applying a SaaS multiple (8x–12x ARR), fully realizing the targeted Enterprise Value growth.

**Board Governance:**
Management will provide the Board with a **Monthly Transition Dashboard** focusing on leading indicators: migration conversion rates, pipeline velocity, churn rate of migrated vs. non-migrated customers, and sales rep quota attainment. Granular visibility will replace anxiety with data-driven oversight.
