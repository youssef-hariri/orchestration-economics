## Executive Summary

**Bumi Rasa** (“Flavors of the Earth”) is a curated four-SKU snack line engineered for the Republic of Indonesia’s dynamic, tradition-rich snacking culture. Built on the cultural imperative of *ngemil* and the national expectation of *berbagi* (communal sharing), each product carries a regional **Edisi** (Edition) badge—Minang, Jawa, or Bali—that honors culinary provenance while delivering modern shelf stability, Halal transparency, and tropical-climate resilience.

The portfolio spans mass-market puffs, accessible premium chewy bites, mid-premium protein crisps, and a heritage-premium gifting cluster. By pairing bold local ingredients with honest occasion targeting and climatically realistic formulation, Bumi Rasa occupies whitespace between imported commodity snacks and unhygienic street food.

---

## Market Context: Why Indonesia

Indonesia’s snack economy is shaped by forces that demand both cultural fluency and technical precision:

*   **Deep Snacking Culture:** With a population exceeding 270 million and rapid urbanization, *ngemil* is an all-day social ritual—not merely sustenance.
*   **Layered Palate Expectations:** Consumers expect simultaneous savory, sweet, spicy, and umami dimensions, anchored by indigenous ingredients such as coconut, cassava, *gula jawa* (coconut palm sugar), pandan, and chilies.
*   **Halal as Trust Baseline:** BPJPH/MUI certification is mandatory; front-of-pack transparency on umami sources eliminates *was-was* (religious doubt) and builds brand credibility.
*   **Tropical Logistics Reality:** Ambient supply chains regularly exceed 30°C/75% RH. Shelf-life claims must be tiered by water activity and validated under ASEAN-standard accelerated conditions (40°C/90% RH).
*   **Social & Seasonal Occasions:** From *ngopi sore* (afternoon coffee) to *buka puasa* and *Lebaran* gifting, format, portioning, and pack structure must support sharing, humidity defense, and ritual appropriateness.

## The Portfolio

### Concept 1: KRIUK TEMPE BUMBU RENDANG  
*Edisi Minang — Crunchy Tempeh with Rendang Seasoning*

**The Concept**  
Irregular clusters of thinly sliced local soybean tempeh, flash-crisped in palm olein, and glazed with a reduction of *rempah rendang* enriched with toasted coconut cream powder. Finished with micro-ground kaffir lime leaf and toasted desiccated coconut.

**Signature Ingredients**  
* Local soybean tempeh  
* Palm olein (frying medium)  
* Coconut cream powder  
* *Gula jawa*, galangal, ginger, turmeric, lemongrass, shallots, garlic, red chilies  
* Candlenut (*kemiri*)  
* Toasted desiccated coconut  
* Micro-ground kaffir lime leaf  

**Sensorial Profile**  
* **Flavor:** Deep umami-forward savory; warm earthy spice; mild, building heat; caramelized sweetness from palm sugar; subtle aromatic lift from lime leaf. The coconut cream powder supplies the unctuous body expected of rendang seasoning without claiming to replicate the braised dish itself.  
* **Texture:** Shatteringly crisp, rugged, and irregular. A slight toothsome resistance from the tempeh grain delivers a “meaty” crunch distinct from uniform potato chips.

**Packaging Engineering**  
Matte stand-up kraft-look pouch with high-barrier aluminum lining and resealable zipper. Four inner **25 g metallized sachets** preserve crunch across repeated openings in high humidity. Color palette: terracotta, gold, and deep forest green with the **“Edisi Minang”** provenance badge front and center.

**Consumption Occasion**  
*Ngopi sore* (afternoon coffee break), long-distance land travel (*mudik*), and social snacking with iced tea or coffee. **Explicitly not positioned as a *buka puasa* first bite.**

**Market Positioning**  
Tempeh is a nationally beloved flexitarian protein, yet the snack aisle is dominated by imported wheat and potato formats. Framing the product as **tempeh *with* rendang seasoning**—rather than a heritage hybrid—respects Minangkabau culinary boundaries while leveraging a prestige global flavor. Naturally Halal and clean-label.

**Retail Economics & Placement**  
* **Price:** Mid-premium — Rp 12,000–16,000  
* **Channel:** Modern-trade supermarkets, hypermarkets, and train/bus terminals serving the *mudik* travel corridor. Avoid airport lounges due to oil residue and strong aromatics.

### Concept 2: KLEPON KEKINIAN  
*Edisi Jawa — Soft Pandan–Palm Sugar Chewy Bites*

**The Concept**  
Shelf-stable, bite-sized spheres inspired by the classic Javanese street sweet. A chewy pandan-flavored glutinous rice shell enrobes a **thick, gooey (not liquid) *gula jawa* center** stabilized with tapioca starch and inulin to bind water and prevent moisture migration. Coated in lightly salted, **toasted desiccated coconut**.

**Signature Ingredients**  
* Glutinous rice flour  
* Real *daun pandan* (pandan leaf) extract  
* *Gula jawa* (thickened with tapioca starch)  
* Desiccated coconut  
* Coconut cream powder  
* Sea salt  

**Sensorial Profile**  
* **Flavor:** Sweet but complex—deep butterscotch and vanilla notes from *gula jawa*; aromatic grassy-floral pandan; a balancing saline-coconut finish that prevents cloying sweetness.  
* **Texture:** Soft, bouncy chew (mochi-like) with a deliberate thick, gooey pull from the center. Toasted coconut provides immediate fibrous contrast. Zero free liquid.

**Packaging Engineering**  
**Individually flow-wrapped** in high-barrier metallized film to prevent stickiness and microbial cross-contamination, then packed in an opaque pastel-green secondary sharing pouch featuring classic *batik parang* geometry. No clear PET windows that create greenhouse heat. Nitrogen flush at pack.

**Consumption Occasion**  
*Arisan* (social gatherings), office meeting breaks, children’s lunchboxes (*bekal*), and pairing with jasmine or green tea.

**Market Positioning**  
*Klepon* enjoys near-universal emotional resonance, yet hygienic, packaged versions remain scarce. The name **“Klepon Kekinian”** preserves the culturally loaded root word while signaling modern factory safety. The stabilized gooey paste replaces the technically naive liquid burst, making ambient distribution feasible without sacrificing the signature sensorial moment.

**Retail Economics & Placement**  
* **Price:** Accessible premium — Rp 10,000–14,000  
* **Shelf Life & Logistics:** 60 days ambient (high-turnover modern trade in Java and Bali only); 90 days chilled (premium grocers such as Farmers Market and KemChik’s). Accelerated validation at 40°C/90% RH is mandatory before launch.  
* **Channel:** High-turnover modern trade (Java/Bali) and premium chilled grocers (national). Avoid general-trade *warung* where turnover is slow.

### Concept 3: SAMBAL MATAH CASSAVA PUFFS  
*Edisi Bali — Balinese-Inspired Herb & Chili Puffs*

**The Concept**  
Air-puffed (never fried) bites crafted from a blend of cassava flour and local corn grit, tumbled in an aromatic **“Gaya Sambal Matah”** (Sambal Matah–Style) coating. Features **visible micro-dried shallot flakes and lemongrass needles**. Umami depth is derived from a Halal-certified seaweed and yeast extract blend.

**Signature Ingredients**  
* Cassava flour  
* Local corn grit  
* Shallots, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaf  
* *Cabe rawit* (bird’s eye chili)  
* Palm olein (puff base)  
* Yeast extract and seaweed powder  

**Sensorial Profile**  
* **Flavor:** Bright and fiery; intense fresh allium and herbaceous top notes; clean citrus aroma; savory, mouth-filling umami; sharp, clean heat that dissipates without grease. Honest about being *inspired by* rather than identical to raw sambal matah.  
* **Texture:** Extremely light, airy, and spherical. Melts on the tongue with a brief, audible crackle. Non-greasy and low in residual oil.

**Packaging Engineering**  
Slim, upright glossy pouch in vivid chili-red and white with bold sans-serif typography. **“Edisi Bali”** badge plus front-of-pack callout: **“Bebas Terasi / 100% Seafood-Free Umami.”** Resealable zip with internal desiccant sachet. Offered in 60 g (personal) and 120 g (*bagi-bagi* / sharing) sizes.

**Consumption Occasion**  
Casual all-day *ngemil*, television streaming, post-school snacking for teenagers, beach outings, and *piknik*.

**Market Positioning**  
*Sambal Matah* carries premium, tourism-driven cachet yet remains absent from the mass snack aisle. By dropping incoherent flavor modifiers, the identity becomes singular. Visible herb inclusions signal authenticity and justify the absence of *terasi* to consumers familiar with the original. A cassava-corn base ensures supply-chain resilience, while palm olein keeps the format economically viable at mass price points.

**Retail Economics & Placement**  
* **Price:** Mass-market — Rp 5,000–8,000  
* **Channel:** Convenience chains (Indomaret, Alfamart), modern *warung*, and e-commerce impulse packs.

### Concept 4: REMPEYEK CLUSTER KELAPA  
*Edisi Jawa — Sweet-Savory Coconut & Peanut Brittle Clusters*

**The Concept**  
Bite-sized brittle clusters inspired by *rempeyek* and the festive *gurih-manis* tradition of Java. Toasted desiccated coconut, roasted Java peanuts, and humidity-resistant rice crisps are bound in a delicate glass of *gula jawa* and glucose-isomalt, seasoned with coriander, candlenut, garlic, and shallot. Formulated specifically to retain crunch in tropical ambient conditions.

**Signature Ingredients**  
* Desiccated coconut  
* Java peanuts  
* Rice crisps  
* *Gula jawa* and glucose syrup  
* Coriander, candlenut (*kemiri*), garlic, shallot  
* Sea salt  
* Palm olein and coconut oil (aroma finish)  

**Sensorial Profile**  
* **Flavor:** The classic Indonesian *gurih manis* equilibrium—deeply toasted coconut and nutty peanut; warm spice aromatics from coriander and candlenut; rich butterscotch notes from caramelized palm sugar; subtle allium savoriness.  
* **Texture:** Crispy, crunchy, and flaky with varied particle sizes. Delivers a satisfying shatter that resolves into smaller crisp bits. Rice crisps extend structural integrity far longer than pure nut brittles under humid conditions.

**Packaging Engineering**  
* **Daily Format:** Kraft stand-up pouch (60 g) with high-barrier metallized lining, resealable zipper, and internal desiccant. Warm brown, gold, and ivory palette. Usage callout: *“Nikmati bersama kopi atau teh.”*  
* **Gift Format:** Premium tall paperboard canister containing four inner nitrogen-flushed foil sachets (30 g each) to preserve crunch after opening. Wax-paper lining, gold-foil lid, and a narrative booklet on *rempeyek* heritage. Designed for *Lebaran* hampers.

**Consumption Occasion**  
Daily *ngopi* (mid-morning or afternoon tea/coffee), post-dinner light dessert, *buka puasa* tea table (as part of the spread, not the first bite), and *Lebaran* gifting (canister tier only).

**Market Positioning**  
The Ramadan gifting market is saturated with imported chocolates and generic biscuits. This product elevates a beloved Javanese snack archetype into a modern cluster, occupying a unique “heritage premium” niche. An accessible daily pouch builds taste trust and brand familiarity before consumers are asked to gift the Rp 38,000+ canister. The glucose-isomalt glass and rice crisps directly combat humidity-induced sogginess, while the format signals local pride to younger, digitally native buyers.

**Retail Economics & Placement**  
* **Price:** Daily pouch — Rp 12,000–15,000; Gift canister — Rp 38,000–48,000  
* **Channel:** Daily pouch through modern-trade supermarkets and convenience stores. Gift canister through premium grocers, e-commerce hamper platforms, hotel partners, and seasonal *parcel Lebaran* channels.

## Strategic Pillars

| Pillar | Implementation |
|--------|----------------|
| **Halal Certification & Transparency** | All ingredients and production lines certified by BPJPH/MUI, with logos prominent on the front of every pack. Seafood-free umami carries explicit *Bebas Terasi / 100% Seafood-Free Umami* callouts to eliminate doubt. |
| **Tropical Shelf Stability** | Dry SKUs (Concepts 1, 3, and 4 daily pouch) target **9 months** via high-barrier metallized films with desiccant, stored at ≤30°C/75% RH. The moisture-bearing Klepon Kekinian targets **60 days ambient** or **90 days chilled**, restricted to high-turnover modern trade. All SKUs must pass accelerated shelf-life trials at **40°C/90% RH** before commercialization. |
| **Brand Architecture** | Masterbrand **Bumi Rasa** anchors modern urban identity. Each SKU carries an **Edisi** provenance badge (Minang / Jawa / Bali) and a categorical signifier (*Kriuk*, *Klepon*, *Puffs*, *Cluster*) so consumers instantly understand product type. Visual language merges clean minimalism with subtle craft motifs (*batik parang*, *tenun*, *songket*). |
| **Pricing Architecture** | **Mass-market:** Concept 3 (Rp 5,000–8,000). **Accessible premium:** Concept 2 and Concept 4 daily pouch (Rp 10,000–15,000). **Mid-premium:** Concept 1 (Rp 12,000–16,000). **Gift/premium:** Concept 4 canister (Rp 38,000–48,000), supported by a heritage narrative booklet to justify status symbolism against imported chocolates. |
| **Distribution Sequencing** | **Phase 1 (Daily Penetration):** Launch Concepts 2, 3, and 4 daily pouch nationally in modern trade to drive trial and build brand familiarity. **Phase 2 (Prestige & Seasonal):** Introduce Concept 1 (supermarkets / *mudik* terminals) and Concept 4 gift canister (premium grocers / *parcel* channels) 6–8 weeks before Ramadan. |
| **Regional Culinary Governance** | Engage Minangkabau, Javanese, and Balinese culinary consultants to audit recipes, *Edisi* narratives, and pack copy, ensuring ethnic accuracy and preventing gastronomic tokenism. |
