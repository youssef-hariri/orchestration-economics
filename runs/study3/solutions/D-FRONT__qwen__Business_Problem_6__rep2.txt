# MARKETING CAMPAIGN STRATEGY: KOMOREBI CALM 
**Market:** Japan | **Category:** Premium Dietary Supplements 

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
**Komorebi Calm** is a premium, plant-based dietary supplement designed to complement a relaxing evening routine, encourage daily mindfulness, and seamlessly integrate into a peaceful morning. The formulation respectfully blends a revered Japanese botanical (Reishi mushroom) with globally respected botanicals and plant-derived amino acids (Lemon Balm, Ashwagandha, L-Theanine). 

This document outlines the go-to-market strategy for launching *Komorebi Calm* in Japan. The campaign is built on a foundation of profound cultural empathy and strict regulatory compliance. By navigating the nuanced social dynamics of Japanese corporate life and adhering to the stringent guidelines of the Consumer Affairs Agency (CAA), the campaign positions *Komorebi Calm* not as a medical intervention, but as a dignified, daily ritual of "gentle reset." 

---

## 2. MARKET CONTEXT & CULTURAL NUANCE
To succeed ethically and effectively in Japan, the campaign navigates a highly specific cultural and regulatory landscape:

*   **Cultural Dynamics (*Honne* & *Tatemae*):** Japan’s health culture is rooted in preventative care and social harmony (*Wa*). However, mental fatigue carries a heavy social stigma; admitting to stress can be seen as burdening others (*Meiwaku*). Consumers often hide their exhaustion behind a public facade (*Tatemae*). **Strategic Imperative:** Messaging must frame the product as a tool for "gentle self-maintenance," allowing individuals to show up fully for their families and teams, rather than an "escape" from their duties.
*   **Regulatory Framework (CAA & Food Labeling Act):** Dietary supplements are regulated by the Consumer Affairs Agency (CAA). Unless the product secures "Foods with Function Claims" (FFC) status, **zero** physiological, efficacy, or structure/function claims are permitted. **Strategic Imperative:** The brand will be positioned entirely on transparency, ingredient purity, and lifestyle alignment, ensuring absolute compliance and zero risk of misleading representations.

---

## 3. TARGET AUDIENCE
**Primary Persona: "The Quiet Achiever" (Ages 30–50)**

*   **Demographics:** Urban professionals in Tokyo, Osaka, and Nagoya. Mid-to-high income, highly educated.
*   **Psychographics:** They navigate the intense emotional labor of Japanese corporate and social life. They value subtlety, quality, and discretion. They are highly skeptical of loud, aggressive Western "biohacking" or "hustle" culture, seeking products that integrate seamlessly without drawing unwanted attention to personal struggles.
*   **Pain Points:** The exhaustion of maintaining their *Tatemae*, the difficulty of transitioning from "work mode" to "home mode," and a desire for holistic self-care that does not feel like "taking medicine."

---

## 4. CORE MESSAGING & POSITIONING
**Positioning Statement:** 
*Komorebi Calm* is not a medical intervention or a physiological stimulant. It is a daily, mindful ritual of "gentle reset" (やさしいリセット), bridging the reverence for global botanical traditions with modern, transparent quality standards.

**Core Message Pillars:**
1.  **Gentle Accompaniment, Not Force:** Positioning the supplement as a quiet companion that "gently supports" (そっと寄り添う) your daily rhythm, rather than forcing the body to change.
2.  **Radical Transparency & Purity:** Highlighting meticulous sourcing, domestic botanical cultivation, and third-party testing to build absolute trust in a market highly sensitive to food safety.
3.  **The Ritual of Transition:** Framing the act of taking the supplement as a sacred, private micro-ritual that helps transition from the public self (*Tatemae*) to the private, relaxed self (*Honne*).

**Campaign Tagline:** 
> **毎日に、そっと寄り添う。** *(Mainichi ni, sotto yorisou.)*
> **English:** "Gently accompanying your every day."

---

## 5. CHANNEL & EXPERIENTIAL STRATEGY
The media mix prioritizes trust, discretion, and seamless integration into the daily routines of urban professionals.

*   **Digital & Social Media:**
    *   **LINE Official Account:** The core of CRM. Users receive weekly "mindful living" newsletters, evening routine tips, and exclusive refill discounts. The tone is empathetic, informative, and never pushy.
    *   **Instagram:** Focus on "quiet aesthetics" (静かなる美学). High-quality, minimalist photography emphasizing texture, dappled light (the *Komorebi* motif), and serene domestic spaces. 
    *   **YouTube:** Partner with highly credentialed, soft-spoken **Registered Dietitians (管理栄養士)**, food scientists, or botanical researchers for educational content on botanical origins and quality testing.
*   **Out-of-Home (OOH) Advertising:**
    *   **Premium Transit & Elevators:** Elegant, uncluttered digital posters in business districts (e.g., Marunouchi, Umeda). Visuals feature the subtle, dappled light of *Komorebi* casting shadows on clean surfaces, offering a visual "deep breath."
*   **Retail & Experiential:**
    *   **Premium Select Shops:** Placement in high-end lifestyle stores (e.g., Loft, Tokyu Hands) alongside premium skincare and wellness goods, rather than traditional supplement aisles.
    *   **The "Refill" Culture:** Partner with select boutique hotels and high-end tea houses to offer the product experience, emphasizing sustainable consumption.
*   **PR & Influencer Strategy:**
    *   Strictly prohibit medical doctors and fitness "gurus." Partner with respected cultural figures (e.g., a renowned ceramicist, a mindfulness author, or a Registered Dietitian) to provide authority and trust without violating advertising codes.

## 6. CREATIVE CAMPAIGN CONCEPTS

### Concept 1: "The Transition Ritual" (Video & OOH)
*   **Visual:** A beautifully shot, quiet sequence. A professional arrives home. The camera focuses on the *Genkan* (entryway). They remove their shoes, wash their hands, and the lighting shifts from the cool, harsh blue of the street to the warm, dappled amber of their home. They prepare a glass of water, take *Komorebi Calm*, and take a slow, deep breath. The harsh light fully gives way to a warm amber glow, and a soft, genuine smile touches their lips as they transition into comfortable home clothes. 
*   **Copy:** 仕事とプライベートのあいだに、やさしいリセットを。 *(A gentle reset between work and private life.)* Komorebi Calm.
*   **Strategic Rationale:** Acknowledges the cultural reality of *Honne/Tatemae*. By focusing entirely on atmospheric, emotional, and behavioral shifts (lighting, smiling, changing clothes) rather than physical or muscular changes, it relies on emotional resonance while strictly avoiding any implied physiological claims.

### Concept 2: "The Origin of Trust" (Digital & Print)
*   **Visual:** Split focus. On one side, a stunning, macro shot of a Reishi mushroom growing in a misty, domestic Japanese forest. On the other, a clean, brightly lit, modern laboratory where a technician in a pristine coat is conducting quality checks. 
*   **Copy:** 自然の恵みを、科学の誠実さで。 *(Nature's bounty, with scientific integrity.)* 100% transparent sourcing. Rigorously tested for your peace of mind.
*   **Strategic Rationale:** Japanese consumers are highly skeptical of "black box" supplements. This visually proves the brand's commitment to both natural tradition and rigorous, ethical quality control.

## 7. ETHICAL, REGULATORY & QUALITY SAFEGUARDS
To ensure the campaign remains above reproach, the following strict guardrails are enforced:

1.  **Zero Physiological Claims (CAA Compliance):** Because FFC certification is not the focus of this initial launch, **zero** physiological, efficacy, or structure/function claims will be made. Terms like "adaptogen," "stress management," "anxiety relief," and "sleep support" are strictly banned from all consumer-facing marketing. 
2.  **Influencer & Endorsement Compliance:** Under Japan’s Fair Competition Code, using medical doctors to endorse dietary supplements is prohibited. All expert testimonials are strictly limited to Registered Dietitians, food scientists, and botanical researchers.
3.  **Botanical Authenticity & Precision:** The product blends a Japanese botanical (Reishi) with global botanicals (Lemon Balm, Ashwagandha) and plant-derived amino acids (L-Theanine). The campaign will accurately position this as a "curated global blend" that *honors and includes* revered Japanese ingredients, avoiding false categorization as traditional *Kampo* medicine.
4.  **Ethical Packaging & *Mottainai* (Waste Reduction):** The product launches in a durable glass bottle, followed by eco-friendly, minimalist refill pouches (詰め替え) to honor the concept of *Mottainai*. **Quality Mandate:** R&D and packaging teams must rigorously test these refill pouches to ensure they provide the exact same moisture, oxygen, and light barriers as the glass bottle, guaranteeing zero degradation of sensitive botanical extracts.
5.  **Mental Health Nuance & Dignity:** The campaign strictly avoids imagery of exhausted, dark-circled, or "broken" individuals. We will not exploit the stigma of mental fatigue; instead, we focus on the positive aspiration of maintaining vitality and dignity.

---

## 8. MEASUREMENT & KPIs
Success will be measured through a balanced scorecard of brand health, commercial performance, and strict compliance:

*   **Brand Trust & Sentiment:** Quarterly brand lift studies tracking metrics related to "trust," "quality," and "natural." Social listening to monitor organic mentions in the context of "self-care" and "quality of life."
*   **CRM & Refill Adoption:** The ultimate metric for success is the **refill conversion rate**. We will track the percentage of first-time glass-bottle buyers who purchase the eco-refill pouches, indicating both product satisfaction and alignment with sustainable values.
*   **Engagement Quality:** Measuring "saves" and "shares" on Instagram and LINE, which indicate high-value, educational content resonance, rather than superficial "likes."
*   **Compliance Metric:** **Zero** regulatory warnings, **zero** consumer complaints regarding misleading claims, and a **100% pass rate** on internal ethical and legal audits.
