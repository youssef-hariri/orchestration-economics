#### B. Country-Specific Risk Assessment

**Tiered Risk Classification:**

| Tier | Countries | Risk Profile | Audit Frequency | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (Green)** | Denmark, Singapore, Japan, Germany, Canada | Stable governance, strong labor laws, low corruption | Every 2-3 years | Standard monitoring |
| **2 (Yellow)** | Mexico, Vietnam, Thailand, Poland, Czech Republic | Emerging risks, evolving standards, moderate corruption | Annually | Enhanced due diligence, diversification planning |
| **3 (Orange)** | India, Indonesia, Brazil, Turkey, Morocco | Regulatory gaps, multi-state complexity, geopolitical transitions | Semi-annually | Capacity building, secondary sourcing, active monitoring |
| **4 (Red)** | Myanmar, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Nigeria, North Korea | High governance risk, compliance challenges, conflict zones | Quarterly or monthly | High-touch partnerships, contingency plans, or phase-out |

**Detailed Country Risk Profiles:**

**VIETNAM (Tier 2)**
- Primary concerns: Labor law complexity, political stability, capacity constraints
- Key regulations: Vietnamese Labor Code (maximum 8-hour workday, trade union requirements), environmental regulations
- Geopolitical risk: South China Sea tensions, US-China trade friction
- Supplier concentration: 40-50% of electronics and assembly sourcing
- Mitigation: Annual labor audits per Vietnamese Labor Code requirements; USMCA alternative sourcing from Mexico planned; geopolitical monitoring of trade policy changes

**MEXICO (Tier 2)**
- Primary concerns: USMCA compliance, wage inflation, cartel violence impact on logistics
- Key regulations: Federal Labor Law (LFT) - mandatory annual salary increases, profit-sharing requirements, union representation
- Trade advantage: USMCA preferential tariff treatment (0% duty under Rules of Origin compliance)
- Wage trends: Current $8-12/hour → Expected $16-20/hour by 2028 (USMCA labor cost floor increasing)
- Geopolitical risk: Regional cartel activity may impact transportation, but not supplier operations
- Mitigation: USMCA RoO compliance system established; nearshoring program underway; wage inflation modeling in cost projections

**INDIA (Tier 3)**
- Primary concerns: Multi-state labor law framework, Tier 2/3 supplier concentration, capability variability
- Key regulations: Different minimum wages by state (range $0.30-0.50/hour), Shop & Establishments Act, Gratuity Act (5-year vesting), Provident Fund (12% employer contribution)
- Geopolitical risk: Regional conflicts (Naxalite zones), political transitions
- Supplier concentration: 35-40% of fasteners, components from Indian supply base
- Mitigation: State-specific labor law audits; quarterly compliance verification; worker wage monitoring; geographic diversification to other regions

**EUROPEAN UNION (Tier 1)**
- Primary concern: CSDDD compliance (mandatory by 2027), Working Time Directive enforcement
- Key regulations: EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD), Working Time Directive (max 48-hour week), GDPR, environmental directives
- Regulatory momentum: Increasing supply chain transparency requirements, human rights due diligence mandates
- Advantage: Mature labor/environmental standards, low compliance risk
- Mitigation: Full CSDDD compliance framework designed; annual audits; transparency reporting

### 1.3 ETHICAL SOURCING & COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

#### A. Supplier Code of Conduct (5 Pillars)

**PILLAR 1: LABOR RIGHTS & WORKER DIGNITY**

```
ZERO-TOLERANCE POLICIES:
├─ CHILD LABOR: Zero employment <15 years old
│  └─ Age 15-18: Light duties only, max 8-hour days, no night shifts
│  └─ Verification: Birth certificate audits, payroll sampling, worker interviews
│
├─ FORCED LABOR: Zero involuntary labor, no human trafficking
│  └─ No wage deductions, ID document confiscation, or debt bondage
│  └─ Verification: Worker contracts, wage payment documentation, private interviews
│
├─ DISCRIMINATION: Zero discrimination on basis of age, gender, race, religion, disability
│  └─ Verification: Payroll equity analysis, promotion records, grievance logs
│
├─ FREEDOM OF ASSOCIATION: Right to join unions, collective bargaining
│  └─ No retaliation for union activity
│  └─ Verification: Union recognition agreements, strike records, worker testimonials

WAGE & WORKING HOURS REQUIREMENTS:
├─ Minimum wage: 100%+ of local legal minimum (actual baseline)
├─ Living wage target: 60% of suppliers by Year 3
│  └─ Living wage definition: Income sufficient for food, shelter, healthcare, education, transportation
│  └─ Benchmark: ILO methodology or Anker Research living wage calculations
├─ Overtime: Voluntary only, 1.5x+ premium pay
├─ Working hours: Max 48/week standard + 8/week voluntary overtime
├─ Rest periods: Minimum 1 day per week, paid leave accrual

VERIFICATION METHOD:
├─ Annual wage audits: Sample 30-50% of workforce
├─ Payroll documentation review: Verify wages paid ≥ minimum
├─ Worker interviews: Private, confidential, multiple languages
├─ Third-party verification: External auditor confirms compliance

CORRECTIVE ACTION FOR GAPS:
├─ Wage below legal minimum: 30-day remediation, back pay + penalties
├─ Living wage gap: 12-month gap closure plan, phased increases
├─ Excessive overtime: Immediate workforce adjustment or equipment investment
├─ Termination violations: Severance compensation per local law

OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH & SAFETY:
├─ Safe, hygienic working conditions
├─ Hazard assessments & risk mitigation
├─ Safety training for all employees
├─ Emergency procedures & evacuation drills
├─ PPE provision & ergonomic assessments
├─ Lost-time injury rate target: <5 per 200,000 hours worked
├─ Annual safety audits minimum

#### B. Audit & Assessment Program

**Two-Tier Assessment Approach:**

```
TIER 1: SELF-ASSESSMENT QUESTIONNAIRE (SAQ) - Annual, All Suppliers

Structure:
├─ Format: 60-question online questionnaire
├─ Time to complete: 2-4 hours per supplier
├─ Frequency: Annual (escalation if red flags)
├─ Cost: Minimal (technology-enabled)

Topics Covered:
├─ Labor practices (child labor, forced labor, wages, hours)
├─ Environmental management (emissions, water, waste, chemicals)
├─ Health & safety (facilities, equipment, training)
├─ Governance & compliance (anti-corruption, transparency, grievances)
├─ Supply chain (sub-tier visibility, sub-tier compliance)

Scoring Methodology:
├─ Compliance score: 0-100 based on responses
├─ Red flags: Automatic escalation to on-site audit
├─ Improvement trajectory: Tracked year-over-year
├─ Targets:
│  ├─ Year 1: Average 70 score (baseline establishment)
│  ├─ Year 2: Average 80 score (20% improvement)
│  └─ Year 3: Average 85 score (sustained improvement)

Escalation Triggers (→ Tier 2 Audit):
├─ SAQ score <60/100
├─ Contradiction with previous responses
├─ Missing critical documentation
├─ Unverifiable claims (no supporting evidence)
└─ Public allegations of labor/environmental violations
```

**TIER 2: ON-SITE AUDIT - Risk-Based Frequency**

```
AUDIT FREQUENCY BY RISK TIER:

Tier 1 (Green): Every 2-3 years
├─ Denmark, Singapore, Japan, Germany, Canada
├─ Low corruption, mature regulatory environment
├─ Streamlined 2-day audit (governance + sampling)

Tier 2 (Yellow): Annually  
├─ Mexico, Vietnam, Thailand, Poland, Czech Republic
├─ Moderate risk, evolving standards
├─ Standard 5-day audit (comprehensive coverage)

Tier 3 (Orange): Semi-annually (every 6 months)
├─ India, Indonesia, Brazil, Turkey, Morocco
├─ Emerging risks, regulatory gaps
├─ Enhanced 5-day audit + quarterly follow-up calls

Tier 4 (Red): Quarterly or monthly
├─ Myanmar, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Nigeria
├─ High-risk governance, compliance challenges
├─ Monthly certifications, quarterly on-site audits
├─ Contingency plans for phase-out/replacement

TYPICAL 5-DAY ON-SITE AUDIT STRUCTURE:

Day 1: Governance & Management Systems
├─ Opening meeting: Scope confirmation, process overview
├─ Management interviews: Ownership, compliance responsibility, financials
├─ Document review: Licenses, permits, certifications, financial statements
├─ Organizational structure: Supply chain mapping, sub-tier visibility

Day 2: Labor Practices & Workforce Management
├─ Personnel file review: 30-50 random employee files
├─ Wage audit: Payroll verification vs. local minimum standards
├─ Contract review: Employment terms, termination procedures
├─ Worker interviews: 20-30 private, confidential conversations
│  ├─ Questions: Working hours, safety, wages, treatment, grievances
│  ├─ Setting: Private space, translator if needed, confidentiality assured
│  └─ No retaliation: Worker protection communication
├─ Union engagement: Verify recognition agreements, collective bargaining

Day 3: Environmental, Health & Safety (EHS) Compliance
├─ Facility tour: Equipment, waste management, emissions controls
├─ Chemical management: MSDS verification, storage, handling procedures
├─ Safety procedures: Training records, incident logs, PPE availability
├─ Energy/water systems: Consumption monitoring, efficiency programs
├─ Environmental records: Permits, inspections, violation history
├─ EHS audit checklist: 30+ point assessment (machinery, ergonomics, signage)

Day 4: Supply Chain & Ethical Sourcing
├─ Sub-supplier list: Identification of all key material suppliers
├─ Sub-tier risk assessment: Sampling of 3-5 critical sub-suppliers
├─ Grievance mechanism: Verification of worker complaint processes
├─ Documentation: Supplier contracts, compliance records
├─ Traceability: Material sourcing documentation (conflict minerals if applicable)

Day 5: Findings & Corrective Action Planning
├─ Audit summary: Detailed findings review with supplier management
├─ Scoring: 0-100 point assessment (see below)
├─ Severity rating: Critical, major, minor findings
├─ Corrective Action Plan: Development timeline based on finding type
├─ Management closeout: Preliminary findings, next steps, communication

SCORE INTERPRETATION:

100-90 = Full compliance, no findings
80-89  = Minor findings, immediate corrections required
70-79  = Moderate findings, 30-60 day CAP
60-69  = Significant findings, enhanced monitoring, possible volume restrictions
<60    = Critical violations, 90-day remediation or supplier replacement

CORRECTIVE ACTION PLAN (CAP) PROCESS:

Minor Finding (Score 90-99):
├─ Immediate correction within 7 days
├─ Documentation: Email confirmation sufficient
├─ Follow-up: No additional verification required
└─ Volume impact: None

Moderate Finding (Score 80-89):
├─ Root cause analysis due within 7 days
├─ CAP submission due within 14 days
│  ├─ Root cause statement
│  ├─ Corrective actions (specific, measurable)
│  ├─ Timeline (30-day target)
│  ├─ Responsible party assignment
│  └─ Prevention measures for future
├─ Follow-up: Remote verification or 30-day site visit
├─ Volume: Continued at
