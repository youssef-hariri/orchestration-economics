**BOARD RECOMMENDATION: THE SOVEREIGN STACK v3**  
*Growth-Financing Strategy to Fund $32M Expansion, Preserve Absolute Control, and Capture the Market Window*

---

### EXECUTIVE RECOMMENDATION

The board is asked to **reject** three unacceptable alternatives: covenant-heavy bank debt that transfers operational control to credit committees; a “low” minority-equity round that permanently dilutes founders to ~64% and forces board seats; and a go-slow organic plan that concedes the market to a well-funded rival.

Instead, we recommend **The Sovereign Stack v3**: a hybrid, control-preserving capital structure that closes within 60–90 days, funds simultaneous capacity, hiring, and market expansion, and limits founder dilution to **3.5% non-voting warrants**—keeping founder equity above **75%**.

| **Metric** | **Target** |
|:---|:---|
| Total Initial Commitments | **$32M** |
| Non-Dilutive Customer Deposits | **$3–5M** |
| Post-Financing Founder Ownership | **~75.3%** |
| Blended All-In Cost of Capital | **~13.5%** |
| Time to Initial Close | **10 weeks** |

### THE STRATEGIC IMPERATIVE: WHY NOW

- **The competitive window is 18–24 months.** A rival is raising aggressively. Sequential, self-funded expansion cedes customer relationships and talent permanently.
- **Control is non-negotiable.** The founders’ 78% stake is a strategic asset, not merely a wealth metric. Any instrument requiring a board seat, budget veto, or governance veto is disqualified.
- **The ramp will compress EBITDA.** Simultaneous capacity, hiring, and geo-launch will temporarily depress margins. Financing must accommodate this compression without triggering technical default.

### RECOMMENDED FUNDING STRUCTURE

#### **Priority 1: Senior Growth Debt — $10M**  
*($7M initial draw + $3M delayed-draw term loan)*

- **Purpose:** Hiring, working capital, market-launch OpEx, equipment deposits.
- **Terms:** 42-month term; **18 months interest-only (I/O)**; then 24-month straight amortization at **11.5%** cash interest.
- **Warrants:** **2.0%** at a **$100M strike** (defensible to current 409A FMV).
- **Security:** First priority lien on all corporate assets *contingent* on executing a **PMSI intercreditor** that grants the equipment lender super-priority on new equipment.
- **Covenants:** **Months 1–18:** Minimum unfettered cash of **$3.5M** only. **Months 19+:** Net Debt / EBITDA < **4.0x**. No FCCR or leverage tests during the ramp.
- **Recourse:** Limited personal guarantees **capped at $2.5M aggregate**, burning down 1:1 as leverage drops below 3.0x. No unlimited recourse.

#### **Priority 2: Equipment Financing (PMSI) — $12M**

- **Purpose:** Hard capacity expansion (machinery, tooling, infrastructure).
- **Terms:** 6-year term; **12 months I/O** during installation/commissioning; then 60-month amortization at **7.5%** fixed.
- **Advance Rate:** 70% of appraised orderly liquidation value (OLV).
- **Security:** **Purchase Money Security Interest (PMSI)** in new equipment grants this lender **super-priority on those specific assets**, senior even to the growth-debt blanket lien. Junior lien on general corporate assets via intercreditor.
- **Recourse:** Corporate guaranty **capped at $3M**, burning down 1:1 as principal amortizes below $6M. **No personal guarantees.**

#### **Priority 3: Subordinated Mezzanine Note — $5M**

- **Purpose:** Market-expansion buffer and contingency reserve.
- **Terms:** 5-year bullet maturity (no amortization until Month 48). **8% cash pay + 6% PIK** (14% all-in).
  - *Cash-pay holiday:* If liquidity drops below $4M, cash interest defers for up to 6 months.
- **Warrants:** **1.5%** at $100M strike.
- **Security:** Subordinated lien on corporate assets. **Non-voting. No board seat. No protective provisions. No MAC clause.**

#### **Priority 4: Customer Capacity Reservations — $3–5M**

- **Purpose:** Non-dilutive validation capital.
- **Structure:** Non-refundable production-slot deposits (10–20%) from anchor customers to secure future capacity. Standard in industrial contract manufacturing and CPG.
- **Alternative:** Supply-chain finance (2–3% net discount for accelerated payables).
- **Rationale:** Replaces Revenue-Based Financing, which is incompatible with 30–40% gross-margin industrial economics.

- **No personal financial ruin:** Recourse is quantified, capped, and burns down with performance.

### STAGED DEPLOYMENT: THE SIX GATES

Debt documents close at signing, but **capital deployment is operationally gated** through founder-controlled budgets, delayed-draw mechanics, and availability periods.

| **Gate** | **Timing** | **Capital Action** | **Operational Milestone to Unlock** |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Gate 0: Close** | Month 0 | Draw Senior $7M; activate Equipment $12M commitment; lock Mezz $5M commitment. | Signed term sheets and intercreditor. Board-approved CapEx plan. |
| **Gate 1: Deploy** | Month 0–2 | Spend $5M on equipment deposits/POs; $3M on strategic hiring. | Hired VP Ops and Geo-Launch lead. Vendor contracts signed. |
| **Gate 2: Deliver** | Month 3 | Draw Equipment $12M against invoices. | Asset delivery and inspection. |
| **Gate 3: Ignite** | Month 4 | Draw Mezzanine $5M. | New capacity **booked to >60%** for Q3 OR new-market qualified pipeline >3x Q3 sales target. |
| **Gate 4: Accelerate** | Month 9 | Draw Senior DDTL $3M. | Trailing 3-month blended gross margin **up >100 bps vs. Month 3** AND new-market CAC payback <14 months. |
| **Gate 5: Accrete** | Month 12 | Activate Customer Capacity Reservations $3–5M. | Monthly expansion cash burn <$400K AND unfettered liquidity >$4.5M. |
| **Gate 6: Refinance** | Month 18–24 | Pay off or refinance Mezzanine. | Net Debt / EBITDA <3.0x on LTM basis. |

**Governance:** Founders retain unilateral authority to unlock each gate. Lenders do not vote on operational milestones.

### WALK-AWAY CONDITIONS (RED LINES)

If any counterparty demands the following, **terminate discussions immediately.**

#### Senior Growth Debt
- Personal guarantees exceeding **$2.5M aggregate** or lacking a burn-down provision.
- Warrants **>3.0%** or strike price **<90%** of defensible 409A FMV.
- Cash interest **>13%** or I/O period **<15 months**.
- Leverage, FCCR, or DSCR covenants tested **before Month 18**.
- **MAC clause** with subjective triggers or demand/acceleration features.
- Refusal to execute a **PMSI intercreditor** acknowledging the equipment lender’s super-priority.

#### Equipment Financing (PMSI)
- **Personal guarantees** from founders.
- **Cross-collateralization** of all corporate assets beyond a capped corporate guaranty.
- Advance rate **<65%** of OLV.
- Amortization start **before Month 6** or interest rate **>9%**.
- Senior lender refusal to sign **PMSI acknowledgment**.

#### Subordinated Mezzanine
- Cash interest **>10%** (excluding PIK) or warrants **>2.5%**.
- **Board seat, voting rights,** or protective provisions requiring supermajority consent.
- Scheduled amortization **before Month 36**.
- Financial covenants other than a liquidity floor, or **any personal recourse**.

#### Customer Capacity Reservations / Supply Finance
- Pricing discounts **>5%** on standard SKUs.
- **Exclusivity** >12 months.
- Refund obligations without matching supplier cancellation rights.

#### Minority Equity (Future Round Only, Month 24+)
- Pre-money valuation **<4.0x trailing revenue** or **<$200M**.
- Liquidation preference **>1.0x non-participating** (no participating preferred).
- **Full-ratchet anti-dilution** (weighted average is market).
- **Board seat** for a <20% holder.
- **Drag-along rights** without founder consent below a **$300M** exit threshold.

#### Disciplined Organic Growth
- If competitive intelligence confirms the rival will achieve **>25% share of the target TAM within 18 months**, abandon organic-only sequencing entirely.

**Fallback A — Shrink the Raise:** Fund **$20M** only (Equipment $12M + Mezz $5M + Customer Reservations $3M). Fund capacity and core hiring; defer geo-launch 9–12 months. Accept slower market capture but retain absolute control.

**Fallback B — Strategic Minority Equity (Last Resort):** Accept equity at **$140M pre-money** **only if** all of the following are met:
- Non-voting preferred.
- **1x non-participating** liquidation preference.
- Observer only; **no board seat**.
- Weighted-average anti-dilution only.
