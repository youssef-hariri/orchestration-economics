## SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1 PAGE)

### Strategy Overview
This document presents a **controlled 36-month transition from perpetual-license to subscription revenue** for a $5M software business. The strategy reduces projected customer churn from 20% (abrupt transition risk, per competitor precedent) to **6–8%**, protects gross margin dollars (+70% over baseline by Year 1), and unlocks **$85M–$95M shareholder value** via recurring-revenue multiple expansion (3–4x → 8–10x).

### Core Value Proposition
- **Customer-centric:** Tiered offers (Track A: Grandfathered Perpetual + 40% discount; Track B: 45% discount + 2–5 year tenure; Track C: Freemium entry) preserve optionality and acknowledge customer tenure/entitlement
- **Risk-managed:** Churn reduced via maintenance credits, right-of-return guarantees, and cohort pricing locks (no discount-cliff spike in Year 2)
- **Financially accretive:** Year 1 gross margin dollars +70% ($3.6M → $6.1M) despite revenue trough of –6% net; break-even on transition costs by Q4 Y1; Year 3 revenue inflection to $12.88M (+158% vs. baseline)
- **Operationally clear:** Sales force realigned via blended commission (+125% Year 1 comp attraction); CSM staffing scaled to 6 FTE (1:65 customer ratio); quarterly board decision gates prevent surprises

### Timeline & Key Milestones
| Phase | Quarter | Deliverable | Success Metric |
|-------|---------|-----------|---|
| **Preparation** | Q2–Q3 Y0 | Product roadmap lock; sales comp finalized; systems go-live | Enablement complete by Q4 Y0 |
| **Launch** | Q4 Y0 – Q2 Y1 | Migration offers deployed; Track A/B/C segmentation executed | 160 customers (32%) adopt subscription by Q2 Y1 |
| **Inflection** | Q3 Y1 – Q1 Y2 | Subscription revenue exceeds perpetual; renewal cohort stabilizes | Subscription revenue >60% of total by Q1 Y2; <80% churn on renewals |
| **Maturity** | Q2 Y2 – Q4 Y3 | Perpetual support scaled down; recurring revenue normalized | 500 customer base on subscription; $12.88M revenue; 91–92% 3-year retention |

### Investment Required & Financial Payoff
- **Transition cost:** $1.99M (sales comp $1.5M attrition, systems $250K, CSM hiring $400K, enablement $550K, contingency $290K)
- **Year 1 margin dollars:** $6.1M (vs. $3.6M baseline) = +$2.5M cash generation to fund transition
- **Break-even:** Q4 Y1 (cumulative contribution margin $5.6M exceeds transition cost)
- **Year 3 NPV (10-year horizon, 8% discount rate):** +$45M–$55M (recurring subscription base alone)

## SECTION 4: SALES COMPENSATION REDESIGN

### Current (Perpetual License) Model

**Commission Structure:**
- Base salary: 50% of OTE = $200K
- Perpetual license commission: 20% of deal value
- Maintenance commission: 5% of 3-year bundle value
- **Example:** $100K license + $24K maintenance (3-yr value)
  - License commission: $20K
  - Maintenance commission: $1.2K
  - **Total Year 1 cash payout: ~$21.2K**
  - **Total OTE with base: $200K + $21.2K = $221.2K**

**Drawback:** Large, infrequent deals create lumpy compensation; high variable % makes income unpredictable.
