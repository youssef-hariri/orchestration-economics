## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Recommendation: PROCEED WITH 7-SKU PORTFOLIO (3 HERO + 4 SUPPORT), SUBJECT TO CRITICAL REFORMULATIONS**

Rasa Nusantara Premium represents an authentic, strategically coherent entry into Indonesia's USD 3.2B+ snacking market. The line rejects Western clichés, anchoring instead in regionally specific flavors (sambal matah, ubi ungu, gula merah, tempeh fermentation) paired with premium positioning and modern retail execution.

**Key Metrics:**
- **Investment Required:** USD 1.2–1.5M (6–9 months to commercial production)
- **Year 1 Revenue Forecast:** USD 4.2–5.8M (conservative ramp; Heroes only through Month 6, Support phased Months 6–12)
- **Portfolio Gross Margin:** 55–62% (average 59%)
- **Target Segments:** Urban Millennials (25–35), Health-Conscious Fitness Enthusiasts (20–35), Value Families (35–50), Young Students (12–18)

**Critical Path Issues (Must Resolve Pre-Production):**
1. **SKU 5 (Nougat):** Gelatin sourcing unresolved (pork vs. beef-halal vs. agar-agar); must lock specification before Month 2
2. **SKU 2 (Tempeh):** Petis (fermented shrimp) supplier halal certification unvalidated; requires supplier vetting Month 1–2
3. **SKU 3 (Tuna Crispy Chip):** Halal-certified tuna processor not identified; recommend Month 9–12 contingent launch
4. **Regulatory Roadmap:** No SKU includes MUI halal submission timeline or supplier pre-approval protocol; add as implementation detail

**Verdict:** Strong market fit + authentic flavor logic; feasibility contingent on ingredient sourcing hardening and regulatory pathway clarification.

### SKU 1: SAMBAL MATAH PUFFED RICE CLUSTERS
**Local Name:** Keripik Sambal Matah Nusantara

**Format:** Puffed rice clusters (3–5mm diameter, aerated bite-sized spheres)

**Flavor/Ingredients:**
- Puffed jasmine rice (East Java sourced)
- Sambal matah spice blend: red chili, shallot, garlic, lime leaf, galangal, coriander seed
- Coconut oil (5–7% bind) + natural antioxidant (vitamin E, rosemary extract)
- Sesame seeds (2% accent)
- **NO kecap manis** (fresh, not sweet—health positioning)
- **NO artificial colors/flavors**

**Texture:** Crispy, light crunch; fresh sambal aroma (herbal, citrus-forward); medium spice heat (3–4/10)

**Pack Sizes & Price:**
- 35g Single-Serve: IDR 8,500 (~USD 0.57)
- 150g Sharing: IDR 22,000 (~USD 1.47)
- 5×20g Premium Gift Box: IDR 35,000 (~USD 2.33)

**Primary Occasion:** Office/commute snacking (daily consumption frequency driver)

**Target Segment:** Urban Millennials, Health-Conscious Professionals

**Why It Wins:**
- **Authenticity:** East Java sambal matah (non-cooked, herb-forward) = distinctive vs. mainstream sambal ulek
- **Health Halo:** "Zero kecap manis" = appeals to macro-conscious, sugar-aware consumers
- **Format Innovation:** Puffed (lighter perception) vs. fried chips mainstream
- **Margin:** 62–65% gross margin (commodity puffed rice + spice blending)
- **Differentiation:** vs. Chitato (fried, sweet baseline) + vs. local kripik (inconsistent, limited distribution)

**Shelf-Life:** 4 months (antioxidant-preserved; nitrogen-flushed pouch required)

**Allergen:** Contains sesame; may contain tree nuts from processing

**Halal Status:** Inherently halal; MUI certification recommended (no special processing required)
