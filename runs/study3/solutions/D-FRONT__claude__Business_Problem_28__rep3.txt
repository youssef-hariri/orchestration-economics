# INDUSTRIAL SERVICES UNIT: TURNAROUND-OR-EXIT ASSESSMENT
## Board-Ready Final Decision Plan

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**SITUATION:**  
A diversified group's industrial-services unit has lost €25M cumulatively over three years despite two restructurings. Revenue has fallen 12% to €88M; EBITDA is –€12M (–13.6%); order book has collapsed 47%; leadership churn is severe. The unit serves ~18–22% of group's top-40 customer accounts (€12–15M cross-sell revenue). The board has mandated a credible path to profitability or exit within 12 months.

**VERDICT:**  
The proposed turnaround plan cannot proceed as currently written. **Two sequential cost-centric restructurings have failed and worsened EBITDA**, indicating the root cause is **revenue-side (structural), not cost-side (execution).** The unit exhibits a **65% structural problem** (cost base oversized for declining market; backlog collapse; customer mix degradation) and **35% execution opportunity** (SG&A overhead reduction, pricing discipline, utilization gains). 

**Turnaround success probability is 45–50% at best, contingent on customer commitments and backlog recovery that have not yet been validated.** Exit is a credible and potentially preferable alternative.

**RECOMMENDATION:**  
**DO NOT PROCEED with full 11-initiative turnaround immediately.** Instead, execute a **90-day diagnostic sprint (Months 1–3)** to validate:
1. Whether customer churn is reversible (execution/pricing-driven) or permanent (structural/competitive).
2. Whether backlog recovery is achievable (committed customer bookings ≥€95M annualized).
3. Whether profitable customer segments and service lines exist (>50% of revenue from lines with ≥20% gross margin).

**Month-3 Hard Gate:** If diagnostics pass, proceed full turnaround with revised Month-12 (–€2M to +€1M EBITDA) and Month-18 (€3–6M) targets. If diagnostics fail, **pivot immediately to exclusive exit process (dual-track sale to PE or strategic buyer) with close target Month 11–12.**

**DECISION RULE:**  
- **Month 3 PASS:** ≥5 of 6 diagnostic gates achieved → authorize €6–10M turnaround funding; proceed Months 4–12.
- **Month 3 FAIL:** ≤4 of 6 diagnostic gates achieved → authorize investment-banking mandate; exclusive exit process begins immediately.
- **Month 6 Kill-Switch:** If booking rate is declining, customer churn >20% YoY, utilization <65%, or EBITDA <–€10M, board pivots to exit regardless of Month-3 decision.

**IMMEDIATE 30-DAY ACTIONS (Months 1–2):**
1. Establish steering committee (Division Head + Group Finance + Group Ops); deploy KPI dashboard (15–20 metrics).
2. Initiate diagnostic sprint: customer profitability audit (top 50 accounts); service-line P&L (24-month history); win/loss interviews (30 deals); backlog quality analysis; crew utilization audit; SG&A benchmarking.
3. Deploy foundational initiatives (Initiatives 4, 5, 6): hire project managers; activate scheduling system (50% of crew base); begin procurement vendor consolidation.
4. Prepare exit alternatives brief (four options: liquidation, PE carve-out, trade sale, group absorption) with valuation scenarios and timelines.
5. Obtain board approval for Month-3 diagnostic gates and commit to hard pivot decision.

## 4. EXIT ALTERNATIVES: FOUR DISTINCT OPTIONS WITH VALUATION LOGIC

### 4.1 Option A: Orderly Wind-Down (Liquidation)

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Definition** | Systematically exit contracts, retire workforce, liquidate assets over 12–18 months. |
| **Process** | (1) Issue contract termination notices (30–90 day notice periods). (2) Transition customer relationships to group or third-party providers. (3) Offer retention packages to key employees (1–3 months severance). (4) Liquidate fleet/equipment over 6–9 months (Sunbelt, private buyers, auctions). (5) Wind down facilities and consolidate to group. |
| **Timeline** | Close by Month 18. |
| **Proceeds
