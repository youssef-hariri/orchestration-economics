# Executive Summary: Strategic Subscription Transition Plan

## **Strategic Mandate**
To pivot the business from a "lumpy" perpetual license model to a predictable, high-multiple Subscription-as-a-Service (SaaS) model. The transition is designed to maximize long-term Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) and Net Revenue Retention (NRR) while maintaining cash-flow stability and minimizing customer churn through a "gravity-led" adoption strategy.

---

## **1. The Financial Bridge (Risk Mitigation)**
To avoid a liquidity crisis and manage the "revenue trough," we will adopt a **"Minimum Cash Floor"** strategy.
*   **The Floor:** All migration contracts must ensure a minimum cash collection equal to 100% of the previous year’s annual maintenance fee. 
*   **Value Application:** Any migration discounts (Loyalty Credits) will be applied to future renewal years (Years 2 and 3) rather than the initial contract, protecting current-year cash flows.
*   **Visual Narrative:** We will provide investors with a **"Transition Bridge Waterfall"** chart—a multi-year projection overlaying declining legacy revenue with rising ARR to clearly visualize the "Value Inflection Point."

## **2. Offer & Migration Structure**
We are shifting from forced conversion to **"Value-Based Gravity."**
*   **Hybrid Bridge:** Existing customers maintain access to legacy versions, but all new feature development will occur exclusively in the SaaS environment.
*   **Upgrade Indicators:** The software will include UI-integrated "Upgrade Indicators" that contrast manual legacy workarounds with automated SaaS productivity gains, reframing the transition as a value-add upgrade.
*   **Loyalty Incentives:** 18–24 month pro-rated credit for remaining maintenance contracts and a 3-year "Subscription Price Lock" for early adopters.

## **3. Sales Alignment & Compensation**
We are re-orienting the sales organization from "one-time deal closing" to "account growth."
*   **Incremental Growth Multiplier:** The 1.5x commission multiplier applies **only to Incremental ACV** (SaaS price minus the legacy maintenance fee), forcing reps to sell product value rather than just administrative migration.
*   **Escrow-Based Retention:** 20% of the commission is held in escrow, paid out only after the customer completes a successful first-year renewal, ensuring long-term "stickiness" and preventing low-quality "shelfware" sales.

## **4. Strategic Implementation Roadmap**
| Phase | Focus | Key KPI |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1 (M0–6)** | Education & Pilot (Top 50 Accounts) | Migration NPS |
| **Phase 2 (M6–18)** | Incentivized Migration Rollout | Incremental ACV |
| **Phase 3 (M18+)** | Controlled EOL of Perpetual | Recurring Revenue Mix |

---

## **Board Action Items**
1.  **Approve the "Minimum Cash Floor" Policy:** Ensure treasury oversight on all migration contracts to prevent liquidity erosion.
2.  **Ratify the "Bridge Commission" Structure:** Formalize the pivot to *Incremental ACV* as the primary sales metric.
3.  **Adopt "Recurring Revenue Mix" as the Primary Financial North Star:** Pivot board-level quarterly reviews away from GAAP total revenue and toward ARR and NRR growth.
4.  **Monitor "Circuit Breaker" Metrics:** Board review of churn rates at the end of each phase; authority to pause the Phase 3 (EOL) transition if churn exceeds pre-defined thresholds.

## **Risk Mitigation Summary**
*   **Liquidity Risk:** Mitigated by the *Minimum Cash Floor* and future-dated loyalty credits.
*   **Churn Risk:** Mitigated by the *Hybrid Bridge* (no hard cut-off) and the *Capability Roadmap* (feature-led value proposition).
*   **Investor Sentiment:** Mitigated by transparent "Waterfall" financial reporting and the aggressive push to improve NRR/LTV.
