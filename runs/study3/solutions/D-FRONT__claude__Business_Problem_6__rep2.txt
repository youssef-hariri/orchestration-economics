## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This integrated digital-first campaign introduces a premium dietary supplement brand to South Korea's USD 8–9B market via science-backed positioning, transparent sourcing, and culturally-respectful messaging honoring both traditional health wisdom and modern clinical evidence. The campaign targets three affluent consumer segments (Preventive Health Optimizers 55%, Filial Duty Advocates 25%, Beauty-Wellness Seekers 15%) across YouTube, Instagram, Naver Cafe, e-commerce, pharmacy partnerships, and direct-to-consumer channels.

**Core Strategy:** Position supplement as evidence-driven, ethically-sourced vitality enabler—not miracle cure, not appearance-fixing product, not exploitative marketing. All claims FHC-approved by MFDS; zero disease/cure language; explicit protection of vulnerable audiences (aging adults, health-anxious consumers, menopausal women).

**Key Success Metrics (Year 1):** 35% aided brand awareness | 50,000 units sold | CAC ≤ KRW 15,000 | NPS ≥55 | Zero regulatory violations | 1,500+ Naver Cafe community members

### 1.2 Core Health Beliefs & Values Framework

**Prevention > Treatment Orientation:**  
South Korean consumers view health through preventive lens ("Prevention is better than cure" — *예방이 최고의 약*). Supplements position as long-term wellness investment, enabling professional performance and family presence rather than crisis intervention.

**Seasonal Body Balance (*사계절 체질*):**  
Traditional Sasang constitutional medicine and Yin-Yang philosophy inform beliefs:
- **Spring:** Renewal, liver support, light energy  
- **Summer:** Cooling, hydration, sustained energy  
- **Autumn:** Moisture, respiratory support, immune preparation  
- **Winter:** Warmth, immunity, vitality preservation  

*Campaign Approach:* Year 1 = single formula with seasonal educational messaging (no efficacy claims). Year 2 = explore seasonal variants if consumer research validates demand AND regulatory pathway clarified with MFDS.

**Holistic Wellness Concept:**  
Health encompasses physical + mental + emotional + social dimensions. Integrated narrative: "Better energy → better work focus → more family presence → reduced stress → improved sleep" resonates more than siloed benefit claims.

**Trust in Evidence + Tradition Fusion:**  
Korean consumers respect both Western scientific rigor AND Korean herbal heritage. Ideal positioning: "Korean ginseng has 1,000+ year heritage; modern research validates bioavailability and sustained energy effects" (heritage + evidence, never heritage-only).

### 1.4 Skepticism Triggers & Risk Mitigation

| Trigger | Why It Damages Trust | Campaign Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| **Disease/Cure Claims** | MFDS violation; signals illegitimacy; triggers enforcement | Prohibited language audit; zero "treats," "cures," "prevents disease"; monthly compliance scan |
| **Vague "Miracle" Language** | Conflicts with preference for scientific precision; breeds skepticism | Specific timelines ("results in 2–4 weeks"); measured language ("supports," "helps maintain"); realistic expectations |
| **Over-Reliance on Celebrity** | Koreans skeptical of paid celebrity ads; assume no actual use | Avoid celebrity contracts; prioritize micro-influencers with health credentials; require 3+ collaborations per influencer (not one-off) |
| **Lack of Korean Language Depth** | Signals disrespect; raises safety concerns | All packaging, website, customer support in detailed Korean; zero English-only sections |
| **Unsubstantiated Heritage Claims** | Conflates tradition with efficacy without evidence | Separate "Heritage Context (Educational)" from "Functional Benefits (FHC-Approved)"; visual demarcation required |
| **Before/After Imagery** | MFDS-prohibited; triggers feminist backlash; body-shaming risk | ZERO appearance before/after; functional testimonials only ("My energy improved"); lifestyle focus in video content |
| **Comparative Advertising** | Culturally taboo; damages brand perception; no competitive advantage | Never name competitors; avoid "better than" framing; let consumers compare; lead on absolute benefits |
| **Undisclosed Influencer Sponsorship** | #광고 tag legally required; non-compliance = KFTC fine + viral backlash | Mandatory #광고 on ALL influencer posts; contract clause with non-compliance penalties; monthly audit via social listening |
| **Body-Shaming Language** | Violates feminist values; triggers Gen Z backlash; reputational damage | Prohibited: "fix," "hide," "erase," "flabby," "saggy"; Approved: "support," "enhance," "vitality," "natural evolution" |

### 4.4 Claim Substantiation & Regulatory File Requirements

**By Campaign Launch Date, Campaign Must Have Compiled:**

1. **Approved Claims List (MFDS Written Approval Letter)**
   - Specify each claim, corresponding FHC category, approval status  
   - Approval letter from MFDS (or confirmation of pre-filing inquiry results)  
   - Timeline: Submit pre-approval request by Week 2 of campaign planning; expect 6–8 week MFDS review

2. **Clinical Evidence Documentation (For Each Approved Claim)**
   - PubMed study citation (author, year, journal, DOI)  
   - Study summary in English + Korean translation (500 words max)  
   - Key data: sample size (n), effect size (Cohen's d or similar), p-value, confidence interval  
   - Verification that supplement dosage ≥ study dosage; if lower, justification provided  
   - Limitations of study transparently noted (e.g., "small sample size," "short duration")

3. **Safety & Adverse Event Data**
   - Full safety profile document (common side effects, rare adverse events, contraindications)  
   - Drug-interaction guide (specifically tested against common Korean medications: antihypertensives, diabetes medications, NSAIDs, anticoagulants, statins, thyroid medications)  
   - Allergy risk assessment (if any allergens present, clearly disclosed)  
   - Pediatric/pregnancy/nursing safety status (e.g., "Not recommended for pregnant women; consult healthcare provider if nursing")

4. **Ingredient Sourcing & Manufacturing Documentation**
   - Full supply chain documentation (supplier → processor → manufacturer → importer → distributor → consumer)  
   - GMP facility certification (foreign manufacturers must be certified; obtain documentation BEFORE import)  
   - Third-party testing results (heavy metals, microbial contamination, identity verification)  
   - Ingredient origin & specifications (e.g., "Korean ginseng: Geumsan region, 6-year cultivated, organic certification number: [XX]")

5. **Legal Review & Compliance Certification**
   - Internal legal counsel review of all claims + marketing materials (website, video scripts, influencer briefs, packaging mockups)  
   - Sign-off document: "This campaign has been reviewed for MFDS compliance and FHC adherence; claims are approved for public distribution"  
   - Dated, signed by responsible legal counsel

**Regulatory Liaison Contact:**  
Appoint single point of contact for MFDS inquiries; maintain documentation of all communications with MFDS for regulatory audit trail.
