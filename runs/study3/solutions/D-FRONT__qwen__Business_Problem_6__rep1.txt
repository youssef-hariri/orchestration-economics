# MARKETING CAMPAIGN BRIEF
**Project:** Ikiiki Daily Greens – Japan Market Launch  
**Document Type:** Strategic Marketing & Activation Brief  
**Status:** Finalized for Execution  

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
**The Product:** *Ikiiki Daily Greens* is a premium, plant-based dietary supplement formulated in a convenient stick-powder format. It is designed to support gut health, sustained daily vitality, and mental clarity. 

**The Market:** Japan. A market characterized by a rapidly aging population, high life expectancy, and a deep cultural reverence for preventative health (*Ishoku Dogen*). Japanese consumers are highly discerning, deeply skeptical of exaggerated Western-style "miracle" health claims, and operate within a strictly regulated advertising environment.

**The Strategic Vision:** To position *Ikiiki Daily Greens* not as an "anti-aging" fix, but as a daily ritual that supports physical and mental vitality for a high Quality of Life (QOL). By anchoring the campaign in the authentic cultural concepts of **"Ikiiki" (vitality)** and **"Well-Aging,"** and strictly adhering to Japan's rigorous ethical and regulatory frameworks, this campaign will build unshakeable institutional trust and drive long-term subscription loyalty.

---

## 2. CAMPAIGN CONCEPT & CORE MESSAGING

### Campaign Title
**毎日に、いきいきを。** *(Bring Vitality to Every Day.)*

### Core Concept
We are shifting the narrative away from the anxiety-driven "anti-aging" tropes and the over-commercialized Western interpretation of "Ikigai." Instead, we focus on **"Ikiiki"** (a state of being lively, energetic, and full of vitality) and **"Well-Aging."** The campaign positions the supplement as a seamless, scientifically backed morning ritual that honors the body's natural need for nourishment, bridging traditional respect for natural ingredients with modern gut-health science.

### Key Messaging Matrix
* **Primary Message:** "Support your daily vitality with the wisdom of nature. Gentle nourishment for a healthy, vibrant everyday life."
* **Secondary Message (Gut Health):** "A harmonious intestinal environment is the foundation of a clear mind and a vibrant body." *(Note: Strictly contingent upon approved Foods with Function Claims (FHC) data).*
* **Secondary Message (Daily Ritual):** "Just one stick a day, blending seamlessly into your morning routine."

---

## 3. TARGET AUDIENCE & CULTURAL CONTEXT

* **Demographic:** Health-conscious adults aged 35–65+, with a specific focus on the active, affluent "Well-Aging" cohort.
* **Psychographic:** Values preventative health, transparency, and quality over quick fixes. Skeptical of aggressive marketing. Seeks products that integrate effortlessly into a refined, mindful lifestyle.
* **Cultural Nuance:** The audience respects *Ishoku Dogen* (the philosophy that food and medicine share the same origin) but requires clear distinctions between daily food and regulated supplements. They demand modern, dignified representations of aging, rejecting outdated, frail stereotypes.

---

## 4. CREATIVE & VISUAL DIRECTION

**Aesthetic Style:** *Modern Minimalist & Authentic Vitality*
The visual identity will starkly contrast with aggressive, neon, "shred-and-bulk" Western supplement aesthetics, drawing inspiration instead from high-end Japanese lifestyle and beauty brands.

* **Color Palette:** Soft, natural earth tones. Matcha green, warm beige, soft white, and muted terracotta.
* **Typography:** Clean, modern sans-serif fonts for English, paired with elegant, highly legible Mincho (serif) or clean Gothic fonts for Japanese text to convey both trust and modernity.
* **Photography & Videography:**
  * **Lighting:** Soft, natural *Komorebi* (sunlight filtering through trees) lighting. Absolutely no harsh studio flashes.
  * **Subject Representation:** Modern, active, and dignified. We will feature a 65-year-old woman taking a brisk, joyful walk in a modern park, or a 60-year-old man actively video-calling his grandchildren. The focus is on *modern capability, mobility, and joy*.
  * **Product Styling:** The stick-powder packaging will be showcased in serene, uncluttered environments—next to a steaming cup of hojicha or on a clean wooden countertop.
* **Motion Graphics:** Slow, deliberate, and calming. "Gut harmony" will be visualized through subtle, flowing animations of botanical ingredients, strictly avoiding clinical or overly anatomical graphics.

## 5. CHANNEL & ACTIVATION STRATEGY

### Digital & Social Media
* **LINE Official Account:** The core hub for customer retention and subscription management. All health-related quizzes and personalized tips will be strictly opt-in, ensuring full compliance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI).
* **Instagram:** Aesthetic, lifestyle-driven content. Reels demonstrating the "morning ritual" of mixing the supplement, alongside high-quality photography of the product in beautiful, minimalist Japanese homes.
* **YouTube:** Long-form educational content. Partnering with Registered Dietitians to explain the science of the gut-brain axis in an accessible, polite, and authoritative tone.

### Offline & Experiential
* **"Well-Aging" Pop-Up Cafes:** Located in high-foot-traffic, affluent areas like Daikanyama (Tokyo). These cafes will serve matcha and healthy foods infused with the supplement's core ingredients, offering a tactile, low-pressure brand experience.
* **Strategic Print Media:** Full-page, beautifully designed advertorials in premium lifestyle magazines targeting our core demographics (e.g., *SARAI* for active older men, *Croissant* for health-conscious women).

### PR & Influencer Strategy
* **Micro-Influencers over Mega-Celebrities:** Partnering with niche micro-influencers in the yoga, mindful living, and healthy cooking spaces who genuinely use the product.
* **Expert Education:** Featuring Registered Dietitians and Licensed Pharmacists in educational videos to build institutional trust ethically, without crossing into unauthorized medical endorsements.

## 6. ETHICAL, LEGAL & REGULATORY GUARDRAILS

To ensure unimpeachable brand trust and strict legal compliance in Japan, the following guardrails are non-negotiable:

* **Foods with Function Claims (FHC) Compliance:** All functional claims regarding gut health (and mental clarity, if clinically substantiated) must use exact Consumer Affairs Agency (CAA)-approved phrasing. Every advertisement must prominently display the mandatory CAA disclaimer regarding disease diagnosis/treatment.
* **Premiums Act Adherence:** Strict prohibition of superlatives or exaggerated claims (e.g., "miracle," "cure," "powerful"). Messaging will focus solely on "nutritional support," "maintaining health," and "daily vitality."
* **Ethical Endorsements:** **Strict prohibition** of actors in white coats or paid medical doctors endorsing the product, respecting the Japan Medical Association's ethical guidelines. Only Registered Dietitians and Licensed Pharmacists may be featured for scientific education.
* **Influencer Disclosures (JFTC):** All sponsored influencer content must strictly comply with the Japan Fair Trade Commission’s 2023 guidelines, mandating clear, upfront labeling (e.g., "PR" or "広告") on all posts.
* **Subscription Transparency:** Auto-renewal subscriptions will fully comply with the Specified Commercial Transactions Act. Cancellation policies, minimum purchase cycles, and total costs will be crystal clear and displayed *before* the consumer completes the purchase.
* **Ingredient Transparency:** Full transparency regarding ingredient sourcing. Vague terms like "proprietary blend" are banned; all functional ingredients and their exact quantities will be clearly listed.

---

## 7. MEASUREMENT & KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)

Success will be measured not just by acquisition, but by trust, retention, and compliance:

1. **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) & Subscription Retention:** The ultimate metric of success in the Japanese supplement market. **Target:** >65% auto-renewal retention at the 3-month mark.
2. **Brand Trust & Sentiment:** Measured via quarterly brand lift studies and social listening, specifically tracking positive sentiment velocity around the keywords "trust" (信頼), "natural" (自然), and "reliable" (安心).
3. **LINE Engagement & Privacy Compliance:** Tracking open rates and click-through rates on health tips, coupled with a strict internal audit ensuring **zero** APPI (privacy) breaches.
4. **Regulatory & Ethical Compliance:** Maintaining **zero** warnings, consumer complaints, or flags from the Consumer Affairs Agency (CAA), the Japan Health Food and Nutrition Food Association (JHF), or industry watchdogs.
