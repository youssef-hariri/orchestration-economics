**CAMPAIGN DELIVERABLE**
**CHŌ-WA (腸和): Harmony for the Gut**

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**CHŌ-WA** is a daily dietary supplement positioned for launch in Japan as a **General Food** with permitted **Nutrient Function Claims**. The campaign targets urban Japanese women aged 40–55, leveraging the mainstream cultural momentum of *chō-katsu* (intestinal activity) while strictly respecting Japan’s high-trust, soft-sell advertising norms and regulatory boundaries.

Following multi-stage review, the campaign has been refined to eliminate regulatory ambiguity, cultural dissonance, and commercial gaps. The product architecture, creative platform, and channel mix have been rebalanced to prioritize **LINE** as the primary engagement and conversion hub, supported by print, retail trial infrastructure, and disciplined out-of-home awareness. Before launch, four residual compliance gaps must be closed: (1) abstraction of hero visuals to avoid implied structure/function claims; (2) removal of national-university academic credentials from advertising unless formal institutional approval is obtained; (3) substitution of registered dietitians with non-licensed lifestyle creators to avoid professional ethics conflicts; and (4) full disclosure architecture for subscription commerce under Japan’s Act on Specified Commercial Transactions.

---

## MARKET & CULTURAL RATIONALE

**Japan is selected for three strategic imperatives:**

1. **Cultural Primacy of Gut Health**  
   *Chō-katsu* (腸活) is a mainstream wellness pillar. Japanese consumers widely accept the gut-skin axis and gut-brain axis as components of holistic well-being, yet they expect brands to discuss these topics with scientific restraint rather than hyperbole.

2. **Regulatory Clarity & High Trust Barriers**  
   Japan’s Consumer Affairs Agency (CAA) enforces strict boundaries among pharmaceuticals, Foods with Function Claims (FFC), and general foods. Trust is built through craftsmanship (*shokunin*) storytelling and data transparency, not celebrity hype or “miracle cure” positioning. Operating as a **General Food** with Nutrient Function Claims allows immediate launch while avoiding the notification burden and disclosure complexity of FFC status.

3. **Seasonal & Demographic Fit**  
   An April–June launch aligns with Japan’s fiscal and academic new year, a cultural window when consumers actively seek new personal rituals. Urban women aged 40–55 control household wellness spending, face “sandwich generation” stress, and specifically seek **non-burdensome** (*mendokusakunai*) daily rituals compatible with commuting and compact living.

## MESSAGING & POSITIONING

### Campaign Title
**CHŌ-WA: Chō ni, Wa wo.** (腸和：腸に、和を。)  
*Subtitle: 「整う、日々のリズム。」* (A balanced daily rhythm.)

### Core Pillars
1. **Continuity (継続):** A quiet ritual that requires no audience.
2. **Precision & Heritage (科学と発酵):** Modern nutrient science alongside the mellowness of domestic fermentation.
3. **Invisible Support (見えないサポート):** CHŌ-WA does not transform; it supports the body she already cultivates.

### Permissible Claim Architecture

| Ingredient | Permitted Claim (CAA-Aligned) | Forbidden (Strictly Removed) |
|---|---|---|
| **Zinc** | “Zinc is a nutrient necessary to maintain normal taste and helps maintain healthy skin, mucous membranes, and normal immune function.” | “Boosts immunity,” “fights fatigue,” “prevents colds.” |
| **Vitamin C** | “Vitamin C helps maintain healthy skin and mucous membranes and has antioxidant properties.” | “Anti-aging,” “whitening,” “cures melasma.” |
| **Galactooligosaccharides** | “Contains galactooligosaccharides, a dietary fiber.” *(Fact statement only)* | “Supports bowel rhythm,” “improves constipation,” “balances gut flora,” “detox.” |
| **Fermented Adlay** | “A mellow-flavored extract of domestically grown Job’s tears, produced via fermentation.” *(Taste/origin only)* | “Kampo beauty cure,” “ancient skin remedy,” *yokuinin*, “detox.” |

**Universal Disclaimer (all touchpoints):**  
> ※本品は疾病の診断、治療、予防を目的としたものではありません。  
> *(This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.)*

### Emotional Guardrails
- **Say:** “supports your daily rhythm,” “maintain today’s self,” “at your own pace.”
- **Do Not Say:** “reset,” “reclaim,” “reverse aging,” “cure,” “treat,” “prevent.”
- **Golden Rule:** CHŌ-WA does not transform; it *supports today*.

## ETHICAL & COMPLIANCE SAFEGUARDS

### 1. Product Classification & Claim Boundaries
- CHŌ-WA is marketed strictly as a **General Food** with **Nutrient Function Claims** for Zinc and Vitamin C.
- **GOS** may only be described as a dietary fiber. No structure/function language (e.g., “supports gut flora”) may be used unless the company secures FFC status, at which point the FFC mark, notification number, and full disclosure text must appear on all materials.
- **Fermented adlay** is restricted to taste and origin storytelling. Zero proximity to Kampo, dermatological, or drug efficacy contexts.

### 2. Visual Asset Compliance
- **No biological mechanism imagery** in the hero creative (no CGI bacteria, intestinal walls, or flora blooming).
- **No before/after** body or skin photography.
- **No white-coat talent** or medical settings.
- **No disease vernacular:** *kaizen suru* (improves/cures), *byōki* (disease), or *yobō* (prevention) are banned from all copy.

### 3. Scientific Credential Protocol
- **Named national university professors or emeriti shall not appear** in advertising unless formal, written approval is obtained from the university’s technology licensing office or president’s office, per MEXT side-employment regulations.
- **Recommended alternatives:** Use an abstract “precision of science” brand voice; cite privately employed food researchers; or reference R&D collaboration without naming individual public-university affiliates.

### 4. Influencer & Professional Ethics
- **Registered Dietitians (管理栄養士) are excluded** from paid endorsement roles to avoid violating the Japan Dietetic Association’s ethical code against leveraging professional licenses for commercial product promotion.
- All influencer contracts must mandate **#PR / #提供** placement and prohibit therapeutic anecdotes.

### 5. Subscription Commerce: Act on Specified Commercial Transactions (特商法)
Before activating the LINE auto-delivery subscription:
- **Pre-contract disclosure** must state: total price per shipment, delivery frequency, payment method, cancellation method, seller name/address, and cooling-off rights.
- **Frictionless cancellation** must be available digitally via LINE (no phone-only cancellation hurdles).
- **Labeling:** All subscription pages must carry the “定期購入” (regular purchase) notation and a link to the Specified Commercial Transactions disclosure.
- **Communication:** Order confirmations and pre-shipment reminders must be sent before each auto-delivery charge.

### 6. Retail Staff Protocol
Staff at Matsumoto Kiyoshi and Loft are trained to use this compliant but persuasive script:
> *「腸活を意識されている方に、毎日のサポートとしておすすめです。亜鉛とビタミンCが、毎日の体の基礎を保つのにお役立ちします。7日間のお試しもございます。」*

**Forbidden phrase:** *「飲めば治ります」* (This will cure you.)

### 7. Packaging & Environmental Claims
- Refill pouches are framed directionally: *「詰め替え用パウチで、容器のごみを減らす一歩に。」* *(A refill pouch as a step toward reducing container waste.)*
- **No percentages** (e.g., “40% less plastic”) and no unsubstantiated material claims (e.g., “recycled washi laminate”) until third-party lifecycle certification is obtained.

### 8. Accessibility
- All LINE messages and digital assets targeting the 50+ segment include *furigana* (*yomigana*) above complex kanji.

### 9. Pre-Launch Legal Checklist
Before any asset goes live, confirm:
- [ ] Zinc & Vitamin C claims are verbatim or closely paraphrased from the CAA positive list.
- [ ] GOS is described only as a dietary fiber.
- [ ] Fermented adlay appears only in taste/origin context.
- [ ] Disclaimer appears on all packaging, digital, and print materials.
- [ ] Influencer contracts prohibit therapeutic anecdotes and mandate #PR placement.
- [ ] Hero visual contains no identifiable biological mechanisms.
- [ ] No named public-university scientists appear without institutional written approval.
- [ ] Subscription flow includes full Specified Commercial Transactions disclosure and digital cancellation.
- [ ] Brand name is CHŌ-WA (腸和) across all touchpoints.
- [ ] Emotional promise is limited to “supports daily rhythm” and “maintenance.”
