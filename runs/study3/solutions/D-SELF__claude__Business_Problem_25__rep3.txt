# PART 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1 Page)

## Strategic Context

The company currently operates on a perpetual-license revenue model with paid upgrades, generating $65M annual bookings but suffering from:
- **Revenue volatility** (lumpy upgrade cycles; investors apply 40%+ discount to perpetual-revenue multiples)
- **Customer misalignment** (company incentivizes license sales; customers want predictability and feature velocity)
- **Exit multiple compression** (perpetual software valued at 3–5x revenue; SaaS valued at 8–12x revenue)

**Peer Risk Benchmark:** A competitor transitioned abruptly and lost 25% of installed base within 18 months, triggering a $50M revenue cliff and 40% stock decline.

## Transition Objectives (36-Month Path)

| Objective | Target | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Churn Containment** | <18% installed-base churn (primary covenant); <20% (secondary) | vs. 25%+ peer benchmark; achieved via tiered offers + fairness narrative |
| **Subscription Adoption** | 60%+ of new bookings by Month 12; 75%+ by Month 24 | Signals market traction; enables investor confidence in recurring revenue |
| **Revenue Stabilization** | Bookings inflection to YoY growth by Q2 Year 2; revenue inflection by Q3 Year 2 | Demonstrates controlled transition; not chaotic churn |
| **Stakeholder Alignment** | Board/investor commitment maintained through monthly covenants; sales force exodus <20%; customer satisfaction >Net Promoter Score 40 | Prevents internal collapse during trough |
| **Exit Multiple Expansion** | 2–3x multiple uplift by Year 3 (from 5x to 8–12x revenue multiple) | Justifies temporary revenue trough; creates shareholder value |

## Tiered Offer Architecture (Segmentation-Driven)

### TIER 1: PREMIUM RETENTION (Top 20% by ARR, >$50K)

**Offer Name:** Heritage Premium Subscription

| Component | Specification | Example |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| **Pricing** | Historical perpetual license + 3-year average upgrade spend, locked at –12% | $150K license + $15K/yr upgrade = $195K total → $57.2K/year subscription (3-year commitment) |
| **Lock-in** | 3 years; price escalation 0% Yr1–2, 3% Yr3 | Signals loyalty recognition; below-inflation escalation |
| **Services Bundle** | Dedicated CSM + 4-hour support SLA + complimentary migration + feature parity guarantee | Relationship lock-in; eliminates friction; increases switching cost by ~$50K (CSM + migration value) |
| **Incentives** | Early-mover: +10% Year 1 discount (if convert by Day 60); Loyalty: +5–7% additional (tenure-based); Prepay: +10% (3-year upfront) | Stacked incentives max discount = 10% + 7% + 10% = 27% total; customer saves ~$50K over 3 years |

This isn't an upsell—it's recognition."

# PART 3: PRICING MODEL

## Three-Tier Subscription Architecture

### ESSENTIALS ($99–$199/month)
- **Target:** SMB, new customers, freemium graduates
- **Users:** 3–50 (usage-based scaling)
- **Features:** Core modules (5–10), basic API, self-serve integrations
- **Support:** Email (24-hour SLA), knowledge base, community Slack
- **Billing:** Monthly or annual (15% discount)
- **Gross Margin:** 65% (cloud-heavy)

### PROFESSIONAL ($1,999–$3,999/month)
- **Target:** Mid-market (Tier 2 primary)
- **Users:** Unlimited
- **Features:** Full suite (15–20 modules), advanced API, 50+ integrations
- **Support:** 4-hour email/phone SLA, dedicated support channel, quarterly business reviews
- **Deployment:** Cloud or hybrid on-prem
- **Billing:** Monthly or annual (20% discount)
- **Gross Margin:** 68% (hybrid)

### ENTERPRISE (Custom)
- **Target:** Enterprise (Tier 1 primary), large net-new
- **Users:** Unlimited
- **Features:** All features, multi-cloud, custom integrations, dedicated APIs
- **Support:** 24/7 SLA, dedicated success manager, executive reviews
- **Deployment:** Mandatory on-prem with cloud option
- **Billing:** Annual only, volume discounts (3-year typical)
- **Gross Margin:** 72% (on-prem-heavy)

## Compensation Governance

### Mid-Year True-Up Guarantee (Risk Buffer)

**Tier 1: Guaranteed Minimum Comp Floor**
- Reps earning <85% of prior-year comp by Month 6 are eligible for mid-year "true-up" bonus
- Bonus pool: $250K (allocated at Month 6 review; reduced from original $500K due to improved 11% commission rate offsetting gap)
- Allocation: Weighted toward highest-performers; distributed as lump-sum (not ongoing commission)
- Example: Rep earning $240K (vs. $320K baseline) receives $30K true-up bonus to reach $270K (84% of baseline); still below 85% floor but partially buffered

**Tier 2: Comp Guarantee for Top 20% (Retention Anchor)**
- Top 20 perpetual-deal closers (by Year 0 ACV) receive 3-year guarantee:
  - Year 1: 90% of prior-year comp
  - Year 2: 100% of prior-year comp
  - Vesting: Conditioned on staying through Year 2 (forfeiture if depart before Month 24)
- Cost: $1.5M (vs. $3M original plan; reduced due to improved base comp structure)
- **ROI:** Cost of replacing + retraining top rep = $200K–$300K per rep. $1.5M guarantee retains 15 of 20 reps (~75% retention) vs. unmitigated 25–35% exodus (5–8 reps lost at $1.5M–$2.4M replacement cost). Break-even.

### Compensation Communication (Month 0 Roadshow)

**All-Hands Agenda (90 minutes):**
1. **(10 min)** Transition rationale (why subscription is strategic)
2. **(20 min)** Comp model walkthrough (3 representative examples: high/mid/growth performers; Year 1–2 comp builds side-by-side comparison)
3. **(15 min)** Guarantee + guardrails (explain top-20 guarantee, mid-year true-up pool, bonus mechanics, churn clawback rationale)
4. **(15 min)** Real-world deal examples (perpetual vs. subscription deal comp side-by-side for typical $150K, $50K, $10K deals)
5. **(15 min)** Q&A (address fears; clarify misconceptions about renewal commission, expansion bonus, clawback)
6. **(10 min)** Next steps (comp dashboard launch, monthly reporting, escalation path for disputes)

**Key Message:** "Year 1 comp is protected; Year 2–3 growth resumes. Churn clawback is shared accountability—we're betting on your customer success, not just deal volume. This model aligns our incentives with yours: sticky customers, expansion, and recurring revenue."
