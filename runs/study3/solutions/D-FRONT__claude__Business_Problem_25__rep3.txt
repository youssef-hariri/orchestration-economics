1. **Segmented Migration Offer:** Deploy time-boxed, cohort-specific subscription pricing (LMP 20% discount, LM 35%, LE 15%, MM 25%, SMB 35%) with perpetual license trade-in credits and 2-year price-lock guarantees for early adopters. Target 65% migration (1,200+ customers) by Month 36.

Pre-secure $40–60M credit facility; communicate dual metrics to board and investors from Month 0 to prevent stock re-rating shock.

5. **Quarterly Escalation Governance:** Lock metrics with decision thresholds—churn >10% annual = emergency board session; migration rate <400/quarter = strategic pivot; trough depth <–25% OCF = contingency activation. Executive bonuses tied to outcomes (8% churn target yields 100% bonus; >10% yields 50% clawback).

### 1.2 Perpetual License "Bill of Rights" (Legally Enforceable)

**Published Commitment (Signed, Audited, Binding):**

```
PERPETUAL LICENSE CUSTOMER BILL OF RIGHTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Effective Date: [Transition Launch Date]
Commitment Period: 10 Years (through 2034)

We commit to the following for all perpetual licenses:

1. PERPETUAL LICENSE VALIDITY
   • Perpetual licenses remain valid indefinitely
   • No version expiry date (customer can run v2023.2 in 2034)
   • No subscription requirement to use perpetual license
   • No forced automatic upgrades

2. SECURITY PATCHES & CRITICAL BUG FIXES (Backported)
   • Critical security patches: Backported to last 3 versions for 7 years
   • Critical bugs (P1 only): Backported for 5 years
   • SLA: Released within 30 days of discovery

3. TIERED SUPPORT (By Perpetual License Age)
   • Years 1–5: Standard SLA (48h email, 24h phone; business hours)
   • Years 6–10: Reduced SLA (5-day email only)
   • Year 11+: Community support (no SLA; patches via open repository)
   • Minimum: Email support available through 2034

4. NO FEATURE REGRESSIONS
   • Features in v2023.2 will not be removed
   • Cloud-only features will not retroactively lock perpetual users
   • Graceful degradation for read-only access on cancelled subscription

5. DATA EXPORT & NO LOCK-IN
   • Customer data is always exportable (no holdback)
   • No destruction of data on termination
   • 30-day notice to customer before any service cutoff

6. ENFORCEMENT & REMEDY
   If we breach any commitment, customer is entitled to:
   • 25% credit to annual license cost, OR
   • Full refund of prepaid maintenance/support (pro-rated), OR
   • Termination without penalty (perpetual use rights remain)
   • Binding arbitration (customer's legal fees covered if customer wins)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Signed: [CEO], [CFO]
Audit: Reviewed annually by independent auditor; results shared with Customer Advisory Board
```

**Why This Works:**
- **Removes competitive vulnerability:** Competitor's "we support perpetual forever" claim loses credibility when your 10-year commitment is legally binding and audited.
- **Reduces churn anxiety:** Perpetual customers have written safety net; psychological lock-in improves NRR and reduces objection-driven delays.
- **Prevents legal liability:** Explicit terms eliminate ambiguity; customers cannot later claim "forced migration" if Bill of Rights is published.

### 1.3 Trade-In Credit Mechanics (Respects Existing Investment)

**Unamortized License Value Calculation:**

```
Customer purchased perpetual license: $500k in 2022
Assumed useful life: 7 years
Annual amortization: $500k / 7 = $71.4k

As of Sept 2024 (Year 3 of license):
  Book amortized value: $500k – (3 × $71.4k) = $286k

TRADE-IN OFFER:
─────────────────
Subscription tier (Enterprise): $200k annual list price
Unamortized credit: $286k
Years this covers: $286k / $200k = 1.43 years

CUSTOMER OFFER:
"Convert to 2-year Enterprise subscription. We'll credit your $286k unamortized value.
Net Year 1 cost to you: $200k – $143k = $57k
Net Year 2 cost: $200k – $143k = $57k
After 2 years, renew at standard pricing ($200k/year with +3% annual increases)."

COMPANY P&L (GAAP):
Year 1 Billings: $57k cash received
Year 1 Deferred Revenue: $143k (credit not yet recognized; reverses Year 2)
Year 1 GAAP Revenue: $100k (ratable recognition; rest deferred)

Year 2 Billings: $57k cash received
Year 2 GAAP Revenue: $100k (remaining deferred from Year 1) + $57k (Year 2 recognition) = $157k
Year 2 Deferred Revenue reversal: $143k (credit applied)

Result: Billings-to-revenue mismatch in Year 1 (negative working capital impact), 
        but improved retention (churn down to <8% vs. 30% perpetual maintenance baseline).
```

| **LMP** (10+ yr tenure) | 8 years | $350k | $75k (high amortization) | $75k (offsets Y1 cost) |
| **LM** (lapsed 2–5 yr ago) | 5 years (older stock) | $200k | $85k | $5k (customer sees gesture; actual credit modest) |
| **LE** (complex deployments) | 7 years avg. | $2M+ | $500k–$1M | $120k avg. (significant; justifies enterprise tier upgrade) |

## SECTION 3: PRICING & THE TROUGH-VS-ADOPTION TRADEOFF

### 3.1 Scenario Comparison: Three Pricing Strategies

**Question:** Should we offer 20% discount (slower adoption, shallower trough) or 35% discount (faster adoption, deeper trough)?

| Scenario | Avg. Discount | Y1 Migration Bookings | Y1 Blended ASP | Y2 ARR Subscription | Y2 GAAP Revenue (Subscription) | Y2 Total Revenue | Trough Depth | Recovery Speed |
|----------|---------------|-----------------------|------------------|-------------------|--------|----------|------------|--------|
| **Conservative (20%)** | 20% | $28.5M | $52k | $52M | $35M | $226.5M | –5.2% YoY | Fast (Q2'25) |
| **Base (30%)** | 30% | $38.5M | $48k | $62M | $46M | $228.6M | –6.2% YoY | Q4'25 |
| **Aggressive (40%)** | 40% | $42M | $43.5k | $68M | $52M | $230.2M | –7.1% YoY | Q2'25 |

**Recommendation: Base (30% Segmented Approach)**

**Why Base Wins:**
1. **Migration Velocity:** 635 deals Y1 is achievable with rigorous execution; faster than Conservative (500 deals).
2. **Customer Psychographics:** 30% discount feels like "fair exchange for subscription commitment," not "desperate fire sale."
3. **Perpetual Customer Perception:** 20% discount to LMP feels like "modest upcharge for cloud value"; 40% feels like "being penalized for staying loyal."
4. **Cash Flow Impact:** –18.8% trough is within acceptable range (–15% to –25%); deeper trough risks covenant violation or forced credit facility draw.
5. **Execution Risk:** If sales misses and actual migration is 50% of target, Conservative scenario absorbs variance better; Base scenario falters but Aggressive scenario crashes.

**Segmentation Within Base Case (NOT uniform 30%):**
- LMP (loyal): 20% discount (price-lock + trade-in = sufficient incentive)
- LM (lapsed): 35% discount (competitor risk justifies depth)
- LE (large): 15% discount (volume-inelastic; executive sponsor is primary incentive)
- MM (mid): 25% discount (balanced)
- SMB (small): 35% discount (elastic; deep discount drives 40% adoption target)

This segmented approach achieves 30% blended discount without feeling unfair to any cohort.
