# Executive Summary: Project Velocity
## Strategy for Operational Excellence and Lead-Time Optimization

### The Mandate
To maintain our competitive edge, we are launching **Project Velocity**, a comprehensive operational transformation aimed at reducing our cycle time from **14 weeks to under 6 weeks**. 

Historically, our failure to align Sales, Production, and Quality has led to a "Queue-Heavy" culture, resulting in systemic delays and warranty-inducing "fire-drills." Project Velocity shifts our operational paradigm from reactive batching to **Proactive Continuous Flow**. By prioritizing stability over volume, we will regain market share while simultaneously protecting our quality standards.

---

### The Strategic Pillars

#### 1. The "Constraint Manager" (Neutrality)
We are appointing a **Constraint Manager (CM)**—an independent authority reporting directly to the COO. The CM holds the sole authority over production scheduling and "Lock-in" windows. This eliminates departmental politics, prevents unauthorized "VIP" overrides, and ensures the production floor is shielded from arbitrary disruption.

#### 2. Economic & Behavioral Alignment
We are moving away from siloed KPIs toward **Integrated Incentive Structures**:
*   **Tiered Sales Commissions:** Sales performance is now aligned with schedule stability. Orders fitting the 6-week window are fully incentivized, while custom/expedited orders carry a surcharge that directly fuels a "Velocity Bonus Pool" for the shop floor.
*   **Gainshare Incentives:** Production teams are no longer penalized for "stopping the line." Instead, they are rewarded for "Zero-Defect" cycles through a profit-sharing model funded by reduced warranty claims.

#### 3. Operational Infrastructure
*   **Assemble-to-Order (ATO) Buffer:** We will strategically stock the high-value, long-lead items that constitute 80% of our equipment value, based on predictive forecasting. This removes supply chain volatility from the order cycle.
*   **Process Partnering (Quality 2.0):** Quality control is moving from a back-end "policing" function to an in-process "coaching" function. By embedding inspectors into production cells, we identify failures at the source, preventing defects before they require costly rework.

---

### Implementation Roadmap: The 14-to-6 Sprint

| Phase | Duration | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Foundation** | Weeks 1–2 | Finalize "Constraint Manager" charter and initiate "Shadow P&L" reporting. |
| **Pilot (Cell A)** | Weeks 3–6 | Execute ATO buffering and implement the Gainshare incentive model. |
| **Feedback/Fix** | Weeks 7–8 | Evaluate pilot data against the "Cost of Chaos" and refine buffer inventory. |
| **Full Scale** | Weeks 9–14 | Roll out standard flow to all lines; implement tiered commission structure. |

---

### Executive Commitment
Success in this transformation hinges on **stability**. The C-suite must commit to a "No-Fire-Drill" policy. By granting the Constraint Manager the authority to enforce schedule adherence, we protect the production line from the chaos that previously compromised our quality. 

**Stability is the prerequisite for speed.** By removing the internal friction between Sales, Quality, and Production, we create a scalable, predictable, and highly profitable manufacturing engine.

---

### Path Forward
This plan is designed to be self-sustaining. The **"Shadow P&L"** will serve as the financial proof-of-concept for the Board and CFO, tracking the direct correlation between increased operational speed and the reduction of warranty-related expenses. 

**Next Step:** Formalize the appointment of the Constraint Manager and authorize the Phase 1 "Shadow P&L" data gathering.
