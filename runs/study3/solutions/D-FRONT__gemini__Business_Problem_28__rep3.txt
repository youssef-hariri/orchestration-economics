### **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Strategic Turnaround Office  
**SUBJECT:** Final Directive: Industrial Services Unit Turnaround-or-Exit Plan  

---

#### **1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: THE "BOUTIQUE PIVOT"**
The Industrial Services Unit is currently locked in a "restructuring trap," where repeated cost-cutting has eroded the quality necessary to sustain high-value contracts, accelerating a cycle of talent loss and order-book decline. 

The strategy shifts the mandate from **volume-based cost containment** to **value-based contraction.** We will transition the unit into a high-margin "Boutique" entity within 12 months. Should the unit fail to demonstrate standalone profitability or retain its critical technical core, the Board will execute a pre-packaged exit protocol. 

---

#### **2. THE 12-MONTH TURNAROUND ROADMAP**
| Milestone | Timeline | Strategic Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I. Contractual Scrub** | Month 2 | Eliminate all negative-contribution-margin contracts; isolate true revenue drivers. |
| **II. Talent Retention** | Month 4 | Deploy "Success Incentives" for the top 10% of performers, self-funded by Phase I savings. |
| **III. Pricing Reset** | Month 6 | Migrate "Anchor" customers to market-rate SLAs or formal Group-subsidy accounting. |
| **IV. The "Boutique" Test** | Month 9 | **Hard Gateway:** Achieve 3 consecutive months of positive standalone EBITDA. |
| **V. Final Resolution** | Month 12 | Board vote: Full-scale boutique integration or immediate divestiture. |

---

#### **3. EXIT PROTOCOLS & DECISION GATEWAYS**
If the unit fails to hit the **Month 9 EBITDA milestone** or fails any of the following "Three-Gate Protocol" criteria by Month 10, the Board will trigger an immediate, orderly exit:

*   **Gate 1 (Profitability):** Does the unit maintain a **>8% standalone EBITDA margin**? If no: Execute **Strategic Trade Sale**.
*   **Gate 2 (Synergy):** Is the unit critical to preserving **>90%** of high-value Group-wide client relationships? If no: Execute **Controlled Wind-Down**.
*   **Gate 3 (Talent):** Has turnover of critical technical staff dropped to **<20%**? If no: Execute **Liquidation**.

---

#### **4. OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVES**
To ensure the efficacy of this turnaround, the Board mandates three critical operational changes:

1.  **The "Subsidy Mask" Removal:** All services provided to other Group divisions must be billed at **Fair Market Value**. Any variance between the unit’s cost and the market rate must be explicitly charged as a "Strategic Service Fee" to the benefitting division’s P&L. 
2.  **Leadership Accountability:** The current unit leadership is on notice. Given the history of failed restructurings, the Board will appoint an independent Turnaround Lead with full authority to bypass existing management during the Phase 1–2 transition.
3.  **The "No-Extension" Policy:** There will be no adjustments to the 12-month window. If milestones are missed at Month 9, the transition to the chosen exit strategy (Sale, Liquidation, or Utility Conversion) begins immediately at the Month 10 Board meeting.

---

#### **5. STRATEGIC POSITIONING**
The Group’s tolerance for the status quo has reached its limit. This plan moves the unit away from the "legacy burden" model. By the end of this cycle, the unit will either be a high-margin, value-additive asset or an exited liability. There is no longer a path for "restructuring" that does not include radical contraction and verifiable profitability.

**Recommendation:** The Board should formally adopt the "Boutique or Exit" framework effective immediately.
