**Project:** Sukoyaka Plus – Japan Market Entry & Brand Launch  
**Document Type:** Final Campaign Strategy & Execution Plan  
**Prepared For:** The Executive Board  

---

### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
This document outlines the comprehensive, culturally tailored, and strictly compliant marketing campaign for the launch of **"Sukoyaka Plus" (すこやかプラス)** in the Japanese market. Recognizing Japan’s unique social values regarding aging, health, and familial duty, this campaign pivots away from traditional, fear-based health marketing. Instead, it champions "positive aging" and shared vitality. By aligning deep cultural insights with a rigorous adherence to Japan’s complex regulatory framework, this strategy mitigates legal risk while maximizing brand trust, market penetration, and long-term customer lifetime value.

---

### **1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW & POSITIONING**
**Product:** Sukoyaka Plus (すこやかプラス)  
**Classification:** Registered under Japan’s **"Food with Function Claims" (機能性表示食品)** system.  
**Formulation:** A daily dietary supplement formulated with high-quality, scientifically validated functional ingredients (including CoQ10 and targeted vitamins) designed to support cellular vitality, manage daily stress, and promote healthy aging.  

**Strategic Positioning:**  
Japanese consumers are highly skeptical of "miracle cures" and aggressive Western-style health marketing. Sukoyaka Plus is positioned not as a tool for selfish self-optimization, but as a foundational element for maintaining one's health to actively contribute to and enjoy life with family and community.

---

### **2. TARGET AUDIENCE & CULTURAL STRATEGY**
The Japanese market requires a nuanced understanding of modern aging. Our strategy targets two distinct but interconnected demographics, moving away from "fear of aging" toward "positive aging."

#### **Primary Audience: "The Active Shin-Silver" (Ages 55–70)**
*   **Profile:** Active, digitally literate individuals who view their post-retirement years as a time for travel, hobbies, and personal growth.
*   **Cultural Driver:** *Jiritsu* (Independence) and *Iki-iki* (Living with purpose). While the desire to not burden others (*meiwaku o kakenai*) remains, we frame this positively: maintaining health to actively enjoy life with family.
*   **Ethical Guardrail:** Strict avoidance of "anxiety-inducing" marketing (e.g., fear-mongering about physical or cognitive decline). The focus is entirely on healthspan and empowerment.

#### **Secondary Audience: "The Shared-Experience Generation" (Ages 35–50)**
*   **Profile:** Working professionals and adult children purchasing health products for their aging parents.
*   **Cultural Driver:** *Kizuna* (Bonds) and shared experiences. 
*   **Ethical Guardrail:** Directly gifting a health supplement can inadvertently imply the recipient is physically declining, causing a loss of face (*mentsu*). We reframe the gifting angle from "compensating for aging" to **"investing in our shared future experiences"** (e.g., traveling and enjoying hobbies together).

### **4. REGULATORY & ETHICAL COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK**
Japan’s regulatory environment for health products is governed by a strict "Triad" of laws. Our campaign is engineered to comply with all three simultaneously, ensuring zero regulatory friction.

1.  **Pharmaceutical and Medical Devices Act (Yakji Act / 薬機法):**
    *   Strict prohibition on claims of curing, treating, or preventing diseases. 
    *   No imagery implying medical efficacy (e.g., no doctors in white coats, no anatomical diagrams showing disease reversal).
2.  **Food Labeling Act (食品表示法) & "Food with Function Claims" (FFC):**
    *   **Verbatim Mandate:** Functional claims must use the *exact, verbatim wording* approved by the Consumer Affairs Agency (CAA). Surrounding copy will be carefully audited to ensure it does not accidentally construct an unapproved, generalized functional claim.
    *   **Ingredient Localization:** Western pharmacological concepts such as "adaptogens" are not legally recognized under the FFC system. All consumer-facing claims will strictly rely on specific, CAA-approved ingredients (e.g., CoQ10, specific vitamins).
    *   **Mandatory Disclosures:** All materials will prominently display the legally required CAA disclaimer: *本品は、特定保健用食品と異なり、消費者庁長官による個別審査を受けたものではありません。*
3.  **Premiums and Representations Act (Keihyo Act / 景品表示法):**
    *   Prohibits exaggerated claims. No superlatives (e.g., "The best in Japan") unless backed by verifiable third-party data. No fabricated "Before & After" visuals.

### **5. OMNICHANNEL MARKETING STRATEGY**
The media mix blends high-trust traditional channels with highly targeted digital platforms favored by older demographics.

*   **Digital & CRM:**
    *   **LINE Official Account:** Utilized for gentle, value-add content (e.g., morning stretches, hydration tips) rather than hard selling, fostering long-term engagement.
    *   **Yahoo! Japan & Rakuten:** High-conversion platforms specifically targeted at the 55+ demographic.
*   **Traditional & Print:**
    *   **Targeted Magazines:** Print advertisements in respected publications such as *Kōki* (active older women), *Tarzan* (health-conscious men), and *Otona no Shūkan* (catering specifically to the Shin-Silver demographic).
*   **PR & Authority Building:**
    *   **Scientific Authority:** Partnerships with Licensed Pharmacists (*Yakuzai-shi*) to explain ingredient science.
    *   **Silver Micro-Influencers:** Collaboration with active seniors (10k–50k followers) on Instagram and YouTube who authentically showcase their hobbies while naturally incorporating the product into their routines.

---

### **6. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs) & MEASUREMENT**
To ensure the campaign is culturally resonant, ethically sound, and commercially viable, we will track the following metrics:

1.  **Subscription Retention Rate (*Teiki Bin*):** The ultimate metric for supplement success in Japan. **Target:** >65% retention at Month 3.
2.  **Brand Trust & Ethical Perception Index:** Measured via quarterly consumer panels to track if consumers perceive the brand as "respectful of aging" and "transparent," ensuring our ethical guardrails are effective.
3.  **Regulatory Compliance Rate:** **Target:** 100% pass rate on internal legal audits. Zero warnings or corrections from the Consumer Affairs Agency or local health bureaus.
4.  **"Shared Experience" Gifting Ratio:** Tracking the percentage of sales utilizing the "Gift Wrapping" option, alongside qualitative review analysis, to ensure the gifting message is received as "empowering" rather than "patronizing."
