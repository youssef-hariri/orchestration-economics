**PERPETUAL-TO-SUBSCRIPTION TRANSITION PLAN**
**Board Resolution Document | 36-Month Capital-Protected Migration**

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**The Decision.** Convert lumpy, discounted perpetual revenue into a recurring-revenue model that commands a 3–4x valuation premium. The board must approve a **36-month graduated migration**, accept a **–25% to –30% GAAP revenue trough in Year 1**, and authorize a **$40–60 million pre-announcement equity cushion** to fund the transition without liquidity panic.

**The Trade.** We will exchange $1.00 of non-recurring revenue for approximately $0.70 of subscription revenue in Year 1. By Year 3, compounding ARR and net dollar retention convert this compression into permanently higher enterprise value.

**The Safeguards.** The plan rejects the value-destroying “license buyback” approach that cratered competitors. Instead, we deploy a **cash-positive Migration Loyalty Discount (MLD)**, irrevocable grandfathering for immovable accounts, a **competitive defense fund**, sales compensation tied to cash collection (not phantom bookings), and **pre-committed board abort triggers** to prevent reactive zigzagging during the trough.

---

## PILLAR I: CUSTOMER ARCHITECTURE & MIGRATION ECONOMICS

**Principle:** Migrate the movable. Retain the immovable. Never create a discounted hybrid underclass.

### 1. Customer Segmentation
| Segment | Profile | Migration Path |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| **Elephants** | >$500k ACV, heavy customization, regulatory on-prem requirements | **Perpetual Plus.** Irrevocable license + inflation-indexed maintenance (CPI+2%). May purchase new **Innovation Add-Ons** at full subscription price. No forced migration. |
| **Core** | $50k–$500k ACV, standard modules | **MLD Track.** One-time Year 1 loyalty discount. Minimum 12-month cash commitment. |
| **Velocity** | <$50k ACV, price-sensitive | **MLD Track.** Digital-led migration; self-service with 20% Year 1 discount. |

### 2. Migration Loyalty Discount (MLD)
Replace the value-destroying License Surrender Credit Program. **No customer receives their original purchase price back as a subscription credit.**

| M&S Status | Year 1 Discount | List-Price Lock | Minimum Cash at Signing |
|------------|----------------|-----------------|-------------------------|
| Active (0–24 months) | 30% | 36 months | 70% of Year 1 ACV |
| Lapsed (25–60 months) | 20% | 24 months | 80% of Year 1 ACV |
| Deep Legacy (61+ months) | 10% + waived reactivation fee | 12 months | 90% of Year 1 ACV |

**Cash-Floor Rule:** If the discount reduces Invoice 1 below the minimum threshold, the customer must purchase a multi-year contract or forfeit a portion of the discount. **No deal may generate $0 cash.**

### 3. Innovation Add-Ons (No Discounted Hybrid Tier)
Perpetual holders who want cloud-native capabilities (AI/ML, mobile, advanced APIs) must purchase **full-price Innovation Add-Ons** billed independently. This eliminates the bifurcated “Dual-Run” customer base that cannibalizes core ARR.

### 4. Grandfathering & The Maintenance Lever
- **Legal Rights:** Perpetual licenses remain irrevocable for the installed version. A formal EULA amendment clarifies that grandfathering confers usage rights only, not future features or indefinite innovation.
- **R&D Transparency:** Publicly commit to a **70/30 engineering split** (70% cloud/SaaS; 30% perpetual security patches and critical bugs only).
- **Maintenance Economics:** Legacy M&S renewals frozen in Year 1, rising to +10% in Year 2 and +25% in Year 3 (premium cap). Elephant maintenance is capped at CPI+2% after Year 3 to prevent punitive shocks.
- **Subscription Price Freeze:** Migrating customers lock list prices for 12–36 months, widening the wedge against rising maintenance premiums.

## PILLAR II: PRICING & PACKAGING

**Principle:** Cash positivity first. Prevent down-market leakage.

### 1. Subscription Price Anchor
- **New Logo:** Annual subscription set at **18–22% of current perpetual license list price** (4.5–5.5 year payback, market-standard).
- **Migration:** Same list price as new logos, less the one-time MLD credit applied to Invoice 1 only.

### 2. Modular Packaging
Customers must map to a tier containing *at least* the functionality previously owned, preventing perceived downgrades.

| Tier | Contents | Target Segment |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Base** | Core platform + previously owned modules | Velocity |
| **Professional** | Base + collaboration/analytics | Core default |
| **Enterprise** | Professional + SLA, admin controls, dedicated CSM | Elephants converting fully |

### 3. Trough-Shield: Upfront Commitments
Multi-year upfront deals are restricted to **new logos** and **Elephants converting to full subscription**, where MLD credits cannot wipe out cash.

| Term | Billing | Discount | GAAP Recognition |
|------|---------|----------|------------------|
| 1-Year | Annual in advance | 0% | Ratable monthly |
| 2-Year | Annual in advance | 10% | Ratable monthly |
| 3-Year | Annual in advance | 15% | Ratable monthly |
| 3-Year | Full upfront | 25% | Ratable monthly |

A 3-year upfront deal deposits **2.25x annual cash on Day 1**, funding operations through the GAAP trough.

## PILLAR III: SALES FORCE, TALENT & CHANNEL REDESIGN

**Principle:** Retain the teachable. Hire the native. Pay only on cash collected.

### 1. Talent Segmentation
- **Audit & Grade:** All reps assessed for land-and-expand aptitude. A-players retrained; B-players assigned migration-only quotas; C-players exited with severance.
- **Parallel Hiring:** Recruit SaaS-native hunters to operate independently from migration reps for the first 18 months. Target: **30% subscription-native headcount by Month 12.**
- **Manager Alignment:** District managers compensated on **team ARR growth (40%), cash conversion (35%), and NRR (25%).**

### 2. Transition Economics
- **Recoverable Draw:** **60% of prior W2** for 9 months, ratcheting down quarterly (60% → 40% → 20%). Draw is recovered from future commissions, eliminating the $8M+ fixed liability of a non-recoverable plan.
- **Quota Relief:** First two quarters at **60% of steady-state quota**.

### 3. Commission Mechanics — Cash Received Basis
All commissions calculated on **cash received**, not ACV booked.

| Deal Type | Commission | Paid On |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| New Logo Subscription | 12% of cash received | Quarterly, as cash hits |
| Migration Subscription | 10% of cash received | Quarterly, as cash hits |
| Expansion/Upsell (Year 2+) | 5% of incremental cash | Quarterly |
| Renewal | 2% of renewal cash | Upon PO receipt |

### 4. Accelerators & Risk Controls
- **Upfront Cash Bonus:** Additional **8% of cash collected** on 2+ year upfront deals (new logos).
- **Migration SPIFF:** **$5,000** per Tier-1 Core account migrated, **paid only after first renewal is confirmed** (Month 12+).
- **Holdback Escrow:** 20% of all migration commissions escrowed until the customer’s first renewal invoice is paid.
- **Clawback:** 12-month clawback if customer churns or defaults.

### 5. Partner / VAR Bridge
Close the channel-cash disruption with a **6-month Marketing Development Fund (MDF)** equal to 2% of partners’ trailing 12-month perpetual revenue, plus subscription commissions paid on cash receipt (12% new logo / 8% migration).

## PILLAR IV: COMPETITIVE DEFENSE & OPERATIONAL READINESS

**Principle:** A trough is not navigated in a vacuum.

### 1. The Fortress Fund
Allocate **$5–8 million annually** for competitive defense:
- Executive sponsorship and accelerated roadmaps for at-risk Elephants.
- “Rescue offers” for accounts targeted by competitors.
- Dedicated competitive intelligence team.

### 2. Retention War Room
Weekly cross-functional review (Sales, CSM, Finance, Product) of:
- All maintenance non-renewals >$25k.
- Competitive threats on Elephant accounts.
- Churn-segmentation by propensity score.

### 3. Systems & Customer Success
- **Billing Overhaul:** Budget **$3–5 million and 6 months pre-launch** to implement a subscription management and revenue recognition platform capable of handling MLD credits, hybrid coexistence, and cash-based commission accounting.
- **Customer Success Build-Out:** Hire CSMs at a ratio of **1 per $2 million in ARR**. CSMs own onboarding, NRR, and first-renewal risk scoring directly linked to sales holdbacks.
- **Legal & International:** Jurisdiction-specific review of “irrevocable” language and maintenance term alterations in the EU and other regulated markets.

## PILLAR V: FINANCIAL ARCHITECTURE, GOVERNANCE & COMMUNICATION

**Principle:** Pre-fund the trough. Change the scoreboard before the game begins.

### 1. Capitalization
- **Pre-Announcement Equity Cushion:** Raise **$40–60 million** (equity or convertible note) explicitly earmarked for trough bridging before public announcement.
- **Covenant Renegotiation:** Replace Net Debt / EBITDA with **Net Debt / ARR (<3.5x threshold)** for 36 months. Waive quarterly EBITDA covenants during the transition window.
- **Secondary Liquidity:** Secure a $25 million revolving credit facility as a backstop.

### 2. Trough Scenarios & Cash Targets

| Scenario | Year 1 GAAP | Year 2 GAAP | Recovery | Installed-Base Churn | Cash Trough |
|----------|-------------|-------------|----------|----------------------|-------------|
| **Base Case** | –25% | –12% | Q4 Year 2 | 6% | Negative through M12; break-even M13 |
| **Optimistic** | –18% | –5% | Q2 Year 2 | 4% | Negative through M9 |
| **Pessimistic** | –32% | –18% | Q2 Year 3 | 10% | Negative through M16 |

**Non-Negotiable Target:** **Cash Conversion Ratio (Cash Receipts / ARR Booked) >75%** in Year 1.

### 3. Board Governance: New Scoreboard
Replace the board dashboard immediately. GAAP revenue is reported but de-emphasized.

| Old Metric | New Primary Metric | Transition Target |
|------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Quarterly Revenue | **ARR / Net New ARR** | 15–20% ARR growth |
| Bookings | **Net Dollar Retention (NDR)** | >110% by Month 18 |
| EBITDA Margin | **Rule of 40** | Framed as Year 4 target, not transition promise |
| Cash Flow from Ops | **Cash Conversion Ratio** | >75% |

**Executive Compensation:** Re-weight CEO, CRO, and CFO bonuses to **60% ARR growth / 40% NRR**. **Zero weight on quarterly GAAP revenue.**

### 4. Investor Narrative
*“We are voluntarily exchanging $1.00 of lumpy, non-recurring revenue for $0.70 of recurring revenue in Year 1. Subscription revenue commands a 3–4x valuation premium and compounds via NDR. GAAP revenue in Years 1–2 is a lagging artifact of ratably recognized cash we have already collected. Value the company on ARR and cash conversion.”*

### 5. Communication Cadence
- **Pre-Announcement (T-2 quarters):** Publish pro forma ARR to train investors.
- **Launch Quarter:** Host Investor Transition Day; secure public statements of support from top 3 shareholders.
- **Quarterly Earnings:** Lead with **Net New ARR** and RPO. Frame GAAP as trailing indicator.
- **Employee Retention:** Pre-authorize trough retention grants for top 20% of non-sales talent (50% cash / 50% equity) and a company-wide subscription-completion bonus pool released upon achieving 80% subscription mix.

## PHASED 36-MONTH TIMELINE

| Phase | Timing | Strategic Actions | Financial & Operational Milestones |
|-------|--------|-------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Foundation** | Months 1–6 | Systems overhaul; talent audit; raise $40–60M equity cushion; hire CSMs; launch new-logo SaaS only; pilot MLD with 10 friendly accounts. | Establish ARR baseline. No GAAP trough yet. |
| **Migration Window** | Months 7–18 | Open MLD to Core/Velocity. Perpetual sales require CFO approval. Legacy M&S +10%. Activate Fortress Fund and retention war room. | Target **30% subscription mix**. Accept GAAP trough. Cash conversion >75%. |
| **Rebalancing** | Months 19–30 | MLD steps down (30% → 20% → 10%). Legacy M&S +25%. All new modules subscription-only. Discontinue any remaining hybrid paths. | Target **60% subscription mix**. New-logo ARR must offset >50% of maintenance runoff. |
| **Sunset & Lock-In** | Months 31–36 | Discontinue all new perpetual sales. MLD expires. Lapsed M&S customers may reactivate only into subscription. | Target **80%+ subscription mix**. Trough ends. Pure ARR plan takes full effect. |

## ABORT TRIGGERS & RISK MITIGATION

The board pre-commits to four survival triggers. If triggered, management executes without requiring new debate.

| Trigger | Threshold | Automatic Response |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Churn Spike** | Installed-base churn >10% at Month 12 | Pause migration marketing; deploy Fortress Fund to at-risk tier; halve MLD discounts. |
| **Cash Hemorrhage** | Cash Conversion Ratio <50% at Month 9 | Suspend all SPIFFs; shift all rep comp to 100% cash-received basis; freeze non-essential hiring. |
| **New Logo Failure** | New-logo ARR <50% of maintenance runoff by Month 18 | Temporarily re-open CFO-approved perpetual sales for one quarter to stabilize cash. |
| **Liquidity Crisis** | Unrestricted cash <6 months runway | Activate 15% R&D/peripheral spend cut and emergency Board/CEO contingency protocol. |

## BOARD RESOLUTIONS REQUIRED

**Approve the following to authorize the transition:**

1. **Adopt the 36-month phased timeline** and accept the modeled GAAP revenue trough (–25% to –30% Year 1; –10% to –15% Year 2).
2. **Authorize the $40–60 million pre-announcement equity cushion** (or convertible note) and covenant renegotiation to Net Debt / ARR <3.5x.
3. **Ratify the Migration Loyalty Discount (MLD)** as the sole migration incentive, explicitly rejecting license surrender credits.
4. **Mandate the board metrics transition** to ARR/NDR/Cash Conversion and re-weight executive compensation (60% ARR growth / 40% NRR; zero GAAP weight).
5. **Pre-commit the four abort triggers** as binding governance guardrails for the transition period.
6. **Approve the $5–8 million annual Fortress Fund** and $3–5 million systems overhaul budget.

**End State.** By Month 36, the company exits the trough with **80%+ subscription mix**, **>110% NDR**, positive operating cash flow, and a sales force fully aligned to ARR and cash conversion. The GAAP revenue decline is the deliberate price of repricing our revenue from a 1x to a 3–4x multiple.
