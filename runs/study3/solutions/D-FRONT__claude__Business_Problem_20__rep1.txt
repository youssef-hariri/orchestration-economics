## 4. Non-Negotiable Quality Guarantees

### Mandatory Preservation (Never-Compress List)

| Commitment | Definition | Measurement | Halt Trigger | Owner |
|------------|-----------|-------------|--------------|-------|
| **Burn-in Dwell (Design-Spec Locked)** | Burn-in duration per design spec (4–7 day thermal cycle or runtime) is NEVER shortened, deferred, or waived without formal ECN signed by Quality + Engineering. **BLOCKING:** Design spec must document burn-in requirement + failure modes caught + field correlation from prior failure RCA. No exceptions. | Weekly audit: sample 5–10 orders; verify burn-in dwell matches spec (no shortcuts). Design spec version history in PLM (audit trail). | If burn-in dwell is reduced/skipped even once without ECN, IMMEDIATE halt. Investigation + CAPA before resuming. No exceptions. | Quality Manager (with Engineering escalation) |
| **Incoming FAI Tier System (Strict Criteria)** | Only **Tier 1 (expedited FAI, 1 day)** suppliers have documented proof: ≥95% pass rate (12-month rolling), audit score >85%, zero quality escapes (24 months). Tier 2 (new/risky) always get full 3–5 day FAI. Revert immediately if criteria breached. | Monthly supplier scorecards (defect %, OTD, audits). Quarterly tier review. Dashboard: "Supplier Tier Status" (real-time). Defect traceability: tag Tier-1 parts; track field returns. | If Tier-1 escape rate ≥2% (baseline), immediate revert to Tier 2. If >2 suppliers revert in 6 months, halt Tier 1 globally; revert all to full FAI. | Procurement Manager (Quality veto authority) |
| **Hold-Waiver Discipline (Dual Approval)** | No inspection hold (Stages 9, 12, 14, 16) deferred/waived without **Quality Manager + Production Manager dual signature** (timestamp logged). Every waiver documented with reason code. Post-shipment NCR traced to waived hold triggers CAPA within 3 days. | Daily hold-waiver log (manual or system). Weekly QA audit. Monthly trending (alert if >5% of orders have waivers). | If >2 "schedule pressure" waivers/month, CONWIP cap auto-reduces by 2 units; production halts until root cause resolved. | Quality Manager (daily); Quality Director (escalation) |
| **First-Pass Yield Tripwire (Auto-Halt Rule)** | **FPY ≥94% baseline target (Standard: 95%, Configured: 92%, Engineered: 88%).** If FPY <93%, escalation within 24 hrs. If FPY <91%, production HALTS pending root-cause investigation + CAPA. | **Weekly FPY dashboard by segment** (color-coded: Green ≥target, Yellow –1–3%, Red <–3%). SPC control chart (±2σ alert). Weekly reporting to VP Operations. | RED ALERT: FPY <91% → production halt. Root-cause investigation (supplier defect, design change, process drift, inspection procedure change?). CAPA signed by Quality + Engineering before resumption. | Quality Manager (daily owner); Production Manager (joint halt authority) |
| **Rework Queue Capacity (Dedicated, Scheduled)** | Rework is **dedicated resource** (1–2 technicians, separate FIFO queue, not mixed with new production). Rework lead-time pre-calculated by defect type (electrical ~3 days, firmware ~1–2 days, mechanical ~1 day). If queue exceeds 5 units or 5 days aging, escalation triggered. If 8+ units or 7-day aging, joint halt order issued. | Daily rework queue dashboard: count, age, defect type. Weekly rework rate % (target <3% of orders). Trending SPC. Age of oldest rework unit monitored. | If rework queue ≥8 units AND oldest ≥7 days, Production + Quality jointly authorize production HALT + rework sprint (all hands). No new orders released until queue ≤4 units AND all aging units cleared. | Production Manager (daily owner); Quality Manager (escalation) |
| **Design-Change (ECN) Fast-Track (<48 hrs)** | Any design change post-RFQ requires ECN approval in **<24 hrs preliminary, <48 hrs final**. Evaluates supplier lead-time, cost, customer acceptance, quality/cert risk. Cannot be waived even if "minor" (all logged). | ECN request form (PLM): submission timestamp → evaluation → approval signatures (Engineering + Procurement + Quality if cert-relevant). Dashboard: "ECN backlog" (should be 0; alert if >1 pending >48 hrs). Traceability: order ↔ ECN history audit trail. | If ECN processed without Quality sign-off (cert-relevant) OR takes >72 hrs, halt order pending investigation. | Engineering Manager (owner); Quality Manager (veto for cert changes) |
| **Documentation & Traceability 100% Complete** | Every order has complete audit trail in PLM: customer spec → design release → BOM rev → FAI/CoC → test results (hi-pot, functional, burn-in, FAT) → rework/NCR (if any) → customer FAT sign-off. Retrievable in <5 minutes. | Monthly audit: sample 5–10 orders; verify dossier completeness. % tracking (target ≥98%). Missing doc = NCR issued to responsible function. | If completeness <95%, halt shipments pending recovery. | Quality Manager (documentation owner); Quality Director (escalation) |

## 6. 90-Day Launch Plan (Weeks 1–12)

### Week 1: Diagnostic Completion & Governance Launch
- **Quality & Engineering:** Design spec audit—document burn-in requirement in writing (PLM). Sign-off on mandatory gates.
- **Procurement:** Supplier tier audit—classify current suppliers (Tier 1: ≥95% pass rate + recent audit >85%; Tier 2: all others). Baseline expediting cost ($80–120K/year).
- **Sales & Operations:** Forecast accuracy baseline—measure ±% variance (rolling 12 months). Identify top 3 Standard customers for pilot.
- **Quality Director:** Publish "QUALITY GATES NEVER-COMPRESS LIST" (signed by Quality, VP Ops, CFO). Post on production floor.
- **Deliverable:** Design spec doc (burn-in validated), Supplier tier matrix, Forecast baseline report, Never-compress list (posted).

### Weeks 2–4: Planning & Pilot Selection
- **IT + Product:** Configurator scoping—define BOM auto-generation (5–10 option sets for Standard configurations). Document API requirements to PLM/ERP.
- **Production + Industrial Engineer:** SMED team assembly—audit changeover bottleneck (Power, Control, Burn-in cells). Draft quick-change fixture design.
- **Procurement:** Fast-track supplier RFQ—issue RFQ to 2 candidate suppliers (Motor: target 8-week lead time, <8% price premium; Transformer: target 7-week, <8% premium). Baseline response expected Week 4.
- **Sales:** Pilot customer briefing—secure verbal commitment from 3 Standard customers to accept 6–7-week quotes (vs. current 14-week buffer).
- **Quality:** Tripwire dashboard design—define WIP monitoring, FPY tracking, hold-waiver audit, supplier tier status (real-time system or manual tracker).
- **Deliverable:** Configurator spec doc, SMED quick-change design (CAD), RFQ sent to 2 suppliers, Pilot customer agreements, Dashboard prototype.

### Weeks 5–8: Build & Pilot Prep
- **IT:** Build configurator (internal pilot only; not live to customers yet). Test BOM auto-generation on 5 Standard configs.
- **Production:** Procure SMED tools (quick-change connectors, wire bundles, fixture rails). Begin Cell A (Power Stage) setup.
- **Procurement:** Supplier quotes expected back (Week 5–6). Evaluate price/lead-time trade-off. Make go/no-go decision (Week 7). If go, negotiate terms (volume commitment, demand signal cadence, payment terms). Target: PO issued Week 8 for supplier to begin fast-track ramp.
- **Quality:** Implement tripwire dashboard (weekly FPY update, WIP count, hold-waiver log). Train QA team on daily monitoring.
- **Planning:** Set up rolling 12-week demand forecast (updated weekly). Sync with Procurement on supplier lead-time triggers (Week 8–10 of supplier lead time = place PO).
- **Deliverable:** Configurator code complete (ready for Week 9 pilot). SMED tooling ordered/partial delivery. Supplier contract signed. Dashboard live (manual or automated).

### Weeks 9–12: Pilot Launch & Go/No-Go (Weeks 9 & 12)

#### **Week 9: PHASE 1 GO/NO-GO GATE**
Mandatory approval before proceeding full rollout. Review:
1. **Design spec signed off?** Burn-in requirement documented (signature from Quality + Engineering).
2. **Supplier tiers assigned?** Tier 1 criteria met for any expedited suppliers (<1 week FAI approval).
3. **Tripwire baselines established?** Current FPY, hold-waiver %, expediting cost measured (12-month rolling baseline).
4. **Never-compress list posted?** Quality gates clearly marked; disciplinary consequences stated (management briefed).
5. **RACI clarity?** Hold-waiver dual-approval rule tested (one waiver case walked through; both signatures obtained).

**GO CRITERIA:**
- ✓ Design spec is locked & signed
- ✓ Supplier tiers are assigned (clear criteria documented)
- ✓ Dashboard is live (FPY tracking, WIP monitoring)
- ✓ Never-compress list is posted & acknowledged (all managers signed)
- ✓ Sales has 3 pilot customers committed to 6–7 week quotes

**NO-GO TRIGGERS (HALT & ITERATE):**
- ❌ Design spec is vague (burn-in requirement not quantified)
- ❌ Supplier tiers conflict with current practice (e.g., expedited supplier has 3% defect rate, doesn't meet 95% threshold)
- ❌ Forecast accuracy is ±25%+ (safety stock formula will be inaccurate; revisit demand sensing maturity)
- ❌ Tripwire dashboard is not ready (cannot establish baselines; halt until automated or manual tracker is functional)

**Authority:** Operations Director (with Quality Director consultation for quality gates).

#### **Week 12: PILOT GO/NO-GO DECISION**

**GO Criteria (All Must Be Met):**
- ✓ Configurator BOM accuracy ≥98% (0–1 ECNs in 10 orders due to config errors)
- ✓ CONWIP cycle time ≤6 weeks actual (improvement ≥1 week vs. current 7–10)
- ✓ SMED changeover ≤30 min average (improvement of ≥60 min vs. 90 min baseline)
- ✓ FPY maintained ≥94% (no degradation on parallelized inspection)
- ✓ Hold-waiver rate <5% (no forced waivers due to WIP pressure)
- ✓ Supplier fast-track feasible (8-week lead time confirmed achievable; no cost premium >8%)

**NO-GO Triggers (Any One Halts Rollout):**
- ❌ Configurator BOM errors >5% (config design or system integration is faulty; return to design phase)
- ❌ CONWIP cycle time unchanged or worse (batch queue is not root cause of lead time; CONWIP may not be applicable)
- ❌ SMED changeover >40 min (fixtures/tools are ineffective; additional tooling design needed; defer SMED rollout)
- ❌ FPY drops >2% (parallelized inspection is causing escapes; revert to sequential gates; investigate root cause)
- ❌ Hold-waiver rate >10% (WIP cap is too tight; rework capacity is insufficient; increase cap or reduce incoming order rate)
- ❌ Supplier lead-time not achievable (return to sourcing; activate Supplier B contingency)

**Authority:** Operations Director (joint decision with Sales, Quality, Procurement leads).

**Outcome:**
- **IF GO:** Proceed to full rollout (Weeks 13–26, Phase 1 completion).
- **IF NO-GO (Partial):** Iterate specific failing lever (e.g., re-design SMED fixtures, re-size CONWIP cap, revisit supplier sourcing) and re-pilot 4 weeks later.
- **IF NO-GO (Full):** Pause plan; conduct root-cause analysis (diagnostic assumptions were wrong, or execution was poor?); reconvene steering committee for re-plan (worst case: fall back to incremental process improvements only, not 6-week target).
