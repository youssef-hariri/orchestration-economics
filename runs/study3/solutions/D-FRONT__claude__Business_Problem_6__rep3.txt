This comprehensive marketing plan establishes a science-backed, ethically compliant campaign for a dietary supplement targeting health-conscious Korean adults (18+). **Campaign Theme:** "Your Health, Your Way" (당신의 건강, 당신의 방식)

**Campaign Duration:** 18 months (3 phases)

**Total Budget:** ₩5 billion (~$3.8 million USD)

**Primary Objectives:**
- Establish credible, science-backed brand positioning within 12 months
- Drive sustainable consumer purchase across multiple channels
- Secure healthcare professional endorsements and recommendations
- Maintain zero regulatory violations and 100% compliance across all touchpoints

**Target Audience:** Health-conscious Korean adults 18+; excludes pregnant/breastfeeding women, children, and individuals with contraindicated medical conditions

**Risk Level:** 🟢 COMPLIANT — All critical regulatory and ethical issues resolved before launch authorization

## TARGET MARKET & CULTURAL INSIGHT

### Market Selection Rationale: South Korea

**Why South Korea:**
- High supplement market penetration (68% of adults use dietary supplements; KFDA 2023)
- Convergence of traditional Korean medicine (hanbang) and cutting-edge biotech adoption
- Strict but navigable regulatory environment (MFDS)
- Strong digital/social commerce infrastructure (Naver, Coupang, Instagram dominance)
- Large aging population (65+ reaching 20% by 2025) creating preventive wellness demand
- Market size: ₩7.2B+ annually; growing preventive wellness category

### Core Health Beliefs & Wellness Paradigms

**Prevention Focus:** Koreans prioritize preventive health spending over reactive treatment. **Marketing implication:** Emphasize "daily maintenance" and "lifecycle wellness" rather than disease cure.

**Traditional + Western Medicine Integration:** 72% of Koreans use both Korean medicine (hanbang) and Western medicine simultaneously. **Implication:** Position supplement as complementary to both practices; never position as replacement.

**Science Credibility & Clinical Data Trust:** High trust in published research and MFDS-approved health function claims. **Implication:** Visible clinical evidence, university partnerships, and MFDS approval badges are essential credibility signals.

**Heat/Cold Balance Philosophy:** Koreans categorize foods/supplements as "warm" (온성) or "cool" (찬성); mismatched supplements viewed with suspicion. **Implication:** Clarify thermal properties in educational content; avoid positioning as universally suitable without context.

**Trust in Healthcare Professionals:** 65% of supplement purchasers consult healthcare providers before use. **Implication:** Physician and pharmacist endorsements are gold-standard proof points; healthcare professional education drives consumer recommendations.

**Measurable Results Expected:** Koreans expect visible/testable outcomes within 4–8 weeks. **Implication:** Use biomarker improvements and validated outcome scales in case studies; set realistic expectation messaging.

### Big Idea: "Your Health, Your Way" (당신의 건강, 당신의 방식)

**Concept:** This campaign respects that Korean adults integrate supplements into diverse health practices—Western medicine + Korean medicine, gym routines + family wellness, individual optimization + collective family responsibility. We position the supplement as a **supportive tool that complements (never replaces) professional medical guidance**, adapting to each person's wellness strategy while maintaining full transparency about evidence, limitations, and safety.

**Visual Identity:**
- **Aesthetic:** Calm, trustworthy minimalism; soft sage green, warm white, light gold; natural lighting
- **Imagery:** Real, diverse Korean adults (18–70) in authentic settings (home routines, gym, pharmacy consultation, doctor's office, family dinner)—NOT aspirational/unattainable
- **Tone:** Honest, non-prescriptive, collaborative; emphasizes healthcare provider partnership
- **Safety Visual Cue:** All marketing includes visible disclaimer and "consult your healthcare provider" messaging

## MESSAGING FRAMEWORK & PROHIBITED CLAIMS

### Core Message (North Star)

**"[Product Name] combines peer-reviewed clinical evidence from [Korean University] with deep understanding of Korean health practices, enabling you to integrate daily wellness support seamlessly into your unique lifestyle—with full transparency about ingredients, evidence, limitations, and safety. Always consult your healthcare provider before starting any supplement regimen."**

### PERMITTED CLAIMS LIBRARY (All Claims Require TIER 1 Evidence)

**Governance Rule:** Every claim below requires published, peer-reviewed clinical trial in human subjects (n≥50, duration≥4 weeks, PubMed-indexed journal preferred). No claim may be used without attached evidence and Head of Regulatory Affairs approval.

#### Energy & Vitality
- ✅ "Supports natural energy and healthy vitality" (evidence: fatigue scales, energy biomarkers)
- ✅ "Helps maintain mental alertness and focus during demanding work" (evidence: cognitive performance tests)
- ✅ "Supports muscle comfort and recovery after physical activity" (evidence: DOMS pain scale, post-exercise muscle function)

#### Joint & Bone Health
- ✅ "Supports healthy joint comfort and flexibility" (evidence: WOMAC knee score, joint mobility tests)
- ✅ "Promotes healthy bone density and calcium metabolism" (evidence: bone formation/resorption markers)

#### Digestive Health
- ✅ "Supports healthy digestive function and comfort" (evidence: GI comfort scales, stool frequency)
- ✅ "Promotes healthy gut bacteria balance and microbiome diversity" (evidence: microbiome composition studies)

#### Immune & Wellness Support
- ✅ "Supports healthy immune system function and antioxidant defense" (evidence: immune markers—IgA, IgG, lymphocytes, SOD)
- ✅ "Promotes healthy skin radiance and hydration from within" (evidence: skin hydration, elasticity, collagen markers)

#### Sleep & Relaxation
- ✅ "Supports healthy sleep quality and restful sleep duration" (evidence: PSQI sleep quality index, actigraphy, polysomnography)
- ✅ "Promotes relaxation and calm during periods of normal stress" (evidence: cortisol levels, State Anxiety Inventory, HRV)

### ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITED CLAIMS (Legally Binding)

**The following claims are prohibited in ALL marketing materials, social posts, testimonials, influencer content, and employee communications. Violation triggers immediate content removal + legal review + potential regulatory action.**

| Prohibited Category | Examples | Violation Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Disease Treatment** | ❌ "Cures," "treats," "eliminates," "fixes" [any disease] | ₩50M+ MFDS fine; product seizure; brand reputation damage |
| **Disease Prevention** | ❌ "Prevents," "reduces risk of," "protects from" [disease] | MFDS warning letter; claim retraction required |
| **Drug Claims** | ❌ "Works like," "replaces," "as effective as" [medication] | Criminal liability; product seizure |
| **Unsubstantiated Efficacy %** | ❌ "Increases by X%," "improves Y% more than competitor" (without RCT data) | MFDS warning letter; retraction required |
| **Celebrity Endorsement Without Disclosure** | ❌ "Celebrity X uses [product]" (without #광고 + payment disclosure) | ₩50M+ fine; influencer ban; reputation damage |
| **Vague Wellness Exaggeration** | ❌ "Cures all ailments," "miraculous results," "revolutionary" | FTC/MFDS regulatory action |
| **Medication Interaction Downplay** | ❌ "Safe with any medication," "no interactions with [drug]" | Product liability; regulatory action; consumer harm |
| **Before/After Body Imagery** | ❌ Extreme body transformations (weight, medical condition images) | Consumer complaints; regulatory investigation |
