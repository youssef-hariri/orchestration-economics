# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY FOR THE BOARD
## Industrial-Services Unit: Turnaround-or-Exit Assessment & One-Year Decision Plan

---

## THE SITUATION

A diversified group's industrial-services unit has lost money for three consecutive years ($8–12M annually) despite two restructuring attempts. The Board has mandated one final year to produce either: (1) a credible path to profitability, or (2) a justified exit recommendation.

**Financial Trajectory (Three Years):**
- Year -2: $100M revenue, 30% GM, -$12M EBIT
- Year -1: $95M revenue, 29.3% GM, -$10.3M EBIT  
- Year 0: $88M revenue, 28% GM, -$8.4M EBIT
- **Trend:** Revenue declining -5% annually; losses improving but insufficient; order book collapsed 39% (0.59x vs. healthy 0.8–1.2x)

---

## INTEGRATED DIAGNOSIS

### Root-Cause Assessment: 70% Structural / 30% Execution

| Finding | Evidence | Implication |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| **Margin compression despite headcount cuts** | GM down 2pp while headcount reduced 25% | Pricing power lost; input costs rising; mix degrading. *Not fixable by cost-cutting alone.* |
| **Order book decline outpacing cost reductions** | Revenue -12%, OpEx -10%, losses persist. | Demand/competitiveness problem, not cost problem. Customers departing for capability reasons. |
| **A-player exodus post-restructure** | Attrition peaked Year -1; exit interviews cite "no credible path" | Market views unit as unsustainable; talent sees commoditization. |
| **SG&A at 37.5% vs. 12–18% benchmark** | Despite two restructures, overhead remains 2.5–3x best-in-class | *Fixable execution failure.* Regional redundancy, underutilized corporate functions, structural bloat. |
| **Segment variability: 26–36% margins** | Maintenance 26% stable; Specialized Tech 36% growing; Project -15% declining | Opportunity: Exit commoditized segments; concentrate on defensible niches. |

### Segment-Level Reality Check (Five-Segment Portfolio)

| Segment | Revenue | Margin | Retention | Trend | Verdict |
|---------|---------|--------|-----------|-------|---------|
| **A: Maintenance** | $35M | 26% | 95% | -1% (flat) | ✓ **DEFENSIBLE** – Sticky, low churn, cash generator |
| **B: Emergency Response** | $28M | 32% | 75% | -8% | ⚠ **MARGINAL** – High margin but declining; repricing decision critical (Month 4 gate) |
| **C: Project-Based** | $18M | 28% | 60% | -15% | ✗ **EXIT** – Losing to large EPC competitors; 8% win rate vs. 12–15% industry; scale mismatch |
| **D: Cross-Group** | $7M | 18% | 100% | -2% | ⚠ **RESTRUCTURE** – Captive pricing; hidden $0.5–1M subsidy. Reprice to market or divest (Month 6 gate). |
| **Specialized Tech** (Embedded) | ~$10M | 36% | 95% | +2% | ✓ **GROWTH** – Highest margin, only segment growing; defensible niche; extract & scale to $14M |

### Path Forward (Conditional Feasibility)

**Unit CAN be saved IF:**
1. ≥1 defensible segment achieves >25% EBITDA potential (✓ Maintenance, Specialized Tech, Emergency Response meet criteria)
2. SG&A reduced to <22% of revenue without destroying capability (✓ $12–15M reduction identified; regional consolidation viable)
3. Repricing holds 85%+ (requires Month 2–4 win/loss validation; not yet confirmed)
4. Cross-group customers repriced to market OR segment exited (Month 6 gate decision)
5. Project segment divested cleanly by Month 6 (reallocation of 8–10 staff feasible)
6. Management team rebuilt with credible leadership (talent market assessment pending)

**Unit CANNOT be saved IF any of above fail.**

## ONE-YEAR ROADMAP WITH GO/NO-GO GATES

### Phase 1: DIAGNOSTIC (Months 1–2) → Month 2 Board Gate
**Objective:** Validate turnaround assumptions; decide GO (proceed) or NO-GO (initiate exit)

| Workstream | Owner | Key Deliverables | Gate Criteria |
|-----------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Customer Win/Loss Analysis** | VP Sales | Interview 20 lost deals; quantify % price-driven vs. capability-driven | >60% price-driven = repricing viable; <60% = structural gap |

| **Customer-Level Profitability** | CFO | P&L by customer; identify "profit killers" (loss-making accounts) | Are defensible segments actually profitable at customer level? |

| **Competitive Position Assessment** | VP Commercial | Win rate by segment; competitor benchmarking; market consolidation trends | Can unit achieve 12–15% win rate through repositioning? |

**Month 2 Board Decision:**
- **GREEN:** Defensible segments confirmed + cost reduction viable + cross-selling material → **Proceed turnaround**

## THREE DECISION SCENARIOS AT MONTH 12

### Scenario A: Full Turnaround Success (35% Probability)
- **Financial:** EBIT +$2–3M; Margin 30%+; Order Book $65M+
- **Operational:** Repricing holds 90%+; New customer wins deliver $5M+; SG&A locked at 21%; Leadership stable
- **Decision:** **APPROVE Year 2 operational excellence program; full commitment to growth; exit option off**
- **Year 2+ Outlook:** 8–10% EBITDA margin; sustainable profitable growth trajectory

### Scenario B: Partial Turnaround / Conditional Path (40% Probability)
- **Financial:** EBIT -$1 to +$0.5M; Margin 29%+; Order Book $60–65M; Clear trajectory improvement
- **Operational:** Mixed results on repricing, mix shift, or new customer wins; progress visible but incomplete
- **Decision:** **CONDITIONAL approval for Q1 Year 2; establish Month 6 Year 2 re-gate; contingent exit option remains**
- **Contingency:** If Q1 Y2 shows no acceleration, trigger divestment

### Scenario C: Turnaround Infeasible (25% Probability)
- **Financial:** EBIT <-$2M; Margin <28%; Order Book <$60M; No trajectory improvement
- **Operational:** Repricing fails (churn accelerates), SG&A cuts insufficient, new customer wins miss, management unable to execute
- **Decision:** **MANDATORY EXIT; finalize divestment by Q1 Year 2**
- **Financial Impact:** $15–20M one-time charge Year 1; $10–12M annual EBITDA benefit Years 2+

## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS & RISKS

### Must-Have for Turnaround Success
1. **Month 2 Diagnostic Validation:** Win/loss data + labor productivity audit confirm repricing elasticity sufficient (>60% price-driven losses)
2. **Month 4 Repricing Hold Gate:** Pilot achieves 85%+ hold rate; churn acceleration contained
3. **Month 6 Order Book Stabilization:** Backlog reaches $55M+; new customer wins demonstrating sales execution capability
4. **Month 6 Segment D Decision:** Repricing accepted OR segment divested cleanly (no ambiguity)
5. **Month 6 SG&A Progress:** $7–8M run-rate reductions confirmed; delivery quality stable
6. **Month 9 EBIT Trajectory:** Clear path to breakeven by Month 12 visible in financial forecast

### Turnaround Failure Triggers (RED Flags)
- **Repricing pilot Month 4:** Hold rate <80% OR churn accelerates >20%
- **Month 6 Order Book:** Falls below $52M; pipeline weak
- **Month 6 SG&A:** Stuck >28% of revenue; cost reduction infeasible
- **Segment D repricing:** Rejected by Group CFO; negotiation fails
- **Leadership attrition:** Additional A-player departures post-Month 4
- **Customer communication:** Segment C exit or Segment D repricing triggers major customer backlash
