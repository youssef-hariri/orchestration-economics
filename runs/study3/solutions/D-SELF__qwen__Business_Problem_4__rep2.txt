# EXECUTIVE PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT PROPOSAL
**Project Name:** *Seoul Crave* (서울크레이브)  
**Tagline:** *Our Heritage, Your Craving.*  
**Target Market:** South Korea  
**Document Status:** Approved for R&D and Commercialization  

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
South Korea’s snack market is highly sophisticated, driven by a consumer base that demands complex flavor profiles, high aesthetic value, and functional health benefits. The *Seoul Crave* product line is a premium, culturally authentic snack portfolio engineered specifically for the modern Korean lifestyle. 

By moving away from generic Western interpretations and deeply embedding authentic regional ingredients, *Seoul Crave* bridges the gap between nostalgic culinary heritage and modern, health-forward convenience. The line is strategically aligned with hyper-specific, high-frequency consumption occasions—such as *Honsul* (solo home drinking), *Dang-charge* (afternoon energy boosts), and *Haejang* (hangover relief)—ensuring immediate relevance and habitual demand among local consumers.

---

## 2. PRODUCT LINE OVERVIEW
The *Seoul Crave* portfolio consists of four distinct SKUs, each meticulously calibrated to local taste matrices and modern lifestyle routines.

### Product 1: Chuncheon Dakgalbi Nurungji Clusters
*A savory, spicy, and umami-rich snack inspired by the beloved Chuncheon street food, utilizing traditional scorched rice.*

*   **Core Ingredients:** 
    *   Crunchy *Nurungji* (scorched rice) and puffed black rice.
    *   Glazed with authentic aged *Gochujang* (fermented chili paste) and *Mul-yeot* (traditional rice syrup).
    *   Finished with a dusting of *Deulkkae* (perilla seed powder).
*   **Flavor Profile:** 
    *   **Initial:** Sweet, smoky heat from the *gochujang*.
    *   **Mid-palate:** Caramel-like crunch and deep nuttiness from the perilla seed, mimicking the traditional Dakgalbi marinade.
    *   **Finish:** Savory, earthy, and satisfyingly crunchy.
*   **Health & Functional Benefits:** Baked, not fried. High in dietary fiber with a low glycemic index, sweetened naturally without refined corn syrup.
*   **Target Consumption Occasions:** 
    *   **Modern *Honsul*:** The perfect companion for solo evening drinking sessions (Soju/Makgeolli) at home.
    *   **Yasik (Late-Night Snacking):** Delivers the heavy crunch and savory spice of late-night delivery food in a lighter, guilt-free format.

### Product 2: Jeollanam-do Garu Injeolmi Bites (Pat & Heukimja)
*A sweet, chewy, and comforting dessert snack utilizing authentic stretched rice cake textures and premium regional soybeans.*

*   **Core Ingredients:** 
    *   Chewy, bite-sized *Garae-tteok* (traditional stretched rice cake) center.
    *   Injected with a molten paste of rich *Heukimja* (black sesame) and sweet *Pat* (red bean).
    *   Heavily dusted in premium *Garu* (roasted soybean powder) sourced from Jeollanam-do.
    *   Sweetened exclusively with Allulose.
*   **Flavor Profile:** 
    *   **Initial:** Warm, comforting aroma of roasted soybean.
    *   **Mid-palate:** Highly satisfying, pillowy chew from the rice cake.
    *   **Finish:** Rich, creamy, and subtly sweet depth from the black sesame and red bean core.
*   **Health & Functional Benefits:** Low-calorie and low-GI due to Allulose. Black sesame and red bean are traditionally viewed as "warming" ingredients that promote blood circulation.
*   **Target Consumption Occasions:** 
    *   **3 PM *Dang-charge*:** The ideal mid-afternoon mood and energy booster, specifically paired with an *Iced Americano* (Ah-Ah).
    *   **Premium Gifting:** Elegant flavor profile and high-quality regional ingredients make it perfect for modern holiday gift sets (Chuseok/Seollal).

### Product 3: Jin-ganjang Butter Ssal Puffs with Jinmi-eo
*A rich, savory, and aromatic seafood snack celebrating the Korean love for soy-butter flavor combinations.*

*   **Core Ingredients:** 
    *   Light, airy extruded *Ssal* (Korean short-grain rice) puffs.
    *   Coated in premium European butter powder and deeply savory *Jin-ganjang* (dark, aged soy sauce) crystals.
    *   Studded with real, chewy bits of *Jinmi-eo* (dried shredded squid) and freeze-dried scallions.
*   **Flavor Profile:** 
    *   **Initial:** Rich, creamy, and indulgent notes of European butter.
    *   **Mid-palate:** Salty, fermented umami from the aged soy sauce, cut by the chewy, briny texture of the squid.
    *   **Finish:** Sharp, aromatic lift from the freeze-dried scallions.
*   **Health & Functional Benefits:** Utilizes real *Ssal* and real *Jinmi-eo* for natural protein and minerals, completely avoiding artificial seafood flavorings or MSG.
*   **Target Consumption Occasions:** 
    *   **Hangang Park Picnic:** A premium, mess-free alternative to traditional *Chimaek* (chicken and beer) for weekend outings.
    *   **Savory *Anju*:** An ideal, convenient drinking snack to pair with craft beers or lagers.

### Product 4: Goheung Yuja & Cheongkigwa Spout Jelly
*A refreshing, tangy, and functional drinkable jelly utilizing Korea’s most famous citrus and a traditional hangover-relief ingredient.*

*   **Core Ingredients:** 
    *   Real *Goheung Yuja* (Korean citron) puree and Acacia honey.
    *   Set with agar-agar (100% plant-based).
    *   Infused with *Cheongkigwa* (Hovenia dulcis) extract and 1 billion CFUs of heat-stable probiotics.
    *   Packaged in a sleek, tear-top spout pouch.
*   **Flavor Profile:** 
    *   **Initial:** Bright, intense burst of floral citrus and honey.
    *   **Mid-palate:** Smooth, hydrating, and highly refreshing.
    *   **Finish:** Very subtle, clean herbal note from the *Cheongkigwa*.
*   **Health & Functional Benefits:** Dual-action functional food. Combines gut health (probiotics) with liver support/hangover prevention (*Cheongkigwa*). Zero added refined sugar.
*   **Target Consumption Occasions:** 
    *   **Morning *Haejang*:** Consumed first thing in the morning after a night of drinking to soothe the stomach.
    *   **Jjimjilbang Refreshment:** The ultimate cooling, hydrating snack for the relaxation rooms of Korean saunas.

## 3. PACKAGING & DISTRIBUTION STRATEGY

To succeed in a highly competitive market, physical presentation and retail placement must align with local premium trends.

### Packaging Aesthetics: "Minimalist Boutique"
Moving away from generic illustrative designs, the packaging adopts a "Minimalist Boutique" aesthetic (benchmarking premium Korean lifestyle brands like Tamburins). 
*   **Materials:** Ultra-matte, soft-touch pouches.
*   **Design:** Blind-embossed Korean typography with subtle, monochromatic line-art of the core ingredients. 
*   **Objective:** Justifies a premium price point and appeals to the consumer's desire for sophisticated, "Instagrammable" everyday items.

### Distribution Channels
*   **Primary (Premium Convenience):** GS25 and CU. Products will be placed in specialized "Premium" or refrigerated snack sections to highlight health and quality positioning, rather than standard register aisles.
*   **Secondary (Premium E-Grocery):** Kurly (formerly Market Kurly). Critical for building brand prestige among health-conscious, aesthetically driven early adopters.
*   **Tertiary (Social Commerce):** Naver Shopping via "Live Commerce." Utilizing live shopping for real-time taste-testing demonstrations and multi-pack bundle promotions.

## 4. STRATEGIC EXECUTION & RISK MITIGATION
While the conceptual framework is highly validated, the physical execution requires rigorous R&D and commercial planning to overcome specific food-science and market hurdles.

### Food Science & R&D Directives
*   **Texture & Shelf-Life (Injeolmi Bites):** *Garae-tteok* is prone to starch retrogradation (hardening) at room temperature. R&D must utilize advanced enzyme technology, strict water activity controls, or Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP) to maintain a soft, chewy texture.
*   **Moisture Migration (Nurungji & Ssal Puffs):** Rice bases are highly hygroscopic. Coating them with wet glazes or butter powders risks sogginess. Formulation must utilize Intermediate Moisture Food (IMF) techniques and rigorous moisture-barrier packaging.
*   **Probiotic Stability (Yuja Jelly):** *Yuja* is highly acidic. R&D must implement careful pH buffering to ensure the 1 billion CFU probiotic claim remains viable throughout the product's shelf life.

### Commercial & Pricing Strategy
*   **Impulse-Buy Friction:** The premium ingredients and boutique packaging will elevate the Cost of Goods Sold (COGS). 
*   **Mitigation:** Pricing must be carefully modeled to remain as close to the standard 2,000 - 3,000 KRW convenience store impulse-buy threshold as possible. If the premium COGS necessitates a higher price point (e.g., 3,500 - 4,500 KRW), marketing must heavily emphasize the functional health benefits and premium ingredient sourcing to justify the delta to the daily convenience store shopper.
