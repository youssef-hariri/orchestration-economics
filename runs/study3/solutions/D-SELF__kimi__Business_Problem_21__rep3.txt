**CONFIDENTIAL – FOR BOARD AND C-SUITE REVIEW**

# PROJECT NORTH STAR
## Network Automation & Workforce Transition Roadmap

**Document Purpose:** Request for Board authorization of a phased, risk-calibrated automation program across five regional warehouses.

**Strategic Thesis:** *Automate the work. Finance the transition honestly. Redeploy the people who fit. Severance those who don’t—with dignity and radical transparency. Preserve the social license in Millhaven at all costs.*

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The network faces an unsustainable equation: 35% annual picker turnover, error rates eroding key accounts, and loaded labor costs that automation can materially reduce. Vendor projections promise a 36-month payback. After stress-testing for transition taxes, productivity ramp penalties, brownfield constraints, and workforce demographics, **the financeable payback is 4.5 years**, with break-even targeted at **Month 42** and full cash-flow positivity by **Month 48**.

We will prove the model at Bravo (mechanized foundation, low social risk), scale it at Echo (AMR-assisted growth absorption), and ride the retirement wave at Charlie (ergonomic goods-to-person). We will enter Alpha—our largest site and a regional economic monopsony—only after the playbook is hardened, the vendor is validated, and a sealed Community Compact is in place. Delta’s fragile-SKU precision automation follows last.

Across approximately 350 at-risk picker/packer roles, we will absorb headcount through natural attrition, honest redeployment (~45% ceiling), voluntary separation, and only as a last resort, dignified involuntary reduction. **We will not manufacture make-work roles to hit vanity metrics.**

**Capital is available but not unlimited.** Phases 1–2 will utilize OPEX/Robotics-as-a-Service structures to preserve cash and transfer ramp risk to vendors. Proven savings will recycle into Phases 3–5. A 30% vendor performance escrow, held until an independent audit at Month 42, protects against the integration failures and vaporware that produced our competitor’s national-news collapse.

## 4. WORKFORCE TRANSITION FRAMEWORK

We reject vanity metrics. Across ~350 at-risk picker/packer roles, destination-role demand is **~160 roles (45% ceiling)**. The remaining gap closes through natural attrition, voluntary separation, and—only as a last resort—dignified, well-compensated involuntary reduction.

### 4.1 Network-Wide Workforce Math

| Lever | Headcount Impact | Mechanism |
|-------|------------------|-----------|
| **Natural Attrition + Hiring Freeze** | ~110–120 | 12-month external hiring freeze per site, beginning 90 days pre-go-live. |
| **Redeployment (value-creating roles)** | ~160 (45% ceiling) | G2P operator, QC auditor, AMR monitor, WMS clerk, mechatronics apprentice. **Zero make-work.** |
| **Voluntary Separation / Early Retirement** | ~50–60 | Enhanced buyouts; 1× salary lump-sum for 55+/15+ years at Alpha. |
| **Involuntary Reduction** | ~20–30 | Last resort; objective matrix; 60-day notice; full severance. |
| **Net Absorption** | **~350 displaced roles** | |

### 4.2 Cohort-Based Transition Strategy

| Tenure Cohort | Share of Risk | Economics | Strategy |
|---------------|---------------|-----------|----------|
| **< 2 Years** | ~25% | Severance $3K–$5K; Retrain $5K–$7K | **Prefer severance.** Do not invest in retraining unless exceptional aptitude for a scarce technical role. Streamlined voluntary package offered. |
| **2–8 Years** | ~35% | Severance $8K–$18K; Retrain $6K–$9K | **Targeted retrain.** G2P station operator, WMS clerk, AMR exception handler. Require >45% predicted success via aptitude + attendance matrix. |
| **> 10 Years** (Alpha-heavy) | ~40% | Severance $20K–$35K; Retrain $8K–$12K | **Prioritize retraining.** Cheaper than severance and protects social license. Route into Quality Auditor, Change Champion, Mechatronics Apprentice, or Training Hub instructor. |

### 4.3 North Star Academy – Right-Sized

A centralized, vendor-co-funded retraining campus with **160 seats** (not 400), offering three tracks:

- **Track A – Systems Operator:** G2P stations, AMR exception handling, WMS coordination (**6 weeks**).
- **Track B – Quality & Compliance:** Automated QC tunnel operation, auditor certification, key-account order protection (**4 weeks**).
- **Track C – Mechatronics Apprentice:** Vendor-certified maintenance techs at 1:100 ratio to automation footprint (**12–16 weeks**).

**Certified Wage Premium:** Multi-skilled operators receive **+$1.50/hour** and ergonomic workstation priority. **Tenure-Weighted Entry:** Retrained employees enter new roles at minimum plus $0.50/hr per 5 years of service (capped at new role midpoint) to prevent pay-parity resentment.

### 4.4 Site-Specific Workforce Tactics

**Bravo FC:** Displace permanent FTEs only. The seasonal surge pool of 40–50 is retained and cross-trained as a deployable **network SWAT unit** for go-live support at other sites.

**Echo GC:** Target **avoided cost**, not layoffs. With 15% YoY growth, the site sees **zero net reduction in Year 1**. If AMR fallback triggers, redeploy Echo’s AMR trainees into Bravo’s mechanized zones as floaters.

**Charlie Hub:** Begin automation **as retirements accelerate.** Every voluntary retirement is treated as a transition success.

**Alpha DC – The Millhaven Community Compact**

1. **Public Pledge:** *“No involuntary displacement during Alpha’s first 12 months of transition (Months 20–32).”* Achieved via Wave 1 design (no pick-face displacement), 8% volume growth absorption, natural attrition, and new Training Hub roles.
2. **Training Hub Center of Excellence:** Designate Alpha as the network’s mechatronics training hub. This **adds 10–15 permanent instructional/support roles**, with **3 positions reserved for long-tenured hourly employees** who certify as instructors.
3. **Legacy Employee Covenant:**
   - Guaranteed first right of refusal for all retraining tracks.
   - **Change Champions** (peer trainers): $2,500 stipend + protected status during transition.
   - **Early Retirement Window:** Employees 55+ with 15+ years receive a **1× salary lump-sum** voluntary incentive.
   - **Millhaven Automation Dividend:** If Alpha Wave 1 reduces cost-per-order by >10% while maintaining headcount stability, **25% of those savings** flow back to remaining hourly employees as a quarterly bonus.
4. **Community Transition Office:** Fund a 24-month downtown Millhaven resource center offering job fairs, resume clinics, and small-business counseling for affected household members.
5. **Bridge Employment:** For any involuntarily displaced worker at Alpha, guarantee **interviews with 3 vetted regional employers** within 30 days. Fund a **$5,000 Micro-Enterprise Grant** for displaced workers with >15 years tenure who start a local small business.
6. **Governance:** Quarterly transparency forums with the mayor, workforce development board, and regional community college.

### 4.5 Co-Design & Psychological Contract

Long-tenured employees resist *disrespect*, not technology.

- **Co-Design Committee (CDC):** At each site, **6–8 hourly employees (50% with >10 years tenure), elected not appointed**, hold sign-off authority on ergonomic workstation design, pick-path logic, exception-handling SOPs, and pick-to-light signal logic. **$1,000 stipend** + overtime pay for design-session hours.
- **Sequenced Communication:** Week 0 network-wide all-hands announces the program and site sequence. Bravo is told they are first due to turnover pain. Alpha is told they are *last* because they are most valued, with a sealed Community Compact. Monthly **"North Star Transparency Reports"** are posted on breakroom screens and mailed to homes.

### 4.6 Responsible Separation Escalation

If attrition and redeployment cannot close the gap:
1. **Voluntary Separation Packages:** Enhanced severance (2.5 weeks/year of service, minimum 26 weeks) + 6 months healthcare + outplacement concierge.
2. **Performance/Skills Matrix Review:** Objective scoring on attendance, quality, and retraining aptitude.
3. **Involuntary Reduction:** Last resort. Executed with 60-day notice, full severance, and community notification protocol.

### 4.7 Survivor Syndrome Mitigation

- **Survivor Check-Ins:** Mandatory HR 1:1s with all retained staff at 30, 60, and 90 days post-cutover.
- **Workload Guardrails:** Cap overtime at 10 hours/week during shadow-mode/dual-system operations. Budget for inefficiency; do not extract it from skeleton crews.

## 5. SERVICE CONTINUITY & CUSTOMER PROTECTION PROTOCOLS

We will not suffer the competitor’s fate. No "big bang" cutovers. No simultaneous WMS-plus-automation rip-and-replace.

### 5.1 The "Strangler Fig" Cutover Protocol

At each site:
- **Phase A:** Deploy automation WCS overlay integrating with the *existing* WMS via API bridge (Months 1–3).
- **Phase B:** Run parallel WMS shadow mode (Months 4–5).
- **Phase C:** Cut over WMS **only after** automation is stable for 30 days.

**Zone-by-Zone Execution:**
- One operational zone converts at a time; adjacent zones remain on legacy processes.
- New system runs in parallel with legacy for **14–21 days** before switchover. *Budget for dual-system labor cost.*
- **Rollback Trigger:** If order accuracy falls below **99.0%** or throughput drops >10% for >4 consecutive hours, the zone reverts to manual immediately while engineering resolves.

### 5.2 The Canary Protocol

| Level | Condition | Action | Decision Authority |
|-------|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| **1-Yellow** | 1 zone misses KPIs for 2 hrs | SWAT deploys; vendor L2 on-site. | Site GM |
| **2-Orange** | 1 zone misses KPIs for 4 hrs OR 2 zones miss for 2 hrs | Zone rollback to legacy; adjacent zones frozen. | Site GM + PMO Lead |
| **3-Red** | Site misses SLA for 8 hrs OR customer penalty event | Full site stabilization; next phase program-wide paused. | ESC Chair |
| **4-Black** | Safety incident OR community labor action OR board-level reputational event | Program halt; independent review; vendor default considered. | CEO + Board Risk Committee |

### 5.3 Key Account Protection

- **Surgical Quick Win:** At Bravo, automated scan/weigh/dimensioning at pack stations for key-account SKUs deploys in **Month 3–4**, *before* full zone automation, to immediately attack error rates.
- **Blackout Dates:** No cutovers during peak (November–December) or key account fiscal close periods.
- **Service-Level Insurance:** Offer affected key accounts a **2% invoice credit** during their site’s 45-day stabilization window.
- **Key Account War Room:** Daily 8:00 a.m. standup (Operations, Sales, Customer Success) during each site’s stabilization window.

### 5.4 Surge & Buffer Infrastructure

| Site | Pre-Cutover Buffer | Destination | Trigger to Release |
|------|-------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Bravo | 15% fast-mover safety stock | Echo GC | Day 1 of Zone 1 cutover |
| Echo | 10% + key-account SKUs | Bravo FC | Day 1 of AMR zone activation |
| Alpha | 20% (highest error-rate risk) | Regional 3PL + Bravo/Echo split | Day 1 of QC tunnel go-live |

- **SWAT Labor Pool:** 25 cross-functional "Surge Associates" per region, deployable to any site during go-live weeks.
- **3PL Warm-Standby:** Pre-negotiated 90-day surge agreement with a regional 3PL. If any site throughput drops >15% for >8 hours, 30% of outbound volume diverts within 48 hours. Fixed retainer: $150K/year per site (included in steady-state OpEx).
- **Agency Surge Contracts:** Pre-negotiated 48-hour staffing agreements with two regional temp firms.

## 6. GOVERNANCE & ROI MEASUREMENT DASHBOARD

### 6.1 The Counterfactual Engine

To prevent attributing macro labor-market trends to automation, we will implement a **difference-in-differences model**:
- **Control Group:** A permanent non-automated zone at Alpha (slowest bulk zone) will remain manual through Month 42 to provide a live counterfactual; alternatively, a synthetic control will be constructed from pre-automation data if physical constraints intervene.
- **Attribution Rule:** Only savings in excess of the control-group trend count toward program ROI. Reported monthly to the Board.

### 6.2 Three-Tier KPI Hierarchy

#### Tier 1: Board / Investor Metrics (Monthly Steering Committee)
| Metric | Methodology | Target / Gate |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **Program NPV** | Discounted at WACC; includes Transition Tax | Positive at Month 54 |
| **Cumulative Cash Flow vs. CapEx** | Actual cash out vs. actual labor/quality savings, net of incremental OpEx | Break-even by Month 42 |
| **Vendor Promise vs. Conservative Tracker** | Dual-line forecast: Vendor fantasy (3-yr) vs. Operational reality (4.5-yr) | Board briefed on both monthly; **no executive incentives tied to the fantasy track** |
| **Cost of Quality (COQ)** | Returns + re-ship + customer penalties + key-account churn cost | –35% by end Year 2 |

#### Tier 2: Operational Performance Metrics (Weekly PMO Review)
| Metric | Baseline | Target | Protocol |
|--------|----------|--------|----------|
| Labor $ / Order (network) | Locked Month 0 | –22% by end Year 2 | Adjusted for SKU mix and wage inflation using control group |
| Throughput per Labor Hour (TPLH) | By site, by zone | +30% at automated sites | Time-study validated; excludes training hours |
| OT Spend % of Loaded Labor | Baseline | –40% | Measured against site-level labor budget |
| Automation Uptime (OEE) | N/A | >92% mechanical, >99.5% software | MTTR tracked by vendor; penalties for <95% |
| Order Accuracy (network) | ~98.6% (1.4% error) | >99.5% | Weighted by customer penalty cost |

#### Tier 3: Tactical / Zone Metrics (Daily War Room during cutover)
| Metric | Threshold | Response |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Zone Pick Accuracy | <99.0% for >2 hours | Shadow mode reactivation; manual rollback |
| Zone Throughput vs. Plan | <90% for >4 hours | Invoke SWAT; pause adjacent zone conversion |
| AMR Fleet Utilization | <75% during active shifts | Fleet tuning; deadhead route optimization |
| System Exception Rate | >2% orders requiring manual intervention | WMS-WCS handshake audit |

### 6.3 Governance Structure

**Executive Steering Committee (ESC)**
- **Chair:** COO or EVP Supply Chain
- **Members:** CFO, CHRO, VP Operations, rotating Site GMs, External Independent Engineer
- **Cadence:** Monthly
- **Authority:** Go/No-Go on phase gates; approves deviation >$250K or >30 days
- **Mandate:** Review dual-track forecast. **No executive incentive may be tied to the 3-year vendor track.**

**Independent Audit Council (IAC)**
- **Composition:** Industrial engineering firm; mechatronics specialist; labor economist
- **Cadence:** Months 12, 24, 36, 42
- **Deliverable:** Attestation that savings are real, attributed correctly, and sustainable. **Month 42 audit unlocks final vendor payment holdback.**

**Site Transition War Room**
- **Composition:** Site GM, Operations Manager, HR Business Partner, Vendor Lead, elected Hourly Employee Representative, Safety Officer
- **Cadence:** Daily (07:00) during 45-day stabilization windows
- **Authority:** Can invoke zone rollback; escalates to ESC if 2 consecutive KPIs missed (Canary Protocol).

## APPENDIX A: RISK MITIGATION — TECHNICAL, VENDOR & CYBERSECURITY

### A.1 Vendor & Integration Standards: The "Never/Always" Gate

| NEVER Accept | ALWAYS Require |
|--------------|----------------|
| Vendor-proposed ROI models as contract inputs | Independent 3rd-party reference audits at **3 live sites** with same WMS, similar SKU dispersion, >18 months operational history |
| Greenfield-only references | Brownfield retrofit references with <5% downtime tolerance |
| "Prototype-as-production" deployments | **30-day Production Pilot Clause** on your floor with your SKUs before full fleet commitment |
| Best-of-breed components without a designated System Integrator (SI) "throat-to-choke" | Single-entity contractual accountability for WMS-WCS-equipment handshakes |
| Proprietary communication stacks | **Open architecture**: VDA 5050 (AMR fleet interoperability), OPC-UA / MQTT (IIoT), REST APIs |

### A.2 Integration Stress Test (Pre-Contract)
Before any PO, the vendor must pass a **72-Hour Integration Burn** in a sandbox environment:
- Replay 30 days of actual order waves through the proposed WMS-WCS-automation stack.
- Inject 2× peak volume spikes and 15% SKU-location mapping errors.
- **Pass criteria:** <0.1% system timeouts, 100% inventory transaction accuracy, zero phantom picks.
- **Cost ownership:** Vendor bears sandbox and commissioning engineering costs; company budgets 200–300 internal IT/IE hours for data preparation and validation.
