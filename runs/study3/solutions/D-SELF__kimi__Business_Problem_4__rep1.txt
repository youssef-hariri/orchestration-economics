## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

India is not merely a large snack market; it is a continent of *mini-meals*. The cultural boundary between *khana* (a structured meal) and *nashta* (a fluid, social snack) is sacrosanct, and the modern Indian consumer—urban millennials, Gen Z, and affluent nuclear families—is seeking regional authenticity delivered with industrial hygiene, convenience, and lifestyle coherence.

This proposal presents **The *Swaad Street* Collection**, a four-SKU portfolio architected around India’s **taste trinity** (*teekha-khatta-meetha*: spicy-sour-sweet), its ritualized dayparts, and its predominantly vegetarian dietary guardrails. To avoid the commercial pitfall of stretching a single masterbrand across incompatible margin and frequency expectations, the collection is organized into three distinct equity tiers—**Reserve, Craft, and Daily**—that share backend supply-chain infrastructure while signaling appropriate value to their respective channels.

**Wave 1** (Months 1–6) establishes credibility and margin through the Reserve and Craft tiers. **Wave 2** (Months 7–12) drives penetration frequency through the Daily tier’s ₹10 sachet engine. All four SKUs leverage existing extrusion, vacuum-frying, and enrobing technologies within India’s robust co-manufacturing ecosystem (Indore, Coimbatore, Vadodara), ensuring an 8–10 month speed-to-market with no moonshot capital expenditure.

## 2. THE INDIAN SNACKING IMPERATIVE

### 2.1 Occasion Architecture
Indian snacking is organized around **social ritual, not just appetite**. The four monetizable dayparts are:

| **Occasion** | **Timeframe** | **Consumer Need State** |
|---|---|---|
| *Shaam Ki Chai* | 4:00–6:00 PM | A savory, *kadak* (sturdy) crunch that survives humid afternoons and complements milky, sweet tea. |
| *Sham Ki Bhuk* | 5:00–7:00 PM | A “light-feeling” (*halka*) yet indulgent bite—the premium permission space. |
| *Khaana Khatam* | Post-lunch / dinner | A signal of hospitality and closure; the modern successor to loose *mukhwas*. |
| Tiffin & Travel | Morning / commute | Dry, non-greasy, one-hand formats requiring no refrigeration or accompaniment. |

### 2.2 Taste Trinity
Indian palates crave **layered, sequential contrast**, not blended balance. A single eating experience moves from the frontal heat of red chili to the sour shock of dried mango (*amchoor*), finishing with the mineral tang of black salt (*kala namak*) or the earthy depth of jaggery (*gur*). Bland is the cardinal sin; dissonance is the baseline.

### 2.3 Dietary Guardrails & Cultural Compliance
- **Vegetarian-First:** All formulations are 100% vegetarian (green dot), with two SKUs offering verified **Jain-friendly** sub-variants (no onion, no garlic, no underground roots) to unlock premium distribution in Gujarat, Rajasthan, Mumbai, and Delhi.
- **Clean-Label Bias:** Turmeric, beetroot, and caramelized onion (where permitted) replace synthetic colors. Traditional wellness associations—*cooling* fennel, *light* fox nuts, *digestive* ajwain—are referenced without overstating regulated Ayurvedic claims.
- **Sachet Economics:** Trial and pantry loading in India still run on ₹5 and ₹10 price points; any mass-market SKU must launch in single-serve **price-to-packet** architecture.

## 3. PORTFOLIO ARCHITECTURE: THREE TIERS, ONE MASTERBRAND

To prevent equity dilution, **Swaad Street** deploys a tiered visual and pricing architecture from day one:

- **Swaad Street Reserve** (₹100–₹180+): Premium and luxury gift SKUs with minimalist, provenance-driven aesthetics.
- **Swaad Street Craft** (₹60): Mid-premium, format-led innovation for modern trade and café.
- **Swaad Street Daily** (₹10–₹60): High-frequency, maximalist “Bazaar Pop” graphics for kirana and quick-commerce.

## 4. THE PRODUCT LINEUP

### PILLAR I — Swaad Street Daily
**Chai-Cut Chaat Kadak**

**Concept:** The definitive 4 PM tea companion. Thick, slow-baked lentil-and-rice twirls with deep ridges, engineered to remain audibly crisp (*kadak*) through humid afternoons. The twirl is eaten in the traditional alternating rhythm of sip-and-crunch that defines Indian tea culture—not dunked, but paired.

**Formulation:** Chickpea flour (*besan*), rice flour, urad dal flour, *amchoor* (dried raw mango), black salt (*kala namak*), roasted cumin, *anardana* (pomegranate seed powder), tamarind powder, Kashmiri chili, and a precise trace of asafoetida (*hing*). Masala is applied in two stages (dough + dusting) to prevent palate fatigue.

**Compliance Note:** The Jain sub-variant is formulated without dehydrated potato, onion, garlic, or underground roots, ensuring credibility in core Jain markets.

**Flavor Arc:** Delhi-style Aloo Chaat logic—immediate tang from black salt and *amchoor*, warming chili mid-palate, lingering tamarind *khatta* finish.

**Positioning:** *“Kulhad optional. Kadak mandatory.”* Street-food authenticity delivered with hygienic, shelf-stable manufacturing.
