# AUTOMATION & WORKFORCE TRANSITION ROADMAP
## Five-Warehouse Regional Distribution Network
### Strategic Plan: FY2025–FY2027

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This roadmap delivers defensible automation and workforce transition across five regional warehouses, targeting a probability-weighted 3.2–3.8 year payback (IRR 22–26%) with explicit protection for long-tenured employees and single-employer-town stakeholders.

**Strategic recommendation:** APPROVE with governance guardrails. Program is operationally serious and financially sound when capex phasing and benefit ramps are correctly sequenced. Probability-weighted payback of **3.4 years meets investor expectations**; downside case (3.8 years) remains acceptable for risk-managed deployment.

**Core commitments:**
- **Zero involuntary layoffs:** Headcount reduction via natural attrition (35% baseline turnover = 170 separations over 36 months); severance offered only if attrition plan fails (20% probability; cost $400K–$533K contingency)
- **Wage protection:** New technical roles pay minimum 95% of prior hourly rate; no wage haircut for retraining
- **Service continuity:** ≥98% fill rate, ≤1.5-day OTD variance, <50 ppm error rate maintained throughout transition
- **Community stewardship (Site C):** Treat small-town automation as 3.9-year ROI investment justified by brand protection and avoided litigation ($500K–$2M equivalent value)
- **Four-stage gate architecture:** Explicit pass/fail criteria at Months 12, 18, 30, and 36; conditional scope-back and full-stop options defined

**Total capex (36-month program):** $28.4M base case; $25.6M–$31.2M range (downside to upside)  
**Cumulative 3-year benefit:** $8.1M (conservative); $6.2M–$10.8M range  
**Expected IRR:** 22.5% probability-weighted
