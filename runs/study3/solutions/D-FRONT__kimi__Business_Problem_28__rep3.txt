**TURNAROUND-OR-EXIT ASSESSMENT**
**Industrial Services Unit — Board Decision Document**

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Industrial Services Unit has recorded losses for three consecutive years. Two prior restructurings failed because they treated the problem as excess overhead while the revenue portfolio decayed. The unit is now caught in a self-reinforcing doom loop: losses have calcified into structural behavior, talent is fleeing, and the order book is shrinking.

**Default Recommendation: EXIT.** The burden of proof rests entirely on continuation.

To preserve board optionality and avoid a distressed, time-compressed process, we propose a **two-phase, gated process** operating on a **dual-track basis from Day 1** (restructure the core while preserving exit optionality):

| Phase | Duration | Budget | Purpose |
|-------|----------|--------|---------|
| **Phase 0 — Validation Sprint** | Days 1–45 | **€1.2m** | Forensic evidence-gathering to harden or refute preliminary economics before irreversible capital is deployed. |
| **Phase 1 — Turnaround Execution** | Months 2–12 | **€9.5m** | Conditional release subject to passage of **Gate 0** at Day 45. |

**Pre-Conditions for Board Approval**
Before this plan is activated, three conditions must be satisfied:
1. **Pre-Condition A:** Divisional CEOs of profitable overlapping divisions must execute a **binding SCSA Pre-Commitment Letter** accepting the EGC methodology and referral-fee arbitration mechanism.
2. **Pre-Condition B:** A **Turnaround MD** must be named (or a retained search must present three shortlisted candidates within 14 days).
3. **Pre-Condition C:** Group Legal must circulate a **Labor Risk & Works Council Memo** confirming the timeline and accrual requirements for footprint consolidation.

If any pre-condition is not met, the board should default immediately to exit.

## 1. DIAGNOSIS: STRUCTURAL OR EXECUTION-DRIVEN?

### 1.1 Verdict
**Predominantly structural decay, compounded by executional failure.** The unit’s cost architecture and service-line portfolio are scaled for a market that no longer exists. Cost-cutting could never raise prices or reverse commoditization. While an earlier, portfolio-first restructuring *might* have altered the trajectory, the opportunity is now lost: the doom loop is structural in behavior.

### 1.2 Confirmed Evidence vs. Working Hypotheses

| Category | Status | Evidence / Hypothesis |
|----------|--------|----------------------|
| **3-year losses despite 2 restructurings** | **Confirmed** | Cost cuts were G&A and headcount reductions (~15% each round) with no change to footprint, pricing authority, or service-line mix. |
| **Shrinking order book** | **Confirmed** | Five consecutive quarters of backlog decline. |
| **Talent flight** | **Confirmed** | Voluntary departures of 8 senior managers in 18 months; exit interviews cite irreversible decline. |
| **Portfolio mix viability** | **Hypothesis** | Preliminary data suggests Tier C/D (40% of revenue) generates contribution margins below the cost of capital. *To be validated in Phase 0.* |
| **Footprint overcapacity** | **Hypothesis** | 12 branches vs. peer benchmark of 1 hub per €25–30m addressable revenue. Break-even volume may exceed addressable demand in 5+ regions. *To be validated in Phase 0.* |
| **Overlap customer economics** | **Hypothesis** | Estimated 35% of revenue (€52.5m) from shared customers. Assumed loss-leader status, but EGC untested. *To be validated in Phase 0.* |
| **Peer margin benchmark** | **Hypothesis** | Specialist competitors reportedly earn 8–12% EBITDA. *To be validated by external market study in Phase 0.* |

### 1.3 Diagnostic Tree — Testable Thresholds

| Node | Test | Owner | Validation Method | Decision Threshold |
|------|------|-------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **N1: Gross Margin Structure** | Can any service line bear fully-loaded costs at rational scale? | Controller + External Advisor | Forensic restatement of 3-year P&L by line, customer, branch | If restated Tier A CM <20% or Tier B CM <10%, core is not viable. |
| **N2: Footprint Scale** | Is break-even volume per branch ≤70% of addressable market? | Strategy / Ops | Bottom-up branch P&Ls; drive-time analysis of €25m market catchments | If >5 branches require >70% local share to break even, overcapacity is structural. |
| **N3: Demand Reversibility** | Why are bids lost? | Commercial / Corp Dev | Win/loss root cause on last 24 months; 20 customer churn interviews | If ≥50% of losses are OEM insourcing or secular shift to outcome-based contracts, demand displacement is structural. |
| **N4: Synergy Reality** | Do overlap customers generate real group value? | Divisional CFOs + Corp Dev | EGC audit: divisional profit attribution per account, independently validated | If >50% of overlap revenue exits at ≥10% standalone CM, synergy is illusory. |

**Phase 0 mandate:** All four nodes must be resolved with evidence by Day 45. If preliminary hypotheses are refuted, the board will be advised to accelerate exit immediately.

### 1.4 The Doom Loop
Losses → Uncertainty → Talent flight → Eroded customer relationships → Further order-book decline → Deeper losses. This loop is now **structural in behavior** even if it was initiated by execution failures.

### 2.3 Customer Segmentation & EGC Framework

Approximately **35% of revenue (€52.5m)** comes from customers who also buy from profitable group divisions. For each overlap account, the **Effective Group Contribution (EGC)** will be calculated as:

> **EGC** = (Unit Standalone CM% × Revenue) + (ΔGroup Division Pre-Tax Profit from retained access) – (True Cost to Serve)

**Governance & Enforcement**
- **Pre-Commitment:** Before Day 1, divisional CEOs must sign a binding letter accepting the EGC methodology, agreeing to provide customer-level divisional P&L data to Corp Dev, and accepting Group CEO arbitration for disputes.
- **Independent Validation:** Corp Dev will calculate ΔGroup Division Profit using audited customer margin data.
- **Enforcement:** If a division refuses to pay the SCSA referral fee (2–4% of attributable divisional margin) for a Group Strategic account, the unit is automatically authorized to reprice that account to a **15% standalone CM floor** or exit it without penalty.

| Segment | Est. Revenue Range | EGC Rule | Strategic Action |
|---------|-------------------|----------|----------------|
| **Overlap – Group Strategic** | €20m–€35m | **EGC > +€50k/account/year** | **RETAIN & REPRICE.** Arm’s-length pricing + SCSA referral fee. |
| **Overlap – Standalone Dependent** | €15m–€25m | **EGC –€50k to +€50k** | **REPRICE to ≥10% standalone CM.** Exit if rejected. |
| **Overlap – Value-Destructive** | €5m–€12m | **EGC < –€50k/account/year** | **IMMEDIATE EXIT.** Divisions can procure elsewhere. |
| **Standalone Tier 1** (Top 20) | €55m–€65m | N/A | **RETAIN CORE.** Transition to Tier A/B solutions. |
| **Standalone Tier 2** | €20m–€30m | N/A | **SELECTIVE RETAIN** only if willing to buy Tier A consulting or integrated bundles. |
| **Standalone Tier 3** | €8m–€12m | N/A | **IMMEDIATE EXIT.** Stop bidding; manage run-off. |

### 2.4 Tier B Selective Retention Criteria
An account is retained in Tier B **only if** it meets **all four** conditions:
1. Annual revenue ≥ €800k;
2. Demonstrated CM potential ≥ 12% after branch consolidation and digital scheduling;
3. Contract tenor ≥ 24 months or renewal probability > 75%;
4. Willingness to migrate to an integrated MRO bundle (preventing cherry-picking of low-value break-fix work).

## 3. ONE-YEAR TURNAROUND PLAN: “PROJECT BREAKWATER”

### Guiding Principles
1. **Default to Exit.** The burden of proof is on continuation.
2. **Evidence Before Expenditure.** No irreversible restructuring spend until Phase 0 validates the core.
3. **Portfolio First, Costs Second.** Revenue portfolio is surgically reduced to the addressable core before the cost base is resized.
4. **Talent is the Critical Constraint.** The restructured core is worthless without its practice leaders.
5. **Dual-Track Discipline.** Sell-side advisors prepare a clean data room from Day 1.
