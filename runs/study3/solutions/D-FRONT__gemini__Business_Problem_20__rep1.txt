# Executive Roadmap: The 6-Week Flow Initiative

**Objective:** Reduce quoted lead times from 14 weeks to 6 weeks while increasing First Pass Yield (FPY) by 2%. 
**Mandate:** Speed is achieved through the elimination of wait-states, not by compressing labor time or compromising quality standards.

---

### I. The Core Operating Philosophy: "Flow-with-Confidence"
We are abandoning the "Batch-and-Queue" model. Our new production architecture relies on three pillars:
1.  **Systemic Forcing Functions:** Moving QC from an "end-of-line" bottleneck to "in-process" hard-gates via an Integrated Quality Management System (IQMS).
2.  **Strategic Decoupling:** Utilizing a 4-week "Supermarket" buffer for long-lead components to insulate our delivery timeline from supply chain volatility.
3.  **Shared-Risk Governance:** Aligning departmental KPIs to prioritize **system stability** over departmental silos.

---

### II. Functional Alignment & KPI Architecture
To eliminate the "blame game," we are shifting from individual functional metrics to shared system-health metrics.

| Department | New Primary Metric | Strategic Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sales** | **Forecast Accuracy** | Eliminates sandbagging; enables optimized VMI stocking levels. |
| **Production** | **First Pass Yield (FPY)** | Rewards "getting it right the first time" over output volume. |
| **Quality** | **Process Capability (Cpk)** | Rewards the design of "foolproof" processes over reactive inspection. |

**The Joint Accountability Clause:** All systemic failures (warranty claims/escaped defects) trigger a mandatory, cost-inclusive joint review. Financial impact is shared across Production and Quality, ensuring proactive, rather than reactive, collaboration.

---

### III. The Execution Roadmap

#### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
*   **Digital Integration:** Install hard-gate sensors/check-sheets at the top three critical assembly stations.
*   **Certified Operator Program:** Launch the "Mastery-of-Process" curriculum. Only certified staff are authorized to trigger digital sign-offs.

#### Phase 2: Validation (Weeks 5–8)
*   **Supplier Synchronization:** Activate VMI for the top 20% of long-lead parts based on the new 12-week rolling forecast.
*   **The "First-Unit" Audit:** Shift the 20% "Over-the-Shoulder" audit to the beginning of the production cycle to validate the setup before full-batch flow.

#### Phase 3: Flow (Weeks 9–12)
*   **Single-Piece Flow:** Transition from batch production to Takt-based single-piece movement.
*   **Containment Buffer:** Maintain 15% "White Space" in the schedule—specifically allocated for quality containment and system adjustments, not as idle capacity.

#### Phase 4: Launch (Week 13)
*   **Stress Test:** Soft-launch 6-week lead times on non-critical product lines to calibrate the system under real-world pressure.

---

### IV. Executive Safeguards & Risk Mitigation
*   **The "Andon" Rule:** Any FPY deviation >1% triggers an immediate, non-negotiable line stop. This preserves quality integrity and prevents the historical "two-year warranty fallout" scenario.
*   **Delegated Authority:** To address potential culture shock, management will treat the shift to digital policing as a transition to "high-skill empowerment," emphasizing that the system protects the operator from speed-induced error.
*   **Supplier Contingency:** If VMI partners fail to meet the 12-week rolling forecast threshold, the Purchasing team is authorized to activate pre-vetted secondary sourcing immediately to prevent downstream flow collapse.

---

### V. Strategic Summary
We will not "push" speed into our system. We will pull it by ensuring that every unit produced is verified at the point of creation. **If the process is stable, the 6-week lead time is the natural result.** We are no longer managing a factory; we are managing a synchronized flow.
