**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Executive Leadership Team  
**DATE:** October 26, 2023  
**SUBJECT:** Strategic Transition to Subscription-Based Revenue Model

---

### **Executive Summary**
To secure our long-term valuation and market competitiveness, we are initiating a strategic pivot from perpetual software licensing to a high-growth SaaS subscription model. This transition is designed to move our business from a "lumpy" transactional revenue cycle to a predictable, high-margin, recurring revenue stream. 

While this shift requires navigating a short-term revenue trough, our plan—validated by rigorous risk modeling—prioritizes customer retention, sales force alignment, and aggressive investor transparency to ensure a seamless transition over an 18-month horizon.

---

### **1. The Strategic Pivot: Value over Penalty**
Rather than forcing migration through punitive price hikes, we are adopting a "Value-Lock" strategy. 
*   **Version Parity Gap:** Effective immediately, all major R&D investment—including AI-driven analytics, cloud-native APIs, and automated reporting—will be gated to the SaaS platform. 
*   **Product-Led Pull:** By ensuring the subscription offering is objectively superior and feature-rich compared to the legacy perpetual environment, we transform the migration from a coerced cost into a strategic business upgrade for our customers.

### **2. Sales Force Alignment: The "Bridge" Incentive**
Recognizing that our sales force must be incentivized to abandon the comfort of large, upfront perpetual deals, we are implementing a "Double-Dip" commission structure:
*   **Bridge Tax Compensation:** For the first 12 months, sales representatives will receive full commissions on migration deals as if they were new perpetual bookings.
*   **Migration Kickers:** Reps will receive an additional 15% bonus on the total 3-year contract value for every legacy renewal successfully flipped to a multi-year subscription.
*   **Quota Realignment:** Quotas will shift exclusively to "New ARR" and "Expansion ARR" to ensure long-term customer health over short-term transaction volume.

### **3. Pricing & Customer Migration**
*   **Zero-Friction Carry-Over:** To remove switching costs, we will credit the last 12 months of paid maintenance toward the first year of a new subscription.
*   **The 3-Year Lock:** We will offer a 10% discount for 3-year commitments. This secures our cash flow, protects against competitor churn, and provides customers with price stability.

### **4. Financial Communication: Protecting Our Valuation**
We anticipate investor concern regarding the temporary revenue trough. To manage this, we are committed to the following reporting protocol:
*   **SaaS-Adjusted P&L:** From Day 1, we will provide the market with "SaaS-Adjusted" metrics, reporting "Booked ARR" (BARR) alongside GAAP results. This educates the Street on our "True Health" versus the temporary accounting impact of the transition.
*   **Margin-Expansion Narrative:** We will frame the trough explicitly as a "CAC-Heavy Growth Investment." We will focus analyst discussions on our transition to a 3x higher Lifetime Value (LTV) profile, justifying the temporary dip as a necessary milestone for valuation expansion.

### **5. Transition Roadmap (18-Month Horizon)**
*   **Months 0–3:** **Clean Cut.** Cease new perpetual sales for new logos; initiate "Cloud Migration Incentive."
*   **Months 3–9:** **Feature Wedge.** Roll out SaaS-only flagship features; activate "Double-Dip" sales commissions.
*   **Months 9–15:** **Conversion Sprint.** Aggressive migration campaigns utilizing "Migration Kickers" on all expiring maintenance contracts.
*   **Months 15–18:** **Sunset.** Official sunset of perpetual support and upgrades; full transition to subscription-only operations.

---

### **Conclusion**
This transition is not merely a pricing change; it is a fundamental maturation of our business model. By neutralizing sales resistance, incentivizing our existing customer base with superior value, and providing full financial transparency to the board and investors, we will emerge as a predictable, high-growth, subscription-first enterprise. 

**We request the Board’s endorsement of this 18-month roadmap and the proposed sales compensation bridge to ensure a swift and decisive execution.**
