# PERPETUAL-TO-SUBSCRIPTION TRANSITION PLAN
## Executive-Ready, Board-Approved Blueprint

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Strategic Objective
Migrate a $500M perpetual + upgrade revenue base to SaaS subscription within 36 months, accepting a controlled 15–18% YoY revenue trough in Year 2 to unlock 2–3x investor valuation premium (8–12x ARR multiples vs. 3–4x perpetual), NRR >110%, and sustainable land-and-expand motion.

### The Win Condition
- **70%+ installed-base adoption of subscriptions by Month 24**
- **NRR >110%** to offset 30% non-convertible churn
- **YoY revenue growth resumed by Month 36+**
- **Cash position sustained** through trough via prepay acceleration ($130M+ billings frontload)

### The Trough: Depth, Duration, Duration, and Mitigation
- **Depth:** 15–18% YoY revenue decline (Year 2, Q5–Q8 annualized)
  - Perpetual-only decline: 34–38% (expected, unavoidable)
  - Subscription offset: Grows from $0 → $48M/quarter by peak trough
- **Duration:** 4 quarters (Q7–Q10); full recovery to baseline ARR by Month 36–40
- **Cash Mitigation:** Multi-year prepay deals (30% of bookings) accelerate billings 60–75%, generating $130M+ cash during trough
- **Liquidity Requirement:** Lock $180–220M reserve; establish board-approved tripwires for intervention

### Five Critical Decisions Requiring Board Approval

| **Decision** | **Recommendation** | **Risk Mitigation** |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Accept 15–18% trough & front-load cash via multi-year prepay?** | YES | Lock liquidity reserve; publish monthly "Trough & Recovery" dashboard; establish tripwire triggers for contingency activation. |
| **2. Set blended subscription ASP at $22K; replace credit-based incentives with 3-year price locks?** | YES | Eliminates Month-12 price-shock churn; recovers $62M margin vs. original $96M credit budget; simplifies ops. |
| **3. Offer Legacy Maintenance tier ($10K→$12.1K/yr, escalating, sunset Month 30) for non-converting 25–30% of base?** | YES | Captures revenue tail; enables graceful off-ramp; reduces churn cliff from 20%+ to 25–30% (acceptable). |

## SECTION 1: FIVE BOARD DECISIONS (DECISION REGISTER)

### Decision 1: Revenue Trough Acceptance & Cash Strategy

**Decision:** Accept 15–18% YoY revenue trough in Year 2 (Q5–Q8, measured annualized) as inevitable trade-off for unlocking subscription valuation premium. Front-load cash via multi-year prepay deals.

**Rationale:**
- Perpetual amortization cliff is unavoidable: $80M/Q → $52.5M/Q (Q0–Q8).
- Subscription growth ($0 → $48M/Q) offsets 50% of decline.
- Competitor who tried to avoid trough via aggressive pricing actually extended trough to 8+ quarters and missed 110% NRR.
- Multi-year prepay deals accelerate billings: $176M migration ARR × 30% prepay rate × 2.5yr term = $132M billings frontload.
- **Net result:** Cash flow positive during trough; billings acceleration masks revenue trough in cash-flow narrative.

**Key Assumptions (Illustrative):**
- Perpetual base: 10,000 licenses at $50K average ASP = $500M installed base value
- Subscription adoption: 70% by Month 24 (7,000 licenses)
- Multi-year prepay rate: 30% of new bookings
- Gross retention (subscription): 95% Year 1, 96% Year 2
- NRR trajectory: 105% Year 1 → 110% Year 2 → 112% Year 3+

**Financial Impact:**
| **Metric** | **Year 1** | **Year 2** | **Year 3** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue (GAAP) | $428M | $384M (−10%) | $420M (9% recovery) |
| Billings (Cash) | $520M | $510M | $475M |
| Operating Income | $51M | $38M | $62M |
| Free Cash Flow | $68M | $95M | $112M |
| Cash Position | $280M (starting) | $375M | $487M |

**Board Authority & Conditions:**
- **Authority:** CFO + CEO to manage liquidity; does not require board waiver for quarterly variances ±3%.
- **Tripwire for Board Intervention:** If YoY revenue decline exceeds 22% (vs. 15–18% plan), or cash burn exceeds $50M annually, activate contingency playbook (see Decision 5).
- **Reporting:** Monthly "Trough & Recovery" board dashboard (format provided in Section 8).

**Decision Owner:** CFO  
**Approval Deadline:** Board meeting Month 0, Week 1  
**Dial-Back Trigger:** If cash on-hand falls below $100M or YoY revenue decline hits 22%+, board convenes within 5 business days for contingency review.

### Decision 2: Subscription Pricing & Customer Payback Curve

**Decision:** Set blended subscription ASP at $22,000 (Professional mid-market tier). Replace complex honor-the-investment credits with transparent 3-year price locks (2% annual escalation Years 1–3, then market escalation 5%+). Achieve customer payback by Year 2.7 (maintenance renewers) to Year 3.2 (perpetual replacement buyers).

**Pricing Ladder (Annual, Per Seat):**

| **Tier** | **List Price** | **Target Segment** | **Discount Ladder (Volume)** | **Support SLA** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | $10,000 | SMB (1–5 seats), new logos | 0% (no volume discount) | 24-hr response, 8/5 hours |
| **Professional** | $22,000 | Mid-market (6–50 seats) | −5% to −10% @ 6–100 seats | 4-hr response, 24/7 |
| **Enterprise** | $38,000 | Large enterprise (51+ seats) | −12% to −15% @ 100–250+ seats | 1-hr response, 24/7 + TAM |
| **Legacy Maintenance** | $10K→$12.1K/yr | Perpetual holdouts | N/A (escalates 10%/yr) | Degraded support, sunset Month 30 |

**Price-Lock Model (Alternative to Credits):**

| **Cohort** | **Migration Window** | **Annual Price** | **Lock Duration** | **Escalation Years 2–3** | **Effective 3-Yr Cost** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cohort 1 (Early)** | T0–T+6 mo | $22,000 | 3 years | 2% annual capped | $66,440 (vs. perpetual+maint $145K; saves $78.56K, 54% savings) |
| **Cohort 2 (Standard)** | T+6–T+12 mo | $22,000 | 2 years | 3% Year 2–3 | $72,400 (saves $44.6K, 38%) |
| **Cohort 3 (Late)** | T+12–T+18 mo | $22,000 | 1 year | Market escalation (5%+) | $71,200 (saves $45.8K, 39%) |

**Key Assumptions (Illustrative):**
- Perpetual ASP: $50,000
- Maintenance renewal rate: 75% of perpetual ASP = $10,000/year
- 3-year customer-payback target: Recover perpetual + 1.5–2 years maintenance via subscription
- Blended ASP weighting: 20% SMB ($10K) + 60% mid-market ($22K) + 20% enterprise ($38K) = $22,000

**Financial Impact:**
| **Cohort** | **Volume (Customers)** | **Year 1 ARR (Discounted)** | **Year 2 ARR (Standard)** | **Year 3 ARR** | **3-Year Cumulative Revenue** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cohort 1** | 1,600 | $28.6M | $33.1M | $35.8M | $97.5M |
| **Cohort 2** | 2,500 | $44.0M | $50.6M | $53.1M | $147.7M |
| **Cohort 3** | 1,000 | $17.6M | $20.2M | $21.2M | $59.0M |
| **Total (70% adoption)** | **5,100** | **$90.2M** | **$104M** | **$110M** | **$304.2M** |

**Board Authority & Conditions:**
- **Authority:** VP Sales has discretion to offer volume discounts up to −15% (within ladder) for named accounts; requires CFO approval if discount >−15%.
- **Price Increases Post-Lock:** Year 4+ pricing follows market escalation (5%+/year); no board approval required unless escalation >8%.
- **Tripwire for Board Review:** If <65% of customers accept subscription at list price (vs. 85% target), activate pricing contingency: reduce ASP 10–15%, draw from $5M contingency budget.

**Decision Owner:** CFO + VP Sales  
**Approval Deadline:** Month 0, Week 1  
**Dial-Back Trigger:** If adoption rate falls below 55% by Month 10, reduce pricing by 10–15%; board reviews impact on Year 2 margins.

### Decision 3: Migration Incentive Structure & Fairness Narrative

**Decision:** Replace complex honor-the-investment credit model with transparent price locks + permanent feature access + outcome-based success guarantees. Offer Legacy Maintenance tier for holdouts (escalating $10K→$12.1K/yr, sunset Month 30).

**Migration Offer Architecture:**

| **Cohort** | **Price Lock Mechanism** | **Feature Sweetener** | **Success Guarantee** | **Maintenance Conversion Offer** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cohort 1 (Early)** | 3-year cap @ $22K/yr + 2% escalation | Professional tier free (permanent) | 90-day free return | Auto-convert maintenance renewers @ $16.7K Y1, $22K Y2+ |
| **Cohort 2 (Standard)** | 2-year cap @ $22K/yr + 3% escalation Y2–3 | Professional tier free (12 mo) | 60-day free return | Same as Cohort 1 |
| **Cohort 3 (Late)** | 1-year cap @ $22K/yr | Professional tier @ −20% Y1 | 30-day free return | Same as Cohort 1 |
| **Non-Migrators (Holdouts)** | Legacy Maintenance @ $10K/yr escalating 10%/yr | N/A | N/A | Automatic transition Month 18; support declines Month 25 |

**Fairness Narrative (Customer Conversation Template):**

*Situation: Executive customer claiming perpetual license is indefinite asset; resisting subscription.*

**Our Response:**
> "We invested heavily in perpetual licensing because it was the right model at the time. Today, continuous innovation and cloud hosting require a subscription model.
> 
> Here's what we're honoring: Your perpetual license was purchased [DATE] for [AMOUNT]. After [NUMBER] years of support and updates, you've received strong value. We're crediting you with [DISCOUNT %] of your remaining investment—equivalent to [NUMBER] months free on our subscription. This is more generous than industry precedent (Autodesk offered 50%, PTC 60%; we're at 65%).
> 
> Here's what you gain: Cloud hosting (99.5% uptime), security updates every month (not every 2–3 years), and new features every 6 months (instead of every 3+ years). Your cost breaks even in [PAYBACK YEARS] and drops [SAVINGS $] by Year 3.
> 
> Here's your options: (1) Migrate to subscription by [DEADLINE]; lock in pricing for [YEARS] years. (2) Use Legacy Maintenance tier for [NUMBER] months while you plan off-platform migration. (3) Continue running v4 on-premises (no vendor support; your responsibility).
> 
> Which path works for your business?"

**Key Assumptions (Illustrative):**
- Maintenance-renewal cohort: 7,500 customers; 40% convert to subscription by T+6 (3,000 customers)
- Non-renewing cohort: 2,000–2,500 customers; 70% migrate to Legacy Maintenance or churn
- Holdout rate: 25–30% of base (2,500–3,000 customers); not convertible; expected to churn or remain on Legacy Maintenance through Month 30

**Financial Impact:**

**Board Authority & Conditions:**
- **Authority:** VP Sales + CFO jointly approve any incentive over $10M in Year 1; board approval only if >$50M/quarter impact.

- **Tripwire for Board Review:** If <50% of Cohort 2 (standard adopters) accept subscription by Month 10, activate pricing contingency: offer Cohort 2 "late bonus" (upgrade Cohort 2 to Cohort 1 pricing for 30-day window).

**Decision Owner:** VP Sales + CFO  
**Approval Deadline:** Month 0, Week 1  
**Dial-Back Trigger:** If Cohort 1 adoption falls below 70% by Month 4, or Cohort 2 adoption falls below 60% by Month 10, convene board for contingency review within 5 business days.
