**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Strategic Turnaround Office  
**DATE:** October 26, 2023  
**SUBJECT:** Executive Assessment and Turnaround-or-Exit Plan: Industrial Services Unit

---

### 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Following three years of sustained losses, the Industrial Services unit is suffering from a fundamental business model failure—specifically, a "commodity trap" exacerbated by artificial internal cross-subsidization. The unit is currently not a viable business but rather a cost-center masquerading as a revenue-generator. This plan outlines a 12-month "Market-Validation" strategy. We will either transition the unit to an 8%+ EBIT margin model or execute a structured divestment.

### 2. DIAGNOSTIC FINDINGS
The persistent losses are **structural**, not execution-driven.
*   **The Commodity Trap:** The unit has lost its competitive edge, competing solely on price.
*   **Artificial Subsidization:** Internal group divisions have historically accessed services at "at-cost" rates, preventing the unit from achieving market-based pricing and obscuring true operational efficiency.
*   **Talent Attrition:** The "brain drain" is a rational market response by high-performers to an unsustainable business model, not a failure of HR programs.

### 3. THE 12-MONTH TURNAROUND PLAN (PHASED APPROACH)

| Phase | Duration | Primary Objective | Key Milestone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I: Triage** | Months 1–3 | **Market Realignment.** Remove all subsidies. | Enforcement of "Market-Plus" SLAs for internal divisions. |
| **II: Pruning** | Months 4–6 | **Portfolio Rationalization.** Exit low-margin, high-friction contracts. | Divestment of underutilized assets; exit bottom 20% of contracts. |
| **III: Rebound** | Months 7–9 | **Incentive Alignment.** Implement Phantom Equity for core talent. | Launch of high-margin "Service-as-a-Product" offerings. |
| **IV: Validation** | Months 10–12 | **Final Audit.** Performance assessment against Scorecard. | Final decision: Scalable Unit vs. Managed Exit. |

### 4. STRATEGIC REFORMS
*   **Service-Level Agreements (SLAs):** Effective immediately, the unit will operate under a "Shadow P&L." Any group division requiring services must pay a "Market-Plus" rate. If internal divisions refuse, the unit will be permitted to serve external customers who meet this benchmark.
*   **Phantom Equity Program:** To stem the talent drain, key managers will be issued Phantom Stock. Financial upside will be strictly tied to reaching the 8%+ EBIT threshold by Month 12, ensuring their interests are aligned with the Board’s objective.
*   **Asset Liquidation:** Underutilized equipment will be liquidated via auction or sale-leaseback, providing non-operating cash to fund the turnaround and reducing the depreciation burden.

### 5. EXIT VS. TURNAROUND CRITERIA: THE GO/NO-GO SCORECARD
To ensure an objective outcome at Month 12, we will utilize the following weighted scorecard. A score of **≥ 15 points** is required to justify continued operation.

| Metric | Threshold for Turnaround (3 pts) | Threshold for Divestment (1 pt) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EBIT Margin** | Sustained > 8% (3 months) | < 4% |
| **Client Concentration**| No client > 20% of revenue | Any client > 40% of revenue |
| **Service Model** | > 60% Recurring Revenue | > 70% Project/T&M |
| **Talent Retention** | Key turnover < 10% | Key turnover > 30% |
| **Market Relevance** | Unit services "Hard-to-Replace" | Commodity-based pricing |

### 6. RECOMMENDATION & NEXT STEPS
The Board must authorize the "Shadow P&L" immediately. Without the power to charge market rates to internal divisions and exit non-performing contracts, a turnaround is mathematically impossible. 

**Immediate Mandate:**
1.  **CFO Meeting:** Secure written agreement by Week 2 that all internal group demand will be paid at market rates.
2.  **Renegotiation:** Initiate price-correction notices to the bottom 20% of the customer portfolio by Week 3. 

**Conclusion:** We are moving away from reactive cost-cutting to a disciplined, market-validated business model. By Month 12, the unit will demonstrate a clear path to sustainable profit or be positioned for a clean, value-preserving divestment.
