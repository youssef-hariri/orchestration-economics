**PROJECT “RESTORATION”**
*Repositioning Strategy for Meridian Standard (Est. 1954)*

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Meridian Standard will not modernize. It will restore. Over 24 months, we will replace performative trend-chasing with a return to our 1954 founding specification: **durability over novelty, proof over promise, engineering over marketing.** By treating our 70-year heritage as counter-cultural authority rather than liability, we will earn relevance with under-35 consumers without alienating the loyal base that provides our cash flow and credibility. This is market defense against private-label and DTC erosion—not a vanity pursuit of youth.

---

### 1. POSITIONING: THE ORIGINAL STANDARD

**The Patina Paradigm**
Under-35 consumers are not buying our past; they are pre-buying their own future credibility. They want objects that accumulate *their* story over decades. Heritage is not nostalgia—it is **future-proofing**.

**The Dual-Legitimacy Exchange**
We reject the false choice of “passing the torch.” The brand is a bridge, not a baton. Loyalists lend continuity; under-35s lend cultural energy. The relationship is additive. Never frame this as a generational handoff.

**Heritage Pivot**
Meridian Standard was founded by an engineer who rejected post-war planned obsolescence. We issued the **original specification for permanence.** We are not “vintage” (retired); we are the original anti-fast-consumerism choice—an accidental radical that was practicing longevity before it became an aesthetic.

**Positioning Statement**
*For consumers who treat every purchase as a long-term infrastructure decision, Meridian Standard is the original [category] engineered to a specification that outlasts trends. While others market values they have not yet practiced, our seventy-year proof-of-durability makes us, by accident, the most radical choice you can make today.*

**Resonance Hook for Under-35s**
Frame the brand as **Primary Source** material. This language is native to digitally fluent consumers who research before buying. You are the uncitable source that every challenger is trying to approximate.

### 2. PRESERVE VS. RETIRE

| Element | Verdict | Executive Action |
|---------|---------|------------------|
| **Name** | **PRESERVE** | Seventy years of unaided awareness is compound interest. Non-negotiable. |
| **Primary Logo Wordmark** | **PRESERVE & PROTECT** | Trust mark remains untouched. Introduce a **“Quiet Mark”** variant (debossed/stamped, single-color) for premium contexts only. |
| **Heritage Seal (“Est. 1954”)** | **PRESERVE & ELEVATE** | Move from fine print to front-of-pack. Treat it as a **technical certification/provenance stamp**, not a nostalgia badge. |
| **Primary Brand Color** | **PRESERVE** | Deep navy/barn red is the flag. Own it without apology. |
| **Core Hero SKUs** | **PRESERVE** | Zero formulation changes. Add a printed **“Specification Card”** to packaging showing original tolerances, material weights, or batch tests—translating heritage into engineering proof. |
| **Tagline** | **RETIRE** | Kill “Quality You Can Trust.” **Do not** replace it with “Still the Standard” (*still* signals defensiveness). Adopt **“Built to the Specification”** or eliminate taglines entirely. Silence signals confidence. |
| **Packaging Photography** | **RETIRE** | Eliminate 1990s “aspirational family table” imagery. Replace with **tactile, macro, forensic photography** showing material grain and physical integrity. **Guardrail:** No artful workshop grit or rustic theater. |
| **Packaging Architecture** | **RESTAGE** | Deploy **“Technical Heritage”** minimalism: engineering-manual hierarchy, generous negative space, calibration-stamp treatment of heritage marks. Ban faux-distressed textures. |
| **Tone of Voice** | **RESTAGE** | Adopt the **“Chief Engineer”** voice: obsessive about specifications, generous with process detail, allergic to superlatives. Never say “We’re back” or “Now for a new generation.” |
| **Line Extensions** | **RETIRE / REPLACE** | Kill inauthentic “Extreme/Blast” SKUs. Launch permanent **“Archive Editions”** using original specs/tooling. Position as *“always available to those who require the original standard,”* not limited drops. |
| **Spokesperson / Mascot** | **REDUCE** | Do not erase—erasure signals embarrassment. Reduce to **hallmark scale**: small, dignified, present but not dominant. Retain in loyalty mailers and archive content. |

### 3. CHANNEL & CONTENT APPROACH: THE DUAL-LEGITIMACY MODEL

We reject segregated channels by age. We deploy **shared channels with bifurcated creative tonality**, plus specific outposts for each cohort’s discovery behaviors.

**A. Continuity Track: The Loyal Base (65+)**
- **Traditional grocery / mass retail:** Maintain shelf placement, facings, and promotional rhythm without disruption.
- **Sunday FSIs and direct mail:** Still the highest ROI for this segment. **Do not cut** to fund digital channels; doing so signals abandonment.
- **Email newsletters:** Recipe- and routine-forward. Stable design templates. No sudden rebranded layouts that force loyalists to re-learn the visual system.
- **Custodian Club:** Launch a loyalty subscription or 24-month price lock on core SKUs. Convert the base from passive buyers into sworn stakeholders *before* visual changes reach them.

**B. Discovery Track: Under-35**
- **TikTok / Instagram / YouTube Shorts:** Not for brand documentaries. Deploy **creator-led process access** only:
  - Partner with restoration, “buy-it-for-life,” and factory-ASMR creators.
  - Grant unsupervised access to original tooling, factory veterans, and spec sheets.
  - **Hard rules:** No brand voiceover. No logo in the first three seconds. No scripts. The brand is the patron; the creator is the director.
- **Podcast sponsorships:** Target history of capitalism, industrial design, and labor history podcasts. Read in the “Chief Engineer” voice, never a hype voice.
- **Retail media networks:** Sponsored search on Instacart, Amazon, and Target.com to capture intent.
- **Selective premium brick-and-mortar:** Place **Archive Editions** in Whole Foods, specialty hardware, or design stores. Keep core lines in traditional grocery to prevent price-perception contamination.

**C. Bridge Content (Both Audiences)**
- **The Pack as Primary Content:** Embed the origin story in packaging copy—short, factual, specification-oriented. Do not outsource the brand’s proof to a screen.
- **QR Code:** **Utility-first only.** Link to warranty registration, repair manuals, or ingredient traceability. **Never** link to a brand film or manifesto.
- **The Specification Project:** A content series comparing original 1954 engineering specs with modern challenger failures. Frame as consumer education, not boasting.
- **#MyMeridian UGC Engine:** Invite loyalists to share decades-old product still in use. Invite younger consumers to share their **“first Meridian”** purchase. Curate both into a single gallery: **“70 Years of First Purchases.”**

**D. Messaging Tone by Audience**
- **Loyalists:** *“You kept the specification alive.”* Gratitude-based, continuity-focused. They are the protagonists.
- **Under-35:** *“Primary source materials.”* Discovery-based, proof-focused. They are the researchers.

### 4. TRANSITION SEQUENCING: RESTORATION, NOT REINVENTION

No change may look like panic. We build proof points before touching the package, and frame every external change as a *return* to the founder’s intent.

**Phase 1: Internal Alignment & Story Restoration (Months 1–6)**
*Goal: Shift perception without touching the product.*
- **Heritage Council:** Convene long-tenured employees, factory veterans, and 20 loyal customers to certify what is **Sacred** (name, logo, core formula, primary color) versus **Flexible** (photography, tone, extensions). Publish internally.
- **Youth Veto Cell:** Add five under-35 non-customers with unilateral kill authority over any concept reading as “how do you do, fellow kids.”
- **Digital soft launch:** Release *The Specification Project* short-form series on social/pre-roll. Test under-35 engagement.
- **Soft product test:** Launch one **Archive Edition** SKU online only. Measure new buyer pull-through vs. core cannibalization.
- **Loyalist promise:** Direct-mail *Anniversary Archive* booklet with explicit guarantee: *“The product you rely on is not changing. The story around it is simply being told more clearly.”*

**Phase 2: Packaging Evolution (Months 7–12)**
*Goal: Modernize the visual system while preserving recognition and containing risk.*
- **Go/No-Go Gate:** Proceed only if Phase 1 loyalist NPS holds steady and Youth Veto Cell clears creative.
- **Regional test:** Roll out **Technical Heritage** packaging in **one test region** on **secondary SKUs first**. If loyalists in Region X revolt, you have data, not a national crisis.
- **Untouchables:** Logo, primary color, and heritage seal remain completely unchanged. Shift photography style, typography hierarchy, and negative space only.
- Introduce the **Specification Card** on all packs.
- Launch **Archive Editions** in premium retail; keep core lines in traditional grocery.
- **Loyalist safeguard:** Run a **“Classic Label”** holiday promotion allowing loyalists to request throwback labels. Archival respect, not nostalgia bait.

**Phase 3: Cultural Proof (Months 13–18)**
*Goal: Prove modern relevance through alignment, not advertising claims.*
- **Go/No-Go Gate:** Scale to hero SKUs nationally only if regional loyalist retention exceeds 98% and under-35 engagement hits target.
- **One high-integrity collaboration:** Partner with a functional heritage brand (e.g., toolmaker, canvas goods maker) on a utility object that solves a problem. **Guardrail:** No trendy DTC lifestyle partners. Borrowing borrowed credibility triggers the exact backlash this plan prevents.
- Scale packaging redesign to hero SKUs.
- Retail activation: **“Specification Station”** endcaps displaying original 1954 tolerances alongside modern product.
- **Media:** Shift to streaming/podcast creative using creator footage and factory audio, not actors.

**Phase 4: Institutionalization (Months 19–24)**
*Goal: Lock in the new equilibrium.*
- Portfolio purge: Cut remaining inauthentic line extensions. Expand Archive Editions if velocity supports.
- Formalize **dual-track media planning**: one brand strategy, two execution tracks (continuity vs. discovery).
- **Values Filter:** All future innovation must answer:
  1. *Does this prioritize durability over novelty?*
  2. *Does this increase transparency without performative marketing?*
  3. *Would this compromise the trust of a customer who bought us in 1984?*

### 5. RISK MITIGATION

**Authenticity & Creative Risk**
- **Photography mandate:** Forensic honesty only. Every shot is approved against a “no theater” standard; ban artful workshop grit that reads as gentrification aesthetic.
- **Packaging discipline:** Technical Heritage means engineering minimalism. Faux-distressed textures and yellowed-paper effects are prohibited.
- **Collaboration guardrail:** Partner only with brands that make or repair things. If the collaboration signifies a *vibe* rather than solving a problem, it is vetoed.

**Governance & Internal Risk**
- **Language control:** Ban the word “reinvention.” All briefs must use “restoration,” “reclamation,” or “translation.”
- **Evidence over ego:** Pre-test all packaging and messaging simultaneously with 65+ and 25–34 cells. Shared preference breaks stalemates.
- **Deadlock protocol:** If the Heritage Council splits on a Flexible element, escalation goes to the CEO with a 48-hour decision mandate. The Youth Veto Cell has unilateral kill power but not approval power.
- **Incentive alignment:** Tie leadership bonuses 50/50 to growth metrics (under-35 share) and safeguard metrics (loyalist NPS, retention, Custodian Club churn).

**Operational & Financial Risk**
- **Budget protection:** Continuity Track spend (FSIs, direct mail, Custodian Club) is protected for 24 months. Discovery Track scales only after safeguards are funded.
- **Creator supply risk:** Vet creators for brand safety before granting factory access. Execute NDAs and IP protocols. Maintain a roster of three verified creators per platform to avoid single-point-of-failure.
- **Abort criteria:**
  - If loyalist NPS drops >5 points in the Phase 2 test region, halt packaging rollout immediately.
  - If Archive Edition velocity is <50% of target per door after 90 days, revise assortment before Phase 3.
  - If earned media sentiment ratio (authentic/try-hard) falls below 2:1, pause Discovery Track spend and audit creative.

| Metric | Role | Target |
|--------|------|--------|
| Under-35 Purchase Share | Primary Growth | Increase quarterly |
| Loyalist Retention / NPS | Safeguard | Hold steady or improve |
| Custodian Club Annual Churn | Safeguard | <5% |
| Earned Media Sentiment (Authentic vs. Try-Hard) | Authenticity Pulse | >3:1 |
| Archive Edition Velocity per Door | Innovation | ≥ Core SKU velocity in premium channel |
| Creator Engagement vs. Branded Content | Culture | 2x+ outperform |

### BOTTOM LINE

Meridian Standard’s vulnerability is not its age; it is the appearance of embarrassment about its age. The antidote is not modernization but **restored nerve.** By returning to the original specification—durability over novelty, engineering over marketing, proof over promise—we make the brand accidental food for a culture starving for permanence. The loyal base is protected not by keeping everything the same, but by proving that what made the brand worthy of their loyalty in 1984 is the exact same thing that makes it radical in 2024. Authenticity is not a campaign. It is the discipline of refusing to chase relevance, and thereby becoming the thing that relevance chases.
