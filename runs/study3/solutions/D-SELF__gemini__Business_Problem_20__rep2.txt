# Executive Summary: Project Velocity
## Strategy for Operational Excellence and Lead Time Reduction

**To:** Executive Leadership  
**Subject:** Operational Transformation Plan (14-Week to 6-Week Lead Time)

### 1. The Core Objective
Our current 14-week lead time is a "Management Buffer"—an artificial inflation created to compensate for process instability and organizational silos. **Project Velocity** will transition the business from a reactive, batch-based system to a streamlined, "Pull" flow model, reducing lead times to 6 weeks while improving, rather than sacrificing, quality standards.

### 2. Strategic Pillars

#### I. Operational Flow (The "Flow" Framework)
*   **WIP Capping:** We will transition from batch production to Takt-based flow. By capping Work-in-Progress (WIP) on the shop floor, we force the resolution of bottlenecks immediately rather than masking them with excess inventory.
*   **The Dual-Queue System:** To protect quality, we will isolate the existing 14-week "Legacy Backlog" from the new "Velocity Flow" (6-week) orders. This prevents legacy inefficiencies from contaminating the new high-velocity workflow.
*   **Inventory Decoupling:** We will implement a Kanban-based Vendor-Managed Inventory (VMI) for long-lead items. We will move to an "Assemble-to-Order" model, stocking standardized sub-assemblies to shrink the final assembly window.

#### II. Quality Assurance (Built-in, Not Added-On)
*   **Digital Traceability:** We are abandoning manual checklists. All quality inspections must be digitally logged at the source. If a digital entry is not recorded, the product cannot move to the next station.
*   **Quality Veto Authority:** Quality is no longer a final "gate" but an integrated process. Inspectors hold absolute authority to halt production on a specific line if a defect is identified; this is treated as a safety-level incident, ensuring management visibility and immediate root-cause correction.

#### III. Cross-Functional Alignment (The "Integrated Operations Council")
*   **Shared Metrics:** Departmental silos will be dismantled. Performance will be measured by **Order-to-Cash Cycle Time** and **First-Pass Yield**, creating collective ownership of delivery speed and product integrity.
*   **Capacity-Linked Compensation:** Sales incentives will be adjusted to reflect **On-Time Delivery (OTD)**. Selling beyond verified capacity will no longer be rewarded, aligning Sales with the realistic output of the shop floor.

### 3. Implementation Roadmap

| Phase | Focus | Key Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Diagnostic** | Baseline Assessment | Value Stream Mapping; establish real-time dashboard. |
| **Phase 2: Pilot Flow** | Velocity Launch | Launch "Velocity Queue" on one product line. |
| **Phase 3: Integration** | Supplier Sync | Implement VMI and Kanban for long-lead parts. |
| **Phase 4: Full Scale** | Scaling & Review | Transition all product lines to the Velocity model. |

### 4. Risk Mitigation & Warranty Guardrails
To prevent a repeat of the previous warranty spikes, we are implementing:
1.  **Graduated Velocity:** Lead-time targets will be reduced by 2 weeks per quarter rather than an immediate jump, allowing processes to stabilize.
2.  **"Golden Sample" Validation:** No process change will be finalized until a first-article inspection meets 100% of certified standards.
3.  **Automated Root Cause (A3):** Any warranty claim triggers an immediate executive review, ensuring that quality failures are treated as systemic issues requiring process changes, not individual blame.

### 5. Conclusion
The feud between Sales, Production, and Quality has cost us market share and reputation. By replacing "padding" with "process" and aligning incentives across the entire value chain, we will move to a 6-week lead time that is not only faster but demonstrably higher in quality. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with Phase 1 (Diagnostics) effective immediately.
