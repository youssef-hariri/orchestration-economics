# Executive Report: Operational Lead-Time Reduction Initiative

**To:** Executive Steering Committee (Sales, Production, Quality)  
**From:** Operations Strategy Group  
**Subject:** Transitioning from 14-Week to 6-Week Lead Times

---

### 1. Executive Summary
Our current 14-week lead time is dominated by "non-value-added" waiting periods, not a lack of production capacity. This report outlines a transition to a **Synchronous Flow Model**, designed to reduce lead times to 6 weeks while simultaneously increasing quality assurance. By replacing our siloed processes with integrated, data-driven "Value-Stream Pods," we will eliminate the administrative and logistical "dead time" that currently compromises our competitiveness.

### 2. The Core Problem: Where Time is Lost
Current diagnostics reveal that the product is actively being worked on for only 2–3 weeks. The remaining 11+ weeks are consumed by:
*   **Inventory Queuing:** Batch-and-queue production methods creating massive work-in-progress (WIP) piles.
*   **Functional Silos:** Handoff delays between Sales, Procurement, Quality, and Production.
*   **Reactive Quality:** Inspection-based quality management that catches errors only after they occur, necessitating costly re-work.

### 3. The Operational Improvement Plan

#### A. Structural Optimization (The Flow Model)
*   **Cellular Manufacturing:** We are transitioning from department-based work centers to product-focused cells. This keeps the product moving, eliminates logistical transit time, and allows for immediate identification of issues.
*   **Drum-Buffer-Rope (DBR) Scheduling:** The entire plant will now operate at the pace of our "Drum" (the primary constraint/bottleneck). We will implement precise "Buffers" in front of this constraint to ensure it never starves, stabilizing the entire throughput.

#### B. Supply Chain & Procurement
*   **Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI):** We will move long-lead "A-items" to a Kanban-based procurement model. Suppliers will hold pre-certified safety stock on-site, allowing us to decouple procurement from customer order dates.

#### C. Built-In Quality (The "Double-Lock" Protocol)
*   **In-Process Quality Control (IPQC):** Quality is no longer a final hurdle; it is a step-by-step verification. 
*   **Digital Travelers:** We are deploying a mandatory, ERP-integrated digital sign-off system. If a quality gate is not verified, the unit is system-locked and cannot move to the next station. This removes human error and "rushing" as a possibility.

### 4. Cross-Functional Alignment & Accountability
To resolve the historical friction between departments, we are shifting from **SLA-based goals** to **Systemic Hard-Gates**:

| Department | Primary Focus | Success Metric |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sales** | Configuration Accuracy | "Clean Order" Rate |
| **Production** | Flow Management | First-Pass Yield (FPY) & Drum Utilization |
| **Quality** | Process Auditing | Warranty/Lead-Time Correlation Index |

*   **Integrated Pods:** Quality and Procurement representatives will be physically embedded into production cells, moving from "auditors" to "stakeholders."
*   **The "Evidence First" Rule:** Weekly cross-functional reviews will be governed by data from the Digital Traveler logs. Opinion-based blame is replaced by system-verified logs.

### 5. Risk Mitigation: Protecting Quality
To ensure we do not repeat the warranty spike of two years ago, we have established a **Warranty/Lead-Time Correlation Index**. 
*   If warranty claims rise above the baseline, **lead-time reduction targets are automatically frozen.** 
*   This makes it mathematically impossible for any department to prioritize speed at the expense of quality, as their incentives are tied directly to both.

### 6. Implementation Roadmap (16-Week Phase-In)

*   **Weeks 1–4 (Alignment):** VSM mapping and establishment of "Value-Stream Pods."
*   **Weeks 5–8 (Supply Chain):** Implementation of Kanban and onsite safety stock.
*   **Weeks 9–12 (Pilot):** Transitioning a single line to Cellular Manufacturing with IPQC.
*   **Weeks 13–16 (Stability):** Full system rollout, monitoring FPY and system-locked digital gates.

### 7. Conclusion
A 6-week lead time is not a request for staff to "work faster"; it is a mandate to "work smarter" by removing the 11 weeks of waste currently paralyzing our operations. Through the **Double-Lock Quality Protocol**, we are effectively hardening our quality standards while drastically increasing our velocity. 

**Next Steps:** Immediate approval of the cross-functional "Value-Stream Pod" structure to commence the Weeks 1–4 diagnostic phase.
