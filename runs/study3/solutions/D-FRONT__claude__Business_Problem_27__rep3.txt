## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### The Recommendation

**Deploy a staged hybrid capital stack of $6.5M over 12 months, combining bank debt, revenue-based financing, venture debt, and conditional minority equity—structured to defend competitive market share while preserving founder control above 70% ownership.**

- **Tranche 1 (Months 0–2):** $2.5M senior bank debt @ 6.5% + $0.8M RBF → funds immediate capacity expansion.
- **Tranche 2 (Months 4–6, conditional):** $1.5M venture debt @ 10% + 4% warrant coverage → unlocks if revenue reaches $24M ARR.
- **Tranche 3 (Months 9–12, conditional):** $1.7M minority equity @ $225M pre-money (7.1% stake) → deployed only if revenue reaches $28M ARR and competitive threat persists.

### Headline Structure & Outcomes

| Metric | Today | Post-Financing | Exit (Year 5) |
|--------|-------|----------------|---------------|
| **Founder Ownership** | 78% | 70.9% (if all tranches deploy) | ~50% (accounting for Series C/D) |
| **Total Capital Deployed** | — | $6.5M | — |

### Expected Founder Ownership Range

- **Conservative (Tranche 3 skipped):** 78% throughout Series A; 55–60% by Series C.
- **Expected (all tranches deployed):** 70.9% through Series B; 50–55% by exit.
- **Upside (aggressive growth, Tranche 3 declined):** 78% retained; higher exit multiple offsets lower ownership %.

### Single Biggest Risk

**Covenant breach in months 6–12 if revenue growth stalls below 35% YoY due to competitive pressure or execution miss.** Contractual mitigation: covenant holiday (months 0–6), quarterly (not monthly) testing, and capped amendment fees ($250 + max 1% rate increase). If breach occurs, forced equity raise at lower valuation ($120–150M pre vs. $225M plan) compresses founder ownership below 65%.

## RECOMMENDED FUNDING STRUCTURE

**Instrument 1A: Senior Secured Bank Debt**

| Term | Specification |
|------|---------------|
| **Lender** | Relationship regional bank (SVB, Mercury, Umpqua) or fintech lender (Lighter Capital). Run parallel discussions with 3 lenders; close with best terms. |
| **Amount** | $2.5M |
| **Coupon** | 6.5% (SOFR + 450 bps). Target: founders negotiate down from bank's opening 7.5–8.5%. Leverage multiple lenders to achieve 6.5%. |
| **Tenor** | 4 years (48-month amortization). Interest-only months 0–6, then principal + interest. Monthly debt service (months 6+): $111K ($59K interest + $52K principal). Annual debt service: $1.33M. |
| **Collateral** | First lien on AR, equipment, cash (sweep if DSCR <1.75x). No personal guarantees (corporate-only). If lender insists, cap founder guarantee at $250K (forfeitable on exit). |
| **Covenants (Founder-Protective)** | ✓ **Minimum DSCR:** 1.15x (quarterly testing, not monthly). Holiday: months 0–6 (no DSCR/leverage testing during capex deployment). ✓ **Maximum Leverage:** 2.0x (debt / EBITDA). ✓ **Minimum Cash:** $300K. ✓ **Capex Cap:** $2.5M/year OR 2.0x prior year (whichever lower); exceptions up to $3M/year for competitive hiring (mutual consent, 5-day approval window). ✓ **Debt Sublimit:** No additional debt >$1M without lender consent. **Pre-approved carve-out: Tranche 2 venture debt up to $1.5M is explicitly waived.** ✓ **Amendment Fee Cap:** 0.25% (non-negotiable per amendment instance). Rate increase cap: +1% (temporary, sunsets after 2 quarters of compliance). |
| **Reporting** | Quarterly unaudited (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow). Monthly AR reporting (for sweep triggers). Annual audit. |
| **Walk-Away Thresholds** | If lender insists on: coupon >8.5%, DSCR >1.35x, leverage >2.5x, capex cap <$1.5M, unlimited personal guarantees, or >0.5% amendment fees → **walk and approach Lender #2.** If second lender also exceeds thresholds, pivot to RBF-only + venture debt strategy (decline bank debt). |

**Instrument 1B: Revenue-Based Financing ($0.8M)**

| Term | Specification |
|------|---------------|
| **Provider** | Specialized RBF platform (Lighter Capital, Clearco, Pipe). Non-negotiable: no bank-like covenants. |
| **Amount** | $0.8M (bridges working capital, 3-month revenue float, hiring payroll timing). |
| **Repayment** | 8% of monthly revenue until $1.92M repaid (2.4x multiple). No interest; revenue share only. |
| **Term** | 36 months (or until repaid, whichever sooner). At 40% growth, repaid in ~24 months. |
| **All-In Cost** | $1.12M total repayment (2.4x). Effective IRR ~32% (expensive, but non-dilutive and risk-aligned). |
| **Provisions** | Monthly AR reporting. If revenue declines >30% YoY, repayment % rises to 10% (lender's protection). No personal guarantees; simple UCC filing. If revenue stalls, repayment shrinks proportionally (founder-friendly). |
| **Walk-Away Triggers** | If RBF provider insists on >10% repayment %, personal guarantees, daily reporting, or escalation to >12% if growth <20% YoY → **decline RBF and rely on bank debt + internal cash only.** RBF is optional; accept only if terms are founder-friendly. |

**Instrument 2: Venture Debt with Warrants**

| Term | Specification |
|------|---------------|

| **Amount** | $1.5M (accessed month 4, contingent on Tranche 1 milestones). |
