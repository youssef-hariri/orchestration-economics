## Executive Summary

We are requesting authorization for a **$46.0 million, multi-phase automation program** across five regional warehouses to counter 35% annual picker turnover, rising error-related chargebacks, and escalating labor costs. The program rejects “lights-out” big-bang deployment in favor of an **augment-first, replace-second** strategy that sequences automation by technical risk, business impact, and social license.

**Strategic Revisions from Prior Analysis**
- **Payback:** A **42-month blended network payback at 70% confidence**, supported by a stochastic Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) model. Waves 1–3 target 36 months; WH-Alpha is treated as a separate **48-month community-capital track** to avoid forcing premature workforce reductions at the network’s largest site and primary local employer.
- **Capital:** Funded through a dedicated corporate envelope, eliminating the “self-funding” fiction of using unrealized labor savings as collateral.
- **Labor:** A sized Automation Academy, a **$1.2 million per-site Surplus Labor Absorption Pool**, and pre-authorized Voluntary Separation Packages (VSPs) close the “transition chasm” between automation go-live and natural attrition.
- **Service:** **Shift-level circuit breakers**, pre-negotiated surge-staffing agreements, and mandatory fallback-in-a-box protocols ensure key-account service levels never depend on a 72-hour recovery window.

**Board Conditions Precedent**
Before Wave 1 capital is released, the following must be completed:
1. Independent leadership and cultural readiness assessment at WH-Beta.
2. Union/organized-labor due diligence across all five sites; labor-relations lead named if applicable.
3. Employee representative seated on the Automation Steering Committee.
4. 80% confidence Monte Carlo scenario delivered to the Board.
5. Named Organizational Change Management (OCM) lead and dedicated OCM budget.
6. Vendor reference checks against the competitor’s publicly failed deployment.

## 1. Phased Per-Site Automation Plan

Automation maturity progresses from **Assistive** (AMRs, voice/light) to **Systemic** (goods-to-person, auto-pack) to **Orchestrated** (AI-driven WES, predictive slotting). No site proceeds without clearing **Quality Gate 0**.

| Phase | Site | Profile | Automation Degree | Quality Gate 0 |
|-------|------|---------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Pilot** | **WH-Epsilon** (180k sq ft, newest, lowest headcount) | Low political risk; modern infrastructure | **Assistive:** AMRs for non-value-added transport; voice-picking; smart conveyance to pack stations. Eliminate walking; retain all pickers initially. | **Data Gate:** 100% SKU master-data accuracy (weight, cube, barcode). |
| **Wave 1** | **WH-Beta** (250k sq ft, urban, 40% turnover, highest error rate) | Strongest business case *after* process repair; urban labor market supports external redeployment | **Assistive + Partial Systemic:** GTP pods for top 20% SKUs; AMR replenishment; auto-boxing at pack-out. | **Stabilization Gate:** 90-day manual operational sprint to cut errors to <2% and audit slotting logic. *Independent leadership readiness assessment required.* |
| **Wave 2** | **WH-Gamma** (220k sq ft, aging facility) | Moderate volume; infrastructure constraints | **Standardized Systemic:** Replicate Beta model; predictive WES slotting; mezzanine light-directed pick modules. | **Structural Gate:** Engineering assessment of slab load, clear height, seismic, and fire suppression. $1.0M retrofit contingency budgeted. |
| **Wave 3** | **WH-Delta** (150k sq ft, highly seasonal) | Serves key accounts; 3x volume spike in Q4 | **Right-Sized Flex:** AMRs, voice, and auto-boxing sized for **85th percentile daily volume**; retain scalable temp pool for peak. | **Demand Gate:** Confirm 3-year demand forecast with key accounts. |
| **Wave 4** | **WH-Alpha** (400k sq ft, main employer in Town A, ~300 staff, highest revenue) | Highest sociopolitical risk; economic anchor for community | **Bifurcated Track:** <br>• *Track 1 (Months 18–24):* Inbound automation (dimensioners, receiving conveyance, sortation) displacing only non-tenured/contract roles. <br>• *Track 2 (Months 30–42):* Fulfillment automation (GTP, orchestrated outbound) **only if** attrition exceeds forecast and conditional compact triggers are met. | **Social Gate:** Independent economic impact study; signed **conditional** compact with Town A and state workforce board. |

**Sequencing Logic:** WH-Epsilon is the integration classroom; WH-Beta validates the business case; WH-Alpha is the capstone, protected by volume-linked commitments rather than absolute guarantees. A **synthetic control methodology** (using pre-automation data from Beta/Gamma) replaces the costly manual control-group experiment, freeing Alpha to begin Track 1 earlier without distorting ROI measurement.

## 2. Workforce Transition Plan

**Principle:** No involuntary separation for employees with >5 years tenure until Month 30, except for cause. The 35% annual turnover provides a natural glidepath, but automation creates step-function labor demand reductions that attrition alone cannot match.

### 2.1 The Automation Academy (Sized & Specced)
A 90-day, paid certification program with defined throughput constraints.

| Attribute | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Campuses** | 3 (co-located at WH-Epsilon, WH-Beta, WH-Alpha) |
| **Cohort Size** | 12 per campus |
| **Frequency** | New cohort every 3 weeks |
| **Annual Throughput** | ~180 graduates network-wide |
| **Pass Rate Target** | ≥80% first attempt |
| **Tracks** | AMR Technician (L1/L2), WES Operator, QA Auditor, Inventory Control, Customer Exceptions |
| **Certification Premium** | AMR Tech L2: **+$4.50/hr**; WES Operator: +$3.00/hr; QA/Inventory Control: +$1.50/hr |
| **Remedial Track** | Non-certifying trainees rotate into paid Inventory Control or Vendor Returns roles (+$0.75/hr); termination is not the default outcome. |

### 2.2 Closing the Transition Chasm
Automation go-live reduces labor demand immediately; attrition is continuous. To prevent structural overstaffing:
- **Surplus Labor Absorption Pool:** Budget **$1.2M per site** for a 90-day post-go-live bridge. Displaced pickers rotate into network data remediation, 5S audits, VAS pilots (where customer contracts are pre-secured), and quarterly manual-fallback drills.
- **Pre-Authorized VSPs:** If the absorption pool cannot clear excess labor within 90 days, deploy voluntary separation packages within 48 hours (6 months COBRA + outplacement + 3 weeks/year of service). Recent hires (<2 years) and lowest-performing employees (per legally vetted matrix) are eligible first.
- **Cross-Site Mobility:** Tenured staff (>5 years) have first right of refusal for Track 1 roles at WH-Alpha. Relocation stipend: **$4,000**, grossed up for tax.

### 2.3 Involuntary Reduction Protocol
External labor counsel validates the selection methodology in Month 1 and conducts a disparate-impact analysis before any site go-live.

| Cohort | Handling |
|--------|----------|
| **Tenured >5 years** | No involuntary separation unless role is eliminated *and* employee declines certified retraining or redeployment. If displaced: 4 weeks/year service + 12 months benefits + tuition/apprenticeship bridge. |
| **>20 years** | Personal career concierge; option for part-time mentor/trainer roles. |
| **Recent hires / non-tenured** | VSPs offered first; if involuntary, objective Performance & Skills Matrix (attendance, accuracy, training completion) governs selection. |

### 2.4 WH-Alpha Conditional Social Compact
- **Public Commitment:** No *net* reduction in regular full-time/part-time headcount for 18 months post-Track 1 go-live **provided** site volume remains ≥95% of baseline.
- **Reciprocal Triggers:** If volume declines >10%, or if automation efficiency exceeds 150% of baseline for 2 consecutive quarters, a jointly agreed Adjustment Protocol with the mayor and workforce board permits restructuring via attrition and redeployment only.
- **Contractor Transparency:** The compact explicitly discloses impacts to contractor roles; the community will not distinguish between FTE and local-contractor displacement.
- **Economic Impact Study:** Commissioned in Month 1 to quantify WH-Alpha’s role in Town A’s tax base and calibrate commitments.

## 3. Service Continuity Plan

The competitor’s nationally publicized failure stemmed from a big-bang cutover, immature integration, and no manual fallback. We adopt **parallel-running, zone-by-zone** cutovers with shift-level governance.

### 3.1 Shift-Level Circuit Breakers
| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| **Zone accuracy <98.5% for 2 consecutive hours** | Automatic zone pause. Pickers revert to voice/paper backup within 30 minutes. |
| **OTIF drops >1.0 percentage point below baseline for a full shift** | Site-wide yellow alert. Next shift begins in manual fallback mode until root cause is resolved. |
| **Customer chargeback event linked to automation** | Immediate manual verification and root-cause quarantine for the SKU family; automated path restored only after statistically confirmed resolution. |

### 3.2 Pre-Staged Fallback Capacity
- **Fallback-in-a-Box:** Every automated zone retains 48-hour manual capacity: rolling carts, paper pick lists, bypass lanes, and radio protocols.
- **Surge Staffing Agreements:** Pre-negotiated contracts with **3 regional staffing agencies** guarantee 48-hour injection of trained manual pickers during go-live or rollback events.
- **Quarterly Drills:** Fallback activation drills now include agency floaters to test real-world activation speed. Target: **<2 hours** from trigger to manual zone live by Month 36.

### 3.3 Inventory & Seasonal Protocols
- **Buffer Stock Economics:** Budget **$800K** for incremental carrying cost to pre-position **10 days** of safety stock at a regional 3PL during each site’s cutover. Cost is tracked against the site business case.
- **Seasonal Blackout:** No go-lives in Q4 (October–December). Hard stop.
- **Shadow Mode & FAT:** New WES/WCS runs in parallel with legacy WMS for 30 days. The system must process 100,000 simulated peak orders at **110% of design rate** during Factory Acceptance Testing before write-access is granted.

### Financial Truth Metrics
1. **Avoided Cost Ledger:** Hard, invoice-verified savings only—reduced overtime, avoided temp agency fees, reduced error chargebacks, lowered recruiting spend. No allocated corporate overhead savings permitted.
2. **Cash Flow per Site:** Cumulative site-level EBITDA contribution vs. pre-automation baseline. **Synthetic control methodology** isolates automation impact from market wage inflation.
3. **Rolling Payback Tracker:** Cumulative Capital Invested ÷ Cumulative Monthly Cash Savings. Must trend below the 42-month line by Month 30.
4. **TCO Burn Rate:** Actual OpEx vs. TCO reserve, reviewed monthly.
