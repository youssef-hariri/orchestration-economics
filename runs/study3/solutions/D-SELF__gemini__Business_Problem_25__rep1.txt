# Executive Strategy: Subscription Business Model Transition

## 1. Executive Summary
This document outlines the strategic pivot from a perpetual-license business model to a subscription-based (SaaS) model. The objective is to stabilize long-term revenue, increase customer lifetime value (LTV), and command higher market valuation multiples. To mitigate the "revenue trough" and churn risks identified in industry benchmarks, this plan employs a **Value-Converged Migration** approach, prioritizing predictable ARR growth over short-term transaction volume.

---

## 2. Migration & Customer Retention Strategy
We will move away from a voluntary, "soft-touch" migration to a structured, value-based transition.

*   **The Sunset Roadmap:** Perpetual licenses will be deprecated in stages. After 24 months, we will cease security and compliance updates for legacy versions, effectively forcing migration via "maintenance necessity."
*   **Tiered Incentive Model:** To maximize immediate adoption, we offer a **"Time-Bound Discount"**:
    *   **Early Adopters (Months 0–6):** 50% discount on subscription pricing.
    *   **Late Adopters (Months 7–12):** 30% discount.
*   **The "Cloud-Utility" Pull:** New product features, AI-automation modules, and cloud-native integrations will be exclusive to the subscription tier. Perpetual license holders will remain on a "Legacy/Archive" track with no functional growth.

---

## 3. Sales Force Transformation
The commission structure is redesigned to shift focus from "one-and-done" deals to long-term account health.

*   **Incentive Realignment:**
    *   **Multiplier SPIFF:** Reps receive a **1.5x commission multiplier** for converting perpetual customers to a minimum 3-year subscription term.
    *   **Retention Clawback:** 50% of commissions are withheld until the customer completes a mandatory 90-day "Onboarding Phase," ensuring sales focuses on high-quality, actionable closes.
    *   **Shared Ownership:** Renewals are now a joint KPI between Sales and Customer Success (CSM), incentivizing long-term account management.
*   **Policy Change:** Perpetual license sales will no longer count toward "President’s Club" or accelerators as of the start of Q3.

---

## 4. Financial & Investor Communication
To prevent a "broken business" valuation, we will re-frame performance metrics for the investment community.

*   **Primary Metrics:** Shift reporting focus from GAAP Revenue to **Total Contract Value (TCV) Booked** and **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)**.
*   **The "Transition P&L" View:** We will publish a supplemental, pro-forma report alongside GAAP results to illustrate the trajectory of the business without the distortion of the revenue recognition shift.
*   **The "Rule of 40" Commitment:** To maintain investor confidence, we commit to a "Growth + Profitability" score of 40. Savings from legacy support and infrastructure will be reallocated to drive subscription growth and customer success.
*   **Guardrails:** We will provide full transparency on the "Trough" to the board, defining the exact "crossover point" where subscription revenue eclipses legacy license revenue.

---

## 5. Execution Roadmap

| Phase | Timeline | Focus Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1** | Months 1–3 | Early-adopter beta testing; CRM system integration for ARR tracking. |
| **Phase 2** | Months 4–6 | Launch Sales Comp overhaul; activate "Early Bird" migration incentives. |
| **Phase 3** | Months 6+ | Public "Subscription First" launch; initiate Support Deprecation clock. |
| **Phase 4** | Year 2 | Sunset perpetual licenses for all new business; monitor "Renewal Cliff" risk. |

---

## 6. Risk Mitigation
*   **Preventing the Renewal Cliff:** Automated usage-based monitoring will trigger proactive CS interventions if a customer’s platform adoption drops below established thresholds, allowing for early recovery of "at-risk" accounts.
*   **Margin Protection:** Aggressive consolidation of legacy infrastructure costs will be executed immediately to protect gross margins while the subscription model matures.

**Conclusion:** By treating the legacy installed base as a strategic asset rather than a liability, and by aligning sales incentives with long-term retention, this transition will convert our lumpy, unpredictable revenue into a stable, high-multiple ARR engine.
