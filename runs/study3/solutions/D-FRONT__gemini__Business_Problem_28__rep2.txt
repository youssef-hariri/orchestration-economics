# Executive Strategy: Industrial Services Unit (ISU) Turnaround-or-Exit Plan

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Office of the CEO / Restructuring Lead  
**Date:** October 26, 2023  
**Subject:** Final 12-Month Mandate – Turnaround vs. Divestment Roadmap

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Industrial Services Unit (ISU) is currently a "Dependency Trap," where structural decline and talent attrition have turned the unit into a liability for the group’s brand and capital. This plan initiates a **simultaneous turnaround and sell-side preparation process**. By adopting a "Carve-Out" mindset, we treat the ISU as a standalone entity, shielding the parent group from "brand contagion" and ensuring objective, data-driven exit if milestones are not met.

---

## 2. Structural Diagnosis & The "Contagion Audit"
We have concluded the unit’s losses are **structural**, caused by a commodity-pricing model that fails to cover the unit’s overhead. The "Brain Drain" is a rational response to a perceived terminal path.
*   **Contagion Risk:** The ISU’s service quality currently threatens the group’s reputation. 
*   **Immediate Action:** We have launched a **Contagion Audit** with the Account Managers of the profitable group divisions. If the ISU is found to be causing customer churn in our *profitable* units, we will bypass the 12-month turnaround and move directly to an immediate, orderly divestment.

---

## 3. The "Carve-Out" Turnaround Plan (One-Year Roadmap)

| Phase | Milestone | Objective | Financial Metric |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: Triage** | Day 30 | Customer Reconciliation & De-layering | 20% Overhead reduction; Audit complete. |
| **Q2: Anchoring** | Day 90 | Retention of Tier-1 Clients (New SLAs) | 0% reliance on group-level subsidies. |
| **Q3: Stabilization** | Day 180 | Operational Burn-Rate Elimination | Monthly EBITDA reaches break-even. |
| **Q4: Profitability** | Day 365 | Audited Performance Validation | 3 months of positive, audited EBITDA. |

### Strategic Execution:
*   **Customer Reconciliation:** Top 10 shared accounts must migrate to a "Premium-Tier Managed Service" by Day 30. Clients who refuse are offboarded immediately to stop resource-drain.
*   **"Stay & Grow" Talent Strategy:** A 5% "Retention Pool" (reallocated from overhead cuts) will be used to fund performance-based equity-like participation for the "Shadow Leadership" team. If individual "Profit Pockets" reach sustainability, key leads receive success-based payouts.

---

## 4. Objective Exit Triggers (Hard-Coded)
To remove emotional bias from this turnaround, the Board has authorized the following triggers for an immediate exit:

1.  **Value Decay Trigger:** If cash-burn during the turnaround exceeds 15% of the allocated 12-month turnaround budget, operations stop and liquidation begins.
2.  **Anchor Retention Trigger:** If >10% of shared-customer contract value is lost, the Divestment Committee is automatically convened.
3.  **Market Multiplier Trigger:** If an unsolicited acquisition offer exceeds [Liquidation Value + 10%], we initiate a sale, regardless of turnaround progress.
4.  **Performance Ceiling:** If we cannot demonstrate 3 consecutive months of positive EBITDA by Day 270 (End of Q3), the unit will be shuttered or divested.

---

## 5. Implementation Roadmap (First 30 Days)
*   **Days 1–7:** Execute **Contagion Audit**; initiate "Soft Market Sounding" with an M&A partner to identify potential buyers.
*   **Days 8–14:** Finalize "Stay & Grow" retention packages for the top 5 key managers. Failure to secure these individuals triggers an immediate search for an external restructuring lead.
*   **Days 15–30:** Execute "Truth Sessions" with the Top 10 shared clients. Present the new Premium-Tier service model; finalize offboarding for non-compliant accounts.

---

## 6. Final Recommendation
The ISU must prove its right to exist as a standalone business. If the unit cannot sustain itself without parent-company subsidy by Day 180, or if it continues to tarnish the group's reputation, the **divestment strategy** will be executed in Q4. We are effectively managing the ISU as a "dead-man-walking" asset, prioritizing the preservation of group capital and brand equity above all else.

**Status: Approved for Immediate Execution.**
