## PART 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1-Page Decision Brief)

### PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION: Staged Hybrid Convertible Structure

**Instrument:** $30M total capital via Convertible Senior Preferred (Tranche 1: $18M immediate) + Performance-Linked Convertible Preferred (Tranche 2: $12M conditional)

**Capital Targets & Deployment:**
- **Tranche 1 ($18M, closes 45–60 days):** Funds capex ($3M), hiring phase 1 ($6M), working capital ($2.5M), reserve ($6.5M)
- **Tranche 2 ($12M, released Q4 Y1 if milestones hit):** Scales capex ($4M), hiring phase 2 ($5M), working capital ($3M)
- **Deployment cadence:** ~$4.5M/quarter Y1; maintains competitive GTM speed vs. rival's $75M funding

**Founder Control Outcomes:**
| Scenario | Ownership | Board Control | Valuation Rationale |
|----------|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Base Case (T1+T2 convert @ Series A)** | 62.8% (post-T1+T2 + Series A on $600M valuation) | 2/3 founder seats maintained | Founder value $861M (vs. $546M pure equity path) |
| **Downside (T2 withheld if milestones miss)** | 78% (if growth slows) | Founder supermajority | Company viable at $18M; 78% ownership preserved |
| **Upside (IPO-track with $1B+ exit)** | 62–65% (assuming Series B needed) | Founder board majority through exit | Founders capture disproportionate upside via $180M/$250M conversion caps |

**Expected Dilution & Control:**
- **Tranche 1 only:** 78% → 68.9% (–9.1pp), **founders retain board majority** (2 founder seats + 1 independent)
- **Post-Series A:** 62.8% ownership, **founders keep voting control** (>55% threshold)
- **Downside protection:** If T2 never releases, founders stay at **78% ownership** (only $18M deployed; organic-speed growth but preserved control)

**Key Milestones (T2 Release Gates):**
1. **Revenue ≥$46.5M** (rolling 12-month through Q1 Y2, vs. $25M baseline = 86% growth over 18 months)
2. **EBITDA Margin ≥21%** (allows 1pp compression from 22% baseline; one-time costs normalized)
3. **Customer Concentration <30%** (single largest customer; protects against churn risk; multi-year contracts exempt)

**Walk-Away Triggers (When Tranche 2 is NOT Released):**
- Revenue <$45.5M AND structural margin compression <20.5% AND concentration >35% (without contractual safety)
- **Founder Outcome:** Retain 78% ownership; operate at slower growth pace with $18M capital base (viable but cedes ~20% market share to rival over 3 years)
- **Investor Outcome:** Retains $18M Series Hybrid-A preferred (4% coupon, 1x liquidation preference); no T2 deployment; may convert at 5-year maturity

**Competitive Window Adequacy:**
- ✓ **Tranche 1 closes in 45–60 days** (captures 12–18 month market entry window before rival gains traction)
- ✓ **Full $30M available if T2 releases** (85%+ market opportunity capture vs. organic-only path: 60–70% capture, 20–30% market share loss to rival)
- ✓ **Conditional T2 preserves optionality** (if market slows unexpectedly, founders don't over-deploy capital into weakening demand)

**Risk Mitigation:**
| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|-----------|
| **Covenant breach (bank debt path)** | High (interest coverage 2.14x at –15% revenue) | ✓ Eliminated (convertible = no traditional covenants; only performance gates) |
| **Founder dilution loss (pure equity path)** | High (78% → 50–55% post-Series B) | ✓ Mitigated (hybrid T2 withheld if underperformance; 78% downside option) |
| **Valuation friction** | Medium (hybrid $180M cap vs. DCF $260M blended) | ✓ Reduced (44% higher than growth fund $125M offer; upside participation at higher Series A caps) |
| **Market share loss (organic-only path)** | Existential (25–35% share loss to rival) | ✓ Captured (T1 speed + T2 conditional capital funds competitive GTM) |
| **Milestone dispute (if marginal miss)** | Medium (revenue $44.8M vs. $45M gate) | ✓ Managed (rolling 12-month metric + re-test option + ratcheted release path) |
