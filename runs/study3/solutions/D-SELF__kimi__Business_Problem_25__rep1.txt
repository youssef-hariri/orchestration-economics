## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

We are executing a controlled recapitalization of our revenue model. Today, we are valued as a perpetual-license business: **$100M revenue, ~2.5× revenue multiple, $250M enterprise value**. This plan transitions us to a subscription model that commands **~7× ARR**, targeting a **$700M+ enterprise value** upon maturity.

This is not a pricing change. It is a deliberate balance-sheet restructuring. We will replace lumpy, discounted upfront cash with predictable, compounding annuities. We accept an engineered GAAP revenue trough—compressed to **–9% at nadir** through front-loaded migration mechanics and dynamic cash management—with GAAP growth crossover by **Month 26** and OCF inflection by **Month 17**.

## 2. STRATEGIC RATIONALE & VALUATION BRIDGE

**The Investor Discount on Perpetual Revenue**
Our current model produces lumpy, unpredictable cash flows. Upgrades are discretionary, maintenance is contentious, and investors apply a deep discount to non-recurring revenue. Perpetual software peers trade at **1.5×–2.5× revenue**. High-quality SaaS peers with 110%+ NRR trade at **6×–10× ARR**.

**The Arbitrage**
| Model | Revenue/ARR | Growth | EBITDA Margin | Multiple | Enterprise Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Current (Perpetual)** | $100M | 10% | 20% | 2.5× | **$250M** |
| **Target (Subscription)** | $100M ARR | 25% | 80% gross margin | 7.0× | **$700M** |
| **Value Creation** | — | — | — | — | **+$450M** |

We are paying **$6.4M in net transition cost and tolerating 18–24 months of GAAP compression** to acquire a permanent $450M valuation step-up. The board should view the trough not as underperformance, but as the premium paid to re-rate our equity.

**Contingency Narrative**
If SaaS multiples compress, the investment thesis pivots to intrinsic value: subscription generates **2.5× higher LTV/CAC**, **40% higher gross margins at scale**, and superior cash predictability. A DCF-based sensitivity analysis (Appendix B) demonstrates value creation even at a compressed 3× revenue multiple.

## 3. DETAILED RECOMMENDATIONS

### 3.1 Offer Structure & Migration Architecture

**Core Principle:** *Retention precedes conversion. We will honor the legal and psychological sunk investment of our installed base while making subscription the rational economic choice.*

We will not force obsolescence. Our competitor lost 20% of its installed base by creating a perception of confiscation. We avoid this through four parallel tracks:

#### Program A: The Loyalty Bridge (Active Maintenance Customers)
- **Mechanic:** Convert annual maintenance payments 1:1 into Year 1 subscription ACV.
- **Temporary Bridge Discount (Months 7–12):** To accelerate migration of our inelastic base, maintenance customers migrating during the first six months of the amnesty window receive a **0.85× maintenance rate** in Year 1 (15% discount off standard 1:1). This pulls forward ARR and compresses the GAAP trough.
- **Ramp:** Years 1–4 at **1.0× / 1.15× / 1.30× / 1.50×** historical maintenance.
- **Seat Protection:** Customers must maintain ≥90% of prior seat count during the initial term. Downsizing beyond 10% triggers pro-rata credit clawback.
- **Retention Logic:** These customers already have recurring budget lines. We redirect existing spend, removing procurement friction.

#### Program B: The License Trade-In (Recent & Legacy Perpetual Holders)
- **Mandatory Clean Switch:** Customers must legally surrender the perpetual license via signed termination amendment. No hybrid status exists. Refusers remain on Perpetual-Plus or Sovereign Subscription indefinitely.
- **Scale-Adjusted Credits:**
  - SMB (<100 seats): **100%** credit, capped at **$25,000**
  - Mid-Market (100–2,000 seats): **75%** credit, capped at **$150,000**
  - Enterprise (>2,000 seats): **50%** credit, capped at **$500,000**
- **Conversion Ramp (Anti-Cliff):** After credit exhaustion, customers do not face a binary price jump:
  - Year 3: Pay 50% of the gap between credit-era rate and target rate
  - Year 4: Pay 75% of the gap
  - Year 5: Pay 100% target rate
- **Year 3 Loyalty Lock:** Customers who renew on time in Year 3 receive an additional **10% loyalty discount**. This single mechanism improves Year 3 renewal probability from 71% to 89%, preserving approximately **$6.8M in ARR** that would otherwise churn at the credit cliff.

#### Program C: Perpetual-Plus (The Anti-Churn Safety Valve)
- **Mechanic:** Holdouts keep their perpetual license and maintenance. They may purchase an **Innovation Pack** (cloud access, AI layers, premium support) at **40% of equivalent full subscription ACV**.
- **Consolidation Horizon:** This is a bridge, not a permanent underclass.
  - Years 1–2: Innovation Pack at 40%
  - Year 3+: Price escalates **15% annually**
  - Year 5: Perpetual core enters **feature freeze**; Innovation Pack becomes the only path to new capability
- **All-In Conversion:** Perpetual-Plus customers upgrading to full subscription within 24 months receive a 15% loyalty discount.

#### Program D: Sunset Assurance (All Perpetual Holders)
- **Guarantee:** Security-patch availability for **5 years** from the GA date of the last major perpetual version, explicitly covering critical CVE patches, browser compatibility, and TLS/certificate updates for OS versions current as of the guarantee date.
- **Financial Provision:** Engineering cost quantified at **$2.2M** over 5 years. We will purchase a **3-year cyber/E&O tail-risk rider for $180K** (with a $500K deductible), reducing the required cash reserve by $400K and capping catastrophic liability.

#### Regulated/Air-Gapped Carve-Out: Sovereign Subscription
For government, healthcare, and other regulated customers who cannot move to cloud delivery, we offer an on-premise subscription with annual license key refresh, priced at **120% of the equivalent cloud subscription**. This prevents a compliance-driven segment from becoming a permanent platform drag.

### 3.2 Pricing & Trough Engineering

**Subscription Tier Architecture**

| Tier | Target | Price Anchor (vs. Historical Perpetual + Maint.) | Guardrails |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Essentials** | New logos / SMB | ~60% of Year-1 equivalent | Min 25 seats; no API; **new-logo only** for 24 months |
| **Team Starter** | Sub-25-seat prospects | 75% of Essentials per-seat price | 10–24 seats; no cloud modules |
| **Professional** | Mid-market | ~100% of Year-1 equivalent | Standard cloud, analytics, priority support |
| **Enterprise** | Strategic | ~150–170% of Year-1 equivalent | Unlimited API, SSO, custom AI, dedicated CSM |
| **Sovereign** | Regulated / air-gapped | 120% of cloud subscription | On-prem deployable; annual key refresh |

**Trough-Mitigation Mechanics**

**A. Maintenance Funding Pool (Self-Liquidating)**
We will implement a **10% maintenance price increase** on non-converting customers effective Month 4. This pool is explicitly temporary:
- Year 1 offset: **$1.95M** (35% migration assumed)
- Year 2 offset: **~$1.17M**
- The company must achieve >75% subscription gross margin on new ARR by Quarter 5 to replace this pool with operating margin.

**B. Dynamic Multi-Year Prepay (Liquidity Bridge)**
- **Standard Discounts:** 10% (2-year); 20% (3-year).
- **Dynamic Kicker (Months 9–15 Only):** During the liquidity nadir, the sales commission kicker on 3-year prepay increases from +2% to **+4%** of discounted TCV. This accelerates cash when we need it most.
- **Volume Cap:** Baseline cap of **40%** of quarterly bookings. Expanded to **55%** for Months 9–15 only.
- **Economic Rationale:** The prepay discount carries an effective annualized cost of **7.6% IRR**—cheaper than revolver draw (~9.5%) and vastly cheaper than emergency equity.

**C. In-Flight Pipeline Protection (Harmonized)**
- Direct and partner opportunities with >50% probability as of the transition announcement date may close as perpetual through **Month 9** at a 20% premium.
- **Partner Harmonization:** Partners earn **15% of ACV annually** on subscription deals, plus a 6-month transition advance. To prevent channel arbitrage, partner perpetual deals after Month 3 pay commission as if subscription (no upfront windfall). Post-Month 9, partners are suspended for perpetual sales.
- **Anti-Gaming:** Any deal flipping from subscription back to perpetual pays **1%**. VP Sales must manually certify all Month 4–9 perpetual deals.

**D. Blended ASP Governor**
CPQ will be hard-coded with a **3-month rolling blended ASP index**. If the rolling average falls below **82% of list price**, the system automatically:
- Compresses the standard discount ceiling to 10%
- Requires CRO approval for any deal >10% discount
- Suspends the expanded 55% prepay cap until ASP recovers

The top-quartile enhancement costs an incremental **$380K** but protects an estimated **$4.5M in ARR** that would otherwise walk out the door (11.8:1 ROI).

Top 20% of reps may elect either:
- **Option A:** Phantom stock/RSUs vesting 50% at Month 12 and 50% at Month 24

**Anti-Gaming:** SDRs are penalized only for *new* perpetual opportunities sourced post-announcement. Pre-registered in-flight opportunities are CRM-tagged and exempted.
