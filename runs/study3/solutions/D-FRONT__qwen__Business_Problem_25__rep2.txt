**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Chief Executive Officer and Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Executive Leadership Team  
**DATE:** October 24, 2023  
**SUBJECT:** Strategic Business Model Transition: Perpetual Licensing to Subscription Revenue  

---

### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

To secure our long-term market leadership and maximize shareholder value, we are initiating a comprehensive transition from our legacy perpetual licensing model to a predictable, high-margin subscription (SaaS) revenue model. 

While this transition is a strategic imperative to eliminate lumpy revenue and capture premium valuation multiples, it carries inherent risks: a temporary GAAP revenue trough, sales force misalignment, and potential customer churn. Recent industry precedents demonstrate that aggressive, forced migrations can result in the loss of up to 20% of the installed base. 

To mitigate these risks, we have developed a mathematically sound, customer-centric transition plan. By implementing a "Dual-Track" migration offer, maintenance-anchored pre-pay pricing, and rigorously aligned sales compensation, we will protect our installed base, flatten the Year 1 cash flow trough, and accelerate our transition to recurring revenue. 

**The Strategic Thesis:** We are willingly accepting a short-term top-line revenue adjustment to transition from a 3x revenue multiple business to an 8x+ revenue multiple business. Executed correctly, this strategy will yield a projected **120% increase in Enterprise Value by Year 3**, directly aligning this operational shift with substantial shareholder wealth creation.

---

### **1. CUSTOMER MIGRATION & OFFER STRUCTURE**
*Objective: Transition the existing installed base to subscriptions without triggering churn, while respecting existing contractual entitlements.*

We are deploying a **"Dual-Track Evolution"** strategy that relies on positive reinforcement and legal respect for existing contracts, entirely avoiding the toxic "trade-in" mandates that have caused churn at competitor firms.

*   **Track 1: "Keep & Connect" (Zero-Friction Entry):** Customers retain their perpetual license and receive security patches indefinitely. We will introduce a "Cloud Connect" add-on subscription, allowing them to keep their on-prem software while subscribing exclusively to new cloud/AI modules. This lowers the barrier to entry and integrates them into the SaaS billing ecosystem without threatening their core asset.
*   **Track 2: "Residual License Credit" (Full Migration):** For customers opting for full SaaS migration, we will apply a Residual License Credit based on the unamortized value of their perpetual license (e.g., crediting 40% of the original fee if purchased 3 years ago on a 5-year expected life). This credit is applied directly to their first 1-2 years of SaaS subscription, framing the migration as a financial reward for loyalty.
*   **"No-Brick" Legal Guarantee:** We will explicitly guarantee in our board resolutions and customer communications that existing on-premises perpetual licenses will *never* be remotely disabled, legally revoked, or functionally degraded. Feature innovation will shift to SaaS, but core functionality remains permanently protected.
*   **Legal & Compliance Execution:** All migrations will require a standardized, one-click digital **"Termination and Novation"** agreement. This legally retires the old perpetual EULA in exchange for the new SaaS MSA, ensuring clean compliance and ASC 606 revenue recognition.

---

### **2. PRICING & REVENUE STRATEGY**
*Objective: Set pricing that accelerates adoption to minimize the length of the revenue trough, while utilizing pre-pay mechanics to flatten the Year 1 cash flow impact.*

*   **SaaS List Price Anchor:** The annual SaaS subscription will be priced at **30% of the historical perpetual license price**. This establishes a high Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) baseline while offering a clear 2.5-year payback period for the customer.
*   **Maintenance-Anchored Pre-pay Incentive:** To pull cash forward and flatten the revenue trough, we will heavily incentivize 3-year upfront payments with a **33% discount**. 
    *   *Customer Math:* A $30k/year subscription becomes $60,000 upfront for three years. This is highly palatable to the customer (roughly 3x their old annual maintenance budget) but pulls three years of cash into Year 1, drastically mitigating the trough.
*   **"Budget-Neutral" Migration Tier:** For customers requiring annual billing, we will offer a specific "Migration Rate" equal to their current maintenance fee + 10%. Because this falls into their existing OpEx budget bucket, it requires zero new CFO approval, accelerating deal velocity.
*   **Transitional Net-New Bundle:** To prevent an immediate cliff in net-new cash flow, net-new customers may purchase a "Perpetual License + 1-Year SaaS Cloud Connect" bundle at a 20% premium for the first 12 months. This captures necessary upfront cash while forcing new logos into the SaaS ecosystem on Day 1. Pure SaaS-only sales for net-new will commence in Month 13.

---

### **3. GO-TO-MARKET & COMPENSATION ALIGNMENT**
*Objective: Shift the sales and customer success organizations from hunting lumpy upfront deals to building a predictable, high-quality recurring revenue base.*

**Sales Quota & Commission Structure:**
*   **Quota Allocation:** Total quota will be strictly divided: 60% Net-New ARR, 30% Migration ARR, 10% Expansion ARR.
*   **Base Commission Rates:** 8% on Net-New ARR; **12% on Migration ARR** (premium paid for the complexity of legacy conversion); 5% on Expansion.
*   **Multi-Year Kicker:** Commission is paid on Year 1 ARR. However, reps signing 3-year deals receive a **+3% kicker** on the Year 1 ARR commission (e.g., 8% becomes 11%), forcing the sale of multi-year terms to fund the trough.
*   **Retention Reserve (Quality Gate):** 15% of all earned commission is placed in a "Retention Reserve." This is paid out quarterly *only* if the rep’s specific sold cohort maintains a >90% net retention rate at the 12-month mark. If cohort churn exceeds 10%, the reserve is forfeited.

**Leadership & Customer Success Compensation:**
*   **Sales Management:** VP/Director variable compensation will be tied to: 50% Team ARR Attainment, 30% Net Dollar Retention (NDR), and 20% *Migration Velocity* (percentage of territory converted).
*   **Customer Success (CS):** CS bonuses will shift from "time-to-value" to **Gross Retention Rate (GRR)** and **Time-to-First-Value (TTFV)**. Crucially, 30% of CS leadership’s variable compensation will be tied directly to the Migration ARR target, making them active partners in the transition.

---

### **4. FINANCIAL REPORTING & BOARD GOVERNANCE**
*Objective: Pre-frame the revenue trough, shift the Board’s focus to SaaS valuation metrics, and provide transparent operational telemetry.*

*   **Bifurcated P&L Reporting:** We will cease blending the financials. The Board deck will report the "Legacy Run-Off" business and the "SaaS Growth" business as two separate P&Ls. This prevents the expected decline of the legacy business from masking the hyper-growth of the SaaS business.
*   **The Transition Crossover Point (TCP):** We have defined the TCP as the exact month where SaaS ARR run-rate exceeds Legacy Revenue run-rate (projected Month 26). This will serve as the primary North Star metric for the Board.
*   **Core SaaS Metrics & Targets:**
    *   **cARR (Committed ARR):** Target >45% YoY growth.
    *   **NDR (Net Dollar Retention):** Target >115%. (SaaS expansion must mathematically outpace legacy churn).
    *   **SaaS Magic Number:** Target >0.75 in Year 1, scaling to >1.0 by Year 2 to prove capital efficiency.
    *   **Rule of 40:** Strictly tracked. Even if top-line revenue dips in Year 1, maintaining a Rule of 40 (ARR Growth % + FCF Margin %) will prove the underlying health of the business.
*   **The Valuation Arbitrage Bridge:** We will present a specific valuation model demonstrating the "Multiple Expansion." By modeling the 3x to 8x multiple shift alongside the projected cash flows, we will visually demonstrate how this transition drives a 120% increase in Enterprise Value by Year 3, directly tying operational execution to shareholder wealth.

---

### **5. IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE & NEXT STEPS**

*   **Months 1-2:** Finalize pricing tiers, legally draft "Termination and Novation" agreements, and finalize the revised Sales and CS compensation plans.
*   **Month 3:** Brief the Board on the J-Curve financial model, Bifurcated P&L structure, and Valuation Arbitrage Bridge. Secure Board approval.
*   **Month 4:** Launch the "Transitional Net-New Bundle" for new logos. Roll out the "Dual-Track" migration offers to the existing installed base.
*   **Months 5-18:** Execute the transition. Leverage the "Multi-Year Kicker" and "Retention Reserve" to drive sales rep behavior, and the "Residual License Credit" to drive customer conversion.
*   **Month 18:** Conclude first-year migration discounts. Announce the formal "Feature Sunset" timeline for remaining perpetual licenses to capture final stragglers.
*   **Month 26 (Target):** Achieve the Transition Crossover Point (TCP), where SaaS ARR run-rate officially surpasses Legacy Revenue run-rate.

### **CONCLUSION**

This transition is a complex but entirely manageable evolution. By treating our existing customers with respect, mathematically incentivizing our sales and success teams, and rigorously managing investor expectations through the lens of valuation multiples, we will successfully navigate the revenue trough. We look forward to executing this strategy and emerging as a highly valued, predictable, and dominant SaaS enterprise.
