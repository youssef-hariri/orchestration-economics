## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1-PAGE)

### STRATEGIC THESIS
Deploy phased, site-differentiated automation with embedded labor transition planning to reduce 35% annual picker turnover, lower error rates damaging key accounts, and achieve 18–24 month payback while protecting 1,485 employees and community stability. A "Lead-Follow-Protect" sequence minimizes execution risk and enables learning transfer across sites.

### FINANCIAL HEADLINE
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Total 3-Year Net Benefit** | $49.6M |
| **Capital Investment Required** | $34.8M |
| **Blended Payback Period** | 22 months |
| **Headcount Reduction (3-Year)** | 790 FTE (53% of baseline) |
| **Year 1 Net Benefit** | –$5.5M (investment phase) |
| **Year 2 Net Benefit** | –$18.6M (execution & stabilization) |
| **Year 3 Net Benefit** | –$25.5M (mature operations, repeating) |

### PHASING STRATEGY
- **Phase 1 (Mo. 1–16):** West Coast pilot (mo. 5–13 go-live), HQ East inbound automation (mo. 6–12), Southeast secondary launch (mo. 10–18) — validates tech, labor model, financial thesis
- **Phase 2 (Mo. 17–26):** Midwest Central conservative automation + HQ East picking automation — scales with lessons learned; avoids Q4 peak season
- **Phase 3 (Mo. 25–36):** Northeast conditional deployment + final optimization — contingent on Phase 2 payback realization

### TOP RISKS & MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Key Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|-----------------|
| Vendor delays / system integration failures | HIGH (75%) | VERY HIGH | Dual-sourcing ($750K premium); tiered penalties; 10-week integration testing; 1.5 FTE integration engineers |
| Reskilling completion falls <60% | MEDIUM-HIGH (65%) | HIGH | Pilot cohort (mo. 2–3); 3 parallel tracks (sprint/standard/apprenticeship); $300/week childcare support |
| Service disruption during conversions | MEDIUM (60%) | VERY HIGH | Rehearsed drills; 30% manual fallback; dynamic volume rebalancing; $150K contingency staffing |
| Workforce attrition spike to 40%+ | MEDIUM-HIGH (68%) | HIGH | Enhanced retention incentives ($2.5K vs. $800); 24-month guarantee (vs. 18-month); supervisory career pathways |
| Payback realization 15%+ behind plan | MEDIUM (55%) | HIGH | Monthly assumption validation dashboard; gates at mo. 6, 15, 24, 36 with clear go/no-go criteria; corrective action playbooks pre-built |
| Community backlash at HQ East | MEDIUM (50%) | VERY HIGH | 24-month employment guarantee; $1.8M community college partnership; school apprenticeships (50/year); quarterly public KPI dashboard |
| Account loss (quality perception risk) | MEDIUM (60%) | HIGH | Pre-go-live account agreements (error rate tolerance, SLA terms); VP Sales task force; 48-hour escalation |

### GOVERNANCE & GATES
- **Weekly:** Ops dashboard (labor, productivity, capex); escalation matrix (green/yellow/red thresholds)
- **Bi-weekly:** Steering Committee review (payback, attrition, capex, risks, decisions)
- **Monthly:** All-company communication (KPIs, milestones, reskilling updates)
- **Quarterly:** Board update (strategy, risk, investor confidence)
- **Phase Gates (Mo. 6, 9, 12, 15, 24, 36):** Quantitative go/no-go decisions with pre-defined corrective action playbooks if thresholds missed

- ✓ 790 FTE headcount reduction realized; 68% reskilled to new roles (min. 60% acceptable)
