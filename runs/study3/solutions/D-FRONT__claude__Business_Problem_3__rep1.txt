# SECTION 1: SITUATION DIAGNOSIS (Condensed)

## 1.1 Current State Pain Points

**Five Critical Issues Drive Customer Dissatisfaction:**

1. **No WhatsApp/Messenger Support** — 25–35% of mobile-first contacts have no access to preferred channel; current email/chat insufficient for 85% of emerging market mobile users
2. **Delivery Tracking Opacity** — 15–22% of all contacts are manual tracking inquiries; no proactive notifications; drives 60% repeat contacts
3. **Slow Refund Processing** — 10–21 day cycle vs. 7-day market expectation; creates refund anxiety; correlates with 28% churn rate in refund-seeker segment
4. **Email Response Delays** — 24–48 hour FRT (current) vs. <2 hour expectation (developed) / 4–8 hour acceptable (emerging); 30–40% of contacts via email
5. **Poor First-Contact Resolution** — 45–50% FCR (below 70% benchmark); 27% of customers repeat-contact within 30 days; drives $240K–$768K annual waste + churn

**Revenue at Risk:** $2.2M–$3.6M annually (combined churn + operational waste across these five pain points)

## 1.2 Root Causes (Systemically)

| Cause | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| **Missing channel integration** — No WhatsApp API; email/chat/social siloed | 40% of contacts fragmented; no unified agent view |
| **Reactive staffing model** — Sized for average volume, not peaks; business hours only | Peak season queue times spike to 2+ hours; off-hours contacts unresolved same-day |
| **Fragmented customer data** — CRM, ticketing, ERP, email not synced; agents lack order context | 27% repeat-contact rate; agents re-ask questions; FCR 45–50% |
| **No AI/automation** — Manual categorization slow; no sentiment detection; no suggested responses | Escalations common; agent productivity low; knowledge gaps |
| **Finance-CS process disconnect** — Refunds approved by support but processed weekly by finance in batches | 10–21 day payout cycle; customer refund anxiety persists |
| **Weak agent empowerment** — Escalation required for most decisions; no approval thresholds | Slow resolution; agent frustration; customer feels unheard |
| **Outdated self-serve assets** — FAQ not mobile-optimized; no chatbot; no NLU search | 20–30% of contacts FAQ-answerable but not found; support volume inflated |

## 1.3 Affected Customer Segments

**Priority by revenue at risk + churn risk:**

- **High-Value Repeat Buyers (8–12%, 35–40% of revenue):** Churn 25–35% if service degrades; expect VIP FRT/responsiveness
- **Mobile-First/Emerging Market (20–28%, 15–25% of revenue, fastest-growing):** 50–65% churn if WhatsApp/SMS not available; CoD anxiety critical
- **New/First-Time Buyers (22–28%, 10–15% current revenue):** 60–75% abandon if first-time experience poor; 40% convert to repeat (high CLV potential)
- **Price-Sensitive Segment (25–32%, 8–12% of revenue):** 70–80% churn (loyalty to deals, not brand); lower profitability

## PHASE 2: FOUNDATION BUILD (Weeks 7–16) – Operational Transformation

### Initiative 2.1: AI-Powered Smart Triage & Routing
**Owner:** CRM Admin + ML Engineer  
**Timeline:** Weeks 7–16  
**Investment:** $14–35K setup | $5–15K/month ongoing

#### Pain Point & Business Case
- **Current State:** FCR 45–50%; 27% repeat-contact rate (same customer, same issue, 30 days)
- **Root Cause:** Fragmented customer data; no issue categorization automation; agent knowledge gaps; weak empowerment
- **Year 1 Revenue Recovery:** $450–600K (FCR improvement 15–25%; churn reduction via sentiment-triggered outreach)

#### Deliverables
1. **Auto-Categorization (NLU Model):** Train on 10K+ historical tickets; classifies issue: tracking, refund, product Q, delivery, billing, return, defect, cancellation
   - Accuracy target: 85–92%
   - Tool: Hugging Face BERT (open-source, fine-tuned) or AWS Comprehend
   - Time to build: 2–3 weeks (data prep + training + testing)

2. **Sentiment & Urgency Scoring:** 
   - Detect tone (angry, neutral, happy) + urgency flags (missing, damaged, billing error)
   - Auto-escalate high urgency + angry → manager queue (<15 min SLA)
   - Route medium urgency + neutral → standard queue (<2 hr SLA)
   - Route low urgency + FAQ → bot or junior agent (<4 hr SLA)

3. **Intelligent Routing by Skill:**
   - Issue = "Refund" → route to refund specialist
   - Issue = "Billing" → route to billing specialist or finance
   - Issue = "Damage Claim" → route to senior agent with approval authority
   - Impact: 50–60% first-time routing accuracy (vs. current ~70%); reduces misdirection

4. **Suggested First Response:**
   - AI recommends canned response or knowledge article based on category + sentiment
   - Example: Issue = "Where is order?" + Sentiment = Neutral → Suggest: "Hi [Name]! Your order [#123] is [Status]. Delivery: [Date]. [Tracking Link]."
   - Agent can use as-is (saves 1–2 min) or personalize
   - Adoption target: 40–60% use as-is or as base

5. **Multilingual Routing:** Detect language; route to language-matched agent pool; if unavailable, escalate or suggest email

6. **Performance Dashboard:** Track routing accuracy, suggestion adoption, productivity by category; alert if accuracy drops <80%

# SECTION 3: KPI BASELINE & MEASUREMENT PLAN

## 3.1 Primary Customer Experience Metrics

### CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score)
- **Definition:** Post-interaction survey: "How satisfied are you with service?" (1–5 or 1–10 scale)
- **Current Baseline:** 65–75% "Satisfied or Very Satisfied"
- **Target (12 Months):** 82–88%
- **Mechanism:** Auto-triggered survey after ticket close or chat end; differentiate by channel, issue type, agent
- **Business Impact:** +5% CSAT → ~2% retention lift = +$60K–100K annual CLV recovery
- **Measurement:** Weekly average; monthly trend; segment drill-down

### NPS (Net Promoter Score)
- **Current Baseline:** 20–40 (passive, with detractors)
- **Target (12 Months):** 45–55 (strong loyalty signals)
- **Mechanism:** Monthly pulse surveys; segment by customer lifecycle (new, repeat, churner), region, purchase value
- **Business Impact:** +10 NPS points = +3–5% retention + earned referral word-of-mouth
- **Measurement:** Monthly; quarterly trend analysis; verbatim feedback coded for root cause

### CES (Customer Effort Score)
- **Definition:** "How easy was it to resolve your issue?" (1–5 or 1–7)
- **Current Baseline:** 3.0–3.5 (high effort; customer does work)
- **Target (12 Months):** 4.0+ (80%+ "Easy" or "Very Easy")
- **Business Impact:** Low CES is strongest churn predictor; +0.5 CES = +2–3% retention
- **Measurement:** Post-resolution survey; real-time feedback; segment by channel + issue type
