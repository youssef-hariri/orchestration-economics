## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

We are exchanging a **2.5× lumpy revenue multiple** for a **5.0×+ recurring-revenue multiple** (7–8× as upside if Net Revenue Retention exceeds 120%). This transition converts balance-sheet volatility into contracted, predictable ARR, but requires disciplined execution through a quantified GAAP revenue trough.

**Key Financial Anchors**
- **GAAP Revenue Trough:** –12% in the base case; –18% in the stress case. Nadir at Month 10–12.
- **Cash Receipts Trough:** –3% (base) to –6% (stress), preserving liquidity through the transition.
- **Crossover to Perpetual-Equivalent Run-Rate:** Month 38–42.
- **Return to GAAP Growth:** Q4 Year 2.
- **LTV/CAC Improvement:** From 1.2× (perpetual) to 4.5× (subscription).

**Four Non-Negotiable Principles**
1. **Entitlement Protection:** Perpetual rights are permanently grandfathered. Subscription is a value-based upgrade path, never a confiscation.
2. **Sales Force Economic Neutrality:** The compensation model must make the desired subscription behavior unambiguously more lucrative than the legacy perpetual model.
3. **Accounting Integrity:** Every offer structure is vetted for ASC 606 material-right treatment before it reaches a customer.
4. **Trough Transparency:** Bank covenants, Board metrics, and external guidance are restructured *before* public announcement so that the trough is managed, not hidden.

**Board Decision Required:** Approval of Phase 1 budget, ratification of the corrected sales compensation plan, and authorization of covenant renegotiation and ASC 606 review prior to any external customer communication.

## 3. CUSTOMER OFFER ARCHITECTURE & MIGRATION

### 3.1 The Customer Bill of Rights
To neutralize entitlement risk and avoid the 20% installed-base attrition suffered by competitors who imposed abrupt migrations, we establish the following public commitments:

1. **Perpetual Validity:** Perpetual licenses remain valid in perpetuity. We will never deactivate, audit-pressure, or degrade performance of existing on-premise installations.
2. **Maintenance Guarantee:** Maintenance is guaranteed for **3 years** at current rates (CPI-only escalator). Coverage is limited to security patches and Severity-1 bug fixes; new features are subscription-exclusive.
3. **Extended Support Option:** After Year 3, an Extended Support Program is available at 35% of the original license price per year—expensive enough to incent migration, optional enough to avoid coercion.
4. **Data Portability:** Guaranteed.

### 3.2 Migration Offer: The "Platform Access Incentive"
We have eliminated the previously proposed "Legacy Value Credit," which created an ASC 606 material right and zero-GAAP conversion months. Migration is treated as the purchase of a **new product SKU**:

| Incentive | Mechanics | Accounting Treatment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Platform Access Incentive** | First-year subscription priced at a **maximum 30% discount off list ACV**. Offered to all legacy customers as an introductory price for the cloud tier. | Standard revenue recognition; no deferred material-right liability because the discount is not tied to prior purchase history. |
| **Module Continuity** | Previously purchased add-ons are permanently bundled into the converted tier. | Cost is embedded in a 10–15% higher subscription list price; no renewal cliff. |
| **Migration Concierge** | 40-hour jumpstart package + $5K credit toward billable migration services. | Services revenue recognized on delivery. |
| **Rate Lock** | List price locked for 36 months post-conversion. | Removes future-price risk without creating a material right. |
| **Early Mover Window** | Additional 15% discount on ACV for conversions executed in Phase 1 (Months 1–12), subject to the 30% total discount floor. | Creates urgency and loads ARR early to shorten trough duration. |

**Discount Governance:** A centralized Deal Desk enforces a **hard floor of 30% total discount stack in Phase 1** and 20% in Phase 2. Any exception requires joint CRO and CFO approval.

### 3.3 Segment-Specific Migration Playbooks
- **Enterprise (>500 seats):** White-glove negotiation. Prepaid Subscription SKU is the default proposal. Account-level ROI analysis demonstrating TCO shift. Legacy Conversion Specialists support the AE on account mapping.
- **Mid-Market (50–500 seats):** Standardized trade-in calculator (online + inside sales). Fixed packaging with Professional tier as the default.
- **SMB (<50 seats):** Self-service "Switch & Save" portal. Platform Access Incentive applied automatically at checkout.

## 4. PRICING ARCHITECTURE

### 4.1 Dual-Track List Pricing (Phase 1)
- **Subscription ACV:** Priced at **25% of current perpetual license list price** per annum. This approximates the historical "license + maintenance + upgrade" cycle over a 4-year horizon.
- **Perpetual List Price:** Frozen at current levels in Phase 1. Maintenance is **not** increased to punitive levels; held at current rates to avoid coercive signaling.
- **TCO Framing:** Subscription is positioned as 10–15% cheaper on a 3-year total cost basis when including avoided hardware, DBA labor, and upgrade projects.

### 4.2 Multi-Year & Cash-Flow Mechanics
| Term | Annual Equivalent | Payment Terms | Revenue Recognition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1-Year | 100% of ACV | Annual upfront | Ratable over 12 months |
| 2-Year | 90% of ACV (10% discount) | Full TCV upfront | Ratable over 24 months |
| 3-Year | 85% of ACV (15% discount) | Full TCV upfront | Ratable over 36 months |
| **Prepaid Subscription SKU** | 85% of ACV | 2.5× ACV at signing | Ratable over 36 months |

**Payment Discipline:** Annual upfront is the default. Monthly payment plans are permitted only for sub-$5K ACV transactions and carry an **8% price premium**—the customer pays for their own working capital; we do not subsidize it.

### 4.3 Packaging to Drive Net Revenue Retention
Subscription is not merely a pricing change; it is a packaging upgrade designed to increase NRR and justify the shift:

- **Essentials:** Core functionality parity with perpetual + standard support. Entry land tier.
- **Professional:** Adds real-time collaboration, AI-assisted workflows, advanced analytics, and API access. Target tier for 70% of the base.
- **Enterprise:** Adds SSO, admin provisioning, audit logs, dedicated CSM, and unlimited storage. Upmarket expansion target.

**Expansion Levers:** Usage-based overage for API calls, storage, or transaction volume. This allows ARR to grow within accounts, offsetting GAAP revenue lag and supporting multiple expansion.

## 5. SALES COMPENSATION REDESIGN

Cultural resistance from the sales force is the single greatest internal risk. The new plan must make subscription deals *more* lucrative than legacy perpetual deals.

### 5.1 Corrected Commission Structure
The reference parity for a $100K perpetual license equivalent is **24% of ACV**. The actual commission schedule is structured to strongly incentivize multi-year prepay—the behavior that minimizes company trough:

| Deal Type | Commission Rate | Customer Example ($100K Legacy Equivalent) | Rep Payout | vs. Legacy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Perpetual (Legacy)** | 6% of license TCV | $100K license | $6,000 | 100% |
| **1-Year Subscription** | 20% of ACV | $25K ACV | $5,000 | 83% |
| **2-Year Prepaid** | 12% of TCV | $45K TCV | $5,400 | 90% |
| **3-Year Prepaid / Prepaid SKU** | 10% of TCV | $63.75K TCV | **$6,375** | **106%** |

**Renewal & Expansion:**
- **Renewal:** 3% of Renewal ACV.
- **Expansion:** 10% of Expansion ARR.

**Conversion SPIF:** $5K–$20K per legacy account converted, split **50/50** between the originating AE/hunter and the Legacy Conversion Specialist/CSM. ARR quota credit is also split 50/50.

### 5.2 Quota Structure: No Covert Pay Raise
Total quota dollar value remains identical to historical levels. Composition shifts to force subscription behavior:

| Year | New Subscription ARR | Conversion ARR | Legacy Perpetual | Expansion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Year 1** | 50% | 30% | 20%* | — |
| **Year 2** | 60% | 25% | 15%* | — |
| **Year 3** | 50% | — | — | 50% (with CS overlay) |

*\*Perpetual deals allowed by exception but carry* ***zero SPIFs, zero club points, and zero leaderboard recognition.*** *They are invisible in the CRM incentive layer.*

### 5.3 Transition Protections
- **Rolling 2-Quarter Guarantee:** If trailing 2-quarter earnings fall below 75% of historical variable comp, the company trues the rep up to 75%. The true-up is a **recoverable advance** against future over-performance, not a non-recoverable draw. This maintains hunger while cushioning the transition.
- **Clawback Safety:** Commissions on multi-year TCV are paid at booking, subject to a 12-month prorated clawback **only if the customer cancels** (not on downsell).

### 5.4 Cultural Intervention
On **Day 1**, all perpetual-only SPIFs, President’s Club eligibility, and "Big Deal of the Quarter" recognition are terminated. The CRM leaderboard is renamed the **ARR Champion** board. We will identify 2–3 influential reps for a beta subscription sprint, then showcase their higher W-2s at the national sales meeting to prove earnings upside.

### 5.5 Organizational Overlay
- **Customer Success (CS):** Hired 90 days pre-launch. CS owns renewal and expansion quotas; Sales owns new logos and conversions. CS bonuses are tied to NRR (target: >110%).
- **Legacy Conversion Specialists:** Inside sales specialists tasked solely with migrating SMB accounts, freeing enterprise AEs to hunt new subscription logos.
