## SECTION 1: ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS – WHY THIS PLAN SUCCEEDS WHERE PREVIOUS ATTEMPT FAILED

### The 2-Year-Old Failure: Anatomy & Lessons Learned

**What Was Attempted (Incorrectly):**
The company tried 14 → 10 week compression by **cutting quality inspection corners**, not optimizing processes:

| **What Was Cut** | **Impact** | **Why It Failed** |
|---|---|---|
| **Skipped Gate 1 (dimensional inspection)** | 0.3-week "saving" | Rough-machining surface defects (tool chatter marks) propagated to finished surface; not caught until Gate 3 → expensive rework |
| **Shortened hydrostatic dwell 24→12 hours** | 0.5-week "saving" | Slow micro-fracture stress propagation not observed; defects developed in field under customer pressure cycling |
| **Rushed final assembly** | 0.5-week "saving" | Fasteners under-torqued; joint micro-slip under pressure; discovered only at test (too late for cheap rework) |
| **Eliminated material staging inspection** | 0.2-week "saving" | Supplier porosity defects (internal voids) became stress concentrators; not detected pre-machining |
| **No process visibility/control** | N/A | Production manager unaware of defect trends rising; decisions made without data |
| **RESULT:** 8.2% warranty failure rate; $2.1M cost | **Quality not negotiable** | Compression must cut waiting time, not inspection rigor |

### Why This Plan Is Fundamentally Different

**Three Core Principles:**

1. **Compress Queue Time, Not Inspection Time**
   - Previous: "Gate 1 takes 0.3 weeks; let's skip it"
   - This Plan: "Gate 1 takes 0.3 weeks: 1 hour measurement + 1.5 days queue + 0.5 days paperwork. Eliminate the 2-day queue; preserve the 1-hour measurement."
   - **Mechanism:** Stagger job completions via APS scheduling → no queue clustering; contract surge QA tech → peak days covered; auto-populate docs → paperwork concurrent with measurement

2. **Add Pre-Emptive Quality Layers (Prevent Defects Earlier)**
   - Previous: Defects found at final test (Gate 3); expensive $8K rework
   - This Plan: Defects found earlier (incoming inspection, sub-assembly pre-test); cheaper $3K rework; customer risk eliminated
   - **Mechanism:** Three new detection layers added: incoming supplier inspection (visual porosity), sub-assembly pre-test (fastener torque), in-process SPC (tool wear prevention)

3. **Make Quality Pressure Visible (Prevent Silent Erosion)**
   - Previous: No quality dashboard; production pressure accumulated silently until suddenly quality corners cut
   - This Plan: Real-time QA dashboard + weekly quality review meeting + monthly steering committee → all decisions visible; escalation protocols prevent informal workarounds
   - **Mechanism:** SPC line stops (automatic if >2σ deviation), defect hold hard caps (max 4 hours before engineering review), override logging (every WIP limit override documented)

**Result:** This plan recovers 5.5 weeks of parallelizable, non-value-adding time while **strengthening** quality controls (not weakening them).

### NEW Early Detection Layers (Risk Prevention)

#### **Layer 1: Incoming Supplier Inspection (Pre-Machining)**
- **What:** Visual porosity inspection (100% of castings, 3-minute check) + hardness spot-check (3% sample) + material cert verification
- **Why:** Supplier defects (porosity, hardness variance) become stress concentrators during machining; catching them pre-machining prevents propagation
- **Cost Savings:** $500 incoming inspection vs. $3K–$8K rework if defect discovered at Gate 2 or 3
- **Quality Benefit:** Removes supplier variability before it enters production

#### **Layer 2: Sub-Assembly Pre-Test (Before Gate 3)**
- **What:** Fastener torque verification (3 samples per assembly), alignment check (±0.020"), optional micro-pressurization (50% pressure, 2-hour dwell)
- **Why:** Assembly defects (under-torqued fasteners) were the #1 rework cause in old failure mode; pre-test catches them 3 days early
- **Cost Savings:** $3K rework if caught early vs. $8K rework + customer risk if caught at Gate 3 test
- **Quality Benefit:** Reduces Gate 3 escape rate 50–70%

#### **Layer 3: In-Process SPC Monitoring (During Precision Machining)**
- **What:** Real-time tracking of OD dimensions, surface finish, runout, tool temperature; control charts at machine; operator trained on "stop if alert" protocol
- **Why:** Prevents tool-wear defects before they propagate; shifts from "detect late" to "prevent early"
- **Cost Savings:** $0 defect prevention cost vs. $2K rework cost if detected at Gate 2 inspection
- **Quality Benefit:** Proactive process control; higher Cpk (process capability); fewer defects overall

**Result:** Three new prevention layers added UPSTREAM of existing inspection gates. Old failure mode (skipping Gate 1, rushing assembly, shortening dwell) impossible with early detection infrastructure.
