# EXECUTIVE BRIEF: PROJECT VELOCITY 
**Operations Improvement & Lead Time Reduction Plan**

**TO:** Executive Leadership Team (Production, Quality, and Sales)
**SUBJECT:** Strategic Roadmap to Reduce Lead Time from 14 Weeks to 6 Weeks

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Our current **14-week lead time** is costing us **one in three deals**, while our fastest competitors quote at **6 weeks**. Our previous attempt to compress this schedule resulted in a severe spike in warranty claims because time was cut at the execution phase, compromising certified quality gates. 

**Project Velocity** shifts our operational philosophy: we will not ask our teams to "work faster." Instead, we will systematically eliminate non-value-add wait time, decouple certified material bottlenecks, and hard-lock quality into the process. By redesigning our system for flow and aligning our cross-functional incentives, we will safely and sustainably achieve the **6-week benchmark** without compromising a single certified quality standard.

---

## PHASED TARGET METRICS
Attempting a leap from 14 to 6 weeks in a single phase risks systemic shock. We will execute this transformation in four controlled phases:

*   **Phase 1 (Months 1-3):** Stabilize & Decouple – Target **10 Weeks**
*   **Phase 2 (Months 4-6):** Flow & Modularity – Target **8 Weeks**
*   **Phase 3 (Months 7-9):** Pull & Optimize – Target **6 Weeks**
*   **Phase 4 (Months 10-12):** Sustain & Scale – Maintain **6 Weeks**

---

## PILLAR 1: OPERATIONAL FLOW & MATERIAL DECOUPLING
*Objective: Eliminate the hidden wait times and certified material bottlenecks that artificially inflate our lead time.*

*   **Establish a Certified Material Supermarket:** Certified raw materials inherently carry 6-to-8-week supplier lead times. We will **establish a strategic buffer of certified raw materials and pre-machined blanks** to decouple this wait time from the manufacturing floor. 
    *   *Financial Guardrail:* To protect working capital, this supermarket will be **capped at a maximum of 30 days of cover**. Replenishment will be strictly gated by Sales providing a highly accurate rolling 12-week forecast.
*   **Implement Modular Sub-Assemblies:** Transitioning entirely from Engineer-to-Order (ETO) to Configure-to-Order (CTO) is a multi-year R&D effort. Instead, we will **identify and pre-build the top 5 recurring custom configurations as modular sub-assemblies**. Production will pull these pre-built modules from the decoupling point, performing only final integration on the floor.
*   **Overlap NNVA with Digital Dossier Compilation:** Mandatory non-value-add (NNVA) times (e.g., paint cure, NDT) cannot be skipped. We will **mandate that QA compile the digital certification dossier concurrently** with these physical processes. When the physical process finishes, administrative sign-off will be instantaneous.

---

## PILLAR 2: IRONCLAD QUALITY PROTECTION
*Objective: Shift quality from an "end-of-line gate" to an inescapable property of the process, ensuring zero warranty escapes.*

*   **Deploy MES Hard-Stops (System-Locked Gates):** Human discipline under schedule pressure has failed us in the past. We will **implement Manufacturing Execution System (MES) Operation Locking**. An operator physically cannot scan a part into the next operation until the QA tech has digitally signed off on the previous one. *There is zero ability to bypass the gate.*
*   **Embed QA and Define the Final Inspection Path:** We will **abolish the centralized Final Inspection department** to eliminate massive queues. Dedicated QA technicians will be embedded directly into manufacturing cells, performing **80% of certified inspections in-cell**. 
    *   *The 20% Path:* For the remaining 20% of system-level certifications, we will **establish a dedicated Final Integration Bay** equipped with all necessary calibrated equipment. Embedded QA techs will retain the authority and physical tools to handle integration failures directly at the cell level before escalation.
*   **Automate Data Capture for Certification:** Manual data entry causes delays and errors. We will **integrate smart torque tools, digital calipers, and CMMs directly into the MES**. Tools will automatically log exact values and timestamps, shifting QA’s role from *collecting* data to *verifying* automated data, reducing inspection time by **60%**.

---

## PILLAR 3: CROSS-FUNCTIONAL ALIGNMENT & GOVERNANCE
*Objective: End the blame game by aligning Sales, Production, and Quality around shared financial and operational realities.*

*   **Institute a Margin Hurdle Rate for Expediting:** Sales cannot demand unrealistic lead times for custom builds without financial accountability. If an order requires expediting (overtime, air-freight, or pulling from strategic buffers), it will trigger a **Margin Hurdle Rate**. This legally compliant mechanism **lowers the commission percentage on the expedited deal**, instantly aligning Sales promises with the financial reality of Operations.
*   **Implement Available-to-Promise (ATP) Logic:** We will **configure the CRM/ERP to enforce lead-time realities**. Sales will only be authorized to quote the **6-week lead time for standard/modular configurations**. Custom configurations will automatically quote **10-to-12 weeks** unless an executive-level Exception Request is signed by the VPs of Sales, Operations, and the Quality Director.
*   **Unify KPIs and Bonus Structures:** Local optimization ends today. We will align leadership incentives through a unified scorecard:
    *   **Sales:** Measured on **Forecast Accuracy (MAPE)** and **% of Revenue from Standard Configurations**.
    *   **Production:** Measured on **On-Time In-Full (OTIF)** and **Throughput Time**.
    *   **Quality:** Measured on **First Pass Yield (FPY)** and **Certification Cycle Time**.
    *   **The Shared Anchor:** **40% of the annual bonus** for the Directors of Sales, Production, and Quality is tied to one metric: **Perfect Order Rate** (On time, in full, 100% accurate documentation, zero 90-day warranty claims).
*   **Launch Daily Tier-2 "Traffic Control":** We will **institute a mandatory 15-minute daily stand-up** with the Production Manager, QA Manager, and Sales Operations Lead. The strict agenda is to review only "Critical Path" orders for the next 5 days, identify roadblocks, and reallocate resources. Unresolved issues are escalated to the Ops Council for **24-hour resolution**.

## IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

**Months 1-3: Stabilize & Decouple (Target: 10 Weeks)**
*   **Action:** Deploy MES Hard-Stops and Embedded QA.
*   **Action:** Establish the Certified Material Supermarket (capped at 30 days cover).
*   **Action:** Launch Daily Traffic Control and implement the Sales Margin Hurdle Rate.

**Months 4-6: Flow & Modularity (Target: 8 Weeks)**
*   **Action:** Reorganize shop floor into cellular flows.
*   **Action:** Finalize top 5 Modular Sub-Assemblies and begin pre-building.
*   **Action:** Integrate smart tools for automated quality data capture.

**Months 7-9: Pull & Optimize (Target: 6 Weeks)**
*   **Action:** Implement finite capacity pull-scheduling (CONWIP).
*   **Action:** Transition to the new Shared KPI bonus structure.
*   **Action:** Achieve Dock-to-Stock status for the top 20% of suppliers.

**Months 10-12: Sustain & Scale**
*   **Action:** Achieve full Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP) maturity.
*   **Action:** Expand modular configurations to cover **80% of the product mix**.

---

## CONCLUSION
Project Velocity is not about asking our teams to work faster; it is about redesigning our system so that speed is the natural byproduct of flow. By decoupling certified materials, hard-locking quality gates in the MES, and financially aligning our sales incentives with operational realities, we will eliminate the systemic causes of our past warranty spikes. 

Together, Production, Quality, and Sales will deliver the **6-week lead time** our market demands, with the **zero-defect quality** our reputation requires.
