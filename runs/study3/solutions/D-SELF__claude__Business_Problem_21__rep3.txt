## PART 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1 PAGE)

### Situation
Our five-warehouse network faces structural headwinds: **35% annual picker turnover, 2.1–3.2% error rates damaging customer relationships, and rising labor costs.** Automation vendors promise 3-year payback, but competitor's failed rollout (national media coverage) and our Site 1 employment concentration (20% of local workforce) create substantial execution and reputational risk.

### Recommended Strategy
**Phased 36-month automation roadmap with **revised 3.5-year payback expectation** (not vendor-claimed 3.0 years):**

| **Phase** | **Sites** | **Timeline** | **Automation Scope** | **Capex** | **Workforce Reduction** | **Key Risk Mitigation** |
|-----------|----------|------------|----------------------|----------|----------------------|--------------------------|
| **1 (Low-risk pilot)** | 3, 4 | Mo 1–12 | Sortation + putaway + pick optimization | $5.3M | 65 FTE (32%) via attrition | Parallel running; 95%+ uptime gate |
| **2 (Mid-scale expansion)** | 2, 5 | Mo 10–22 | Sortation + putaway + pick opt. + packing automation | $10.6M | 115 FTE (33%) via attrition + redeployment | Phase 1 lessons applied; decision gate at Mo 22 |
| **3 (Anchor site)** | 1 | Mo 20–42 | Full network: conveyor + sortation + putaway + robotics + packing + redundancy | $18.2M | 140 FTE (37%); 67 FTE voluntary separation + redeployment | **Extended to 22 months** (vs. original 16); community engagement; decision gate at Mo 30 |

**Total investment: $49.75M** (capex $34M + workforce transition $8.3M + contingency $7.4M)
**Total workforce retained: 760 FTE** (81% of baseline 935 FTE; 615 operations + 145 new tech roles)

### Key Risks & Mitigations
| **Risk** | **Severity** | **Mitigation** |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Capex overrun (15–25% probable) | High | $55M authority cap; monthly burn tracking; Mo 12 gate |
| Redeployment failure (40–45% of claimed roles) | High | Competency pre-screening; Core Operator program (40–50 FTE tenure-protected); enhanced VER $2.97M |
| Site 1 service disruption (Phase 3 complexity) | Critical | Extended timeline (22 mo vs. 16); dual-running discipline; weekly customer calls; SLA guarantees |
| Customer churn (1–5% revenue at risk) | High | Customer success playbook; automated service credits; executive relationship reinforcement |
| Union organizing (15–20% probability) | Medium | Pre-negotiation with unionized sites; labor-management committee with decision authority |
| Staff morale collapse / litigation | Medium | Transparent monthly KPI dashboard; Transition Advisory Board (worker voice); enhanced separation packages |

### Expected Returns (Conservative Case)

**Year 3 onward (steady state):**
- Labor cost reduction: $13.44M/year (320 FTE × $42K avg)
- Error/shrink reduction: $1.2M/year (2.8% → 0.8% error rate)
- Throughput efficiency: $2.8M/year (35–50% picks/labor-hour gain)
- Overtime/expedite reduction: $0.6M/year
- **Total annual savings: $17.64M**

**Payback timeline (realistic):**
- **Expected: 3.3–3.5 years** (50% probability range; base case DCF)
- Optimistic: 2.8 years (15% probability; no overruns)
- Pessimistic: 3.75 years (25% probability; moderate overruns + 2% customer churn)
- Worst case: 4.2+ years (10% probability; disaster scenario with Phase 3 delays + union organizing + churn)

**5-year NPV (8% hurdle rate): +$11.4M** ✓ (positive; investment justified)

### Critical Success Factors

1. ✓ **Extend Phase 3 to 22 months** (vs. original 16-month compression)
2. ✓ **Approve $55M capex authority** (vs. $49.75M plan) to absorb overruns without contingency depletion
3. ✓ **Establish hard decision gates** (Month 12, 22, 30) with go/no-go authority to pause/pivot
4. ✓ **Hire Transition Management Office Director** (Month 1) to own workforce strategy
5. ✓ **Reset payback communications** to 3.3–3.5 years internally/externally (not 3.0-year vendor claim)
6. ✓ **Frontload Site 1 community engagement** and pre-negotiate with unions (if present)

### Board Recommendation
**CONDITIONAL APPROVAL:** Proceed with Phase 1 immediate kickoff (capex authority $5.3M). Defer Phases 2 & 3 final approval to Month 12 gate review (based on Phase 1 actual performance vs. redeployment/automation/customer SLA targets).

**Retraining structure:**

- Community college partnerships: Company ~70% funding; college ~30%

**Separation strategy rationale:**
- Phases 1–2: Rely on natural attrition (180 FTE) + redeployment/retraining (150–184 FTE) = cover 330–364 FTE of 320 target (surplus; only need ~180 attrition to reach goal)
