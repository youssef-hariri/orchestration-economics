#### 2.1C Production Process & Technical Specifications

**Manufacturing Flow:**
1. **Raw Material Preparation:** Cassava sliced uniformly (2-3mm using mandoline) → blanched (90°C, 2 min) to reduce bitterness
2. **Drying:** Low-temperature dehydration (65-70°C, 4-6 hours) in commercial tunnel dehydrator to achieve 12% moisture target
3. **Seasoning:** Standardized herb blend + chili + lime + salt applied via tumble-coating (rotating drum, 8-10 min)
4. **Crisping:** Air-frying at 160°C, 12-15 minutes (achieves desired crispness with minimal oil absorption)
5. **Cooling & Stabilization:** Product cooled to room temperature before packaging (prevents condensation in sealed packages)
6. **Packaging:** Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP) — flushed with nitrogen atmosphere; silica gel desiccant packets included

**Equipment & Facility Requirements:**
- Mandoline/slicer: $8-12K
- Commercial dehydrator (tunnel type; 3,000-4,000 kg/batch): $120-180K
- Tumble-coating drum with seasoning hopper: $40-60K
- Air-frying system (commercial; 200-300 kg/hour throughput): $150-200K
- Cooling conveyor: $30-50K
- MAP form-fill-seal packaging machine (horizontal FFFS): $200-300K
- **Total Equipment Package:** ~$550-800K

**Yield & Production Metrics:**
- **Input/Output Ratio:** 1 kg fresh cassava → 0.35 kg finished product (65% moisture loss during drying/frying)
- **Production Throughput:** 150-200 kg/hour (finished product basis); ~2,500-3,000 kg/8-hour shift
- **Monthly Capacity (single shift):** 60-75 metric tons finished product = 1.7M units 35g equivalent
- **Planned Year 1 Utilization:** 850K units/month = ~30 metric tons/month = 40% single-shift capacity (allows growth to Year 2-3)

#### 2.1G Shelf-Life, Food Safety & Packaging Innovation

**Shelf-Life Requirement & Challenge:**

Traditional aluminum-only packaging provides 4-5 months shelf-life in tropical climate (70-85% ambient humidity). Market requirement for Year 1 launch: 6-month minimum shelf-life (to support 3-month retail distribution cycle + 3-month household storage).

**Solution: Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP)**

**Technical Specification:**
- **Film Composition:** 5-layer polypropylene with oxygen-barrier coating
- **Atmospheric Composition:** Nitrogen (N₂) gas flush; target <2% oxygen residual (vs. 21% standard air)
- **Desiccant System:** Silica gel packets (2-3g per 35g package) to absorb moisture
- **Packaging Method:** Horizontal form-fill-seal (FFFS) machine with MAP capability

**Performance Validation:**
- Shelf-life target: 6+ months at 25-28°C, 75% RH (typical tropical storage)
- Expected quality metrics by month 6:
  - Oil oxidation: <5% flavor decline (vs. 20%+ without MAP)
  - Moisture gain: <2% (controlled by MAP + desiccant)
  - Microbial growth: Negligible (oxygen restriction inhibits pathogenic bacteria)

**Cost Comparison:**
- Standard aluminum film: $0.016/35g package
- MAP film (nitrogen-flushed): $0.032/35g package (2x cost, but justified by doubled shelf-life + quality assurance)

**Equipment & Operational Setup:**
- MAP packaging machine (horizontal FFFS with gas flushing): $200-300K capital cost
- Operational cost per unit: $0.032/package (film + nitrogen gas + labor + maintenance allocation)
- Nitrogen supply: On-site generation via liquid nitrogen tank (cost ~$800/month for continuous production)

**Food Safety & Quality Assurance:**

| Testing Protocol | Frequency | Method | Cost/Batch | Pass Criteria |
|-----------------|-----------|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Microbiological (Salmonella, E. coli)** | Every batch | ISO standard methods | $200-300 | Absent in 25g sample |
| **Moisture Content** | Every batch | Karl Fischer titration | $100 | 10-14% target |
| **Water Activity (Aw)** | Monthly composite | AOAC method | $150 | <0.60 (prevents mold growth) |
| **Sensory Evaluation** | Weekly during shelf-life testing | Trained panel (5-10 assessors) | $50/week | No off-flavors; texture consistency |
| **Accelerated Shelf-Life Testing (ASLT)** | Pre-launch (one-time) | 6 weeks at 37°C + 75% RH | $4,000-6,000 | Meets 6-month equivalent performance |

**Annual Food Safety Budget (for 850K units/month):** ~$4,500-5,500
