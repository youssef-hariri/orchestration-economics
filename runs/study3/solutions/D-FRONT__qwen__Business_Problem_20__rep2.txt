### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

We are currently losing one in three competitive bids due to our 14-week lead time, while our fastest competitors quote 6 weeks. Our previous attempt to compress this schedule resulted in a catastrophic spike in warranty claims that took two years to resolve, largely because we attempted to rush value-added work and bypass certified quality checks. Furthermore, our Sales, Production, and Quality departments are currently operating in silos, measuring success by conflicting local metrics.

**Project Velocity & Integrity** is our definitive operational turnaround plan. Our objective is to reduce quoted lead times from 14 weeks to 6 weeks within 24 weeks, **without compromising a single certified quality standard.** 

We will achieve this not by rushing the work, but by ruthlessly eliminating administrative lag and wait times, hard-coding quality into the physical workflow, and structurally aligning our organization around shared financial and operational outcomes. This document outlines the strategic pillars, execution roadmap, and resource requirements required to reclaim our market share.

---

### **STRATEGIC PILLAR 1: PRECISION SCHEDULING & PHYSICAL REALITIES**

Heavy industrial equipment manufacturing is governed by physical and chemical constraints (e.g., weldment stress-relieving, paint curing, thermal treatments). Our previous failure stemmed from treating mandatory process times as "waste." We will compress the schedule by targeting true non-value-added wait times while strictly protecting physical realities.

**1. Realistic Value Stream Mapping (VSM) & Data Governance**
*   **Protect the Physics:** The VSM will explicitly map and protect all mandatory physical and chemical cure/cool times as fixed durations. 
*   **Master Data Cleansing:** We cannot quote accurate lead times with inaccurate data. We will launch a 4-week data sprint to audit and correct Bills of Materials (BOMs) and routing times. *Note: This initial sprint will be strictly scoped to our pilot product family to ensure >98% accuracy before scaling.*

**2. Optimized Flow for Heavy Manufacturing**
*   **Small-Batch & Continuous Flow:** We are abandoning theoretical "single-piece flow." We will implement *optimized small-batch flow* and continuous flow where physically possible, respecting machine bed sizes and crane capacities to drastically reduce Work-in-Process (WIP) queue times.
*   **Cellular Manufacturing:** Reorganizing the shop floor into product-family cells to minimize material movement and keep small batches moving smoothly.

**3. Strategic Inventory & Supplier Integration**
*   **Targeted Decoupling:** We will establish "supermarkets" strictly for highly standardized, low-variability components (e.g., standard fasteners, common harnesses) to prevent obsolescence. Highly engineered sub-assemblies will remain strictly Make-to-Order (MTO).
*   **Expedited Incoming Inspection:** We will allocate dedicated QA headcount, equipment, and floor space to create expedited incoming inspection lanes for critical long-lead components, clearing parts in hours rather than days. *(Budget approval required for dedicated QA FTEs).*

---

### **STRATEGIC PILLAR 2: UNCOMPROMISING QUALITY INTEGRATION**

Faster flow increases the "velocity of defects." If a defect occurs in a continuous flow, it moves downstream faster. We will protect our certified standards by making quality inescapable, physically verified, and integrated into the flow.

**1. Shift Quality Left & Measurement Integrity**
*   **Mandatory Gauge R&R:** We will not rely on operator intuition. We mandate a formal Measurement System Analysis (Gauge R&R) for all operator-led inspections to ensure absolute measurement reliability.
*   **Environmental Compliance:** Embedded floor inspection stations will be upgraded to meet the exact environmental (temperature, vibration) and calibration standards of our central QA lab.

**2. Managing the "Velocity of Defects"**
*   **Andon (Stop-the-Line) Authority:** Operators are granted absolute authority to halt the cell if a defect is suspected. 
*   **Defect Containment Time:** We will track the time from defect discovery to containment. Rapid feedback loops will ensure root-cause analysis happens at the station immediately, not days later in a rework loop.

**3. System Hard-Stops & Controlled Exceptions**
*   **MES Hard-Stops:** The Manufacturing Execution System (MES) will physically prevent an operator from scanning a part to the next operation without a digital QA sign-off.
*   **Exception Management Protocol:** To prevent a "workaround culture," bypassing a hard-stop requires a formal Material Review Board (MRB) sign-off. *To prevent after-hours production halts during true emergencies, we will establish a strict escalation path requiring dual sign-off from the Plant Manager and Quality Director.* All exceptions will be audited weekly.

---

### **STRATEGIC PILLAR 3: DISMANTLING SILOS THROUGH STRUCTURAL ALIGNMENT**

The current blame culture is a symptom of conflicting local metrics. Sales is measured on revenue, Production on utilization, and Quality on defect catch rates. We will unify the organization by structurally balancing these metrics through compensation modifiers and rigorous change management.

**1. Compensation Modifiers**
*   **Sales:** Base commissions remain, but are subject to a modifier. Commissions are paid at 100% *only* if the order achieves On-Time In-Full (OTIF) delivery and First Pass Yield (FPY) targets. This eliminates quoting impossible dates to win bad business.
*   **Production:** Utilization bonuses will be modified by FPY and internal rework costs. Production cannot run massive, inefficient batches just to maximize utilization if it generates scrap.
*   **Quality:** QA’s primary KPI shifts from "defects caught at final inspection" to "Cost of Poor Quality (COPQ) and Defect Containment Time," transitioning QA from an enforcement role to a coaching role.

**2. Data-Gated Available-to-Promise (ATP)**
*   Once the Master Data Cleansing Sprint achieves >98% accuracy, we will activate the ATP system. Sales will no longer quote arbitrary lead times; the system will automatically quote based on real-time, validated capacity and material availability.

**3. Change Management & Cultural Shift**
*   We will launch a dedicated Change Management workstream to address entrenched behaviors. This includes comprehensive training on digital travelers, clear communication on the "why" behind the changes, and active resistance management by floor supervisors.
*   **Joint Gemba Walks:** Mandatory, weekly 30-minute shop-floor walks attended by the heads of Sales, Production, and Quality to observe flow, identify bottlenecks, and build cross-functional empathy.

### **EXECUTION ROADMAP & RISK MITIGATION**

We will execute this transformation in three disciplined, gated phases over 24 weeks. We will not execute a "big bang" reduction; we will earn the 6-week lead time through proven operational stability.

*   **Phase 1: Foundation & Pilot (Weeks 1-8)**
    *   Execute scoped Master Data Cleansing Sprint for the pilot product family.
    *   Conduct Realistic VSM and pilot optimized small-batch flow with embedded QA.
    *   *Target:* Reduce pilot lead time to **10 weeks**.
*   **Phase 2: Scale & De-Risk (Weeks 9-16)**
    *   Roll out targeted supermarkets, expedited incoming inspection, and plant-wide Change Management.
    *   Implement Andon protocols and track Defect Containment Time.
    *   *Target:* Reduce plant-wide lead time to **8 weeks**.
*   **Phase 3: Optimization & System Lock (Weeks 17-24)**
    *   Activate the ATP system (contingent on >98% master data accuracy).
    *   Operationalize MES hard-stops, exception protocols, and compensation modifiers.
    *   *Target:* Achieve the **6-week** quoted lead time.

**Leading Indicator Monitoring:**
We will not wait for warranty claims (a lagging indicator) to detect quality slippage. We will track **First Pass Yield**, **In-Process Rework Hours**, and **Defect Containment Time** weekly. If Defect Containment Time increases or FPY degrades by more than 5% during any phase, schedule compression will be immediately paused until the root-cause is resolved.
