# Executive Operations Improvement Plan: Project "Velocity Integrity"

**To:** C-Suite Executive Committee  
**From:** Office of Operations  
**Subject:** Transitioning to 6-Week Lead Time via Integrated Value-Stream Optimization  

---

### 1. Executive Summary
We are currently quoting 14-week lead times while operating with a process architecture that relies on "inspection-gated silos." This leads to unnecessary rework, inventory bloat, and a 33% loss in sales conversion. **Project Velocity Integrity** transforms our operational model from a sequential, reactive system to a parallel, "built-in-quality" system. This plan achieves a 6-week lead time without compromising quality, utilizing a 90-day pilot-to-rollout roadmap.

---

### 2. The Strategic Pillars of Transformation

#### Pillar I: From "Gatekeeping" to "In-Line Validation"
We will eliminate the final-stage "Quality Wall," which is the primary source of hidden rework.
*   **Action:** Implement **Critical-to-Quality (CTQ) Digital Handshakes.** No assembly step advances without digital verification of compliance.
*   **Result:** Errors are trapped at the point of origin, preventing the "rework-at-the-end" phenomenon that triggered past warranty spikes.

#### Pillar II: Strategic Postponement & Buffer Management
To decouple production from raw material delays:
*   **Action:** Transition to a **Configure-to-Order (CTO) model.** We will build and stock standardized "Base Units" and perform final customization only upon order confirmation. 
*   **Result:** By buffering the 20% of parts that cause 80% of delays, we compress the assembly-to-shipment window to 6 weeks.

#### Pillar III: Supplier & Schedule Synchronization
*   **Action:** Establish a **4-week "Frozen Zone."** During this window, order specs are locked, eliminating the "Urgency Tax" caused by last-minute schedule changes.
*   **Action:** Transition to **Pull-Signal VMI** with Tier-1 suppliers, granting them visibility into our rolling 12-week forecast to automate replenishment.

---

### 3. The "Warranty Firewall": Safety & Compliance
To ensure speed does not degrade reliability, we are implementing a **Dual-Gate Verification Protocol**:
1.  **The Shadow Gate:** For 90 days, we run the new in-line protocol alongside the legacy final-inspection. We will only sunset the legacy gate after 10 consecutive units show zero variance between the two methods.
2.  **Telemetry-Driven Audits:** Every warranty claim will trigger an immediate cross-functional review of the specific unit’s production telemetry, closing the loop on process failures within 48 hours.

---

### 4. Cultural & Functional Alignment
Silo-based feuding will be resolved by replacing functional KPIs with **Value-Stream KPIs**. 

| Role | New Primary Metric |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sales** | Committed Lead-Time Accuracy |
| **Production** | Throughput + First-Pass Yield (FPY) |
| **Quality** | Process Capability (Cpk) Index |

**Joint Accountability:** A portion of the leadership team's quarterly bonus is now linked to **"Perfect Order Lead Time."** If the lead time slips or warranty claims rise, the entire leadership team is incentivized to pivot back to stability.

---

### 5. Implementation Roadmap

*   **Month 1 (Infrastructure):** Install digital log sensors; execute 5-day Value Stream Mapping (VSM) to finalize the "Base Unit" inventory profile.
*   **Month 2 (Pilot Phase):** Launch the "Synchronous Build" on one product line. Initiate the "Shadow Gate" to validate data integrity.
*   **Month 3 (Hardening):** Review pilot data. If metrics align, decommission the final inspection wall and initiate staff training on the new flow.
*   **Month 4 (Go-Live):** Formalize the 6-week quote policy for the sales team, backed by the 4-week "Frozen Zone" schedule.

---

### 6. Stakeholder Approval
The transition to a 6-week lead time is not a reduction in quality; it is a **reduction in idle time.** By shifting our focus from *inspecting the product* to *certifying the process*, we create a leaner, faster, and more reliable manufacturing engine. 

**Recommended Action:** Approve the 90-day pilot phase to begin immediate VSM sessions.
