**New Product Line for the Republic of Korea: Occasion-Locked, Terroir-Anchored Snack Portfolio**

---

### Target Market Rationale

South Korea’s ₩5.8 trillion snack market is governed by **contextual friction**, not merely flavor preference. Over seventy percent of impulse trials occur in convenience stores (CU, GS25, 7-Eleven), where consumers purchase the occasion first and the taste second. Occasion architecture is rigid: snacks must survive late-night *hof* beer-hall tables without losing crunch, silent *gongbibang* study cafés without making noise or greasing keyboards, immersive *PC bang* gaming sessions without soiling controllers, and adult wellness routines without fried-chip guilt.

Local palates demand **layered flavor logic** (*mat-chaeng-joong*: spicy-sweet-salty balance) and extreme textural contrast (*kkadak* shattering crispness, elastic chew, or meltaway crumb). Simultaneously, a powerful **“Newtro”** cultural current rewards heritage ingredients—*jocheong*, *mugeunji*, *gochujang*—when presented through contemporary aesthetics. Finally, Korean planograms require rotational balance; an all-savory launch risks incomplete shelf-set authorization against incumbent giants like Orion and Nongshim.

This five-SKU portfolio solves specific physical, social, and sensory pain points while anchoring every product in **named Korean provenance** (Jeju, Uiseong, Sunchang, Goheung, Wando, Gyeongsang-do). It spans social drinking, silent focus, immersive gaming, controlled wellness, and commute-friendly confectionery—delivering a production-ready line that is technically validated, MFDS-compliant, and retail-merchandising compatible.

## Concept 1: Mugeunji, Kelp & East Sea Skipjack Crunch Twists
**Occasion:** Social Drinking / *Anju* Sharing

**Description**  
Extruded helical rice-and-corn twists engineered with a ridged, wave-cut surface to maximize audible crunch (*kkadak*) and seasoning retention. Designed for resealable table-center sharing during late-night drinking rituals, the format resists humidity and maintains fracture force across a multi-hour *honsul* session.

**Key Flavors & Ingredients**  
- **3-year Jeju/Haenam *mugeunji*** (aged kimchi) for lactic depth and garlic intensity  
- **East Sea skipjack (*donghae won-chamchi*)**—smoked at reduced intensity and backfilled with **Wando kelp** extract and **Wando shiitake** guanylates to deliver clean coastal umami without polarizing fish-funk  
- Gyeongsang-do *gochugaru*, domestic perilla oil, and a honey-malt sweet finish  
- *Mat-chaeng-joong* arc (umami → heat → sweet) achieved via a **Multi-Modal Umami System** that keeps sodium **<480 mg per 30 g** without metallic potassium chloride off-notes  

**Packaging**  
**120 g matte black stand-up pouch** with holographic foil accents, tactile grain finish, and resealable zip-lock for *yasik* fridge stashing. Bold geometric Hangul typography signals “dark luxury anju.” Film carries honest Korean waste-separation labeling for *Resource Circulation Act* compliance.

**Consumption Occasions**  
Late-night *hof* tables, *noraebang* pre-gaming, **Chimaek home-replacement** (beer + snack without fried-chicken delivery), and solo drama-bingeing (*honsul*) consumed over 2–3 hours. Positioned adjacent to craft beer and fruit soju in CVS cooler bays.

## Concept 2: Injeolmi Jocheong Rice Shortbread Cubes
**Occasion:** Silent Grazing / Study & Office

**Description**  
Bite-sized cubes (2 cm³) of tender rice shortbread made from a blend of rice and glutinous rice flours, bound with brown rice syrup and plant-based fat. The texture is **sandy and friable**—yielding a quiet, meltaway crumb with zero adhesive stretch, zero loud shatter, and zero dental risk. Dusted with roasted soybean powder to evoke grandmother’s *son-mat* (hand-taste) in a modern, jaw-friendly shell.

**Key Flavors & Ingredients**  
- **Brown rice syrup (*jocheong*)** as the sole sweetener—signaling *Newtro* clean-label nostalgia and avoiding refined white sugar (*seoltang*)  
- Domestic roasted soybean powder (*konggaru*) and a pinch of **Jeju black sesame** for toasted depth  
- Korean rice flour and glutinous rice flour; vegan-friendly, no egg wash  

**Packaging**  
**35 g individual pillow packs** with quiet tear-notch opening, sold in a **6-count minimalist kraft box**. Non-greasy exterior ensures zero keyboard or touchscreen residue. On-pack messaging frames the format as a **“*kkanbukkkanbu* crispy *son-mat* tribute”** to manage texture expectations against traditional elastic *tteok*.

**Consumption Occasions**  
*Gongbibang* (study café) desk grazing, office 3 PM sugar crashes, subway/bus commuting without *bap-bus* shame, and pairing with iced Americano. Regional micro-editions (Hadong green tea dusting, Damyang bamboo charcoal) drive Instagram unboxing cycles.

## Concept 3: Yangnyeom-Haebaragi Chicken Puffs
**Occasion:** Immersive Entertainment / Gaming

**Description**  
Irregular, highly aerated corn puffs with a knobby, chicken-skin-evoking topography. Coated in a **Resistant Dextrin–Modified Starch Brittle Glaze** that dries to a non-sticky, matte shell, finished with a dry-matte tumble dusting of rice starch and roasted sunflower seed powder. Genuinely dry to the touch, leaving zero finger residue on controllers or touchscreens—**“손 안 묻는 치킨”** (Chicken that doesn’t soil your hands).

**Key Flavors & Ingredients**  
- **Sunchang *gochujang*** base and **Uiseong garlic** (Korea’s garlic PGI)  
- Korean pear (*bae*) puree for natural sweetness, rice vinegar tang, and crispy fried garlic chips  
- **Roasted sunflower seed powder (*haebaragi-garu*)**—positioned as intentional “Yangnyeom-Haebaragi” fusion rather than formulation error, replacing culturally dissonant peanut notes  

**Packaging**  
**80 g loud pop-art pouch** in saturated red/orange with comic-book typography. A clean, flexible format with no external protrusions, ensuring full compatibility with CVS vertical-hook shelving and automated planogram standards.

**Consumption Occasions**  
*PC bang* sessions, the **2 AM “chicken gap”** (when delivery apps are closed and gamers crave *yangnyeom* chicken without the wait), solo mobile gacha gaming, and youth multi-room rentals. Launch exclusively in PC bangs for 3 months before CVS youth-zone rollout.

## Concept 4: Perilla-Yuzu Seaweed Crisps
**Occasion:** Controlled Wellness / Adult *Dosirak* & Hiking

**Description**  
Thin, hexagonal crackers made from **defatted perilla flour** (upcycled from domestic oil-pressing, <12% fat), tapioca fiber, and *dashima* (kombu). Non-fried and shatteringly crisp, offering an aromatic, grown-up savor distinct from pan-Asian sesame. Positioned explicitly for adult palates; perilla’s grassy bitterness is framed as a sophisticated asset, not a pediatric-friendly flavor.

**Key Flavors & Ingredients**  
- Toasted **perilla (*deulkkae*)** seed—aromatic, grassy, and uniquely Korean  
- Wando seaweed (*dashima*) and Korean sea salt for clean umami  
- Bright **Goheung yuzu** zest to balance the perilla base  
- Antioxidant stabilization via rosemary extract and natural mixed tocopherols; nitrogen-flushed packaging prevents rancidity  

**Packaging**  
**60 g premium pouch** in muted sage and sand tones with minimalist line art. Resealable, compact, and aesthetically aligned with adult *dosirak* and wellness photography. Health claim strictly limited to MFDS-permitted **“*shik-yu-son-yu*” (rich in dietary fiber)**; honest multi-layer disposal labeling ensures regulatory compliance.

**Consumption Occasions**  
Adult office *dosirak* prestige side item, office wellness drawer stash, **hiking trail fuel** (*san-nakji*), and post-yoga studio snack. Targets the **“light mouth” (*gabyeoun ip-mat*)** consumer seeking savory satisfaction without fried-chip guilt. CVS distribution deferred until brand equity is established.

## Concept 5: Goheung Yuja & Jocheong Study Drops
**Occasion:** Commute, Study & Palate Cleansing

**Description**  
Slow-dissolving hard candy drops made with **Goheung yuzu** peel extract, **brown rice syrup (*jocheong*)**, and a touch of domestic ginger. Individually wrapped in edible rice paper to prevent sticking and eliminate wrapper noise—ideal for silent study zones. Adult-oriented subtlety delivers bright citrus with warm caramel depth, not medicinal sharpness or sour candy aggression.

**Key Flavors & Ingredients**  
- **Goheung yuzu** peel extract and zest oils  
- **Jocheong** for traditional sweetness and amber color  
- Domestic ginger for a subtle warming finish  
- Korean rice starch (edible rice-paper wrap)  

**Packaging**  
**60 g slim resealable pouch** in warm yuzu-amber and cream tones with clean, modern Hangul logotype. The edible rice-paper wrapping ensures zero crinkling, supporting quiet consumption in libraries, study cafés, and open-plan offices.

**Consumption Occasions**  
Commute mint replacement, post-lunch palate cleanser, *gongbibang* desk grazing, and iced Americano pairing. Provides the planogram-critical sweet anchor required for balanced CVS and H&B shelf sets.
