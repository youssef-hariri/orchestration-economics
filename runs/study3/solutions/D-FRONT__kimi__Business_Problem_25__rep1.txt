**PERPETUAL-TO-SUBSCRIPTION BUSINESS MODEL TRANSITION PLAN**
*Board Decision Package — Final*

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This is a **capital-structure and operating-model transformation**, not a pricing change. The company will voluntarily exchange upfront license monetization for a recurring revenue stream that commands a superior enterprise multiple. The transition is bounded by three non-negotiable outcomes:

1. **Installed-base relationship retention ≥95%** of prior-year ACV through the trough (total relationship value, not logo count alone).
2. **Sales-force earnings protection** for 18 months to prevent talent flight and customer abandonment.
3. **Covenant and cash viability** such that the company does not breach lending agreements or exhaust operating runway before the J-curve inflects.

A competitor that treated this as a billing-system migration lost 20% of its installed base. This plan treats the shift as a **customer-success, product, and financial restructuring** that happens to include a billing change. By preserving perpetual rights, making subscription a feature-superior upgrade, and pre-funding the transition tax, we bound the downside and pull forward the ARR crossover.

**Base-Case Financial Outcome (Indexed to $100M Year 0 GAAP):**
| Metric | Year 0 | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| GAAP Revenue | $100M | $87M (–13%) | $92M (+6%) | $118M (+28%) |
| Ending Net ARR | $20M | $55M | $85M | $120M |
| Subscription Mix (GAAP) | 20% | 37% | 57% | 68% |
| Operating Cash Flow | $25M | $22M | $28M | $38M |

## 3. CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION & OFFER ARCHITECTURE

### 3.1 Segmentation & Sales Policy
A uniform migration path destroys margin on price-inelastic accounts and loses price-sensitive ones.

| Segment | Definition | T-Day Sales Policy | Migration Philosophy |
|---------|-----------|--------------------|----------------------|
| **Strategic / Enterprise** (>$250k ACV) | Top 15%; complex, customized on-prem | Perpetual net-new sales sunset over 12 months; CFO approval required | Bespoke white-glove conversion; negotiated credits; maximally flexible hybrid options |
| **Mid-Market** ($25k–$250k ACV) | Standardized use cases; IT-dependent | Perpetual net-new sales cease immediately; subscription or hybrid only | Guided choice architecture with standardized credit tiers |
| **SMB / Velocity** (<$25k ACV) | Low-touch; cloud-ready | Perpetual sales cease immediately; cloud subscription only | Self-service default; simple credit calculator |

**Product Superiority by Tier:** Subscription must be *feature-superior*, not payment-different.
- **Enterprise:** Private cloud / single-tenant SaaS, dedicated success architect, advanced governance modules, API expansion packs (unavailable on perpetual).
- **Mid-Market:** Standard multi-tenant cloud, versionless updates, mobile-native access, integrated analytics.
- **SMB:** Fully managed SaaS, zero infrastructure footprint, template libraries, community support.

### 3.2 The Perpetual Contract “Bill of Rights” (Legally Binding)
Issued to every license holder before T-Day:
1. **Perpetuity Guarantee:** Existing licenses remain valid in perpetuity; no revocation, degradation, or time-bombing.
2. **Data Portability:** Export in a non-proprietary format at any time, with no fees.
3. **Support Entitlement:** Standard Sustaining Support (security patches and Severity-1 fixes) guaranteed for **4 years** post-T-Day at a fixed 18% of original license list price annually.
4. **No Auto-Conversion:** No license may be converted to subscription without an explicit, signed customer order.

### 3.3 The Loyalty Ledger (Conversion Credits)
A simple, vintage-based credit formula replaces complex depreciation schedules.

| Vintage | Credit (% of Original List Price) |
|---------|-----------------------------------|
| 0–2 years | 50% |
| 3–5 years | 30% |
| >5 years | 15% |

**Application Rules:**
- Credits are applied invoice-by-invoice at a **2:1 ratio** ($1 credit for every $2 billed) for the first 24 months.
- Unused credits expire 30 months after enrollment.
- **ASC 606 Treatment:** Material right under ASC 606-10-55-42. Deferred as a reduction to transaction price and recognized as consumed. **Contra-revenue, not OPEX.**

### 3.4 The Maintenance Bridge
Customers with active Maintenance & Support (M&S) may apply **100% of their last paid annual M&S fee** against Year 1 subscription fees if they convert within 6 months of T-Day.
- **ASC 606 Treatment:** Contract modification reducing subscription transaction price. **Contra-revenue.**

### 3.5 Anti-Stacking & Price Discipline
To prevent combinatorial discounting from destroying unit economics:
- **Election Required:** Customers may use the **Loyalty Ledger OR the Maintenance Bridge**, not both. The customer chooses the more valuable option.
- **Founders’ Rate Interaction:** The 15% early-adopter discount (§4) may be combined with either credit program only with **joint VP-Sales + VP-Finance approval**; Strategic accounts require CRO approval.
- **Effective Price Floor:** The Year 1 cash invoice (after all credits/discounts) must equal the greater of:
  - Trailing 12-month maintenance payment; or
  - If lapsed: 15% of original license list price; or
  - If purchased within 12 months with no maintenance: 25% of original license list price.

### 3.6 Hybrid & Bridge Options
- **Parallel Access (Enterprise / Mid-Market):** Retain on-prem perpetual rights *and* receive cloud access for 12 months, priced at a **10% subscription premium** in Year 1 only. After Year 1, the customer selects one environment.
- **Modular Add-On Subscription:** Keep the perpetual core and subscribe only to cloud-native modules (AI, mobile gateway). Priced at **30% above** the equivalent bundled subscription to create economic gravity toward full conversion.
- **Maintenance Bridge Sunset:** Offer closes 6 months after T-Day. The Loyalty Ledger remains open for 18 months.

### 3.7 Retention & Competitive Defense
- **Legacy Customer Success Pod:** Senior CSMs assigned exclusively to the top 20% of legacy accounts by ACV. Compensation is **70% weighted on Net Revenue Retention**, 30% on logo retention.
- **Red/Yellow/Green Scoring:** Monthly risk assessment. “Red” accounts trigger executive sponsor outreach within 48 hours and automatic authorization for one additional year of sustaining support at no charge.
- **Competitive Response Fund:** 2% of legacy maintenance revenue allocated for emergency retention concessions (e.g., supplemental Loyalty Ledger credits, temporary fee waivers) for accounts under active competitive threat.

## 4. PRICING & REVENUE RECOGNITION

### 4.1 Steady-State & Transitional Pricing
- **Steady-State Target:** Subscription ARR set at **125% of the customer’s prior annual maintenance fee**, capturing expanded value from cloud, AI, and automatic updates.
- **Founders’ Rate:** Single, time-bound **15% discount** off subscription list price for contracts signed within 18 months of T-Day. The only discretionary discount.
- **Ramp Deals (Enterprise):** Contractual ramps permitted (e.g., $60k Y1, $100k Y2, $120k Y3) provided the full TCV is committed at signature.

### 4.2 Term Structure & Cash Protection
- **Default to Annual Prepay.** Monthly billing available only at a **25% premium** with mandatory auto-renewal.
- **Multi-Year Prepay Incentives:** 5% discount for 2-year prepay; 10% for 3-year prepay. Pulls forward cash to offset the GAAP revenue trough.
- **Price-Lock:** Converting customers receive a rate lock on their base package for the initial term (up to 3 years). Annual escalators thereafter capped at **CPI + 2.5%**, with a hard ceiling of **5%**.

### 4.3 Migration Services
- **Enterprise:** Bundled “Migration Success Package” at **20% of Year 1 ARR** (inclusive of data migration, tenant provisioning, admin training). Delivery cost modeled at ~15% of ARR; the 5% margin loss is an accepted customer-acquisition cost.
- **Mid-Market:** Bundled at **10% of Year 1 ARR** with templated, low-touch onboarding.
- **SMB:** Self-service toolset included at no charge; cost absorbed by velocity economics.

### 4.4 Contra-Revenue Quantification (Year 1 Base Case)
All migration incentives reduce recognized subscription revenue, not sales expense:

| Incentive | Y1 Impact | Accounting Treatment |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------|
| Loyalty Ledger consumed | ~$10.9M | Material right (deferred contra-revenue) |
| Maintenance Bridge applied | ~$11.3M | Contract modification (contra-revenue) |
| Founders’ Rate discount | ~$5.3M | Standalone discount (contra-revenue) |
| **Total Y1 Contra-Revenue** | **~$27.5M** | |

**Net Booking Yield:** For every $1.00 of gross conversion ARR signed in Year 1, the company recognizes approximately **$0.62** as collectible value after contra-revenue. This is the primary driver of trough depth and is fully modeled in the base-case financials.

## 5. SALES COMPENSATION & DEAL GOVERNANCE

### 5.1 The TCV Bridge (Months 0–18)
To prevent rep income collapse, commissions are paid on **Total Contract Value (TCV)** during transition, aligning psychology with cash economics.

| Deal Type | Commission Base | Rate |
|-----------|----------------|------|
| New Subscription (3-year) | Full TCV | 7% |
| Legacy Conversion (3-year) | Full TCV | 6% + SPIF |
| Perpetual (Legacy pipeline only) | License TCV | 8% |

**Ramp Deal Reserve:** For contractual ramps, 25% of the commission is held in reserve and released pro-rata as cash is collected. If the customer cancels before the ramp completes, the rep forfeits only the unearned reserve portion—never the full commission.

### 5.2 Income Protection
- **Non-Recoverable Draw:** Quarters 1–4 post-T-Day, reps receive a guaranteed payout equal to **80% of their trailing-12-month average quarterly commissions**, provided they achieve ≥75% of blended TCV quota. Excess commissions are paid in full.
- **SPIFs:** One-time conversion bonuses paid at subscription go-live (not signature): **$1k (SMB), $5k (Mid-Market), $10k (Enterprise).**
- **Clawback Guardrail:** If a subscription customer churns or downgrades >50% within 12 months, unvested commission reserves are forfeited.

### 5.3 Role Segmentation
- **New Logo Hunters:** 100% on new TCV. Quotas in TCV for Years 1–2, shifting to ARR in Year 3.
- **Legacy Conversion Specialists (Overlay):** 50% on conversion TCV, 50% on installed-base NRR. Explicitly prohibited from earning on net-new deals.
- **Account Managers / CSMs:** Comped on renewal rate (target ≥92%) and expansion ARR (paid at **1.5x** the standard new-ARR rate).

### 5.4 Change Management & Enablement
- **Pilot Cohort:** A Mid-Market pilot of 10 reps processes 50 conversions before T-Day to validate Loyalty Ledger mechanics, objection handling, and TCV pacing.
- **Certification Gate:** No rep may sell subscription deals until certified on value-based selling, hybrid SKUs, and ramp structures. Target: **≥90% of reps certified 30 days before T-Day** (Readiness Gate G3).
- **Rep Advisory Council:** Rotating council of 5 top reps meets bi-weekly with CRO and CFO during the first 6 months to surface field friction.
- **Win Rooms:** Mandatory monthly deal reviews for all subscription deals >$50k TCV; Deal Desk, Legal, and Product attend.

### 5.5 Deal Desk RACI
To enforce pricing discipline and prevent ad-hoc stacking:

| Decision | Owner | Authority Limit | Escalation |
|----------|-------|----------------|------------|
| Standard SKU / Founders’ Rate only | Deal Desk Manager | <$50k TCV | n/a |
| Loyalty Ledger + Founders’ Rate stack | VP-Sales + VP-Finance | $50k–$250k TCV | CRO if >15% total discount |
| Maintenance Bridge + Founders’ Rate stack | VP-Sales + VP-Finance | $50k–$250k TCV | CRO if >15% total discount |
| Custom ramp, Parallel Access, or Strategic exceptions | CRO + CFO | >$250k TCV | CEO / Board if >25% total discount |

## 6. FINANCIAL ARCHITECTURE & INVESTOR COMMUNICATION

### 6.1 The Transition Tax (Year 1 Operating Investment)
The following one-time costs are pre-committed in the board-approved budget:

| Cost Driver | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|-------------|--------|--------|--------|
| Sales comp uplift (TCV bridge, guarantees, SPIFs) | $6.5M | $2.0M | $0.5M |
| Dual-track engineering (sustaining pod + cloud scale) | $5.0M | $3.5M | $1.5M |
| Legacy SWAT team & CS Pod | $2.0M | $1.2M | $0.4M |
| Migration services subsidy (cost > price) | $2.5M | $1.0M | $0.3M |
| Partner certification & bounties | $0.8M | $0.5M | $0.2M |
| **Total Transition OPEX** | **$16.8M** | **$8.2M** | **$2.9M** |

**Cash Flow Resilience:** Despite the GAAP trough, operating cash flow declines only modestly ($25M → $22M in Year 1) because deferred revenue builds via annual upfront collections and multi-year prepays. OCF inflects positive in Year 2.

### 6.2 Covenant & Balance-Sheet Management
- **Pre-T-Day Amendment:** All credit facilities must be renegotiated **before** public announcement. Shift from net income/EBITDA covenants to **minimum liquidity ($20M)** and **maximum net leverage (3.0x)** for 24 months.
- **ASC 606 Parallel Forecast:** Maintain a real-time bridge of Bookings → Billings → Deferred Revenue → Recognized Revenue. The board must see contract liabilities building even while GAAP revenue lags.

### 6.3 Investor Metric Transition
| Phase | Timing | Primary Guidance |
|-------|--------|------------------|
| **Metric Addition** | Months 0–6 | Continue total revenue and EPS guidance; add ARR, TCV, and NRR as supplemental primary metrics. |
| **Metric Substitution** | Months 7–18 | Shift primary guidance to **ARR growth %** and **Rule of 40** (ARR Growth % + FCF Margin %). Publish quarterly bridge slide: lost perpetual / new subscription recognized / net GAAP impact / cash flow. |
| **Pure Play** | Month 19+ | Headline metrics: ARR, NRR, gross margin, unlevered FCF. License revenue reported as legacy footnote only. |

### 6.4 Governance Cadence
- **Monthly Board Executive Session (First 12 Months):** 30-minute closed session reviewing legacy churn, conversion pipeline coverage, sales-rep attainment, and cash headroom. No product or HR updates—transition vital signs only.
- **Investor Day (Day 30 post-T-Day):** CFO and CRO walk through TCV math, sales comp mechanics, and trough model to build conviction before the deepest GAAP decline.
- **Crossover Milestone:** Publicly commit to the quarter when **Subscription Bookings > Lost Perpetual Bookings + Maintenance Churn**. Achieving this proves the engine has turned over.

### 6.5 The Competitor Post-Mortem & Pre-Mortem
Every investor deck will explicitly address the competitor’s 20% churn:
- **Their Error:** Abrupt forced migration; 90-day maintenance sunset; zero conversion economics; reps incentivized to antagonize customers.
- **Our Mitigation:** Choice architecture, 4-year sustaining support, Loyalty Ledger, TCV-based rep comp, retention SWAT teams.
- **Circuit Breaker:** If legacy logo churn exceeds **8% in any quarter**, leadership will immediately (1) pause SMB migration mandates, (2) extend sustaining support sign-up windows by 6 months, and (3) deploy the Competitive Response Fund at 2x normal authorization.

## 7. OPERATING MODEL, PRODUCT & PARTNER ECOSYSTEM

### 7.1 Product & Engineering Bridge
- **Dual-Track Development (Months 0–24):** The perpetual codebase enters sustaining engineering only (security patches, Severity-1 fixes). New feature development (AI, collaboration, mobile) is subscription-exclusive. Requires a dedicated pod of **15 FTEs** (~$3.5M annually).
- **Cloud Infrastructure Honesty:** If multi-tenant SaaS is not operationally proven at T-Day, Enterprise begins on **managed hosted single-tenant** with a published roadmap to multi-tenant. Do not promise “versionless cloud updates” until the architecture is validated.
- **Compliance Gate:** Subscription environments must achieve parity with on-prem certifications (SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, industry-specific) before T-Day. A compliance gap is a deal-killer for Enterprise.

### 7.2 Partner & Channel Economics
- **Partner TCV Commissions:** Partners earn full commission on subscription TCV (not just Year 1 ARR), paid quarterly as cash is collected.
- **Migration Co-Sell:** Partners who drive a legacy conversion earn a **Migration Bounty** of 5% of the customer’s Loyalty Ledger value, paid upon subscription go-live.
- **Certification Mandate:** Partners must be certified on the subscription value proposition. Uncertified partners may not sell net-new subscription deals.

### 7.3 Win-Back Program
If a legacy customer defects to a competitor but returns within 12 months, reinstate their full Loyalty Ledger eligibility plus an additional **10% “welcome home” credit** on the original license list price.

## 8. RISK MITIGATION & CIRCUIT BREAKERS

| Risk | Mitigation | Trigger / Circuit Breaker |
|------|-----------|---------------------------|
| **Customer entitlement backlash** | Bill of Rights; perpetuity guarantee; no auto-conversion; 4-year sustaining support | If quarterly legacy churn exceeds 8%, pause SMB migration and extend support windows 6 months |
| **Sales force flight / misalignment** | TCV Bridge commissions; 80% income floor; ramp reserves; SPIFs at go-live | If <70% of reps hit 75% of TCV quota by Q2, expand non-recoverable draw to 85% |
| **Combinatorial discounting destroying unit economics** | Anti-stacking rules (Ledger OR Bridge); dual-approval for Founders’ Rate stacks; hard price floor | If average subscription yield falls below 60% of target, freeze all discretionary discounts for 30 days |
| **Competitor poaching during trough** | Legacy SWAT team; Red/Yellow/Green scoring; Competitive Response Fund (2% of maintenance) | “Red” accounts trigger executive outreach within 48 hours + automatic support extensions |
| **Product / cloud readiness gap** | Sustaining engineering pod; single-tenant bridge option; SOC 2 Type II gate | If beta NPS <40 or Severity-1 outage >2 hours, delay T-Day for Enterprise segment |
| **Prolonged hybrid state** | Subscription-exclusive features; Modular Add-On premium pricing | If Year 1 conversion <15%, activate “Conservative Scenario” cost reductions and maintain perpetual sales in Enterprise for 90 additional days |

**Probabilistic Churn Model:** The competitor’s 20% 2-year churn resulted from forced migration, zero credits, and premature sunset. Our choice architecture, support longevity, and aligned incentives model to **3–5%** 2-year installed-base churn.

**BOTTOM LINE:** This plan exchanges short-term GAAP recognition for durable enterprise value by treating the transition as a **controlled recapitalization of the customer base**. By preserving perpetual property rights, making subscription objectively superior, bounding the trough with pre-committed capital, and aligning every incentive around retention, we avoid the competitor’s 20% exodus and emerge with a high-multiple ARR foundation.
