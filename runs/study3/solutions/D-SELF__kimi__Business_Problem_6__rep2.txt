## 1. Executive Summary

This document is the definitive, market-ready campaign blueprint for the launch of *Innate Harmony* in Japan. It unites cultural resonance, creative discipline, and absolute regulatory compliance into a single, executable strategy.

*Innate Harmony* is classified and marketed strictly as a **General Food** (一般食品 / 健康補助食品): a daily food supplement containing selected lactic acid bacteria strains and dietary fiber, intended to be consumed with breakfast. It is **not** a FOSHU-approved product and does not hold a Food with Function Claims (FFC) notification. Therefore, no structure/function, disease-risk reduction, psychological, or cognitive claims may be attached to the product or brand.

**Strategic Recalibration:** Prior drafts inherited aggressive commercial targets (¥750 million, 500,000 units) from a medically differentiated launch posture. Because this final plan operates under General Food constraints—where differentiation rests on taste, transparency, and morning-table routine rather than permitted efficacy—**all financial and retail targets have been recalibrated to match the velocity of food-retail and voluntary subscription economics.** This eliminates the compliance risk created by forcing a General Food product to meet pharmaceutical-category ambitions.

The campaign anchors itself in two deeply intuitive Japanese cultural territories: the **secular food culture of fermentation and breakfast** (*hakkō*, *asa no shūkan*), and the social value of a **transparent, predictable morning routine**. Creativity is expressed through aesthetic restraint, ingredient honesty, and the quiet dignity of the morning table—not through quasi-medical promise.

Every channel, creative asset, and business mechanic satisfies:

- The Act on Securing Quality, Efficacy and Safety of Products Including Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices (PMDA/MHLW)  
- The Food Sanitation Act and Food Labeling Act (MHLW/CAA)  
- The Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations (景品表示法; CAA)  
- The Act on Specified Commercial Transactions (特定商取引法; CAA/METI)  
- The Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI)  
- The Unfair Competition Prevention Act

## 2. Regulatory & Ethical Foundation

### 2.1 Product Classification & Claims Architecture

| Classification | General Food (non-FOSHU / non-FFC) |
|---|---|
| **Permitted Messaging** | Taste, aroma, texture, form; factual ingredient declarations (e.g., “contains lactic acid bacteria and dietary fiber”); manufacturing origin; dietary routine convenience. |
| **Prohibited Messaging (Zero Tolerance)** | Structure/function claims (e.g., “improves intestinal environment,” “regulates bowels”); psychological/cognitive claims (e.g., “stress reduction,” “mental equilibrium,” “fatigue recovery”); disease prevention or therapeutic implications; implied diagnostic or curative effects. |

### 2.2 Origin Transparency

Under the Unfair Competition Prevention Act, the **exact country of manufacture, facility certification, and parent-company ownership** must appear on all packaging and digital assets. If the product is foreign-owned, this fact must be disclosed alongside any “Japan-market formulation” language. Domestic-origin (*kokusan*) cues may **only** be used if the product is literally manufactured in Japan.

### 2.3 Subscription Commerce Compliance (Act on Specified Commercial Transactions)

- **Pre-contract disclosure:** Price, delivery frequency, seller identity (name, address, contact), and contract term must be displayed before purchase confirmation.  
- **Cooling-off & cancellation:** An 8-day cooling-off period for continuous services and a **one-tap cancellation** pathway (*toroku kaijo*) must be displayed with **equal visual weight** to the sign-up button.  
- **Renewal transparency:** Renewal dates and billing amounts must be notified via LINE/email at least 5 days prior to each charge.  
- **No dark patterns:** Cancellation must not require phone calls, multi-step forms, or guilt-inducing copy.

### 2.4 Advertising & Influencer Governance

- **景品表示法:** All representations must be substantiated. General Food may not bear efficacy claims beyond factual ingredient presence.  
- **Indirect Advertising (間接広告):** Educational content (e.g., YouTube programs on fermentation culture) must be legally firewalled from product promotion. Editorial content may not contain product URLs, QR codes, or branded CTAs within the same frame as the educational material. A separate legal review is required for media adjacency.  
- **Influencer Disclosure:** All paid creator content must carry explicit **#PR** or **広告** labels. Contracts must include a **zero-tolerance rider** prohibiting health-outcome claims (e.g., “my digestion improved,” “I feel calmer”). A **pre-publish legal review workflow** is mandatory (see Appendix B).

### 2.5 Data Privacy & Biometric Ethics

- **APPI Compliance:** The LINE chatbot collects **zero health data**. Onboarding is limited to non-sensitive preferences (breakfast style, flavor preference, delivery timing).  
- **No Biometric Collection:** HRV testing, stress assessments, and all physiological measurement are eliminated from experiential marketing.

### 2.6 Food Safety & Experiential Marketing

Under the Food Sanitation Act, all pop-up sampling of *Innate Harmony* and any accompanying food or drink (e.g., amazake, miso soup) requires:

- A valid food-service permit (*eigyō kyoka*) for each location.  
- Food-handler certifications for all staff.  
- Hygienic, allergen-aware preparation protocols.  
- Clear labeling of all sample ingredients at the point of service.

### 2.7 Performance Ethics & Subscription Governance

To prevent organizational pressure from eroding consumer autonomy:

- **Retention targets are decoupled from product-team incentives.** The 8% churn ceiling is treated as a **maximum tolerance**, not a performance benchmark.  
- **Cancellation parity:** UX teams are prohibited from A/B testing cancellation-path friction. The cancellation button must remain as accessible as the sign-up button for the life of the campaign.  
- **LTV ethics:** The CAC:LTV ratio is calculated net of voluntary cancellations. Marketing leadership explicitly rewards low return/refund rates and high satisfaction scores, not forced retention.

## 4. Target Audience Segmentation

*All need-state language has been sanitized to remove physiological or therapeutic implication. Segments are defined by behavior, routine, and food preference only.*

### Primary Segment: “Daily Rhythm Professionals” (日々のリズムを大切にする働き手)

- **Demographics:** Urban professionals, ages 30–50, all genders, Tokyo/Osaka/Nagoya corridors.  
- **Psychographics:** Value predictability and self-management. Seek to maintain a consistent morning routine amid demanding schedules. View breakfast as a foundational social and personal ritual.  
- **Values:** Discretion, efficiency, transparency, and sustainable personal habits without social burden (*meiwaku* avoidance).  
- **Behavior:** Heavy LINE and YouTube users during commutes; research via Yahoo! Japan and word-of-mouth forums; shop for function; appreciate clear labeling.  
- **Need State:** A discreet, transparent food product that fits into their existing breakfast routine as part of normal daily food life.

### Secondary Segment: “Active Seniors” (活力的シニア)

- **Demographics:** Active retirees, ages 60–75, financially secure.  
- **Psychographics:** Focused on maintaining daily mobility and social engagement. Interested in dietary traditions and long-term food-based wellness rather than anti-aging interventions.  
- **Values:** Independence, family harmony, evidence-based nutrition, and domestic transparency.  
- **Behavior:** Consume print media (*Nikkei Business*, weekly magazines), watch terrestrial TV, and shop at established supermarkets and department-store food halls.  
- **Need State:** A trusted daily food item to accompany their morning meal and sustain an active, socially engaged lifestyle.

### Tertiary Segment: “Household Wellness Partners” (家庭の健康パートナー)

- **Demographics:** Adults, ages 35–50, in dual-income or varied-income households, **all genders**, who participate in household purchasing decisions.  
- **Psychographics:** Prioritize safe, clean-label products for family nutrition. Seek reliable, low-burden additions to the household morning routine.  
- **Values:** Ingredient purity, child safety, environmental responsibility, and value-for-money.  
- **Behavior:** Active on Instagram and lifestyle media; influenced by peer recommendations and explanatory native content about food and family routines.  
- **Need State:** A safe, transparently produced food product that can be incorporated into any family member’s morning meal.

## 5. Core Messaging & Positioning

### Positioning Statement

*For the modern Japanese professional and active senior who values a steady morning rhythm, Innate Harmony is a daily food supplement containing lactic acid bacteria and dietary fiber, designed to be taken with breakfast. Unlike aggressive stimulants or unverified health powders, it offers a transparent, clearly sourced formulation, intended as a simple addition to your morning table.*

### Value Proposition

“Transparent ingredients and a quiet place at your morning table.”

### Messaging Pillars

1. **Transparent Origin & Process (原料と製造、正直に):** Clearly state manufacturing location, strain documentation, and third-party analysis. Trust is built through verifiable facts, not vague “domestic trust” cues.  
2. **Morning Food Culture (朝の食文化):** Position the product within Japan’s heritage of fermented morning foods (miso, natto, pickles). Frame the supplement as a convenient, modern complement to that tradition—not a replacement for whole foods or rest.  
3. **Unforced Continuity (無理のない継続):** Emphasize that dietary habits succeed when they are gentle and sustainable. No urgency, no “correction,” no before/after narrative.  
4. **Dietary Routine (日々の食生活):** All consumer-facing copy addresses the product solely as part of daily food intake and routine. No connection to workplace productivity, mental function, or emotional states.

### Tagline

**“毎朝、食卓から始まる。”**  
*(It begins from the breakfast table, every morning.)*

### Tone of Voice

Humble, transparent, grounded in food culture, and understated. Absent of all therapeutic, psychological, or urgency-driven language.

### Mandatory Regulatory Footer (All Communications)

> *「本品は、特定保健用食品とは異なり、消費者庁長官による個別審査を受けたものではありません。」*  
> *(This product has not undergone individual review by the Consumer Affairs Agency Commissioner and is not a Food for Specified Health Uses.)*  
>  
> *Additional advisory: “This product is a food supplement, not a medicine. Persistent physical discomfort should be discussed with a medical professional.”*

## 7. Creative Concept Overview

### Concept Name: “朝のリズム” (*Asa no Rhythm* / Morning Rhythm)

#### Strategic Rationale

This concept replaces all prior abstract or appropriative metaphors with a culturally intuitive, secular focus on the Japanese morning. It honors *nagare* (flow) and the social value of a predictable daily start without medicalizing the body or commodifying religious heritage. The product is framed not as a mechanism to control the body, but as a familiar, welcome object at the breakfast table.

#### Visual Identity

- **Color Palette:** Moss green (natural food), warm stone grey (urban stability), washi off-white (simplicity), and soft amber (morning light). **Avoid pure white as a dominant field** due to strong mourning associations.  
- **Photography Style:** Morning tablescapes, diffused dawn light, steam rising from a cup, and honest macro shots of the powder/sachet alongside whole fermented foods (miso, vegetables, rice). Soft focus, abundant negative space, and natural shadows. No clinical laboratory imagery, no “inner body” graphics implying therapeutic mechanism.  
- **Packaging:** Ultra-slim, pocket-sized daily pouches for discretion and portability. Exterior uses recycled paper texture with minimal kanji, a clear ingredient list, and a simple circular motif evoking a plate or morning sun. **Removed:** *Hanko* seal motif and allusions to legal authority.  
- **Typography:** Clean, humanist sans-serif for maximum legibility. No brush-stroke accents.

#### Hero Asset: Launch Film (60 seconds)

- **Opening:** Dawn over a quiet Tokyo residential street; light entering a kitchen window.  
- **Narrative Arc:** A professional tearing open a sachet beside a bowl of rice and miso soup; a retiree stirring supplement into amazake at a wooden table; a parent pouring the powder into a glass of water while a child eats breakfast nearby. Diverse ages and genders shown with equal narrative weight.  
- **Voiceover:** *“Morning begins with what you choose for the table. A simple addition to your breakfast ritual. Every morning, it begins from the breakfast table.”*  
- **Closing:** Product on a breakfast table beside whole foods. No price, no urgency CTA. Tagline and QR code to the LINE recipe channel.

#### Key Copy Lines

- **“毎朝、食卓から始まる。”** *(It begins from the breakfast table, every morning.)*  
- **“無理なく、続けられる朝の時間。”** *(A morning time you can continue without strain.)*  
- **“原料と製造、正直に。”** *(Honest about ingredients and manufacturing.)*

## 9. Measurement & Optimization Framework

### Performance Metrics

- **Brand Health:** Quarterly brand lift studies measuring aided awareness, consideration, and perceived transparency.  
- **Digital:** LINE open/click-through rates, YouTube view-through rate (VTR), Instagram engagement rate, native article time-on-page.  
- **Commerce:** DTC conversion rate, subscription sign-up rate, 90-day retention, average order value (AOV), **voluntary churn rate**.  
- **Retail:** Sell-through rate by chain, trial-to-full-size conversion from sachets, staff feedback on display clarity.

### Ethical & Regulatory Guardrails

- **Quarterly Legal Review:** Conducted by a Japan-based food-law attorney and regulatory affairs specialist.  
- **Quarterly Bioethical Review:** Conducted by an independent bioethicist to monitor for medicalization of social stress or body-shame triggers.  
- **Zero-Tolerance Policy:** Any consumer-facing copy implying disease treatment, fatigue recovery, cognitive benefit, stress reduction, or “travel digestion” protection triggers immediate withdrawal and revision.  
- **Influencer Pre-Approval:** All creator content must pass legal review **before** publication. Post-hoc auditing is insufficient. Target: 100% pre-publish legal approval rate.  
- **Comment Monitoring:** Active monitoring for consumer health claims in comments; brands are responsible for moderating user-generated efficacy assertions on their channels.

## 10. Compliance Appendices

### Appendix A: Manufacturing Transparency Checklist

- [ ] Country of manufacture stated on primary packaging.  
- [ ] Parent company ownership disclosed on website and digital assets.  
- [ ] If foreign-owned: “Marketed in Japan to domestic standards” used in place of *kokusan* (domestic origin) claims unless literally manufactured in Japan.  
- [ ] Third-party analysis certificates available upon consumer request.

### Appendix B: Influencer Governance Protocol

- **Contract Rider:** Explicit prohibition on health-outcome claims, symptom references, and before/after narratives.  
- **Pre-Publish Workflow:** Creator submits caption, story text, and video rough-cut to brand legal team **72 hours** before posting. Approval stamp required before content goes live.  
- **Mandatory Disclosure:** #PR or 広告 in the first line of caption; verbal disclosure within first 10 seconds of video content.  
- **Penalty Clause:** Immediate contract termination and content takedown for undisclosed medical or efficacy claims.  
- **Creator Vetting:** All 75 micro-creators screened for prior undisclosed health claims or pseudoscience associations before contract execution.

### Appendix C: Subscription Ethics & Cancellation Governance

- **Principle:** Retention must be earned through satisfaction, not friction.  
- **UX Standard:** The cancellation button must remain visually and functionally equivalent to the subscription sign-up button for the duration of the campaign.  
- **Incentive Structure:** Marketing and product-team bonuses are tied to **Net Satisfaction Score** and **organic referral rate**, not to churn suppression or cancellation-path obstruction.  
- **Audit:** Quarterly UX audit of the subscription portal by an independent consumer-rights consultant to verify compliance with the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions.

### Appendix D: Internal Language Sanitization Protocol

To prevent quasi-medical claim leakage from strategy documents into consumer-facing copy:

- **Banned Terms in Creative Briefs:** “Digestive regularity,” “gut health,” “intestinal balance,” “bowel comfort,” “stomach relief,” “recovery,” “boost,” “optimize,” “align the body,” or any physiological outcome framing.  
- **Permitted Brief Language:** “Morning routine,” “breakfast habit,” “food culture,” “taste,” “texture,” “convenience,” “ingredient origin,” “morning rhythm,” “daily table,” “continuation,” “simplicity.”  
- **Approval Workflow:** All creative briefs must receive compliance sign-off before release to agency partners.  
- **Training:** Quarterly workshops for marketing and agency personnel on General Food claim boundaries under Japanese law.
