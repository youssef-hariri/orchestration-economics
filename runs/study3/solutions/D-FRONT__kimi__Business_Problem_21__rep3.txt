**NETWORK AUTOMATION & WORKFORCE-TRANSITION ROADMAP**  
*Five-Site Distribution Network | 48-Month Program | Board-Ready Final*

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The network faces rising labor costs, 35% annual picker turnover, and error rates that threaten key accounts. This roadmap authorizes a **$58.0M total capital program** ($53.7M base scope + $4.3M Board-controlled contingency) to automate five sites over 48 months, displace 390 picker/entry roles responsibly, and deliver a risk-adjusted financial return without replicating the competitor’s national-news failure.

**Strategic spine:** Stop the bleeding first, prove integration second, scale third, and transform the flagship last. Lakeside receives an immediate Accuracy Sprint to save key accounts, while Centralia— the town’s largest employer—is locked until a Month 14 Go/No-Go gate certifies live vendor performance. The workforce compact abandons the illusion of a pure “attrition-only” exit and replaces it with a funded, staggered transition capped at **<3% involuntary separation (10 heads)** network-wide.

**Financial bottom line:** The vendor’s 3-year payback claim is replaced by an honest, fully loaded **~5.5-year payback** on total authority (5.1 years on base capital). Incremental steady-state OPEX—maintenance, software, energy, and wage premiums—is explicitly priced. Cumulative net cash flow turns positive between **Months 44 and 50**. A sensitivity table models payback out to 6.6 years under low-turnover/high-bridge scenarios.

**Governance:** Capital is released by gate, not by calendar. A 15% vendor holdback ($6.9M), a Month 14 Centralia Commitment Gate with objective technical criteria, a Month 18 independent audit, and a monthly Board payback tracker ensure disciplined execution.

## 1. PER-SITE AUTOMATION PLAN

**Guiding Logic:** Pilot for *integration* maturity, not scale; prove on a complex mid-size site; and transform Centralia only after the WES and vendor have live operational credentials.

| Site | Profile | Wave | Timing | Automation Degree & Technology | Labor Impact (Net Reduction) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Lakeside** | 500K sq ft, 130 staff, **high-error specialty** | **Accuracy Sprint** | **Months 1–4** | **Manual-process patch:** Vision weigh-stations, scan-verification gates, directed-pack protocols. Goal: stem key-account defections *now* while automation is engineered. | 0 (retain all; upskill) |
| **Gulfport** | 400K sq ft, 100 staff, lowest complexity | **Integration Pilot** | **Months 1–12** | **Level 2 – Assisted:** AMR-to-person, voice/ring scanning, conveyor pack-out, WES overlay on existing WMS. Proves software and human-machine orchestration. | –30 pickers |
| **Lakeside** | High-error specialty | **Standardize** | **Months 5–14** | **Level 3 – Accuracy-First:** GTP shuttles (top 50% SKUs), vision pick-assist, in-line weight/cube/dim verification, automated print-and-apply. | –40 pickers |
| **Riverside** | 850K sq ft, 220 staff, high SKU variety | **Prove** | **Months 10–22** | **Level 3 – GTP Core:** Shuttle-based GTP (top 60% SKUs), AMR restocking, auto-bag/tape, WES-directed labor. First true stress-test of congestion logic. | –80 pickers |
| **Piedmont** | 750K sq ft, 190 staff, stable grocery/CPG | **Scale** | **Months 18–28** | **Level 3 – Proven Clone:** Riverside playbook adapted; GTP + sort-to-route + auto-manifesting. | –70 pickers |
| **Centralia** | 1.4M sq ft, 420 staff, **town’s largest employer** | **Transform** | **Months 28–44** | **Level 4 – Dense:** Shuttle-based AS/RS, GTP overflow, robotic depalletizing/palletizing, high-speed shipping sorter, WES as system of orchestration. | –170 pickers over 18 months |

**Capital Lock-Up Discipline:** Centralia’s long-lead AS/RS is **not ordered until Month 14**. By then, Gulfport has 90 days of live WES data and Riverside’s design is finalized. Vendor contracts for Centralia include **performance bonds tied to throughput guarantees** and a termination-for-convenience clause if Riverside fails to hit design UPLH by Month 20.

**Network Fulfillment Contingency:** By **Month 2**, Operations will complete a **lane-level SKU dual-sourcing analysis**. Any SKU picked exclusively at one site must have a pre-qualified alternate facility, a **10% inter-facility surge buffer**, and pre-negotiated transfer rates before that site enters Shadow Build. Centralia-unique large-format SKUs must have manual fallback racking identified before AS/RS commissioning.

## 3. WORKFORCE-TRANSITION PLAN

**The Attrition Reality**  
The 35% network turnover is concentrated among 0–12 month hires, not the tenured population the company seeks to retain. Under staggered, site-specific hiring freezes, natural attrition is expected to capture **120 heads (31%)** of the 390 total displacements. If automation succeeds in stabilizing the workforce (target: <15% voluntary turnover), the Transition Reserve absorbs the gap via bridge roles and expanded voluntary slots—not involuntary layoffs.

**Reconciled Workforce Outcome Math**

| Outcome Channel | Heads | % of Total | Funding Mechanism |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Natural attrition (staggered freezes) | 120 | 31% | Baseline payroll lapse |
| Network redeployment / traveling go-live teams | 40 | 10% | Baseline payroll |
| Direct tech retrain (Automation Tech I/II, Flow Coordinator) | 70 | 18% | Company-funded certification |
| Indirect redeploy (QC, inbound, exception mgmt, safety) | 110 | 28% | Baseline payroll (lateral/promotion) |
| Voluntary separation (enhanced package) | 40 | 10% | Workforce Transition Reserve |
| **Involuntary separation (last resort)** | **10** | **3%** | Workforce Transition Reserve |
| **TOTAL** | **390** | **100%** | |

**Guardrails**
- **Involuntary Cap:** Target is **<3% (10 heads)** network-wide. The $2.0M Reserve funds management of up to **15% (60 heads)** through voluntary/bridge expansion without breaking the payback baseline.
- **Concentration Limit:** No single site may account for >30% of involuntary separations in any quarter. If displacement at a small site would breach this, the Workforce Council triggers traveling-pool placement or temporary bridge-role creation.
- **Certification Screening Gate (Centralia):** All internal candidates for technician roles must pass an electromechanical aptitude screen. The company funds tutoring. If the internal pass rate is <70%, a **6-month remediation window** opens before any external hiring is permitted, preserving the “Local First” covenant while protecting operational readiness.

**Centralia-Specific Covenant (Legally Segregated)**
- **No involuntary layoffs for 18 months post go-live** (Months 44–62). The 170-head reduction is achieved through: (a) the hiring freeze begun Month 22, (b) natural attrition (~110 expected over 18 months), (c) redeployment to other sites or new internal roles, and (d) voluntary packages.
- If attrition falls >20% below forecast, the program activates **bridge roles** (community coaching, safety audit, training assistant) at partial cost to avoid forced exits.
- **$400K Community Transition Fund** chartered as a restricted account (workforce coaching, local small-business micro-grants, library tech lab). Audited quarterly and reported publicly.
- **“Local First” hiring:** 100% of entry-level robot-operator and technician roles filled from existing Centralia staff for the first 12 months, subject to the certification gate above.

## 4. SERVICE-CONTINUITY SAFEGUARDS

Every site follows a **Parallel Run → Ramp → Full Cutover** protocol with explicit labor and space costs budgeted.

| Phase | Action | Duration | Cost / Resource Note |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Shadow Build** | Automated zone installed alongside legacy racking. 15% of daily volume through new system with dedicated pilot crew. | Weeks 1–4 | Duplicate crew budgeted |
| **Buffer Stock** | Pre-position **2 weeks of safety stock** for A-tier SKUs and key accounts before volume shifts. | Weeks –2 to 0 | Working-capital hold |
| **Staged Ramp** | Shift 25% → 50% → 75% → 100% volume by week, contingent on accuracy >99.5% and fill rate >97%. | Weeks 5–10 | $400K/site for SWAT temp labor and duplicate supervision |
| **SWAT Temp Labor** | National staffing partner provides 20% surge capacity. Temps absorb the learning curve; tenured staff supervise quality. | Weeks 4–9 | Charged to program OPEX, not site labor |
| **Rollback Triggers** | If accuracy <99.0% or fill rate <95% for >72 hours, automated lane reverts to legacy process until root cause resolved. | Ongoing | Legacy lanes remain staffed through Month 3 post go-live |

**Customer & Network Protections**
- **90-day advance notice** to key accounts with assigned transition liaisons.
- **Service guarantee:** Automation-attributable errors trigger automatic Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI) replenishment at company cost.
- **Network surge protocol:** If a site drops below 90% throughput during ramp, the network capacity model triggers automatic order splitting to the next-best facility.
- **Blackout:** No go-lives in November–January.

## 6. DECISION GATES & GOVERNANCE

**Monthly Rhythm**  
**Green/Yellow/Red Payback Tracker** published to the Board, showing CPU, labor-cost avoidance, and cumulative net cash flow vs. baseline.

**Remediation Gate**  
If any site is >10% behind its CPU or savings trajectory, capital release for the next wave is halted until a recovery plan is approved. Draws against the **$4.3M Board-controlled contingency** require Remediation Gate authorization, chaired by the CFO with veto authority.

**Month 14: Centralia Commitment Gate (Go/No-Go)**  
Before any Centralia long-lead equipment is ordered, the following criteria must be met and Board-certified:

| Criterion | Minimum Standard |
|:---|:---|
| **Gulfport** | WES uptime >99.5% over 90 consecutive days; UPLH sustained at ≥+35% vs. baseline for 30 days; exception-handling latency <100ms. |
| **Riverside** | Vendor performance bonds executed; detailed throughput guarantee contracts signed; facility design frozen. |
| **Lakeside** | Automation pilot zone order accuracy >99.5% for 60 days; error-credit run-rate reduced ≥50% from Month 0 baseline. |
| **Financial / Legal** | Workforce Transition Reserve ($2.0M) legally funded; Community Transition Fund charter executed. |

*Decision protocol:* Yellow defers capital release 90 days. Red triggers a pivot to a lower-automation variant (Level 2/3 hybrid) for Centralia.

**Month 18: Independent Audit**  
A third-party firm validates baseline assumptions (labor inflation, error-cost data, actual incremental OPEX) to confirm the 5.5-year payback is mathematically sound, not vendor fantasy.

**Quarterly: Workforce Council Review**  
Reviews attrition vs. displacement by site. If attrition falls >20% below forecast, Reserve funds are automatically released to bridge-role creation to prevent surprise layoffs.

**Vendor Holdback Policy**  
Fifteen percent of each vendor contract (totaling **$6.9M**) is held back until 30 days post go-live at target service levels. These holdbacks are explicit, auditable cash-flow timing items within the base scope.
