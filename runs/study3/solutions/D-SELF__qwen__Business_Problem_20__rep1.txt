**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: PROJECT VELOCITY 2.0**
**Operations Turnaround & Lead Time Compression Strategy**

**The Mandate**
Our industrial equipment division is currently quoting a 14-week lead time, resulting in the loss of one in three deals to competitors who quote 6 weeks. A previous initiative to compress this schedule resulted in a severe two-year warranty crisis due to the bypassing of critical quality steps. Furthermore, systemic silos between Sales, Production, and Quality have created conflicting priorities that stall execution. 

**Project Velocity 2.0** is a comprehensive, data-driven operational turnaround plan designed to sustainably reduce our quoted lead time to 6 weeks without compromising certified quality standards. The core philosophy of this plan is to aggressively eliminate *queue and wait times* while strictly protecting and error-proofing *processing and inspection times*.

---

### 1. STRATEGIC LEAD TIME REDUCTION TARGETS
To achieve a 6-week lead time, we must recover the 10 weeks of hidden waste currently embedded in our value stream. We will execute a phased public quote reduction (14 weeks $\rightarrow$ 8 weeks $\rightarrow$ 6 weeks) as operational stability is proven.

**Time Recovery & Process Compression:**
*   **Engineering & Administration (Target: 3 weeks $\rightarrow$ 1 week):** Implement a "Design for Manufacturability" (DFM) standard parts library and institute a strict 48-hour engineering release SLA. Custom configurations will be routed to a dedicated "Fast-Track" desk to prevent bottlenecks.
*   **Production Flow (Target: 6 weeks $\rightarrow$ 3 weeks):** Transition from batch-and-queue to continuous flow. Execute Single Minute Exchange of Die (SMED) on bottleneck CNCs to reduce batch sizes from 50 to 5. Implement a CONWIP (Constant Work in Process) loop to strictly cap Work-in-Process inventory and expose bottlenecks immediately.
*   **Supply Chain & Final Testing (Absorbing 5 weeks):** We will not compress the 48-hour final burn-in or supplier lead times. Instead, we will *absorb* this time through strategic decoupling. We will implement a Make-to-Forecast strategy for long-lead raw materials (holding 3 weeks of safety stock for 'A' items) and utilize Assemble-to-Forecast sub-assembly supermarkets, effectively moving supplier and sub-assembly wait times *ahead* of the customer order drop.

---

### 2. BULLETPROOF QUALITY ASSURANCE MECHANISMS
To ensure a repeat of the historical warranty crisis is physically impossible, we are shifting Quality from a reactive "police" function to a proactive "flow enabler." We will decouple inspection time from production flow and utilize hard-system interlocks to eliminate human error and management pressure.

**Key Quality Initiatives:**
*   **MES Hard-System Interlocks:** Integrate smart torque tools and digital curing ovens directly with the Manufacturing Execution System (MES). If an adhesive requires a 2-hour cure, the MES will physically lock the part's barcode; the operator cannot scan the part to the next station until the timer expires. Final burn-in cycles will be hardcoded to prevent the generation of a Certificate of Conformance (CoC) until the exact sequence is completed.
*   **Shift-Left Automated Inspection:** Integrate in-process Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM) probes directly into CNC machines to verify tolerances *before* unloading, eliminating the queue to the quality lab. Laser-etch 2D Data Matrix codes on critical sub-assemblies to halt the build automatically if a prior quality gate is missed.
*   **Embedded Quality & "SWAT" Rework:** Co-locate Quality technicians on the production floor to coach operators on First Pass Yield (FPY). Establish a dedicated "Quality SWAT Team" to immediately pull non-conforming parts to a shadow rework cell, ensuring the main production line never halts.
*   **Supplier Excursion Contingency:** For top-tier suppliers granted "Ship-to-Stock" status (bypassing incoming inspection), we will institute an automatic line-stop and quarantine protocol triggered by any quality excursion, followed by an expedited supplier audit.

---

### 3. CROSS-FUNCTIONAL ALIGNMENT & GOVERNANCE
The historical friction between Sales, Production, and Quality stems from misaligned incentives. We will dismantle these silos by restructuring compensation, standardizing governance, and enforcing strict Rules of Engagement (ROE).

**Alignment & Governance Strategies:**
*   **Unified "Perfect Order" Compensation:** Tear down siloed scorecards. Implement a shared dashboard tracking the **Perfect Order Percentage (POP)** (On-time + Complete + Defect-Free + Correct Documentation). **40% of the annual bonus** for the VPs of Sales, Production, and Quality will be tied directly to the company-wide POP score. Additionally, frontline Sales commissions will be restructured to pay out upon *delivery and cash collection*, rather than order booking, eliminating the incentive to sell unrealistic dates.
*   **ERP "Three-Point Sign-off" & Escalation:** Redesign the ERP workflow so an order cannot move from "Quoted" to "Booked" without digital sign-off from Sales (pricing), Production (capacity), and Quality (compliance). In the event of a deadlock on a high-value custom order, an automatic escalation path to the COO will serve as the final tie-breaker.
*   **14/14 Scheduling Rule & Sales Frozen Zone:** The first 14 days of the master schedule will be a **Hard Freeze** (no changes without Plant Manager approval). The subsequent 14 days will be a **Sliding Window** allowing quantity adjustments (+/- 15%) but no product mix changes. This provides Production with the predictability required to execute lean flow.
*   **The "Obeya" War Room:** Establish a physical Obeya room on the shop floor for daily 15-minute cross-functional stand-ups. The focus will be strictly on visual management, bottleneck resolution, and material/quality holds—eliminating finger-pointing and email delays.

---

### 4. PHASED IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP
Recognizing the complexities of heavy industrial equipment manufacturing, the integration of MES interlocks and supplier certifications will follow a realistic, phased 9-month rollout rather than an aggressive 90-day sprint.

**Phase 1: Stabilize & Lock the Gates (Months 1-3)**
*   Launch the "Three-Point Sign-off" in the ERP and implement the 14/14 Hard Freeze scheduling rule.
*   Restructure VP and Sales compensation models to align with the POP metric.
*   Install MES hard-system interlocks on all critical cure times, torque stations, and final test equipment.
*   Establish the physical Obeya room and initiate daily cross-functional huddles.

**Phase 2: Flow & Inventory Decoupling (Months 4-6)**
*   Execute SMED on top bottleneck machines and implement the CONWIP card system to cap WIP.
*   Establish the raw material decoupling point (3 weeks safety stock) and launch two-bin Kanban systems for sub-assemblies.
*   Roll out in-process CMM probing on CNC machines and transition Quality to the shop floor.
*   **Milestone:** Officially reduce the public quoted lead time to **8 weeks**.

**Phase 3: Supplier Integration & Scale (Months 7-9)**
*   Launch "Dock-to-Line" pre-kitted delivery with the top 5 suppliers, bypassing the receiving dock.
*   Ramp up "Ship-to-Stock" supplier certifications based on the Supplier Quality Rating (SQR) dashboard.
*   Expand cellular manufacturing flow to remaining product families.
*   **Milestone:** Upon 30 days of stable POP and warranty metrics at the 8-week quote, officially reduce the public quoted lead time to **6 weeks**.

---

**Conclusion**
Project Velocity 2.0 transitions our operational strategy from "hurrying the work" to "eliminating the wait." By utilizing hard-system interlocks to protect certified quality, decoupling our inventory to absorb supplier lead times, and financially aligning our leadership team around a single definition of success, we will sustainably capture market share, restore our competitive 6-week lead time, and protect our brand's quality reputation.
