**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Strategy & Turnaround Taskforce  
**Subject:** Industrial-Services Unit (ISU) Strategic Turnaround & Exit Readiness Plan

---

### 1. Executive Summary
Following three years of recurring losses and failed restructuring, the Industrial-Services Unit (ISU) currently sits in a "Structural Commodity Trap." Our analysis confirms that previous cost-cutting measures targeted labor and variable inputs while ignoring the core issue: the erosion of the unit’s value proposition. 

This assessment establishes a rigorous 12-month turnaround roadmap predicated on high-margin focus and operational "hard-stops." We recommend a **"Parallel Path" strategy**: aggressively pursuing a turnaround to prove market viability while simultaneously preparing the unit for divestiture to protect the Group’s capital.

---

### 2. Strategic Diagnosis
*   **Structural Failure:** The ISU has been competing as a commodity provider in a market where it lacks scale. Previous restructurings failed because they treated the unit as a cost center rather than a value-driver.
*   **Execution Decay:** The loss of top-tier talent ("Brain Drain") has led to operational drift, causing a feedback loop where service quality drops, driving further contract churn.
*   **The Synergy Risk:** The ISU currently relies on "Strategic Anchor Accounts." To be viable, these services must be independently competitive; otherwise, the ISU acts as a hidden tax on the Group’s profitable divisions.

---

### 3. 12-Month Turnaround Roadmap

| Phase | Strategic Focus | Primary KPI (Hard Target) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: Rationalization** | Exit low-margin services & non-core clients. | Eliminate all projects with <12% contribution margin. |
| **Q2: Cash Floor** | Operational break-even; stabilization. | Achieve Monthly EBITDA Neutrality. |
| **Q3: Value Pivot** | Upsell "Value-Added" services to Anchor clients. | Secure renewals at 10%+ price premium vs. historicals. |
| **Q4: Sustainability** | Final validation of long-term health. | Sustained >8% Trailing 3-Month EBITDA Margin. |

---

### 4. Governance & The "3-Strike" Exit Trigger
To prevent the "sunk-cost fallacy," the Board will utilize the following divestment triggers. If any single trigger is activated, the turnaround is deemed failed, and the Board will immediately shift to a full liquidation or sale process.

*   **Strike 1: Capital Consumption:** Any requirement for cash injections beyond the initial Q1 allocation.
*   **Strike 2: Anchor Churn:** A reduction in contract value or loss of any of the top 3 "Strategic Anchor" accounts.
*   **Strike 3: Revenue Velocity:** Two consecutive quarters of >5% sequential order book decline.

---

### 5. Tactical Execution: The "Silent Data Room"
We will implement a **Parallel Path** to maximize Board leverage:
1.  **Months 1–3:** Internal cleanup and process hardening.
2.  **Month 4:** Engage M&A counsel to socialize the unit as a "Strategic Carve-out."
3.  **Outcome:** If the turnaround succeeds, the Group holds a high-margin, profitable asset. If it fails, the "Silent Data Room" ensures we are positioned to divest immediately, preventing further value erosion.

---

### 6. Board Recommendation
The Board should authorize the Q1 Turnaround mandate effective immediately. However, management must prepare a data room by the end of Month 4. **Success is not defined by "trying harder," but by hitting the objective profitability markers defined in this plan.** If the Q2 break-even target is missed, the Board should pivot to an immediate divestment process. 

**This dual approach ensures the Group remains the master of its own exit, regardless of the unit's operational outcome.**
