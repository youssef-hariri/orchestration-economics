# Internal Directive: Crisis Management & Cultural Reconciliation

**To:** Global Executive Leadership, Regional Management Teams, Marketing/Creative Departments  
**From:** Office of the Chief Communications Officer  
**Subject:** Strategic Framework: International Crisis Reconciliation Model  
**Status:** Immediate Implementation Required

---

### 1. Executive Summary
Following the recent marketing campaign failure in the [Target Market], the brand is transitioning from a "Global Mandate" approach to a model of **Radical Local Humility.** We recognize that this event was not merely a creative error, but a failure of our responsibility to the culture that hosts us. We are moving from a strategy of "crisis containment" to one of **relational accountability.**

### 2. Immediate Operational Directives (Phase 1)
*   **Total Content Halt:** All campaign assets—digital, physical, and broadcast—have been suspended indefinitely. 
*   **The Apology Protocol:** All external communication regarding this event will be issued by **local leadership** in the native language. 
    *   **Mandate:** Do not use the "Global Values" defense. Do not use the passive voice. Do not attempt to explain the "intent" of the campaign. 
    *   **Focus:** Acknowledge the specific cultural/value-based breach and express accountability for the loss of community trust.
*   **Leadership Responsibility:** Regional heads are to lead the response; the Global CMO will serve only in a support capacity to ensure resources are available.

### 3. Structural Reform (Phase 2 & 3)
*   **Formalized Consultative Engagement:** We are moving from informal "listening sessions" to a **Formal Advisory Council.** 
    *   Impacted cultural leaders and community advocates will be onboarded as **paid external consultants.** 
    *   This is not a "teaching session"; it is a professional engagement where their expertise is treated as a strategic asset.
*   **Power Delegation:** All future creative concepts for this market must undergo a **mandatory ideation-phase review** by the Local Advisory Council. Local leadership now holds absolute veto power over any content that risks cultural insensitivity.
*   **Legacy-Led Philanthropy:** We are abandoning brand-led "purpose" initiatives. Instead, we will provide long-term, unconditional financial support to existing, non-profit community legacies that embody the values we were perceived to have disrespected.

### 4. Implementation Guidelines for Staff
*   **The "Silence" Policy:** During all stakeholder engagement, corporate representatives are to maintain an 80/20 listening ratio. Documentation of feedback is the priority; rebuttal is strictly prohibited.
*   **Avoiding the "Translation Trap":** Never attempt to explain a joke or wordplay. If a message is misunderstood, it has failed. We own the result, not the intent.
*   **Communication Cadence:** We reject "time-boxing" our resolution. We will provide monthly, public-facing updates on our progress in revising our internal processes until the Advisory Council determines the reconciliation period has concluded.

### 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)
We are moving away from traditional sentiment metrics. Success will be evaluated via:
1.  **Stakeholder Participation:** The willingness of local cultural leaders to continue collaborating with us on our internal process reforms.
2.  **Council Feedback Quality:** The evolution of input from our Advisory Council—moving from "correction" to "proactive guidance."
3.  **Process Integration:** The successful implementation of the Local Advisory sign-off as a permanent, non-negotiable step in our regional go-to-market process.

---

### Closing Statement
The market is not a laboratory for global creative ideas; it is a relationship that must be earned and maintained. We have violated the trust placed in us. We are now committed to the slow, necessary work of earning that trust back through consistent, respectful action. 

**This directive is effective immediately. Please align all regional operations accordingly.**
