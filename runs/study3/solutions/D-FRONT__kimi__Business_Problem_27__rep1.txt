**GROWTH-FINANCING STRATEGY**
**The Independence Stack**

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Company Profile**
*   **Revenue Run-Rate:** $40M
*   **EBITDA:** $6M (15% margin)
*   **Founders’ Ownership:** 78%
*   **Revenue Trajectory (unconstrained):** ~41% CAGR (doubling every 24 months)
*   **Competitive Window:** 18–24 months before a well-funded rival locks tier-1 supply and distribution
*   **Capital Required:** $25M over 18 months (capacity: $10M; hiring: $8M; market expansion: $7M)
*   **Market Equity Bid:** $80M pre-money
*   **Founders’ Valuation Floor:** $100M pre-money

**Strategic Imperative**  
Capture the market window without ceding board control, without accepting a down-market equity valuation as a first resort, and without encumbering operations with maintenance covenants that conflict with aggressive growth capex.

**Recommendation**  
Deploy a **simplified, milestone-staged hybrid capital structure**: a **$12M covenant-lite senior unitranche** and a **$10M subordinated growth note**, supplemented by **$2–3M in retained earnings** and a **pre-negotiated minority equity break-glass**. This preserves founder ownership at **~76%** in the base case (vs. ~59% under a pure $80M pre equity round), funds the full expansion, and closes in 8–10 weeks. If debt terms deteriorate or the rival accelerates faster than modeled, a defined equity escape hatch prevents a distress spiral.

## 2. COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS

| Dimension | Disciplined Organic | Covenant-Heavy Senior Debt | Minority Equity | Hybrid (Recommended) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Capital Available** | $2–3M/year internal only | $8–12M | Full $25M @ $80M pre | $22M external + $2–3M internal |
| **Speed to Close** | Immediate | 6–8 weeks | 8–12 weeks | **8–10 weeks** (two documents, not four) |
| **Founder Control** | Absolute | Moderate (maintenance covenants, capex limits) | Poor (board seats, vetoes, ratchets) | **Strong** (no board seats; incurrence covenants only) |
| **Dilution** | None | None (warrants possible) | **~24%** at $80M pre | **~2.5%** warrants (base case) |
| **Competitive Window Fit** | **Poor.** Slows CAGR to ~20%; rival captures 30%+ share by Year 3. | Moderate. Covenants may block aggressive hiring/capex precisely when speed matters. | **Good.** Full funding immediately. | **Good.** Full funding with no operational veto rights. |
| **Downside Resilience** | **High.** No fixed charges; survives revenue dips. | **Low.** Fixed claims on $6M EBITDA. A miss to $3M EBITDA triggers default. | **High.** Investor absorbs downside; no mandatory cash distributions. | **Moderate.** Fixed cost, but PIK toggle and bullet maturity create cushion. |
| **True Cost of Capital** | Opportunity cost: foregone EV of $50–70M if window closes. | 9–11% cash | Variable: cheaper in downside, expensive in upside (~25%+ IRR to investor). | **13–16%** all-in (cash + PIK + warrant coverage). Cheaper than equity in upside; riskier than equity in severe downside. |

**Trade-off Assessment**  
Equity is a variable cost that shares downside risk. Debt is a fixed cost with a bankruptcy put. The hybrid structure is selected not because it is "cheaper" in every scenario, but because the company is profitable, the founders prioritize independence, and the structure below contains explicit guardrails to manage downside risk.

## 3. RECOMMENDED FUNDING STRUCTURE

| Tranche | Instrument | Amount | Use of Funds | Key Terms | Dilution / Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Senior Secured Unitranche** | **$12M** | Capacity, equipment, hard assets | SOFR + 475–550 bps (~10.0–10.5%); 4-year term; interest-only for 12 months, then 36-month amortization. **Covenant-lite**: incurrence-based net leverage cap (≤3.5x EBITDA) tested only on additional borrowings. Secured by 1st lien on receivables, equipment, and IP. No personal guarantee. | **0%** equity |

| **C** | **Retained Earnings / WC Optimization** | **$2–3M** | Working capital buffer | Generated via price optimization, customer pre-deposits for capacity, and disciplined net working capital management. | **0%** |

**Pro Forma Ownership**
*   **Pre-Financing:** Founders 78.0%
*   **Post-Hybrid (Base Case):** Founders **76.1%**
*   **Post-Break-Glass (If Triggered):** Founders **~72.8%**
*   **vs. Pure Equity @ $80M Pre:** Founders **~59.4%** (before option pool refresh)

**Intercreditor Framework**  
A single, standard intercreditor agreement governs both external lenders. The senior lender holds a 1st lien and receives a **120-day standstill** on collateral enforcement ahead of the subordinated lender’s 2nd lien. No competing revenue sweeps or negative-pledge conflicts.

## 5. WALK-AWAY CONDITIONS

### Founder Walk-Aways (Reject the Source)

| Source | Walk-Away Trigger |
|---|---|
| **Senior Unitranche** | • Advance rate on receivables/equipment < **65%**<br>• Personal guarantee required<br>• Quarterly maintenance covenant (vs. incurrence-only)<br>• Hard capex limit < **120%** of budget<br>• Cash sweep triggered if FCCR < **1.15x**<br>• MAC clause without a materiality threshold |
| **Subordinated Growth Note** | • All-in cost (cash + PIK + warrant value) > **16%**<br>• Warrant coverage > **3.0%** or strike price < **$100M**<br>• Maturity < **4 years**<br>• Operating covenants restricting dividends or additional debt<br>• Cross-default tied to unitranche without a **30-day cure period** |
| **Minority Equity (Break-Glass)** | • Pre-money valuation < **$100M**<br>• Demand for a **board seat** or voting veto<br>• Participating liquidation preference or cumulative dividends<br>• Veto rights on budgets, hiring, or M&A<br>• Full-ratchet anti-dilution |
| **Organic / Retained Earnings** | • If organic growth drops below **25% CAGR**<br>• If working capital consumption exceeds **10%** of incremental revenue<br>• If competitive win-rate drops below **50%** for two consecutive quarters |

### Lender / Market Walk-Aways (Source Abandons or Rejects the Company)

| Source | Lender Walk-Away Trigger |
|---|---|
| **Senior Unitranche** | • EBITDA at close < **$4.5M**<br>• Net senior leverage > **3.0x**<br>• Top 3 customers churn to competitor<br>• Material litigation or tax lien discovered |
| **Subordinated Growth Note** | • Revenue run-rate at Month 6 < **$38M**<br>• Senior default event<br>• Loss of >20% of recurring revenue base<br>• Founder departure or material fraud |
| **Minority Equity** | • Company declines standard information rights<br>• Founders refuse 12-month exclusivity on control changes<br>• Rival secures < **15%** market (no urgency to deploy expensive equity capital) |

### Cross-Default Protocol
If either senior or subordinated debt triggers a default or Material Adverse Change, founders have a **45-day Equity Escape Hatch** to raise a forced minority round at a **$90M floor** (down from $100M if distress) to repay the sub note and cure the senior facility. If uncured, the board will initiate an orderly sale process rather than a value-destructive death spiral.

## 6. KEY RISKS & MITIGANTS

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigant |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fixed-Charge Burden** | Moderate | High | The unitranche is interest-only for 12 months. The sub note is a 5-year bullet with a PIK toggle, preserving cash in a downturn. Monthly FCCR monitoring ensures early warning. |
| **Refinancing Gap** | Moderate | High | By Month 18, the company must secure a refinancing path for the unitranche amortization or a credible equity path to repay the sub note. This is a board-level KPI. |
| **Lender Coordination / Intercreditor Friction** | Low | High | Using only two external lenders and a single intercreditor agreement eliminates the documentation paralysis of a multi-creditor mosaic. |
| **Severe Demand or Margin Compression** | Low | Severe | If EBITDA compresses to $3–4M, the fixed debt service becomes punitive. The PIK toggle and 45-day equity escape hatch ($90M floor) provide a structured off-ramp. |
| **Competitive Window Closes** | Moderate | Very High | If the rival secures >20% exclusivity by Month 6, the break-glass equity is triggered immediately. If >30% by Month 18, the "no equity" constraint is abandoned for a full minority round to remain competitive. |
| **Working Capital Drain** | Moderate | Moderate | Rapid scaling can consume 5–12% of revenue in NWC. The $2–3M internal generation tranche is sized conservatively, and pricing/pre-deposit programs are deployed to keep NWC days flat. |

**Stress Test Summary**

| Scenario | Revenue CAGR | EBITDA Margin | Annual Cash Debt Service | FCCR | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Base Case** | 41% | 14% | ~$2.0M | **2.8x** | Comfortable. Refinance path clear. |
| **Moderate Stress** | 30% | 11% | ~$2.0M | **1.8x** | Manageable. PIK toggle preserves cash. |
| **Severe Downside** | 25% | 8% | ~$2.4M* | **1.2x** | **Trigger zone.** Activate PIK toggle, cut opex, and initiate 45-day equity escape hatch if FCCR stays below 1.25x for two quarters. |
| **Distress** | <20% | <7% | >$2.4M | **<1.0x** | **Breach.** Execute orderly sale or emergency equity raise; do not pursue debt-funded expansion. |

*\*Assumes blended cash service after unitranche amortization begins.*
