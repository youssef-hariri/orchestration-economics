### 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Recommendation:** Approve a milestone-gated **$20 million “Independence Stack 2.0”** to fund 18 months of parallel capacity expansion, hiring, and market growth without priced equity today and without covenant-heavy senior debt.

**The Strategic Equation:** Demand exceeds capacity, a well-funded rival is attacking the market, and our **78% ownership** position is too valuable to dilute at a cyclical valuation low. Organic growth would cede the competitive window and destroy an estimated **>$90 million** in terminal enterprise value. Covenant-heavy bank debt would throttle the simultaneous investments—capacity, hiring, and market expansion—required to win. The Independence Stack 2.0 treats our profitable, doubling revenue profile as a **credit story**, not an equity story.

**Outcome:** We preserve **~75–76% founder ownership**, maintain full board control, capture the profitable demand cohort, and create a 24-month option to reset valuation before any future equity conversation.

### 3. RECOMMENDED STRUCTURE: THE INDEPENDENCE STACK 2.0

A **$20 million, sequenced capital stack** designed to survive a 6-month revenue lag without breaching covenants or choking operations.

| Layer | Instrument | Purpose | Facility Size | Draw Sequence | Terms & Control Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Asset-Backed Term Loan | Hard capacity (equipment, facilities) | **$10.0M** | **$6M** at Month 0; **$4M** at Month 6 | 7.5–9.0% cash cost; 6-month interest-only; lien limited to specific assets; **zero governance rights**. |
| **B** | Revenue-Based Financing | Hiring, S&M, geo expansion | **$6.0M** | **$4M** at Month 1; **$2M** at Month 6 | 18–22% IRR; revenue-share **capped at 6%**; repayment cap at **1.4x** principal; **zero equity; zero board seat**. |
| **C** | Venture Debt | Competitive reserve / WC bridge | **$4.0M** | **Undrawn** until Month 9+ (contingent) | 12% interest + warrants **capped at 3%** fully diluted; no equity co-investment; drawn only upon competitive escalation or liquidity gap. |
| **TOTAL** | | | **$20.0M** | **$10M initial** | **~16% blended cash cost of capital** |

**Core Design Principle:** Front-load the cheapest capital (ABL), rightsize the flexible capital (RBF) to avoid a Year-1 liquidity crunch, and hold the most expensive capital (venture debt) as an undrawn defensive reserve.

### 5. WALK-AWAY CONDITIONS

These are non-negotiable red lines. If a counterparty refuses, we move to the next provider.

| Source | Walk-Away Triggers |
|---|---|
| **Asset-Backed Lender** | • Covenant requires FCCR **>1.30x** on a company-wide TTM basis<br>• Lien extends beyond specific equipment to blanket IP or enterprise lien<br>• No 6-month interest-only option available<br>• Personal guarantees required from founders |
| **Revenue-Based Finance** | • Revenue-share structure implies blended IRR **>22%** (e.g., >7% share with >1.5x cap)<br>• Total repayment cap exceeds **1.5x** principal<br>• UCC lien filed on all assets or restrictions on senior equipment debt<br>• Repayment is a fixed obligation rather than true revenue-linked percentage |
| **Venture Debt** | • Warrant coverage exceeds **3%** fully diluted or exercise price below latest internal valuation<br>• Minimum cash covenant exceeds **4 months** of projected burn<br>• Requires equity co-investment, success fee, or “equity kick”<br>• MAC clause triggered by competitor fundraising (not our own performance) |
| **Convertible / SAFE** | • Valuation cap below **2.0x TTM revenue** or discount above **20%**<br>• Maturity below **24 months** or automatic conversion on any financing (even an inside round) |
| **Contingent Equity (Layer D)** | • Pre-money below **2.5x TTM revenue** or **2.0x forward revenue**<br>• Liquidation preference **>1.0x non-participating**<br>• Board seat, veto over ordinary course, or drag-along below **66.7%** threshold<br>• Full-ratchet anti-dilution or cumulative dividends **>8%** |
| **Organic Baseline** | Abandon if capacity utilization exceeds **90%** for 6 consecutive months or rival revenue growth exceeds ours by **>2x** for two consecutive quarters |

### 6. RISK FACTORS & MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Year-1 Liquidity J-Curve** | High | Phased draws reduce Year-1 fixed service by 35%. ABL interest-only period + undrawn venture debt keeps FCCR at **~1.7x** even under a 6-month revenue lag. Maintain **$4.5M minimum cash floor**. |
| **RBF Cost Escalation** | Medium | Capped at 6% revenue-share and 1.4x repayment. Cost is high (18–22% IRR) but fixed; it does not compound or dilute further. |
| **Rival Price War** | Medium | Layer C ($4M) is held in reserve specifically for competitive response. Our profitable unit economics allow us to defend share selectively while the rival burns capital. |
| **Milestone Execution** | Medium | Month-3 validation gate requires customer LOIs + cash deposits, not firm orders for unbuilt capacity. This de-risks the ABL follow-on draw. |
| **Exit Value Leakage** | Low | Total Stack transfer at a $250M exit is **~$6–9M** versus **~$39M** for equity today. Even in downside scenarios, the Stack preserves >$30M of founder value. |
