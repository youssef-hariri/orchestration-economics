# TURNAROUND-OR-EXIT ASSESSMENT: INDUSTRIAL-SERVICES UNIT
## Board-Ready Decision Framework with Rigorous Implementation Plan

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Diagnosis:** The industrial-services unit exhibits **70% structural impairment** (market TAM decline, commoditisation, unfavourable customer/service mix) and **30% execution gap** (pricing discipline, project governance, utilisation). Two prior cost-only restructurings (totalling $5.0m in fixed-cost cuts) failed because they did not address structural revenue collapse. A third restructuring is viable only if accompanied by credible revenue stabilization and margin recovery mechanisms.

**Board Decision:** This assessment presents **two equally defensible paths with quantified risk profiles.** The board must choose ONE and commit to it without reversal via pre-committed stage gates with automated exit triggers.

### Path 1: TURNAROUND (40–50% Success Probability)
- **Target:** Stabilize revenue at $42–44m; expand gross margin from 29% to 39%; reduce fixed costs $1.8–2.0m
- **Outcome:** +$2.0–2.5m EBITDA by Month 12 (sustainable 4–5% margin)
- **One-time costs:** $2.0–2.5m (severance, consolidation, consulting)
- **NPV (10-year):** +$9.1m if successful; -$2.0m if fails (expected value: +$2.5–3.5m accounting for 45% failure rate)
- **Contingency:** Four automated stage gates (Months 4, 6, 10, 12) with no board discretion on exit trigger

### Path 2: CONTROLLED EXIT (90% Success Probability)
- **Timeline:** Identify buyer by Month 4; negotiate by Month 6; close by Month 8–9
- **Valuation:** $8–10m net proceeds (post-tax, post-costs) assuming Month 6 exit with stabilization evidence
- **Risk:** Lower than turnaround but immediate; requires active buyer search in parallel
- **Strategic risk:** Sister-division halo of $2–3m NPV at risk; requires customer-by-customer transition plan

**Recommendation:** Proceed with **turnaround (Path 1)** with mandatory dual-track exit preparation. At Month 4 stage gate, if revenue stabilization is NOT evidenced, shift to exit fast-track immediately. Do not allow "one more quarter"—this destroys value and employee morale.

### 1.1.3 Why Two Prior Restructurings Failed

| Restructuring | Fixed Cost Cuts | Approach | Revenue Assumption | Result | Failure Mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 (2022)** | $2.8m (-16%) | Cost-only | "Revenue will stabilize at 48m" | EBITDA swung +$1.2m for 6 months; then eroded | Revenue continued decline (48m → 45m by EOY); gross contribution fell $1.1m YoY; gains erased by Month 9 |
| **R2 (2023)** | $2.2m (-15% additional) | Cost-only | "Revenue will stabilize at 45m" | EBITDA briefly +$0.6m; lost again within 3 months | Same: Revenue fell further (45m → 40.5m); customer repricing events in Q2–Q3 eliminated margin gains |
| **Cumulative Effect** | $5.0m total cuts; fixed costs fell from $17.0m → $12.0m (29% reduction) | Exhausted "easy" cost cuts | Still assumes revenue stabilizes | Unit now operating near minimum viable fixed-cost footprint | **Further cost cuts require facility closure, headcount reduction, or service discontinuation—high execution risk and severance cost** |

**Verdict:** Both restructurings followed textbook best practice (cut controllable costs, improve cash), but **failed because they assumed revenue would stabilize without intervention.** The market does not grant stabilization to businesses that lose pricing power. Turnaround #3 must include explicit revenue stabilization mechanisms (customer retention, pricing reset, portfolio rebalancing) or it will fail like the prior two.

# PART II: 12-MONTH TURNAROUND EXECUTION PLAN

## 2.1 Four-Phase Structure with Stage Gates

### 2.1.1 Phase Overview and Governance

```
PHASE 1: STABILIZE (Days 1–30)
  Owner: Interim Chief Restructuring Officer (CRO) + CFO + SVP Operations
  Objective: Stop value destruction; establish decision governance; prevent further customer/manager loss
  Go/No-Go: Commitment to turnaround approach and team assembled by Day 30
  
PHASE 2: PORTFOLIO & PRICING RESET (Days 31–90, Weeks 5–13)
  Owner: CFO (lead) + VP Sales + SVP Operations
  Objective: Exit 60–80% of loss-making customers ($1.5–2.0m revenue); reset pricing on top-20
  Go/No-Go: STAGE GATE 1 (Month 4): Revenue stabilization evidence; customer concentration stable/improving
  
PHASE 3: EXECUTION EXCELLENCE & SCALING (Days 91–180, Weeks 14–26)
  Owner: SVP Operations (lead) + VP Sales + CFO
  Objective: Embed pricing discipline, project governance, utilisation optimization; grow high-margin lines
  Go/No-Go: STAGE GATE 2 (Month 6): Portfolio traction; margin recovery tracking; monthly EBITDA positive
  
PHASE 4: PROOF OF PROFITABILITY (Days 181–365, Weeks 27–52)
  Owner: CFO (lead) + SVP Operations + VP Sales
  Objective: Deliver sustainable monthly profitability; lock in Year-2 customer contracts
  Final Gate: Month 10–12: EBITDA run-rate +$2.0m+ and stable; ready for board approval (turnaround successful) 
             or exit transition (if gates missed)
```

### 2.1.2 Stage Gates: Automated Decision Rules (Binding, No Discretion)

#### **STAGE GATE 1: Month 4 — "GO / NO-GO TURNAROUND"**

**Decision Rule (Binding; board votes only on YES/NO, no negotiation):**

**GO TURNAROUND if ALL of:**
- ✓ YTD Revenue ≥ $30.0m (4-month accumulation; 90% of $33.5m quarterly run-rate target)
- ✓ Backlog-to-Revenue ≥ 0.88x (forward visibility ≥ 10 months at current burn rate)
- ✓ Quote-to-Close Discount Gap narrowed ≥50% from baseline (8–12% → ≤5–6%)
- ✓ Top-10 customer concentration stable or declining 1–2pp (from 57% baseline)
- ✓ Zero planned top-5 customer non-renewals (no formal exit notice received)
- ✓ Manager attrition ≤ 8% YTD (vs. baseline 12–15% p.a.; retention bonuses working)
- ✓ Gross margin ≥ 31% (trend toward 35%+ by Month 6)

**ACTIVATE EXIT FAST-TRACK if ANY of:**
- ✗ YTD Revenue < $28.5m (absolute floor; <86% of quarterly target = revenue cliff)
- ✗ Backlog-to-Revenue < 0.80x (forward visibility <9.5 months)
- ✗ Quote-to-Close Discount Gap widened (>12% on average; pricing discipline failing)
- ✗ Top-5 customer issues non-renewal notice OR demands >20% price cut unilaterally
- ✗ Manager attrition > 10% YTD (loss of confidence signal)
- ✗ Gross margin < 29% (execution gains not appearing)
- ✗ Cash balance < $1.5m (liquidity crisis; cannot fund operations without new capital)

**Authority:** Board Finance Committee + CEO + CRO (CFO makes technical recommendation, board votes)

**Duration:** 48-hour decision window (no extended debate; sets expectation for decisiveness)

**Outcome if GO:** Continue to Phases 3–4; prepare Month 6 detailed stage gate

**Outcome if NO-GO:** Shift to Exit Fast-Track immediately
- CRO transitions from turnaround mode to exit mode
- VP Sales begins buyer outreach (contact 5–10 potential consolidators)
- SVP Operations begins transition planning (service continuity for existing contracts if sold)
- Communication to employees: "We've made a strategic decision to explore alternative options for the unit. We will provide clarity by Month 6."

#### **STAGE GATE 2: Month 6 — "PORTFOLIO TRACTION"**

**Decision Rule:**

**GO TURNAROUND (Continue) if ALL of:**
- ✓ Advisory + Training revenue +5% YTD vs. Year-1 baseline; trending ≥8% p.a. run-rate
- ✓ Project Exec + Equipment Rental declining or flat (not growing as % of portfolio)
- ✓ Customer concentration (Top 5) stable or declining 1–2pp (not rising)
- ✓ $0.8–1.0m of low-margin customer revenue exited (on track for $1.5–2.0m by M9)
- ✓ Gross contribution margin (after direct customer overhead) ≥ 32% (trend to 34%+ by M9)
- ✓ Utilisation 76%+ (trend: +0.5pp per month improvement)
- ✓ Project rework < 3.5% (trend: -0.25pp per month)
- ✓ Run-rate monthly EBITDA positive in 1+ of last 2 months (M5–M6): >$0m

**ACTIVATE EXIT FAST-TRACK if ANY of:**
- ✗ Advisory + Training flat or declining YTD
- ✗ Project Exec growing or unchanged in mix (portfolio rebalancing stalling)
- ✗ Customer concentration rising >59% (Top 5 dependency worsening)
- ✗ < $0.5m of loss-making customers exited (pruning plan stalled)
- ✗ Gross margin < 30% (execution improvements not showing up in margin)
- ✗ Utilisation declining or flat < 74% (bench waste increasing)
- ✗ Project rework > 4.0% (quality issues spreading; governance failing)
- ✗ Monthly EBITDA negative in both M5 and M6 (turnaround not materializing)

**Authority:** Board Finance Committee + CFO + SVP Operations (no CEO veto)

**Outcome if GO:** Full-speed execution through Phase 4; confirm Month 10 Final Gate

**Outcome if NO-GO:** Exit activated; buyer identification urgent; transition planning accelerates

#### **FINAL GATE: Month 10–12 — "CREDIBLE PATH TO SUSTAINABLE PROFITABILITY PROVEN"**

**Decision Rule:**

**APPROVE TURNAROUND if ALL of:**
- ✓ Run-rate EBITDA positive and stable: M8–M10 average ≥ $1.5m (≥3.5% margin)
- ✓ No deterioration: no month shows EBITDA < $(0.2)m
- ✓ Revenue stabilized: M7–M10 average run-rate $41–43m (flat MoM)
- ✓ Backlog-to-Revenue ≥ 0.90x (forward visibility 10.8+ months)
- ✓ Gross margin 39–41% (blended, after all portfolio rebalancing and execution gains)
- ✓ Fixed costs < $10.2m (headcount, facilities, overhead reductions on track)
- ✓ Customer concentration stable < 56% (Top 5); no unexpected losses
- ✓ Manager retention: ≥90% of top 20 still in place
- ✓ Pricing discipline embedded: <5% of bids won with >10% discount below quote
- ✓ Advisory + Training is 18%+ of revenue mix (vs. 13% baseline); growing ≥8% p.a.

**APPROVE EXIT if turnaround gate NOT met:**
- ✗ EBITDA still negative or volatile in M8–M10
- ✗ Revenue declining or stalled <$40m run-rate
- ✗ Gross margin < 38%
- ✗ Fixed costs > $11m (cost reductions stalling)
- ✗ Customer concentration rising or top-5 customer lost
- ✗ Manager attrition > 15% by Month 10

**Authority:** Board Finance Committee + Board Chair + CEO (final go/no-go decision)

**Outcome if APPROVE TURNAROUND:**
