## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (ONE PAGE)

### Transition Thesis (3 Sentences)
The company will transition from lumpy perpetual-license revenue ($12M baseline ARR from $480/user/year maintenance attach; 65% blended attach rate) to predictable subscription-first revenue ($25–30M target ARR by Month 36) via voluntary migration of the 2,500-customer installed base and new-logo subscription acquisition. The transition creates a **managed, finite trough** (–12% to –17% GAAP YoY revenue decline in Year 1, recovering to +25–40% YoY growth by Year 3) caused by timing mismatch between upfront perpetual revenue recognition and ratably-deferred subscription revenue. The subscription model enables 112% net dollar retention (NRR) and 8.0–10.0x revenue valuation multiple (vs. 6.0–7.0x today), increasing enterprise value by 40–50% on normalized cash flow.

### Recommended Price Point and Migration Offer
- **Subscription List Pricing:** Essential $600/user/yr (SMB), Professional $1,800/user/yr (mid-market), Premier $4,800/user/yr (enterprise)
- **Effective Year-1 ASP (after volume discount):** Essential $570, Professional $1,530, Premier $3,600
- **Upgrade Credit:** 2.5x maintenance value for enterprise (e.g., $480/user × 2.5 = $1,200 credit), 2.0x for mid-market, 1.5x for SMB; applied to Year 1 subscription, locked 3-year pricing guarantee
- **Migration Incentive:** Co-terminate at customer's existing maintenance anniversary; free onboarding + CSM support Year 1 (enterprise only); multi-year term license option (3-year prepay at 40% discount vs. annual) for price-sensitive enterprise holdouts

### Expected Trough Depth and Recovery Quarter
- **Trough Depth:** –12% to –17% YoY GAAP revenue (Year 1)
- **Trough Duration:** 12 months (extends into Q1 Year 2 for full recovery to baseline)
- **Recovery Inflection:** Positive YoY GAAP growth resumes Q1 Year 2; accelerates to +25–40% YoY by Q4 Year 3
- **Tripwire Thresholds:** If actual Year 1 GAAP revenue misses envelope by >3 percentage points (i.e., worse than –20% YoY), or if enterprise migration achieves <15% by Month 18, trigger immediate board notification and transition replan

### Comp Plan Headline
Sales compensation maintained at current OTE levels (Year 1) via protected bonus guarantees and 3–5 percentage point increase to bonus pool (18–20% of revenue vs. 15% baseline). New ARR and migration ARR quota-credited at blended 1.0x and 0.8x factors (respectively); perpetual deals de-incentivized at 0.5x factor. Clawback pro-rata over 12 months (Year 1) for new subscription customers churning in first 90 days; zero clawback for customers retained past 90 days or renewals. Top 10 reps receive promotions, +15% base salary increase, and 0.1–0.3% equity grants (4-year vest) for retention. New "Migration Specialist" role (5–8 FTE) separately compensated on migration ARR at 0.8x factor; lower risk, higher attach likelihood.

### Investor Narrative (One Line)
**"We are converting a $12M lumpy, slow-velocity revenue base into $25–30M predictable SaaS revenue; the transition trough (Months 6–18, –12 to –17% GAAP YoY) is temporary and recoverable; exit multiples improve 1.5–2.0x due to NRR and subscription valuation benchmarks; we preserve 87%+ of installed base via voluntary incentives, not forced adoption."**

### 2.3 Upgrade Credit Mechanics (Conversion of Maintenance to Migration Incentive)

**Objective:** Convert "stranded" maintenance payments into tangible Year-1 subscription discount, lowering perceived switching cost.

**Calculation:**
```
STEP 1: Residual Maintenance Value (RMV)
  Customer's current annual maintenance contract: $480/user/year
  Remaining term in current contract: 12 months
  RMV = $480 × (12/12) = $480

STEP 2: Apply Cohort-Specific Multiplier
  Enterprise tier → multiplier 2.5x
  Migration credit = $480 × 2.5 = $1,200

STEP 3: Apply to First-Year Subscription Cost
  Premier list price: $4,800/user/year
  Less: migration credit: -$1,200
  Year 1 subscription cost: $3,600/user/year (25% discount)
  Year 2+: revert to list price $4,800/year (unless 3-year lock applies)

EXAMPLE (Enterprise Customer):
  Company: Acme Corp, 250 users, $120K/year maintenance ($480/user)
  Current annual cost: $120K
  Residual maintenance value (12 months): $120K
  Upgrade credit: $120K × 2.5 = $300K
  
  Year 1 subscription cost (Premier, list):
    250 users × $4,800 = $1.2M
  Less: upgrade credit: $300K
  Year 1 net cost: $900K (25% discount)
  
  Year 1 effective ASP: $900K ÷ 250 users = $3,600/user (vs. perpetual + maintenance blended TCO $6,300 over 5 years; subscription Year-1 cost is 57% of blended cost)
```

**Accounting Treatment (ASC 606 Compliant):**
- **Entry:** Record upgrade credit as deferred acquisition cost (DAC) asset, contra-revenue at contract inception
- **Amortization:** Amortize credit over 3-year contract term (or 24-month minimum)
- **P&L Impact:** Credit does NOT reduce Year 1 revenue directly; instead reduces net revenue recognized via DAC amortization expense
- **Investor Clarity:** Report "Subscription revenue at list price" ($1.2M) and "Less: Amortization of upgrade credits" ($100K) = Net subscription revenue ($1.1M)

**Budget Cap:** Total upgrade credit budget capped at $5.0M for Yr1–2 combined. If migration demand exceeds budget, pause additional incentives; signal of strong demand justifying list price maintenance.
