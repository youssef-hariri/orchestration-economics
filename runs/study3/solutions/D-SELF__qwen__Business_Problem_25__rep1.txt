**CONFIDENTIAL BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors & Executive Leadership Team  
**FROM:** Office of the Chief Strategy Officer  
**DATE:** October 26, 2023  
**SUBJECT:** Project Catalyst: Business-Model Transition to Subscription (The "Economic Gravity" Strategy)  

---

### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
To unlock enterprise valuation multiples and eliminate the volatility of lumpy perpetual license revenue, we must transition our business model to a predictable, recurring subscription framework. However, this transition inherently creates a "J-Curve"—a multi-year trough in both cash flow and GAAP revenue. 

Our competitor’s abrupt transition resulted in a catastrophic 20% installed-base churn because they relied on punitive coercion. We will avoid this by deploying the **"Economic Gravity" framework**. This strategy makes the subscription path economically irresistible and the perpetual path economically irrational over time, driving organic migration without triggering hostile churn. 

This memorandum outlines our comprehensive, four-pillar execution plan to navigate the "Dual Trough" (Cash and GAAP), align our go-to-market engine, and maintain unwavering Board and investor confidence through the transition.

---

### **STRATEGIC PILLAR I: CUSTOMER MIGRATION & OFFER STRUCTURE**
*Objective: Migrate the installed base by applying economic pressure and value-addition, avoiding the punitive "feature freezes" that drive churn.*

We will transition our existing perpetual base through three distinct, economically calibrated paths:

*   **Path A: The "Modernization Commitment" (Target: 60% of Base)**
    *   **The Offer:** Customers convert to a subscription at a **15% discount** to standard list price in exchange for a 3-year commitment. 
    *   **The Value:** Immediate access to all new cloud/AI modules, premium SLA support, and a 36-month price lock. This protects our long-term margins while offering genuine, immediate value.
*   **Path B: The "Perpetual Buyout" (Target: 25% of Base)**
    *   **The Offer:** Customers "trade in" their legacy perpetual license. We appraise the license and apply 100% of its current depreciated book value as a credit toward the upfront cost of a new 3- or 5-year subscription.
    *   **The Value:** Validates the customer's past investment, soothes entitlement issues, and pulls forward vital cash to soften the transition trough.
*   **Path C: The "Legacy Economics" (Target: 15% of Base)**
    *   **The Offer:** Customers retain their perpetual license and current feature set. However, **annual maintenance and support fees will increase by 20% year-over-year**, and all *new* cloud, mobile, and AI modules are strictly subscription-only.
    *   **The Value:** Avoids the hostile "feature freeze" that caused our competitor's churn. The software continues to work, preventing panic. However, the compounding 20% maintenance hike and the gating of innovation make the perpetual option economically irrational after 24-36 months, creating organic pull toward Paths A or B.
    *   *Risk Mitigation:* Customer Success will be heavily deployed to Path C accounts to proactively manage the narrative, demonstrate ROI, and prevent competitive poaching.

---

### **STRATEGIC PILLAR II: PRICING & FINANCIAL ENGINEERING**
*Objective: Architect pricing mechanics that explicitly mitigate both the Cash Flow Trough and the GAAP Revenue Trough.*

A critical failure in past transitions is conflating cash flow with GAAP revenue. Under ASC 606, subscription revenue is recognized ratably. We have engineered distinct levers for both metrics:

**1. Mitigating the GAAP Revenue Trough (Year 1 & 2)**
*   **Unbundled "Transition Implementation" Fee:** We will bundle a mandatory "Cloud Onboarding & Data Migration" professional services engagement with all new subscriptions (typically 15-20% of first-year ACV). *Crucially, Legal and RevOps will structure this as a distinct, separable deliverable to ensure ASC 606 compliance for upfront revenue recognition.* This artificially bridges the Year 1 GAAP gap.
*   **Net-New Perpetual Price Hike:** The list price for any *new* perpetual licenses will be increased by **150%**. This effectively kills the old model for net-new business, forcing new logos into subscriptions and maximizing Year 1 recognized subscription revenue.

**2. Mitigating the Cash Flow Trough**
*   **Realistic Multi-Year Upfront Discounts:** We will offer industry-standard upfront discounts to pull forward cash without destroying long-term IRR: 5% for 3-year (paid annually), 10% for 3-year (paid upfront), and 15% for 5-year (paid upfront).
*   **Net-New "Land and Expand" Tiering:** Introduction of a "Starter" subscription tier priced at 70% of the old annualized perpetual maintenance cost to drive high-velocity net-new logo adoption and expand the total addressable market.

---

### **STRATEGIC PILLAR III: SALES COMPENSATION & GTM ALIGNMENT**
*Objective: Shift sales behavior to Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) without triggering mass attrition or destroying performance culture.*

*   **The "Quota Glide Path":** To prevent top reps from fleeing due to unattainable new-model quotas, we will blend their targets over three years:
    *   *Year 1:* 60% Legacy Perpetual / 40% New ARR
    *   *Year 2:* 30% Legacy Perpetual / 70% New ARR
    *   *Year 3:* 0% Legacy / 100% New ARR
*   **President’s Club Protection:** Reps who achieved >110% of quota in the prior year are granted a "Quota Holiday" in Year 1 (overall quota reduced by 15%). This low-cost retention tool protects our most valuable assets.
*   **Migration Bounty & Accelerators:** Reps receive a **1.2x commission multiplier** on the first year’s ARR for migrating existing perpetual customers (capped at 30% of total quota). Multi-year accelerators are calibrated at 1.15x for 3-year and 1.25x for 5-year deals.
    *   *Risk Mitigation:* RevOps will ensure migration paperwork and provisioning are entirely frictionless; if the administrative burden is too high, reps will abandon migrations for easier net-new deals.
*   **Ramp Draws:** Replacing the fatal "blanket OTE guarantee" with a standard 3-month non-recoverable ramp draw for new hires/territory transitions to maintain accountability and performance culture.

---

### **STRATEGIC PILLAR IV: INVESTOR RELATIONS & BOARD GOVERNANCE**
*Objective: Control the narrative, shifting the market's focus from short-term revenue decline to long-term revenue quality and multiple expansion.*

*   **The "Dual-Trough" Narrative:** At our pre-transition Investor Day, we will explicitly model *both* troughs. We will demonstrate that while GAAP revenue dips in Year 1 due to ratable recognition, our upfront onboarding fees and multi-year cash commitments will result in a **Cash Flow crossover in Year 2**, and a **GAAP Revenue crossover in Year 3**. We will frame the trough not as a failure, but as a deliberate, mathematically sound investment in higher-quality revenue.
*   **Shifting the KPI Dashboard:** We will de-emphasize GAAP Revenue and heavily emphasize leading SaaS indicators:
    1.  **ARR Growth Rate** (Momentum)
    2.  **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** (Target: >115% to prove churn is avoided)
    3.  **Migration Velocity** (Installed base conversion rate)
    4.  **Rule of 40** (Balanced, efficient growth)
*   **Executive "Skin in the Game":** For Years 1 and 2, **0% of executive short-term incentive (STI) bonuses will be tied to GAAP Revenue**. 100% will be tied to ARR growth, NRR, and Migration Velocity. This provides undeniable proof to the market that leadership is fully aligned with the long-term thesis.

### **RISK MITIGATION & CONTINGENCY PLAYBOOK**
To ensure Board confidence, management has defined the following tactical levers to be deployed if base-case metrics lag:

| Risk Trigger | Contingency Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Migration Velocity lags <X% by Month 6** | Deploy targeted tactical discounts for Path A/B, or increase marketing spend by 15% to accelerate pipeline generation. |
| **ASC 606 Audit rejects upfront fee recognition** | Shift to standard ratable recognition; rely heavier on multi-year cash pull-forward discounts and accelerate net-new volume to bridge the GAAP gap. |
| **Path C churn exceeds 10% annually** | Accelerate Customer Success outreach; offer a one-time, 90-day "buyout grace period" to convert at-risk accounts before they defect to competitors. |
| **Net-new bookings freeze (Days 30-60)** | Deploy a temporary "Transition Accelerator" discount (10%) for net-new annual deals to maintain cash flow momentum while the market digests the 150% perpetual price hike. |

### **EXECUTION ROADMAP**

By executing this structured, economically sound, and behaviorally aligned plan, we will avoid the catastrophic churn of our competitors, successfully navigate both the cash and GAAP revenue troughs, and emerge in 36 months as a highly predictable, high-multiple SaaS business.
