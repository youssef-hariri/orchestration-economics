## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A global marketing campaign has violated local cultural values in [Market], triggering active consumer backlash with spillover risk into diaspora communities and global media. Standard Western crisis playbooks—rapid template apologies, headquarters-controlled messaging, and performative social media statements—will compound the injury. 

**The Board must authorize an immediate shift from corporate reputation management to structural moral repair.** This requires ceding creative and communicative sovereignty to the local market, accepting material accountability, and embedding permanent governance changes that prevent recurrence. Speed is essential, but haste without cultural precision is a second offense.

**Immediate Decision Required:** Authorize the Local Market GM as final decision-maker on all external communications for 72 hours, bypassing standard global marketing approval loops.

---

## I. STRATEGIC IMPERATIVES: NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES

1. **Impact Erases Intent.** The creative rationale is dead. The only operative narrative is the harm experienced by the community. Internal discussions of "what we meant" are banned from all external and internal communications.
2. **Local Sovereignty Within Global Compliance.** The local Market GM owns the apology, the ritual, and the timeline. Global functions (Legal, Compliance, Digital) provide enabling infrastructure—legal guardrails, intelligence, and funding—not cultural veto.
3. **Repair Is Material, Not Performative.** The community must see a permanent transfer of power and resources. Temporary donations or sentimental campaigns will be rejected as the purchase of absolution.

## II. PHASE 1: IMMEDIATE CONTAINMENT & INTELLIGENCE (0–72 Hours)

**Priority: Halt harm, protect employees, map exposure, and listen.**

### A. Crisis Command Cell (Activate Within 2 Hours)
| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| **Local Incident Commander** (Market GM) | Final authority on all external statements, ritual acts, and community engagement. **Failover:** If the Market GM is compromised or culturally disconnected, elevate immediately to the pre-identified Local Cultural Ombudsperson (see Section V). |
| **Global Crisis Counsel** (CCO) | Manages global media, investor relations, and diaspora narrative containment. |
| **Regional Legal & Compliance** | Maps regulatory exposure, mandatory hearing deadlines, and product seizure risks in parallel to the reputational response. |
| **Digital War Room Lead** | Native-language monitoring of narrative velocity, bot amplification, boycott mechanics, and cross-border hashtag migration. |
| **Cultural Systems Lead** | External local anthropologist/ethicist mapping community power structures and legitimate interlocutors. |

### B. Absolute Operational Silence
- **Cease all activity:** Paid media, programmatic retargeting, CRM triggers, influencer seeding, and partner co-marketing globally for this campaign.
- **Remove assets:** All owned channels, retail environments, and partner portals.
- **Influencer protocol:** Personally request takedowns. Acknowledge the brand has compromised their standing. Do not issue algorithmic copyright claims.
- **Global gag order:** No statements, emojis, or "we hear you" posts from global executives for 72 hours.

### C. Parallel Legal & Regulatory Triage (Hours 0–24)
- Local Counsel to immediately assess: advertising standards complaints, consumer protection investigations, potential fines, mandatory hearing deadlines, and credible risk of regulatory seizure or product ban.
- Prepare defensive filings in parallel with the reputational response; do not let legal strategy delay the cultural response, but do not ignore legal reality.
- Investor Relations to brief markets if [Market] contributes >3% of global revenue or if boycott threatens quarterly guidance.

### D. Digital War Room Activation
- **Native-language sentiment tracking:** Monitor register (outrage vs. mockery vs. organized boycott), not just volume.
- **Boycott monitoring:** Identify organic vs. inorganic amplification; map distributor inventory pullbacks in real time.
- **Diaspora spillover detection:** Monitor adjacent markets (UAE, UK, Canada, etc.). Activate pre-approved, culturally attuned holding statements in top 3 diaspora markets immediately.
- **Bot assessment:** Distinguish authentic grievance from manufactured amplification to calibrate proportionality.

### E. Employee Protection
- Convene an **unrecorded** town hall in the local language within 24 hours. Do not stream to global HQ.
- Offer discretionary leave under existing local statutory frameworks without requiring medical documentation, where permissible.
- Activate a confidential support line staffed by **local counselors**, not HQ HR.
- Equip frontline staff with one validated sentence: *"We recognize we caused offense, and we are following the guidance of our community’s leaders to understand how to make it right."*

### F. Conditional First Statement
If local advisors confirm silence signals contempt, publish a brief acknowledgment in the **local language only**. No timeline promises unless culturally expected.

> *"We have stopped all activity related to our recent campaign. We recognize we have caused real offense in [Market], and we are listening before we speak further."*

## III. PHASE 2: ACCOUNTABILITY ARCHITECTURE (Days 2–10)

**Priority: Co-design the apology with the community; never arrive with a draft to be "localized."**

### A. Community Power Mapping
The Cultural Systems Lead must rapidly map:
- **The directly injured party:** Specific ethnicity, faith, historical trauma, or indigenous nation targeted.
- **Contested authority:** Differentiate legitimate cultural custodians from political opportunists using transparent filters (non-partisan track record, grassroots accountability, recognized expertise).
- **Intermediary protocols:** In high-context/shame-oriented cultures, rebuke may travel through indirect speech or symbolic withdrawal. Learn to read these as legitimate moral correction.

### B. Linguistic & Ritual Due Diligence
Before drafting, convene:
- **Rhetoricians and honorific specialists** (not translators) to audit register, pronoun choice, and speech levels. A single incorrect honorific can convert an apology into an insult.
- **Cultural practitioners** to determine whether public apology is appropriate, or whether private reparation to elders/institutions must precede any public statement.
- **Local temporal sovereignty:** If the community requires days of deliberation, the brand waits. Rushing to meet a Western news cycle is a second act of imperialism.

### C. The AIC-R Apology Model
Draft using **Accountability-Impact-Commitment-Ritual**, legally reviewed **only for criminal or severe civil liability exposure** (e.g., admissions triggering regulatory seizure), not for tone:

| Element | Directive |
|---------|-----------|
| **Accountability** | Active voice only. *"We failed to see…"* Never: *"Mistakes were made."* |
| **Impact** | Validate the specific cultural value or historical wound trivialized, proving education by local voices. |
| **Commitment** | Concrete, material actions announced simultaneously. Never let words stand alone. |
| **Ritual** | Incorporate locally prescribed acts (audiences, bows, offerings, silence). **Never improvise.** |

### D. Messenger, Posture & Medium
- **Default messenger:** The local Country Manager, not the global CEO, unless local authorities explicitly request global leadership.
- **Physical posture:** Seated lower, no podium, deference to local protocol. Legal confirms physical safety of any public appearance.
- **Language:** Deliver in the local language. Use subtitles; never dub over the local manager.
- **Medium:** Publish where the moral public sphere resides—physical community sites, respected national newspapers, or native digital platforms (KakaoTalk, WeChat, Line, local TikTok iterations).

## IV. PHASE 3: STAKEHOLDER SEQUENCING (Days 2–14)

Engage in order of **moral proximity to the harm**, not corporate power.

1. **Directly Affected Communities (Days 2–7):** Hold small, closed listening sessions. Compensate participants for time and emotional labor. Do not record. Ask: *"What does repair look like?"*
2. **Local Employees (Days 1–3):** Share the draft apology before public release.
3. **Retail & Distribution Partners (Days 2–5):** Offer contractual flexibility to pause orders without penalty. Protect relationships by providing empathetic customer messaging they can use.
4. **Community Elders & Institutions (Days 3–7):** Request counsel, not endorsement. Fund their work through unrestricted, unbranded grants. No naming rights.
5. **Government & Regulators (Days 3–7):** Proactively brief authorities. Present the internal audit plan and self-regulation framework. Acknowledge legal exposure openly; do not lobby for immunity.
6. **Influencers & Creators (Days 3–5):** Release unconditionally. Honor all contracts in full. Permit termination without penalty. Do not invite them into a redemption narrative.
7. **Global Containment:** Deploy diaspora-specific holding statements. Investor Relations issues factual, non-defensive guidance on material impact.

## V. PHASE 4: STRUCTURAL REMEDIATION (Weeks 2–12)

**Priority: Prove change through governance, not gestures.**

### A. Independent Cultural Autopsy
Commission a respected local institution to audit the campaign approval chain. Publish findings in the local language. Name institutional failures (ignored research, absent local review, timeline pressure). Sanitize individual personnel data per privacy law, but avoid anonymized "process failures" that read as impunity.

### B. Silent Reparations: The Local Cultural Restoration Trust
- Establish an **independent, registered local trust** with a majority-local board (including community representatives).
- Brand provides seed capital, documented transparently for auditors/shareholders, but **unbranded and without naming rights.**
- Independent board disburses unrestricted grants to vetted community organizations after standard due diligence.
- Brand publishes one line in its annual report acknowledging the trust’s existence and funding.

### C. The Local Cultural Sovereignty Council
Operationalize under a pre-ratified **Market Creative Charter** (Board to ratify within 14 days; full operationalization target: 90 days if no Charter exists).
- **Authority:** Binding veto over all local marketing execution—concept, script, casting, visual language, media placement. This is governance, not advice.
- **Scope:** Cultural execution in the market only. Global trademark, product safety, and fiduciary standards remain under global oversight.
- **Composition:** Paid professional rates. Selected via transparent, multi-stakeholder criteria. Includes linguists, historians, ethicists, youth representatives.
- **Integration:** Review begins at **concept genesis**. Global Marketing cannot override; escalation to CEO permitted only on global trademark conflict, never cultural judgment.

### D. Executive Accountability (Privacy-Compliant)
Disclose that *"appropriate personnel actions, including employment separations and compensation adjustments, have been taken against individuals responsible for the campaign’s approval."* If local law prohibits naming, describe consequence categories with specificity.

### E. Abolish Extractive Co-Creation
Do not launch public contests for redemption ideas. Issue professional RFPs to local agencies, paying market rates. License ideas; do not confiscate them.

### F. Rewrite the Creative Brief
Permanently embed a **Cultural Immersion Clause:** No concept proceeds to production until local teams and the Sovereignty Council confirm the idea was generated *with*, not merely reviewed by, cultural insiders.

## VI. PHASE 5: LONG-TERM REPUTATIONAL RECOVERY (Months 3–24+)

- **Embedded Apprenticeship:** Require global creative directors and marketing VPs to spend **minimum two weeks residing in the market**, apprenticing with local cultural institutions. Co-signed reflective brief required to renew budget authority.
- **Glocal Creative Sovereignty:** Grant local markets autonomous creative budgets and authority to originate campaigns locally. Target: majority-local campaign origination within 24 months.
- **Economic Transparency:** Publish an annual market-specific report detailing percentage of marketing spend with local vendors (not global agencies with local outposts), wages paid to local talent, and local tax contribution.
- **Permanent Office of Local Relations:** Maintain a standing, local-staffed Office of Local Relations and Cultural Accountability with authority to halt any campaign at any stage.

## X. CLOSING DIRECTIVE

The objective is not to "win back" consumers or return to a pre-crisis baseline. The objective is to become a brand that is **structurally incapable of repeating the offense** because it has permanently surrendered the unilateral power to define what is funny, acceptable, or respectful in a culture that does not belong to it.

Reputation is not managed. It is granted daily by the community. The only acceptable outcome is a company that has transferred the authority to prevent harm to those always entitled to hold it.
