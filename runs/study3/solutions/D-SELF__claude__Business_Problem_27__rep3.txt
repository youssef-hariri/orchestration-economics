## DECISION FRAMEWORK

### Primary Recommendation: HYBRID ($2.0M Debt + $2.5M Equity)

**When to Choose:**
- Founders want 25%+ growth BUT also want to retain 70%+ ownership
- Market window is tight (18–20 months to consolidation)
- Revenue mix is stable with predictable unit economics
- Comfortable with modest board observation (equity investor observer seat)

**Terms Summary:**
- **Venture Debt:** $2.0M at 7.5% interest + 1.0% warrant, 24-month amortization, lite covenants
- **Equity:** $2.5M at $50M pre-money → $52.5M post-money (4.8% dilution)
- **Effective Cost:** 6–7% blended (debt 7.5% + equity 4.8% dilution cost)
- **Founder Outcome (M24, Base Case):** $14M revenue, 72% ownership, $100.8M wealth

**Contingency Triggers for Hybrid:**
- If venture debt interest > 8.5% OR warrant > 1.5%: Negotiate down or activate Debt-Only backup
- If equity valuation < $50M post-money: Reduce equity check to $2.0M at $50M pre-money, or add debt instead
- If Series A not committed by M18: Hybrid is still viable (debt amortizes, founders can prepay and continue bootstrap)

### Backup Plan 1: DEBT-ONLY ($2.0–$3.0M Venture Debt)

**When to Choose:**
- Founders absolutely require 78%+ equity (control is paramount)
- Willing to accept 8–10% YoY growth and market share loss
- Can live with covenant restrictions (but see "lite" covenant structure below)
- Prefer independence over aggressive growth

**Trigger to Activate:**
- Series A valuation offered is < $50M post-money (undervalues business)
- Series A equity dilution demanded > 6% (too much)
- Founders explicitly state "I must keep 78%+ ownership, market window be damned"

**Terms Summary:**
- **Loan Amount:** $2.0–$3.0M
- **Interest:** 7.5% APR
- **Warrant:** 1.0% per annum (cumulative ~2% by M24)
- **Covenants (Lite):** Min. cash $1.5M, EBITDA >$1M annually, leverage <3.5x
- **Amortization:** 24–36 months (quarterly payments)
- **Founder Outcome (M24, Base Case):** $10M revenue, 78% ownership, $5.85M wealth (at 3x EBITDA valuation)

**Critical Warning:** Debt-only achieves 8–10% YoY growth, which FAILS the market window test. Competitor growing 40% will own 60%+ market share by M18–M24. Founders accept becoming a niche player (high CAC, retention-focused, limited upside).

### Backup Plan 2: PURE EQUITY ($5.0M Series A)

**When to Choose:**
- Growth velocity is paramount (32% YoY target acceptable)
- Founders confident they can hit revenue targets ($16M M24)
- Market window is extremely urgent (rival consolidating faster than expected)
- Willing to accept dilution path to 58% by Series B for rapid scaling

**Trigger to Activate:**
- Rival raises $50M+ and is consolidating market rapidly (M3–M6)
- Founders see clear path to $100M+ revenue and want to dominate market
- Series A investor offers valuation ≥ $60M post-money (feels fairly valued)

**Terms Summary:**
- **Investment:** $5.0M at $60M pre-money → $65M post-money (7.7% dilution)
- **Investor Rights:** 1 board seat, pro-rata Series B rights, standard governance
- **Founder Outcome (M24, Base Case):** $16M revenue, 70% ownership post-round (58% post-Series B), $104M wealth
- **Series B Dependency:** If revenue misses 20%+ ($12.8M actual vs. $16M target), Series B valuation drops to $85–110M pre-money, founder wealth haircut to $52–72M (50%+ loss)

**Critical Warning:** Pure equity has **extreme downside optionality.** If revenue shortfalls occur (common in 30–40% growth plans), Series B becomes jeopardy, and founders face cram-down dilution. Wealth impact: –39% to –68% in downside scenarios.

### Fallback Plan 3: ORGANIC GROWTH (Bootstrap)

**When to Choose:**
- Founders explicitly accept market share loss (willing to be niche player)
- Prioritize lifestyle business + independence over wealth accumulation
- Revenue is durable + churn low (>95% NRR), so 10% organic growth sustainable
- Personal financial runway (savings) sufficient to weather slow growth

**Trigger to Activate:**
- All external fundraising falls through (no acceptable debt/equity terms available)
- Founders want to "test market" 12 months before raising capital
- Market consolidation is acceptable trade-off for 100% founder ownership

**Terms Summary:**
- **Capital Raised:** $0
- **Founder Outcome (M24, Base Case):** $6.75M revenue, 100% ownership, $13.5M wealth
- **Market Position:** Relegated to SMB segment; rival owns 60%+ enterprise market by M24
- **Exit Scenario:** Acqui-hire by larger platform (valuation $75–150M), or lifestyle business indefinitely

**Critical Warning:** Organic path FAILS the market window test by M12–M15. Rival's 40% growth + $25M capital creates winner-take-most dynamics. By M24, founder's company is perceived as niche player, lowering exit multiple (2–3x revenue vs. 5–8x for growth companies). **Founder wealth: $13.5M vs. $100M+ hybrid path = 7–8x opportunity cost.**

#### Venture Debt Terms (Exact)

| Term | Specification |
|------|---|
| **Principal** | $2,000,000 |
| **Draw Schedule** | Tranche 1: $1.0M upfront (M0), Tranche 2: $500K (M3), Tranche 3: $500K (M6) |
| **Interest Rate** | 7.5% APR (paid monthly, ~$12.5K/month initially) |

| **Warrant Exercise** | Only in-the-money if exit value >$70M; cumulative dilution 2% by M24 if exercised |
| **Prepayment** | No penalty; encouraged if cash position >$15M (founders can retire debt early to minimize warrant dilution) |
| **Amortization** | 24 months, quarterly principal payments of $166.7K beginning Q1 after close |

**Covenant Structure (Lite, Non-Binding):**

| Covenant | Threshold | Measurement | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| **Minimum Cash Balance** | ≥$1.5M | Monthly | Unlikely to breach if revenue tracks; founders have $8M starting cash |
| **EBITDA Floor (Annual)** | ≥$1.0M (first 12 months: $800K) | Semi-annually (M12, M18, M24) | Relaxed for Year 1; tightens M12+ as company scales |
| **Leverage Ratio** | Net Debt / EBITDA ≤3.5x | Semi-annually | Permissive; company never approaches this in base or shortfall case |
| **Prohibited Actions** | Require lender consent: • New debt >$500K • Dividends if EBITDA <$1.5M • Asset sales >$1M | As applicable | Standard restrictions; don't impede growth |
| **Reporting** | Monthly P&L + balance sheet; quarterly board updates | Monthly / quarterly | Minimal overhead; standard investor reporting |

**Why These Covenants Are Founder-Friendly:**
- EBITDA floor of $1.0M is low; at $50M revenue at 25% margin = $12.5M EBITDA, very safe cushion
- Leverage ratio of 3.5x is permissive; company would never approach this unless distress
- Prohibited actions don't restrict hiring, marketing, or product investment (only new debt + dividends + major asset sales)

- Observer seat (not board seat) = input without control override
- Broad-based anti-dilution = down-round protection without founder penalty
- Drag-along only if 5x+ return = protects founder upside (can't be forced into 2–3x exit)
