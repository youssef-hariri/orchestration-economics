## 1.3 Why Prior Restructurings Failed

### Restructuring Wave 1 (18 months ago): Headcount cuts + SG&A consolidation
- **Aim:** Cut fixed costs by $3–4M/year
- **Result:** Costs fell, but EBITDA loss widened (–$18M → –$22M) because:
  1. Revenue fell faster than costs ($10M drop); fixed-cost absorption worsened
  2. **Margin compression** (discounting, overruns, utilization) persisted unaddressed
  3. Customer base was NOT rationalized; still heavy in low-margin, high-touch segments

### Restructuring Wave 2 (9 months ago): Further headcount trim + procurement savings
- **Aim:** Cut $2–3M via headcount + achieve $1.5–2M in procurement savings
- **Result:** EBITDA loss worsened again (–$22M → –$28M) because:
  1. "Low-revenue" customer exits eliminated small-customer SG&A but kept low-margin revenue
  2. Procurement wins were one-time; didn't offset accelerating fixed-cost deleverage
  3. Broken operations (pricing, utilization, overruns) were untouched

### Root Cause of Failure
**Both waves treated unit as "overhead problem."** Wrong diagnosis. Unit is a **"margin problem masquerading as overhead problem."**

### Segment B: Fixable ($40M, 2–8% CM) + B2 Sub-Segment ($10M, –5% to +2% CM)

**Profile (B):** 12 mid-size customers; mixed tenure; contract-based + transactional; price-sensitive.

## 3.2 Phase 0: Emergency Stabilization (Days 0–14)

### Milestone 0.1: Leadership Authority & Governance (Days 1–5)

**Owner:** Board + Group CFO + Unit CEO

**Actions:**

1. **Appoint or confirm Unit CEO with turnaround credibility**
   - If incumbent CEO lacks change-management track record, deploy interim co-executive or secondment from group for 60-90 days
   - Decision authority: CEO approves all decisions <$2M without Steering Committee; all >$2M require weekly sign-off

2. **Establish Turnaround Steering Committee**
   - Members: Group COO, Unit CEO, Group CFO, Group Chief Commercial Officer, External Restructuring Advisor
   - Cadence: Weekly, Friday 2pm; 1-hour sessions; rolling 4-week look-ahead
   - Authority: Approve customer exits, repricing, headcount, capex, capability imports

3. **Communicate turnaround intent to unit, customers, employees**
   - **Day 1 Internal Message:** "Board has approved 18-month turnaround. We will exit unprofitable customers, renegotiate pricing with profitable accounts, reduce overhead, and focus on high-margin segments. Jobs at risk in unprofitable areas; jobs protected in strategic roles."
   - **Day 2 Customer Message (Top 30):** "No changes to your delivery team or terms. We are streamlining to deliver better value. Account leader will reach out Month 1 to strengthen partnership."
   - **Day 3 Employee Town Hall:** Honest assessment of loss trajectory, commitment to preserving strategic roles, timeline for organizational changes.

**Quantified Impact:** Reduce voluntary attrition from 25–30% to <15% by Day 30 (via transparency + hope); proactive communication prevents customer defections pre-emptively.

**Go/No-Go (Day 5):**
- ✅ CEO decision-making authority is clear and endorsed by Board
- ✅ Steering Committee first meeting is scheduled
- ✅ Top 20 customers have been contacted; no surprise defections in first week
