# EXECUTIVE BRIEFING: Industrial Services Unit (ISU)
**Subject:** Strategic Reassessment, Turnaround Mandate, and Exit Trigger Protocol  
**Date:** October 26, 2023  
**Status:** Board-Ready Final Assessment

---

## 1. Executive Summary: The Structural Verdict
The Industrial Services Unit (ISU) is currently in an **execution-driven death spiral caused by a structural value-proposition mismatch.** Previous attempts to solve losses through cost-cutting were insufficient because they did not address the unit’s core economics: **the cost to serve is decoupled from market pricing.** 

The unit has reached a "tipping point." It is currently functioning as a drag on the parent group’s balance sheet while its "best-in-class" talent is defecting. The following plan mandates a 12-month pivot toward a "Specialized Premium" model. If this model fails to yield specific margin hurdles by Q2, the Board must initiate a predefined exit strategy to protect the Group’s broader client relationships.

---

## 2. The Turnaround Framework: "Lean-to-Win"
The goal is to stop the bleed by transitioning from a "Volume-Provider" to a "Strategic Niche-Specialist."

### Strategic Milestones
| Timeline | Focus | Key Performance Indicator (KPI) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: The Firewall** | Implement Internal Transfer Pricing | Formalized SLAs; Group divisions absorb cost of "Relationship Hedges." |
| **Q2: Margin Sanitization** | Force-Rank & Purge Contracts | Exit bottom 30% of legacy revenue; reach monthly break-even. |
| **Q3: Talent Retention** | Equity-Linked Stay Bonuses | 70%+ retention of "Critical Talent" via carve-out participation. |
| **Q4: Growth Validation** | Audit New Order Book | Weighted average margin on new wins must exceed 15%. |

---

## 3. Risk Mitigation: The "Contagion" Buffer
To prevent the turnaround from damaging the Group’s profitable divisions, we are implementing two safeguards:

*   **The Communication Protocol:** Before the 30% contract purge in Q2, the Group’s executive sponsors must deliver a unified message to key clients: *“We are moving to a premium, boutique service model to ensure higher quality and reliability.”* This frames the exit as a strategic improvement rather than a sign of instability.
*   **The "Kill Switch":** If monthly EBITDA does not trend toward break-even by the end of Q2, the board shall trigger the **Immediate Divestment Track.** This removes the "wait-and-see" risk and prevents further erosion of the Group’s reputation.

---

## 4. The Exit Protocols (Contingency)
Should the Q2 milestones be missed, the Board will choose between these three pathways based on the criteria below:

1.  **Strategic Divestment (Primary):** Sell to a competitor with lower overhead. 
    *   *Condition:* Must include a transition service agreement (TSA) to maintain Group client continuity.
2.  **Management Buyout (Secondary):** Transition the unit to a private vehicle led by the current leadership.
    *   *Condition:* The parent group retains a 15–20% minority interest and a "Right of First Refusal" for future services.
3.  **Managed Shutdown (Tertiary):** A structured liquidation.
    *   *Condition:* Triggered only if the unit’s market value is zero and its continuation poses an ongoing operational liability.

---

## 5. Board Decision Framework: Stay vs. Exit
The Board is requested to greenlight this plan with the following commitment: 

**We will pursue the turnaround ONLY IF:**
*   The Group divisions agree to transparent, internal "Transfer Price" subsidies for strategic support.
*   The unit leadership demonstrates the authority to terminate legacy accounts that drag down project-level margins.
*   The "New Order Book" (post-restructuring) proves the market will support a 15%+ margin on our core service lines.

**Failure to hit the Q2 EBITDA break-even target will be interpreted as a failure of market fit, triggering an automatic move to divestment.**
