## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**RECOMMENDATION: Proceed with Focused 12-Month Turnaround Plan with Parallel Exit-Option Preparation**

The industrial-services division's three-year losses are **35% Structural / 65% Execution-Driven**, requiring surgical intervention on two fronts:

1. **Exit the Structural Decline:** Custom Engineering and Retrofit services ($5M revenue; −41% CAGR) are permanent market casualties. Divesting these lines ($1.5M–$2.5M proceeds; 8–12 month timeline) removes ~$800K–$1.2M annual loss and clarifies strategic focus.

2. **Turnaround the Execution Failures:** Core Maintenance & Reliability services ($15.5M; 22–28% margins) are viable. The critical insight is that **~$8.6M–$9.8M revenue (42–48%) comes from customers who also buy from profitable group divisions.** These shared-customer accounts show only **8–12% share-of-wallet with this unit**—a classic execution failure, not market unavailability.

**Financial Target (Month 12, if turnaround succeeds):**
- **Revenue:** $20.5M → $22–$23M (+5–10% via shared-customer penetration + utilization recovery)
- **Gross Margin:** 22% → 24.5–25% (+250–300 bps via pricing discipline + labour efficiency)
- **EBITDA:** −$2.0M → **+$0.3M–$0.8M** (breakeven-to-modest profit; path to profitability Year 2)
- **Order Book:** 12-month visibility restored; churn <8%; shared-customer retention 95%+

**Decision Framework:**
- **Month 6 = PRIMARY GO/NO-GO:** If revenue ≥$21M, margin ≥23%, SLA ≥93%, and shared-customer wins ≥$250K, continue turnaround. Otherwise, initiate exit process (target Month 18–20 divestment).
- **Month 9 = FINAL COMMITMENT:** Confirm breakeven trajectory or activate urgent wind-down.
- **Exit Value:** $5M–$8M standalone divestment; $3.5M–$5M core-services retention. Shared-customer relationships remain embedded in group (via other divisions).
