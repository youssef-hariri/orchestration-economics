### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The division will reduce quoted customer lead time from 14 weeks to 6 weeks on certified modular products by eliminating systemic wait time—not by waiving certified quality boundaries. Today, approximately 80 percent of the 14-week quote is non-value-added queue, batch, and buffer. The improvement strategy rests on four pillars:

1. **Pre-certified modularity**—front-loading design validation so that only document release, not engineering certification, sits on the order critical path;
2. **Strategic decoupling inventory**—pre-positioning long-lead metallurgical components to remove supplier cycle dominance;
3. **Flow discipline**—integrating independent quality verification into production cells and treating inspection, test, and paint as finite-capacity work centers;
4. **Structural quality independence**—fortifying code-mandated hold points while compressing the administrative and physical queues around them.

Six-week quoting will be available only for product families that have completed full platform validation and earned certifying-body approval—a 12- to 18-month prerequisite. Until then, a disciplined two-track quoting model protects both speed and integrity. This plan includes statistically rigorous validation thresholds, digital data-integrity controls, and unambiguous rollback triggers to prevent the boundary erosion that drives warranty exposure.

### 1. CURRENT-STATE DIAGNOSIS: WHERE THE 14 WEEKS ARE LOST

Value-stream analysis (70 working-day baseline) reveals that value-adding touch-time is roughly 14 days. The remaining 56 days are pure waste: queue, batch, serial hand-offs, and unmanaged variation buffers.

| Value-Stream Stage | Current Days (Working) | Nature of Time Loss |
|---|---|---|
| **Order Processing** | 3 | Config errors, credit-hold batching, manual BOM hand-off |
| **Engineering Document Release** | 2 | BOM/router drafting, ERP entry, revision control |
| **Engineering Design Certification** | 5 | PE calculations, WPS/PQR, submittal review—serial, expertise-bound |
| **Procurement** | 28 | Long-lead castings/motors; weekly PO batching; supplier raw-material cycles |
| **Receiving / Incoming Verification** | 4 | Queue for laydown, clerical processing, and inspection staging |
| **Production & Assembly** | 10 | Large transfer batches; expedite chaos; unbalanced cycle times |
| **In-Process Inspection Queues** | 6 | Units wait for remote inspectors; NCRs sit in batch review |
| **Final Certification & Test** | 4 | Test-lab bottleneck; queue for certified sign-off and burn-in |
| **Paint, Prep & Shipping** | 3 | Batch painting schedules; freight consolidation; documentation delays |
| **Management Buffer** | 5 | Protective padding added because upstream variation is unmanaged |
| **TOTAL** | **70 days (14 weeks)** | **~56 days are non-value-added wait/queue/buffer** |

**Root Causes:**  
- Engineering design certification is treated as a document-release task, serializing procurement.  
- Zero strategic inventory exists for metallurgical long-lead items, making the entire quote hostage to foundry and motor-build cycles.  
- Quality is organized as a remote function, creating physical distance between verification and value-adding work.

### 2. RECOMMENDED CHANGES

#### A. Process Changes

| Initiative | Action | Lead-Time Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Configure-to-Order (CTO) Modularity** | Pre-engineer and pre-certify standard mechanical platforms, electrical architectures, and weldment groups. Design dossiers are submitted to the Notified Body / Authorized Inspector (AI) *before* any 6-week quote is published. CPQ automates BOM/router document release only; it is validated to block orders lacking current design approval. | Document release: 2 days → 0.5 days. Design certification: 5 days → 0 days for certified modules. |
| **Parallel Final Prep** | Crating, dunnage, and commercial documentation may be pre-staged during final test. **Certification labels, nameplates, and serialization may not be affixed until the AI digitally signs the final test report.** | ~1 day compression without contaminating the certification boundary. |
| **CONWIP Flow Lanes** | Fabrication and assembly operate under WIP caps with FIFO sequencing. Modeled overflow containment lanes isolate quality-hold units so that a single Andon does not stall the entire line. | Eliminates production queues and batching delays. |

#### B. Inventory & Postponement Changes

| Initiative | Action | Lead-Time Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategic Decoupling Inventory** | Pre-position the top 12 longest-lead raw and semi-finished items (rough castings, large drive motor subassemblies) in a company-owned hub or supplier-held buffer. Procurement exposure drops from 28 days to **10–12 days** for configured units. | Procurement: 28 days → 10–12 days |
| **VMI / Pull Systems** | B/C commercial parts (seals, fittings, hardware) move to vendor-managed inventory with blanket orders. Lot traceability is captured at consumption via barcode/RFID before the part enters the first operation. | Eliminates PO administration and receiving queues for non-code items. |
| **Honest Inventory Economics** | The procurement compression is explicitly inventory-driven. The plan budgets the associated working-capital increase and obsolescence reserve; no metallurgical cycle-time claims are made. | Transparent capital-for-speed trade. |

#### C. Scheduling Changes

| Initiative | Action | Lead-Time Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Finite Capacity Scheduling (APS)** | Replace infinite MRP queues with finite scheduling that loads inspection, test, and paint as capacity-constrained work centers with dedicated time buckets. | Removes hidden queue in quality and test. |
| **Two-Track Quoting** | *Fast Track*: 6-week quote only for SKUs in the certified modular catalog. *Standard Track*: 8–10 weeks for custom engineering where design certification remains serial. | Prevents special-order chaos from consuming standard-line capacity. |

#### D. Supplier Changes

| Initiative | Action | Lead-Time Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Administrative / Setup Compression** | Joint kaizen with machined-component and fabrication suppliers to reduce order-cycle batching and setup time. Realistic target: **15–25%** reduction in administrative/processing lead time. | Modest, credible procurement compression. |
| **Dual-Source / Near-Source** | Add a domestic or near-shore source for the single-source rough casting on the critical path. Backup stock held on consignment or firm commitment. | Reduces catastrophic tail risk. |
| **Streamlined Receiving for Code Items** | **100% MTR verification and positive material identification (PMI) are maintained** for all pressure-boundary, alloy, and NACE-rated materials. Compression is achieved through pre-staged inspection bays and pre-populated digital dossiers (supplier transmits MTR electronically before arrival). | Receiving: 4 days → 0.5 days for code items. |
| **Dock-to-Stock for Non-Code Commercial** | Proven suppliers of commercial hardware may bypass receiving inspection if lot traceability is digitally captured. | 0 days queue for qualified commercial receipts. |

### 3. QUALITY GATE PROTECTIONS

All improvements are governed by a live **Regulatory Impact Register** maintained by the Chief Quality Officer (CQO) and reviewed by the external certifying body. Every change is classified before execution:

| Classification | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Compressed** | Same rigor, less queue/wait | Pre-staged test fixtures; digital MTR review |
| **Moved** | Inspection relocated upstream into flow, with equivalent capability | Central lab radiography → independent cell technician |
| **Unchanged** | Code-mandated hold point; no schedule-driven alteration | Hydrostatic witness; AI final sign-off; burn-in duration |
| **Removed** | Gate eliminated entirely | *Forbidden without written Notified Body / AI approval* |

**Fortified Boundaries:**

- **Independent Cell Quality Technicians.** Quality personnel are physically embedded in production cells but report **solid-line to the CQO**, not to Production or Procurement. They perform in-process dimensional, weld, and assembly verification immediately in the flow. No CTQ parameter may be verified by production personnel unless a formal delegation matrix—approved by the certifying body, backed by gage R&R studies, and blind-audit validated—is in place.
- **Untouched Code Hold Points.** Hydrostatic tests, AI witness points, pressure-test hold times, and burn-in profiles are **not reduced**. Final nameplate/label affixing occurs only after the AI’s digital signature.
- **Shift-Left Prevention.** Critical parameters (weld voltage, machining tolerances, torque) are monitored via live SPC with automatic machine lock-out on drift. Poka-yoke fixtures prevent physical misassembly.
- **Digital Data Integrity (ALCOA+).** The NCR and inspection systems enforce electronic signatures, timestamped audit trails, read-only archives for certified-hold-point records, and edit-history preservation. Retention aligns to the longer of warranty period plus five years, regulatory requirement, or customer contract.
- **Traceability Architecture.** Serial numbers are assigned at order release, but **lot-to-unit traceability** is locked at the first physical operation. VMI hubs must maintain lot segregation; commingled lots are rejected. Every pressure-boundary heat number is married to the unit serial before weld or machine.
- **Validation Discipline.** No process change scales beyond a pilot cell until:
  1. Gage R&R <10% on all cell inspection devices;
  2. Blind-audit detection rate ≥95%;
  3. 25-subgroup SPC demonstrating statistical control;
  4. 100% witnessed First Article Inspection on the first 3 units of each new module;
  5. 4 consecutive weeks of stable production at rate with zero critical NCRs attributable to the change;
  6. Updated PFMEA and Control Plan proving RPN does not increase.

### 5. CROSS-FUNCTIONAL ALIGNMENT PLAN

The lead-time, quality, and margin objectives are jointly owned. No function can optimize one variable at the expense of another.

#### A. Organizational Independence

- All Quality Technicians, Quality Engineers, and Supplier Quality Engineers report **solid-line to the CQO**. Physical co-location in cells or supplier sites is permitted for speed; organizational control by Production or Procurement is not.
- Supplier Quality Engineers collaborate with Procurement but are evaluated solely on supplier conformance and verification integrity, not on purchase-price variance.

#### B. Deal Desk — Exception Governance

A permanent **Fast-Track Deal Desk** staffed by Directors from Sales, Production, and Quality governs all quoting and routing exceptions:

| Exception Rule | Decision Protocol |
|---|---|
| Quote 6 weeks on certified module within capacity | Auto-approved per catalog |
| Quote <6 weeks, or quote on non-certified variant | Unanimous required; **Quality holds unilateral veto** on code or warranty risk |
| Production requests FIFO/CONWIP bypass for expedite | Unanimous required; Quality vetoes if it bypasses a certified hold point |
| Quality initiates value-stream freeze for defect investigation | Auto-approved for 48 hours; extension requires VP-level review |

#### C. Shared Metrics — The “Triangle”

| Metric | Target | Production | Quality | Sales |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Order-to-Certified-Ship Cycle Time** | ≤30 days (runners) | Owns build/test execution | Owns independent inspection cycle time | Owns clean order/config accuracy |
| **On-Time-In-Full (OTIF)** | ≥95% | Owns build to takt | Owns cert release on schedule | Owns promise-date accuracy |
| **First Pass Yield (FPY)** | ≥98% | Owns defect-free build | Owns CTQ definition & verification | Owns config/order accuracy |
| **Warranty Cost per Certified Unit** | ≤Baseline | Owns process control adherence | Owns gate integrity & field correlation | Owns field feedback loop closure |
| **Quoted Lead Time Accuracy** | ≥95% | Inputs capacity data | Inputs cert duration by SKU | Owns customer promise & catalog discipline |

#### D. Incentive Redesign

- **Sales:** Commission paid on **delivered margin × on-time delivery**. A 10% clawback applies to orders with config errors causing engineering rework. No commission on orders that bypass the Deal Desk.
- **Production:** Bonus tied to **throughput × FPY × safety**. High throughput with declining FPY pays zero.
- **Quality:** Bonus tied to **FPY × inspection cycle time × warranty PPM**. Perfect quality that stalls flow is unacceptable; fast certification with escapement is unacceptable.

#### E. Governance Rhythms

- **Daily War Room (15 minutes):** Cell Lead, independent Quality Technician, and Sales Order Specialist review OTIF, open NCRs, and order exceptions.
- **Weekly Steering Committee (1 hour):** Functional VPs review metrics, adjudicate capacity trade-offs, and review Deal Desk logs. Unresolved issues escalate to the CEO in the same session.
- **Monthly IBP / S&OP:** Sales provides 90-day demand by certified module; Operations commits to material pre-positioning; Quality commits to cell certification status and platform validation schedules.
