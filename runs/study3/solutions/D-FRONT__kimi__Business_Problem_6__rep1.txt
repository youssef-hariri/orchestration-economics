**Campaign:** SHIZUKA NA CHIKARA (静かな力 / Quiet Strength)  
**Client:** HANA BOTANICALS  
**Product:** Daily Equol & Calcium Blend  
**Market:** Japan  
**Launch Window:** 12 Months  
**Budget:** ¥300,000,000 JPY (~$2.0M USD)

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This campaign launches HANA BOTANICALS into the Japanese market with a strategy built on cultural discretion, regulatory precision, and emotional restraint. The target audience—women aged 45 to 60—experiences midlife transition (*konenki*) not as a medical crisis to be broadcast, but as a private, natural life phase managed with quiet dignity. 

The strategy rejects fear-based, symptom-centric, and public-facing “anti-aging” rhetoric in favor of **ritual integration**, **scientific humility**, and **privacy-first communication**. Legally, the campaign employs a **dual-track regulatory posture**: calcium and vitamin D are positioned under Japan’s Food with Nutrient Function Claims (FNFC / 栄養機能食品) framework, permitting standardized, legally pre-approved nutrient-function language, while the equol and L-theanine components remain in general food territory with soft, non-physiological wellness framing.

All mass-media creative uses abstract, seasonal metaphors and dignified maturity narratives (*keinen-bika* / 経年美化). Explicit life-stage language is reserved exclusively for opt-in, privacy-controlled channels—closed webinars, LINE Open Chat, and print publications—to prevent shame-by-exposure in family-visible or professional contexts.

Over 12 months, the campaign targets 18% aided brand recall, 150,000 first-unit trials, and a 25% 90-day repurchase rate, with a strict compliance mandate of **zero warnings** from JARO or the Consumer Affairs Agency.

## 1. MARKET & CULTURAL CONTEXT

**The Konenki Lens.** Japanese women predominantly frame the 45–60 life stage through *konenki* (更年期) as a holistic, natural transition rather than a biomedical deficiency. However, public identification with this stage remains stigmatized in workplaces and often within families. Campaigns that label women as “menopausal” in broadcast, OOH, or open social channels risk shame and alienation.

**Privacy as a Strategic Imperative.** The demographic is highly digitally literate (LINE, Instagram, Yahoo! Japan), but LINE push notifications visible on shared lock screens create acute privacy risk for sensitive health topics. All explicit symptom or life-stage references must be confined to **opt-in ecosystems** where the consumer controls visibility.

**Trust Architecture.** Persuasion equity flows vertically through institutional credibility, but it is strictly conditional on **non-commerciality**. Medical professionals—pharmacists, physicians, and Registered Dietitians (管理栄養士)—cannot ethically endorse food products under Japanese professional ethics and the Pharmacists Act. Trust is instead built through:
- Channel proximity (drugstore credibility by environment);
- Neutral, institutional traceability (“Fermented in [Prefecture], tested in [Lab]”);
- Authentic peer “monitor” (*monitā*) voices and anonymous aggregate reviews.

**Consumption Rituals.** Japanese wellness culture privileges *shoku-yōjō* (食養生—dietary nourishment) over pill-taking (*kusuri* / 薬). Capsule formats can signal medicalization. The campaign therefore positions consumption within existing food rituals—morning tea, miso soup, or breakfast—using polite, invitational *desu/masu* register rather than imperative commands.

**Household Economics.** For married consumers in this cohort, recurring purchases (¥3,500–¥4,500/month) may require justification within *okozukai* (household allowance) structures. The campaign supports this with family-friendly explanatory materials that normalize the expense as routine nutritional accompaniment.

## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK & PRODUCT POSITIONING

### Dual-Track Regulatory Strategy

| Component | Regulatory Classification | Permitted Positioning |
|---|---|---|
| **Calcium + Vitamin D** | **FNFC (栄養機能食品)** | *「カルシウムは、骨や歯の形成に必要な栄養素です。ビタミンDは、腸管でのカルシウムの吸収を促進し、骨の形成を助ける栄養素です。」* |
| **Fermented Soy Isoflavones (Equol)** | **General Food (一般食品)** | *「発酵大豆由来の植物性成分、エクオールを含みます。」* (Contains equol, a plant-derived compound from fermented soy, as part of your daily dietary rhythm.) |
| **L-Theanine** | **General Food (一般食品)** | *「緑茶に含まれるアミノ酸。夕方のお茶の時間になじむ、静かな味わいです。」* (An amino acid found in green tea; a gentle addition to your evening tea ritual.) |

**Mandatory Safety Advisory (All Packaging & Product Detail Pages):**  
*「大豆イソフラボン（エクオール）を含みます。現在医師の治療を受けている方、特にホルモンに関連する疾患をお持ちの方は、事前に医師にご相談ください。」*

**Mandatory FOSHU/FFC Disclaimer (All Materials):**  
*「本品は、特定保健用食品とは異なり、消費者庁長官による個別審査を受けたものではありません。」*

**Disclaimer Placement Rules:** Minimum 8pt font in print, placed adjacent to the product name within 1–2 cm. On digital assets, the disclaimer must appear within one screen of any health-adjacent keyword and must be readable without magnification.

## 5. MESSAGING & VALUE PROPOSITIONS

**Master Narrative:**  
*“Life transitions quietly. Your support can be quiet too.”*  
*(人生は静かに移ろいます。あなたの支えも、静かであればいい。)*

**Strategic Pillars:**

1. **Scientific Humility & Legal Precision.** Calcium and vitamin D claims use only standardized, legally approved FNFC wording. The product is framed as daily nourishment, never as a treatment, cure, or medical alternative.
2. **Cultivated Maturity (*Keinen-bika* / 経年美化).** All “anti-aging,” “reversal,” and “young skin” rhetoric is eliminated. Maturity is framed as accumulated refinement. Approved terms: **“aging-well” (エイジングウェル)**, **“jinsei no chūban” (人生の中盤—the middle movement of life)**, and **“honoring the seasons” (季節を大切に)**.
3. **Ritual Integration.** The daily dose is a 30-second moment of self-respect taken with existing rituals—tea, miso soup, or breakfast. It is never “medicine”; it is *“a gentle addition to your table.”*
4. **Traceable Transparency.** Supply chain openness (“Fermented in [Prefecture], tested in [Lab]”) builds institutional trust where celebrity charisma cannot.

**Linguistic Mandate:** All consumer-facing copy uses **polite *desu/masu* form**. Direct imperatives (e.g., “Buy now,” “Take this”) are prohibited. Use invitational language: *“朝のお茶と一緒に、いかがでしょうか。”* (How about enjoying this with your morning tea?)

## 6. CHANNEL STRATEGY

### Digital (40% — ¥120M)
- **LINE Official Account:** Central CRM hub. Push notifications limited to **generic seasonal wellness tips** (recipes, stretching) to prevent sensitive health keywords from appearing on family-shared lock screens. **Sensitive life-stage content** is housed in **LINE Open Chat** and **closed webinar registrations**, where opt-in ensures psychological privacy.
- **Instagram:** Aesthetic content featuring authentic women (not models) in natural light. Reels show the 30-second morning table ritual. No explicit *konenki* hashtags in public posts.
- **Yahoo! Japan Display & Native Ads:** Contextual targeting on recipe, travel, and lifestyle columns.
- **YouTube Pre-Roll (6s & 15s):** Placed before morning wide show clips, cooking channels, and travel vlogs. **Zero mention of symptoms or life-stage labels.**

### Retail & Trade Marketing (25% — ¥75M)
- **Drugstore Endcaps:** Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Cocokara Fine, Sugi Drug. Shelf-talkers and signage must **never** state “Pharmacist Recommended.” Use neutral proximity language: *「薬剤師がいるお店で」* (At a store with a pharmacist on duty) or *「店頭でご相談ください」* (Please consult our store staff).
- **Trial Sachets:** 7-day **powder stick packs** at ¥500 near checkout to minimize *kusuri* (medicine) connotations. Bundled with point-card promotions.
- **In-Store Digital Screens:** Silent loop videos showing the food-ritual aesthetic (powder dissolving into tea).

### Influencer, Peer Voice & Education (15% — ¥45M)
- **Tier 1:** General wellness advisors and nutritionists (non-credentialed commercial voices). **Registered Dietitians (管理栄養士) are excluded from product endorsement.** If expert education is desired, engage credentialed professionals **only** for non-branded, general-interest webinars (e.g., “Calcium in the Japanese diet”) with no product display, full disclosure, and JDA-compliant contracts.
- **Tier 2:** “Yutori Lifestyle” creators—women 50+ sharing tea culture, hiking, or pottery. Authentic, non-glamorous.
- **Tier 3:** User Monitors (*Monitā*) and anonymous aggregate social proof: *「10,000人の選択」* (The choice of 10,000 people).
- **Exclusion List:** No bikini-fitness influencers, no “biohacking” creators, no transformation narratives, and **no white-coat imagery** in proximity to the product.

### TV & Video (10% — ¥30M)
- **Terrestrial TV:** 15-second spots on morning wide shows (*Sukkiri*, *Zip!*). Language strictly limited to “daily rhythm support,” “seasonal care,” and “middle movement of life.” No *konenki*, no symptoms, no before/after.
- **CTV/OTT:** Hulu Japan, TVer for cost-efficient, measurable reach.

### Print & OOH (5% — ¥15M)
- **Magazines:** *Halmek*, *Lee*, *VERY*, *Story*. Explicit life-stage language is permissible because the reader **opts in** to the publication’s demographic targeting.
- **Train Station Domination:** JR Yamanote Line (Shinjuku, Tokyo, Shinagawa) during Obon and year-end gifting seasons. OOH uses seasonal imagery and the master narrative only; no symptom references.

### Research, Compliance & Testing (5% — ¥15M)
- Mock-JARO review of all creative. Pre-flight focus groups with women 45–60. Regulatory counsel sign-off on all FNFC label copy.

## 7. CREATIVE CONCEPTS

### Concept A: “Seasons of Vitality” (活力の季節)
- **Visual:** A woman prepares morning tea or miso soup. Beside the bowl, a small sachet dissolves gently. Through the window, **autumn leaves coexist with early spring buds** (*haru no me*)—symbolizing harvest and simultaneous renewal, never decline.
- **Copy:** *“Seasons turn gently. So do we. A quiet spoonful for your morning table.”*  
  *(季節はそっと移ろいます。私たちも。朝の食卓に、そっと添えるひとさじ。)*
- **Media:** Magazine spreads, Instagram, in-store posters, LINE Open Chat banners.

### Concept B: “Mienasa” (見えなさ / The Invisible Gentleness)
- **Visual:** A professional woman walks through a Tokyo station corridor. A **silk scarf floats gently** around her shoulders, or she places a structured tote on a café table. The suggestion of support is atmospheric—felt, not seen, and entirely within her control. **No backpacks, no burden metaphors, no pity.**
- **Copy:** *“Not a weight, but a breath. For the days you navigate with quiet resolve.”*  
  *(重さではなく、そっとした息吹。静かな意志で歩む日々に。)*
- **Media:** YouTube pre-roll, JR station OOH, CTV.

### Concept C: “Kisetsu no Salon” (季節のサロン / Seasonal Salon)
- **Format:** Monthly **closed webinars** and **LINE Open Chat live sessions** (registration-required, privacy-protected). **All-female expert panels** (female dermatologist, female general nutrition advisor, 50+ peer host). **Male authority figures are excluded** to avoid paternalistic gender dynamics.
- **Content:** General education on seasonal nutrition, skin hydration through diet, and calcium-rich Japanese foods. The supplement appears only as **one option among many** in the closing frame, with full sponsorship disclosure.
- **Ethical Safeguards:** No patient testimonials. No before/after photos. No symptom relief narratives. Archived for 7-day replay.

## 10. ETHICS & COMPLIANCE SAFEGUARDS

**Regulatory Precision**
1. **FNFC Protocol.** Calcium and vitamin D claims use **only** the standardized, legally approved FNFC wording. All equol and L-theanine copy remains in general-food territory with non-functional, descriptive language.
2. **Disclaimer Governance.** The FOSHU/FFC disclaimer appears on all materials with mandated font size, placement adjacent to the product name, and screen-proximity rules on digital assets.

**Authority & Endorsement Boundaries**
3. **No Medical Endorsement.** Pharmacists, physicians, and Registered Dietitians are **never** used to endorse the food product. White-coat imagery is **banned** from all product-adjacent creative. Trust is signaled by channel credibility and neutral staff availability only.
4. **Influencer Transparency.** All sponsored content carries clear **提供** (sponsored) labeling per the Consumer Affairs Act. Credentialed experts participating in education must disclose the absence of any commercial product arrangement.

**Content & Cultural Boundaries**
5. **Explicit Content Boundary.** The terms *konenki*, menopause, hot flashes, bone loss, and other symptom-specific language are **prohibited in broadcast TV, OOH, YouTube pre-roll, and public unpaid social feeds.** Permitted only in **opt-in, privacy-controlled channels** (magazines, closed webinars, LINE Open Chat, registered landing pages).
6. **Body Neutrality & Dignified Aging Mandate.** Creative briefs ban “anti-aging,” “reverse,” “bijukujo” (美熟女), and the erroneous *teinenpi* (低年齢皮). Approved framing: **“aging-well” (エイジングウェル)**, **“keinen-bika” (経年美化)**, and **“jinsei no chūban” (人生の中盤)**.
7. **Gender & Dignity Protocol.** Male authority figures are excluded from intimate health discourse to avoid paternalistic *sanfujinka* echoes. All expert panels and KOL partnerships in this domain feature female voices only.

**Consumer Safety & Transparency**
8. **Hormone Sensitivity Warning.** The equol advisory appears on **all packaging, product detail pages, and sampling materials** without exception.
9. **Evidence Repository.** A QR-linked page on the brand website lists peer-reviewed studies supporting **general ingredient categories** (not product-specific claims), translated into plain Japanese.
10. **Accessible Pricing.** Monthly supply is priced at ¥3,500–¥4,500. A 7-day trial sachet is available at ¥500. Powder stick formats are prioritized for trial to reduce *kusuri* association.

**Commercial Ethics**
11. **Gift Ethics.** The tertiary segment is framed as **“respectful accompaniment” (*o-sasae*)**, not diagnosis or deficiency. Every gift-oriented SKU includes a neutral, family-friendly explanatory leaflet.
12. **Subscription Consumer Protection.** Subscription (*teiki-bin*) sales comply with the Specified Commercial Transactions Act. Features include transparent monthly pricing, a **one-click pause** function, and **SMS-based cancellation** (not email-only). Auto-renew dark patterns are prohibited.

**Privacy by Design**
13. **Notification Discipline.** LINE Official Account pushes generic wellness content only. Sensitive life-stage education is delivered via registration-required closed ecosystems to prevent shame-by-exposure in shared notification environments.

**Self-Regulatory Review**
14. **JARO Prohibitions.** Strict ban on before/after imagery, efficacy testimonials, numerical improvement statistics, and white-coat + food-product juxtaposition.
15. **Pre-Flight Audit.** All broadcast and OOH creative undergoes **mock-JARO review** and external Japanese regulatory counsel sign-off before media booking.
