# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Our organization is currently losing one in three competitive bids solely on delivery speed. While our fastest competitors are quoting 6-week lead times for heavy industrial equipment, our current standard remains at 14 weeks. Previous attempts to compress this schedule resulted in a catastrophic spike in warranty claims, taking two years to recover from, and have entrenched a culture of blame between Sales, Production, and Quality.

**Project "Six-Week Standard"** is a comprehensive, 12-month operational transformation plan designed to reduce our lead time by 57% (from 14 to 6 weeks) without compromising a single certified quality gate. 

This plan moves beyond theoretical lean concepts, grounding our strategy in the physical realities of heavy equipment manufacturing and the strict regulatory mandates of certified inspections. By restructuring our engineering approach, optimizing physical flow, digitizing compliance, and fundamentally realigning commercial incentives, we will eliminate 8 weeks of non-value-add time, protect our quality certifications, and transform cross-functional friction into unified execution.

---

# 1. STRATEGIC CONTEXT & TIME-LOSS DIAGNOSTIC

To eliminate 8 weeks of lead time, we must first acknowledge where time is actually lost. In heavy equipment manufacturing, physical value-add time is less than 30% of the total lead time. The remaining 70% is consumed by administrative delays, physical bottlenecks, and process friction:

*   **Order-to-Release Delay (3 Weeks):** Sales quoting unengineered custom configurations, forcing Engineering to spend weeks generating custom Bills of Materials (BOMs).
*   **Supply Chain & Procurement (3 Weeks):** 8-to-10-week lead times for custom castings, compounded by incoming quality holding parts for days to manually verify material test reports (MTRs).
*   **Production Wait & Flow (4 Weeks):** Sub-assemblies waiting for overhead crane availability, batch-and-queue processing, and WIP clutter.
*   **Final Test & Certification Bottleneck (4 Weeks):** Certified inspectors spending 70% of their time compiling paperwork rather than physically inspecting, leading to massive delays at the final hydro-test and commissioning stages.

# 2. STRATEGIC PILLARS OF TRANSFORMATION

## Pillar I: Engineering & Physical Process Transformation
*Transitioning from theoretical lean to physical reality.*

*   **Deploy Phased Configure-to-Order (CTO) Parametric Engineering:** Engineering will pre-design and pre-cost 80% of our equipment into standardized modules. To mitigate execution risk, we will phase this rollout, launching the configurator for our top 20% highest-volume standard models in Month 1, expanding to the full 80% by Month 6. Sales will quote exclusively from this pre-approved library, eliminating the 3-week engineering delay.
*   **Implement Fixed-Position Assembly with Kitted Sub-Assemblies:** Recognizing that moving a 10-ton machine through "lean cells" is physically impractical, we will transition to a Fixed-Position Main Assembly Bay. Small components will be built in traditional flow cells, kitted on carts, and delivered just-in-time to the main bay. This eliminates WIP clutter and reduces overhead crane dependency.
*   **Design for Manufacturability (DFM) for Long-Lead Items:** We will mandate a DFM initiative to replace custom castings with standardized weldments or modular off-the-shelf castings wherever structural integrity allows. For unavoidable custom castings, we will shift to rapid-pattern machining and 3D sand printing to halve lead times.

## Pillar II: Supply Chain & Flow Optimization
*Strategic decoupling and constraint management.*

*   **Bifurcated Inventory Strategy:** 
    *   *Standard Components:* Implement strict Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI) for high-volume parts (motors, valves), located in point-of-use supermarkets.
    *   *Custom/Long-Lead Components:* Implement Capacity Reservations with foundries. We will purchase and hold raw material buffers (e.g., specific steel grades) at the supplier to secure machine time, rather than holding finished custom inventory.
*   **Protect the Constraint (Drum-Buffer-Rope):** We will explicitly identify **Final Test & Certified Inspection** as the system constraint. We will schedule this constraint first and utilize a 3-day time buffer immediately preceding it to ensure inspectors never starve for work, while preventing WIP from flooding the floor.
*   **Overhead Crane "Milk Run" Scheduling:** The overhead crane will be treated as a shared, scheduled resource. Supervisors must schedule heavy lifts 24 hours in advance; unscheduled "emergency" lifts will require VP-level approval.

## Pillar III: Uncompromising Quality & Regulatory Compliance
*Accelerating flow while maintaining strict structural independence.*

*   **Embed Independent Inspectors (Cross-Shift Protocol):** To eliminate the wait for centralized quality inspectors, we will reassign certified Quality personnel directly to the production floor. To guarantee auditable independence and satisfy ASME/ISO mandates, we will implement a **Cross-Shift/Cross-Team Inspection Protocol**: Inspectors will only perform certified sign-offs on work built by different shifts or distinct production teams. 
*   **Strategic Backfilling:** To mitigate transition risk and prevent resource shortfalls during the floor embedding phase, we will hire 1-2 external certified inspectors in Month 1 to backfill the centralized lab before moving existing staff to the floor.
*   **Strict Separation of Staging and Verification:** Production operators will be responsible for 100% physical staging of the certified gate (cleaning, grinding, gathering evidence). Independent Quality inspectors will only perform the certified test and sign-off. This separation of duties satisfies regulatory independence while drastically reducing non-value-add time.
*   **Digital Pre-Staging & Hard-Coded Poka-Yoke:** We will replace paper travelers with ruggedized digital tablets. Operators input data in real-time, automatically compiling digital weld maps and MTRs. Inspectors will spend 10 minutes verifying the physical part against the pre-staged digital record, rather than 2 hours doing paperwork. Furthermore, Bluetooth-enabled tools will physically lock out the next assembly step in the digital traveler until exact certified parameters (e.g., torque, pressure) are met.

## Pillar IV: Commercial Alignment & Cultural Integration
*Resolving the Sales-Production-Quality feud through structural alignment.*

*   **Implement Tiered Quoting Strategy:** Sales will no longer promise 6 weeks for highly custom equipment. We will introduce three tiers:
    *   *Tier 1 (Standard/CTO):* 6-week lead time (Heavily incentivized via higher commission).
    *   *Tier 2 (Configured Custom):* 10-week lead time.
    *   *Tier 3 (True Custom/Engineered):* 14+ week lead time.
*   **Unified Cross-Functional KPIs:** We will replace siloed metrics with shared goals: **OTIF** (On-Time In-Full) for delivery, **FPY** (First Pass Yield at Certified Gates) for quality, and **Standard Product Mix %** to drive the business toward fast-flow CTO models.
*   **Rules of Engagement Charter:** A formal, signed charter dictating operational boundaries. Sales will not accept "phantom" orders; Production will build to the Drum schedule and execute 100% quality staging; Quality will respond to digital holds within 2 hours and maintain absolute structural independence.

# 3. IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

**Phase 1: Stabilize, Standardize & Align (Months 1-2)**
*   Hire 1-2 external certified inspectors to backfill the centralized lab.
*   Finalize the parametric CTO configurator and Super BOMs for the top 20% highest-volume models.
*   Conduct the warranty spike post-mortem; implement physical Poka-Yoke fixtures.
*   Sign the Rules of Engagement Charter, launch Tiered Quoting, and implement the 1-week hard schedule freeze.

**Phase 2: Supply Chain, Digital Quality & Floor Embedding (Months 3-5)**
*   Launch VMI for standard components; establish capacity reservations for custom castings.
*   Deploy ruggedized digital travelers; begin digital pre-staging of quality docs.
*   Reassign existing certified Quality inspectors to embedded floor roles under the cross-shift independence protocol.
*   Launch the 18-month cross-certification program for select production staff (with mandatory administrative transfer to Quality upon completion).
*   Reorganize shop floor into sub-assembly flow cells supporting the fixed-position main assembly.

**Phase 3: Flow & Constraint Protection (Months 6-8)**
*   Fully transition to Drum-Buffer-Rope scheduling, pulling from the Final Test constraint.
*   Complete the DFM initiative to eliminate remaining custom long-lead castings.
*   Expand the CTO configurator to cover the full 80% standard model library.
*   Formalize the Quality Department structure into "Floor Certification Inspectors" and "Quality Assurance & Compliance."

**Phase 4: Sustain & Scale (Months 9-12)**
*   Fully operationalize the embedded independent inspection model and 18-month cross-certification pipeline.
*   Conduct a rigorous internal audit of the 6-week lead time process for Tier 1 products, specifically verifying strict adherence to quality independence protocols.
*   Officially launch the 6-week standard quote to the market; shift Sales compensation to heavily favor Tier 1 products.

# 4. CONCLUSION & CALL TO ACTION

Project "Six-Week Standard" is not merely an operational tweak; it is a fundamental realignment of how we engineer, build, inspect, and sell heavy industrial equipment. By acknowledging the physical constraints of our products and the regulatory rigidity of our certifications, we have designed a plan that ruthlessly attacks non-value-add time without cutting corners on quality.

Through the implementation of Tiered Quoting, we will immediately stop losing deals to faster competitors on standard products, while protecting our margins on custom work. By embedding *independent* certified inspectors on the floor and digitizing compliance, we will eliminate the administrative bottlenecks that currently strangle our throughput. Finally, by replacing siloed metrics with shared KPIs and strict Rules of Engagement, we will transform a culture of blame into a culture of continuous, compliant flow.
