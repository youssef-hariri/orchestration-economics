---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Crisis Overview:** A global consumer brand's advertising campaign in [MARKET] has drawn significant public backlash for disrespecting [specific cultural element]. The offensive content was approved through a process that lacked mandatory cultural expert review and community consultation.

**Response Philosophy:** Authentic accountability through transparent acknowledgment of organizational failure, immediate harm mitigation, genuine community partnership in solutions, and verifiable systemic change—not defensive communication or performance gestures.

**Strategic Approach:**
- **Immediate (0-24 hours):** Stop continued harm; acknowledge specific offense
- **Short-term (Days 2-8):** Private community engagement before public response; conduct root cause analysis
- **Medium-term (Days 9-30):** Public apology with accountability; implement structural process changes; launch training
- **Long-term (Days 31-180):** Demonstrate sustained cultural competency improvement; rebuild trust through consistent action

**Key Differentiator:** This strategy prioritizes community decision-making authority (not advisory consultation), independent verification of change, and documented accountability—not theatrical corporate response.
