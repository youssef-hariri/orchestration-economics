- **Payback Reality (Realized Cash Basis):**
  - *Gross Operational Payback:* Month 28
  - *Net Cash Payback:* **Month 40**
  - *Strategic Breakeven (including quantified customer-retention value):* Month 34
- **Binding Constraints:**
  1. **Strictly sequential construction waves.** No Wave *N+1* equipment procurement or construction funding is released until Wave *N* passes a 90-day operational stability gate. Design/planning for the next wave proceeds only under the Program Management budget.
  2. **No customer-visible disruption; no go-lives between October 15 and January 15.**
  3. **Zero involuntary layoffs at Site Alpha,** backed by a funded, three-tier pressure-release mechanism with defined activation criteria and reserves.

If the board demands a strict 36-month net payback, we must break the Alpha covenant, defer Delta entirely, or reduce Alpha to a lower automation degree. We do not recommend any of those trade-offs.

## 3. AUTOMATION TAXONOMY: THE 5 DEGREES

| Degree | Name | Technology Stack | Use Case |
|--------|------|------------------|----------|
| **1** | **Assistive** | RF/voice upgrades, ergonomic conveyors, semi-automated packing, WMS optimization | Low-volume, stable, aging workforce; preserve roles, reduce physical strain |
| **2** | **Targeted Accuracy** | Vision-based scan/sort, weight/cube audit tunnels, robotic palletizing/print-apply | Error-critical sites; fast payback with low disruption |
| **3** | **Collaborative** | AMR fleet (goods-to-person), pick-to-light/voice hybrid, outbound scan tunnels, WES | Moderate complexity; pilot proving ground for flagship stack |
| **4** | **Phased Collaborative** | AMR-based GTP pods, automated packing, WES-directed picking; **no AS/RS** to avoid construction freeze | High-volume community anchor; zone-based brownfield cutover |
| **5** | **Full Automation** | Shuttle-based AS/RS, robotic depalletizing/induction, auto-bagging, full WES/WCS | High-volume urban site; replaceable labor pool; maximize throughput density |

| Site | Volume | Turnover | Error Rate | Community Role | Degree | Sequence |

### Sequencing Logic & Strict Decision Gates

1. **Wave 1: Epsilon (Months 1–9).** The highest error rate threatens key-account renewals *today*. Targeted scan/sort/verify automation is low-disruption and protects revenue immediately.
   - *Gate 1:* Epsilon must achieve >99.0% order accuracy, zero key-account SLA breaches, and operational stability for **90 consecutive days** before Wave 2 construction funding is released (Gate clears Month 12).

2. **Wave 2: Gamma (Months 13–22).** Serves as the **integration pilot** for the AMR/WES stack. Design may begin Month 4 under PM budgets, but **no equipment is procured until Gate 1 clears.**
   - *Gate 2:* Gamma AMR fleet must demonstrate ≥98% target throughput and ≥99.5% accuracy for 5 consecutive days, pass ghost-shift protocols, and achieve absolute eNPS >+10 before Wave 3 construction funding is released (Gate clears Month 25).

3. **Wave 3: Beta (Months 25–34).** High volume + urban location = maximum financial return. Union partnership is formalized through a **Labor-Management Automation Appendix** written into the CBA before capital is committed.
   - *Gate 3:* Beta Zone 1 must stabilize for 90 days before Zone 2 build begins.
   - **Plan B Fallback:** If CBA demands exceed the $1.5M Labor Partnership Reserve or impose network no-layoff clauses that would extend net payback beyond Month 48, Beta automatically downshifts from Degree 5 (shuttle AS/RS) to Degree 4 (phased collaborative AMR/GTP), reducing capex by $2.5M and adjusting savings accordingly.

4. **Wave 4: Alpha (Months 37–43).** By the time we touch the town’s main employer, the playbook is de-risked through three prior waves. Phased zone cutovers prevent employment shocks.
   - *Gate 4:* Alpha Community Council and the Horizon Workforce Transition Committee must approve the human capital plan before equipment is ordered.

5. **Wave 5: Delta (Months 44–46).** Low volume and a stable, aging workforce mean the business case is retention, ergonomics, and quality of work—not headcount reduction. **Delta is carved out as an EHS/retention investment and is excluded from the automation ROI pool.**
