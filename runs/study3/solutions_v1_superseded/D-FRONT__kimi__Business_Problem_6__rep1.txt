**INTEGRATED MARKETING CAMPAIGN PROPOSAL**
**Campaign Title:** *Trust for a Whole Day* (온전한 하루를 위한 믿음)  
**Product:** MFDS-Notified Health Functional Food (HFF) Stick Supplement  
**Target Market:** South Korea (Republic of Korea)  
**Campaign Duration:** 12 Months (June 2025 – May 2026)

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This proposal outlines the launch of an MFDS-recognized Health Functional Food (HFF) in South Korea, a market where wellness is simultaneously a cultural tradition, a social performance, and a rigorously regulated science. The campaign, *Trust for a Whole Day*, positions the product as the daily meeting point of authentic Korean wellness heritage and certified modern bioscience—delivered in a three-second, stick-type ritual designed for *Ppalli-ppalli* (speed and efficiency) lifestyles.

The strategy rejects high-pressure, fear-based, or miracle-cure advertising in favor of **radical transparency, institutional credibility, and cultural respect**. All health claims are anchored exclusively to MFDS-approved functional language for immune support and fatigue recovery. The campaign builds a “Naver-first” ecosystem of expert-led education, drives revenue through Confucian gifting calendars (Chuseok and Seollal), and enforces a strict ethics and compliance framework that respects Korea’s age-stratified social values, collective decision-making culture, and zero-tolerance regulatory environment.

**Primary Business Goals:** Achieve Top 5 Naver organic search rankings within 90 days; capture 45% of Year 1 revenue during seasonal gifting peaks; maintain **zero** MFDS/KFTC advertising violations; and establish the brand as a legitimate, respectful participant in Korean health culture, not an exploitative outsider.

---

## 2. MARKET RATIONALE

South Korea was selected for its unique fusion of traditional Korean medicine (*Hanbang*) and biotech-driven wellness, its hyper-digital research culture, and its high consumer spending power. Key market dynamics include:

- **Sophisticated Supplement Penetration:** Probiotics, red ginseng, and stick-type HFFs are mainstream. Korean consumers conduct microscopic ingredient analysis before purchase, making *Naver* blogs and expert YouTube reviews the true point of sale.
- **Regulatory Stringency as a Trust Anchor:** The Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) and Korea Fair Trade Commission (KFTC) enforce heavy penalties for false advertising. Compliance is not merely a legal requirement but a competitive advantage that signals legitimacy to skeptical consumers.
- **Cultural Gifting Economy:** Health supplements are a primary *sebaet-deon* (gift) during Chuseok and Seollal, driven by Confucian filial piety. This creates predictable, high-volume revenue windows that demand respectful, non-exploitative creative.
- **Digital-First Collective Proof:** Purchase decisions rely on *Naver Café* community consensus and peer validation. A product achieves “national item” (*국민템*) status only after earning collective digital trust.

## 3. AUDIENCE INSIGHTS

### Segment A: The “Data-Driven Preventor” (Primary: 60% of target)
**Demographics:** Millennials and young Gen X (ages 28–42), white-collar workers, new parents.  
**Psychographics:** They view health as a managed portfolio. They perform exhaustive *Naver* research on bioavailability, raw-material origins, and MFDS notification numbers. They are ad-literate and deeply skeptical of celebrity hype, preferring licensed pharmacists and nutritionists.  
**Motivations:** Sustained energy for life outside work, long-term disease prevention, and family responsibility.  
**Revised Insight:** While they seek productivity, they are increasingly wary of brands that normalize exploitative overwork (*Gwarosa* culture). They respond to **evidence-based messaging that respects boundaries** and acknowledges that supplements support—but do not replace—rest.

### Segment B: The “Inner Beauty Optimizer” (Secondary: 25% of target)
**Demographics:** Gen Z and young Millennials (ages 19–29), all genders.  
**Psychographics:** They integrate supplements into lifestyle aesthetics and self-care routines. However, they are also vulnerable to appearance-anxiety pressures in Korea’s intense visual culture.  
**Revised Insight:** This segment engages with **routine integration and format aesthetics**, not physical transformation promises. They reject filtered perfection and respond to **body-neutrality and balance messaging**. They will not be targeted with “glow” or skin-outcome implications, nor will the product be visually paired with topical cosmetics, to avoid suggesting unapproved cosmetic efficacy.

### Segment C: The “Filial Silver Investor” (Tertiary: 15% of target)
**Demographics:** Adults ages 45–65 purchasing for themselves and aging parents (70+).  
**Psychographics:** Driven by gratitude and Confucian care ethics, not guilt. They value tradition, brand legacy, and TV home shopping authority, but demand dignity for elder recipients.  
**Revised Insight:** They seek to honor parents who “worked hard their whole lives,” but resent brands that weaponize filial obligation (*효도 압박*). They require accessible price tiers, formal linguistic registers (*jondaemal*), and **large-print safety information** for senior end-users who may be on conflicting medications.

## 5. CREATIVE CONCEPT

### Visual & Narrative Identity: “Lab + Heritage Duality”
Creative assets juxtapose clean, white laboratory aesthetics (glass, molecular structures) with tactile, authentic *Hanbang* textures (fermented root cross-sections, earthenware *onggi* motifs). The MFDS HFF mark is displayed as a prominent “seal of trust” in all layouts.

### The Stick Pack as Hero
The tear-and-pour stick format is the visual centerpiece: torn open in one motion, poured directly into the mouth or a tumbler of barley tea. No measuring, no water required. This honors *Ppalli-ppalli* efficiency and office-friendly social currency.

### Packaging Architecture
- **Daily Line:** Minimalist matte white/sage green sticks with Korean-dominant, bilingual ingredient callouts.  
- **Gifting Line:** Ceremonial crimson and gold rigid boxes in symmetrical paired configurations, tied with *maedeup* knots sourced from accredited Korean craft cooperatives. Gift SKU unboxing is restricted to dignified, formal-register media (TV home shopping, pharmacy displays) only. Casual Gen Z unboxing on TikTok/Instagram is prohibited to prevent trivialization of ceremonial symbols.

### Imagery & Language Guidelines (Compliance-First)
- **Strictly Prohibited:** Before/after comparisons; imagery of hospital gowns, syringes, or doctors “prescribing” the supplement; visual narratives of transformation (low energy → high energy); snow/thermometer imagery implying cold/flu prevention.
- **Gen Z Content:** Shot in morning contexts (breakfast, journals, gym bags) **never** on vanity tables beside skincare.
- **Silver Content:** Adult children present gifts with both hands and a subtle bow; parents are depicted as dignified and active, never frail. All copy uses formal *jondaemal*.
- **Worker Content:** No imagery of exhausted employees at 11 PM. Subjects are shown leaving work with energy for hobbies and family, not sustaining impossible hours.

## 10. ETHICS AND COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK: RESPECT FOR LOCAL VALUES, BELIEFS, AND NORMS

This campaign operates under a **“Compliance-First, Culture-Respectful”** governance model. The following framework ensures the brand honors South Korea’s social values, religious and cultural pluralism, and strict advertising norms while achieving commercial objectives.

### 10.1 Regulatory Compliance & Pre-Clearance
- **MFDS & KBA Pipeline:** All creative assets— including video storyboards, *Naver* banner copy, and TV home shopping scripts—are submitted for pre-clearance before flight. No exceptions.
- **Claim Adherence:** Only MFDS-approved functional wording (e.g., “면역기능에 도움을 줄 수 있음”) is used. Absolute language (“proven,” “guaranteed,” “100%”) and comparative superiority (“replaces four pills”) are banned.
- **Pharmaceutical Affairs Act Boundary:** The product is never positioned as a quasi-drug (의약외품). TV home shopping hosts verbally disclaim *“This is a Health Functional Food, not a medicine”* at open and close of every segment.

### 10.2 Health Claims Integrity & Anti-Exploitation
- **No Disease Subtext:** Creative never implies prevention or treatment of specific diseases (e.g., colds, flu, cancer). No winter-virus imagery or thermometer/snow visual motifs are used.
- **Anti-Overwork Safeguard:** Fatigue-recovery messaging explicitly includes the boundary affirmation: *“This supports your body’s recovery; it does not replace rest.”* The campaign partners with workplace wellness educators rather than productivity-maximization influencers to avoid normalizing *Gwarosa*.
- **Body Neutrality for Youth:** Gen Z creative omits “glow,” “radiance,” and skin-transformation language. The product is never placed adjacent to topical cosmetics to prevent implying unapproved cosmetic efficacy. Filtered skin imagery is prohibited in branded UGC.

### 10.3 Cultural Authenticity & Religious Sensitivity
- **Hanbang Authenticity:** A licensed 한의사 (Korean Medicine doctor) sits on the Brand Ethics Board. Traditional references are backed by transparent ingredient percentages. If traditional input is nominal, claims are downgraded to “inspired by” provenance only.
- **Gifting Dignity:** Filial messaging is framed as **gratitude**, never guilt. A mid-market **“Respect Tier”** gift option reduces class-based pressure. Ceremonial *maedeup* knots are sourced from accredited craft cooperatives, and their display is restricted to formal media to prevent trivialization.
- **Linguistic Hierarchy:** All Silver-segment creative uses formal honorifics (*jondaemal*). Casual register (*banmal*) is never directed at elderly consumers or used when handling ceremonial gift packaging.

### 10.4 Vulnerable Audience Protection
- **Elderly Dignity:** Elderly end-users are depicted as active and autonomous, never frail or infantilized. Gift packaging includes a standalone, large-print safety leaflet advising medication consultation because adult-child purchasers often discard outer boxes before gifting.
- **TV Home Shopping Safeguards:** Artificial scarcity (countdown timers, “only X left”) is banned for gift sets targeting older viewers. A 7-day unconditional return promise is displayed, and a “Consult your family” interstitial runs before purchase.
- **Child & Adolescent Exclusion:** The campaign does not target minors. All influencer partnerships are age-gated to 19+.

### 10.5 Influencer Transparency & Authenticity
- **Disclosure Mandate:** 100% of paid partnerships carry clear *광고 / 협찬 / “Paid Partner”* labels. Expert influencers disclose professional license numbers and avoid white-coat or pharmacy settings in branded content.
- **Anti-Astroturfing:** Naver Café sponsored reviews are capped at <20% of community mentions per month. Copy-paste briefs are banned; creators must include a standardized critical “Consideration” section. A third-party auditor scans for >15% phrase overlap across paid reviews.
- **Claim Prohibition:** Influencers sign an addendum banning 20+ high-risk phrases (e.g., “cure,” “prevent flu,” “doctor replacement”). AI + human moderation monitors comments and DMs within a 2-hour window.

### 10.6 Data Privacy & Consumer Safety (PIPA Compliance)
- **Health Data Wall:** KakaoTalk Open Chat auto-populates a disclaimer prohibiting personal diagnosis sharing. Nutritionists cannot answer medical contraindications in-chat; users are directed to physicians. Chat logs containing health keywords are not stored or used for retargeting.
- **E-Gift Consent:** KakaoTalk e-gifting uses a **Recipient Consent Gate**; the gift is held pending until the recipient accepts data terms. Recipient PII auto-deletes 30 days after delivery confirmation.
- **Anonymized SEM:** Search engine marketing uses contextual category targeting, not behavioral profiling based on disease-state keywords.

### 10.7 Monitoring, Enforcement & Redress
- **Quarterly Ethics Audits:** An independent ethics committee (including the 한의사 advisor, a consumer protection lawyer, and a cultural anthropologist) reviews campaign materials for cultural insensitivity and regulatory drift.
- **Whistleblower & Redress:** A public-facing ethics hotline and *Naver* Brand Blog channel allow consumers to report misleading claims or undisclosed ads. Substantiated complaints trigger immediate takedown and corrective action within 24 hours.
- **Stakeholder Accountability:** Marketing leadership signs an annual **Ethics & Compliance Charter** tying performance bonuses to violation-free quarters, not solely to revenue.
