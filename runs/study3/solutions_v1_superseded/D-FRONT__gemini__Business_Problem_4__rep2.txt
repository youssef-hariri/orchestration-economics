### Final Business Proposal: "Khai-Vị" (The "Appetizer" Snack Line)

After evaluating the feasibility, cultural resonance, and market potential, we have selected the **Umami-Prawn Whisps** concept. This product line will be branded as **"Khai-Vị"** (Vietnamese for "Appetizer/Starter"), signaling that these snacks provide a gourmet, street-food-inspired sensory experience in every bite.

---

### 1. Target Market & Strategic Rationale
*   **Target Market:** Urban youth, office professionals (ages 18–35), and the "commuter" demographic in Tier-1 cities (Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City).
*   **Strategic Rationale:** The Vietnamese snack market is moving away from generic, deep-fried snacks toward "modernized traditions." By selecting the *Bánh phồng tôm* (prawn cracker) archetype, we leverage high cultural familiarity, significantly reducing the "risk of the unknown." We differentiate ourselves through **superior texture engineering** (the "shatter" factor) and **gourmet flavor layering**, moving the product from a low-value commodity to a premium, craving-satisfying treat.

---

### 2. Product Line Identity
*   **Brand Name:** **Khai-Vị (KHAI-VI)**
*   **Flavor SKUs:**
    1.  **Golden Yolk & Calamansi:** The "Hero" SKU. Savory salted egg yolk balanced by the sharp, acidic punch of native calamansi peel.
    2.  **Tamarind, Chili & Seaweed:** A complex, "five-flavor" experience—tangy tamarind, lingering bird’s eye chili heat, and earthy seaweed notes.
*   **Ingredient Sourcing:** 
    *   **Base:** Premium, high-amylose jasmine rice flour for the essential structural integrity.
    *   **Umami:** Marine-certified, sustainably sourced prawn extract.
    *   **Botanicals:** Locally sourced calamansi zest (dried to maintain potency) and fresh chili oils to ensure authentic, aromatic profiles.
*   **Packaging Format:**
    *   **Structure:** A rigid, nitrogen-flushed tray-pack sealed inside a matte-finish, foil-lined pouch.
    *   **Function:** The tray prevents the delicate "Whisps" from shattering during transit on motorbikes, while the zip-lock seal provides critical humidity protection in Vietnam’s tropical climate.

---

### 3. Fitting Local Consumption Occasions
*   **The Commute/After-School (Portability):** The ultra-thin, shatter-crisp texture provides an immediate, rewarding sensory experience that fits the "grab-and-go" culture of motorbike commuters.
*   **Social Drinking (*Nhậu*):** The intense umami of the salted egg and the acidity of the calamansi are specifically formulated to act as the perfect foil to the crisp, clean finish of light Vietnamese lager, making it an ideal bar/pub accompaniment.
*   **Mid-Afternoon Slump:** The "Whisps" are portioned to provide a light, airy hit of flavor—enough to satisfy a mid-day craving without the "heavy" feeling that traditional, oil-saturated snacks cause.

---

### 4. Go-To-Market & Positioning Strategy
*   **Positioning:** **"Hương vị phố thị, đẳng cấp mới"** (City flavors, new sophistication). We are not selling "chips"; we are selling "deconstructed street-food intensity." We avoid the "industrial snack" stigma by highlighting the authentic, non-artificial ingredients (calamansi/tamarind).
*   **Distribution Strategy:**
    *   **Phase 1 (Penetration):** Secure exclusive "shelf-end" placement in convenience store chains (Circle K, GS25, FamilyMart) where the urban target demographic clusters.
    *   **Phase 2 (Expansion):** Partner with popular local *Nhậu* (beer garden) chains to offer "Khai-Vị" as a premium table snack, creating trial opportunities in a social setting.
*   **Marketing Strategy:** 
    *   **Digital:** Influencer-led "Shatter-Challenge" content on TikTok and Instagram, focusing on the audio and visual satisfaction of the extreme-thinness crisp. 
    *   **Sampling:** High-traffic "pop-up" sampling booths outside of major office towers during the 3:00 PM peak, emphasizing the "freshness" and "refreshing" calamansi finish. 
    *   **Pricing:** Premium-mass (mid-range). Priced 15-20% higher than standard prawn crackers to reflect the quality, but accessible enough for daily repeat consumption.
